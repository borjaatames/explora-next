## The route

You start at the **Calahorra Tower**, the fortress guarding the Roman Bridge, then head up with your guide to **two private rooftops** in the centre to see Córdoba from a new angle: the outline of the Mosque-Cathedral, the rooftops and towers of the old caliphal city. You finish on a rooftop with a glass of **D.O. Montilla-Moriles** wine, watching the sun set over the **Roman Bridge**.

## Times

- 1 October to 31 May: 6pm.
- 1 June to 30 September: 7:30pm.
- Monday to Friday only.

## Who it's for

- Couples and friends who already know Córdoba at street level and want another view.
- Anyone after a relaxed early-evening plan with great photos.
