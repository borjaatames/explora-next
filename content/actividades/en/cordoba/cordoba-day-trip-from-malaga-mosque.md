## What you will see

- The **Mosque-Cathedral** inside, with a guide.
- The **Jewish Quarter**: synagogue, souk and the **Maimonides** monument.
- The old town and **Calleja de las Flores**.

## How it works

You leave Málaga by coach. In Córdoba you do the guided Mosque visit and the Jewish Quarter walk, then have free time for lunch and a stroll before heading back to Málaga.

## Who I recommend it for

- People staying in **Málaga** or on the Costa del Sol who don't want to drive.
- Anyone wanting **Córdoba's essentials** in a single day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People who prefer **small groups**.
- Anyone with **reduced mobility**.
