## What you will see

- Abd al-Rahman III's **palace city**.
- The **House of Ja'far** and the great portico.
- The outside of the **Salón Rico**.
- Views over the **Guadalquivir plain**.

## How it works

With transfer, you leave Córdoba in an air-conditioned van; without it, you meet at the Visitor Centre. The archaeologist guide takes you through the main areas of the site for about three hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone into **archaeology and Al-Andalus**.
- Anyone wanting to see something **beyond the centre**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't speak **Spanish**: the guide only speaks Spanish on this tour.
