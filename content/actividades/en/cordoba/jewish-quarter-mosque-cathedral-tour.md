## What you'll see

A route that links the Jewish quarter and Córdoba's great monument with an official guide.

- **The Jewish Quarter:** the Calleja de las Flores, the 14th-century Synagogue and the maze of white alleys.
- **The Mosque-Cathedral:** the forest of columns, the mihrab and the cathedral inside, with entry included.
- **The thread of three cultures:** how Muslims, Jews and Christians lived together in Caliphal Córdoba.

## How it works

The meeting point is in the Jewish Quarter and is confirmed on booking. It's about **2 hours 15 minutes** with an official guide, with Mosque entry included.

## Who it's for

- If you want to see the essentials of Córdoba in one guided visit.
- If you're interested in the history of the city of three cultures.
- If you'd rather not arrange tickets and tours separately.
