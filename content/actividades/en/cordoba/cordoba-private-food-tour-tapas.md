## How it works

You meet your guide, an expert in Córdoba's food, in **Plaza del Triunfo**. Over two hours you visit **2 or 3 traditional bars** and try local specialities: **3 to 4 tapas** and **3 to 4 drinks** per person, including local wines, beer and vermouth. You finish with a **traditional dessert**.

Along the way, your guide tells you about Córdoba's food heritage and the stories behind its best-known tapas and wines.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to eat where the locals eat.
- Groups of four or more: the group price rises little.
