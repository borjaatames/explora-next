## How the day works

At the agreed time you're picked up in Córdoba and driven to the **Montilla** area in the south of the province: rolling hills of white **albariza** soil covered in vineyards and olive groves. There you visit a **family winery**, authentic and free of tourist frills, that makes wines for buyers across Spain and for some of Andalusia's best restaurants.

You start in the **vineyard**, learning how the local grape is grown; continue to the **winery** to see how the wine is made; and finish with **lunch overlooking the vines**, paired with several of the house wines. In good weather, lunch is served in the vineyard itself. Then it's back to Córdoba.

## Who it's for

- Wine lovers who want to discover Montilla-Moriles at source.
- Groups of three or four: the price per person drops a lot.
