## What you'll see

The **Palacio de Viana** is a 15th-century manor house that grew around **12 patios** and a **garden**. Each patio is different: orange trees, columns, wrought-iron grilles, the chapel patio, the cats' patio…

It is the best place in Córdoba to see patios **all year round**, without depending on the May Patio Festival.

## How it works

Entry to the patios and garden. The guided tour of the palace rooms is paid separately. Closed on Mondays. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
