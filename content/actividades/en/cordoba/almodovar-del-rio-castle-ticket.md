## What you will see

- The **towers and walls** of a fortress of Arab origin.
- The **keep**, with views over the Guadalquivir plain.
- The **chapel** and **medieval hall** in the owners' private area.
- Spots used as a **Game of Thrones** filming location.

## How it works

You make your own way to Almodóvar del Río, walk up to the gate and pick up a map. You explore the towers and walls at your own pace, with boards at each point and two projections on the castle's history, and finish in the chapel and medieval hall.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with a **car**, or happy to take the bus from Córdoba.
- **Families** and **Game of Thrones** fans.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: there are lots of steps.
- Anyone wanting a **guided visit** with live commentary.
