## What you will see

- The **Mosque-Cathedral**: columns, mihrab, maqsura and choir.
- **Calle Judíos** and the synagogue.
- The **souk** and the **Maimonides** statue.
- The Jewish Quarter's **flower-filled alleys**.

## How it works

You meet the guide in Plaza del Triunfo, go into the Mosque without queuing at the ticket office and, afterwards, walk through the Jewish Quarter together.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with **one day in Córdoba**.
- Anyone wanting the **Mosque and Jewish Quarter** with the same guide.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see inside the **Alcázar**.
