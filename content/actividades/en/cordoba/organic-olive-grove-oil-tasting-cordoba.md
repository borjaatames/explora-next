## What you will see

- A family-run **organic olive grove** on the outskirts of Córdoba.
- How the trees are looked after and how the **olives are harvested**.
- A **professional tasting** of three oils, with glasses and tasting sheet.

## How it works

You make your own way to the grove, where a family member welcomes you. You walk among the olive trees while they explain organic farming, do the guided tasting and finish with local produce drizzled with their oil. You leave with a gift bottle.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone interested in **olive oil** and where their food comes from.
- People with a **car** looking for a relaxed countryside plan.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without **their own transport**.
- **Wheelchair** users.
