## What you will see

- Five **private patios** in San Basilio.
- Their **flowerpots, wells and arches**.
- The **Santos Mártires hermitage** by the Guadalquivir.

## How it works

You meet the guide by the Mosque and walk to the Alcázar Viejo. You go into five houses, where the owners explain how they decorate and care for their patios. The tour ends by the river.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone visiting Córdoba in **spring**.
- **Flower lovers** and anyone curious about neighbourhood life.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for **monuments**: these are private homes.
