## What you will see

- The **Roman Bridge**, the **Calahorra Tower** and the **Mosque-Cathedral** from outside.
- **Plaza de la Corredera** and the **Jewish Quarter**.
- The churches of **San Lorenzo** and **Santa Marina**, the **Manolete** statue and the **Viana Palace**.

## How it works

You set off by bike from Calle Moriscos with a guide and a small group. Over two hours you stop at the key sights and a few lesser-known corners while the guide shares the history and suggests where to eat and what to see for the rest of your trip.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone arriving in Córdoba who wants to **get their bearings** on day one.
- People who've already seen the Mosque inside and want to **explore the neighbourhoods**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't speak **English** or Dutch.
- People wanting to go **inside monuments**: here you see them from outside.
