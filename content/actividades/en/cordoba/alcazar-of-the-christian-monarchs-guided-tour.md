## What you'll see

The **Alcázar of the Christian Monarchs** was the residence of the **Catholic Monarchs** during the Granada war; this is where they received **Christopher Columbus**. The guide covers its history, from fortress to Inquisition seat and prison.

The highlights are the **gardens**, with pools, fountains and cypresses, and the **towers**, which look out over the Mosque-Cathedral.

## How it works

One hour with a guide in Spanish or English, entry included. **Note:** because of works, the interior may be closed and the tour limited to the gardens. There are many stairs.
