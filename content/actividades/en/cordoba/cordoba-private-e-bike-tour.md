## What you will see

- **Moorish Córdoba**, with the Mosque and the Jewish Quarter.
- The **Reconquest-era churches** and the modern centre.
- Corners that cars and tour buses can't reach.

## How it works

You meet the guide on Calle María Cristina, choose a standard or electric bike and ride around the city for two hours, just your group, at the pace that suits you.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with young children, thanks to the child seat.
- Anyone wanting a **private tour** without paying a group rate.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People wanting to **go inside monuments**: it's an outdoor route.
