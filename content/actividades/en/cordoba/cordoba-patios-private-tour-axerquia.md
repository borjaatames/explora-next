## What you'll see

After pickup at your accommodation, a local guide takes you through the **Axerquía**, Córdoba's most authentic quarter. You go inside **three patios** in private homes, whose owners open their doors to share a tradition listed by UNESCO as **Intangible Cultural Heritage**: whitewashed walls, flowers, fountains and the sound of water.

Your guide explains the Roman and Moorish origins of these courtyards, how they changed over the centuries and the role they still play in daily life.

## Who it's for

- Visitors who've seen the Mosque and want Córdoba behind closed doors.
- Families: strollers are fine.
- Spring travellers, when the patios are in bloom.
