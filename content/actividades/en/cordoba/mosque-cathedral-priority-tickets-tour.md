## What you'll see

The tour covers the heart of the Mosque-Cathedral with an official guide and priority access.

- **The forest of columns:** 856 columns and red-and-white arches, the monument's iconic image.
- **The mihrab:** the jewel of Caliphal art, with its golden Byzantine mosaics.
- **The cathedral inside:** the Renaissance nave raised in the centre of the mosque, the symbol of the city of three cultures.
- **The Courtyard of the Orange Trees:** the old ablutions courtyard at the entrance.

## How it works

The meeting point is next to the Mosque and is confirmed on booking. **Priority entry** lets you skip the general queue. The tour lasts a little over an hour with an official guide.

## Who it's for

- If you only have time for one thing in Córdoba.
- If you want to understand the monument, not just photograph it.
- If you're travelling in high season and don't want to waste time queuing.
