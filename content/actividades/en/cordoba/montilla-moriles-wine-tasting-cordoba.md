## What you will see

- Four **Montilla-Moriles wines**, Córdoba's own denomination of origin.
- The differences between **fino**, **oloroso** and **Pedro Ximénez**.
- A **typical Córdoba tapa** with each glass.

## How it works

You meet the guide in a small wine shop that opens just for the group. Over an hour they walk you through the local wines and Córdoba's winemaking history while you taste four glasses with their tapas, and you can ask anything you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Wine lovers** who want to try something other than sherry.
- **Couples** looking for a short plan before dinner.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone travelling with **minors**: minimum age is 18.
- People expecting a **winery or vineyard visit**: the tasting is in town.
