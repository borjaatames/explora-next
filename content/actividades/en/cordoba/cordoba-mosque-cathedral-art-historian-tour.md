## What you will see

The tour follows the history of the building in order:

- **The Visigothic basilica of San Vicente**, on which the first mosque was built.
- **The mosque of Abd al-Rahman I** (8th century), with the famous **double arches of red and white voussoirs**, a solution that gave the building height.
- **The extensions** by Abd al-Rahman II, Al-Hakam II (with the **mihrab** and its mosaics) and **Almanzor**.
- **The cathedral** built in the centre after the conquest by Ferdinand III and the conversion of the building into a Christian church.

## Tip

After the visit, step out into the **Patio de los Naranjos** and cross the **Roman bridge** to see the Mosque from the other bank of the Guadalquivir.
