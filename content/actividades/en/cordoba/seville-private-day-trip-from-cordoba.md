## How the day works

You're picked up in Córdoba and cross the countryside to **Seville**. On arrival, a **guided visit to the Cathedral**, the largest Gothic church in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Then the **Royal Alcázar**, one of the oldest palaces still in use anywhere, with the **Patio de las Doncellas** and the **Patio de las Muñecas**. Tickets are included.

The morning ends in the **Santa Cruz quarter**, among alleys scented with orange blossom. Free time for lunch (you'll get suggestions on where to book) and, in the afternoon, **Plaza de España**, Aníbal González's regionalist masterpiece for the 1929 Exposition. Then back to Córdoba, or finish in Seville.

## Who it's for

- Visitors based in Córdoba who want Seville in a day, hassle-free.
- Families or groups of four: the price per person drops a lot.
