## How the day works

You leave Córdoba for the **Subbética**, a natural park in the heart of Andalusia. First stop: **Priego de Córdoba**, capital of Córdoba's Baroque. You stroll the old **Villa quarter**, all narrow lanes and white squares, the **Adarve balcony** with its view over the district, the castle (entry included) and the **Fuente del Rey** with its 139 spouts, next to the **Fuente de la Salud**.

Then lunch at a local restaurant (not included). In the afternoon, **Carcabuey castle**, which once controlled the roads between five Andalusian provinces and shelters the hermitage of the Virgen del Castillo. On the way back to Córdoba you stop at the **Araceli hermitage** in Lucena, where legend says Noah's Ark ran aground.

## Who it's for

- Visitors who already know Córdoba city and want to see inland villages.
- Groups of three or four sharing car and guide.
