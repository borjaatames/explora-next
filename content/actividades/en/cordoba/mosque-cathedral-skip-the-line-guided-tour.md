## What you will see

- The **Courtyard of the Orange Trees** and the bell tower.
- The **forest of columns** and red-and-white arches.
- The **mihrab** and the maqsura.
- The Renaissance **cathedral** built inside.

## How it works

You collect your headset, hear the introduction in the Courtyard of the Orange Trees and go in without queuing. The guide explains the successive expansions, the mihrab and the Christian cathedral.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Córdoba.
- Anyone wanting to understand the **history of Al-Andalus**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers to **go alone** without set times.
