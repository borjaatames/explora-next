## What you will see

- The **Mosque-Cathedral**: the two-tone arches and how a cathedral was built inside it.
- The **Jewish Quarter**: Calleja de la Hoguera, the souk and the statue of **Maimonides**.
- The **gardens of the Alcázar** of the Christian Monarchs.

## How it works

You meet at the Puerta del Perdón, enter the Mosque through a separate gate and spend the first hour and a half inside. Then the guide walks you through the Jewish Quarter and you finish in the Alcázar gardens. If you want to see the Fernandine Churches on your own later, ask the guide for the ticket before saying goodbye.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with **just one day** in Córdoba who wants the essentials with context.
- People who prefer a **single-language guide**, with no mid-sentence translations.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility** or travelling with a **stroller**.
- Anyone set on seeing the **inside of the Alcázar**: for now only the gardens are visited.
