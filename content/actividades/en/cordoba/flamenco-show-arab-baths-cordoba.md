## What you will see

- The **Arab Baths of Santa María**, from the 10th century.
- A show of **singing, guitar and dance** with no microphones.
- **Nationally and internationally known** artists up close.

## How it works

Arrive a quarter of an hour early, step into the baths and take your seat with your included drink. The performance lasts an hour, fully acoustic; if you want to eat, book a ticket with a menu or order tapas.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone after **authentic, intimate** flamenco without amplification.
- People interested in **Andalusian heritage** as well as the show.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting **dinner included** with the basic ticket.
