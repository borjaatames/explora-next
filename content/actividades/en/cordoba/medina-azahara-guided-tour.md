## What you'll see

A guided tour of the great Caliphal site on the outskirts of Córdoba.

- **The palatial city:** the ruins of the capital built by Abd al-Rahman III in the 10th century.
- **The Rich Hall:** the reception hall with its restored arches, the most spectacular part of the complex.
- **Gardens and terraces:** the caliph's city laid out in levels, looking over the plain.

## How it works

Depending on the option, the meeting point is in Córdoba or at the site entrance; check whether transport is included when booking. The guided tour lasts about **3 hours**.

## Who it's for

- If you're staying overnight in Córdoba and want a different morning.
- If you're interested in the history of the Caliphate beyond the Mosque.
- If you come in spring, with good weather for the open-air site.
