## What you'll see

Medina Azahara is the palace city that **Abd al-Rahman III** had built at the foot of the Sierra Morena, eight kilometres from Córdoba, after proclaiming himself caliph. Marble, gold and the finest stonemasons made it the emblem of his power.

The guided walk covers the whole city: the **Salón Rico**, the **mosque**, the **houses**, the walled area and the **Upper Garden**. About two and a half hours, tickets included.

## How it works

You meet at the **visitor centre** and take the **shuttle bus** (included) up to the site entrance. Getting there from Córdoba is up to you.

## Who it's for

- Anyone interested in the history of al-Andalus who wants to understand what they're seeing.
- Visitors with a car, or happy to take a taxi to the site.
