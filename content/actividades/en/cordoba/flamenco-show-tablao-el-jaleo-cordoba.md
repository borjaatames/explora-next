## What you will see

- A live **flamenco group** with singing, guitar and dance.
- Artists who have won **national awards** for singing and guitar.
- An intimate tablao on a **Mozarabic square** in the Jewish Quarter.

## How it works

You arrive, take your seat and order your included drink. If you chose the menu option, a traditional dinner is served around the one-hour performance.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **quality flamenco** without paying much.
- People who want **dinner and flamenco** in one place.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers a show **without table service** during the performance.
