## What the day looks like

You are picked up at your accommodation in Córdoba at 9:00 and head into the province, through thousands of hectares of **olive groves**. The day is all about **white villages**: narrow whitewashed streets, flower-filled courtyards, churches and viewpoints, and a cuisine inherited from the many cultures that passed through.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €974 for 2 people and €1,160 for 4. Private transport is included; lunch is paid separately.

## Who I recommend it to

- Travellers who have seen Córdoba and want rural Andalusia.
- Groups of 4, who share the cost.
