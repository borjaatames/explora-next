## What you will see

- A live **singer**, **guitarist** and **two dancers**.
- Artists with **national awards** for singing, dance and guitar.

## How it works

Arrive a little before 9 pm at the tablao on Calle San Felipe, get your drink and the show begins: one hour with a 10-minute break. If you pick the menu option, you have a starter, main and dessert.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone after **affordable**, high-level flamenco.
- People who want **dinner and flamenco** in one go.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting flamenco on a **Monday**: there is no show.
