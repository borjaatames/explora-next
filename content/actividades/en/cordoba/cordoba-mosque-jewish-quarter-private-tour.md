## The route

You start with **90 minutes inside the Mosque-Cathedral**, ticket included, with a guide revealing its corners and secrets. Then another 90 minutes through the lanes of the **Jewish Quarter**: its squares, its patios and the **Synagogue** (on morning tours), while you learn about Córdoba's role in Western history and the people born here.

## How it works

- It's **100% private**: the guide tailors the visit to your interests.
- The **price is per group**.
- The Synagogue is closed on Mondays and in the afternoons: book a morning tour if you want to see it.

## Who it's for

- Anyone with a single day in Córdoba who wants it all explained.
- Families and groups: the per-group price works out well.
