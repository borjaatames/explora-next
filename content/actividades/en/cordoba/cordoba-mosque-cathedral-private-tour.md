## What you'll see

You meet your guide by the **bell tower** and go into the **Mosque-Cathedral** together, ticket already sorted. For an hour and a half they show you the monument corner by corner —the forest of columns and red-and-white arches, the mihrab, the Christian cathedral built inside— and tell you its story and the city's.

## How it works

- It's **private**: the guide is just for your party and adapts to your interests.
- The **price is per group**, not per person.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors to Córdoba who want to understand the Mosque.
- Families: it's stroller and wheelchair accessible.
