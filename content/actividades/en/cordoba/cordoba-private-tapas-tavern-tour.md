## The route

You meet in **Plaza de Capuchinos** and the guide takes you through some of the city's most emblematic corners, stopping at three **traditional taverns**. At each one you try a tapa with a drink: well-known Córdoba dishes and others visitors rarely find. The tour ends where it began.

## How it works

- It's **private**: just you and the guide.
- Minimum 2 people, maximum 9.
- There's a **vegetarian option**; ask for it when booking.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to get to know Córdoba's food beyond the tourist menus.
- Groups of three or four friends: the price per person drops a lot.
