## How the day works

You're picked up in Córdoba and driven straight to **Granada**. There you enjoy a **private guided visit to the Alhambra**, free of the big-group crush: the **Nasrid Palaces**, the gardens and palace of the **Generalife** and the **Palace of Charles V**. Tickets are included.

Then there's free time for lunch at one of the restaurants your guide suggests. In the afternoon you walk through the **Albaicín** to the **San Nicolás viewpoint** for a last look at the Alhambra from one of the finest views anywhere. You're back in Córdoba in the late afternoon, or you can finish in Granada.

## Who it's for

- Visitors based in Córdoba who want the Alhambra without changing hotel.
- Anyone who couldn't get tickets on their own.
