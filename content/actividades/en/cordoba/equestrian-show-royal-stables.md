## What you will see

- **Purebred Spanish** horses.
- **Classical dressage**, vaquera and high school.
- **Flamenco** in the arena alongside the horse.
- The 16th-century **Royal Stables**.

## How it works

You get your seat and QR code by email, enter the Royal Stables and watch the roughly 70-minute show.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**.
- **Horse lovers**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **take photos**: it's not allowed.
