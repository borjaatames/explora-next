## What you will see

- The **Puerta del Perdón** and the Courtyard of the Orange Trees.
- The **forest of columns** and the mihrab.
- The cathedral's **choir stalls**.
- Outside: the **Jewish Quarter**, the synagogue, Plaza del Potro and the Roman temple.

## How it works

You get the ticket by email, download the app and audio guide before you go and enter at your booked time. Listen at your own pace inside and then around the centre.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers to **visit at their own pace**.
- **Tight budgets**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting an **in-person guide** or the option to cancel.
