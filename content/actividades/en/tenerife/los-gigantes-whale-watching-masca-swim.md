## What you will see

Off Los Gigantes the seabed drops away steeply, which makes it one of the best places in Tenerife to see **short-finned pilot whales** and **dolphins** close to shore. This two-hour **speedboat** trip combines wildlife watching with the most dramatic stretch of the west coast.

- **Pilot whales or dolphins**, depending on what's around that day.
- The **Los Gigantes cliffs**, lava walls more than 500 metres high, seen from their base.
- **Masca bay**, at the mouth of the gorge, where you stop to swim.

## How it works

You check in at the **Marítima Acantilados** office (or Nashira Uno) at Los Gigantes harbour, in Santiago del Teide. From there the boat runs for about 30 minutes to the cetacean area and stays around a quarter of an hour watching.

It then heads for **Masca bay**, another 30 minutes along the cliffs, and stops for about 15 minutes for swimming, photos or snorkelling. During the stop you get a **cold drink** (water, soft drink or beer). The return to harbour hugs the Los Gigantes wall, with time for photos.

A practical note: the GetYourGuide listing says **snorkelling gear** and **pickup** are included, but the description asks you to bring your own gear and gives no pickup points. Bring a mask and snorkel and plan to get to the harbour yourself.

On board there's no smoking or vaping, no alcohol of your own and no music from speakers.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Los Gigantes, Puerto de Santiago or Playa de la Arena**: you can walk there.
- Anyone who wants **whales and a swim** on one trip without giving up half a day.
- Anyone after the **classic cliff photo** from the sea.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **gets seasick**: a speedboat moves far more than a catamaran.
- Anyone wanting **lots of time with the animals**: it's about 15 minutes.
- Anyone staying in the **tourist south** without a car: there are trips from Los Cristianos and Puerto Colón.
