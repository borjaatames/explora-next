## What you will see

- **Teide National Park** and the **Roques de García**.
- The **Drago Milenario** in Icod de los Vinos.
- **Garachico** and El Caletón's lava pools.
- The hamlet of **Masca** in the Teno massif.

## How it works

You're picked up in your area and the coach climbs to Teide for a stop at the Roques de García, drops to Icod de los Vinos and Garachico on the north coast and finishes on the mountain road to Masca before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting the island's **essentials** in a day.
- Anyone who **doesn't want to drive** in the mountains.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **ride up Teide** by cable car.
- Anyone who gets **carsick** on bends.
