## What you will see

- The hamlet of **Masca**, tucked between ravines and once a pirate hideout.
- The **Los Gigantes cliffs**, over 600 metres high.
- Volcanic **natural pools** for a swim.

## How it works

You are picked up in the south or in Puerto de la Cruz and travel up to Masca in a small-group vehicle. Then you stop for photos at Los Gigantes and finish at natural pools chosen to suit the tide, with a picnic, water, juice and fruit. You can optionally have lunch at a guachinche, paid separately.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **Masca and Los Gigantes** in half a day in a small group.
- **Couples** and solo travellers after nature and a swim.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with a **fear of heights** or who get carsick on winding roads.
