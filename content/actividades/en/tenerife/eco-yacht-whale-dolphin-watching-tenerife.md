## What you will see

- **Short-finned pilot whales** and dolphins in the wild.
- Tenerife's volcanic southern coast.
- The **Cueva del Amor**.
- Marine life such as **turtles**, with luck.

## How it works

You board at Los Cristianos pier and the yacht cruises the south coast looking for pilot whales and dolphins. After passing the Cueva del Amor it stops in clear water for a swim before returning to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and anyone prone to seasickness.
- Anyone who values **responsible** wildlife watching.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **long trip** with food.
