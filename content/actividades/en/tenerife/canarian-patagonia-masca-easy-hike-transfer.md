## What you will do

The western tip of Tenerife looks nothing like the rest of Spain: jagged volcanic ridges, shifting clouds and wild highland plateaus that feel closer to the Andes than to the Atlantic. On this small-group trip you stop at the **Masca viewpoint**, walk an **easy, flat hike across the highland plateau** and reach the **westernmost edge of the island** inside the **Teno Rural Park**.

## How it works

The group is **small, up to 8 people**, and the trip lasts about **6.5 hours** with **hotel pickup and drop-off**. It costs €156 for one person, €258 for two and €392 for four, with trekking poles included; bring water and snacks. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families with children from 7.
- Anyone who wants Teno and Masca landscapes without a hard hike.
- Photographers who like wild, open scenery.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If you want a challenging hike: choose the Teno waterfalls hike.
- If you need a guide in Spanish: this tour is in English only.
