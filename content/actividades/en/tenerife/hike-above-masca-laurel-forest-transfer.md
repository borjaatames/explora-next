## What you will do

A hike that combines walking and sightseeing in the **Teno mountains**. You start in a **pine forest** with views of **Teide** and of the site of the last eruption in **1909**, and after about 70 minutes reach a **viewpoint at 1,300 m** over the mountains, the ocean, the **Masca Gorge** and the famous winding Teno road.

The main route then continues through a **relict laurel forest**, with an unusual aerial view of the village of Masca, avoiding the crowded tourist trails. In total it is **7.5 km with 300 m of ascent**.

## How it works

The group is **small, up to 6 people**, and the outing lasts about **5 hours** with **transfer included**. It costs €96 for one person, €176 for two and €288 for four, with hiking poles and ponchos. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- If you want Masca views without descending the gorge.
- Walkers looking for a half-day hike at a good price.
- Anyone who prefers quiet trails to busy ones.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- Under-18s: the operator sets a minimum age of 18.
- If you want water and swimming: choose the Teno waterfalls hike.
