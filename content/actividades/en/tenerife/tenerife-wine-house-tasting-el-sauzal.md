## What you will see

- The **Casa del Vino**, a 17th-century estate house with a wine press in its courtyard.
- Tenerife's **Wine Museum**.
- A **garden** with traditional grape varieties and native plants.
- Views of **Mount Teide** and the Atlantic.

## How it works

Make your own way to El Sauzal and pick one of three tasting menus. The basic one includes a white, a rosé and a red from the island with cheese, toast, almogrote and honey. Afterwards you can explore the museum, gardens and shop.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **discover Canary wine** on a budget.
- Visitors staying in the **north** or with a hire car.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **guided tour** of a working winery.
- Anyone without a car: transport is not included.
