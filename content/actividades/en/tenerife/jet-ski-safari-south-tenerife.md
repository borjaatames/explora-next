## What you will see

A guided jet ski safari along **Tenerife's south coast**, between Marina San Miguel and El Médano. It's a stretch of volcanic shoreline far less built up than Las Américas, with coloured cones dropping straight into the sea.

- **Montaña Amarilla**, an old volcano of yellow tuff in Costa del Silencio.
- **Costa del Silencio** bay and the fishing village of **Las Galletas**.
- **Montaña Roja**, next to El Médano, if sea and weather allow.
- With luck, **dolphins, turtles** or a whale along the way; nothing is guaranteed.

## How it works

The meeting point is **pontoon H at Marina San Miguel**, at the far end of the Amarilla Golf marina. There's free parking at the marina entrance. Be there **30 minutes early** for paperwork and the safety briefing.

**No licence or experience is needed**: the instructor explains how to ride safely, then leads on their own craft, setting the pace. Groups are **8 people at most**. Each jet ski takes **one or two people**, up to 200 kg. Drivers must be 16 (16- and 17-year-olds need written parental consent) and passengers at least 8 and strong enough to hold on.

Before setting off you leave a **€50 cash deposit per jet ski**, which ensures you keep your distance from the rest of the group. **Phones aren't allowed**: the operator takes photos and videos during the ride, sold separately.

The exact route depends on the sea and on whether you choose **40 minutes or an hour**. Back at base there's a shower, toilets, lockers and coffee, tea, soft drinks or juice.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **try a jet ski** for the first time with a guide.
- **Couples or friends** staying in the south (Golf del Sur, Las Galletas, Los Cristianos).
- Anyone who prefers a **quieter** stretch of coast than Playa de las Américas.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Non-swimmers** or anyone with back problems.
- Anyone who wants to **bring their phone** to film.
- Families with **children under 8**: a whale-watching boat trip suits them better.
