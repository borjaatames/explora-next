## What you will see

- The **sunset** over La Gomera, La Palma and El Hierro.
- The national park's **lava** flows.
- **Planets, galaxies and nebulae** through the telescope.
- The **constellations** with a laser pointer.

## How it works

You head up into the national park at sunset, toast with cava at a viewpoint beside a lava flow and, after dark, move to another viewpoint to observe with the telescope and laser pointer.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and astronomy fans.
- Anyone wanting a different **night out**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't understand English**.
- Anyone who suffers **altitude sickness**.
