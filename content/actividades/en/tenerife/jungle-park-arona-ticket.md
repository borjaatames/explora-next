## What you will see

- **400+ animals** from around 100 species in a jungle-style park.
- **Humboldt penguins**, eagles, vultures, flamingos, pelicans and toucans.
- The **free-flight aviary**, the orchid garden and the **lemur walk**.

## How it works

Get to Jungle Park in Las Águilas del Teide (Arona) and enter through the main ticket desk. During the day you can watch the birds of prey, exotic birds and sea lion shows, plus penguin feeding. Last entry is at 4:00 PM.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** staying in the south of the island.
- Anyone wanting a **calmer** animal park than Loro Parque.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting big rides or orca and dolphin shows.
