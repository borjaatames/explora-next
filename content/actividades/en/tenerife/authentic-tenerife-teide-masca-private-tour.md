## What you will do

A private day across three regions of Tenerife and several climates. It starts in **Teide National Park**, driving through the volcanic landscape with photo stops at viewpoints around **2,200 m**. Then you head west to **Masca**, the village in its gorge that visitors compare to Machu Picchu.

From Masca the route returns along the **north coast**, with cliffs, viewpoints and a stop at one of the guide's favourite beaches, and ends at a local restaurant to taste **wine and local food**.

## How it works

The tour is **private**, lasts about **8 hours** and includes **pickup at your accommodation** in an air-conditioned vehicle. It costs €378 for one person, €396 for two and €472 for four; lunch and tips are extra. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- First-time visitors who want the island highlights in one day.
- Couples, families and small groups who want their own guide.
- If you do not want to drive the Teide and Masca roads.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If you are travelling alone on a budget.
- If you want to hike: choose one of the Teno or Anaga hikes.
