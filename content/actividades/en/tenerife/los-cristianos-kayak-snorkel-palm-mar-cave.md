## What you will see

You set off from **Los Cristianos** with local guides and paddle west, beneath **volcanic cliffs** and rock formations, to the **Palm-Mar sea cave**, which can only be reached from the sea.

On the way there is a stop for **snorkelling** in clear water. Tenerife's south coast is one of the best places in the Canary Islands to see **turtles** and, with luck, **dolphins**, always from a respectful distance. The guide explains the ecosystem and takes photos during the trip.
