## The best whale watching day in Tenerife

This is the premium whale watching experience in Tenerife: a **full day on the water** with a catamaran, a buffet lunch on board, and a swimming stop — and a 4.8/5 rating from over 2,200 verified travellers.

### Why a full day makes a difference

Most whale watching trips run 2–3 hours. That is enough to see pilot whales, but the **full-day format** gives more time to find and observe animals without the pressure of returning to port. Encounters tend to be longer and more relaxed. The swimming stop adds a beach day element.

### What to expect on board

- **Morning departure** and briefing by the naturalist guide
- **Navigating into the Tenerife–La Gomera channel** to find cetaceans — typically 30–60 minutes from port
- **Time with pilot whales and dolphins** — usually 1–2 hours of observation
- **Buffet lunch** served on the catamaran: local tapas, grilled fish, salads, soft drinks
- **Swimming stop** in open Atlantic water, clear and warm
- **Return to port** in the afternoon

### Wildlife

The channel between Tenerife and La Gomera is home year-round to **short-finned pilot whales** — family pods of 10–30 individuals. **Bottlenose and spotted dolphins** are very common. The longer trip increases the chance of bonus sightings: sperm whales, loggerhead turtles, and occasionally blue whales in spring.
