## What you will do

A relaxed private day through the most iconic landscapes of Tenerife. You start in the volcanic heights of **Teide National Park** and cross the island to the ancient, misty **laurel forest of Anaga**, focusing on viewpoints rather than walking.

On the way you explore the historic streets of **San Cristóbal de La Laguna**, a UNESCO World Heritage city, and visit a **black-sand beach**. The day ends with a tasting of **traditional wine and local delicacies**.

## How it works

The tour is **private**, lasts about **8 hours** and includes **pickup at your accommodation** in an air-conditioned vehicle. It costs €362 for one person, €376 for two and €516 for four. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families and older travellers who want to see a lot without walking.
- If you only have one day and want both Teide and Anaga.
- Anyone who enjoys old towns and local wine.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If you get tired of long drives.
- If you want to hike in Anaga: choose the Anaga hikes.
