## What you will see

A two-in-one activity for anyone who's never dived: a **try dive to a maximum of 12 metres** and a spell of **snorkelling** off Costa Adeje, in an area where **sea turtles** are a regular sight.

- **Turtles**, Atlantic fish and rays over a volcanic seabed.
- The Costa Adeje coastline from the boat.
- Sometimes **dolphins or pilot whales** on the way out.

## How it works

It starts at the reception of **Travel Sub Dive Center**, a PADI 5-star centre in the Escuela Náutica building at **Puerto Colón**, opposite pier 5. You're kitted out (wetsuit, fins, mask) and given a **safety briefing of about 30 minutes**. They have prescription masks; you can't dive in ordinary glasses.

Then it's about 15 minutes by **speedboat** to the dive site. The dive lasts around **25 minutes**, with **one PADI instructor for every two people**, and goes no deeper than 12 metres. That's followed by another 25 minutes or so of **snorkelling** to look for turtles from the surface, and the boat brings you back to the centre. Groups are **10 people at most**.

Photos and videos of the dive are available at the centre. Towels and swimwear are provided, but it's best to bring your own along with flip-flops.

Two important conditions: you can't **fly or go to high altitude** (Teide) for 12 hours after diving, and people with heart or breathing conditions, epilepsy, diabetes or other pre-existing conditions can't take part, nor can pregnant women or anyone over 70. Children aged 8 to 17 must be accompanied by an adult who also pays for the activity.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **try diving** for the first time with plenty of instructors.
- Anyone staying in **Costa Adeje or Playa de las Américas**.
- **Families with children aged 8+** who are confident swimmers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone planning to **go up Teide or fly** the same day.
- Anyone with one of the excluded **medical conditions**.
- Certified divers: a guided fun dive is better value.
