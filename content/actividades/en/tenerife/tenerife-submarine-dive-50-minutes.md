## What you will see

The yellow submarine leaves from **Amarilla Marina**, at the southern tip of Tenerife, and descends to about **30 metres**. During the trip you can see **trumpetfish, octopuses, barracudas, parrotfish and a family of rays**, as well as a shipwreck on the seabed.

At one point the submarine settles on the bottom and **divers attract the fish to the windows** so you can take photos. The audio guide, in 15 languages, explains the marine life and ecosystem of the Canary Islands.

## Getting there

The operator runs a free bus from the tourist areas in the south (Los Cristianos, Las Américas, Costa Adeje). If you are driving, there is free parking at the port.
