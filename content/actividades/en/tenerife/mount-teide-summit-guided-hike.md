## What you'll see

A guided high-mountain route to the **summit of Mount Teide**, at **3,718 metres**, the highest point in Spain. It covers about **10 km** with **1,500 m of ascent**, across lava flows and volcanic landscape.

At the top you'll see the **crater** with its fumaroles and, on a clear day, the other Canary Islands above the sea of clouds.

## How it works

Meet at the cable car car park. Mountain guide in Spanish or English, group of 8 at most. The cable car ticket (about €22) is not included. Good fitness required. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
