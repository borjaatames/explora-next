## What you will see

- **Puerto Colón** and the beaches of **Fañabé** and **La Enramada**.
- The **Caldera del Rey**, an ancient volcano.
- A **viewpoint** over Costa Adeje, Las Américas, La Gomera, La Palma and El Hierro.
- The seaside village of **La Caleta**.

## How it works

You hop on the electric tuk tuk in Adeje and your driver-guide takes you from the coast to the hills for two hours, with photo stops and short walks in La Caleta and at the viewpoint. Along the way you learn about the area’s volcanic past and where locals eat.

## Who I recommend it for

- New arrivals who want to **get their bearings** in Costa Adeje.
- **Families** and small groups after a short outing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to explore the interior or the north of the island.
