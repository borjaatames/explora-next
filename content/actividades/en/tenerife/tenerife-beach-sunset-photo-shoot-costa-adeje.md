## What you'll do

A local photographer takes you around **Costa Adeje**: the sand of Playa del Duque, the seafront promenade and the volcanic rock coves. They show you where to stand and how to move so the photos look **natural**, with no forced poses. The best time is late afternoon, with the **sun setting over the Atlantic**.

## How it works

You choose the length when you book:

| Option | Length | Edited photos | Price per session |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus | 40 min | 30 | €89.99 |
| Elite | 60 min | 50 | €109.99 |
| Ultimate | 90 min | 75 | €139.99 |

The price is **per session**, not per person. You meet at **Playa del Duque**, and the photographer confirms the exact spot after booking. Within 24 hours you receive the edited photos in a **private online gallery**.

## Who it's for

- Honeymooners and anniversary couples staying in Costa Adeje or Playa de las Américas.
- Families who finally want a photo with everyone in it.
- Solo travellers who want good beach photos of themselves.
