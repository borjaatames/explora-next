## What you will do

A **private boat trip just for your group** from **Las Galletas**, in the south of Tenerife, to see the **pilot whales and dolphins** that live in the waters between Tenerife and La Gomera all year round. There are two options:

- **2 hours · €450 per boat:** whale and dolphin watching.
- **3 hours · €550 per boat:** the same 2 hours of watching plus **1 more hour with a swim or snorkel stop**, sea conditions permitting.

## How it works

The price is **per boat, not per person**, for groups of **1 to 6 people** (babies and children count towards that maximum). Meet at **pontoon 5 of Marina del Sur** in Las Galletas, 15-20 minutes before departure. Trips leave at **9:00, 11:00, 13:00 and 15:00**. Drinks are included and the guide speaks **Spanish and English**. There is no hotel pickup.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families or groups of 4 to 6: the cost per head drops a lot and you have the boat to yourselves.
- Couples looking for something special, without the crowds of the big catamarans.
- Anyone staying in the south (Las Galletas, Los Cristianos, Las Américas, Costa del Silencio).
- If you want something cheap, a shared catamaran from Los Cristianos or Costa Adeje is a better fit.
