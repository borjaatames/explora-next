## What you will see

**Castillo San Miguel** is a medieval-style castle built in Aldea Blanca, in the municipality of San Miguel de Abona, a few minutes from the southern resorts. It isn't a historic monument but a venue designed for this show: an evening **tournament with a banquet**.

- **Knights, nobles and ladies of the court** in a good-versus-evil storyline.
- A live **jousting tournament** with **Spanish horses**.
- A **birds of prey** display.
- A **three-course banquet** served during the show.

## How it works

The show runs only on **Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays**. Doors open at **6:30 pm**, and before it starts there's a bar selling snacks and drinks. The show runs from **7:30 to 9:30 pm**.

You can drive there yourself, with **free parking** at the castle, or add **pickup** when booking. From the south there's a transfer on all three days; from the north, Saturdays only.

The menu is fixed: **cream of vegetable soup**, **baked chicken leg** with roast potatoes and dressed tomato, and **ice-cream cake** for dessert. Wine, beer, water and soft drinks are included during the show. The **VIP option** adds a welcome glass of cava and front-row seats, which give the best view of the arena.

Souvenirs and photos with the Count and Queen are sold during the evening; all of that costs extra. The operator warns that tickets sell out fast, so book a few days ahead in high season.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**: they love the horses, birds and tournament.
- **Groups** looking for a different night out in the south.
- Anyone staying in **Golf del Sur, Las Américas or Los Cristianos**, close to the castle.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **gourmet dinner**: the food is fine, not memorable.
- Anyone on a **special diet** who hasn't checked first: the menu is fixed.
- Anyone staying in the **north** who can't go on a Saturday: the transfer won't work on other days.
