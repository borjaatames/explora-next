## What you will see

- A **three-part** show with stand-up, mime routines and live vocals.
- **Drag queens, dancers** and big group numbers.
- Plenty of **audience interaction**.

## How it works

You arrive at ViVO Show Bar in Playa de las Américas, meet the cast and sit down to a three-course dinner with table service. Then the show starts and runs for over two hours. Drinks are extra and you can add a VIP upgrade for better seats.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends**, birthdays and celebrations.
- Anyone with **good English** looking for a night of laughs.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who does not speak English.
- Anyone after a **gourmet dinner**.
