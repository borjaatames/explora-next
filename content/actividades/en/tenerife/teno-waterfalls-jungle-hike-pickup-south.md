## What you will do

A guided hike in the **Teno mountains**, in the wild north-west of Tenerife. It starts with the spectacular winding road down towards the **Masca Gorge**, often called the Canarian Machu Picchu, with a short stop for the view. The trail passes banana, avocado, fig and citrus plantations and then enters a jungle-like ravine.

You follow a dry riverbed between rock walls until the sound of water grows: the stream forms **cascades and natural pools**. This is the wet part of the route, where you spend about an hour taking photos and swimming, before returning the same way. The route is **7.2 km with 350 m of ascent**.

## How it works

The group is **small, up to 4 people**, and the day lasts about **7 hours** with **pickup from the south**. It costs €168 for one person, €284 for two and €432 for four, with hiking poles and insurance included. Bring shoes that can get wet, swimwear and a change of clothes. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- Fit, adventurous hikers who do not mind getting wet.
- If you want a different, little-known side of Tenerife.
- Small groups of friends or couples.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- Children under 12 or anyone uneasy scrambling over rocks.
- If you want an easy walk: try the hike above Masca or the Canarian Patagonia walk.
