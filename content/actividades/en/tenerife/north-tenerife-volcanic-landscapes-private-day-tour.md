## What you will do

A private day across the green north of Tenerife and back over the volcano. You walk through **Garachico**, rebuilt after a volcanic eruption, with the **Castillo de San Miguel** and the natural lava pools of **El Caletón**; then **Icod de los Vinos**, with its historic centre, the **Church of San Marcos** and the famous **Dragon Tree**, where there is time for lunch or coffee.

Next comes the wild north coast: the natural pool of **Charco de la Laja**, the **Mirador de San Pedro** over palm groves and banana plantations, and a short walk at **Rambla de Castro** around a historic hacienda. The last town is **La Orotava**, with the Casa de los Balcones and traditional Canarian architecture. The way back south climbs through pine forests, past the **Piedra La Rosa** rock formation, and across the volcanic landscape of **Teide**.

## How it works

The tour is **private for 1 to 4 people** and lasts about **8 hours**, by car with an English-speaking guide. You are **picked up at your accommodation**. It costs **€150 per person** and includes parking; food, drinks and entry fees are extra. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- If you are staying in the south and want to see the north without driving.
- If you like old towns, coastal walks and viewpoints more than beaches.
- Couples or small groups who want a private guide and their own pace.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If you want to go up to the summit of Teide: this tour crosses the national park but does not include the cable car.
- If you have reduced mobility: it includes walks in old towns and along a coastal path.
