## What you will see

The **Canary banana** is the crop that covers most of the low ground in northern Tenerife, and the La Orotava valley is full of farms. This short visit takes you into one of them, **BananaECOplantation**, an organic plantation run by its owners.

- A walk along the **rows of banana plants**, among bunches of fruit and leaves several metres long.
- How they've built an **almost self-sufficient (95%)** ecosystem producing more than 150,000 kg of bananas a year.
- The role of the **aromatic plants and cereals** sown between the banana plants so they can avoid pesticides and chemical fertilisers.
- The secrets of how bananas **ripen**.

## How it works

The visit starts at the farm itself, on the **El Rincón road** in lower La Orotava, very close to Puerto de la Cruz. The GetYourGuide listing includes "transport" without explaining what it is and **doesn't mention hotel pickup**, so the practical option is to drive or take a taxi.

It lasts about **50 minutes**, in Spanish or English. The owners walk you through the rows explaining the farming and their sustainable approach, and the visit ends with a **tasting of fresh bananas** and a **shot of banana liqueur**, served only to over-18s.

Pets aren't allowed. On accessibility, **only some areas** of the farm are wheelchair-friendly, there are accessible toilets and a companion is required.

The farm is a few minutes from the historic centre of **La Orotava** and from **Puerto de la Cruz**, so it fits easily into a day in the north of the island.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone spending the day in **La Orotava or Puerto de la Cruz** who wants a short visit.
- **Families** looking for something different and educational for the children.
- Anyone interested in **organic farming** and local produce.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the **south without a car**: the journey isn't worth it for a 50-minute visit.
- Anyone expecting a full **food tasting**: it's bananas and a shot.
- Anyone with no interest in **farming**: there are more striking things to do in the north.
