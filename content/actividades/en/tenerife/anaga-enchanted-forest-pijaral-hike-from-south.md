## What you will do

A guided hike in **Anaga**, the wild north-east of Tenerife and a **UNESCO Biosphere Reserve**. After a stop at the **Mirador del Bailadero**, you start at **La Ensillada** and walk **5.5 km with 350 m of ascent** through ancient **laurel forest**, including a short stretch of the protected **El Pijaral** path, for which the guide handles the permits.

The route passes viewpoints that are not open to the public, with views over the mountains, the Atlantic and the rugged coastline. At one of them, endemic birds such as the **Tenerife blue chaffinch** and the local robin come very close, because they are not afraid of people. It is a quiet trail that sees very few tourists.

## How it works

Pickup is **door to door in the south of Tenerife**, from Santiago del Teide to Santa Cruz; the operator confirms the time after booking. The drive to Anaga takes about an hour from Santa Cruz. The group is **small, up to 4 people**, and the whole day lasts about **7.5 hours**. On the way back you pass Playa de las Teresitas. The price is **€158 for one person, €276 for two or €476 for four** and includes trekking poles and insurance; bring water and snacks. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- If you like hiking and want to see the most authentic forest in Tenerife.
- If you are staying in the south and do not want to drive to Anaga.
- Anyone curious about El Pijaral who does not want to deal with the permit.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- Children under 9, or anyone with back or heart problems.
- If you only want viewpoints and villages: a driving tour of Anaga is easier.
