## What you will see

The trip follows the coast of south-west Tenerife inside the **Teno-Rasca marine strip**, a protected area where pilot whales and dolphins live year-round. There are two versions: **3 hours** or **5 hours**, the longer one with more sailing and lunch.

- **Pilot whales and dolphins**, and with luck other passing species.
- The **Costa Adeje** shoreline and, on clear days, **Teide** rising above it.
- On the long option, the **Los Gigantes cliffs** and **Masca beach** seen from the sea.

## How it works

You set off from **pier 8 in Puerto Colón**, Costa Adeje: look for the boat with the **White Tenerife** logo. GetYourGuide lists hotel pickup and drop-off as included; check the time and pickup point after booking.

The boat is a sailing catamaran. When wind and sea allow, it **sails without the engine**, which makes for quieter wildlife watching. The crew explains what you're seeing and at some point drops a **hydrophone** so you can hear the cetaceans underwater.

Before heading back there's a **swim stop** for swimming or snorkelling. Snorkelling gear **isn't included**: it's sold on board, so bring your own mask and snorkel if you have them. Sandwiches and drinks (beer, soft drinks, water) are served; the 5-hour option adds chicken and rice with wine. Vegetarian and gluten-free food is available with 24 hours' notice; **vegan isn't**.

If no cetaceans are spotted, the operator lets you **repeat the trip for free** on another day, subject to availability.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants **half a day at sea** with a swim and some food, not just whale watching.
- Anyone staying in **Costa Adeje or Playa de las Américas**, close to Puerto Colón.
- Anyone who'd like to see **Los Gigantes from the water** without travelling there (5-hour option).

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **gets seasick**: five hours on a sailing boat is a long time. A 90-minute trip is wiser.
- Anyone on a **vegan diet**.
- Anyone short on time: there are quicker sightings trips from Los Cristianos.
