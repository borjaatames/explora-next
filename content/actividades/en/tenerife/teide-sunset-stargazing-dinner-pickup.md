## What you will do

An evening in **Teide National Park** to watch the **sunset above the sea of clouds** and then look at one of the darkest skies in Europe. Once night falls, the guide picks the best spot for the conditions, explains the **constellations and main stars with laser pointers** and sets up **professional telescopes** to see the Moon, planets, nebulae and star clusters. You also get tips to photograph the sky and the Moon with your phone.

## How it works

There are four options:

- **With pickup and dinner (6.5 h):** €85 per adult. Includes a 3-course dinner with local wine.
- **With pickup, no dinner (6.5 h):** €70 per adult.
- **Driving yourself, with or without dinner (3-4 h):** from €40 per adult. You meet the group in the park.

Pickup is from **Costa Adeje, Playa de las Américas and Los Cristianos**. The operator sends you the exact time on the morning of the tour. Tours run **Tuesday to Sunday**. Bring **warm clothes**: at 2,000 metres the night is cold. The booking is **non-refundable**.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone staying in the south of Tenerife who wants to see the stars of Teide without driving up in the dark.
- Couples and families with children aged 4 and up.
- If you want a shorter evening, take the drive-yourself option without dinner.
