## What you will see

- **Canary pine forest** and viewpoints over **Mount Teide** and the **Chinyero** volcano.
- The cliffs of the **Masca gorge** and Los Gigantes from above.
- A **laurel forest** full of moss, ferns and mist.

## How it works

You are picked up from your accommodation in the south and reach Teno Rural Park in half an hour. You hike about 8 km in two two-hour sections, with a picnic stop at a historic site. Groups are capped at 8 and afterwards you get a link to the photos from the hike.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **proper hiking** without driving to Anaga.
- **Families** with kids used to walking, and solo travellers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with a **low fitness level**.
- Anyone wanting to hike down the Masca gorge to the sea.
