## What you will see

- **Teide National Park**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- The views from the **upper station** at 3,555 m.
- The **Pico Viejo** and **La Fortaleza** viewpoints.
- The lunar landscape of **Las Cañadas**.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel and go up into the national park with the guide. At the base you take the cable car (8 minutes) and have free time at the top on the open trails before coming down and heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **go up Teide** without driving.
- **Families** with children over 3.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **breathing or heart problems**.
- Anyone wanting to reach the **summit** without a permit.
