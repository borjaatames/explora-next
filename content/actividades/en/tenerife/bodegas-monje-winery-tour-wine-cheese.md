## What you will see

- **Bodegas Monje**, a family winery running since 1750.
- The **vineyards** and grapes grown on volcanic soil.
- How **Canary wine** is made.

## How it works

You arrive at the winery in El Sauzal and a guide shows you around and tells the story of the Monje family. Then you taste four wines, from a young white to a barrel-aged Listán Negro, each paired with a goat cheese from a different island.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Wine** and **cheese** lovers.
- Anyone who wants to see a **real winery**, not just a tasting room.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without a way to get there: transport is not included.
