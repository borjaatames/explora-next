## What you'll see

A guided route through **Anaga Rural Park**, in north-east Tenerife, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. The path runs through **laurel forest**, a humid woodland that covered southern Europe millions of years ago.

The best-known stretch is the **Forest of Enigmas**, with trees covered in moss and mist. The guide explains the endemic plants and wildlife.

## How it works

Meet at the **Cruz del Carmen** visitor centre. Guide in Spanish or English. Easy difficulty; length depends on the option. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
