## What you will see

- The hamlet of **Masca** in the Teno massif.
- The walls of the **Masca Gorge**.
- The hidden **cove** at the end of the trail.
- The **Los Gigantes cliffs** from the sea.

## How it works

You meet in Santiago del Teide, are driven to Masca and hike 5 km down the gorge with the guide to the cove. There you board a boat to Los Gigantes harbour, from where you're taken back to the start.

## Who I recommend it for

- Experienced **hikers**.
- **Nature and photography** lovers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Children under 8** and people over 70.
- Anyone without **hiking boots**.
