## Two world-class parks, one ticket

This combo ticket gets you into the **two parks that share the title of "best in the world"** in their respective categories: Loro Parque (voted Europe's best zoo) and Siam Park (consistently voted the world's best water park). Both are in Tenerife.

### Loro Parque

Located in Puerto de la Cruz on the north coast, **Loro Parque** started as a parrot collection in 1972 and has grown into one of the finest zoos in the world.

**Highlights:**
- **Orca Ocean** — a spectacular show with orcas in a 6-million-litre oceanarium
- **PlanetPenguin** — the largest penguin facility in Europe
- **Gorilla Island** — western lowland gorillas in a naturalistic habitat
- **Shark Tunnel** — walk through a transparent tunnel surrounded by sharks
- **Dolphin show** and **sea lion show** — included in the ticket
- One of the world's largest and most diverse parrot collections (over 400 species)

### Siam Park

On the Costa Adeje in the south, **Siam Park** is a Thai-themed water park on a different scale from any other in Europe.

**Highlights:**
- **The Tower of Power** — one of the world's most extreme water slides (straight drop, shark tunnel at the bottom)
- **The Wave Palace** — enormous wave pool with 3-metre waves
- **The Dragon** — enclosed family tube ride with spectacular views
- **The Mekong Rapids** — lazy river through Thai-themed gardens

## How to use the ticket

The combo gives you **one full day at each park**. Most visitors use them on two consecutive days. Both parks open at 10:00 and fill up in July and August, so arrive early.
