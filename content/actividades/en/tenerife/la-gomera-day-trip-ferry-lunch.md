## What you will see

La Gomera is under an hour by ferry from Los Cristianos and is the greenest, most mountainous island in the western Canaries. This full-day trip covers the essentials with an official guide:

- **Garajonay National Park**, a World Heritage Site, with its laurel forest and the **Laguna Grande**.
- The **Hermigua valley**, with its farming terraces, and the village of **Agulo**.
- **Roque de Agando**, the island's most photographed volcanic plug.
- A demonstration of **silbo gomero**, the whistled language listed by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage.
- Free time in **San Sebastián de La Gomera**, the capital.

## How it works

You're picked up at one of **about 40 points in south Tenerife** (hotels, apartments and bus stops, including those of the free Siam Park bus) and taken to **Los Cristianos harbour**. There you board the **Fred. Olsen** ferry, included in the price, to San Sebastián de La Gomera. You need an **ID card or passport**: without one you can be refused boarding.

On La Gomera an air-conditioned coach is waiting. It's about an hour's drive to the Hermigua valley, via La Palmita, then a stop in **Agulo** for lunch: a Canarian meal with bread, mojo, water and wine, with a vegetarian option. The **silbo demonstration** happens during lunch.

In the afternoon you head up to **Garajonay** and the Laguna Grande, with a photo stop at **Roque de Agando**, then down to San Sebastián for some free time before the return ferry. The order can change due to logistics, weather or road closures.

The guide speaks **Spanish and English daily**; French and German only on some days, depending on the departure point (south or Puerto de la Cruz).

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **see another island** without hiring a car or arranging the ferry.
- Anyone more interested in **Canarian nature and culture** than the beach.
- Anyone staying in **south Tenerife**, near the pickup points.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: some stops involve steep climbs.
- Anyone who gets **seasick or carsick** on winding roads.
- Anyone wanting **proper hiking** in Garajonay: the stops are short.
