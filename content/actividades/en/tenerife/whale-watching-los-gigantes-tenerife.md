## Tenerife's best whale watching

The channel between Tenerife and La Gomera is one of the richest cetacean habitats in the Atlantic. **Short-finned pilot whales** live here year-round — they are not migratory, which means sighting rates are consistently high regardless of season.

### Los Gigantes: a spectacular setting

The trip departs from **Puerto de Los Gigantes**, at the foot of the most dramatic sea cliffs in the Canary Islands. The basalt walls rise vertically up to **800 metres** from the waterline — a geological spectacle in their own right.

### What you'll see

**Pilot whales** are the star attraction: large, sociable cetaceans that travel in family pods of 10 to 30 individuals. They surface frequently and approach boats with curiosity.

**Bottlenose dolphins** and **Atlantic spotted dolphins** are also very commonly sighted, often bow-riding alongside the boat.

In calm seas, **loggerhead turtles** are regularly spotted at the surface.

### About the trip

The trip lasts 2–3 hours on a small boat (better for spotting wildlife than a large cruise vessel). A naturalist guide provides commentary on the animals and the conservation regulations that govern whale-watching in the Canaries.

## Best time to go

Year-round, but **spring and autumn** typically offer the calmest seas. Morning departures (9–10 AM) have better light and smoother water.
