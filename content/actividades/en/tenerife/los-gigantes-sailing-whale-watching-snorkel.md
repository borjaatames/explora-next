## What you will see

The sailing boat leaves from **Los Gigantes harbour** and sails through the channel between Tenerife and La Gomera, one of the few areas in Europe where **short-finned pilot whales** and several species of **dolphins** live all year round. With luck you will also see turtles.

The boat then anchors in a **cove beneath the cliffs**, where you can swim and snorkel while the crew prepares sandwiches, fruit and drinks. The return trip follows the **Los Gigantes cliffs**, up to 600 metres high.
