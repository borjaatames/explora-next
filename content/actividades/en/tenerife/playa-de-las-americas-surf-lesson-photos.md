## What the lesson is like

The school is **30 metres from the waves of Playa de las Américas**, one of the best places in the south of Tenerife to learn. First you are given the **wetsuit, booties and rash vest**; then come **20 minutes of theory** on the sand: paddling, the take-off and standing up on the board.

After the warm-up, into the water. The instructor adapts the lesson to your level, whether it is your first time or you want to improve your technique, and a team member takes **photos** during the session.
