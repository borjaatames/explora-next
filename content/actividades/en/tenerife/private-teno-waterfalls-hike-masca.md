## What you will do

A private guided hike in the **Teno mountains**, in the wild north-west of Tenerife. It starts with the spectacular winding road down towards the **Masca Gorge**, often called the Canarian Machu Picchu, with a short stop for the view. The trail passes banana, avocado, fig and citrus plantations and then enters a jungle-like ravine.

You follow a dry riverbed between rock walls until the sound of water grows: the stream forms **cascades and natural pools**. This is the wet part of the route, where you spend about an hour taking photos and swimming, before returning the same way.

## How it works

The hike is **private for 1 to 4 people**, lasts about **7.5 hours** and includes **pickup at your accommodation**. It costs €356 for one person, €376 for two and €448 for four. Bring shoes that can get wet, swimwear and a change of clothes. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- Groups of friends or families with older children who want their own guide.
- Fit travellers who do not mind getting wet.
- If you prefer not to share the trail with strangers.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- Anyone uneasy scrambling over rocks.
- If you want an easy day: choose the Canarian Patagonia walk.
