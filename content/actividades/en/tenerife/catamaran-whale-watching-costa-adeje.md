## Whale watching from the south of Tenerife

If you're staying in the Costa Adeje resorts — **Las Américas, Los Cristianos, El Médano** — this is the whale watching trip to book. It departs from the south coast and heads into the **Tenerife–La Gomera channel**, one of the most productive cetacean habitats in the Atlantic.

### What lives here

**Short-finned pilot whales** are the resident species. Unlike migratory whale species, they live in this channel year-round in family pods of 10 to 30 individuals. Sighting rates are consistently high throughout the year.

**Bottlenose dolphins** are very frequently seen, often bow-riding alongside the catamaran. **Atlantic spotted dolphins** are also common.

### The trip

Three hours on a catamaran, with a naturalist guide providing commentary. The trip usually includes a **swimming stop** in open Atlantic water — bring your swimsuit.

### Responsible whale watching

Whale watching in the Canary Islands is strictly regulated. Boats must maintain minimum distances from cetaceans and may not chase or surround them. Operators on this route work within the regulations.
