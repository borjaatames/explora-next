## What you will do

An afternoon in **Teide National Park** that ends at sunset. First you visit **lava tubes**, a geological formation created by ancient eruptions. Then comes the main part: a moderate **2.5-hour hike to a 2,000 m peak** through the area of the most recent eruptions, where you meet almost no other hikers.

The views keep changing: red, brown and ochre volcanic peaks, distant islands, the blue ocean and the green forests far below, and finally the **sunset**. The route is **5.5 km with 200 m of ascent**.

## How it works

The group is **small, up to 4 people**, and the outing lasts about **5 hours** with **pickup**. It costs €116 for one person, €184 for two and €328 for four, with trekking poles and insurance. Bring warm clothes and a head torch. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- If you want a sunset on Teide away from the crowds.
- Families with children from 5 who like walking.
- Anyone curious about volcanoes and lava tubes.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If you want to reach the summit of Teide: that needs a permit and the cable car.
- If you feel the cold a lot and do not want to be outdoors after dark.
