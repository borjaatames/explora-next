## What you will see

- The **volcanic coast** between Los Cristianos and Guaza.
- The **Cueva de los Enamorados**.
- Volcanic seabeds at the **snorkel stop**.
- With luck, **turtles or dolphins**.

## How it works

After the safety briefing you paddle out from Los Cristianos beach to the Cueva de los Enamorados, stop to snorkel in a secluded bay and paddle back to the beach.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Beginners** and couples.
- Anyone wanting **photos** without bringing a phone.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Children under 14**.
- **Non-swimmers**.
