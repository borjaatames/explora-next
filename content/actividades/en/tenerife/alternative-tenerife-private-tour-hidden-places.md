## What you will do

A private day on the side of Tenerife that most visitors only see from the motorway. The route starts on the dry **east coast**: the natural rock arch of **Arco de Tajao**, the ancient man-made **caves of Chimiche**, the abandoned **Presa del Río** dam, the unfinished **leprosarium of Abades** with its graffiti-covered church, and the **Punta de Abona lighthouse**. Depending on time and weather you can also stop at Los Roques de Fasnia or other spots along the coast.

From the ocean you climb through the **Güímar Valley**, with an optional stop at a small local winery for a glass of Canarian wine and something to eat, then through Arafo up to the **Izaña Observatory** and the volcanic landscape around **Teide**. On a clear day you look down on the sea of clouds.

## How it works

The tour is **private for 1 to 4 people** and lasts about **8 hours**, by car with an English-speaking guide. You are **picked up at your accommodation**. It costs **€150 per person** and includes parking; food, drinks and any entry fees are extra. Some stops are fixed and others depend on weather and access on the day, and at the abandoned sites you only go where it is safe. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- If you have already seen the classic Teide and Masca and want something different.
- If you like photography, abandoned places and odd landscapes.
- Groups of 3 or 4, for whom the private price works out better.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If it is your first trip and you want the main sights: start with a classic Teide tour.
- If you have reduced mobility: several stops involve short walks on uneven ground.
