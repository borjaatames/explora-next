## What you will see

- A musical journey **from the Middle Ages to today**: Mozart, Beethoven, Elvis, The Beatles, Queen, Abba and Michael Jackson.
- Live **musicians, singers, dancers and acrobats**.
- **Film soundtracks and Broadway musicals**.

## How it works

Buy your ticket and go straight to the Pirámide de Arona in Playa de las Américas. The show lasts about two and a half hours, with audiovisual montages, costume changes and dance numbers. Drinks are paid separately at the bar.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and groups looking for an evening plan for all ages.
- Anyone staying in **Las Américas, Los Cristianos or Costa Adeje**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a local Canarian experience: this is a big international show.
