## What you will see

- The **parrot** collection that gives the park its name.
- **Planet Penguin** and its king penguins.
- The animal **shows**.
- **Kinderlandia** and the tropical gardens.

## How it works

You enter with your ticket and explore the park at your own pace, fitting your visit around the show times.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children.
- **Animal** lovers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't want to see **cetacean shows**.
