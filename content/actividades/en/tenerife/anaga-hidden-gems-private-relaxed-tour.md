## What you will do

A private, relaxed day in **Anaga**, the green north-east of Tenerife and a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. You stop at several **viewpoints** over the mountains and the ocean, walk through the **ancient laurel forest** and visit a **village where people built their homes in caves**.

The highlight is **Benijo**, where you try local Canarian food and see one of the most striking beaches on the island. If you feel like it, there is an optional **easy 2 km (40-minute) walk** through the Bosque de los Enigmas.

## How it works

The tour is **private for 1 to 4 people**, lasts about **8 hours** and includes **pickup at your accommodation**. It costs €368 for one person, €376 for two and €472 for four; food and drinks are extra. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- If you want to see Anaga without hiking.
- Couples, families or small groups who want their own guide.
- People who like viewpoints, villages and good food.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If you are on a tight budget and travelling alone.
- If you want a real hike: choose the small-group Anaga hike.
