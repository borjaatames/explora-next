## What you will see

- The **Tower of Power**, dropping through a shark tank.
- The world's biggest **artificial wave**.
- The **lazy river** and beaches.
- The Thai **floating market**.

## How it works

You enter with your ticket on your phone and spend the day at your own pace among slides, pools and beaches, with restaurants inside the park.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and groups of friends.
- Anyone after **adrenaline** on slides.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who can't stand **queues** in high season.
