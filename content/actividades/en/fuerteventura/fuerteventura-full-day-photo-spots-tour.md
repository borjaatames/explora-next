## What you will see

- **Betancuria**, the first capital of the Canary Islands, and inland viewpoints.
- The **Ajuy caves and cliffs**.
- The crater of a northern **volcano**, reached on foot.
- **Popcorn Beach** and the **Corralejo Dunes**.
- Photos at the **Sotavento lagoon** (optional stop).

## How it works

You're picked up at a southern hotel and the van heads up through the centre and west of the island towards the north. There's a coffee stop in Betancuria, a walk down to the Ajuy caves, a hike to the rim of a volcano, and the afternoon is spent on the north coast, with free time in Corralejo, before heading back south.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with **only a few days** who wants to see the whole island without driving.
- Travellers with a **camera** looking for varied landscapes.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **limited mobility** or who doesn't want to walk on uneven ground.
- Anyone who prefers **fewer stops** with more time at each.
