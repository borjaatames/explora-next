## What you'll see

- The **Corralejo** coast and **Lobos** islet from the sea.
- **La Caldera**, Lobos's half-submerged volcano.
- **La Concha beach**, a lagoon of white sand and calm water.

## How it works

You're picked up at one of eight points in **Corralejo** (several hotels, Burger King, Fuerchina or the Fuertecharter office) and a 15-minute van ride takes you to the boat. The catamaran sails for **about an hour** along the coast, passes **La Caldera** and anchors off **La Concha**.

There you have about **40 minutes** for **snorkelling, kayaking or paddleboarding**, a **motorboat** ride with a guided mini-tour of the islet, and **an hour for lunch**: paella with drinks. The trip back to Corralejo is a relaxed half-hour sail. The morning tour runs from **10:00 to 14:00**.

## Practical tips

- **Permit:** if you set foot on the island you need the council's authorisation; ask the operator whether they handle it. We explain it in our [guide to getting to Lobos](/en/guides/fuerteventura/lobos-island-how-to-get-there).
- **Sea:** the crossing is short, but with a strong trade wind the boat moves. The itinerary may change.
- **What to bring:** swimwear, a hat and sunglasses. Towels and sunscreen are included.
- **Photos:** the crew takes underwater photos during the snorkelling.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to see Lobos **without organising anything**: boat, food and activities in one payment.
- **Couples and families** looking for a sea day with swimming.
- Anyone staying in **Corralejo**.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to **walk on Lobos** to the lighthouse: the ferry gives you more hours ashore.
- Travellers on a tight budget: the ferry costs much less.
