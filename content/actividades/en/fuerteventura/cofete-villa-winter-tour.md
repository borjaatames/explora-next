## What you'll see

- The **Cofete track**, which crosses the Jandía peninsula over the island's highest peaks.
- The **Cofete viewpoint**, with the whole beach below you.
- **Villa Winter**, the house of German engineer Gustav Winter, with a guided visit.
- **Cofete beach** and the small cluster of houses beside it.

## How it works

You're picked up in **Jandía** in a vehicle for a group of **up to eight people**. The published itinerary is: a 10-minute secret stop, the **Cofete viewpoint** (10 minutes), a **guided visit to Villa Winter** (35 minutes) and **free time on the beach** (35 minutes), then back to Jandía. About **four hours** in all.

There's an access fee at Villa Winter; the GetYourGuide listing mentions it both as included and as an additional fee, so confirm it in your booking. The house is private property and its history (built in the mid-20th century, with disputed dates) has fuelled all sorts of legends.

**Language:** the tour runs in **German, English or Polish**, with one main language per group.

## Practical tips

- **Weather:** in bad weather the track may close and the itinerary changes.
- **Swimming:** Cofete has very strong surf and currents and no lifeguards. Walk and paddle instead.
- **What to bring:** drinks, comfortable shoes and something for the wind, which blows hard at Cofete.
- More details in our [guide to getting to Cofete and Villa Winter](/en/guides/fuerteventura/cofete-villa-winter-how-to-get-there).

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who **doesn't want to take a hire car** on the track.
- Anyone staying in **Morro Jable or Jandía**.
- Anyone interested in the **story of Villa Winter**, with someone to explain it.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone who needs a **Spanish-speaking guide** (or another language not offered).
- Anyone who wants to **spend the day at Cofete**: beach time is about 35 minutes.
