## What you will see

- **Salinas del Carmen**, where sea salt is still made the traditional way.
- The **Majorero Cheese Museum** in Antigua.
- The **Windmill Interpretation Centre**, with a taste of kneaded gofio.

## How it works

You buy a single ticket and visit the three centres on your own, in any order and on different days if you like, over a whole year. Show the voucher at each centre. You need a car or motorbike to get between them.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with a **car** wanting a culture day around the centre of the island.
- Anyone after a **cheap** plan for a cloudy day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without **their own transport**.
- Anyone who prefers a **guided** visit: this is self-guided.
