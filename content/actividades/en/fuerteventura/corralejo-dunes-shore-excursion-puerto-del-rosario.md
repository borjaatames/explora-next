## What you will see

- The **Corralejo Dunes**, with white sand and turquoise sea.
- The Corralejo **seafront**, lined with cafés, shops and terraces.
- The **El Campanario** shopping centre.

## How it works

The guide meets you at Puerto del Rosario port as you leave the ship, and the coach heads north while you hear about how the island and its dunes formed. You get free time in Corralejo until early afternoon, then move on to the dunes and coves to walk or swim before returning to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Cruise passengers** stopping in Puerto del Rosario.
- Anyone who understands **German** and wants a cheap, easy outing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't speak **German**.
- Anyone not arriving by **cruise ship**: it starts at the port.
