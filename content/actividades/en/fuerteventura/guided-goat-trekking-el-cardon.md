## What you will see

- Five tame **Majorero goats** that walk with you.
- Quiet trails near **El Cardón** mountain.
- Views over the **desert landscape** and inland mountains.

## How it works

The guide introduces you to the goats and you feed them corn. Then you set off on a hike of about 6 km over rough ground; the goats run free at times and you take turns walking them on a lead. Halfway there is a snack with goat cheese and local produce before you return to the start.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone looking for something **different** from the beach.
- **Families with children over 9** who like animals and walking.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **low fitness** or mobility problems.
- Anyone wanting a short, flat stroll.
