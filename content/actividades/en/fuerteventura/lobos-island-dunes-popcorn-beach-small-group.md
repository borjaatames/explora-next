## What you will see

- **Lobos Island**, with its beaches, lagoons and volcanic landscape.
- **Popcorn Beach**, made of white fragments of coralline algae.
- The **Corralejo Dunes**, the largest in the Canary Islands.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel and the van drives up to Corralejo for the ferry to Lobos. On the island you get about two and a half hours to explore, swim or snorkel; on the way back you stop at Popcorn Beach and walk through the dunes before returning to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the **south or centre** who wants to visit Lobos without driving.
- Those after a **beach and nature** day in a small group.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone prone to **seasickness**.
- Anyone staying in **Corralejo**: going to Lobos independently works out better.
