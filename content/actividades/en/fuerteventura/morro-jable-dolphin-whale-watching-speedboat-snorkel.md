## What you will see

The speedboat leaves from **Morro Jable harbour** and follows the coast of the **Jandía peninsula**, where you often see **bottlenose dolphins, spotted dolphins and, depending on the season, pilot whales and other whales**. The guides explain the species and how to behave around them.

About halfway through there is a **30-minute snorkelling stop**, with colourful fish in clear water. Then it is back to the harbour with a cold drink and a transfer to your pickup point.
