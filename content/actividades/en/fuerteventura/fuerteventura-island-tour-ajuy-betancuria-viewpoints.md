## What you will see

- The **Morro Velosa and La Peña viewpoints** and the statues of the Majorero kings.
- The mountains of **Betancuria Rural Park**.
- The village of **Ajuy**, its black-sand beach and caves.
- A **beach** stop for lunch or a drink.

## How it works

You are picked up in your area and cross the island by minibus with a local guide who explains Majorero history and traditions. You stop at the viewpoints, drive the Betancuria mountain roads, walk down to the Ajuy caves and have free time at a beach before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see the **interior and west coast** without driving.
- Travellers comfortable in **English, German or Dutch**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Jandía** or arriving by cruise ship.
- Anyone who gets **carsick** on mountain roads.
