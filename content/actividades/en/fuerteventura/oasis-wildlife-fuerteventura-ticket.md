## What you'll see

- More than **3,000 animals** from around 250 species along a route through different regions of the world.
- **Live shows** with reptiles, parrots, sea lions and birds of prey.
- A **botanical garden** with cacti and dry-climate plants.
- The **Sabana Splash** water area and a playground for younger children.

## How it works

You buy a ticket for a specific day and show it at the park entrance in **La Lajita**, in the municipality of Pájara. Entry is between **9:00 and 17:00** and you go round at your own pace, choosing the order and how long to spend in each area. If you book the option with the **camel ride** (30 minutes) or the **lemurs**, your time is given at the ticket office; sessions run roughly every 20 minutes between 9:30 and 16:00, so it's worth stopping at the ticket office as soon as you arrive.

There are **two restaurants** inside. Food for the giraffes and camels is bought separately in the park.

## Practical tips

- **Getting there:** La Lajita is on the FV-2 between Gran Tarajal and Costa Calma. From Costa Calma it's a few minutes; from Caleta de Fuste or Corralejo it's much further. You can add a transfer when booking.
- **When to go:** first thing, when it's cooler and the animals are more active. The grounds are large and some stretches are in full sun.
- **Accessibility:** the park is partially accessible and rents electric wheelchairs.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- **Families with children**, for whom it's the easiest full-day plan on the island.
- Anyone staying in **Costa Calma, Esquinzo or Morro Jable**.
- Anyone who wants a day off from beach and wind.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- People who **don't like zoos** or animal shows.
- Anyone staying in the north without a car: the journey eats up a good part of the day.
