## What you will see

- The **Jandía Natural Park** coast and its beaches from the sea.
- The seabed through the **glass viewing deck**.
- With luck, **dolphins**, **whales** or turtles.

## How it works

You are picked up at your hotel in the south and board the Odysee 3. You sail along the Jandía coast looking for whales and dolphins, with music on board, and stop to swim, snorkel, kayak or use the waterslide. A Canarian lunch with paella is served at midday, you can go below to look through the glass bottom, and at the end you are driven back to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**, for the slide and the glass bottom.
- Anyone staying in **Morro Jable, Jandía or Costa Calma** wanting an all-inclusive boat day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **small group** or quiet sailing: up to 90 people come aboard.
