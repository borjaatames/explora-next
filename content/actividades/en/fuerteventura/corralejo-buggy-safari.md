## What you'll see

- **Dirt tracks** between dunes and old **volcanic craters** in the north.
- **Panoramic views** of Lobos Island and Lanzarote.
- The **lava fields** and volcanic cones around Corralejo.

## How it works

You're picked up from your hotel in **Corralejo** (or you go to the operator's office if you have a car) and, after the safety briefing and helmets, you set off in convoy behind the guide. You drive the buggy for about **two and a half hours** on tracks you can't use with a hire car, with stops at viewpoints and one of about **20 minutes at the Don Pepe bar** for a drink or some tapas, paid separately.

The operator stresses that it's a **sightseeing tour, not a race**: the guide sets the pace. There's a minimum number of participants; if it isn't reached, you're offered another date or a refund.

## Practical tips

- **Licence:** the driver must be 18 with their original licence. A photo won't do.
- **Dust:** the tracks are dry most of the year. Sunglasses and something to cover your mouth.
- **Clothing:** closed shoes and clothes you don't mind getting dirty.
- **Best time:** if you can choose, avoid the midday sun.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone looking for **adrenaline** who enjoys driving.
- Anyone staying in **Corralejo** who wants to see the northern interior in a short time.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- **Pregnant women** or people with back problems.
- Anyone **without a licence** who isn't travelling with someone who has one.
- Anyone staying in the south: pickup is only in Corralejo.
