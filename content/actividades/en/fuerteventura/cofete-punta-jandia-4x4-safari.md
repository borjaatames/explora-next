## What you will see

- **Punta de Jandía lighthouse**, at the island's southern tip.
- **Playa de los Ojos**, wild and rarely visited.
- **El Puertito de la Cruz**, a small fishing village.
- The **Cofete viewpoint** and **Cofete beach**.

## How it works

After pickup and a safety briefing in Morro Jable, the off-road vehicles take the tracks out to Punta de Jandía. You stop at Playa de los Ojos, get almost an hour free in El Puertito to eat or wander, and finish with the viewpoint and around 50 minutes on Cofete beach. Along the way the guide tells the story of Villa Winter.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **the whole wild south** in one outing.
- Anyone staying in **Morro Jable or Jandía**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone travelling with **babies in strollers**.
- Anyone who gets carsick on long **dirt tracks**.
