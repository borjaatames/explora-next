## What you'll see

- **Dolphins and, with luck, whales** off the Jandía coast.
- The southern coast of the **Jandía peninsula** from the sea.
- **Morro Jable harbour** and the Matorral beaches.

## How it works

You board the **Magic Dolphin**, a modern multi-deck boat, in **Morro Jable** harbour. For about **two hours** the crew looks for cetaceans off the south coast while a guide explains which species live in these waters and how to approach them without disturbing them: the operator follows a marine mammal protection protocol. There's a **swim stop** and drinks on board.

If you book with a transfer, you're picked up at points in **Costa Calma, Esquinzo, Morro Jable or Jandía**, about a five-minute walk from hotels in the area. If you go on your own, you need to be at the harbour 30 minutes beforehand.

## Practical tips

- **Sightings:** not guaranteed. These are wild animals.
- **Sea:** the south of Jandía is more sheltered from the trade wind than the north, but in strong wind the boat moves. If you get seasick, take something before boarding.
- **What to bring:** swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a layer for the way back.
- **Age:** from 2 years old.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone staying in **Jandía, Morro Jable or Costa Calma**.
- **Families** who want a short sea trip with a swim included.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone who **gets seasick easily**.
- Anyone looking for a **serious wildlife trip** in a small group: it's a large tourist boat.
- Anyone staying in the north: it's more than an hour and a half from Corralejo.
