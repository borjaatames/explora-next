## What you will see

- A **cheese farm** making internationally awarded majorero cheese.
- An **olive farm** where they explain how the oil is made.
- The island's interior countryside, greener than you'd expect.

## How it works

You're picked up in Costa Calma and driven to the cheese farm, where you meet the majorero goats and taste several cheeses. Then, at a second farm, there's an oil tasting, a green mojo demonstration, prickly pears and cactus jam, with tapas and wine. Each stop has a guided visit and the group stays at 16 people or fewer.

## Who I recommend it for

- **German speakers** keen on local produce.
- **Cheese and olive oil lovers**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't understand German** and doesn't want to rely on translation.
- Anyone after **beaches or scenery** as the main event.
