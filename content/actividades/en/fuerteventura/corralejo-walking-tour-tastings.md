## What you'll see

- **El Campanario**, with its 28-metre tower, where the walk starts.
- **Avenida Grandes Playas** and **Avenida Nuestra Señora del Carmen**.
- Corralejo's **old town**, dating from the early 19th century.
- The **harbour**, with views of Lobos and Lanzarote.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the **windmill next to the El Campanario shopping centre**, opposite the taxi rank. For about **two and a half hours** you walk through town while the guide tells the story of Corralejo and Fuerteventura, stopping at corners you won't find in guidebooks and at **local restaurants and shops** for the tastings.

You try **more than six products**: goat's cheese, wrinkled potatoes with red and green mojo, local tomato with island salt and oil, caramelised banana with coconut, fig bread, gofio mousse and honey rum. The walk ends at the **harbour**, right among the beaches, bars and shops, and the guide gives you **tips and routes** for the rest of the island.

## Practical tips

- **Time:** the published start is **10:30**. Arrive 10-15 minutes early.
- **Sun:** much of the route is outdoors. Bring a hat, sunscreen and water.
- **Eating afterwards:** it finishes around midday by the harbour, a good spot to carry on with fresh fish.
- **Accessibility:** the operator lists it as wheelchair accessible.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who has just arrived in **Corralejo** and wants to get their bearings.
- Anyone who wants to **try Majorero food** without gambling on random restaurants.
- Travellers on a **tight budget**.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone looking for a **full sit-down meal**.
- Anyone who already knows Corralejo and Canarian food well.
