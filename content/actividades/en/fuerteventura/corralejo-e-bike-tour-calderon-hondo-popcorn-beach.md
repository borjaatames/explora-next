## What you will see

- The **Bayuyo** and **Calderón Hondo** craters on the northern volcanic chain.
- The village of **Lajares** and the fishing village of **Majanicho**.
- **Popcorn Beach** and the coast on the way back to Corralejo.

## How it works

You're picked up at your Corralejo hotel and, after a briefing, ride off on an e-bike towards the volcanoes. At Calderón Hondo you walk about 15 minutes to a viewpoint over the crater, stop for a drink at a bar in Lajares, pass through Majanicho and a sandy stretch, and return along the coast with a stop at Popcorn Beach.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Corralejo** who wants something active.
- Those who already ride a **mountain bike** and want volcanic scenery.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone not confident **cycling off-road**.
- Families with **children under 12**.
