## What you will see

- The village of **Ajuy** and its sea caves.
- The church of **Nuestra Señora de Regla** in Pájara.
- **Betancuria**, the island's old capital.
- An **aloe vera farm** and a **majorero cheese** farm.
- **Tindaya** mountain and the Valle de la Concepción.

## How it works

You're picked up early to reach Ajuy before the big groups. Then you visit the church in Pájara, the aloe farm, the cheese farm and Betancuria, where you stop for tapas. Stops last from 20 minutes to an hour and drives from 10 to 40 minutes, with the guide sharing stories about the island.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families or groups** wanting a private day around the island.
- Anyone keen on the island's **interior and local culture**, not just beaches.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers or couples** after the lowest price.
- Anyone wanting **beach time** on the trip.
