## What you will see

- The **Corralejo dunes**, with Lobos and Lanzarote in the background.
- The crater of **Calderón Hondo**.
- **Playa del Mejillón**, known for its popcorn-shaped sand.
- **El Cotillo**, an old fishing village on the west coast.
- **Corralejo**, with free time to eat or wander.

## How it works

You're picked up in the south and driven by van up to the dunes. Then you walk for a while along the rim of Calderón Hondo, get an hour free in Corralejo, stop at Popcorn Beach and finish in El Cotillo before heading back past Tindaya mountain. The group is capped at eight people.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Costa Calma or Jandía** who doesn't want to drive north.
- Anyone who wants to see **lots of different places** in one day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who can't face **long hours on the road**.
- Anyone who needs the tour **in Spanish**.
