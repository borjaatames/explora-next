## What you will see

- **Cofete beach**, 14 km of undeveloped sand.
- **Villa Winter** and the stories around it.
- The **Punta Jandía lighthouse**, at the island's southern tip, and the village of **El Puertito**.
- The **viewpoints** above Cofete along the track.

## How it works

You are picked up in your southern resort and head up the Jandía Natural Park tracks by 4x4. You stop at the viewpoints above Cofete, go down to the beach and Villa Winter while the driver-guide explains the plants, wildlife and history, then continue to the Punta Jandía lighthouse with free time in El Puertito before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Morro Jable, Jandía or Costa Calma** who does not want to drive the track.
- Anyone wanting **Cofete and the lighthouse** in one outing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone prone to motion sickness on **bumpy dirt tracks**.
- Anyone staying in the **north**: there is no pickup there.
