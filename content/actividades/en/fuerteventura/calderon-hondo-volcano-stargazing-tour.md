## What you'll see

- The **Calderón Hondo crater**, a volcano near Lajares about 70 metres deep.
- From the rim, **Lajares, the Corralejo Dunes and Lanzarote**.
- At night, **constellations, planets and the Milky Way** over the crater.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the **Repsol h2go petrol station** in Corralejo or, with the transfer option, you're picked up by bus in **Corralejo, El Cotillo or Puerto del Rosario**. From the Lajares area you climb the volcanic trail to the **crater rim**, where the guide explains the volcano's geology and the legends of the island's first inhabitants about fire and sky. Once it's fully dark, the **stargazing** begins. About **three hours** in all.

The start time depends on the time of year: the guide emails it **24 hours before**. Some participants say they caught the sunset before it got dark.

## Practical tips

- **Book three days ahead:** the operator checks the forecast.
- **Clouds:** there's no refund if you can't see the stars. Check the forecast first.
- **Warm layer:** at night, with the wind, it gets cold even in summer.
- **Footwear:** closed shoes; loose lava is sharp.
- **By day:** the trail is free and open if you'd rather go on your own.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone staying in **Corralejo or El Cotillo** looking for a different evening plan.
- Anyone interested in **astronomy** or the island's history.
- Anyone who wants a short walk with a big reward.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- **Pregnant women, people over 65 or anyone with a fear of heights**: the operator itself advises against it.
- Anyone with only a few days who can't book with some slack in case it's cloudy.
