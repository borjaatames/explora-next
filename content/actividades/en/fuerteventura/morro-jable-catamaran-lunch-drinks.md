## What you'll see

- The **volcanic south coast of Jandía** from a 23-metre catamaran.
- **Dolphins** and other cetaceans, if they show up.
- Turquoise coves for a **swim**.

## How it works

You board the **Magic Select** in **Morro Jable** harbour, or you're picked up in **Costa Calma, Esquinzo, Jandía or Morro Jable** if you choose the transfer. After the safety briefing, the published itinerary splits the **four hours** like this: **an hour and a half** looking for dolphins, a **30-minute** swim stop, **an hour for the meal** on board and an hour sailing back.

The meal is **chicken mozárabe with rice and vegetables** and drinks are an **open bar** of water, soft drinks, beer and wine. It's the long version of the Morro Jable dolphin trips: more hours, with food and drinks included.

## Practical tips

- **Book ahead:** the operator itself warns that it sells out easily.
- **Pickup:** it doesn't reach every hotel; the points are about a five-minute walk away.
- **Sea:** the south coast is more sheltered, but in strong wind the catamaran moves.
- **What to bring:** swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a layer for the way back.
- **Special diets:** the menu is fixed; if you have allergies, tell the operator when booking.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone staying in **Jandía, Morro Jable or Costa Calma** who wants a full boat day.
- **Groups and couples** looking for a relaxed plan with food and drinks included.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone who **gets seasick**: it's four hours at sea.
- Anyone who only wants to see dolphins: the two-hour boat does the job for less.
- Anyone staying in the north of the island.
