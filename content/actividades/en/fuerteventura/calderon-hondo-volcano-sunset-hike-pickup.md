## What you will see

- The **Calderón Hondo crater**, the most visited volcano in the north.
- The **lava fields** and moon-like landscape around Lajares.
- The **sunset** from the top of the volcano.

## How it works

A bus picks you up in Corralejo, El Cotillo or Puerto del Rosario and takes you to the trailhead. With the guide you climb through the volcanic landscape to the crater rim, where you wait for the sunset. You walk down in the last light and are taken back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone after a **short hike** with a great sunset.
- Travellers without a car staying in the **north** of the island.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with a **baby carriage**.
- Anyone wanting **stargazing**: book the night departure for that.
