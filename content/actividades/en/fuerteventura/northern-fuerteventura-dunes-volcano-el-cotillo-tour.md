## What you will see

- The **Corralejo dunes**, inside the natural park.
- The **Calderón Bayuyo** crater, near Lajares.
- **El Cotillo**, a fishing village on the west coast.
- The **El Tostón lighthouse**, with views of Lanzarote and natural rock pools.

## How it works

You are picked up at your hotel in the north or centre and start at the Corralejo dunes. Then you climb the Calderón Bayuyo crater, continue to El Cotillo to see the village and finish at El Tostón lighthouse, where you can swim in the pools if the sea allows. There is a stop to buy something to eat.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Corralejo, Puerto del Rosario or Caleta de Fuste** without a car.
- Anyone wanting to see the **north** in a small group.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the **south**: there is no hotel pickup.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
