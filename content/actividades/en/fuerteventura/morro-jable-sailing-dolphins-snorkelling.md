## What you will see

- The **manta rays** that live in Morro Jable harbour.
- Jandía's **south coast** from a sailing yacht.
- **Dolphins** and, with luck, **whales** in open water.

## How it works

Before boarding you watch the harbour's manta rays and get a short briefing. Once out, the captain raises the sails, cuts the engine and follows fishermen's radio reports to approach dolphins if they're nearby. After about two hours you anchor to swim and snorkel, and sail back with tapas and a drink.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples and families** staying in Jandía or Costa Calma.
- Anyone who prefers a **small boat** to a crowded catamaran.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone prone to **seasickness**.
- Anyone who needs **guaranteed** dolphin sightings.
