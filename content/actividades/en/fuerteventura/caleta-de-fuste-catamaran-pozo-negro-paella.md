## What you will see

- Fuerteventura's **east coast** from the sea.
- **Pozo Negro** bay, by a small fishing village.
- With luck, **pilot whales**, **dolphins** or **turtles**.

## How it works

You board at Caleta de Fuste marina and sail south to Pozo Negro. There the catamaran anchors so you can swim, snorkel or sunbathe on deck. The crew cooks paella on board, and drinks are included for the whole trip, including the sail back to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Caleta de Fuste** or the centre of the island.
- **Families** and couples after an easy day at sea.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **small group**: up to 80 people come aboard.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
