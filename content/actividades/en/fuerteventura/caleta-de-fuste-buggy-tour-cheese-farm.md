## What you will see

- Inland **valleys and tracks** surrounded by old volcanoes.
- Traditional **Majorero-style** villages.
- A **goat farm** making artisan cheese.
- The **salt pans** and the Caleta de Fuste coastline.

## How it works

You're picked up at your Caleta de Fuste hotel, get your gear and a safety briefing, and follow the guide in a single or double buggy. You stop at a farm to see how goat cheese is made and taste it, carry on along tracks by the sea, and finish at the salt pans for photos.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Caleta de Fuste** who wants to drive off-road.
- **Couples** and friends after something active with a food stop.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without a **driving licence** or looking for **speed**.
- Anyone who can't stand **dust** and bumps.
