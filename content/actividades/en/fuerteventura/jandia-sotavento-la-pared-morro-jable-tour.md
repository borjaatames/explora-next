## What you will see

- The **La Pared cliffs** and **Viejo Rey** beach on the west coast.
- The **Sotavento lagoon**, with turquoise water and golden sand.
- The old town and seafront of **Morro Jable**.

## How it works

You're picked up at your southern hotel and the van goes first to La Pared, where you walk down to Viejo Rey beach. Then there's almost an hour free at Sotavento to swim plus a viewpoint stop, and the tour ends with an hour and a half in Morro Jable to stroll and eat on your own before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Costa Calma or Jandía** without a car.
- Those who want **beach and scenery** in one morning.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **limited mobility**.
- Anyone after a **full-day** island tour.
