## What you will see

- The north coast from the track leaving **El Jablito**.
- The **Villaverde volcanic area**, overlooking the whole north.
- Lava fields and dirt tracks.

## How it works

You leave the Corralejo meeting point and drive by road to Parque Holandés, where the track begins. You follow the coast and climb towards the volcanic area before returning to Corralejo on mixed roads. There are two stops for photos and to swap drivers. You can pick a sportier buggy, a more comfortable one or a 4-seat family model.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **families** who want to drive together.
- Anyone staying in **Corralejo** looking for a two-hour plan.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Groups with **no licensed driver**.
- **Pregnant women**.
