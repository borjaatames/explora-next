## What you will see

- The dirt track between **Morro Jable** and the west coast.
- The **Mirador del Viento**, overlooking the whole area.
- **Cofete** village and the story of **Villa Winter**.
- **Cofete beach**, over 12 km of untouched sand.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel in the south and, from Morro Jable, the 4x4 climbs the track to the viewpoint. Then you drop down to Cofete, where the guide tells the story of the village and the villa, and you get around 40 minutes free on the beach before heading back. Each vehicle carries up to six people.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Jandía or Costa Calma** who wants to see Cofete without driving.
- Anyone **short on time** looking for a half-morning trip.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets carsick on **bumpy, winding tracks**.
- Anyone wanting a relaxed **swim**: beach time is short.
