## What you will see

- The Conatvs **vineyard** and how vines are grown on volcanic ground.
- The **winery** where the wine is made and bottled.
- The **tasting room**, with wine and local produce.

## How it works

You make your own way to the winery in La Oliva. You start with a walk among the vines while the guide explains the grape varieties and the techniques adapted to the island, move into the winery to see how the wine is made, and finish with a tasting of several wines with bread, olive oil, olives and Majorero cheese.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Wine lovers** curious about Canarian wines.
- Anyone after a **short plan** in the north of the island.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who does not speak **English**.
- Families with **young children** (minimum age 16).
