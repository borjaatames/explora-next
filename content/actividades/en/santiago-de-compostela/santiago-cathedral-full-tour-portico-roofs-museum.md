## What you will see

- The **Portico of Glory**, Master Mateo's Romanesque masterpiece.
- The cathedral interior and the **cloister**.
- The rooms of the **Cathedral Museum**.
- The **rooftops**, with views over Santiago's roofs and squares.

## How it works

You meet at the Galicia Experience office on Rúa de Trás Salomé, and an official guide takes you through every visitable part of the cathedral complex over about four hours. Tickets are included, so there is no ticket-office queue.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **the whole cathedral** in one visit.
- Fans of **Romanesque art** and history.
- Travellers who couldn't get standalone tickets for the Portico or the rooftops.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Visitors **short on time** in Santiago.
- People with **reduced mobility**, because of the rooftop stairs.
