## What you will see

- The **Sil Canyon** from the water, with its vineyard-covered slopes.
- The terraces of the Ribeira Sacra's **heroic viticulture**.
- The **monastery of San Pedro de Rocas**, one of Galicia's oldest.
- **Ourense**, the spa city, with a guided visit.

## How it works

You leave Santiago by coach for the River Sil, where you board a boat through the canyon. Then you stop at a viewpoint, visit San Pedro de Rocas, have free time for lunch in Ourense and finish with a guided walk around the city before heading back, after about ten hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants the **Sil boat ride** without paying extra.
- Travellers who need a **live English guide**.
- Lovers of **inland landscapes** and wine.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who can't face **long coach days**.
- Travellers after a **small group**: there can be up to 50 people.
