## What the tour is like

You meet the guide at **Praza do Obradoiro**, in front of the cathedral. In **2 hours** you walk through the squares around it (**Obradoiro, Platerías, Quintana and Inmaculada**), the old-town streets, the **Abastos Market** and **Alameda Park**, which has the best view of the cathedral. The guide is official and tells you about the city's history, the Camino and its legends.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone arriving in Santiago (on the Camino or not) who wants their bearings on day one.
- Families and groups: from 4 people it costs about the same as a free tour plus tip.
- If you mainly want to see inside the cathedral, see the Pórtico de la Gloria or rooftop tours.
