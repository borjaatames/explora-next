## What you will see

- **Padrón**, linked to the arrival of Saint James.
- **Combarro**, with granaries and stone crosses facing the sea.
- **A Toxa** and its shell-covered chapel.
- **Cambados**, the Albariño capital, and Fefiñáns Square.
- **A Illa de Arousa**, joined to the mainland by a long bridge.

## How it works

You leave Santiago at 9:30 AM and stop with the guide in Padrón, Combarro (the long stop, for lunch and the optional boat), A Toxa, Cambados and Arousa. You are back in Santiago around 7:30 PM.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **several Rías Baixas villages** in a day.
- Fans of **seafood** and **Albariño**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers **fewer stops** with more time at each.
