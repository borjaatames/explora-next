## What you will see

- **Praza do Obradoiro** and the cathedral's main facade.
- The squares and streets of the **old town**, a World Heritage Site.
- Whatever you ask for: the **Camino de Santiago**, legends, architecture or local curiosities.

## How it works

You book, choose the time and meeting point, and an official Galician guide spends 2 hours with just your group. The route is not fixed: the guide adjusts it to your interests and pace.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and small groups who want to go at their own pace.
- Anyone with **specific interests** who wants a guide to adapt.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers** looking for the cheapest option: a group tour works out better.
