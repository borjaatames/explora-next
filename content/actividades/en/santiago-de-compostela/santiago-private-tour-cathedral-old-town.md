## What you will see

- Santiago's great **squares**: Obradoiro, Praterías and Quintana.
- The streets of the UNESCO-listed **old town**.
- If you choose it, the **cathedral** interior and its museum.

## How it works

When booking you pick one of three options: old town, cathedral and museum, or complete. You meet the guide on Obradoiro Square and for about two hours only your group covers what you chose; on the cathedral options, tickets are included.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and groups who want to go at their own pace.
- Anyone who wants the tour in **Italian or Portuguese**.
- Travellers with **reduced mobility**: it's wheelchair accessible.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Solo travellers after the **lowest price**: a shared tour is cheaper.
