## What you will see

- The **Sil Canyon** from one of its viewpoints.
- The terraced vineyards of **heroic viticulture**, from the boat.
- The **San Pedro de Rocas** monastery, carved into the rock.
- **Allariz**, one of the best-preserved medieval towns in Ourense.

## How it works

You are picked up in Santiago and the coach heads south to the province of Ourense. You stop at a canyon viewpoint, visit San Pedro de Rocas, have lunch on your own in Allariz and take a cruise of about 75 minutes on the Sil River before returning to Santiago.

## Who I recommend it for

- Travellers without a **car** who want to see the Ribeira Sacra in a day.
- Anyone who enjoys **river scenery** and wine country.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who dislikes **long coach rides**.
- Anyone after a small group on the standard option.
