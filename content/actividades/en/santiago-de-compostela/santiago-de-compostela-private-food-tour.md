## What the tour is like

You meet the guide at **Praza do Obradoiro**. During the morning you walk through the old town stopping at bars and shops where locals buy: **tortilla**, Galician produce and **regional wine**, with explanations about the food and customs. Along the way you pass the **cathedral** and the main squares. The tour ends with a **traditional Galician lunch and dessert**, with coffee.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to get to know Santiago through its food, without wasting time looking for good places.
- Groups of friends or families: from 4 people the price per person drops.
- If you want something shorter and in a group, see the tapas tour with live music.
