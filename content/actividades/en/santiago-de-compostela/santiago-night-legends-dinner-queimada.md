## What you will see

- Santiago's **old town** at nightfall.
- The **legends** and beliefs surrounding the city.
- A **Galician dinner** in a traditional restaurant.
- The **queimada** ritual and its spell.

## How it works

You start with a guided walk of about 90 minutes through the old town, hearing its legends. Then you have dinner of typical Galician dishes with a drink and finish with the queimada ritual.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and groups of friends after a different kind of night out.
- Anyone who enjoys **folklore** and Galician food.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers to **choose their own dinner** rather than a set menu.
