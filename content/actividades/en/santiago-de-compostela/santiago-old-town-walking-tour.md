## What you will see

- **Praza do Obradoiro**.
- The **Praterías**, **Quintana** and **Inmaculada** squares.
- **Praza de Cervantes**.
- The **Mercado de Abastos**.

## How it works

You meet in the Obradoiro and the guide takes you round the squares surrounding the cathedral, Praza de Cervantes and the Mercado de Abastos, finishing in the old-town streets.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone arriving **on day one** who wants to get oriented.
- **Tight budgets**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **go inside the cathedral**.
