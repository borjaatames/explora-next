## What you will see

- An **Albariño winery** with tasting.
- **Cambados** and the Fefiñáns manor.
- The **mussel rafts** from the boat.
- **A Toxa**, **A Lanzada** and **Combarro**.

## How it works

You leave Santiago by coach for a winery tasting, continue to Cambados and O Grove, where you board to see the mussel rafts while eating mussels. In the afternoon you visit A Toxa, A Lanzada and Combarro before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Seafood and wine lovers**.
- Anyone wanting to see **estuary Galicia**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
