## What you will see

- The **Alameda** and the cobbled streets of the old town.
- **Praza do Obradoiro** and the cathedral from outside.
- **Quintana** and **Praterías** squares.
- The **Mercado de Abastos**, at the end of the walk.

## How it works

You meet by Capela do Pilar and spend 2 hours walking the old town with a local guide, in a group of up to 10. The route takes in the squares around the cathedral and finishes at the market.

## Who I recommend it for

- First-time visitors who want to **get their bearings**.
- Anyone who prefers **small groups**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **go inside** the cathedral: you need a separate visit.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
