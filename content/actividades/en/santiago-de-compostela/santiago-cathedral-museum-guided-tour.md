## What you will see

- The **Obradoiro façade**.
- The **Romanesque interior** and the high altar.
- The **botafumeiro** and the relics chapel.
- The **museum**: cloister, royal pantheon and library.
- The **Pórtico de la Gloria**, if you choose that option.

## How it works

You meet the guide by the cathedral, go in without queuing and tour the church and the museum. If you chose the Pórtico de la Gloria, you visit it in a timed slot.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Santiago.
- **Pilgrims** who want to understand the cathedral.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
