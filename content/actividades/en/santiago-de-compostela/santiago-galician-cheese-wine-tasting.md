## What you will see

- **Three Galician cheeses**, each with its own story.
- **Three Galician wines** chosen to go with them.
- A restaurant in Santiago's **old town**.

## How it works

You meet at the Galicia Travels office on Rúa das Casas Reais and walk to a nearby restaurant in the historic centre. Over an hour you taste three wines and three Galician cheeses in pairs, with bread and water, while each product is explained.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors **short on time** who want to try local produce.
- **Couples** and friends who enjoy wine.
- Anyone needing a **wheelchair-accessible** activity.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Under-18s**.
- **Vegans**.
