## What you will see

- The **Alameda**, the city's large historic park.
- **Rúa do Franco** and **Rúa do Vilar**, with their coats of arms and inscriptions.
- The **Praterías** facade and the cloister of **Pazo de Fonseca**.
- **Praza do Obradoiro** and the area around **San Martín Pinario**.

## How it works

You meet the guide in the Alameda and walk through the old town for 2 hours in a group of up to 10. At each stop you learn to read details on the facades and in the street layout. The walk ends at the Obradoiro.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone interested in **history**, **architecture** and **urban design**.
- Visitors who know the basics and want to **see the city differently**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **does not speak Spanish**.
- Anyone looking for a light legends walk.
