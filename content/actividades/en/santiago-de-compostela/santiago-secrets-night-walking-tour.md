## What you will see

- Santiago's **old town** after dark, with a very different atmosphere.
- The city's former **medieval Jewish quarter**.
- The outside of Santiago's **oldest house**, from the 12th century.
- Corners linked to the **mysteries and enigmas** of Compostela.

## How it works

You meet at Casa da Conga on Quintana Square, and for about two hours an official guide leads you through the historic centre, telling little-known stories and uncomfortable topics, always backed by facts. There's a small surprise gift at the end.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who has already seen the cathedral and wants **another side of Santiago**.
- Travellers who prefer **real history** to made-up legends.
- People travelling with a **dog**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't speak Spanish**.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
