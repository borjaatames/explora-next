## What you will see

- The **Finisterre lighthouse** and the **km 0** marker.
- **Muxía's** sanctuary on the rocks.
- The **Ézaro waterfall**.
- The **Carnota hórreo** and the **Ponte Maceira** bridge.

## How it works

You leave Santiago by coach and stop at Ponte Maceira, Muxía and Cape Finisterre. Lunch is in Finisterre town, and in the afternoon you see Ézaro and Carnota before returning along the Muros estuary.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Pilgrims** wanting to reach km 0.
- Anyone wanting to see the **Galician coast** without driving.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets **carsick**.
