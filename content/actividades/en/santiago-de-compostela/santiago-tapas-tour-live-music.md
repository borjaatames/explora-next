## What you will see

- Three old-town **bars**.
- **Galician tapas**: octopus, Padrón peppers, mussels or tortilla.
- A Galician **liqueur tasting**.
- **Live music**.

## How it works

You meet in Praza de Cervantes and go with the guide to three bars, with a drink and a tapa at each, a liqueur tasting at a traditional shop and live music.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Food lovers**.
- Anyone arriving **on the first night** with no idea where to go.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't drink alcohol** or wants a full dinner.
