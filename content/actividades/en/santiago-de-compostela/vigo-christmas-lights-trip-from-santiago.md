## What you will see

- **Vigo's Christmas lights**, among the most famous in Europe.
- The **Vigo estuary**, crossed by boat from Moaña.
- The city-centre atmosphere: Porta do Sol, Gran Vía and the Christmas stalls.

## How it works

You leave by coach from Capilla del Pilar for Moaña and cross the estuary by boat to Vigo. There you have about three and a half hours to see the lights at your own pace, with tips from the coordinator. At the end you return to Santiago together.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and groups in Santiago over Christmas.
- Anyone who wants to see Vigo's lights **without driving**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **guided tour** of Vigo: most of it is free time.
