## What you will see

- The **rock arches** of Las Catedrales beach.
- **Lugo's Roman walls**.
- The **Illa Pancha lighthouse**.

## How it works

You leave from Capilla del Pilar for Lugo, where you walk the walls and old town. You continue to Illa Pancha and finish at Las Catedrales beach at low tide before heading back to Santiago.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **Las Catedrales** without worrying about tides and permits.
- **Photographers**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't want a **long day** on the coach.
