## What you will see

- The **Cathedral Museum**, which explains how the church was built over the centuries.
- The **naves and chapels** of the recently restored cathedral.
- The **crypt** with the tomb of Saint James.
- Spaces such as the **treasury**, the chapter house and the cloister.

## How it works

You meet the guide on Rúa de Trás Salomé, start in the museum and then enter the cathedral through a separate access. Over about two hours you cover the complex with commentary on art, history and traditions such as hugging the Apostle and the botafumeiro.

## Who I recommend it for

- First-time visitors who want to **understand the cathedral**.
- Travellers who prefer a tour in **Italian, Portuguese or French**.
- **Pilgrims** finishing the Camino who want to see the church calmly.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**.
- Anyone who wants the **rooftops** or the Portico of Glory: they are not included.
