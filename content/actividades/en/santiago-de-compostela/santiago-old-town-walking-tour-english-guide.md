## What you will see

- The Romanesque **Praterías** facade and the Casa do Cabildo.
- **Praza do Obradoiro** and the former pilgrims' hostel.
- **Azabachería** and **Quintana**, with the Holy Door.
- Lesser-known old town streets and a **viewpoint** over the cathedral.

## How it works

You meet an official guide in Praterías and spend 2 hours walking, in English, around the cathedral squares and several old town streets. The walk ends at a panoramic viewpoint.

## Who I recommend it for

- **English-speaking** travellers wanting a good first walk.
- Anyone after a **budget** tour with an official guide.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **does not understand English**.
- Anyone wanting to go inside the cathedral.
