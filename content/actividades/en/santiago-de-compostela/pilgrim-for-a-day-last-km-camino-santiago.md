## What you will see

- Trails of the **French Way** on its final kilometres.
- **Monte do Gozo**, where the cathedral towers first come into view.
- The arrival in **Praza do Obradoiro**, the end of the Camino.

## How it works

You meet in central Santiago, get a staff and hat, and are taken about 10 km out of the city. From there you walk with the guide, who shares stories and legends of the Camino, until you enter the Obradoiro. You go home with photos and a small souvenir.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors to Santiago who **have not walked the Camino**.
- Anyone who enjoys **walking** and wants something different from a city tour.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **reduced mobility** or who cannot walk long distances.
