## What you will see

- The medieval old town of **Betanzos**, famous for its tortilla.
- The **Tower of Hercules**, A Coruña's Roman lighthouse.
- Central **A Coruña**, with free time and a guided walk.

## How it works

You leave by coach from Capilla del Pilar for Betanzos, where you take a guided walk and, depending on the option, a tortilla tasting. Then on to A Coruña: free time for lunch, a visit to the Tower of Hercules and a guided walk before heading back to Santiago.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with a **free day** in Santiago who wants to see another Galician city.
- Travellers **without a car**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **does not speak Spanish** and wants a live guide.
