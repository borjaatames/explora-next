## What you will see

- The cathedral's **stone rooftops**.
- **Praza do Obradoiro** from above.
- The **Carraca tower** and the bells.
- The **museum**: cloister, chapter house and library.

## How it works

You meet in the Obradoiro, tour the museum highlights with the guide and, after a short break, climb the 150 steps to the rooftops and the Carraca tower.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who already knows the cathedral and wants **a new angle**.
- **Photographers**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **vertigo** or reduced mobility.
- Anyone who **doesn't speak Spanish**.
