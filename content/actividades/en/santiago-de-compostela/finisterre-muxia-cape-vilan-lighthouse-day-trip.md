## What you will see

- The **Ézaro waterfall**, where the Xallas river drops straight into the sea.
- **Cape Finisterre** and its lighthouse, the Romans' end of the world.
- **Muxía** and the Virxe da Barca sanctuary facing the Atlantic.
- **Cape Vilán lighthouse**, above the cliffs of Camariñas.

## How it works

You leave from Rúa dos Bautizados in Santiago and travel the Costa da Morte in an air-conditioned vehicle with an official guide. Stops include Ézaro, Cape Finisterre, the town of Fisterra (an hour and a half for lunch), Muxía and Cape Vilán before returning to Santiago after about nine hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **end of the world** and Galicia's wildest coast in a day.
- **Pilgrims** who want to reach Finisterre without walking.
- Travellers who already know Carnota and would rather add **lighthouses and cliffs**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets carsick on **long road trips**.
- Travellers after **small groups**: there can be up to 60 people.
