## What you will see

- Salamanca's **old town** with someone who actually lives there.
- **Lesser-known spots**: courtyards, cafés and streets off the usual circuit.
- Whatever you choose: monuments, street art, shops or places to eat.

## How it works

You book, choose a time and meet your local guide. There is no script: you say what interests you and the walk takes shape over roughly two hours, with tips for the rest of your stay.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** who want to get their bearings.
- **Couples, families or friends** who prefer a chat to a lecture.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **entrance fees included**.
- Anyone after an **in-depth**, structured history tour.
