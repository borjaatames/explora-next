## What you will see

- **Plaza Mayor** and the **Casa de las Conchas**.
- The **Clerecía** and the Pontifical University.
- The **Sky of Salamanca**.
- Inside the **University** and the New and Old **cathedrals**.

## How it works

You meet on Plaza Mayor and walk with the guide to the Casa de las Conchas, the Clerecía and the Sky of Salamanca, go inside the University and finish visiting both cathedrals.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Salamanca.
- Anyone wanting to see the essentials **inside** in one morning.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't speak Spanish**.
