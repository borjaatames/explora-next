## What you will see

- The Baroque **Plaza Mayor** and the **Clerecía**.
- The façade of the **Casa de las Conchas**.
- The courtyards of the **University of Salamanca**.
- The **Sky of Salamanca** and the **Old and New Cathedrals**.

## How it works

You meet your guide in the Plaza Mayor and walk the old town for about 2 hours: the Clerecía, the Casa de las Conchas, the University courtyards, the Sky of Salamanca and, to finish, the cathedrals. With the extended version you add two more monuments.

## Who I recommend it for

- First-time visitors who want to **get their bearings** on day one.
- Anyone who prefers a **smaller group** to crowded free tours.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**, as the provider states.
