## What you will see

- The ham **cellar** and **drying rooms**.
- How **Iberian ham** is graded.
- A **tasting** of ham and cured sausages.

## How it works

You arrive at Ibéricos Torreón, attend the class on breeds, feeding and curing, tour the cellar and drying rooms and finish with the tasting and a glass of wine.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Ham** and food lovers.
- Anyone wanting to learn to **tell qualities apart**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Vegans** or **vegetarians**.
