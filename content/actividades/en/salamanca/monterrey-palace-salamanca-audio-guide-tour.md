## What you will see

- The **Monterrey Palace**, a gem of the Spanish Renaissance.
- Rooms the **House of Alba** still uses.
- Paintings by **Ribera**, **Sánchez Coello** and **Carreño de Miranda**, plus period furniture.
- A **panoramic view** of Salamanca from one of its towers.

## How it works

You enter at your booked time and explore the palace at your own pace with the audio guide, which combines narration, music and images, for about an hour. With the extended option, the visit continues through the kitchens and the servants' corridor.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who has seen the essentials and wants a **quieter monument**.
- Visitors interested in **art** and aristocratic life.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**.
- Anyone who needs the option to **cancel** for a refund.
