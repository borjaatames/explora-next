## What you will see

- The Gothic-Renaissance **New Cathedral**.
- The Romanesque **Old Cathedral** and its altarpiece.
- The **cloister** and Santa Bárbara chapel.
- The **Episcopal Palace** exhibitions.

## How it works

You enter with your booking, pick up the audio guide and explore both cathedrals, the cloister and the exhibitions at your own pace.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers to visit **at their own pace**.
- **Art lovers**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to climb the **towers**: that's a separate visit.
