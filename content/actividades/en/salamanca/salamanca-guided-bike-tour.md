## What you will see

- The **Tormes river** and the cathedral skyline.
- The **Roman bridge**.
- **Palacio de Monterrey** and **Casa de las Conchas**.
- The **cathedrals** and the **University**.

## How it works

You meet the guide, pick up your bike and ride down to the Tormes for the Roman bridge and the city skyline, then climb into the old town via Monterrey, the Conchas and the cathedrals to the University.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **more in less time**.
- Active **couples** and **families**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **can't ride a bike**.
