## What you will see

- The **Cave of Salamanca** and its legends.
- The **House of Deaths** and the **Fonseca Palace**.
- The **Garden of Calisto and Melibea**.
- Tales of the **Inquisition** and Salamanca's picaresque past.

## How it works

You meet the guide outside the tourist office on the Plaza Mayor and walk the centre after dark for about two hours, stepping into the free-access sites. At the end you get a pass for the cured-meat tasting and the Charro Filigree Museum, to use whenever you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors who know the basics and want **another side of the city**.
- Anyone who enjoys **stories** more than dates.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone **not fluent in Spanish**.
- Anyone after a classic monuments tour.
