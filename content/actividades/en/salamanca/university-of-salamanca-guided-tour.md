## What you will see

- The **Plateresque façade** and its frog.
- The **Escuelas Mayores** cloister.
- The **Fray Luis de León** lecture hall.
- The **historic library**.

## How it works

You check in with the guide on Calle Libreros, start at the façade and go inside to tour the cloister, lecture halls and the staircase up to the library.

## Who I recommend it for

- **History** and literature lovers.
- Anyone wanting to understand **university Salamanca**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't speak Spanish**.
