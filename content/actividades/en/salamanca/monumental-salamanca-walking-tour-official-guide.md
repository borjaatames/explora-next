## What you will see

- The **Plaza Mayor** and the **Casa de las Conchas**.
- **La Clerecía**, now home to the Pontifical University.
- The façades and history of the **University of Salamanca**.
- The **Old** and **New Cathedrals**.

## How it works

You meet your official guide at Plaza del Poeta Iglesias and walk through the historic centre for an hour and a half. Everything is explained from outside, with no tickets, and the walk ends at the New Cathedral so you can carry on at your own pace.

## Who I recommend it for

- New arrivals who want a **quick overview**.
- Travellers on a **tight budget**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a guided visit **inside** the University or the cathedrals.
- Anyone who prefers a **small group**.
