## What you will see

- The University of Salamanca's **Historic Building**, with its lecture halls and library.
- **Fonseca College**, the only old college still intact, and its cloister.
- **Colleges, palaces, cloisters and gardens** in the university quarter.

## How it works

Your guide picks you up at your hotel on the day and time you choose. For 1 hour 30 minutes you explore the university quarter with just your group: the Historic Building, Fonseca College and other corners, with stories about university traditions dating back to 1218. Tickets (€5 per person) are paid separately.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families or small groups** who want a guide to themselves.
- Anyone on a **tight schedule** who needs to pick the time.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers** after the cheapest option: a shared group tour works out better.
