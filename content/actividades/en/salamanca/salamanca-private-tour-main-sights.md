## What you will see

- The **Plaza Mayor** and its surrounding architecture.
- The **Casa de las Conchas**, now a library.
- The **University**, one of Europe's oldest, and the **cathedrals**.
- Palaces and corners linked to writers and noble families.

## How it works

The guide picks you up at your hotel or an agreed point and you walk through the old town for an hour and a half, stopping at the main monuments. As it is private, you set the pace and the questions.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and **groups** who want a guide to themselves.
- Anyone **short on time** who wants the essentials on a flexible schedule.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers** or couples on a tight budget.
- Anyone expecting entrance fees to be included.
