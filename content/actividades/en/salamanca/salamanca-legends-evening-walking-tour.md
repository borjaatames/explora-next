## What you will see

- **Plaza Mayor** at dusk.
- The **Palacio de la Salina** courtyard.
- The **San Esteban** façade.
- **Underground galleries** and a **snow pit**.

## How it works

You meet on Plaza Mayor as evening falls and the guide leads you through courtyards, palaces, towers and gardens telling legends, with entry to underground galleries and a snow pit.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who's seen the essentials and wants **another side** of Salamanca.
- **Couples** and families with older kids.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't speak Spanish**.
