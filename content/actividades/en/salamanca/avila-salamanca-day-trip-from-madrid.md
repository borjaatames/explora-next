## What you will see

- **Salamanca Cathedral**, with its Romanesque and Gothic parts.
- The **University of Salamanca**, its Plateresque façade and historic lecture halls.
- The **walls of Ávila** and the **Convent of Saint Teresa**, built on her birthplace.

## How it works

You leave Madrid from Puerta de O'Donnell in a vehicle for up to 8 people. In the morning you tour Salamanca with a guide, going inside the Cathedral and the University. You stop for lunch at a traditional restaurant (everyone pays their own) and in the afternoon continue to Ávila to explore the walled old town and the convent, with a coffee, tea or wine on a terrace before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone based in **Madrid** who wants to see Salamanca and Ávila in one day.
- Anyone who prefers **small groups** to large coaches.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **explore Salamanca at leisure**: better to stay overnight.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
