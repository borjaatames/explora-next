## What you'll see

A beach day at **Cala Granadella**, in Jávea — regularly voted one of Spain's most beautiful coves. Turquoise, crystal-clear water ideal for **swimming and snorkelling**, framed by pine woods and cliffs, and a Blue Flag beach every year since 1989.

## How it works

**Round-trip transport from Valencia** with a guide escort; the meeting point in Valencia is confirmed when you book. It's a full beach day (about 8 hours) — bring beach gear, water and food, since food, drinks and snorkelling equipment aren't included.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants one of the Costa Blanca's best coves without the summer parking nightmare.
- Beach and snorkelling lovers.
- Not a guided cultural tour — it's a transfer to spend the day at the beach.
