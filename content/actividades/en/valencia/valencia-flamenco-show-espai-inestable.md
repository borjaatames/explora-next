## What you will see

- **Live flamenco** with guitar, singing, clapping and dance.
- Different **styles and rhythms** performed by several artists.
- An **intimate venue** in a historic building in the centre.

## How it works

You arrive with your ticket, sit close to the stage and enjoy an hour-long show. No dinner or drinks are included, so you can pair it with a meal in the centre before or after.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **quality flamenco** without paying for a set menu.
- **First-timers** who prefer a short format.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a **dinner and show** in the same place.
