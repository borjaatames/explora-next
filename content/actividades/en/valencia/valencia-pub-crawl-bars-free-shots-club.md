## How the night works

At 10:30 pm you meet in **Plaça de Sant Jaume** in the old town and head out with the group to **three lively bars or pubs**, with a **free shot** at each. The night ends at **a big nightclub** in the centre, with entry included.

The route changes by day so you always hit the busiest spots, with backpackers, students and locals.

## Good to know

- Clubs enforce a dress code: no tank tops, caps, sandals or sportswear (for men).
- Bring ID.

## Who it's for

- Solo travellers keen to meet people.
- Groups of friends who don't know Valencia's nightlife.
