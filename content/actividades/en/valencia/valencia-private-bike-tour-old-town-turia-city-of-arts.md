## What you'll see

From the **old town** (Cathedral, Plaza de la Virgen, Plaza de la Reina and City Hall) to the **Turia garden** and the futuristic **City of Arts and Sciences**.

## How it works

Meet on Carrer de la Corretgeria, next to the Cathedral. Bike and helmet included. Price **per group**: €240 for 2, €280 for 4 and €360 for 6.

## Who it's for

- Travellers who want Valencia's essentials in a morning.
- Families with kids who can ride.
