## The route

You start at **Estación del Norte**, an Art Nouveau gem, and the historic **bullring**. In the old town you skip the queues at the **Silk Exchange**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the church of **San Nicolás**, known as «Valencia's Sistine Chapel». You stroll through the bustling **Central Market** and see the Gothic **Serranos Towers** and the Baroque facade of the **Marqués de Dos Aguas palace**.

At lunchtime, a **Valencian paella** with wine, water and bread. In the afternoon, a **horchata** with a traditional pastry and, to finish, the **City of Arts and Sciences** (from outside; tickets aren't included).

## Who it's for

- Anyone with a single day in Valencia who wants to see it all, lunch included.
- Families who prefer a guide just for them.
