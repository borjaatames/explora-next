## What you will see

- The **Silk Exchange**, a gem of civil Gothic.
- **San Nicolás** church and its Baroque frescoes.
- The **cathedral** and the Holy Grail.
- The **Torres de Serranos**, Central Market and Plaza de la Virgen.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the Torres de Serranos and walk through the old town, going inside San Nicolás, the Silk Exchange and the cathedral. You pass the Central Market and the Almoina and finish in Plaza de la Virgen.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Valencia.
- Anyone into **history and art**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers to **explore alone** at their own pace.
