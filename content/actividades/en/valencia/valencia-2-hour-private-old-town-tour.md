## The route

In two hours you walk the main squares of the centre: **Manises**, **la Virgen** and **la Reina**, where your guide shares stories of local politics, religion and traditions. You see the **Roman ruins** in Plaza de l'Almoina, the Baroque facade of the **Marqués de Dos Aguas palace** and quirky **Plaza Redonda**.

You discover **La Estrecha**, Europe's narrowest building, admire the Gothic **Silk Exchange** and finish amid the bustle of the **Central Market**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who's just arrived and wants to get oriented fast.
- Families: children go free.
- Anyone short on time in the city.
