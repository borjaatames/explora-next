## What you will see

- The **navigable underground river** of the San José Caves.
- **Stalactites**, **stalagmites** and **cave paintings**.
- The cave's dry gallery, explored on foot.

## How it works

You leave Valencia by bus for La Vall d'Uixó while the guide explains the history of the area. At the caves you board a boat that covers about 850 metres of river, then continue on foot through the dry gallery. Before heading back there are 30 free minutes for a coffee or the shop. About four hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** looking for a short, different excursion.
- Anyone with **only half a day** free in Valencia.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **claustrophobia**.
- Anyone expecting a **long route** inside the cave.
