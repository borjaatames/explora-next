## What you will see

- The **savannah** with giraffes, rhinos and lions.
- The **equatorial forest** with gorillas and leopards.
- The **Kitum cave** and the elephants.
- **Madagascar** and its lemurs.

## How it works

You pick a day and time slot, enter with the ticket on your phone and explore at your own pace. Check the feeding routes and talks schedule when you arrive.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**.
- **Animal lovers** and nature photographers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without **half a day** to spare.
