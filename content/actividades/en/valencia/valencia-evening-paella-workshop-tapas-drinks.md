## What you will see

- How a **Valencian** or **seafood paella** is made, step by step.
- Classic tapas such as **steamed mussels**, **Spanish omelette** or **bravas**.
- Valencian desserts like **coca de llanda** and **mistela**.

## How it works

You arrive at the school, start with tapas and a sangria workshop, and then the chef walks the group through making the paella you chose. Once it is ready, you sit down to eat it together and finish with fruit, coca de llanda, coffee and mistela. About three hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Food lovers** who want to learn the authentic recipe.
- **Couples** and **groups of friends** looking for a different dinner.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone not comfortable in **English**.
- Anyone who just wants to **eat paella** without cooking.
