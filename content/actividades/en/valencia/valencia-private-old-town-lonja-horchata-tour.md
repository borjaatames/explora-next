## The route

You start at the **Serranos Towers**, the old Gothic gate in the city walls. From there, **Plaza de la Virgen**, **Plaza de l'Almoina** and the **cathedral** on Plaza de la Reina. You stop at the **Horchatería de Santa Catalina** for horchata and a traditional pastry. Then on past Europe's narrowest house, the bustle of the **Central Market**, the **Silk Exchange** (a UNESCO World Heritage Site, ticket included), Plaza Redonda and the Baroque facade of the **Marqués de Dos Aguas palace**. The walk ends crossing the **Exposición bridge** and the **Alameda** to the **Monforte gardens**.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors who want the centre explained.
- Families: it's stroller accessible and the pace adapts to you.
