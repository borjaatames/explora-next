## What you will see

- Four **neighbourhood bars and restaurants** popular with locals.
- Traditional dishes such as **esgarraet**, **cocas** and ham or seafood croquettes.
- **Valencian wines** and local drinks like **mistela**.

## How it works

You meet the guide in Plaza del Patriarca and walk to four stops over about three hours. At each one you share several tapas with a paired drink, building up to a full dinner, while the guide explains what you are eating and how Valencians do their "picaeta".

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants a **good first-night dinner** without hunting for a table.
- **Couples** and **solo travellers** who like a small-group atmosphere.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Vegetarians**, **vegans** or **coeliacs**.
- Families with **children under 16**.
