## What you will see

- The **Ruzafa** district and the **North Station**.
- The old town: **Central Market**, **Silk Exchange**, **Cathedral** and **Serranos Towers**.
- The **Turia Gardens** and the **City of Arts and Sciences**.

## How it works

You pick up your bike in Ruzafa and ride with the guide to the North Station and Town Hall Square. Then you weave through the old town, drop into the old Turia riverbed and follow it to the City of Arts and Sciences before heading back. There are photo stops and explanations at each point.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone **short on time** who wants the essentials.
- **Couples** and **families** comfortable on a bike.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone not **confident on a bike**.
- Anyone wanting a **very small group**: it can be up to 28 people.
