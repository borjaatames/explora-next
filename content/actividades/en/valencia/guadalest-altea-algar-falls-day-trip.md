## What you will see

- **Altea's** old town and its blue-domed church.
- **Guadalest**, its castle and the turquoise reservoir.
- The waterfalls and pools of the **Fuentes del Algar**.

## How it works

You leave by coach from the Torres de Serranos. First stop is Altea with a guided walk and free time, then up to Guadalest for the visit and lunch, finishing at the Fuentes del Algar for a swim before heading back to Valencia.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see the **Costa Blanca** without a car.
- Anyone after **nature and a swim** in summer.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets **travel sick** or doesn't want long coach rides.
