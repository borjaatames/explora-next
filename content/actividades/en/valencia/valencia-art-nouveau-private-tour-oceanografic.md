## The route

With your private guide you explore Valencia's centre and Ensanche district, uncovering the stories behind its most beautiful facades: **Estación del Norte**, the **bullring** and elegant **Mercado de Colón**, alongside lesser-known gems such as **Casa Judía**, **Casa Cortina** and **Casa Ferrer**. Then you take the bus to the futuristic **City of Arts and Sciences**.

## The options

- **Standard:** the 3-hour tour, no tickets.
- **Full:** the tour plus skip-the-line tickets to the **Oceanogràfic**, which you visit on your own afterwards.

## Who it's for

- Architecture lovers who already know the old town.
- Families who want to combine a walk and the Oceanogràfic in one day.
