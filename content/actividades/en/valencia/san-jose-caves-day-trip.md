## What you'll see

A half-day escape underground, with the boat as the star.

- **Underground river:** the San José Caves hold the longest navigable underground river in Europe.
- **Boat ride:** glide through galleries, lakes and rock formations aboard a small boat.
- **No car needed:** round-trip transport from Valencia is included.

## How it works

Pickup is in central Valencia and is confirmed on booking. The trip lasts about **4 hours** in total, including travel. The cave has sections with steps and low ceilings.

## Who it's for

- If you already know central Valencia and want a different escape.
- If you're travelling with kids: the boat ride through the cave is usually a hit.
- If you prefer a comfortable trip with transport sorted.
