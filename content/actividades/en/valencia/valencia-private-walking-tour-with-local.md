## What you will see

- The Valencia neighbourhoods that match **your interests**.
- **Lesser-known corners**, such as courtyards or local cafés.
- **Street art** and local culture, if that appeals.

## How it works

You book, agree the time with your guide and, if you like, get picked up at your accommodation. For two hours you walk the city with no fixed script: you say what interests you and the guide picks streets, stories and tips on where to eat or shop.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** who want to get their bearings.
- Travellers who prefer **chatting with a local** over memorised facts.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **visit with entry** to the major monuments.
- Anyone after a cheap **group tour**.
