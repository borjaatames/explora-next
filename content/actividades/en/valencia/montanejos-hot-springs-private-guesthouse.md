## What you will see

- The **Bridal Veil waterfall** (Salto de la Novia), 60 metres high.
- The **Fuente de los Baños**, with water at around 25 °C all year.
- Viewpoints over the Mijares **canyons** and the Montanejos **dam**.

## How it works

You leave Plaça de Saragossa at 9:15 am by minibus. The first stop is the waterfall; next comes the Fuente de los Baños, where the private guesthouse lets you change, shower and leave your things. There is an optional lunch stop in the village and, before heading back, the canyon and dam viewpoints. You are back in Valencia around 5:30 pm.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **swim in comfort**, with showers and lockers.
- **Couples**, **friends** and **solo travellers** not looking for a tough hike.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after **hiking** or adventure sports.
- Anyone looking for the **cheapest way** to visit Montanejos.
