## The route

You set off from the **Serranos Towers**, the medieval city gate, and pass through historic **Plaza de la Virgen**. Then you ride along the **Turia Gardens**, the old riverbed turned into a park, to the futuristic **City of Arts and Sciences**.

You carry on to **El Cabanyal**, the district of old fishermen's houses, and the **Marina**, finishing by the sea along the **seafront promenade**. Halfway, you stop at a **local bar** for a soft drink.

## Who it's for

- Active families and couples: it's a flat, easy-going route.
- Anyone who wants the centre, the City of Arts and Sciences and the beach in one outing.
