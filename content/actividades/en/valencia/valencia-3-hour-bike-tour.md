## What you will see

### Old town
Departure next to the City Hall and a ride through the historic centre: the **Cathedral**, Plaza de la Virgen and corners only locals know.

### Jardín del Turia
The old riverbed, turned into a 9-kilometre park. You cycle among palm and orange trees, passing the **Palau de la Música** and the **Gulliver park**, a giant slide inspired by *Gulliver's Travels*.

### City of Arts and Sciences
The white buildings by **Santiago Calatrava**: the Hemisfèric, the Science Museum and the Palau de les Arts, before heading back to the centre.
