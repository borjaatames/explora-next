## What you'll see

A panoramic route that links the two faces of Valencia in a single morning, with a local guide and at an easy pace.

- **Old town:** Plaza de la Reina, the Cathedral, the Silk Exchange and the Central Market from outside, winding through El Carmen.
- **Turia Gardens:** the nine kilometres of the old riverbed turned into a park, the city's green backbone.
- **City of Arts and Sciences:** Calatrava's buildings, the best finale of the route.

## How it works

The meeting point is in the centre and is confirmed on booking. Bike and helmet are included. It's about **3 hours** at a gentle pace, suitable for almost any level: Valencia is flat with bike lanes along most of the route.

## Who it's for

- If you arrive with little time and want to get oriented fast.
- If you'd rather roll than walk.
- If you want the big picture before deciding what to see at leisure.
