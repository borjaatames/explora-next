## What you will see

- A **flamenco show** in a tablao with its own restaurant.
- Live **singing, guitar and dance** after dinner.
- With the VIP option, a **table by the stage**.

## How it works

You book the option you prefer: show only with a drink, Premium menu or VIP menu. If dinner is included, you arrive when the restaurant opens, eat at a relaxed pace and the show follows, at 10:30 pm on weekdays or 8:30 pm on Sundays.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** wanting a special night with dinner included.
- Groups who would rather **not move** between restaurant and show.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Early sleepers: on weekdays the show starts at **10:30 pm**.
- Anyone after a short, cheap **flamenco taster**.
