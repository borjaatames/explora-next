## How the tour works

You start at the **Serranos Towers**, the medieval city gate, with entry included. In the old town you look for the quirky **Casa de los Gatos** and stop for **horchata** for the adults and **hot chocolate with churros** for the kids.

Then a **taxi** takes you to the **City of Arts and Sciences** to visit the **Science Museum**, with interactive exhibits designed for the whole family. Along the way there are **games** so the children learn local traditions while they play.

## Who it's for

- Families with children who want to see Valencia without the kids getting bored.
- Anyone wanting the old town and the City of Arts and Sciences in one morning.
