## What you will see

- **Olhöps**, a pioneering Valencia craft beer bar.
- Four styles of local and Spanish **craft beer**.
- Tapas made with **Ruzafa Market** produce.

## How it works

You arrive at Olhöps, take your seat at the tasting table and the expert serves the four beers one by one, each with its tapa, explaining styles, ingredients and brewing.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Beer lovers**.
- Anyone wanting a **local** experience in Ruzafa.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't drink alcohol**.
