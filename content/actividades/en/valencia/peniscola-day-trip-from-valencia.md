## What you'll see

A day in **Peñíscola** (Castellón), the walled town that juts out over the Mediterranean on a rock. You'll stroll its old town of whitewashed streets and walls and see the **Pope Luna Castle**, one of the best known on the coast, with time for the beach too.

## How it works

A **full-day** trip from Valencia, with round-trip transport included. Castle entry is not included. Booking and payment happen right here; free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who it's for

- If you like charming towns and history.
- If you want to combine a stroll, a castle and a swim in the sea.
- If you'd rather travel from Valencia without a car.
