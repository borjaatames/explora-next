## What you'll see

Valencia's most photographed complex from outside: the eye-shaped **Hemisfèric**, the whale-skeleton **Science Museum**, Félix Candela's **Oceanogràfic** and the **Palau de les Arts**.

## How it works

Meet at Hotel Barceló Valencia, next to the complex. Price **per group**: €240 for 1 or 2 and €320 for 4.

## Who it's for

- Travellers interested in contemporary architecture.
- Those who want context before visiting the Oceanogràfic or Museum.
