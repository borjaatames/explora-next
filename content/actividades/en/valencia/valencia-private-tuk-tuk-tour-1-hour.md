## What you will see

- The **Serranos Towers** and the old town.
- The **Marina**, **Malvarrosa** and **Las Arenas** beaches and the **Cabanyal** district.
- The **City of Arts and Sciences** and the **Colón Market**.

## How it works

You meet next to the Serranos Towers, board the electric tuk-tuk with your group and the driver-guide takes you for an hour around the old town, port, beach and City of Arts, stopping for photos. Commentary is in English or Spanish.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** and anyone who would rather not walk.
- Anyone **short on time** who wants an overview.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for an **in-depth visit** of the monuments.
