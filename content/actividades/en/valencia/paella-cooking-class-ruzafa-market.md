## What you'll do

A complete experience that goes from the market to the plate, with Valencian paella as the star.

- **Ruzafa Market:** walk the market with the chef and pick the fresh produce for the recipe.
- **The class:** learn the method of authentic Valencian paella —the sofrito, the stock, the rice timing and the coveted socarrat.
- **The table:** eat the paella you've cooked, with a sangria workshop included.

## How it works

The meeting point is in Ruzafa and is confirmed on booking. It's about **3.5 hours** in a group, with a chef. Vegetarian options and allergies are accommodated if noted when booking.

## Who it's for

- If you want a single experience that sums up Valencia: produce, farmland and rice.
- If you're travelling as a couple, family or group and want a hands-on plan.
- If you love to cook and want to take the technique home.
