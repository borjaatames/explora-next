## What you will see

- The **Oceanogràfic**: belugas, sharks, jellyfish and sea lions.
- The **Príncipe Felipe Science Museum** and its hands-on exhibits.
- **Calatrava's** architecture and the Hemisfèric.

## How it works

You book the day and the Oceanogràfic time slot. The same day you can enter the Science Museum whenever you like, with the ticket on your phone and no ticket-office queue.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** wanting to make a day of it.
- Anyone wanting the **essentials** of the City of Arts.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with only **half a day**: there's no time for both.
