## What you will see

- Europe's longest **underwater tunnel**, with sharks.
- The **beluga whales** and the Arctic area.
- Félix Candela's **water-lily-shaped building**.
- The Mediterranean, Red Sea and wetland areas with flamingos.

## How it works

You book a day and time slot, get the ticket on your phone and walk straight in. Explore both levels at your own pace: outdoor exhibits by the lake upstairs, the big aquariums downstairs.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**.
- Anyone visiting the **City of Arts and Sciences**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **flexibility**: the ticket can't be changed.
