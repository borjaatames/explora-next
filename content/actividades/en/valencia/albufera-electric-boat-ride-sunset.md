## What you will see

- The **canals** and the **Albufera lagoon** from the water.
- The natural park's **birdlife**, with no engine noise.
- On the latest departure, the **sunset** in the middle of the lake.

## How it works

You make your own way to the jetty, board the electric boat and the guide takes you through the canals out to the lagoon. Along the way you hear about fishing and rice growing here, the Moorish legacy and the wetland's wildlife. It lasts 45 minutes by day and about an hour at sunset.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone visiting the Albufera **independently** who wants to get on the water.
- **Families** and people with **reduced mobility**, as it is accessible.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who needs **transport from Valencia** included.
- Anyone looking for a **long excursion** with paella included.
