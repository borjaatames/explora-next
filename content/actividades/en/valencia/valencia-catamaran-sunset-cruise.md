## What you will see

- The **Marina de Valencia** and the Veles e Vents building.
- The **harbour mouth** and the Malvarrosa coastline.
- **Sunset** over the Mediterranean, if you pick that option.

## How it works

You board at the Marina, the catamaran sails along the coast for 50 minutes or 1.5 hours depending on the option, and drinks are served on board. You return to the same spot.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** after a sunset plan.
- Anyone wanting a **cheap, short** outing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **swim stop** or a small boat.
