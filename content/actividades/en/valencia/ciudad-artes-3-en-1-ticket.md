## What you'll see

The **Ciudad de las Artes y las Ciencias** (City of Arts and Sciences) is Valencia's flagship cultural complex — a series of Santiago Calatrava buildings set in a long reflecting pool. This 3-in-1 ticket covers the three main scientific attractions.

### Oceanogràfic
**Europe's largest aquarium**, with 45,000 animals across 500 species. Its signature features:
- **Shark tunnel:** walk through an underwater tunnel as sharks and rays pass overhead.
- **Beluga whale enclosure:** one of the few places in Europe to see beluga whales.
- **Arctic and Antarctic habitats:** penguins, walruses and polar species in climate-controlled environments.

### Hemisfèric
An **IMAX dome cinema** inside Calatrava's eye-shaped building. The dome screen creates an immersive visual experience during documentary screenings.

### Museu de les Ciències
An **interactive science museum** housed in Calatrava's 'skeleton' building — Spain's most impressive science museum for children and adults alike.

## Who it's for

- Families with children of any age.
- Architecture enthusiasts who want to see Calatrava's masterpiece from the inside.

## Who it's not for

- Visitors with only a few hours in Valencia: this complex needs a full day.
