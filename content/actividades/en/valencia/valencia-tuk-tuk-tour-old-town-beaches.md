## What you will see

- The **Torres de Serranos**, the cathedral and the Micalet.
- The **Mercado de Colón** and the Turia gardens.
- The **harbour**, Malvarrosa and Cabanyal.
- The **City of Arts and Sciences**.

## How it works

You leave the Torres de Serranos in an electric tuk tuk. The driver-guide covers the old town, follows the Turia down to the harbour and beaches, and returns via the City of Arts with photo stops.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**.
- Anyone **short on time** or who'd rather not walk.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **go inside monuments**.
