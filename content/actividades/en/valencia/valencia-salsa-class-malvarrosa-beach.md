## What you will see

- **Malvarrosa Beach** and the seafront promenade atmosphere.
- The **basic steps** of salsa and your first partner moves.

## How it works

You arrive at the spot by the beach, the instructor teaches the fundamental steps and body movement, and then you practise in pairs to music for an hour. A refreshment is included and the group stays at 15 people or fewer.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Beginners** who want to try salsa with no pressure.
- **Solo travellers** looking to meet people.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Advanced** dancers after technique.
- Anyone uncomfortable **dancing in pairs** with strangers.
