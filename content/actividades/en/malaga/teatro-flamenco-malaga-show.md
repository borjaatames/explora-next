## What you will see

**Teatro Flamenco Málaga** stages "**Esencia**" every day, a one-hour show with a cast that changes from one night to the next: **dancers, singers, guitar and percussion**. The venue is a theatre, with good visibility and carefully tuned sound.

## How to plan it

It is on Calle Lazcano, in the historic centre, 5 minutes from the **Picasso Museum** and Calle Larios. A well-rounded plan: an afternoon of museums, the show and a dinner of pescaíto (fried fish) in the centre.
