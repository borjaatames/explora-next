## What the day looks like

The driver picks you up at your hotel in Marbella and takes you to the start of the **Caminito del Rey**. There you walk the roughly 3-hour route along the walkways of the Gaitanes gorge, on the official guided group visit (the transfer is private; the guiding on the Caminito is not). Afterwards the car takes you back to Marbella. The provider contacts you after booking to confirm the time.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €570 for 1 or 2 people and €880 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Marbella and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Travellers staying in Marbella who do not want to drive to El Chorro.
- Groups of friends and families with children aged 8 and over.
