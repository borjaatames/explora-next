## What you'll see

The **Benalmádena cable car** climbs from Arroyo de la Miel to the top of **Mount Calamorro**, with views over the whole **Costa del Sol**. It is the only cable car on the coast.

At the top there are viewpoints and the start of **six hiking trails** among Mediterranean plants and wildlife. The **bird show** at the summit is a separate, paid activity.

## How it works

Cable car ticket. It closes in strong wind. Adults from 140 cm; children 100 to 140 cm; under 100 cm ride free. Non-refundable.
