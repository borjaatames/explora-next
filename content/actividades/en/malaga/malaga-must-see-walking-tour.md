## What you'll see

- The **Roman Theatre**, with the Alcazaba above it.
- **Santiago Church**, the oldest in town, where Picasso was baptised.
- **Calle Larios** and the streets of the historic centre.
- The **cathedral**, from outside.

## How it works

You meet the guide on the Alameda Principal and explore the centre on foot for about two hours. At the end, you get tips for the rest of your visit.

## Who I recommend it to

- First-time visitors, including **cruise passengers** with a few hours.
- Families or groups who prefer a **private tour**.
