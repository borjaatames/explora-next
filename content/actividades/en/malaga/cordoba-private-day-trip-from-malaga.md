## What the day is like

You're picked up from your accommodation in Málaga and driven in comfort to **Córdoba**, where your local guide meets you for a **2.5-hour private walking tour**. The centrepiece is the **Mosque-Cathedral**, entry included, followed by the **Jewish Quarter** with its narrow lanes and patios.

The thread running through it is the city's mix of **Islamic, Jewish and Christian** heritage. Afterwards you have **free time** to eat, shop or wander before heading back to Málaga.

## How it works

About **8.5 hours**, **private**. The price is **per group**: €750 for 1 or 2 people and €920 for 4. Lunch is not included.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to see the Mosque with a guide without sorting trains or tickets.
- Families and groups of 4, who share the cost.
- Those interested in the story of the three cultures.
