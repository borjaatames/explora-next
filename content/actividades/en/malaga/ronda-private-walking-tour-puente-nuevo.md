## The route

You start at Ronda's **bullring**, seen from outside. The guide tells the story of bullfighting in the town, its weight and its controversy, and of the local bullfighters.

Then you reach the **Puente Nuevo**, Ronda's symbol and one of Andalusia's most spectacular sights. You cross it on foot and look down into the **gorge**, with the mountains as a backdrop.

## How it works

About **2 hours on foot**, in private. The price is **per group**: €200 for 1 or 2 people and €260 for 4. Transport to Ronda is not included.

## Who it's for

- Visitors already in Ronda or arriving by car who want a guide for their group alone.
- Families, thanks to the per-group price.
- Anyone with half a day who wants the essentials explained well.
