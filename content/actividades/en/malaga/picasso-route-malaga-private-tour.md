## The route

It's a walk through **Picasso's roots** in Málaga. It starts at the **Church of Santiago**, where he was baptised, and continues to the **Birthplace Museum**, the building on Plaza de la Merced where he was born in 1881, built by master builder Diego Clavero.

It ends at the **Picasso Museum Málaga**, whose permanent collection traces his development as an artist. With the background on his family and early years, the museum reads very differently.

## How it works

**2.5 hours**, private and on foot. **Tickets to both museums** are included. The price is **per person, minimum 2** and maximum 9.

## Who it's for

- Visitors drawn to Málaga by Picasso.
- Families and groups of 3 or 4, who pay much less per person.
- Anyone who'd rather see a museum with a guide than an audio guide.
