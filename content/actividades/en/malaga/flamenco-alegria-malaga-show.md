## What you will see

**Alegría Flamenco y Gastronomía** combines a small stage with tables for enjoying wines and Andalusian tapas. The show opens with the whole cast on stage; then come the **guitarist's solo** and a **solo by each dancer**, each with a different palo (style) and emotion: joy, pain, melancholy.

## How to plan it

The venue is next to **La Malagueta**: you can stroll along Muelle Uno and the Paseo del Parque beforehand and then have dinner by the sea.
