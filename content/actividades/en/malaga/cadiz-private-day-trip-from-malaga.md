## What the day is like

You're picked up from your hotel in Málaga and driven privately to **Cádiz**, a city with **over 3,000 years of history**. There you take a **2-hour private guided walk** through the **old town**: its centuries-old quarters, its Phoenician origins and its maritime importance.

Then you have **free time**: the beach at **La Caleta** or lunch in a bar in the centre, where fried fish and shrimp fritters are the local classics. In the afternoon, back to Málaga.

## How it works

About **10 hours**, **private**. The price is **per group**: €731 for 1 or 2 people and €998 for 4. Lunch is not included.

## Who it's for

- Anyone with a single day who wants to see Cádiz without trains or changes.
- Families and groups of 4, who share the cost.
- Those who want a mix of history, sea and good food.
