## What the day is like

You're picked up from your accommodation in Málaga. In Granada the **guided visit to the Alhambra** begins, lasting **about 3 hours**: the **Nasrid Palaces**, the **Generalife** gardens and palace and the **Palace of Charles V**. It's the jewel of Nasrid architecture and the last trace of the kingdom of Granada.

After lunch, a guided walk through the **Albaicín**, a World Heritage Site: narrow lanes, fountains and squares that show how life in the city worked. Then back to Málaga, or the trip ends in Granada if you're staying there.

## How it works

About **9 hours**, all **private**. **Alhambra and Nasrid Palaces tickets** are included. The price is **per person, minimum 2** and maximum 7. Lunch is not included.

## Who it's for

- Travellers with one day for Granada who don't want to gamble on tickets.
- Families and small groups who'd rather have a guide to themselves.
- Anyone who wants to see the Albaicín too, not just the Alhambra.
