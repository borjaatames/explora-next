## What you'll see

A 90-minute guided tour of **Málaga's Renaissance cathedral**, which locals call **"La Manquita"** because its second tower was never finished. The guide explains the styles and building phases, with stories about the Catholic Monarchs and the Civil War.

Inside you'll see the **choir**, the **main chapel**, the Gothic altar in the **Santa Bárbara chapel** and *The Beheading of Saint Paul* by **Enrique Simonet**. The walk also takes in Plaza del Obispo and Calle Santa María.

## How it works

Skip-the-line entry included. Guide in Spanish or English. Be on time.
