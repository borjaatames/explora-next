## What the day looks like

The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Nerja and takes you along the coast to **Málaga**. There a local guide shows you on foot for 2 hours **Calle Larios**, the **Cathedral**, the **Alcazaba** from the outside and the legacy of **Picasso**. Then you have about 3 hours free for lunch and a stroll before heading back to Nerja.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €620 for 1 or 2 people and €760 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Nerja and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families staying in Nerja who want to see Málaga without a car.
- Groups of 4.
