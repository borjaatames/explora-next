## What the day looks like

In an hour you are in **Málaga**. Your guide shows you the centre on foot for 2 hours: **Calle Larios**, the area around the **Cathedral** and the **Alcazaba**, and the legacy of **Picasso**. Then you have about 4 hours free for lunch, a museum or a stroll by the port before heading back to Marbella.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €620 for 1 or 2 people and €760 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Marbella and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Groups and families who prefer door-to-door.
- Anyone who wants a guide plus time for museums or shopping.
