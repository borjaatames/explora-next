## What you will see

- The **walkways** clinging to the rock.
- The **Gaitanes gorge** and Guadalhorce river.
- The **suspension bridge** across the gorge.

## How it works

You leave central Málaga by coach, walk the 3-hour route with an official guide and return to Málaga in the afternoon.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting the Caminito **without a car**.
- **Families** with kids aged 8 and up.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with strong **vertigo**.
- Children under 8.
