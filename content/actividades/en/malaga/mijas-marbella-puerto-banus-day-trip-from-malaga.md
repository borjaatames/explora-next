## What you'll see

A full day along the **Costa del Sol** by coach from Málaga, with a host in Spanish and English:

1. **Mijas Pueblo** (about 75 minutes): whitewashed houses, viewpoints and time for breakfast.
2. **Puerto Banús** (about 30 minutes): the yacht harbour.
3. **Marbella** (about 2 hours): the old town and Plaza de los Naranjos, with time for lunch.
4. **Colomares Castle** (about 35 minutes), in Benalmádena, devoted to Columbus's voyage; entry included.

## How it works

Leaves from in front of Málaga City Hall. Lunch and hotel pickup are not included. The itinerary may change.
