## What you will see

- Málaga, **La Malagueta** and **Gibralfaro** from the sea.
- **Sunset** over the bay, if you choose that option.

## How it works

You pick a time, board at the port and sail the bay for one to two hours with music on board.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **families**.
- Anyone after a **cheap**, relaxed plan.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets seasick easily.
