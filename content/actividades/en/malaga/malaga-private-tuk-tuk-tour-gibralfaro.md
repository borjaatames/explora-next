## What you will see

A **1-hour electric tuk tuk ride** around the best of Málaga: the **old town**, the **port** and the climb to the **Gibralfaro viewpoint**, with the best views over the city and the bay. The driver is from Málaga and shares the history of each place, fun facts and tips for the rest of your day.

## How it works

The tuk tuk is **just for your group, 1 to 4 people**, and the price is **per vehicle: €89**. You are picked up from your **accommodation in the centre or the cruise terminal** and dropped back at the same point. Departures every half hour from **9:30 to 20:00**. There are photo stops and cold water on board.

## Who I recommend it to

- Cruise passengers with a few hours in Málaga.
- Families and groups of up to 4 who do not want to walk up to Gibralfaro.
- Anyone who wants a first look at the city before going back to their favourite spots.
