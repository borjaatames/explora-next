## What the day looks like

The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Nerja and you head up towards Sierra Nevada. The first stop is **Pampaneira**, with its flower-filled streets and craft shops. Then in **Bubión** you go inside a **traditional Alpujarra house** to see how mountain life worked. The day ends in **Capileira**, the highest village in the Poqueira gorge, with huge views and a good spot for lunch before heading back to Nerja.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €700 for 1 or 2 people and €920 for 4. The guide is with you all day. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to see mountain Andalusia from the coast.
- Families and groups of 4 who would rather not drive winding roads.
