## What the day is like

You're picked up from your accommodation in Málaga. The first stop is a **traditional olive oil mill** in the Antequera area, one of Andalusia's key extra virgin olive oil regions. You see how the oil is made and finish with a **tasting of its oils**.

Then on to **Antequera**, a World Heritage city. The visit takes in the **Collegiate Church** and the **alcazaba**, with views of the **Peña de los Enamorados**. In the afternoon, back to your hotel in Málaga.

## How it works

About **7 hours** with a **private transfer**. The mill visit is private. The price is **per person, minimum 2** and maximum 7. Lunch is not included.

## Who it's for

- Anyone interested in olive oil who wants to see it at source.
- Those after a quiet inland trip from Málaga.
- Groups of 3 or 4: the price per person drops a lot.
