## What you'll see

A half-day trip from Málaga combining two World Heritage sites:

- **Antequera dolmens**: a 90-minute guided visit to **Menga**, **Viera** and the **El Romeral** tholos, megalithic tombs about 5,000 years old.
- **El Torcal**: 90 minutes among karst rocks and fossils, with a guided walk and free time.

## How it works

Out by van from Málaga with a guide (Spanish, English or French), in a group of 8 at most. Afterwards you have free time in Antequera and return to Málaga by **scheduled bus**; the guide gives you the card. Check the return timetable before booking.
