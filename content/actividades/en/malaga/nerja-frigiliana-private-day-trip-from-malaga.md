## What the day is like

You're picked up in Málaga and head along the coast with your **driver-guide**, who fills you in on local traditions on the way. The first stop is the **Nerja Caves**, with their huge rock formations: the visit lasts about **45 minutes**.

Then the **Balcón de Europa**, Nerja's lookout over the Mediterranean. The day ends in **Frigiliana**, a white village of narrow lanes, with a **guided tour** of its history.

## How it works

About **6.5 hours**, **private**. **Cave entry** is included. The price is **per group**: €700 for 1 or 2 people and €880 for 4.

## Who it's for

- Families who want a day of coast and village without driving.
- Groups of 4, who share the cost better.
- Anyone who'd rather not battle for parking in Frigiliana.
