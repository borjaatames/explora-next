## What you'll see

**Bioparc Fuengirola** recreates four habitats without visible barriers: **Equatorial Africa**, **Madagascar**, **South-East Asia** and the **Indo-Pacific**, with over **200 species**. In Madagascar the **lemurs** roam freely around you.

Through the day there are **animal feedings** with keeper talks and bird displays. The park takes part in over 40 breeding programmes for endangered species.

## How it works

One-day ticket. Once you leave you cannot re-enter. Non-refundable.
