## What the day is like

Your driver picks you up from your accommodation in Málaga and about two and a half hours later you're in **Seville**. There, a **2-hour private guided walk** with a local guide covers the must-sees: the old town, the **Giralda** and the **Cathedral**, and the lanes of the **Santa Cruz quarter**, the old Jewish quarter.

Then you have **free time** for lunch, **Plaza de España** or the **Alcázar**, or a stroll by the Guadalquivir. In the afternoon, back to Málaga.

## How it works

It's **private**, priced **per person**: €250, from 1 person. Monument tickets and lunch are not included.

## Who it's for

- Solo travellers and couples who want to see Seville privately.
- Anyone who'd rather go door to door by car than by train and bus.
- Those who want a short guided walk and a free afternoon.
