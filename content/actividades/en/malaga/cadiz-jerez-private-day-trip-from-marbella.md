## What the day looks like

You are picked up at your accommodation in Marbella and the first stop is **Jerez de la Frontera**, where you visit a **bodega** and taste sherry wines (small group, not private). Then you continue to **Cádiz**: your guide takes you on a 2-hour walk through the old town, ending at the **food market**. There is time for lunch before heading back.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €750 for 1 or 2 people and €1240 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Marbella and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone interested in sherry who also wants to see Cádiz.
- Groups of 4 staying on the Costa del Sol.
