## What you will see

- The **warm, hot and cold baths**.
- The **steam room**.
- The **relaxation area** with mint tea.

## How it works

You book a time, arrive with a swimsuit and have 90 minutes to move between the baths. With the massage option, it's given during the session.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples**.
- Anyone wanting to **unwind** after sightseeing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Pregnant women.
- Anyone wanting a modern spa circuit.
