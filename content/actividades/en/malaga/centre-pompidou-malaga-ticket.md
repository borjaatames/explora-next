## What you'll see

The **Centre Pompidou Málaga** opened in 2015 beneath **the Cube**, the coloured-glass structure on Muelle Uno. It is the only Pompidou outside France.

It shows about **70 works** from the 20th and 21st centuries from the Paris collection, rotated regularly: **Brâncuși**, **Giacometti**, **Francis Bacon** and **Picasso**, among others. There are also two or three **temporary exhibitions** at a time.

## How it works

Ticket with audio guide included. Bring ID and headphones. Non-refundable.
