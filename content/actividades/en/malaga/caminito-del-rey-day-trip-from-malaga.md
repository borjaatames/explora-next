## What you'll see

A day trip to one of the most spectacular gorges in Spain.

- **The walkway:** the path hung on the wall of the Gaitanes gorge, high above the river.
- **The setting:** the natural area of El Chorro, reservoirs and vertical climbing walls.
- **No logistics:** round-trip transport, entry and guide included.

## How it works

Pickup is in Málaga and is confirmed on booking. The walkway route is **one-way** (about 7.7 km in total including the access paths). Helmet compulsory, included. Usual minimum age: 8.

## Who it's for

- If you love nature and outdoor experiences.
- If you want to see the Caminito without fighting for tickets (they sell out).
- If you have a free day from Málaga or the Costa del Sol.
