## What the day is like

You're picked up in Málaga and an hour later you reach **El Torcal de Antequera**, one of Europe's most important geological and palaeontological sites and a World Heritage Site. There you walk a **circular route of about 5 km** with a **qualified mountain guide**, among limestone formations and fossils millions of years old.

Then **Antequera**, one of the oldest towns in the West: a walk to see the **alcazaba** and the **Collegiate Church** from outside. Then back to Málaga.

## How it works

About **7 hours**, all **private** with transfer. The price is **per person, minimum 2** and maximum 7. It runs Tuesday to Saturday. Lunch is not included.

## Who it's for

- Walkers who want nature close to Málaga.
- Families with fit children aged 8 and over.
- Anyone heading to Seville, Córdoba or Granada who wants to make use of the journey.
