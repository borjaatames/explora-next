## What you'll see

An introduction to Málaga with an official guide, from the old town to the fortress.

- **Old town:** the Cathedral, the Roman Theatre and the squares of the historic centre.
- **Alcazaba:** the best-preserved Moorish palace-fortress in Spain, terraced above the city.
- **The views:** the lookout over the port, the bullring and the Mediterranean.

## Who it's for

- If you arrive in Málaga and want to get oriented on day one.
- If you'd rather understand the city with a guide before exploring on your own.
- If you want a complete visit at a good price.
