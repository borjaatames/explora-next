## What you will see

- The **Roman Theatre**.
- The **Alcazaba**, with its courtyards and views.
- The **Cathedral**, "La Manquita", with its unfinished tower.

## How it works

You meet the guide by the Roman Theatre and visit the three monuments with priority access over about 3 hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors with **only one day** in Málaga.
- Anyone who wants to understand the city's history.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with reduced mobility.
