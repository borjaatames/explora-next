## What you'll see

A three-hour cooking class in Málaga. On the morning class (Monday to Saturday) you start with a walk through **Atarazanas market**, the big central market, then head to the **Kulinarea** kitchen in the Soho district.

With a chef you cook a seasonal tapas menu; the sample includes **fried noodles, Spanish omelette, sirloin in Pedro Ximénez sauce and churros**. Then you eat it with sangria and local wine, beer or a soft drink.

## How it works

Class in English. You take home the recipes and an apron. Not suitable for vegans. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
