## What the day looks like

The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Nerja and takes you straight to **Granada**. A local guide then takes you on a private, skip-the-line visit to the **Alhambra**: the **Nasrid Palaces**, the **Generalife** gardens and the **Alcazaba** fortress. Then you have about 3 hours free to walk up to the Albaicín, have tapas or stroll in the centre before heading back to Nerja.

## How it works

It is **private**. The **Alhambra tickets** are included. Lunch is not. Bring each traveller’s **original passport or ID card**.

## Who I recommend it to

- Travellers staying in Nerja or the Axarquía who want the Alhambra without worrying about tickets or transport.
- Families who prefer a guide just for them.
