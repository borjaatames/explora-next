## What you'll see

The **1-hour** option covers the main viewpoints and monuments: seafront, old town and views. The **2-hour** option adds **Pedregalejo**, **Baños del Carmen**, **Plaza de la Merced** and **Atarazanas market**.

## How it works

You meet the driver by the tuk tuk on Paseo de la Farola. You sit back while they explain each stop, and the route adapts to what interests you most.

## Who I recommend it to

- Travellers **short on time** or who don't want to walk much.
- **Families** with children aged 4 and up.
