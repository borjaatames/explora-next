## What you will see

- Ronda's **Puente Nuevo** and **gorge**.
- **Ronda**'s old town.
- **Setenil de las Bodegas**, with houses under the rock.

## How it works

You leave Málaga in the morning, spend about 4 hours in Ronda, self-guided or guided, then an hour in Setenil before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **white villages** without driving.
- Anyone after a **cheap** day trip.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting more time in Setenil.
