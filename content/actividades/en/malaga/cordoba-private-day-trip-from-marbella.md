## What the day looks like

You are picked up at your accommodation in Marbella and reach **Córdoba** in about two and a half hours. Your guide meets you there for a two-and-a-half-hour walking tour: the **Mosque-Cathedral**, with its forest of red and white arches, and the **Jewish Quarter**, with its narrow lanes and patios. Then you have about two and a half hours free for lunch and a stroll before heading back to Marbella.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €760 for 1 or 2 people and €1080 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Marbella and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Travellers staying on the Costa del Sol who want to see the Mosque in a day.
- Families and groups of 4, who share the cost of the car.
