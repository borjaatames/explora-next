## The route

The tour starts at **Atarazanas market**, where you try typical produce from the province: olives dressed Málaga-style, craft wine and handmade Ronda-style spreads. Inside the market there's **fried fish** or a tasting of freshly carved **Iberian ham**.

Next, a stop at an **old grocery** from the last century, now a tavern and antiques shop. The thread running through it all is Málaga's cooking: the sea side, with its fish, and the mountain side, with Iberian pork and olive oil.

## How it works

About **3 hours on foot**, in private. It includes **3 tapas and 3 drinks per person**. The price is **per person, minimum 2** and maximum 10.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to get to know Málaga's food with someone to explain it.
- Groups of 4 or more: the price per person drops a lot.
- Travellers with allergies or intolerances who want a tour that adapts.
