## The route

You start in **Plaza de los Naranjos**, the heart of Marbella's **old town**, and wander its whitewashed lanes, flower-filled squares, craft shops and tapas bars. Along the way you'll pass remains of the old **Moorish walls** and centuries of history tucked into every corner.

The second half heads down to the sea: **Alameda Park**, the **Dalí sculptures** on **Avenida del Mar** and the finish by the Mediterranean.

## How it works

About **2 hours on foot**, in private. The price is **per group**: €240 for 1 or 2 people and €264 for 4. Getting to Marbella is up to you.

## Who it's for

- Costa del Sol visitors who want to see Marbella's historic side.
- Families and groups, since the price hardly rises with more people.
- Cruise passengers or anyone passing through with a couple of hours.
