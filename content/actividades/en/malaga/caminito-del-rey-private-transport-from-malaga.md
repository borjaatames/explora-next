## What the day is like

You're picked up from your hotel in Málaga and **a little over an hour** later you're in **El Chorro**, where it all begins. The **Caminito del Rey** is a path of **about 3 km** pinned to the walls of the **Gaitanes gorge**, with walkways barely a metre wide and stretches **more than 100 metres above the river**.

The route is flat and technically easy: what makes it special are the walkways, the bridges over the reservoir and the lookouts. Afterwards the driver takes you back to Málaga.

## How it works

**Transport is private**; **entry and the guide** on the Caminito are a **group visit**. The price is **per group**: €610 for 1 or 2 people and €960 for 4. Bring your passport or ID.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to do the Caminito without worrying about transport or tickets.
- Families and groups of 3 or 4, who share the cost of the car.
- Not for anyone with severe vertigo.
