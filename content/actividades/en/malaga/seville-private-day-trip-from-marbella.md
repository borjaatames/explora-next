## What the day looks like

You are picked up at your accommodation in Marbella and cross the Andalusian countryside to **Seville**. There you take a 2-hour private walking tour with a guide past the best-known sights from the outside: the **Cathedral** and the **Giralda**, the **Santa Cruz quarter** and its squares. Then you have free time for tapas or to visit the Alcázar or Plaza de España on your own before heading back to Marbella.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €720 for 1 or 2 people and €940 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Marbella and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Travellers staying on the Costa del Sol who want to see Seville in a day.
- Groups of 4 who prefer not to rely on buses.
