## What the day is like

You're picked up from your hotel in Málaga and in **under an hour and a half** you're in **Ronda**, one of Andalusia's most dramatic towns: the **Puente Nuevo** over the **gorge**, the town perched on the cliff and the bullring of the birthplace of bullfighting.

## The three options

- **Ronda on your own:** you're driven there and back, and the operator gives you tips on what to see and where to eat.
- **With a private guided tour:** a guide just for you for **2 hours**.
- **Ronda and Setenil:** adds **Setenil de las Bodegas**, with its houses tucked under the rock.

The price is **per group**: for 1 or 2 people, €580, €720 or €800 depending on the option.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to see Ronda without relying on the bus.
- Families and groups of 4, who share the cost of the car.
- Those who want to fit Setenil into the same day.
