## What the day is like

You're picked up at your accommodation in Málaga and in just under an hour you reach the **Caminito del Rey** access point. From there, the guide walks the route **with your group only**: about **8 km and 3 hours** along walkways and bridges hanging more than 100 metres above the river in the Gaitanes Gorge. Afterwards the car takes you back to Málaga (lunch is not included).

## Who I recommend it to

- Groups of 4 to 7: per person it costs less than many shared-guide trips.
- Anyone who wants to set their own pace and ask the guide questions without sharing.
- If there are 2 of you, see the other private Caminito trip: the transfer is private and the visit on the trail is in a group, and it costs less.
