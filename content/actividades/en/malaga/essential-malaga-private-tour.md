## The route

You start in **Plaza de la Constitución** and walk down **Calle Larios** to **Málaga Cathedral**, seen from outside and nicknamed «the one-armed lady» for its unfinished tower. Then along **Calle San Agustín**, home to the **Picasso Museum**, and past **El Pimpi**, the city's best-known bodega.

The last stretch is old Málaga: the **Roman Theatre** and the **Alcazaba**, with tickets included, plus the **Málaga Museum**.

## How it works

You choose the length when booking: **2 or 3 hours**. It's **private**, just for your party, and priced **per group**: €200 for 1 or 2 people on the 2-hour option and €240 on the 3-hour one. The 2-hour option and the 3-hour option with the Cathedral are wheelchair accessible; the 3-hour option with the Alcazaba is not.

## Who it's for

- New arrivals who want to get the measure of Málaga on day one.
- Couples and families who'd rather have a guide to themselves.
- Cruise passengers with a few hours ashore (note: there's no port pickup).
