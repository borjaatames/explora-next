## What the experience is like

It starts in **El Pedregalejo**, Málaga's fishing quarter. You walk the area and step into a **craft boatyard** where **jábegas**, the traditional boats of the Málaga coast, are built and restored. This is the part that explains the city's bond with the sea: boats, nets and trade.

Then comes the **espeto class**: how the sardines are threaded onto the cane and grilled by the fire, as they have been on this coast for centuries. You finish with the **tasting and a drink**, by the beach.

## How it works

It's a **private experience** of about 3 hours, starting at 11:00. The price is **per person, minimum 2** and maximum 15, and it drops the more of you there are. Getting to El Pedregalejo is up to you.

## Who it's for

- Couples and families who want something other than a monuments tour.
- Anyone into local food who wants to learn to make espetos.
- Groups of 3 or 4: the price per person drops a lot.
