## What you will see

- The **Nerja Cave** and its stalactite halls.
- The **Balcón de Europa** and central Nerja.
- The white streets of **Frigiliana**.

## How it works

You leave Málaga in the morning with a guide, visit the cave with an audio guide, have free time in Nerja for lunch and finish with a walk round Frigiliana before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families**: kids love the cave.
- Anyone wanting to see the **eastern coast** without a car.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
