## What the day looks like

You are picked up at your accommodation in Marbella and drive up through the mountains to **Ronda**. With your guide you walk for 2 hours past the highlights, from the outside: the **Puente Nuevo** over the gorge, the **bullring** and the old town. Then you have 2 hours free for lunch or the viewpoints before heading back.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €440 for 1 or 2 people and €640 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Marbella and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants Andalusia’s most dramatic white town without driving the mountain road.
- Families and groups of 4.
