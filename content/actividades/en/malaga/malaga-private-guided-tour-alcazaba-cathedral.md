## What the tour looks like

You meet your guide next to the Cathedral and start with the **Alcazaba**, the 11th-century Moorish fortress: from its walls you see the port and the Mediterranean. Then you go inside the **Cathedral**, «La Manquita» (the one-armed lady) because of its unfinished tower, and finish with a walk through the **historic centre**, from the city’s Phoenician roots to today’s Málaga.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €332 for 2 people and €384 for 4. Tickets are included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone with a morning in Málaga who wants to understand the city.
- Cruise passengers: the meeting point is a short walk from the port.
