## What you will see

- The **Rock of Gibraltar** and its views over the Strait.
- The **macaques** of the nature reserve.
- **Main Street** and central Gibraltar.

## How it works

You leave Málaga by coach in the morning, cross the border on foot and get almost 6 free hours. To go up the Rock, book the tour there. You head back to Málaga in the afternoon.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **Gibraltar without driving**.
- Anyone who prefers **exploring on their own** once there.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting an all-inclusive guided day.
