## What you will see

- The **Mosque-Cathedral** and its forest of arches.
- The **Jewish Quarter** and the **Synagogue**.
- The **Roman Bridge** over the Guadalquivir.

## How it works

You leave Málaga in the morning, take the guided tour of the Mosque and Jewish Quarter, have free time for lunch and head back in the afternoon.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **Córdoba in a day** without driving.
- Anyone after a **very low price** with tickets included.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see Medina Azahara or the patios inside.
