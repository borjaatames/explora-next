## What you'll see

A day trip from **Málaga port** to **Granada**, designed for cruise passengers. With an expert guide you visit the **Alhambra**: the **Nasrid Palaces**, one of the finest examples of Islamic architecture in the world, and the **Generalife** gardens. If the ship's schedule allows, there's free time in Granada city centre at the end.

## How it works

They wait for you **as you leave the ship** and you travel to Granada in an air-conditioned vehicle; the drive takes about 1 h 30 min each way. At the Alhambra groups are **18 people maximum** per guide, who speaks **English**. The operator adapts the duration to the docking time and includes a **back-to-ship guarantee**.

**Important: the Alhambra ticket isn't included.** As soon as you book, send the operator each person's full name, passport number and date of birth. It costs €23 and covers the Alhambra, Nasrid Palaces and Generalife, but depends on tickets being left.

## Who I recommend it for

- Cruise passengers calling at Málaga who want to see the Alhambra.
- Anyone who prefers an operator that takes responsibility for getting them back to the ship.
- Travellers booking early who can sort the ticket in advance.
