## What you'll see

A three-and-a-half-hour walk through central Málaga with a food guide and four stops:

- A **traditional bodega** with local wines, including a Málaga **Pedro Ximénez**.
- A **family grocery** where Iberian ham is carved to order.
- A bar for **cured meats, cheese and vermouth**.
- Well-known Málaga bars, with the story of the city's cooking from traditional to modern.

In all, **8 tapas and 4 wines**, and at each stop you can swap the wine for beer or a soft drink.

## How it works

Meet at Calle Larios 18, in front of Women'Secret. The tour is **in English only**. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
