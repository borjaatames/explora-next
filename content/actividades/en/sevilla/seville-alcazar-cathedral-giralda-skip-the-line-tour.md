## What you will see

### Seville Cathedral and the Giralda
The largest Gothic cathedral in the world: soaring naves, 16th-century stained glass, the huge main altarpiece and the **tomb of Christopher Columbus**. Then up the **Giralda**, the former Almohad minaret, for views across the old town.

### Real Alcázar
Europe's oldest royal palace still in use: the **Courtyard of the Maidens**, the Mudéjar halls of Peter I, the Ambassadors' Hall and the gardens with orange trees and fountains.

## Tip

Arrive 15 minutes early at the Fuente de Indias for check-in, and dress for the Cathedral's dress code.
