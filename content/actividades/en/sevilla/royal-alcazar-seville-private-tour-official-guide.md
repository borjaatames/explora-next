## What the tour is like

You meet your guide in **Plaza del Triunfo**, next to the Alcázar entrance, and go in with your tickets already bought. For about **2 hours** you explore the palace **with your group only**: its Islamic origins, the **Palace of Pedro I** and the **Patio de las Doncellas**, the halls of the Catholic Monarchs and Charles V, and the **gardens**, with stories and legends you won't get from the audio guide.

## How it works

It's **private** and priced **per group**, tickets included: €191 for 2 people and €222 for 4. When booking you're asked for each person's name and ID because Alcázar tickets are issued in each visitor's name.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to understand the Alcázar, not just walk through it.
- Families and groups of 3 or 4: per person it costs about the same as a group tour.
- If you can't find standalone tickets, this tour includes them.
