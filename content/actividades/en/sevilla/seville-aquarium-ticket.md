## What you will do

- A route that follows **Magellan's voyage** from the Guadalquivir to the Pacific.
- **40 tanks** with over 400 species.
- The **shark tank**, 9 metres deep.

## How it works

You buy a ticket for one day and show it at the entrance. The route is self-guided and takes about 90 minutes.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with kids aged 5 to 12**.
- Anyone looking for an **indoor plan during the heat**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who has seen big aquariums like Valencia's Oceanogràfic: it will feel small.
