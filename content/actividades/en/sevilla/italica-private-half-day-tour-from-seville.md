## What the visit looks like

The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Seville and in about 20 minutes you reach **Itálica**, the Roman city where the emperors Trajan and Hadrian were born. For 2 hours your guide shows you the **amphitheatre**, one of the largest in the Empire, the houses’ **mosaics** and the streets of the new town. Then you are driven back to Seville.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €339 for 1 or 2 people and €481 for 4. Admission is included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone interested in Roman history with a free morning in Seville.
- Families who do not want to arrange transport.
