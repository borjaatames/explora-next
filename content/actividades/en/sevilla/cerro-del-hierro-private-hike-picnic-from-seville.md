## How the day works

You're picked up from your accommodation in Seville and, after a short coffee stop, reach the top of **Cerro del Hierro** in the **Sierra Norte**. Here the guide explains the mining landscape: an almost 'Martian' scene where nature has taken over the old mines worked by the English from the 19th century.

You then follow the **Sierra Norte Vía Verde**, an old railway line, through **dehesa** and **Mediterranean woodland** to the **source of the Huéznar river**. There's a picnic, a visit to the **Huéznar waterfalls** and, according to the operator, a lunch of local mountain food before heading back to Seville.

## How it works

- A route of about **7-8 km, easy and downhill**.
- **Private transport**, **picnic** and **water** included.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 6.

## Who it's for

- Families and groups wanting a day in nature near Seville.
- Walkers who prefer gentle terrain.
- Photographers: the Cerro del Hierro rock formations are very photogenic.
