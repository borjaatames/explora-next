## What you'll see

A **PREMIUM small-group walking tour (max 10 people) of 2 hours** through Seville's most iconic places. 1,475 reviews at 4.9/5 — the highest rating of Seville's general walking tours.

- **Seville Cathedral (exterior):** the largest Gothic cathedral in the world, viewed from Plaza del Triunfo, Avenida de la Constitución and Pla de la Catedral. Context on Seville's 3 UNESCO sites.
- **Giralda (exterior):** 12th-century 105 m minaret tower, symbol of Seville. Viewed from several angles.
- **Royal Alcázar (exterior):** view of exterior walls and the famous Patio de Banderas. Context of the oldest royal Mudéjar palace in Europe still in use.
- **Barrio Santa Cruz:** Seville's former Jewish quarter, narrow whitewashed alleys, patios with plants, hidden squares (Plaza Santa Cruz, Plaza Doña Elvira, Plaza Refinadores with the Don Juan Tenorio statue, Calle del Agua).
- **Plaza de España:** semicircular 1928 complex, tiled benches of the 48 provinces, navigable canal.
