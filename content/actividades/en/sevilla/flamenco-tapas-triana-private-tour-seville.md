## How the evening works

You start in **Triana**, one of Seville's oldest neighbourhoods and the birthplace of many flamenco artists, with a **professional flamenco show**. Then your **local food guide** takes you to **2 or 3 bars** for tapas and wine, explaining the origins and evolution of the tradition. You can let them steer you towards local wines and spirits.

## What's included

- **Flamenco show** in Triana.
- **Tapas** in 2 or 3 bars and **4 drinks per person**.
- A local guide just for your party for **4 hours**.
- Departures at **18:30** or **19:00**.

## Who it's for

- Couples and groups wanting a full flamenco-and-tapas night with nothing to organise.
- Anyone who wants context: the guide explains what you eat and drink.
- Groups of 4: the per-group price works out at €135 each.
