## What you'll see

A **private 11-hour day trip** from Seville to Granada. With the guide you tour the **Alhambra**, including the **Nasrid Palaces**, the Palace of Charles V, the Generalife and its gardens, and then take a **guided walk through the Albaicín** up to the **San Nicolás viewpoint**.

## How it works

A driver **picks you up at your accommodation in Seville** in a private vehicle. The drive takes about 2.5 hours, with one stop. **Alhambra tickets** are included; lunch is not. When booking you must give each participant's passport details and bring the original on the day. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2**.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in Seville without a spare night to sleep in Granada.
- Families or small groups who want everything taken care of.
