## The route

A walk through Seville's old town with an **official guide** just for your party. From outside you see the **Cathedral**, Spain's largest, and the **Giralda**, the former Moorish minaret. You pass the **Royal Alcázar**, Spain's oldest palace still in use and a *Game of Thrones* location. Then **Plaza de España** and the **Santa Cruz quarter**, the old Jewish quarter, with its flower-filled lanes.

## Options

- **2 hours:** €164 in total for 1 or 2 people; €220 for 4.
- **3 hours:** €200 in total for 1 or 2 people; €280 for 4.

Tours run from **9:00 to 19:00**. You don't go inside the monuments or up the Giralda.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors who want to get their bearings on day one.
- Families and groups: the price is per group and pays off from 3 or 4.
- Anyone who'd rather have their own guide than a crowded free tour.
