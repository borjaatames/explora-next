## What you will do

- **Triana** and the **Torre del Oro**.
- **San Telmo Palace** and the outside of the **Alcázar**.
- **María Luisa Park** and **Plaza de España**.

## How it works

You pick up your bike at the shop in the Arenal and ride the route with a guide in a small group, stopping to explain each place.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families whose kids ride confidently**.
- Anyone who wants to see a lot quickly without tiring on foot.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Kids who can't yet ride well.
