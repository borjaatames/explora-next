## What you'll see

A **private 8-hour trip** from Seville to the heart of the **Aracena mountains** in Huelva. You visit a **family farm** where Iberian pigs are raised and a **curing house**, where the whole artisan ham-making process is explained and you do an **Iberian ham tasting**. Then there's a **guided walk around Aracena** and its castle, free time to buy local products and a stop in **Alájar**, with spectacular views.

## How it works

The driver-guide **picks you up at your accommodation in Seville**; give the exact address when booking, as some streets in the centre have restricted access. The **tasting** is included; lunch in Aracena isn't. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 8.

## Who I recommend it for

- Food lovers who want to really understand Iberian ham.
- Anyone looking for a day in the countryside and mountain villages off the usual circuits.
- Families: children love seeing the pigs on the farm.
