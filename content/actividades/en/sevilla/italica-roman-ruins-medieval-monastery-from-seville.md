## What you'll see

Half-day excursion (4 hours) to the **Roman ruins of Italica + Medieval Monastery of San Isidoro del Campo** from Seville. The first Roman city founded outside Italy (206 BC) and cradle of emperors Trajan and Hadrian.

- **Roman amphitheater for 25,000 spectators** (the third largest in the Roman Empire) — DRAGONPIT location in Game of Thrones season 7.
- **Noble houses with mosaics:** House of the Planetarium, House of the Birds, House of Neptune — spectacular mosaics preserved in situ.
- **Roman forum and theater** (partially preserved).
- **Roman thermal baths** and urban remains.
- **Monastery of San Isidoro del Campo (14th c.):** Mudéjar + Gothic + Baroque architecture. Main altarpiece by Martínez Montañés.
- **Official licensed guide** in Spanish, English, French or Italian.

## How it works

**Book your date and language** (min 4 people same language).

**Arrive 15 min early** at pickup (3 options in Seville centre, or hotel pickup in private version).

**Trip to Santiponce/Italica** by bus (~20 min, 9 km).

**Guided visit to Italica ruins** (~1.5-2h) — amphitheater + houses + forum + thermal baths.

**Visit to San Isidoro del Campo Monastery** (~30-45 min).

**Return to Seville** (~20 min). Total ~4 hours.

- **Game of Thrones fans** (Dragonpit scene at the amphitheater).
