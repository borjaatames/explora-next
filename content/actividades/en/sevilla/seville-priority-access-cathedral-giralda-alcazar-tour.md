## What you'll see

The **most booked tour in Seville** — 9,797 reviews at 4.8/5 — covers the city's 3 UNESCO monuments in one 2.5-3 hour guided tour with priority access at all 3 sites.

- **Seville Cathedral (45 min guided):** 42 m central nave, Pierre Dancart's main altarpiece (over 1,000 figures), 44 chapels with works by Goya/Murillo/Zurbarán, Christopher Columbus's tomb.
- **Giralda (25-30 min climb):** 35 ramps to the 105 m minaret with panoramic views of Seville. Islamic Almohad origin of the 12th century.
- **Patio de los Naranjos** (10 min): original ablutions courtyard with century-old orange trees.
- **Royal Alcázar (75-90 min guided):** Mudéjar Palace of Pedro I + Patio de las Doncellas + Royal Chambers + Gothic Palace + Alcázar Gardens.
- **Personal headsets** throughout the tour — hear the guide clearly even in vast patios.
- **Optional Crown Tours app** with additional content (maps, extra photos, fun facts).

## How it works

**Book your date, time and language** (English, Spanish, Italian, French or Portuguese). Verify that the option INCLUDES the Giralda climb (some options exclude it).

**Arrive 15-20 min early** at the **Inmaculada Concepción monument** in the centre of **Plaza del Triunfo**. Crown Tours staff wear **purple t-shirts** — easy to identify.

**Show your mobile voucher and passport/ID.** Staff does check-in and groups by language.

**Receive your personal headsets** and meet your official licensed guide in your language.

**Cathedral + Giralda tour** (75-90 min) with priority access via express security check. Tour through the nave, altarpiece, Columbus's tomb, chapels and Giralda climb.

**Walking transfer to Plaza del Triunfo** (5 min) and entry to the Royal Alcázar.

**Royal Alcázar tour** (75-90 min) through Mudéjar Palace, Patio de las Doncellas, Royal Chambers, Gothic Palace and Alcázar Gardens.

**The tour ends in the Alcázar gardens** — you can stay exploring if your original ticket allows re-entry or go directly to eat (Plaza del Triunfo has restaurants 3-5 min away).
