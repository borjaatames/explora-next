## What you'll see

A **premium 2-hour small-group tour (max 10 people) of the Royal Alcázar of Seville** with licensed local guide from Seville Unique Experiences. English only. The intimate format allows deeper conversation about the palace's history and architecture than standard group tours.

- **Mudéjar Palace of Pedro I (14th century)** — the architectural jewel of the Alcázar. Detailed exploration of patios, plasterwork, alfarjes (coffered ceilings) and alicatados (tile mosaics).
- **Justice Room and Palace of Plaster** — key spaces of the early Mudéjar palace with rich decoration.
- **House of Trade and Admiral's Room** — related to Seville's role in the discovery of the Americas (Seville was the exclusive Atlantic port for 200 years).
- **Gothic Palace** — added by Alfonso X after the Reconquest. Halls with Flemish tapestries and Gothic vaults.
- **Baths of María Padilla** — the famous underground baths, one of Game of Thrones' Dorne filming locations.
- **Alcázar Gardens** — 7 hectares of Mudéjar, Renaissance and Romantic gardens. After the guided portion, you have free time to explore at your own pace.
