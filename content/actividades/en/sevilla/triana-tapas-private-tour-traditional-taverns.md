## The route

You start at **Triana Bridge** and cross into the neighbourhood along **Calle Betis**, with its seafaring past. First stop is **Bar Santa Ana**, one of Triana's most traditional taverns. Then **Triana Market**, with its tile-decorated stalls, and the old **Inquisition alley**, as Triana was once the Inquisition's headquarters. You finish at **Las Golondrinas**, a 19th-century tavern, with one of its signature dishes.

## What's included

- **4 tapas and 4 drinks per person** across **4 bars**.
- A private guide for **3 hours**.
- Two slots: **13:00** or **19:30**. Say which when booking.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 6.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to eat where locals do and understand Andalusian food.
- Groups of 4 to 6: the price per person halves.
- Not for big appetites: it's 4 tapas, not a full dinner.
