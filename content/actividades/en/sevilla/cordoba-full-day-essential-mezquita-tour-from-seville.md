A **10-hour guided day trip to Córdoba** — the former capital of the Muslim Caliphate of the 10th c. — from Seville. Includes transport, official guide, Mezquita-Cathedral entry, breakfast and lunch.

- **Mezquita-Cathedral (UNESCO):** 1h30min guided visit to Córdoba's most important monument — 856 marble columns forming bi-color red-white arches, 16th-c. Renaissance chapel integrated in the 8th-c. Islamic mosque.
- **Jewish Quarter:** 14th-c. Synagogue (one of the 3 remaining in Spain), Calle de las Flores (most photographed alley), Maimonides statue, Callejón del Pañuelo.
- **Alcázar of the Christian Kings:** exterior view + Mudéjar gardens.
- **Traditional Cordoban meal** included at local restaurant.
- **Free time** to explore at your pace (Mercado Victoria, souvenir shopping, coffee).
- **Seville ↔ Córdoba transport** in air-conditioned tourist bus.
