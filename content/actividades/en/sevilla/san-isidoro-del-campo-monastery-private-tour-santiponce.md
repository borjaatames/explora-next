## What you'll see

The **Monastery of San Isidoro del Campo**, of the **Order of St Jerome**, in **Santiponce**. The translation of the Bible into Spanish, the so-called **Bear Bible**, began here, and it was the **first monument in Seville declared a Historic-Artistic Site of National Interest**.

You explore its rooms, with **15th-century murals**, works by **Martínez Montañés** and unique architecture that is key to understanding Seville's history.

## How it works

- A **private** 2-hour visit at **10:00**, **tickets included**.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 10.
- Transport to Santiponce isn't included.

## Who it's for

- Anyone visiting Itálica the same day: the monastery is right next door.
- Art and religious-history enthusiasts.
- Visitors looking for a monument off the usual circuit.
