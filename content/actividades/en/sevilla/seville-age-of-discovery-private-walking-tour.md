## The route

You start by the **Cathedral**, with an introduction to Seville as an **inland port** and **capital of trade with the Americas**. In **El Arenal** you learn about the logistics of exploration: the **Casa de la Contratación**, the **Royal Shipyards**, the science of navigation and the control of routes and goods. You pass the **old port** and its defences.

Then you cross the river to **Triana**, the sailors' quarter, with the **Brotherhood of Mariners** and **Santa Ana church**, to learn about life before the transatlantic voyages and the legacy of **Magellan**.

## How it works

- A **private** 2-hour walking tour with an **official guide**.
- **Priced per group**: €200 for 2 people; €280 for 4.
- Tours every half hour from **9:00 to 18:00**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who's done a general Seville tour and wants a specific theme.
- Naval history and Age of Discovery enthusiasts.
- Families with curious teenagers.
