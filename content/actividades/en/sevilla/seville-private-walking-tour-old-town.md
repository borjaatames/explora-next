## What the tour looks like

You meet your guide at **Plaza de San Francisco**, or wherever you choose, and for two and a half hours you walk through Seville’s old town, one of the largest in Europe. You see the **Cathedral** and the **Royal Alcázar** from outside, plus hidden corners and legends, while the guide explains how the city went from Phoenician and Roman to Moorish and Christian, with figures such as Ferdinand III.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €160 for 1 to 4 people. Tickets are not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone arriving in Seville who wants to get their bearings on day one.
- Families and groups of up to 4: it works out at €40 each.
