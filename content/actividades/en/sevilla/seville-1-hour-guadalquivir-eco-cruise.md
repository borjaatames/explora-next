## What you'll see

A **1-hour panoramic cruise** on an eco-sustainable boat on the Guadalquivir. 11,157 reviews at 4.2/5 — Seville's MOST BOOKED cruise by volume (6× more than the second).

- **Torre del Oro:** 13th-century Almohad defensive tower next to the pier.
- **Plaza de Toros de la Maestranza:** view from the river of Spain's oldest bullring facade.
- **Triana (Calle Betis):** the flamenco neighborhood on the other side of the river — painted facades, ceramicists.
- **Bridges:** Triana, San Telmo, Alamillo (Calatrava).
- **Isla de la Cartuja:** Expo 92 area (Pavilion of Navigation).
- **Live guide** in Spanish, English or French depending on session.

## How it works

**Book your date, session and language** (ES, EN or FR). Private group option available.

**Arrive 15-20 min early** at the pier (next to Torre del Oro). Verify exact address in voucher.

**Show your mobile voucher.** Board via the gangway.

**Enjoy the 1-hour cruise** with live guide. Drinks available on board at reasonable price (~€3-4) — not included in price.

**Disembark back at the pier.** Combine with Torre del Oro, walk along Calle Betis or dinner at Barrio El Arenal.
