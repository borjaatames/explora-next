## The route

A guided walk through Seville's **historic green spaces**, with Arab and French influences and **water** at their heart from the 12th to the 20th century. You explore **Plaza de América**, the 'pigeon park' to locals, the inner corners of **María Luisa Park**, **Plaza de España** and the **Murillo Gardens**: the regionalist Seville dressed up for the **1929 Ibero-American Exhibition**.

The walk ends with a stop to rest and have a drink at a well-known spot in the **Santa Cruz quarter**.

## How it works

- A **private** tour of **2.5 hours**, at **10:00** or **17:30**.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 15.
- Meeting point: **Plaza de Santa Cruz**.

## Who it's for

- Visitors who know the centre and want an outdoor walk.
- Garden and landscape lovers.
- Groups: with 4 people the price nearly halves.
