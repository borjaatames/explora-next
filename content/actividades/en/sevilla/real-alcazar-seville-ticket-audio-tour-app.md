## What you will see

The audio tour takes you through the rooms, **courtyards** and **gardens** of the Real Alcázar, one of the finest examples of Mudéjar architecture in Spain, with the history of the palace from the 10th century to today.

## How it works

After booking you receive a link to download the free **WeGoTrip** app and your tour. Download it before you go (it works **offline**), put your earphones in and press play at the first stop. The app shows the route on a live map and each stop has audio and written text, so you can **pause, skip or repeat** whenever you like. There is no live guide and no group. Your **entry ticket** comes with the booking: keep it on your phone and show it at the entrance.

## Who I recommend it to

- To those who want the Alcázar ticket with explanations included.
- To travellers who like to go at their own pace, without a group or a fixed schedule.
- Not for those who want to ask a live guide questions: in that case a guided tour is a better fit.
