## What you will do

- The **Torre del Oro** and **Triana bridge** from the water.
- The **Arenal** and **Triana** riverbanks.

## How it works

You arrive at the club, get a kayak and life jacket, listen to the safety briefing and paddle for about 2 hours with an instructor nearby.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with kids aged 8 and up** who can swim.
- **Teenagers** who get bored of monuments.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who can't swim.
- Children under 8.
