## What you will see

The tour starts at the operator's office, a few steps from the Cathedral; on the way, the guide explains the exterior and the history of the neighbourhood. Once inside:

- **The central nave and the main altarpiece**, the largest in Christendom.
- **The tomb of Christopher Columbus**, carried by four heralds.
- **The Giralda**: up to 30 minutes to climb its 35 ramps to the former minaret, with views over Seville.
- The **Sacristía Mayor** and the **Patio de los Naranjos**, remains of the old mosque.
