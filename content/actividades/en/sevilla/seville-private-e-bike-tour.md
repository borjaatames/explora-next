## The route

You start in **Triana**, the historic neighbourhood and birthplace of flamenco. Then you ride to the **Royal Alcázar** and the **Cathedral**, seen from outside, and the 18th-century **Royal Tobacco Factory**. On to **Plaza de España**, through **María Luisa Park** and up to the **Metropol Parasol**, **Las Setas**. Much of the tour runs along the **Guadalquivir**.

## Options

- **Bike, 2 hours:** from €240 in total for 1 or 2 people; €320 for 4.
- **E-bike, 3 hours:** from €280 for 1 or 2 people; €440 for 4.

Prices may vary slightly by date.

## Who it's for

- Anyone wanting to see a lot of Seville in a short time.
- Families with children over 1.50 m and groups of friends.
- In summer, go for the e-bike and an early start.
