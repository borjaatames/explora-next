## What you'll see

**Mérida**, ancient **Emerita Augusta**, is Spain's great Roman city and a World Heritage Site. With a guide you explore the **Roman theatre**, the largest in the country, and the **amphitheatre**, tickets included. You also see the **Temple of Diana** and the **Roman circus**.

After lunch (not included), you drive to the **Basilica of Santa Eulalia** and the **Los Milagros aqueduct**.

## How it works

- A **private** trip of about **8.5 hours**, leaving at **8:30**.
- **Pickup from your accommodation** in Seville and private transport.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 7.

## Who it's for

- Roman history and archaeology enthusiasts.
- Groups of 3 or 4: the price per person drops a lot.
- Not for wheelchair users: it isn't accessible.
