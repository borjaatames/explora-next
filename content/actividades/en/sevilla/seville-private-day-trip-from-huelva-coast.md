## What the day looks like

You are picked up at your hotel on the **Huelva coast** and go straight to **Seville**. With your guide you visit the **Cathedral** and the **Royal Alcázar**, tickets included, and stroll through the **Santa Cruz quarter** and **Plaza de España**, built for the 1929 Ibero-American Exposition. The visit also includes a walk through **Triana**. There is time for lunch in a tavern before heading back.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €688 for 2 people and €756 for 4. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone holidaying on the Huelva coast who wants to see Seville without driving or parking.
- Groups of 4: the price per head drops a lot.
