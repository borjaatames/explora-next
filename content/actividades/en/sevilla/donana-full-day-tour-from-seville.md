## What you'll see

An **8.5-hour trip** from Seville to **Doñana National Park**, one of Europe's most important wetlands and a stopover for hundreds of bird species on their way to Africa. From **El Acebuche** visitor centre there's a **4x4 tour of about 3.5 hours** through marshes, dunes and scrubland. Then you stop for lunch in the village of **El Rocío** and, on the way back to Seville, visit the almost Martian landscape of the **Río Tinto**.

## How it works

You're **picked up at your accommodation in Seville** and brought back there. The 4x4 inside the park **may be shared** with other visitors. Lunch isn't included. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 9.

## Who I recommend it for

- Nature lovers and birdwatchers.
- Anyone who has already seen Seville's monuments and wants something different.
- Not a good choice with back problems or during pregnancy, given the bumpy 4x4 ride.
