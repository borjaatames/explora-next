## What the tour is like

You meet your guide at the **stairs of the Setas** as evening falls and for **1 h 30**, with your group only, you explore the **historic centre**: the **legends**, crimes and mysteries hidden in streets and squares that go unnoticed by day.

## How it works

It's **private** and priced **per group**: €125 for 1 to 4 people and €175 for 5 to 10. It's outdoors, with no tickets.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who has seen the monuments and wants a different Seville.
- Couples, families and groups of friends: for 4 people it's about €31 a head.
