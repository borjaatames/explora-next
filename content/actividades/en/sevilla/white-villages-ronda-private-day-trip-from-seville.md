## What you'll see

A **private 9-hour day trip** through the **White Villages** of the Cádiz and Málaga mountains. The suggested route takes in **Zahara de la Sierra**, **Setenil de las Bodegas**, with its houses under the rock, and **Ronda**, with the **Puente Nuevo** over the gorge, where you stop for lunch. It ends in **Olvera**, with a visit to its local bakery.

## How it works

The driver-guide **picks you up at your accommodation in Seville**. Since it's private, the route can be **adapted**: say when booking if you'd prefer other villages. Lunch isn't included. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 7.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the White Villages without the rush of a coach tour.
- Families and groups of 3 to 6, where the price works out best.
- Amateur photographers: the scenery is worth it.
