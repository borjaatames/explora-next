## What the ride looks like

You board the carriage at **Plaza del Triunfo**, next to the Giralda and the Cathedral, and for an hour the driver takes you past the **Torre del Oro**, the former **Royal Tobacco Factory**, Avenida de la Palmera and **María Luisa Park**, to **Plaza de España**, where you stop for photos.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per carriage**: €99 for up to 4 people. The driver drives but **does not guide**.

## Who I recommend it to

- Couples and families who want the classic ride without haggling.
- Anyone who wants to see Plaza de España and María Luisa without walking.
