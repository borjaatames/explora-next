## What you'll see

- **Seville Cathedral:** the largest Gothic cathedral in the world, with the main altarpiece, the tomb of Christopher Columbus and chapels with works by Murillo and Zurbarán.
- **The Giralda:** the former Almohad minaret turned bell tower. You climb it via 35 ramps, and from the top there are views over the whole centre.
- **Patio de los Naranjos:** the ablutions courtyard of the old mosque.

## How it works

1. When booking, give the full name and ID details of every participant.
2. Download the audio guide to your phone before leaving your hotel.
3. Go to the office at Calle Arfe 3 ten minutes before your time and collect the ticket.
4. Enter the Cathedral (a 4-minute walk away) and follow the audio guide at your own pace.
