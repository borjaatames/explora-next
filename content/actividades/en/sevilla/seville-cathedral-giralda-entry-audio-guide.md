## What you'll see

Combined Cathedral + Giralda entry with **downloadable mobile audio guide** in 5 languages (EN, ES, IT, FR, DE). Ideal product for Spanish speakers wanting autonomy + audio in their language + cancellation flexibility.

- **Seville Cathedral (interior):** 42-metre central nave, Pierre Dancart's main altarpiece (over 1,000 carved figures), 44 chapels with works by Goya, Murillo and Zurbarán, 15th-century Renaissance choir, great organs.
- **Christopher Columbus's tomb** in front of the Puerta del Príncipe, held by the four allegorical figures of Castile, León, Aragón and Navarre.
- **Giralda (climb):** 35 wide gentle ramps (~95 m accumulated height) with panoramic views of Seville. The audio guide covers the Islamic origin as minaret of the 12th-century Almohad mosque.
- **Patio de los Naranjos:** original mosque ablutions courtyard with century-old orange trees and the Muslim fountain.
- **Audio guide of ~75 min** structured by points of interest. Follow your own pace and pause whenever.

## How it works

**Book your date and monument access time.** When booking, provide full names and passport/ID details for ALL guests.

**Before leaving the hotel, download the audio guide** in the operator's app (~100-200 MB per language) using your own wifi. The app doesn't work offline if not pre-downloaded.

**Arrive at the operator's office on Calle Arfe 3 (Old Town) at least 1 HOUR BEFORE** your booked monument access time. They give you the validated ticket and resolve any technical doubts.

**Walk 4-5 min from Calle Arfe to the Cathedral** (Puerta del Lagarto, online access on Avenida de la Constitución).

**Show your ticket at the entrance.** Access is with an initial operator accompaniment; afterwards you enjoy at your own pace.

**Tour the cathedral following the audio guide** (~45 min). Connect your headphones to your phone and follow the points of interest.

**Climb the Giralda** via the 35 ramps. The audio guide covers the climb. ~25 min round trip with explanations.

**Visit the Patio de los Naranjos** before exiting.
