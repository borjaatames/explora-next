## What you will see

### Seville Cathedral and the Giralda
The largest Gothic cathedral in the world, with the **main altarpiece**, the **tomb of Christopher Columbus** and the climb up the **Giralda**, the former Almohad minaret turned bell tower, with views over the whole centre.

### Real Alcázar
The oldest royal palace still in use in Europe: the **Patio de las Doncellas**, the Mudéjar halls of Peter I and the **gardens** with orange trees and myrtles. The guide explains how Islamic, Gothic and Renaissance styles blend here.

## Tip

Wear comfortable shoes and bring water in summer: it is two and a half hours on your feet, much of it in the sun in the courtyards and gardens.
