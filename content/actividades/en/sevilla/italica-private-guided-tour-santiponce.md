## What you'll see

A **private 2-hour tour** of **Itálica**, the Roman city founded in 206 BC by Scipio next to today's Santiponce. You'll see the **mosaics in the House of the Planetarium** and the House of the Birds, the **baths**, a restored Roman bakery and the city's streets, finishing at the **amphitheatre**, one of the largest in the Empire.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the **entrance to the archaeological site**. **Tickets are included**; transport is not. From Seville there's a bus from **Plaza de Armas** station (platforms 41-42), or you can take a taxi, and at the end the supplier can call you one back. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 8.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone interested in Roman history who wants to make sense of the site.
- Families with older children: 2 hours go by quickly.
- Game of Thrones fans: the Dragonpit was filmed here.
