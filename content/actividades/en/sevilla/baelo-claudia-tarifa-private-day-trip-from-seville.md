## How the day works

You're picked up at **8:00** from your accommodation in Seville and, after a coffee stop, reach **Baelo Claudia**, a Roman city on the seashore. It was the leading producer of **garum**, the fish sauce prized by Rome's upper classes. The **guided visit lasts about 90 minutes** through its streets and religious and industrial buildings, between the **Natural Park** and **Bolonia beach** with its dunes.

Then on to **Tarifa**, with its white streets and its role in the Moorish conquest 1,300 years ago. You visit **San Mateo church**, the **Moorish quarter** and **Guzmán el Bueno castle**, from where you can see the **African coast, 14 km away**. Lunch is in Tarifa (not included).

## How it works

- A **private** trip of about **10 hours**.
- Includes **transfer**, **guide at Baelo Claudia**, **tickets**, **breakfast** and a private visit to Tarifa.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 7.

## Who it's for

- Roman archaeology enthusiasts.
- Anyone wanting to see the Cádiz coast and Tarifa from Seville without a car.
- Groups of 3 or 4: the price per person drops a lot.
