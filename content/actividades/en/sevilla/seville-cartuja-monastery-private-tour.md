## What you'll see

The visit starts at the **Puerta de Tierra** and first explores the monastery's **orchards and gardens**, among the oldest in Andalusia. Then you go inside the building, now home to the **Andalusian Centre of Contemporary Art**: the **chapter house**, with the crypts of the monastery's patrons, and the **Mudéjar cloister**.

You'll also see the **bottle kilns** of the pottery factory Charles Pickman set up here in the 19th century, and hear the site's story: the thirty years it kept **Christopher Columbus's remains**, its time as a French army barracks and the Guadalquivir floods. It ends in the gardens.

## How it works

It's a **2-hour private visit** with **tickets included**. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 15. Getting to La Cartuja is up to you.

## Who it's for

- Visitors who've seen the Alcázar and Cathedral and want a quieter monument.
- Anyone interested in Columbus and Seville's trade with the Americas.
- Groups of 4 or more: the price per person drops a lot.
