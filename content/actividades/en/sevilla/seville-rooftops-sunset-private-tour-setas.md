## What you'll see

Seville from above at **sunset**, when the domes of churches and buildings light up at the same time. You visit **two rooftops**, where your guide shares legends and stories that only make sense looking across the roofs. The second and last is the **Metropol Parasol**, **Las Setas**, where you have a **cocktail**.

## How it works

- **Private walking tour** of **2.5 hours**, for **2 to 6 people** aged 14+.
- **Price per person**, minimum 2.
- The start time follows the sunset: **17:00** in winter, **17:30** in autumn, **18:30** in spring and **20:00** in summer.
- Las Setas entry and a drink are included.

## Who it's for

- Couples or small groups after a different evening in Seville.
- Photographers: sunset light over the Giralda is the best of the day.
- Not families with young children: it's 14+ only.
