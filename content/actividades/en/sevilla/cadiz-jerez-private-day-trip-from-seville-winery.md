## How the day works

You're picked up from your accommodation in Seville and in just over an hour reach **Cádiz**. A local guide gives you a **2-hour private tour** of the **old town**, the **City Hall** and the **Market**, where the visit ends. Then you have **an hour free for lunch** (not included).

From Cádiz your driver takes you to **Jerez de la Frontera** to visit a **winery** and do a **sherry tasting**. Afterwards, back to Seville.

## How it works

- An **8.5-hour** trip with **private transport**.
- The Cádiz tour is **private**; the **winery visit is in a small group**.
- **Priced per group**: €870 for 1 or 2 people; €1,160 for 4.
- **Non-refundable booking.**

## Who it's for

- Families and groups of 3 or 4 who want the day sorted, with no trains or timetables.
- Anyone wanting a guided Cádiz and sherry at its source.
- Not solo travellers or couples on a budget: group tours are cheaper.
