## What you'll see

A **1-1.5 hour guided cruise** on the Guadalquivir, Spain's only navigable river. Top Rated 4.7/5 with 1,746 reviews — Seville's best-rated river cruise.

- **Torre del Oro (13th c.):** Almohad defensive tower next to the pier. We leave in its shadow heading south.
- **Plaza de Toros de la Maestranza:** view from the river of the white and gold facade of Spain's oldest bullring (1761).
- **Triana (Calle Betis):** the historic flamenco neighborhood on the other side of the river — painted facades, tapas bars, ceramicist streets.
- **Historic bridges:** Triana Bridge (forged iron, 1852) and San Telmo Bridge.
- **Isla de la Cartuja:** modern area where Expo 92 was held (Pavilion of Navigation, La Cartuja Monastery).
- **Alamillo Bridge:** Calatrava's work (1992), one of modern Seville's icons.
- **1 drink included** during the trip: soft drink, beer, glass of wine or water.

## How it works

**Book your date, session and language** (Spanish, English or bilingual). Private group option available.

**Arrive 15-20 min before the session** at the **Muelle de Turismo** (next to Torre del Oro, Paseo de Cristóbal Colón). Look for the orange and white flag «Navega en Sevilla».

**Show your mobile voucher.** Board via the gangway (flat shoes mandatory — no heels).

**Enjoy the 1-1.5 hour cruise** with live guide. The guide points out points of interest and answers questions.

**Specify your drink** when boarding (soft drink, beer, glass of wine or water).

**Disembark back at the Muelle de Turismo.** Combine with walk along Paseo de Cristóbal Colón (Torre del Oro 50 m), Calle Betis in Triana (10 min walking via Triana Bridge) or dinner at Barrio El Arenal restaurants.
