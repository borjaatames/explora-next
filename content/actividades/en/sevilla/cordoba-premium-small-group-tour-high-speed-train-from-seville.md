## What you'll see

A **PREMIUM Córdoba day trip by AVE** — group maximum 8 people + high-speed train (45 min vs. 1h45min by bus). The fast and exclusive alternative to the standard tour for visitors with 1 day who want to maximize Córdoba.

- **AVE train Seville Santa Justa → Córdoba Central:** 45 minutes in both directions, air-conditioned wagon, reserved seat.
- **Mezquita-Cathedral (UNESCO):** guided visit with included entry (1h30min).
- **Jewish Quarter of Córdoba:** Synagogue, Calle de las Flores, Callejón del Pañuelo, Maimonides statue.
- **Córdoba Patios:** hidden patios open to the public (when in season — May is the official Patios Festival).
- **Free time:** 1-2 hours in Córdoba for lunch and personal exploration (several options available).
- **MAX 8 person group:** exclusive experience, direct interaction with guide.

## How it works

**Book your date, option (with/without free time) and language** (Spanish or English).

**Arrive at Seville-Santa Justa 30 min before the AVE.** Metro line 1 direct from Puerta de Jerez (10 min).

**AVE trip Seville → Córdoba:** 45 minutes, meet the guide upon arrival at Córdoba Central.

**Guided tour of Mezquita + Jewish Quarter** (~3-4h).

**Free time for lunch and exploration** (if you booked the free time option).

**Return AVE to Seville.** Estimated arrival at Santa Justa mid-afternoon.

## Who I recommend it to

- Visitors with **1 day in Seville** who want to maximize Córdoba.
- Couples valuing **small group max 8 people**.
- **Mid-to-high budget** travellers who prefer AVE over bus.
- **Foodies** who prefer choosing restaurant independently vs. fixed menu.

## Who I do NOT recommend it to

- **Tight-budget** visitors (standard t89625 is cheaper).
- **Large groups (6+)** — already fill almost the whole 8 capacity.
- Those valuing **traditional meal included** without organizing (better t89625).
- **Strict wheelchair users** (not suitable per operator).
