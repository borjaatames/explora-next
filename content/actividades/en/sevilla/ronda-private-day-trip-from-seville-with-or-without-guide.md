## How the day works

You're picked up from your accommodation in Seville and driven to **Ronda**, one of Andalusia's most beautiful towns, perched above the **Tajo gorge** and seen as the **birthplace of bullfighting**.

Depending on your option, you take a **2-hour private guided tour** of Ronda or explore on your own with the operator's recommendations. Then there's **free time** to wander and have lunch. At the end, your driver picks you up in Ronda and takes you back to your hotel.

## Options

- **Without a guide:** €500 in total for 1 or 2 people; €720 for 4.
- **With a 2-hour private guide in Ronda (in English):** €700 for 1 or 2 people; €880 for 4.

Departures at **8:00** or **10:30**.

## Who it's for

- Families and groups of 4 who prefer a private car to a coach.
- Anyone who wants to go at their own pace, without a group.
- If you want Ronda plus the white villages, see our private white villages and Ronda trip.
