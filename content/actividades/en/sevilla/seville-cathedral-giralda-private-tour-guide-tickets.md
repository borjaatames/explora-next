## What the tour is like

You meet your guide in **Plaza Virgen de los Reyes**, at the foot of the Giralda. You go in with your tickets sorted and for about **2 hours** explore the **Cathedral** with your group only: the main altarpiece, the chapels, **Columbus's tomb** and the works by **Murillo**. Then you climb the **Giralda** by its 35 ramps to see Seville from above and finish in the **Patio de los Naranjos**, the courtyard of the former mosque.

## How it works

It's **private** and priced **per group**, tickets included: €184 for 2 people and €208 for 4.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to understand the largest Gothic cathedral and not miss what matters.
- Groups of 3 or 4: the price per person drops a lot.
- If you're interested in both the Alcázar and the Cathedral, see the same guides' private Alcázar tour too.
