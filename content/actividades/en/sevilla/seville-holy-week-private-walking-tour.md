## What the tour looks like

You meet your guide at **Plaza Nueva** and for two and a half hours you go out into the streets to see how Seville lives its **Holy Week**: the **floats**, the nazarenos and the devotion of the locals. You go inside a **brotherhood** to see how everything is prepared and talk to its members, and stop at a **Holy Week bar** for a drink.

## How it works

It is **private**, for up to 6 people. The route changes with each day’s processions.

## Who I recommend it to

- First-timers in Seville during Holy Week.
- Anyone who wants to understand the celebration, not just watch it go by.
