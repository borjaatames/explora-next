## How the day works

You're picked up from your accommodation in Seville at **8:30** and head into **Huelva** province. In the morning you visit the **Río Tinto mines**, worked since Roman times, with a **train ride through the industrial area**, and the mining company's **old English quarter**.

After lunch (not included) you go to **La Palma del Condado** and the **Río Tinto natural area**, with its **19th-century flour mills** and the red-water landscape NASA studies for its likeness to Mars. Then back to Seville.

## How it works

- A **private** trip of about **7 hours**, with **transport, tickets and guide**.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 7.
- Breakfast and lunch are extra.

## Who it's for

- Families and nature lovers after a different kind of landscape.
- Photographers: the river's colours are spectacular.
- Not for pregnant travellers or people with heart conditions, as the operator notes.
