## What the ride is like

For **90 minutes** you ride around Seville in a **private horse-drawn carriage**, just for your party. A **guide versed in Andalusian history** comes along, explaining the places you pass and the role horses have always played in the city's life. To finish, there's a glass of **traditional local wine**.

## How booking works

- The price is **per carriage**, not per person: **€298** for up to **4 passengers**.
- When booking, select **1 person**: that's how the operator reserves the whole carriage.
- It leaves from **Calle Adriano 33** in El Arenal.

## Who it's for

- Families and groups of 3 or 4, when the per-carriage price pays off.
- Anyone after a relaxed ride round Seville with someone telling its story.
- Not for wheelchair users: the carriage isn't accessible.
