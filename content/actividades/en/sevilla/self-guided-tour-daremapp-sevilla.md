## What you'll discover

The DareMapp app guides you through the Giralda, the Plaza de España steps, Triana Bridge and the Torre del Oro, plus more than 20 points of interest in total, all narrated with audio in your own language. **There's no physical guide**: it's a fully self-guided experience, built so you decide what to see, how long to stay, and in what order.

## How it works

After booking you get a **reference code on your ticket**. Enter it in the DareMapp app (available in Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian and German) and start the route whenever you like, **wherever in Seville you choose**. There's no meeting point and no fixed schedule: you can pause and resume the audio at any time, pick only the stops that interest you, and enjoy it **24 hours a day, 365 days a year** — the access never expires. The app also includes food and family-activity recommendations for the rest of your visit.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Independent travellers who'd rather not be tied to a schedule or a group.
- Families with young children or a stroller, who need the flexibility to stop whenever they need to.
- Anyone who wants to explore Seville at a relaxed pace, without a live guide setting the tempo.
