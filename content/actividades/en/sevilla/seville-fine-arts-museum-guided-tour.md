## What the tour is like

You meet your guide in **Plaza del Museo**, by the statue of Murillo, and for **2 hours** explore the **Seville Fine Arts Museum**, housed in the former Convent of La Merced: the great Seville Baroque painters —**Murillo**, **Zurbarán**, **Valdés Leal**— and the stories behind each work, told by an art historian.

## How it works

It's a **small group** of 8 people at most, priced **per person**, with entry included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who likes painting and wants to go beyond the labels.
- For a hot afternoon: it's indoors and cool.
