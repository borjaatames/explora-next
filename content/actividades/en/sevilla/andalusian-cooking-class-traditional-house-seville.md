## How the day works

You're **picked up from your accommodation** and head first to a **traditional market**, where you get to know and choose the produce you'll cook with. Then it's on to a **traditional Andalusian house** for the **cooking workshop**, led by a cook with **more than 45 years** in the trade. You work with fresh produce and **centuries-old recipes**, leaving the usual clichés about Andalusian and Spanish food behind.

At the end, you **sit down to eat what you've made**.

## How it works

- A **private** experience of about **5 hours**.
- Two slots: **11:00** or **17:30**.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 8.
- Mention any intolerances when booking.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who likes cooking and wants to take home real Andalusian recipes.
- Families or groups of friends: with 4 the price per head nearly halves.
- Not for a quick meal: it takes half a day.
