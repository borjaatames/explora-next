A **2.5-hour paella show-cooking experience** in Seville: a chef prepares paella step-by-step while you sip artisan sangria, then you enjoy the full paella meal with unlimited drinks.

- **Paella SHOW-COOKING** (~45-60 min): chef demonstrates technique, ingredients and history.
- **Artisan SANGRIA** served while the paella cooks (house-made with fresh fruit, 12-24 h marinated).
- **Paella meal** — your choice of vegetarian or seafood option.
- **Unlimited drinks during dinner:** red wine, white wine, beer, soft drinks, water.
- **English-speaking instructor** with culinary background.
- **Optional upgrades:** cava sangria, saffron box, sweet sherry, coffee & dessert (paid extras).

## How it works

**Book your date, time and paella option** (vegetarian OR seafood). Read the email for exact venue.

**Arrive 10 min early** at the venue specified in your booking email. Bring ID.

**Watch the chef** prepare paella for ~45-60 min while you sip sangria and ask questions.

**Sit down for the full meal** — paella + unlimited drinks served by staff. Total dining ~60-75 min.

**Tour ends** at the venue — walking distance to most central Seville attractions.

- Travellers who want an **EVENING activity around food** without effort.

- **Groups mixing vegetarians and seafood eaters** (both options available simultaneously).
- **Couples** seeking a relaxed dinner with chef demonstration.
