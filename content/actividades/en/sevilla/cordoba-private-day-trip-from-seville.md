## What you'll see

A **private 7.5-hour day trip** from Seville to Córdoba. The highlight is the **guided visit to the Mosque-Cathedral**, with its forest of red and white arches and its gilded mihrab. Then the **Jewish Quarter**: its narrow streets, flower-filled patios, the **Synagogue**, one of the few preserved in Spain, and the artisans' **Zoco**.

## How it works

The **private transport** picks you up at your accommodation in Seville and brings you back there. **Mosque-Cathedral and Synagogue tickets** are included, as is the **private guide**. Lunch is paid separately. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 7.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in Seville who wants to see Córdoba without dealing with trains or tickets.
- Families and small groups who prefer a guide just for them.
- Anyone interested in the history of the three cultures.
