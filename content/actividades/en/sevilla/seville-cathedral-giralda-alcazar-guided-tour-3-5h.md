## What you'll see

A **relaxed 3.5-hour guided tour** of Seville's 3 UNESCO monuments with a unique added value: initial photo stop + 1 hour of FREE TIME in the Alcázar Gardens at the end.

- **Initial photo stop (15-30 min):** panoramic view of the 3 sites (typically from Plaza del Triunfo or Plaza Virgen de los Reyes). The guide gives general historical context before entering the monuments.
- **Seville Cathedral guided (45-60 min):** 42 m central nave, Pierre Dancart's main altarpiece, 44 chapels with Goya/Murillo/Zurbarán, Christopher Columbus's tomb.
- **Giralda (25-30 min climb):** 35 ramps to the 105 m minaret with panoramic views. Guide audio during the climb (not in large groups without headphones).
- **Royal Alcázar guided (1.5h):** Mudéjar Palace of Pedro I + Patio de las Doncellas + Royal Chambers + Gothic Palace + Admiral's Halls.
- **FREE TIME in Alcázar Gardens (1h):** explore the 7 hectares at your own pace — peacocks, century-old orange trees, fountains, House of Charles V, Baths of María Padilla (Game of Thrones setting).

## How it works

**Book your date, time, language** (ES, EN, IT, FR) and modality (Shared or Private).

**Arrive 15-20 min early** at Plaza del Triunfo / Plaza Virgen de los Reyes (specific location in the voucher). The guide groups by language.

**Start with the PHOTO STOP** (15-30 min) — the guide explains general context of Seville, Islamic and Christian history, and why the 3 UNESCO sites matter before entering.

**Cathedral + Giralda** (75-90 min) with skip-the-line — guide covers the cathedral and then you climb the Giralda with their audio.

**Walking transfer to Royal Alcázar** (5 min).

**Royal Alcázar guided** (1.5h) — Mudéjar Palace, Patio Doncellas, Royal Chambers, Gothic Palace, Halls.

**FREE TIME in the Gardens** (1h) — the guide points out the key spots and leaves you to explore at your own pace. Exit via Puerta del León or Patio de Banderas when you're ready.
