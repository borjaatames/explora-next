## What the tour looks like

You meet your guide at **Puerta de Jerez** at 12:30 and for about 3 hours you walk through Seville’s **historic centre**, stopping at taverns to try **3 traditional tapas** and **3 drinks**. Between stops the guide shares stories, legends and curiosities about the city and its food.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €270 for 2 people and €360 for 4. It is outdoors, with no monument tickets.

## Who I recommend it to

- Couples and families who want to eat tapas with someone who knows the places.
- Anyone who prefers a tapas tour without strangers.
