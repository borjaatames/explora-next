## What the tour looks like

You meet your guide at **Plaza del Triunfo** at 12:30 and for two and a half hours you walk through central Seville, stopping at taverns to try **3 traditional tapas** and **3 drinks**. Between stops the guide shares legends, anecdotes and the story of Seville’s cooking.

## How it works

It is a **small group** of up to 8 people. It costs €60 per adult, with tapas and drinks included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Solo travellers and couples who want to eat tapas in company.
- Anyone who wants to know what to order, and where, for the rest of the trip.
