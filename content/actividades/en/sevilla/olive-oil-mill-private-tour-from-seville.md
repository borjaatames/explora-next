## What the visit is like

You're picked up from your accommodation and in about **40 minutes** reach **Basilippo**, a **family-run mill** on a **historic hacienda** surrounded by **6,000 Arbequina olive trees**. With an expert you follow the making of extra virgin olive oil, from growing to extraction and bottling, and do a **tasting**.

The visit, of about **90 minutes**, ends with a **creative dish** served in the hacienda's private rooms. You can buy oil to take home.

## How it works

- A **private** 3.5-hour trip with **private transport**.
- **Priced per group**, minimum 2 people: €440 for 2; €600 for 4.
- Departures at **9:00, 10:00, 11:00 and 12:00**.
- **Non-refundable booking.**

## Who it's for

- Food and olive oil lovers.
- Anyone with a free morning who doesn't want a full-day trip.
- Families and groups of 4: the price per head drops quite a bit.
