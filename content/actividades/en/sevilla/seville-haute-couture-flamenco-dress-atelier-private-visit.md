## What the visit is like

For **two hours** you step inside a **Seville haute couture flamenco atelier** and, guided by its **designers**, see how a dress comes to life: from the first **sketch** to the **patterns**, fabrics, colours and the **final embroidery**. The atelier has created pieces for figures such as **Queen Sofía** and has worked for **Yves Saint Laurent**.

During the visit you can **try on authentic flamenco dresses**. If you fall for one, you can buy it or commission a made-to-measure dress, at extra cost.

## How it works

- A **private** 2-hour visit at **10:00**.
- Price **per person**, minimum 2, maximum 6.
- Meeting point: **Hotel Inglaterra** on Plaza Nueva.

## Who it's for

- Anyone interested in fashion and craftsmanship.
- Visitors coming to Seville with the April Fair in mind.
- Groups of friends or families: with 4 the price per head nearly halves.
