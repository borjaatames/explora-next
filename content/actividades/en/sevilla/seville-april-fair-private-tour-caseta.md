## What the experience looks like

You are picked up at your accommodation by **horse-drawn carriage** and arrive at the **fairground**, with its more than a thousand **casetas**, sandy lanes and paper lanterns. With your guide you walk the site and learn how Seville’s most international festival works. You finish with **Andalusian food and sherry** in a **private caseta**, something a visitor cannot get on their own.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €1,644 for 2 people and €2,008 for 4. The return to the hotel is not included. **It only makes sense during Fair week.**

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone in Seville during the Fair who wants to experience it from inside a caseta.
- Groups of 4, who share the cost better.
