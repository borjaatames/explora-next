## How the day works

You take the **train from Santa Justa** and in **under an hour** you're in Córdoba, with return tickets already included. Your **private guide** meets you there and takes you first to the **Mosque-Cathedral**, a unique blend of Moorish and Christian architecture: the visit lasts just over an hour, with **tickets included**. Then you explore the **Jewish Quarter**, its lanes and its history. In total you spend **2.5 hours** with your guide.

Afterwards your guide recommends where to eat and you have **free time** until the train back to Seville.

## How it works

- **Train tickets**, **entry tickets** and **private guide** included.
- **Priced per group**, minimum 2: €432 for 2; €668 for 4.
- The operator sends tickets and the guide's contact within 24 hours.
- **Non-refundable booking.**

## Who it's for

- Anyone wanting Córdoba in a day without paying for a private car.
- Couples and families who'd like a guide to themselves.
