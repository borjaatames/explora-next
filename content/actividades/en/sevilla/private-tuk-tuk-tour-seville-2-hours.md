## What you will do

- The **Plaza de España** and **María Luisa Park**.
- The **Torre del Oro**, the **Maestranza** and **Triana**.
- **Cartuja island**, the **Macarena** and the **city walls**.

## How it works

You book a day and time, go down to level -1 of the Saba car park at Plaza de Armas, and the driver takes you round for two hours in an electric tuk tuk just for your group, with photo stops.

If you'd rather go shorter, there's a [1-hour version](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/private-tuk-tuk-tour-seville).

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with kids**.
- Anyone wanting to see **all of Seville** quickly.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to go inside monuments.
- Wheelchair users.
