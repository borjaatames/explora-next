## What you'll see

A **1-hour guided tour of Seville Cathedral** with priority access through a separate entrance + Giralda climb afterwards. 2,362 reviews at 4.5/5 make it the highest-volume guided cathedral tour in Seville on GetYourGuide.

- **Guided tour (1h) inside the Cathedral of Santa María de la Sede** — built between 1248 and 1507 on the site of the former Almohad mosque. Your guide walks you through:
  - The 42-metre central nave (the largest Gothic nave in the world by volume).
  - The **Dancart main altarpiece** — over 1,000 figures carved in wood and gold, the largest altarpiece in the world.
  - **Christopher Columbus's tomb** in front of the Puerta del Príncipe — held by four allegorical kingdoms (Castile, León, Aragón, Navarre).
  - The **44 chapels** with works by Goya, Murillo and Zurbarán.
  - The **Patio de los Naranjos** — the former Almohad ablutions courtyard with century-old orange trees.
- **After the guided portion**, you climb the **Giralda Bell Tower** on your own (still included in the ticket). 35 wide ramps to the top — panoramic views of Seville (Plaza del Triunfo, Alcázar, Guadalquivir river, old town).
- **Personal audio system** — you hear the guide clearly in the cathedral's vast spaces, even in the back of a 25-person group.
