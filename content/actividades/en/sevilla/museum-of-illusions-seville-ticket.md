- **Optical illusions** and holograms.
- The **vortex tunnel** and **anti-gravity room**.
- Rooms designed for **impossible photos**.

## How it works

You buy a ticket for one day, show it at the door and explore the museum at your own pace, touching and interacting with everything.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with kids aged 6 to 14**.
- Anyone looking for an **indoor** plan in the centre.

## Who I don't recommend it for
