## What you'll see

A **self-guided entry to the Metropol Parasol** (popularly «Las Setas de Sevilla»), the world's largest laminated wood structure — designed by Jürgen Mayer in 2011. The ticket combines 3 experiences in one:

- **360° viewpoint** at 28 m — serpentine walkways with unique views over the Giralda, the Cathedral, the historic centre and the Guadalquivir river.
- **Feeling Sevilla** (15 min): immersive film on a curved-screen room — travel through Seville via its senses (music, scent, rhythm).
- **Aurora**: LED light show at nightfall with AI — Seville's largest light installation, changing every night based on temperature, humidity and crowd data.

**Downloadable audio guide in 8 languages** (ES, EN, IT, FR, DE, PT, KO, ZH) accessed via QR scan at entry. Ticket valid for 2 entries in 48 h — perfect for combining day + night.

## How it works

**Book your date and time slot** (open access within the booked hour).

**Head to Level -1** of Plaza de la Encarnación, next to the escalators. Do NOT try to climb up from street level — viewpoint access is via lift from the basement.

**Validate your mobile ticket** at the access control. Scan the QR to download the audio guide in your language BEFORE going up.

**Take the lift** to the viewpoint. Walk the walkways at your own pace (~20-30 min). Follow the audio guide as you stroll.

**Enjoy «Feeling Sevilla»** (15 min) in the immersive room — included in the ticket.

**Return at nightfall** within 48 h to see Aurora (no extra charge) — the show starts at sunset.

## Who it's for

- **First-timers in Seville** who want orientation from above.
- **Amateur photographers** — sunset views are among the city's best.
- **Families with children** — the shaded Plaza Mayor + Aurora is a hit at all ages.
- **21st-century architecture fans** — the structure is a laminated-wood engineering feat.
- **Visitors with limited time** who want Seville «at a glance» before diving into specific monuments.

## Who it's NOT for

- Visitors with **severe vertigo** (28 m, suspended walkways).
- Anyone who **already climbed the Giralda** and just wants a similar view (redundant).
- Visitors with **only 1 hour** who'd rather see UNESCO culture (better Cathedral or Royal Alcázar).
- Anyone looking for a **live guide** — this is self-guided via app.
- Mid-summer noon visitors without sun protection — the upper level is harsh under direct sun.
