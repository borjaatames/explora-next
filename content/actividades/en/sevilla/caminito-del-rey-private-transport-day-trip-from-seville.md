## How the day works

You're picked up from your accommodation in Seville and in about **2 hours** reach **El Chorro**, where the **Caminito del Rey** begins. The route covers about **3 km** of almost flat ground through the **Gaitanes gorge**, on walkways clinging **more than 100 metres** above the river, bridges over the reservoir and dizzying viewpoints.

The path is named after **King Alfonso XIII**, who walked it when he opened the dam. Afterwards, back to Seville.

## How it works

- **Private return transport** and **tickets included**.
- The route is done with a **guide in a group**.
- **Priced per group**: €620 for 1 or 2 people; €880 for 4.
- Minimum age: **8**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone staying in Seville who doesn't want to drive to Málaga.
- Families and groups of 4: the per-group price pays off.
- Not for people with severe vertigo or heart conditions.
