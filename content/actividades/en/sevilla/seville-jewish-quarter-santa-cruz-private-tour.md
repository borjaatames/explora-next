## What the tour is like

You meet your guide in **Plaza del Triunfo** and in **1 h 30**, with your group only, you walk the streets of Seville's old **Jewish Quarter**, today the **Santa Cruz neighbourhood**: the medieval layout, hidden squares, former synagogues turned into churches and the **legends** behind some of the street names.

## How it works

It's **private** and priced **per group**: €125 for 1 to 4 people and €175 for 5 to 10. It's an outdoor walk with no tickets.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone interested in Seville's Jewish history.
- Families and small groups: for 4 people it's about €31 a head.
- To combine with the Alcázar, right next door.
