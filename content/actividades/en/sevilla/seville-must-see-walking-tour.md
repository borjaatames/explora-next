## What you'll see

- The **Santa Cruz** quarter and its lanes.
- The **cathedral** and the **Giralda**, from outside.
- The area around the **Royal Alcázar** and other spots you choose with the guide.

## How it works

You meet at Plaza de los Refinadores in Santa Cruz. If you go **private**, the route adapts to your interests and pace. At the end, the guide helps you plan the rest of your visit.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone arriving in Seville who wants to get oriented in two hours.
- Families or couples who want a **private tour** without spending much.
