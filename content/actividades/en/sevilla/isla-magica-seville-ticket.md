## What you will do

- **Rides** for all ages, with an area for little ones.
- Pirate and period **shows** throughout the day.
- **Agua Mágica** water park, if you choose that option.

## How it works

You pick a day, add Agua Mágica if you like and go in without queuing at the ticket office. The ticket is valid for that day.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with kids aged 4 to 11**.
- Anyone visiting Seville in **summer** (with Agua Mágica).

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Teenagers after extreme rides.
- Anyone visiting November to March: it's closed.
