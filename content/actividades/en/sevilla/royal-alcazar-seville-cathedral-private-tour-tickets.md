## What you'll see

**Seville Cathedral**, built over a former mosque, is the largest Gothic cathedral in the world. Inside is the **tomb of Christopher Columbus**. You also climb the **Giralda**, the former minaret almost 100 metres high, with some of the best views in the city.

The **Royal Alcázar**, begun in Islamic times, blends styles across its halls, courtyards and gardens, and is still the Spanish royal family's residence when they visit Seville.
