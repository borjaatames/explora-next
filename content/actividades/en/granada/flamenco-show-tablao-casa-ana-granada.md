preguntasFrecuentes:
  - pregunta: "How does it differ from the Sacromonte caves?"
    respuesta: "Tablao Casa Ana is a theatre-style tablao in Granada's historic centre, not in Sacromonte. It has a curtain, dynamic lighting, immersive sound and the capacity of a conventional hall. The Sacromonte caves (Los Amayas, La Faraona, Los Tarantos) are caves carved into the hillside, with no stage and no amplification. Casa Ana wins on technical production and seating comfort; the caves win on authenticity and closeness to the artists. If you have limited time and don't want to walk up to Sacromonte, Casa Ana is the most comfortable central option."
  - pregunta: "How does it compare with La Alboreá or Teatro Flamenco Granada?"
    respuesta: "All three are central tablaos. La Alboreá has MANY more reviews (5,480 versus 435 for Casa Ana) but both share the same format. Teatro Flamenco Granada is a larger theatre hall with 5-language presentations and ticket options with drinks or VIP. Casa Ana is the most intimate of the three: smaller capacity, no complicated ticket options, just the pure show."
  - pregunta: "How much does a drink cost at the tablao?"
    respuesta: "Drinks are not included and are ordered at the bar. Budget €3-5 for a glass of wine or beer, €8-12 for a spirit. There's no obligation to consume."
  - pregunta: "Is it suitable for children?"
    respuesta: "Only from age 6 (operator restriction). Not an option for younger children. From age 6 the format is manageable (seated hall, 1 hour duration) but bear in mind there's clapping, foot-stomping and loud singing."
  - pregunta: "Is there parking nearby?"
    respuesta: "The tablao is in the pedestrian historic centre. The closest options are the public car parks at Plaza San Agustín, Puerta Real or La Caleta — all 5-10 minutes' walk. Better to get around by taxi or on foot from your accommodation if you're staying in the centre."
