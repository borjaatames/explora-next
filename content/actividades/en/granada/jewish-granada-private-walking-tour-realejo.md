## What the tour looks like

You meet your guide at **Plaza Isabel la Católica** and for 2 hours you walk through the **Realejo**, Granada’s old Jewish quarter. The guide explains the **Sephardic legacy**, the places linked to Jewish life in medieval Granada and how the city changed after 1492, with stops at **viewpoints** looking up to the Alhambra.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €300 for 1 or 2 people and €440 for 4. It is an outdoor walk.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone interested in Spain’s Jewish history.
- Travellers who have seen the Alhambra and want another side of Granada.
