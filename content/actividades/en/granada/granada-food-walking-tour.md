## What you'll taste

- **Ham**: Granada serrano and Iberian, and how it's cured in the Alpujarra villages.
- **Wines** from Granada province and tinto de verano.
- **Traditional cooking** at the Chikito restaurant, a meeting place for artists and poets in the early 1900s.

## How it works

You meet the guide near Puerta Real and walk through central Granada for 3.5 hours. You stop at family businesses, tapas bars, one of the city's oldest taverns and gourmet shops, and between stops the guide explains the Sephardic, Berber, Nasrid and Roman influences on Granada's cuisine.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Food lovers** who want to understand what they're eating.
- First-time visitors who want to get their bearings in the centre.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers to tapas-hop on their own: in Granada the tapa comes free with your drink.
