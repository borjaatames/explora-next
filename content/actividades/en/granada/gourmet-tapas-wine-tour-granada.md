## The evening

Three hours through **four bars** in Granada, each with its own style: old taverns, neighbourhood bars and tucked-away bodegas. A local guide leads a small group of up to 11.

## Food and drink

**Ten tapas and four drinks** across the evening, enough for dinner. Along the way you learn how Granada's tapas custom differs from the rest of Spain.

## Who it's for

Travellers who want to eat like locals and meet people. It runs in English and is not suitable for vegans.
