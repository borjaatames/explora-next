## How the visit works

About **15 minutes from Granada**, inside the **Sierra Nevada Natural Park**, this traditional mill produces oil at over **900 metres**.

You start with a **guided walk through the olive grove**, covering the story of the olive since the Greeks introduced it. Then you go into the **mill** to see how **extra virgin olive oil** is made and sold, a craft handed down through generations. You finish with a **tasting** to tell the oils we use apart.

## Practicalities

You're picked up from your accommodation in Granada at the agreed time and dropped back afterwards. Everything is private. Bookings are **from 2 people** (up to 7).

## Who it's for

- Food lovers and anyone who cares about good olive oil.
- Families looking for a relaxed outdoor activity.
- Anyone with half a day free who doesn't want to stray far from Granada.
