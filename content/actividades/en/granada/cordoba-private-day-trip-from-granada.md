## How the day goes

You're picked up from your hotel in Granada and travel in comfort to **Córdoba**. There you enjoy a **2½-hour private guided tour** with an expert local guide: the World Heritage **Mosque-Cathedral**, tickets included, and the **Jewish Quarter**, with its narrow lanes and historic synagogue.

Then you have **2½ hours of free time** for lunch at an Andalusian restaurant, craft shopping or more wandering. At the agreed time, it's back to Granada.

## How it works

Transport and guide are **private**. The **price is per group** and it can be booked for one. Food and drinks aren't included.

## Who it's for

- Anyone based in Granada who wants to see Córdoba in a day.
- Families or groups of 3-4.
- Travellers who like a mix of guided visit and free time.
