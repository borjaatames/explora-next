## The route

A walk through **Granada's three oldest quarters**, where its cultures meet:

- The **historic centre**, Christian Granada.
- The **Albaicín**, the Moorish quarter.
- **Sacromonte**, the Roma quarter.

It starts in **Plaza Nueva** and is entirely on foot.

## Options

Choose **2 hours** or **3 hours**. The **price is per group**, not per person, and it can be booked for one. The guide speaks **English, Spanish, German, Italian, French or Portuguese**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone short on time in Granada who wants the big picture.
- Couples and solo travellers: the per-group price is great value.
- Travellers who'd like a guide in German, Italian, French or Portuguese.
