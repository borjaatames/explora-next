## What you'll see

A **private 2.5-hour tapas tour** around central Granada. You stop at **three traditional bars** and at each one you try **a tapa with a drink**. The guide, a food specialist, explains recipes handed down from Roman and Moorish times to today, and on the way you pass the **main monuments of the centre**.

## How it works

You meet the guide at **Plaza del Carmen**, in front of City Hall. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 9, and includes the **3 tapas and 3 drinks**. If you have any allergies, mention them when booking.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to find the classic tapas bars without wasting time searching.
- Families, couples and groups of friends who prefer a guide just for them.
- As an evening plan after visiting the Alhambra.
