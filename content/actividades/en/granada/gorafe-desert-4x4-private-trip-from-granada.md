## What you'll see

The **Gorafe desert** looks like the seabed it was millions of years ago. The **4x4** route passes the **devil's fillets**, the **gypsum kilns**, **Cerro de la Bandera** and the **fairy chimneys**, and stops at the **Colorados viewpoint**. It also takes in the **Caballo ravine**, the **Toril irrigation channel** and Gorafe's **angular unconformity**, with the chance to see the **Desert House**.

Along the way you learn about the geological, archaeological, ecological and cultural value of the **Granada Geopark**.

## How it works

You're picked up in Granada and both transfer and route are private. There's a **cold breakfast** during the tour. Bookings are **from 2 people** (up to 7), **Monday to Saturday**. Minimum age 8.

## Who it's for

- Anyone after scenery and nature off the beaten track.
- Families with children aged 8 and over.
- Photographers: the ravine colours change a lot with the light.
