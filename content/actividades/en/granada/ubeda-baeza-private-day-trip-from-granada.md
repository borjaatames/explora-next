## How the day goes

In **Úbeda** you cover the essentials: the **Sacra Capilla del Salvador**, built by Francisco de los Cobos as his pantheon; the **Vázquez de Molina palace**, whose courtyard leads to the **Town Hall square**; **Plaza 1º de Mayo**; the **basilica of Santa María de los Reales Alcázares**; the **Bishop's Prison** and the **Marquis of Mancera palace**.

After lunch, **Baeza**: the **old butchers' hall**, the **Civil Court and Notaries' building**, the **Puerta de Jaén**, the **old university** with its great hall and **Antonio Machado's classroom**, and the **cathedral**, with the Puerta de la Luna and Puerta del Perdón.

## How it works

Transfers, guide and tickets are private and included. Bookings are **from 2 people** (up to 7). Lunch is extra.

## Who it's for

- Anyone interested in Renaissance art and architecture.
- Visitors who know Granada and want a less obvious day trip.
- Groups of 3 or 4, for whom the price per head drops a lot.
