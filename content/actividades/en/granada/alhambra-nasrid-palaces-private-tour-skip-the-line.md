## What you'll see

A **private tour of about 3 hours** around the whole Alhambra. It starts at the **Generalife**, the sultan's retreat with its orchards and gardens. It continues to the **Palace of Charles V**, a Christian palace in the heart of the Alhambra, and the **Alcazaba**, the military quarter, with probably the best views of Granada. It ends in the **Nasrid Palaces**, the centre of power of the kingdom.

## How it works

You meet the guide at **La Mimbre restaurant**, next to the Alhambra entrance. The **skip-the-line tickets** are already included. The price is **per group**: a single traveller can book, and for 1 or 2 people it costs between €278 and €360 depending on the date. **The booking can't be cancelled.** Bring your original passport or ID card.

## Who I recommend it for

- Solo travellers or couples who want a private guide at a good price.
- Anyone who prefers the tour in Italian, French or Portuguese.
- Travellers with fixed dates, since cancellations are not accepted.
