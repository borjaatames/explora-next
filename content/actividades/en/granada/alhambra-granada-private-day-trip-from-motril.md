## What the day looks like

You are picked up in Motril, including at the port if you arrive by cruise ship, and drive up to **Granada**. An official guide meets you for a 3-hour private visit to the **Alhambra**: the **Nasrid Palaces**, the **Generalife** gardens and the **Alcazaba**. Then you have about 3 hours free for lunch and a stroll in the centre or the Albaicín before heading back to Motril.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €760 for 1 or 2 people and €1040 for 4. The driver picks you up at your accommodation in Motril and takes you back there. Lunch is not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Cruise passengers calling at Motril.
- Travellers on the Costa Tropical who want the Alhambra without worrying about tickets.
