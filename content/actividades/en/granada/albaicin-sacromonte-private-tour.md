## The route

You start at the **Mirador de San Cristóbal** and walk down through the **Albaicín**, the quarter that predates Nasrid Granada: narrow lanes, whitewashed houses, fountains and cisterns. One of the stops is the **Mirador de San Nicolás**, with the Alhambra right opposite.

From there you carry on to **Sacromonte**, where the first Roma to arrive in Spain settled in cave houses, the world Federico García Lorca captured in his *Romancero gitano*.

## How it works

It's a **private tour**: the guide is just for your party. Bookings are **from 2 people** (up to 10) and the price per person falls the more of you there are. Transport isn't included: you meet at the viewpoint.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors who want to see more of Granada than the Alhambra.
- Families and groups of friends: from 4 people the price per head drops a lot.
- Not for anyone with mobility issues: it's slopes and cobbles all the way.
