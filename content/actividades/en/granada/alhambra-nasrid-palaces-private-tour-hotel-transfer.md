## What you'll see

A **private 3-hour tour** of the whole Alhambra complex. It starts at the **Generalife**, the Nasrid kings' summer palace, and its gardens, where water is everywhere. It continues to the **Palace of Charles V**, which the emperor had built after his wedding in Seville in 1526, and ends in the **Nasrid Palaces**: the Mexuar, the Comares Palace and the Palace of the Lions.

## How it works

A **private transfer** picks you up at your hotel or apartment and, at the end, takes you back or to the city centre. The **tickets, Nasrid Palaces included**, are already bought. The exact time depends on the Nasrid Palaces slot assigned by the Alhambra. Bring your **original passport or ID card**. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2** and a maximum of 9.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the whole Alhambra without worrying about tickets or transport.
- Families and small groups who prefer a guide just for them.
- Travellers short on time who want to make every minute at the monument count.
