## How the day goes

Your guide and driver pick you up in Granada. On the way they tell you about Andalusia's **olive trees** and the **tropical coast**, where avocados, mangoes and papayas grow.

The first stop is the **Nerja Caves**, tickets included: about **45 minutes** among the formations. Then the views from the **Balcón de Europa**, and finally a **guided tour of Frigiliana**, a white village of steep lanes.

## How it works

Guide and driver are **private** all day. The **price is per group** and it can be booked for one. Food and drinks are extra.

## Who it's for

- Families or groups who want to see the coast without hiring a car.
- Anyone based in Granada after a day of sea and white villages.
- Anyone into geology: the Nerja Caves are impressive.
