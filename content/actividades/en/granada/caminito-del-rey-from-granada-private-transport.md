## How the day goes

The driver picks you up from your hotel in Granada and takes you through the Andalusian countryside to the **Caminito del Rey**, the walkway path hanging **100 metres** above the Gaitanes gorge. There you walk the route with a **guide in a group**, **tickets included**. Then it's back to Granada in the same private vehicle.

## How it works

**Transport is private** and the **price is per group**, even for one person. On the trail the guide is **shared**. Minimum age 8.

## Who it's for

- Anyone based in Granada without a car.
- Families or groups of 3-4, for whom the per-group price works out better.
- Anyone who wants guaranteed tickets without battling the official website.
