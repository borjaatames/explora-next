## The route

A walk through the history of **Granada's Sephardic Jews**, who in Moorish times rose to be viziers and poets to the Zirid and Nasrid sultans. It starts at the defensive **Torres Bermejas** and goes on to the **Puerta del Sol** wash-house, where poets and rabbis used to meet.

You head down the **Cuesta del Realejo**, past the church of **Santo Domingo**, founded by the Catholic Monarchs, and into the **Cuarto Real de Santo Domingo**, an Almohad palace. Then **Calle San Matías**, site of the Great Synagogue, and **Plaza del Padre Suárez**, with its old baths and the rabbi's house. You finish at the statue of **Ibn Tibbon**, the 11th-century Jewish poet and translator.

## How it works

It's a **private tour** for your party only, bookable **from 2 people** (up to 8). Under-14s go free. Entry to the Cuarto Real is included.

## Who it's for

- Anyone interested in Jewish and Sephardic history.
- Visitors who've done the Alhambra and want a different quarter with a different story.
- Families with under-14s, who go free.
