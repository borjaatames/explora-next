## What you'll see

The route covers the full Alhambra complex with fast entry, but what really defines this visit is that you can choose how to do it.

- **Nasrid Palaces:** the heart of the complex, with its assigned time slot. The royal halls and the Nasrid plasterwork.
- **Alcazaba:** the military fortress and the oldest part, with views over Granada.
- **Generalife:** the summer palace and its water gardens.
- **Palace of Charles V:** the Renaissance building within the grounds, included in the ticket.
- **Formats to choose from:** from the standard tour to a small group, a private tour or a workshop on the fragrances of Al-Andalus.

## How it works

The meeting point is always **inside the Alhambra grounds**, but the exact location depends on the format you book — there are up to seven, across languages, group sizes and the themed workshop. The operator sends the details before the tour. **Arrive 15 minutes early.**

The **duration ranges from 3 to 4.5 hours** depending on the option. The route runs with an **expert guide**, in Spanish, English, German, French or Italian, and groups of more than 6 people carry **headsets**. Hotel pickup is not in the base price, but it's available as an add-on on some formats.

Alhambra entry is **nominative** and limited: you must give passport details at booking and bring the original on the day of the visit. The time can be adjusted for operational reasons, always with advance notice.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If you need a less common language: it's one of the few options with German and Italian in addition to Spanish, English and French.
- If you want to choose the format — group, small group or private — within a single operator.
- If you're interested in a slightly different experience, like the workshop on the fragrances of Al-Andalus.
- If you want the option of hotel pickup as an add-on.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you're after the best value for money. At €96 it's one of the most expensive; the [entry with guided tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-entry-and-guided-tour) covers the essentials for much less.
- If a long-established operator reassures you: there are 432 reviews here, against the thousands of other visits in the comparison.
- If you need guaranteed wheelchair accessibility: the operator does not specify it.
- If you want a short, simple visit. The variable duration and the seven formats may be more than you need.
