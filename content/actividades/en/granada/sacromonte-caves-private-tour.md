## What you'll see

Everyone knows the Sacromonte caves where flamenco is performed. This tour goes elsewhere: the **caves of San Miguel and Sacromonte** where the way of life has barely changed. **Self-sufficient caves**, without running water or electricity as we know it, in the middle of the city.

The guide explains **how a cave is dug**, what **daily life** is like, their **legal status**, the residents' demands and their run-ins with the police. The tour includes **access to a private cave**, and views over Granada follow you all the way.

## How it works

It starts in **Plaza Nueva** and is on foot, with **lots of uphill on dirt paths**. The **price is per group** and it can be booked for one. Minimum age 6.

## Who it's for

- Anyone looking for a less touristy, more real Granada.
- Travellers curious about alternative ways of life.
- Not for people with back or heart problems, according to the operator.
