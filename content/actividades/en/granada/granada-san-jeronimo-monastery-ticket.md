## What you'll see

The **Catholic Monarchs** founded the monastery in 1492, and building on its current site began in 1503. Hieronymite monks lived here until the 1835 confiscations and, since 1977, a community of **Hieronymite nuns**.

The route takes in the **Compás**, the **processional cloister**, the refectory, the "de profundis" hall, the chapter rooms, the sacristy (with the **Niño Jesús de las Batallas**) and the **church**, with a facade by **Diego de Siloé**.

## How it works

Ticket with an audio guide on your phone. It is non-refundable.
