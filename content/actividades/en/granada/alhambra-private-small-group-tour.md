## What you'll see

The Alhambra is a complete palace-city, and this visit covers it in around 3 hours with a guide, with entry to the whole complex guaranteed and skip-the-line. The distinctive feature is the format: you choose between a small group or private with the same booking.

- **Palace of Charles V:** the circular Renaissance building next to the Alhambra, the first stop on the route.
- **Nasrid Palaces:** the set of three palaces — the Mexuar, the Comares Palace and the Palace of the Lions, with the Court of the Lions — residence of the kings of Granada.
- **Generalife:** the rural estate of the Nasrid sultans on the Cerro del Sol, with its gardens and historic irrigation system.
- **Guaranteed access:** entry to the complex, which has a limited daily quota, is secured and skip-the-line.

## How it works

The meeting point is the **Welcome Visitor Center**, on Paseo de la Sabica, next to the Hotel Guadalupe. If the operator needs to adjust the start time, it notifies the traveller the day before. Arrive 15 minutes early.

With the same booking you choose between a **small group** (up to 15 people, sharing a guide) or **private** (guide just for your group). The route lasts around **3 hours** — between 2.5 and 3 depending on the group's pace — and passes through the Palace of Charles V, the Nasrid Palaces and the Generalife. It does **not include headsets**: the guide explains live.

Two important things: the operator requires the **full name and passport details of every participant at booking** — without them the Alhambra can deny access — and the booking is **non-refundable**.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If you want to decide between a small group or private without switching product.
- If the guaranteed, skip-the-line access to the whole complex matters to you.
- If you are travelling as a group and the private option works out worthwhile for the pace and closeness.
- If your plans are fixed and you don't need to be able to cancel.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If your plans could change. The booking is non-refundable; check the options with free cancellation in the comparison, like the [private tour with an Arabic-speaking guide](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-private-tour-arabic-guide).
- If not having headsets bothers you. This visit does not include them; other options in the comparison do.
- If on the group option you expected something very small: it can reach 15 people.
- If you don't want to provide passport details at booking: here it is mandatory.
