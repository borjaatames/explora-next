## What you'll see

- The **Nasrid Palaces**: the Mexuar, the Comares Palace and the Court of the Lions.
- The **Alcazaba**, the military fortress, with views over the Albaicín.
- The gardens and fountains of the **Generalife**.
- The **Palace of Charles V**.

## How it works

You meet Crown Tours staff by the Generalife ticket office. The licensed guide leads the group with headsets, and you enter the Nasrid Palaces at your assigned time.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to see **the whole Alhambra with commentary** without worrying about tickets.

## Who I don't recommend it to

- Travellers without a fixed date: only 50% is refunded.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
