## What you'll see

The **Cathedral of the Incarnation** is one of the great works of the **Spanish Renaissance**: architecture and religious art your guide explains without rushing. Next door, the **Gothic Royal Chapel** holds the tombs of **Isabella I of Castile and Ferdinand II of Aragon**, the Catholic Monarchs, and tells their story and that of their age.

## How it works

You meet a **private official guide** in **Plaza Isabel la Católica**. The visit lasts **2 hours** and **full tickets** to both monuments are included. The **price is per group** and it can be booked for one. Hotel pickup isn't included.

## Who it's for

- Fans of Spanish history and art.
- Couples and solo travellers: the per-group price works out well.
- Anyone who's seen the Alhambra and wants to understand Christian Granada.
