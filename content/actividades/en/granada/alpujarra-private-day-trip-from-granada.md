## How the day goes

You're picked up from your accommodation in Granada and spend the day in the **Alpujarra**, on the southern side of Sierra Nevada, with a guide who explains the area's historical role, its **traditions and customs** and what sets it apart from the rest of Andalusia. Entry to the **Casa Alpujarreña Museum** is included.

## How it works

It's **private**: the car and guide are just for your party, and the route adapts to your interests. To **add a village** or focus on something specific, contact the operator. The **price is per group** and it can be booked for one person. Food and drinks aren't included.

## Who it's for

- Families or groups of 3-4 who want to go at their own pace.
- Anyone who prefers a tailored trip to a coach tour.
- Not for people with heart conditions or pregnant travellers, according to the operator.
