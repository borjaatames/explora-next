## What you'll see

- The lanes and little squares of the **Albaicín**.
- **Sacromonte** and its cave houses.
- **Viewpoints** looking onto the Alhambra and Generalife.

## How it works

You meet at Plaza de los Cuchilleros, get your e-bike and helmet, and ride up with the guide, stopping at the viewpoints. It takes about two hours.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to see the Albaicín and Sacromonte **without struggling up the hills**.

## Who I don't recommend it to

- People who aren't confident on a bike: there are cobbles and slopes.
