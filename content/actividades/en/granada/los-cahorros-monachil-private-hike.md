## The hike

The driver picks you up from your hotel and in **half an hour** you're in **Monachil**, at the foot of Sierra Nevada. From there, with your private guide, you walk **Los Cahorros**: **suspension bridges** over the water, rock formations and gorges.

The route usually takes **about 4 hours** and covers **around 10 km**, but it can be adapted to your pace and preferences.

## How it works

Transport and guide are **private**. The **price is per group** and it can be booked for one. **It can't be cancelled.** Minimum age 6.

## Who it's for

- Families with children aged 6+ who are comfortable walking.
- Anyone wanting a half-day nature outing from Granada.
- Not for people with back problems or pregnant travellers, according to the operator.
