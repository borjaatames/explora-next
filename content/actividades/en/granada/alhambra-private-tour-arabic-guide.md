## What you'll see

The Alhambra is covered in Arabic inscriptions: mottoes, verses and dedications that most visitors see as decoration. This private tour translates them. The official guide speaks classical Arabic and reads out what the walls and ceilings say as you walk through the monument.

- **Nasrid Palaces:** the full visit of the palaces, with the translation of the inscriptions from Arabic into the group's language.
- **Alcazaba:** the military fortress from which the royal guard operated, one of the oldest parts of the complex.
- **Palace of Charles V:** the Renaissance building, visited inside and out.
- **Generalife:** the summer palace of the sultans, with the microclimate they created through gardens and water.

## How it works

The meeting point is the **main entrance of the Alhambra**, opposite the ticket offices and the wall map, on Paseo del Generalife. Start times are approximate because they depend on the availability of Nasrid Palaces tickets; the operator sometimes adjusts the schedule to fit the access slot. Arrive 15 minutes early.

It is a **strictly private tour**: only the booker's group takes part, with the guide dedicated to you. The duration is **2.5 to 3 hours** depending on the group's pace. The route covers the Nasrid Palaces, the Alcazaba, the Palace of Charles V and the Generalife.

The operator is very strict about documentation: you must bring the **ID document in physical form** — copies and digital documents are not accepted — and the **name on the booking must match exactly** the one on the document.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If you are genuinely interested in Nasrid art and want to understand what the inscriptions say, not just see them.
- If you want the Alhambra privately, with the guide dedicated to your group alone.
- If being able to cancel matters to you: it is the private Viator Alhambra option with free cancellation 24h.
- If you are travelling as a group and the per-person price of the private tour works out worthwhile.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you are travelling as a pair on a tight budget. The per-person price of a private tour is high in small groups; consider a [small-group option](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-private-small-group-tour).
- If you can only present the document digitally or as a copy: here the physical original is required, no exceptions.
- If the inscriptions part doesn't interest you: you are paying for a differentiating value you won't use.
- If you need a fixed, set schedule: start times are approximate and depend on the Nasrid Palaces quota.
