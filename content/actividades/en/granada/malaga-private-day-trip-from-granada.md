## How the day goes

You're picked up from your accommodation in Granada and driven to **Málaga**, capital of the Costa del Sol, for a full guided tour of its main sights.

First the **cathedral**, with its 84-metre north tower and the unfinished south tower that earned it the nickname **«La Manquita»**; you go inside. Then the **Alcazaba**, the Moorish citadel at the foot of Gibralfaro, beside the Roman Theatre. A walk through the **old town** and the **former Jewish quarter** leads to a local restaurant for lunch.

In the afternoon, the **Museo Picasso Málaga**, with works from every stage of the artist, who was born in the city in 1881.

## How it works

Private transport and guide; tickets to the cathedral and Alcazaba included. Bookings are **from 2 people** (up to 7). Lunch is extra. You can ask to **finish in Málaga** instead of returning to Granada.

## Who it's for

- Anyone based in Granada who wants to see Málaga in a day.
- Travellers heading on to the coast or flying out of Málaga.
- Picasso fans.
