## What you will see

### Alcazaba
The oldest part of the Alhambra, a military fortress with the **Torre de la Vela**, from which you can see the Albaicín, Sacromonte and the Granada plain (vega).

### Nasrid Palaces
The heart of the complex: the **Mexuar**, the **Palace of Comares** with the Court of the Myrtles and the **Palace of the Lions**, with its stucco work, muqarnas domes and the fountain of the twelve lions.

### Generalife
The sultans' country estate, with the **Patio de la Acequia** and terraced gardens overlooking the Alhambra.

## Tip

Bring the same ID document you booked with: it is checked at every entrance. In summer, the early-morning tours are the most pleasant.
