## What you will see

The **Basílica de San Juan de Dios** (18th century) was built to house the remains of the founder of the Hospitaller Order and is one of the high points of Spanish **Baroque**: gilded altarpieces, paintings and sculptures covering every corner.

The most impressive part is the **camarín**, behind the high altar, which holds the urn of **San Juan de Dios**, co-patron saint of Granada. The audio guide explains the work of artists such as **Tomás Ferrer** and **Atanasio Bocanegra** and the story of the saint and his charitable work.
