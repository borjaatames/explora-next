## How the day goes

You're picked up from your accommodation in Granada and, on the way to the coast, stop at **Señorío de Nevada**, one of the leading wineries in eastern Andalusia. The visit, with a **wine tasting**, lasts about **an hour and a half**.

Then you carry on to **Almuñécar**, on the **Costa Tropical**, for a private guided tour of the **old town**, **San Miguel castle** and the **botanical garden**. There's **free time** for lunch and shopping before returning to Granada in the afternoon.

## How it works

Everything is private: transport, guide and winery visit. Bookings are **from 2 people** (up to 7). Lunch isn't included.

## Who it's for

- Couples or small groups who enjoy wine.
- Anyone wanting to see Granada's coast without hiring a car.
- Travellers after a gentler day between monument visits.
