## What you'll see

**Granada's Carthusian monastery** was founded in 1516 and sums up three centuries of architecture, from late Gothic to the most lavish Baroque. The visit covers:

- The **church**, with altarpieces by **Sánchez Cotán**, an elliptical vault and a gilded baldachin.
- The **Sancta Sanctorum**, with frescoes by **Antonio Palomino**.
- The **sacristy**, all marble and stucco, a high point of Spanish Baroque.
- The refectory, the chapter house and the **cloister**.

## How it works

Ticket with an audio guide on your phone. It is non-refundable and flash photography is not allowed.
