## How the day goes

You're picked up from your hotel in Granada and travel about **2 hours** in a private air-conditioned vehicle to **Ronda**, perched over its gorge, birthplace of bullfighting and with a food scene of its own.

## Options

- **Without a guide:** explore Ronda at your own pace, with the operator's tips on what to see, where to eat and lesser-known corners.
- **With a guide:** on top of the transport, a **2-hour private guided tour** of the town.

Either way, the **price is per group** and it can be booked for one. **It can't be cancelled.**

## Who it's for

- Anyone wanting to see Ronda from Granada without driving or relying on buses.
- Groups of 3 or 4, for whom the per-group price works out better.
- Not for anyone unsure of their dates, as it's non-refundable.
