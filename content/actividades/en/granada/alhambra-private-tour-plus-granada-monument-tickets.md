## What's included

A **private tour of the Alhambra** with tickets to every area of the monument, guided just for your party. Afterwards there's plenty of time to carry on alone, because the tour also includes **unguided tickets** to:

- Granada **Cathedral**.
- The **Royal Chapel**, burial place of the Catholic Monarchs.
- **San Jerónimo Monastery**.
- **Sacromonte Abbey**.
- The **Science Park**.

## How it works

You meet the guide at **La Mimbre restaurant**, by the Alhambra entrance. The operator sends the other tickets by message to use before or after. The **price is per group** and it can be booked for one person. **It can't be cancelled.**

## Who it's for

- Anyone wanting the Alhambra with a guide and the rest of Granada at their own pace.
- Couples and solo travellers: the per-group price works out well.
- Not for anyone unsure of their dates, as it's non-refundable.
