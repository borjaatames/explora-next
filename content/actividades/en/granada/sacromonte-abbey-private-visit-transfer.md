## What you'll see

In 1595, Roman-era kilns were found on Mount Ilipulitano containing remains and a tombstone attributing them to **Saint Cecilius**, first bishop of Iliberis (today's Granada). The hill later became known as **Sacromonte**, the sacred mount, and the abbey was built there.

The visit takes in the **Holy Caves**, where the lead books and the martyr's remains were found; the **cloister**; the **collegiate church**; the **Venancio Blanco courtyard**; the **Patio of the Stars** and the **permanent exhibition**, with a valuable documentary legacy.

## How it works

You're picked up from your **hotel or apartment** in Granada, do the private visit with tickets included and return in the same transfer. Bookings are **from 2 people** (up to 7).

## Who it's for

- Visitors who've seen Granada's essentials and want something different.
- Anyone interested in religious history and legends.
- Small groups who'd rather not walk up Sacromonte.
