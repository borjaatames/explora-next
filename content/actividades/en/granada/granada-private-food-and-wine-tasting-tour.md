## How the tour works

A local food expert picks you up from your hotel and takes you to **2 or 3 of Granada's most iconic bars**. Along the way you'll have **up to 4 drinks** —local wines, house vermouths, regional beer or alcohol-free options— and **3 or 4 different tapas** and regional dishes each, chosen to give you a real taste of local cooking. It ends with a **local dessert**.

At each stop, your guide shares the story behind what you're eating and drinking.

## Practicalities

It's **private** and lasts **3 hours**. The **price is per group**, all-inclusive, and it can be booked for one.

## Who it's for

- Couples or small groups who want to eat well without hunting for bars.
- Vermouth and wine lovers.
- New arrivals who want their bearings for dinners in the days ahead.
