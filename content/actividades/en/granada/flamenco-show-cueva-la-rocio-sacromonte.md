## What you'll see

A **1.5-hour flamenco show** at **Cueva La Rocío**, one of the caves on Camino del Sacromonte. It's **pure, traditional** flamenco: dance, song and guitar in a whitewashed cave where the audience sits just a few metres from the artists.

## How it works

You make your own way to the cave at **Camino del Sacromonte 70**. The price, **€35 per person**, includes **one drink**. Dinner isn't included: the supplier recommends eating beforehand, although you can ask about a table before the show. Transfers aren't included either.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see flamenco in the atmosphere of the Sacromonte caves.
- Couples and solo travellers: it's booked per person, with no minimum.
- Anyone looking for good-value evening plans.
