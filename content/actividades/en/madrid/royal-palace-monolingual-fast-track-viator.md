## What you'll see

1.5-hour tour of the Royal Palace with Amigo Tours Spain. The proposition: monolingual tour (Spanish only or English only) with fast track to the palace.

- **Plaza de Isabel II and Plaza de Oriente:** 5-minute introductory walk before entering.
- **Throne Room:** royal canopy, lions and Tiépolo's vault.
- **Royal Armoury:** collection of armours, swords and items.
- **Main halls:** furniture, tapestries, Spanish marble, ornate mahogany.
- **History of the monarchy:** the guide's narrative on Spanish dynasties.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Isabel II Statue at Plaza de Isabel II, 4 — in front of the Teatro Real. Metro Ópera (lines 2/5) is right there. **The guide carries an «Amigo Tours» sign**.

**Arrive 10-15 minutes early**. The Viator confirmation does NOT work for direct palace entry — meeting the guide is mandatory.

The tour lasts **1.5 hours** in a group of up to 30 people with included radio guide. **It's monolingual**: choose Spanish or English at booking.

Some reviews mention **schedule changes without warning**. Check your email the night before and confirm with the operator if in doubt.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone valuing 100% tour in their language without bilingual mixing.
- Anyone tight on time who prefers a shorter tour (1.5 h vs 2 h).
- Anyone needing confirmed wheelchair accessibility, stroller access or service animal.
- Anyone with flexible time who can wait for the guide without stress.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone valuing maximum operational reliability: there are complaints about schedule changes here.
- Anyone preferring the highest palace rating: Golden Tour Guide (4.7-4.8) is more conservative.
- Anyone with a very tight schedule: if the guide doesn't show, there's no quick fix.
- Anyone wanting a simultaneous bilingual tour: this one is monolingual.
