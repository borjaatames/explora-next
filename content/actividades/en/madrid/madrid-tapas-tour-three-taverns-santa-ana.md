## The route

You meet at 5 pm in **Plaza de Santa Ana** and walk to **three taverns** in the centre, each with its own character:

- A tavern styled on Ancient Greece.
- A restaurant open for **over 100 years** that serves a dish unique to the city.
- A modern neighbourhood tavern mostly frequented by locals.

Across the three you try **paella, Spanish omelette, garlic prawns, croquetas, patatas bravas and meatballs**, with one drink of your choice (beer, wine, sangria or tinto de verano), a soft drink and water.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors who want to try the classics.
- Couples or small groups comfortable in English.
- Not vegans: there are no vegan tapas.
