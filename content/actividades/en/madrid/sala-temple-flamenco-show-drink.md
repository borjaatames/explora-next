## What you will see

**Sala Temple** is a small tablao, with about **40 seats**, on Cuesta de San Vicente, between the Royal Palace and Príncipe Pío. Here the flamenco is **improvised**: the artists do not follow a fixed choreography and every show is different.

The ticket includes a drink and, if you like, you can order from the menu of **traditional Spanish dishes**. A good way to end the evening after watching the sunset at the **Templo de Debod**, 10 minutes away.
