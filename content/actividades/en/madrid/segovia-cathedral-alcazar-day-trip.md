## What you'll see

Segovia packs into its historic centre a Roman aqueduct almost 2,000 years old, a Gothic cathedral and an alcázar that looks like something out of a fairy tale. This half-day trip covers it with a local guide from Madrid, with the option to enter the Cathedral and the Alcázar.

- **Aqueduct of Segovia:** the great Roman engineering work, raised without mortar, the city's symbol.
- **Casa de los Picos and Plaza Mayor:** the walking route climbs through the historic streets, passing the Casa de los Picos — with its 617 granite points on the facade — up to the Plaza Mayor.
- **Segovia Cathedral:** one of the last Gothic cathedrals in Spain, on the Plaza Mayor. Visitable inside depending on the option you book.
- **Alcázar of Segovia:** the castle on the crag at the meeting of two rivers, which inspired Disney's Snow White castle. Entry included depending on the option.

## How it works

The meeting point is the **Julià Travel office at calle San Nicolás, 15**, next to Plaza de Ramales. **Arrive at least 15 minutes early**: one review warns of missing the tour for arriving late. The trip ends at **Plaza de Oriente**, in the underground car park (Level -2).

The journey is by air-conditioned coach and the visit is done with a **local guide and an audio-guide system**. The total duration is around **5 hours**, including travel; depending on the group size it can extend by up to 30 minutes.

The key thing about the format: entries to the **Cathedral and the Alcázar are options**. There are versions with Alcázar entry, with a guided visit to the Cathedral, with Cathedral entry on your own, or without entries. Check carefully which version you book.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If Segovia is your priority and you'd rather give it half a day than link it with another city.
- If you want an affordable trip with a local guide.
- If you want to be able to choose whether or not to go into the Cathedral and the Alcázar.
- If a half-day trip that leaves the rest of your day free suits you.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you assume you get into the Cathedral and the Alcázar without checking the option. Here the entries are extras; confirm the version carefully.
- If you tend to arrive at the last minute: punctuality is strict and there are reviews from travellers who lost the tour.
- If your language is English and you want a flawless commentary: one review notes the guide's level as improvable.
- If mobility is an issue. The Segovia visit is on foot through the historic centre, with slopes and cobblestones.
