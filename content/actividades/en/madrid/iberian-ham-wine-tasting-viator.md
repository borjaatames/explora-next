## What you'll see

A 2.5-hour guided tasting of Iberian ham, cured meats, Spanish D.O. cheeses and selected wines. Operated by Madrid Tapas Trip, hosted by Brian (owner and guide).

- **Iberian hams:** multiple categories (cebo, cebo de campo, occasionally bellota).
- **Denominaciones:** Guijuelo, Jabugo, Los Pedroches — how to tell them apart.
- **Cured meats:** chorizo, lomo, salchichón.
- **Spanish D.O. cheeses:** Manchego, Cabrales, Idiazábal, etc.
- **Wine pairing:** Rioja, Ribera del Duero, occasionally a white or a fino from Jerez.

**Meeting point:** confirmed in booking email (gourmet shop or tasting space, central Madrid). **Arrive 10 minutes early.**
