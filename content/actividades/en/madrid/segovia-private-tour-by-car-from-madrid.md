## What you'll see

The **Roman aqueduct**, the **cathedral**, Spain's last Gothic cathedral, and the **Alcázar**, the castle overlooking the Eresma valley. The guide adapts the route: if you prefer art or history, you spend more time on what you choose.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You leave Madrid in a private car or van with a driver and official guide. Skip-the-line tickets are included; lunch is not. It lasts **8 hours** in total.
