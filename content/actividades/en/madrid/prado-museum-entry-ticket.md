## What you will see

The Prado holds one of the finest collections of European painting in the world. With the general ticket you explore the **Villanueva Building** at your own pace, with more than 1,300 works on display:

- **Velázquez:** *Las Meninas*, *The Spinners* and *The Surrender of Breda*.
- **Goya:** the royal family portraits, *The Third of May* and the **Black Paintings**.
- **Bosch:** *The Garden of Earthly Delights*.
- **Titian, Rubens, El Greco and Dürer**, plus the **Dauphin's Treasure**.

## How it works

You buy the ticket for a specific day and carry it on your phone. Entry is through the **Jerónimos Entrance**, on the east side of the museum (in high season it may move to the Velázquez Entrance; your confirmation email will say so).

There is no guide or audio guide. The museum suggests **masterpiece routes** so you don't get lost in the galleries.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors who already know **which paintings they want to see** and prefer to go at their own pace.
- Anyone looking for the **cheapest option** without relying on the free hours, which usually have queues.
- Those who want to spend **more than 2 hours** in the museum with no group schedule.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who needs flexible dates: the ticket is **non-refundable**.
- First-time visitors who want to understand what they see: the **guided tour** is better.
- Under-18s or students aged 18-25: they enter free by getting a ticket at the museum box office.
