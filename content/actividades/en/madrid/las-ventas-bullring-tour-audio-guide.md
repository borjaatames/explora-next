## What you will see

The **Las Ventas bullring** opened in 1931, designed by José Espeliú, and is the great example of Madrid's **Neo-Mudéjar** style: exposed brick, horseshoe arches and glazed tiles. With more than 23,000 seats, it is the largest bullring in Spain.

### The route
- **Stands and boxes**, with a full view of the arena.
- **The arena**, where you can stand in the centre and see the bullring from below.
- **Bullfighters' courtyard, chapel and infirmary**, the part the public never sees during a bullfight.
- **The Puerta Grande**, through which triumphant bullfighters are carried out on the crowd's shoulders.
- **Bullfighting museum**, with traje de luces costumes, posters and portraits of historic figures.

Hemingway, Ava Gardner, Picasso and Orson Welles all sat in its stands, and the audio guide tells their stories.
