## What you'll see

A **private 3-hour tour** of the Prado Museum focused on the must-see works: **Bosch, Raphael, Titian, El Greco, Velázquez, Rubens and Goya**. The visit centres on **The Garden of Earthly Delights, Las Meninas and the Black Paintings**, and the guide also explains how art evolved over the centuries.

## How it works

Guides are **officially licensed** and most are art historians. They can **pick you up at your hotel** and tell you about the streets on the way, or you can meet at the **museum ticket office, by the Goya statue**, which the supplier recommends if your hotel isn't nearby. **Skip-the-line entry** is included. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2**.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- First-time visitors who don't want to get lost among the galleries.
- Small groups or families who'd like a guide just for themselves.
- Anyone who wants to understand the works, not just see them.
