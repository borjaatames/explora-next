## What you'll see

Half-day combined tour covering the panoramic landmarks of central Madrid + a visit to Santiago Bernabéu Stadium. Booked through Viator. Total approximate duration: 4-4.5 hours.

- **Panoramic city tour:** Plaza Mayor, Puerta del Sol, Plaza de Cibeles, Gran Vía, Royal Palace (exterior), Plaza de España, Templo de Debod.
- **Some stops with disembark:** photo opportunities at selected landmarks.
- **Bernabéu Tour:** entry to Real Madrid's stadium with museum, panoramic views, dressing rooms and benches (subject to match-day closures).
- **Transfer included:** from the centre to the stadium without taking the metro.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** confirmed in booking email. Arrive 15-20 minutes early so you don't miss the bus departure.

**Duration:** 4-4.5 hours total — city tour + transfer + Bernabéu Tour.

**Languages:** Spanish or English. Choose at booking.

**Match-day notice:** Bernabéu dressing rooms and benches close on the day and the day before the match. Check the Real Madrid calendar before picking a date.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Travellers with a single day wanting to combine centre + Bernabéu.
- Real Madrid fans who also want to orient themselves in the centre.
- Those who'd rather not take the metro to the stadium.
- Regular Viator users.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone wanting cultural depth (this is panoramic, you don't enter museums).
- Travellers with reduced mobility expecting full Bernabéu access.
- Anyone with 2 days or more: better split centre and stadium across different days.
- Anyone visiting on match day without confirming the schedule.
