## How the day works

You leave central Madrid at 10:30 am for a **historic family winery** in the countryside just outside the capital. You visit the winery and its cellar and have a **guided tasting of 3 wines**, from local grapes such as **Malvar and Tempranillo**.

Then it's on to **Chinchón**, known for its medieval square and Castilian character, where you sit down to **lunch at a traditional restaurant**: starters, main, dessert, coffee and a drink (or a selection of tapas). In the afternoon you head back to Madrid.

## Who it's for

- Couples and friends after wine, a village and a good meal in one day.
- Anyone who hasn't seen Chinchón and doesn't want to drive.
- Those looking for something more relaxed than a multi-winery route.
