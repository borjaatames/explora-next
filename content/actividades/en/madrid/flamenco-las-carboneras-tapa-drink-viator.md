## What you'll see

A 1-hour flamenco show at Tablao Las Carboneras with one drink included. The tablao sits in the basement of the former Conde de Miranda palace, in the heart of Madrid de los Austrias.

- **Fixed flamenco cuadro:** José Maldonado, Candy Román, Juan Herrera, Shin Yamasawa, among other established artists.
- **Cante, guitar and dance** in a traditional format with genuine improvisation.
- **One drink included:** a light beverage during the show.
- **Intimate capacity** with close-up view of the artists' work.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Plaza del Conde de Miranda, 1. Ópera or Sol metro 5 minutes away.

**Arrive 15-20 minutes early.** Tables are assigned by arrival order — side seats have a worse view.

**Duration:** 1-hour uninterrupted show. **The show is 100% improvised.**

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Travellers looking for intimate flamenco with one drink included.
- Couples or small groups.
- Travellers with reduced mobility (one of the few accessible tablaos).
- Regular Viator users.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Families with children under 7.
- Anyone wanting full dinner (look at show + dinner options).
- Those wanting maximum social validation — GetYourGuide has 758 reviews of the same tablao.
- Those arriving last-minute — you'll get a side seat with worse view.
