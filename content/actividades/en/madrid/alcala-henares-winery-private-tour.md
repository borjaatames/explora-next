## What you'll see

Private 6-hour trip from Madrid to Alcalá de Henares (UNESCO World Heritage, birthplace of Miguel de Cervantes) with a visit to a local winery and wine tasting. Private round-trip transport and expert guide throughout the day.

- **Alcalá de Henares (UNESCO):** 15th-16th century Renaissance university layout.
- **Cathedral of Santos Niños:** 16th-century Gothic architecture.
- **Palacete de Laredo:** 19th-century neo-Mudéjar building.
- **Calle Mayor:** the old town's main street, one of the longest arcaded streets in Spain.
- **University of Alcalá:** founded in 1499 by Cardinal Cisneros, with one of the country's most beautiful cloisters.
- **Local winery:** guided cellar visit with explanation of the production process (30 min).
- **Wine tasting:** the day closes with a tasting of local wines.

## How it works

**Pickup:** point to coordinate with the operator at confirmation (usually a central Madrid hotel). PRIVATE tour — not shared with other groups.

**Total duration:** 6 hours, including:
- 40 min bus to Alcalá
- Guided city tour
- 30 min winery visit + tasting
- 30 min bus back

**Languages:** Spanish and English. Confirm at booking.

**Base price:** around €398 for groups up to 2 people (€199 per person). In a group of 4, the individual share drops significantly.

**Hospitality policy:** student discount up to 25 (valid student card required).

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Closed groups of 4-6 people with a generous budget.
- Anyone who wants total flexibility and exclusive guide attention.
- Anyone who needs to cover Alcalá + winery in half a day without arranging transport themselves.
- Families or groups wanting a tour adapted to their pace.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Couples wanting to save (the group version costs less than half per person).
- Serious wine enthusiasts (the winery stop is only 30 min).
- Travellers who value massive validation (only 16 reviews — low volume).
- Anyone who can travel independently: Alcalá is 30 min by Cercanías RENFE for under €5.
