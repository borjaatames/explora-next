## What you'll see

A 2.5-hour PRIVATE walking tour through Madrid de los Austrias with a dedicated guide for your group. Flexible itinerary adaptable to specific interests.

- **Puerta del Sol:** kilometre zero, La Mariblanca, the bear and strawberry tree.
- **Plaza Mayor:** monumental space, Madrid life across the centuries.
- **Sobrino de Botín:** the world's oldest restaurant per Guinness.
- **Mercado de San Miguel:** iron-and-glass market architecture.
- **Plaza de la Villa:** former medieval town hall.
- **Almudena Cathedral:** façade and the story of its late construction.
- **Royal Palace:** exterior view and Plaza de Oriente.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** La Cúpula at Hotel Palace, Pl. de las Cortes 7, Centro. Sevilla metro (line 2) or Sol (lines 1/2/3) 5 minutes away.

**Arrive 5-10 minutes early.** **Duration: 2.5 hours.**

**PRIVATE tour** — just your group. Itinerary adaptable to interests (literature, architecture, gastronomy).

**Languages:** typically Spanish and English. Other languages on request.

**Does not include monument entries** — this is an exterior visit.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Closed groups (families, friends) of 4-6 people.
- Couples with generous budgets who prefer privacy.
- Travellers with specific interests requiring personalisation.
- Those valuing dedicated pace and stops at their discretion.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Solo travellers (extremely expensive per head).
- Couples on tighter budgets (better a small-group reduced tour).
- Those wanting to enter monuments (this is exterior only).
- Those valuing mass social validation: groups have more reviews.
