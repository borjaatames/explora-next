## What you'll see

In **Toledo**, **Santo Tomé church** with El Greco and the **Santa María la Blanca** synagogue; in **Segovia**, the **aqueduct** and the **Alcázar**, with entry. At midday, a **tasting of Iberian ham and Manchego cheese**.

## How it works

It is a **small group of up to 8 people**, not mixed with other operators. You are picked up in Madrid in a premium vehicle for a small group. The three tickets and the tasting are included. It lasts **12 hours and 30 minutes** in total.
