## What you'll experience

A private tuk-tuk where **your group controls the vibe**: hop on with your own playlist and set the tone as you cruise Madrid's most famous avenues for **90 minutes**. It's nonstop energy, built for celebrating something with your people, day or night.

## How it works

The meeting point is arranged with you when you book, and the ride **ends wherever in central Madrid your group chooses**, so you can tie it straight into whatever's next on your plan. The price is per tuk-tuk. It's built for groups in a party mood: **no alcohol is allowed on board during the ride**, and the vehicle isn't wheelchair or stroller accessible.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Groups celebrating a birthday or a bachelor/bachelorette party who want to kick things off from the tuk-tuk.
- Anyone who wants energy and music over historical or cultural commentary.
- Anyone who wants the ride to end near whatever's next on the agenda in central Madrid.
