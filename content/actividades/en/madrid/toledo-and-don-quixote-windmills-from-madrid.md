## What you will see

Two very different Castilla-La Mancha landscapes in one day:

- **Consuegra windmills:** the row of white windmills on Calderico hill that inspired Cervantes. Entry to the **Rucio windmill** is included.
- **La Muela castle**, next to the windmills, overlooking La Mancha.
- **Toledo:** a short walk through the medieval centre and about 3 hours to explore at your own pace.
- **Mirador del Valle:** the panoramic view of Toledo over the Tagus.

## How it works

You meet at **Paseo del Prado, 3**, by the operator's minibus. Groups are **2 to 8 people**, with a guide in English, Spanish or Italian and headsets to hear clearly.

First Consuegra, with a coffee stop; then Toledo; and finally the Mirador del Valle. Lunch is not included and is on your own in Toledo. About 10 hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **Don Quixote windmills** without renting a car.
- Travellers who prefer a **small group** and a relaxed pace.
- Photographers: it is one of the most photogenic day trips from Madrid.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- If you want to **go inside the Cathedral or Santo Tomé** with a guide: see the full-day Toledo tour.
- If you are on a tight budget: big-coach tours are cheaper.
- Wheelchair users: it is not accessible.
