## What you'll see

A **1 h 30 min** ride through Madrid's **Christmas lights**. From the **Neptune Fountain**, with the trees along Paseo del Prado lit up, you pass **Cibeles**, **Puerta de Alcalá** and the **Retiro** area, head up **Calle Serrano** and **Plaza de Colón**, ride along **Gran Vía** and finish by the **Spanish Parliament**.

## How it works

It's a **Christmas-season** tour: check dates in the booking calendar. The tuk-tuk waits at **Plaza de Cánovas del Castillo**, opposite the Thyssen Museum. It's **private**, seats **up to 4 people** and the price is per tuk-tuk. Commentary plays through **headphones in 11 languages**. The vehicle is open-sided, so bring a warm coat.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Families with children who want to see the lights without walking through crowds.
- Couples or groups visiting Madrid at Christmas with just one evening.
- Anyone after photos of Cibeles and Gran Vía lit up from a different angle.
