## What you'll see

The **Museo Reina Sofía** houses Spain's national collection of 20th and 21st-century art — one of the finest modern art museums in the world.

### Guernica
Pablo Picasso's **Guernica** (1937) is one of the most important paintings of the 20th century. Created as a response to the Nazi bombing of the Basque town of Guernica during the Spanish Civil War, it has become a universal symbol against war. It is displayed in its own room (206) on the second floor.

### The wider collection
Beyond Guernica, the museum holds exceptional works by:
- **Salvador Dalí** — surrealist masterpieces including The Great Masturbator
- **Joan Miró** — large-scale works that almost fill their rooms
- **Spanish Surrealism and Abstraction** — a rich collection spanning the 1920s to the 1970s

### The building
The 18th-century Sabatini building was extended by Jean Nouvel with a striking glass addition. The rooftop terrace is open and offers views over Madrid.

## Who it's for

- Anyone with even a passing interest in modern art.
- Art lovers who consider Guernica a must-see.

## Who it's not for

- Very young children with no interest in art (the museum is extensive and quiet).
