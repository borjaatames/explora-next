## What you'll see

A **5-hour tour** covering Madrid's two essential monuments in half a day, with the same guide and skip-the-line entry to both. The route runs in this order:

1. **Royal Palace of Madrid** (~1h 30min): royal apartments, throne room, grand staircase, royal chapel. Still the official residence of the Crown, although the royal family does not currently live here.
2. **Guided walk through the historic centre** (~30–40 min): Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, Cervantes' house, Spanish Parliament.
3. **Prado Museum** (~1h 30min): masterpieces by Velázquez, Goya, Bosch, Rubens and other great European painters.

## How it works

The Naturanda team meets you at their office at **Plaza de España 9** at 9:30. The tour starts on time: if you're late, the group has already entered the Royal Palace and you won't be able to join.

After the Palace, the guide walks you through the centre to the Prado, stopping at the highlights along the route. Total walking distance is around **1.5 km (1 mi)**.

You enter the Prado through the priority ticket window, no queue, and the guide takes you through the main galleries for 90 minutes. The tour ends at the Prado: you're free to keep exploring at your own pace.

## Who it's for

- **One-day visitors** who don't want to complicate logistics: the essentials with one guide.
- Travellers who value **logistical efficiency**: two skip-the-line entries and a single point of contact.
- Anyone **comfortable walking** for 5 hours with short stops.

## Who should skip it

- **2–3 day visitors**: better to split the Royal Palace and Prado into separate visits and enjoy each one properly.
- **Families with young kids** or anyone with reduced mobility: the pace and the walking are demanding.
- Anyone wanting a **deep dive into the Prado**: 90 minutes at the end of a long day, already tired, isn't ideal. For that, take the dedicated Prado tour.
