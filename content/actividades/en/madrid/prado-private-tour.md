## What you'll see

A **two-hour Prado tour** with a guide dedicated only to your group. Before the museum, the guide picks you up at your hotel and you transfer to the Prado by taxi (included). The selection of works is tailored to the time available and to your interests.

- **Spanish Golden Age painting:** Velázquez's *Las Meninas*, court portraits, and Rubens's *The Three Graces*.
- **Goya:** from the cartoons for tapestries to the **Black Paintings** from the Quinta del Sordo.
- **Flemish and European painting:** Bosch's *The Garden of Earthly Delights*, Titian, El Greco, Rembrandt.

If you have preferences — say, you're especially into Goya, or you want to see all the Italian painting — tell the guide at the start. They'll shape the route around it.

## How it works

You receive an instant confirmation email with the operator's details (Madrid Discovery). The guide waits for you in your **hotel lobby** at the agreed time and identifies you by name. You transfer to the Prado by taxi and enter through the **priority ticket window**, no queue.

After the two guided hours, the guide leaves you inside the museum and you have **free access** to keep exploring at your own pace until closing.

## Who it's for

- **Groups of 3 or more**: the per-head cost gets close to the standard tour but you gain flexibility and a dedicated guide.
- Travellers **short on time in Madrid** who want to make every minute count: start when you want, no fixed group schedule.
- Anyone with **specific interests** in a school or painter: the guide can prioritize what matters to you.

## Who should skip it

- **1 or 2 people who don't mind sharing**: the standard €39 tour is the better deal and the experience is broadly similar.
- **Repeat visitors** who only want to see two or three specific things: for less money you can buy the skip-the-line ticket (€15) and head straight to them.
