## What you will see

After a short safety briefing, the route heads to **Plaza de Oriente**, with the statue of Philip IV, the **Royal Palace**, the **Almudena** and the church of San Nicolás. Then:

- **Plaza de la Villa**, with some of Madrid's oldest buildings.
- **Mercado de San Miguel**, **Plaza Mayor** and **Plaza de Santa Ana**.
- The **CaixaForum vertical garden** and the **Prado Museum**.
- **Retiro Park**, with the boating lake and the Palacio de Cristal.

It covers 11 km along cycle lanes, pedestrian streets and park paths, with photo stops and stories from the guide.
