## What you'll see

Self-guided entry to Riyadh Air Metropolitano Stadium (formerly Wanda), home of Atlético Madrid since 2017. Full route through the stadium and the club's interactive museum.

- **Stadium view from inside:** roof structure and stands.
- **First-team dressing rooms** (always open, don't close for matches like at the Bernabéu).
- **Presidential box.**
- **Press room.**
- **Players' tunnel onto the pitch.**
- **Interactive Museum:** VR, touchscreens, games on Atlético's history.

## How it works

**Access point:** Riyadh Air Metropolitano Stadium, Av. de Luis Aragonés, 4. Estadio Metropolitano metro (line 7) 10 minutes away.

**Arrive 15 minutes early** for security check. **Duration: 1.5 hours.**

**Check whether the audio guide is included** or an extra in your Viator option. Without audio, the tour loses context.

**Tip:** after 5:30pm the stadium is nearly empty — best enjoyment of the interactive experiences.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Atlético fans or European football fans.
- Families with children (interactive museum, VR, games).
- Those wanting guaranteed dressing-room access (they don't close like at the Bernabéu).
- Viator users.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Travellers with little time (the stadium is outside the centre).
- Those wanting maximum social validation: GetYourGuide has more reviews.
- Those without sporting interest.
- Those unwilling to pay for the audio guide as an extra.
