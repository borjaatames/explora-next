## What you'll see

**Aquópolis Villanueva de la Cañada** is the most popular waterpark in the Madrid region, open from June to September with slides and pools for all ages.

### Speed slides and family slides
Multiple slides ranging from gentle family runs to steep speed slides. There's a section dedicated to children under 10 with smaller slides and shallow pools.

### Wave pool and lazy river
Two essentials: a **large wave pool** for the beach feeling, and a **lazy river** to float around in when you need a break from the slides.

## Who it's for

- Families with children of all ages.
- Anyone who wants to cool down in Madrid's summer heat.

## Who it's not for

- Visitors in autumn or winter: the park is seasonal (June–September only).
