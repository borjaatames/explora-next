## What you'll see

A tour on a **latest-generation electric tuk-tuk**, quiet and sustainable, combining luxury, closeness and easy movement through the city. It reaches **historic corners and narrow streets** in Madrid that other vehicles can't enter, while your guide shares stories and secrets only a local would know, pacing the tour to what you ask for.

## How it works

The best part of this experience is the **door-to-door pickup**: you're collected directly from your hotel or accommodation within Madrid city (no airport pickup). The reference duration is **1 hour**, but it's flexible — you can agree on a 30-minute express tour or a luxury experience of up to 4 hours when booking. The guide speaks French, English and Spanish. It's stroller accessible, but **not wheelchair accessible**, and children under 2 are not admitted.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone staying in Madrid who doesn't want to travel to a meeting point.
- Travelers who value a quiet, sustainable, luxury format over a classic tuk-tuk.
- Anyone wanting a quick visual overview of the city before exploring it on foot at a slower pace.
