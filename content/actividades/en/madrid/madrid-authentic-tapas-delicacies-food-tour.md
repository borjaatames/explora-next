## What you'll see

Secret Food Tours runs the same format in 30+ cities worldwide and the Madrid version is a tight, curated introduction to the city's food culture. The structure is built around four pillars: classic tapas, paired wines, central historic Madrid, and one "secret dish" that the operator doesn't reveal until you're at the stop.

- **Multiple central Madrid stops.** The route covers the Sol / Plaza Mayor area — the historic centre, where most first-time visitors are staying.
- **Classic tapas + wine pairings.** Traditional bar food (jamón, croquetas, regional plates) with local wines at each stop. Recent menus emphasise quality of the wine selection.
- **The "secret dish".** One dish per tour that the operator keeps unrevealed. It's not weird food — just a less obvious Spanish speciality.
- **Two formats.** Choose 2-hour express (lighter introduction) or 3-hour full (more stops, more food) at booking.

## How it works

The exact meeting point is shared in your booking confirmation. Arrive 10-15 minutes early. Typical starting points are around Sol or Plaza Mayor in central Madrid, depending on which format you've booked.

The guide is bilingual — Spanish and English. The narration adapts to the group composition. Confirm with the operator at booking if you specifically need one language or the other.

The pace depends on the format you choose: 2-hour express is faster, with fewer stops and lighter portions; 3-hour full is more leisurely with 1-2 extra stops. Both formats end in central Madrid, easy to continue your evening independently.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- First-time visitors to Madrid wanting a curated introduction to Spanish food.
- Travellers who like the Secret Food Tours brand format from other cities and want consistency.
- Spanish and English speakers — this is a genuinely bilingual product.
- Anyone on a tighter budget — €59 is the cheapest bilingual option on our shortlist.
- Travellers who want a 2-hour option (most tapas tours are 3+ hours).

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Travellers wanting depth on a specific neighbourhood — this is a central Madrid overview tour.
- Anyone wanting wheelchair accessibility — confirm with the operator, but the standard product is not accessible.
- Premium-seekers wanting a small private group — the Adventurous Appetites premium tour is the better fit at €135.
- Travellers with severe dietary restrictions where the operator can't reliably adapt the menu.
