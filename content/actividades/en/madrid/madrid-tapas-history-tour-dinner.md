## What you'll see

A **3-hour tour** mixing **tapas and Madrid history**. You start at **Plaza de Isabel II** and stop at a tavern on **Calle del Arenal** famous for its croquettes since 1860. You continue through **Puerta del Sol** (and the story of the twelve grapes), **Plaza Mayor**, where you try **ham and chorizo** at a tavern on the square, pass **Botín**, the world's oldest restaurant, and reach **Plaza de la Villa** and the **Royal Palace**.

## How it works

The guide waits with a **Native Spanish Tapas** sign at the statue in the middle of Plaza de Isabel II, five minutes from Sol. After the tapas stops, you finish with a **full sit-down dinner** at a traditional restaurant: **paella, more tapas and homemade sangria**, with a Castilian liqueur shot at the end. The tour is **in English**. A vegetarian option is available if requested when booking.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- International travelers who want a first night in Madrid with dinner sorted.
- Anyone who prefers a complete evening plan to bar-hopping on their own.
- Groups of friends or couples who speak English.
