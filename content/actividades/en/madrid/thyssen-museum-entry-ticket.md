## What you will see

The Thyssen's permanent collection covers **eight centuries of European painting** in chronological order, starting on the top floor:

- **Early Italian and Flemish masters:** Duccio, Van Eyck and Renaissance portraits such as Ghirlandaio's Giovanna Tornabuoni.
- **Baroque:** Caravaggio, Rembrandt and Rubens.
- **Impressionism and Post-Impressionism:** Monet, Degas, Renoir and Van Gogh, one of the museum's highlights.
- **20th century:** German Expressionism, the avant-garde, American painting and Pop Art.

## How it works

You buy the ticket for a specific day and carry it on your phone. The museum is in the **Villahermosa Palace** (Paseo del Prado, 8), opposite the Neptune fountain and a five-minute walk from the Prado.

The **audio guide** is included. The route starts on the second floor and works down chronologically, so it is hard to get lost.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors with **time for only one museum** who prefer a manageable one.
- Fans of **Impressionism** or **modern art**, which the Prado does not cover.
- Anyone who wants to go **at their own pace** with explanations, without a group.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who needs flexible dates: the ticket is **non-refundable**.
- Under-18s: they enter free with a ticket from the box office.
- Anyone happy to queue for free entry: on **Mondays 12:00-16:00** the permanent collection costs nothing.
