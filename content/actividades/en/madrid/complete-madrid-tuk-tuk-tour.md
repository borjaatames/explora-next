## What you'll see

Tuk Tuk Zero's most complete route: **2 h 15 min** combining monumental and modern Madrid. From the **Neptune Fountain** you head up past **Cibeles and Colón** to the **AZCA** business district and **Santiago Bernabéu Stadium**, then back through **Plaza de los Delfines, El Viso, the Salamanca district and Calle Serrano**, passing **Puerta de Alcalá, the Retiro, the Stock Exchange and the Ritz and Palace hotels**. The second half covers the historic centre: **Santa Ana, Parliament, the Art Walk, Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, La Latina, the Almudena, the Royal Palace, San Miguel, Plaza de España and Gran Vía**.

## How it works

The tuk-tuk picks you up at **Plaza de Cánovas del Castillo**, opposite the Thyssen Museum. It's **private** and seats **up to 4 people**; the price is per tuk-tuk. Commentary plays through **headphones in 11 languages**, with water, wifi and phone charging on board.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to see all of Madrid in one morning or afternoon.
- Football fans who want to pass the Bernabéu without a separate visit.
- Families or groups of up to 4 who'd rather not walk.
