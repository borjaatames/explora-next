## What you'll see

The **City of Arts and Sciences**, Calatrava's masterpiece, and the old town: **San Nicolás church**, the **Silk Exchange**, the **Serranos Towers**, the **Carmen quarter** and the **Central Market**.

## How it works

It is a **small group of up to 8 people**, not mixed with other operators. You leave at 7:00 from Calle Alfonso XI, next to Retiro Park, in an air-conditioned van. Meals are on your own. It lasts **15 hours** in total.
