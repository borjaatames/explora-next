## What you'll discover

The DareMapp app guides you through Plaza Mayor, Puerta del Sol, Gran Vía, El Retiro park and the Prado Museum, plus up to 7 themed routes (essential Madrid, Barrio de las Letras, La Latina, Paseo del Arte, Chueca...) with hundreds of points of interest in total — DareMapp's most complete catalogue, all narrated with audio in your own language. **There's no physical guide**: it's a fully self-guided experience, built so you decide what to see, how long to stay, and in what order.

## How it works

After booking you get a **reference code on your ticket**. Enter it in the DareMapp app (available in Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian and German) and start the route whenever you like, **wherever in Madrid you choose**. There's no meeting point and no fixed schedule: you can pause and resume the audio at any time, pick only the stops that interest you, and enjoy it **24 hours a day, 365 days a year** — the access never expires. The app also includes food and family-activity recommendations for the rest of your visit.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Independent travellers who'd rather not be tied to a schedule or a group.
- Families with young children or a stroller, who need the flexibility to stop whenever they need to.
- Anyone who wants to explore Madrid at a relaxed pace, without a live guide setting the tempo.
