## The route

You meet at 7 pm on Calle de Fuencarral and spend about three hours with your guide visiting **four taverns in Malasaña**, one of central Madrid's liveliest neighbourhoods. At each one you try **Spanish craft beers** and the tapa the place is known for.

Between stops your guide explains how craft brewing has taken off in Spain, a much younger scene than the country's wine.

## How it works

- Price per person, with a minimum of 2 people per booking.
- The tapas across the four stops add up to dinner.
- A vegetarian option is available: request it when booking.

## Who it's for

- Beer lovers who want to discover Spanish craft beer.
- Couples or friends looking for an evening out with dinner included.
- English speakers: the tour runs in English.
