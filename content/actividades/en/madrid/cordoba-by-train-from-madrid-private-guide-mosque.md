## What you'll see

The **Mosque-Cathedral**, with its forest of columns and two-tone arches, and the **Jewish quarter**: narrow lanes, flower-filled patios, the **Calleja de las Flores** and the **synagogue**. Then you have free time for lunch and a stroll before the train back.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You leave Atocha by train and the guide meets you at the Mosque in Córdoba. Tickets and entry are included; lunch is not. It lasts **9 hours and 30 minutes** in total.
