## How the day works

You leave at 9 am from Plaza del Marqués de Salamanca for the heart of **Ribera del Duero**. You visit **two wineries** chosen for the quality of their wines, their family heritage or their architecture.

At each one you have a **guided tasting of 3 wines** —six in total, in Roble, Crianza and Reserva styles— and learn about **Tempranillo** (locally Tinta Fina), high-altitude viticulture and the contrast between traditional cellars and more modern projects.

## Lunch

Not included. You can eat locally (the operator estimates about €45 per person with wine and coffee) or do both visits in the morning and head straight back to Madrid.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to see Ribera del Duero without a long lunch taking up the day.
- Red wine fans who'd rather do two good wineries than three in a rush.
