## What you will see

The tour focuses on the Prado's **unmissable masterpieces**. The guide suggests a chronological route so you understand how painting evolves, although the visit can be adapted:

- **Velázquez:** *Las Meninas*, *The Surrender of Breda* and *Apollo in the Forge of Vulcan*.
- **Goya:** from court portraits to the **Black Paintings**, via *The Third of May* and the two *Majas*.
- **Bosch and Flemish painting:** *The Garden of Earthly Delights*, Van der Weyden and Dürer.
- **Venetian school and Rubens:** Titian, Tintoretto and *The Three Graces*.

## How it works

Your guide from **The Guides You Need** waits next to the **Goya statue**, in front of the museum ticket office, holding a company sign. Arrive 15 minutes early.

The visit lasts **between 90 minutes and two hours**. Afterwards you can **stay in the museum on your own** until closing time.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to the Prado who want to understand what they see, not just see it.
- Travellers **short on time** who need to know what to prioritise in such a big museum.
- Anyone who prefers a **small group** with an official guide.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- If you only want to see two or three specific paintings: the **official entry ticket** is enough and cheaper.
- Families with **young children**: two hours of painting can feel long for them.
- If you want to take photos: the museum does not allow it.
