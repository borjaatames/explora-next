## What you'll see

The premium end of the Madrid tapas market is a different category from the standard 3-hour crawl. This tour pushes the format on three axes at once: group size, food volume and time per venue.

- **Small group cap.** Maximum 10 travellers, often fewer. The standard tapas tour runs at 12-15. Fewer people means the guide can actually answer questions, the bars can plate properly, and the conversation around the table works.
- **More food.** Between 12 and 20 tapas across 3-4 bars, depending on group size. That's a full dinner, not an aperitif. Recent reviews are consistent: travellers leave full.
- **More time per bar.** 3.5 hours over 3-4 venues = around 50-60 minutes per stop. The standard crawl is closer to 30 minutes per bar. The difference is whether you eat standing up or actually sit down.

## How it works

You meet your guide at Plaza de Isabel II, in front of the Teatro Real. Arrive 10-15 minutes early. The guide carries Adventurous Appetites branding for identification — the bookings are confirmed immediately so check your email for the exact meeting point if anything has changed.

The route is not fixed — the operator rotates between 3-4 traditional bars depending on what's good that night, what's open, and how the group is moving. Expect a starting point in central Madrid and a finish line in the same area: Plaza Mayor, Plaza Santa Ana, and the streets in between.

Drinks: one per bar, included. Beer, wine, or soft drink. Spirits are not included. If you want a cocktail tour, this isn't the right format.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- English-speaking travellers who want the most substantive tapas experience in Madrid and don't mind paying €135 for it.
- Couples or pairs who specifically want a small-group format with attention from the guide.
- Anyone who's done a standard 3-hour crawl elsewhere and wants more depth.
- Travellers who care about the social dynamic of the table, not just the food.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Spanish-only speakers — this tour is in English only. Gourmet Madrid's €77 bilingual tour is the better option.
- Travellers on a budget. €135 is real money. The €60-80 tier covers similar ground with bigger groups.
- Anyone wanting a cocktail or spirits tour — only beer, wine and soft drinks are included.
- Vegans or travellers with severe dietary restrictions — traditional menus don't always have substitutes.
