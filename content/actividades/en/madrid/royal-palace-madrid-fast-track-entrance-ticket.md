Your ticket gets you into the **Royal Palace**, the official residence of the Spanish royal family and one of the largest palaces in Europe, with **over 3,000 rooms**. You visit the state rooms at your own pace.

You receive your ticket by email right after booking. Show it on your phone at the entrance at your time slot and visit the palace at your own pace. This option does not include an audio guide.
