## What you will see

The guide takes you through the highlights of the Thyssen-Bornemisza's permanent collection, one of the most complete private collections of European art:

- **Old Masters:** Renaissance portraits and the Baroque of **Caravaggio** and Rembrandt.
- **Impressionism:** **Monet**, **Degas** and the Post-Impressionists.
- **20th century:** works by **Picasso** and **Dalí**, the avant-garde and American painting.

## How it works

Your guide from **The Guides You Need** meets you next to the **bust of Baron Thyssen**, right in front of the museum door on Paseo del Prado. The group is **7 people at most** and the visit lasts about **90 minutes**.

The entry ticket is included, so you go straight in without queuing at the box office.

## Who I recommend it for

- First-time visitors who want to understand the collection, not just walk through it.
- Travellers who prefer a **small group** over the large tours of the Prado.
- Anyone who wants to combine it with the Prado or Reina Sofía the same day: the Thyssen is in between.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Spanish speakers or anyone needing another language: it is **only in English**.
- If you prefer to go at your own pace: the **entry ticket with audio guide** is cheaper.
- Families with **young children**: 90 minutes of painting can feel long.
