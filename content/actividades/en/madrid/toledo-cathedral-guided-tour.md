## What you'll see

The tour starts in **Plaza del Ayuntamiento**, facing the west front, and covers the cathedral's history since the 13th century. Inside you'll see:

- The **Treasury chapel**, with **Enrique de Arfe**'s monstrance.
- The **high altarpiece**, the **choir** stalls and the organs.
- **El Transparente**, Narciso Tomé's great Baroque work.
- The **sacristy**, with paintings by **El Greco**, Velázquez, Goya and Caravaggio.

## How it works

Local guide in Spanish or English, with entry included. There is a dress code: shoulders and legs covered.
