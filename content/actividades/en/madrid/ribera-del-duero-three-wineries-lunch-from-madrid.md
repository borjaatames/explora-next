## How the day works

You're picked up at 7:30 am from your Madrid accommodation and head to **Ribera del Duero**, Spain's great inland red wine region. Your sommelier picks **three wineries** with different styles —from small artisan producers to family-run estates— based on availability and the group's preferences.

Over the day you taste **at least 9 Tempranillo (Tinta Fina) wines**, from young reds to Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva. When possible you'll also try an **aged Albillo Mayor white**, a rarity: Ribera whites have only been permitted since 2019.

After the first two visits you stop for **wood-fired roast suckling lamb** with wine. Then the third winery and back to Madrid.

## Who it's for

- Red wine lovers who want to really understand Ribera del Duero.
- Couples or small groups who'd rather not drive.
- Anyone keen on an authentic Castilian lunch as part of the day.
