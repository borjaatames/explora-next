## What you'll see

Devour Tours runs this product as a breadth tour rather than a depth-on-tapas tour. The route mixes a covered market visit, two small family restaurants, a chocolate maker and Galician empanadas — six stops spread across 3 hours in central Madrid.

- **Mercado Antón Martín**, around 40 minutes inside the market. Tasting plus a guided walk through the stalls.
- **Chocolate Madrid**, a specialty chocolate maker. 30 minutes for a tasting and the story behind the product.
- **MOEGA empanadas**, Galician savoury pastries from the north-west of Spain. A 15-minute stop.
- **Casa González**, a traditional deli-bar. Around 35 minutes for a sit-down tasting.
- **Bar La Campana**, a traditional tapas stop. 30 minutes.
- **Plaza Mayor**, a guided walk to close the tour. 30 minutes of context, no tasting.

## How it works

You meet your guide at Plaza de Santa Ana, in central Madrid. The exact spot in the plaza is shared in your booking confirmation. Arrive 10-15 minutes early.

The route is rotated by the operator depending on availability — what's open, what's good that day, how the group is moving. Don't expect an identical itinerary to the one above, but expect the same logic: 6 stops, mix of market + sit-down + walking commentary, 3 hours.

The tour ends at Plaza Mayor, the central square of old Madrid. From there you can continue independently — Plaza Mayor is a 10-minute walk to most central Madrid neighbourhoods.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Travellers who want breadth rather than depth — chocolate, market, empanadas, tapas in one route.
- Anyone who values the operator brand. Devour has been running food tours in Spain since 2012 and the quality is consistent.
- Vegetarians, pescatarians or dairy-free travellers — the operator explicitly accommodates these at booking.
- English-speaking travellers wanting a curated culinary introduction, not a pure tapas crawl.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Spanish-only speakers — this is an English-only tour.
- Anyone who specifically wants a tapas-only experience. The chocolate maker and empanadas are great, but if you want six tapas bars, book a different product.
- Travellers on a tight budget — €79 for a 3-hour tour is mid-market, not cheap.
- Vegans or travellers with severe restrictions — the operator can adapt, but the menu is built around traditional Spanish food.
