## What you'll see

A **1 h 15 min electric tuk-tuk ride** through the historic heart of Madrid. You start at the **Neptune Fountain**, pass along the **Art Walk** (Prado, Thyssen and Reina Sofía), **Plaza de Santa Ana** and the **Spanish Parliament**, then head into Habsburg Madrid: **Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, La Latina, Almudena Cathedral and the Royal Palace**. The way back takes in **San Miguel Market, Plaza de España, Gran Vía and Cibeles**.

## How it works

The tuk-tuk waits in the parking area of **Plaza de Cánovas del Castillo**, opposite the Thyssen Museum. It's **private**: the price is per tuk-tuk and it seats **up to 4 people**. Commentary plays through **headphones in 11 languages** while the driver follows the route, and there's water, wifi and phone charging on board. If there are more than 4 of you, book one tuk-tuk per group.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- First-time visitors who want an overview of Madrid in a short time.
- Groups of 3 or 4, when the per-tuk-tuk price is best value.
- Anyone who'd rather not walk much but still wants to see the landmarks up close.
