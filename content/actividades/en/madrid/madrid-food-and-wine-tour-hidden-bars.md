## The route

You meet in **Plaza de los Carros** in La Latina and walk to a selection of **intimate bars, traditional taverns and market stalls** (when available), all independent and chosen for the quality of their produce.

At each of the **four stops** you try regional dishes paired with **at least one glass of Spanish wine**, while learning about regions, grapes and traditions. By the end, you've had dinner.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to understand Spanish wine without visiting a winery.
- Couples and travellers looking for places off the tourist trail.
- There's a midday (11:30 am) and an evening (6:30 pm) departure.
