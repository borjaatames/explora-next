## What you'll see

Hands-on 3-hour workshop to prepare authentic sangria and cook traditional paella with a local chef. The group gathers in a kitchen space in central Madrid (Calle Montera, next to Sol and Gran Vía).

- **Sangria preparation:** mix of wine, fruit and proportions to achieve the refreshing balance.
- **Paella history:** brief introduction to the Valencian origin and basic ingredients.
- **Paella workshop:** step-by-step preparation in a traditional pan with chicken and seafood (vegetarian and allergies upon advance notice).
- **Group lunch or dinner:** the paella you cook is your meal, in a festive atmosphere with sangria and soft drinks.
- **Take-home recipes:** detailed document with everything learnt, to replicate at home.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Calle de la Montera, 24. **Note:** the visual reference is the **Bon Vivant** tattoo studio at the doorway next door. The host waits outside the building — DO NOT enter the doorway, wait outside. The operator strongly insists on this point.

Metro Sol (lines 1/2/3) or Gran Vía (1/5) 3-5 minutes away. **Arrive 10 minutes early.**

**Duration:** 3 hours. It's a hands-on workshop — you'll cook yourself, not observe the chef. Shared group (not private).

**Languages:** Spanish and English. If you have restrictions (vegetarian, allergies), message the operator AS SOON AS YOU BOOK — it's not guaranteed if you mention it on the spot.

**Kitchen space:** upstairs in the building. If you have mobility issues, confirm with the operator beforehand whether there's a lift.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Travellers wanting a hands-on gastronomic experience.
- Couples and small groups who enjoy cooking together.
- Beginners in Spanish cooking.
- Anyone wanting to take recipes home to replicate.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Experienced amateur chefs (introductory workshop, may feel basic).
- Anyone wanting a private version (here it's a shared group).
- Travellers with reduced mobility (kitchen upstairs).
- Anyone after quick eat-out options (here it's 3 dedicated hours).
