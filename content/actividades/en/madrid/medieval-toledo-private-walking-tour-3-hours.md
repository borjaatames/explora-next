## What you'll see

The **Santa María la Blanca** synagogue, the Gothic **Monastery of San Juan de los Reyes**, El Greco's **Burial of the Count of Orgaz** in **Santo Tomé church**, and finally the **Cristo de la Luz** mosque, where Islamic and Christian art meet.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You meet the guide at Zocodover or Toledo train station. The three tickets are included; the cathedral is not. It lasts **3 hours** in total.
