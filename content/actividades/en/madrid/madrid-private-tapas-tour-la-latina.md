## What you'll see

The bars of **La Latina** and the historic centre: family-run taverns, traditional tapas such as ham, cheese and croquettes, and Spanish drinks like vermouth and wine, with the story behind each dish.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You meet the guide at Plaza de la Cebada. Food and drinks are included. It lasts **3 hours** in total.
