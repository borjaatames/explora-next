## What you'll see

About 1.5-2 hours of guided access to Santiago Bernabéu Stadium, home of Real Madrid, with a professional guide and full access to the museum and stadium. Booked via Viator.

- **Panoramic view of the renovated stadium.**
- **Real Madrid Museum:** the full trophy collection (15 Champions Leagues, Copas, La Ligas).
- **First-team dressing rooms** (subject to match-day closures).
- **Benches and pitchside area.**
- **Memorabilia, historic kits and interactive exhibitions.**

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Bernabéu Tour entrance at the stadium. Santiago Bernabéu metro (line 10) is 100 m away.

**Arrive 15 minutes early.** Duration: 1.5-2 hours. Languages: Spanish or English.

**Match-day notice:** dressing rooms and benches closed on the day and the day before the match.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Real Madrid fans interested in narrated historical context.
- Regular Viator users.
- Travellers who prefer a human guide over self-guided panels.
- Families with football-loving teens.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Travellers with reduced mobility expecting full pitchside access.
- Those looking for the cheapest option: self-guided is less expensive.
- Visitors on match days without flexibility to change dates.
- Travellers wanting maximum validation: GetYourGuide has products with more reviews.
