## What you'll experience

A guided night out through **five of the liveliest venues in central Madrid**, with VIP entry included at clubs like Icon Club, Manama Club, Teresa Club, Moondance and Shoko. Every stop comes with a welcome drink and a free shot, and over the four hours you party alongside your guide and other travellers on the same night out.

## How it works

The exact meeting point is sent by chat or email once your booking is confirmed, always somewhere in central Madrid. You'll need a **valid passport or ID card** to get into the venues, and this activity is **strictly 18+**. Some venues on the route can change depending on the day of the week. One thing to flag: this is a **final sale** booking, with no cancellations or date changes, so make sure your night is locked in before you pay.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to hit several of Madrid's popular clubs in one night, without queuing separately at each one.
- Solo travellers or couples who want a group atmosphere and the chance to meet people.
- Anyone who already has the date sorted, since it can't be cancelled.
