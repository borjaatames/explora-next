## What you will see

The tour starts by the **Orpheus fountain**, a few steps from the **Plaza Mayor**, and walks through the small but very historic old town, the **Madrid of the Habsburgs**. The guide explains how an isolated hill in the middle of the plateau became a capital, and solves some local riddles along the way: why people avoided the Plaza Mayor for decades, which monument left the whole city open-mouthed and whether the most decisive "accident" in Madrid's history really was one.

There is a stop at a **viewpoint** for photos, and plenty of advice on Madrid's food and wine traditions and where to eat like a local.
