## What you will see

The **Royal Palace of Madrid**, built in the 18th century on the site of the old Alcázar, has more than 3,000 rooms; it is the largest royal palace in Western Europe. The tour covers the main rooms:

- **Throne Room**, with Tiepolo's ceiling and the bronze lions.
- **Gala Dining Room**, where state banquets are still held.
- **Charles III's rooms**, with works by **Goya** and **Luca Giordano**, tapestries and clocks.

The guide also tells the story of Plaza de Oriente, which you cross at the start, and of the Spanish royal family.

## Tip

When you finish, look into **Almudena Cathedral**, right opposite, and the Sabatini or Campo del Moro gardens.
