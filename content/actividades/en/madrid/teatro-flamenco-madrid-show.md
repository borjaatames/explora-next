## What you will see

**Teatro Flamenco Madrid** is a venue devoted solely to flamenco, on Calle del Pez, between Gran Vía and Malasaña. Each show is one hour with a full ensemble: **dancers, singer and guitarist**, with no narration or gimmicks.

The format is that of a small theatre: the stage is close and the acoustics and lighting are designed for the show, so you can see well from any row.

## How to plan it

There are several shows a day. The early-evening one leaves the night free for dinner in Malasaña or around Plaza de España; the last one fits in after an afternoon on Gran Vía.
