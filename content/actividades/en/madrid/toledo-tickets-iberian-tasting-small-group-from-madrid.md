## What you'll see

The old town of **Toledo**, a World Heritage Site, with entry to its main monuments, legends told by your host and a **tasting of acorn-fed Iberian ham, Manchego cheese and loin** in a historic cellar. Then free time for the craft shops.

## How it works

It is a **small group of up to 7 people**, not mixed with other operators. You leave from the Naval Museum, next to Cibeles, in a Mercedes. Tickets and the tasting are included. It lasts **10 hours** in total.
