## What you'll see

The old town of **Toledo**, where Christians, Jews and Muslims lived side by side: the **cathedral**, the **Jewish quarter** and its synagogues, the viewpoints over the **Tagus** and the cobbled streets. The guide suggests monuments and you choose which to visit.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. The driver picks you up at your Madrid hotel and the guide meets you in Toledo for 3 hours. Tickets are extra. It lasts **7 hours and 30 minutes** in total.
