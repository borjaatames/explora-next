## What you'll see

Toledo in private format: Mercedes-Benz vehicle with a professional driver, your own dedicated licensed guide, hotel pickup and drop-off. The duration is flexible between 5 and 8 hours — you set the pace with the guide on the day.

- **Toledo's old town:** cobbled streets connecting the Cathedral, Plaza de Zocodover, the Alcázar and the Jewish quarter, in a UNESCO city blending Arab, Jewish and Christian heritage.
- **Primate Cathedral (ticket separate):** one of the most important Gothic cathedrals in Europe. The guide enters with you to explain it; the ticket is paid at the box office.
- **Church of Santo Tomé and Synagogue (tickets separate):** *The Burial of the Count of Orgaz* by El Greco, plus the Mudéjar architecture of the Sinagoga del Tránsito or Santa María la Blanca, depending on the route you agree with the guide.
- **Whatever you want:** free time to eat, buy marzipan or head up to the Mirador del Valle. The day is organised with your guide.

## How it works

The driver picks you up **directly at your hotel**, apartment, Madrid-Barajas Airport, Atocha or Chamartín station. You set the time at booking. The drive to Toledo takes about an hour in a Mercedes-Benz sedan or minivan depending on group size.

In Toledo your **dedicated private guide** stays with you exclusively all day. They speak English or Spanish (you choose at booking) and have a license to enter and explain inside the monuments. The route and the time at each spot are decided with the guide on the fly: 5-hour formats (faster pace) and 8-hour formats (with free time for lunch and wandering) are both available.

**Important: Cathedral, Santo Tomé and Synagogue tickets are not included.** The guide goes inside with you but the tickets are paid at the box office (€12-15 per person for all three). At the end of the day the driver takes you back to the original pickup point.

- If you travel as a couple with budget: at €155/person it's the best way to do Toledo properly.
