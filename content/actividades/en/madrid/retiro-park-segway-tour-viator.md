## What you'll see

A 1-hour segway tour through Retiro Park. Comfortable ride without physical effort, with prior instruction for first-timers.

- **Palacio de Cristal:** iron-and-glass structure among the trees.
- **Retiro lake and Alfonso XII Monument.**
- **La Rosaleda** with over 4,000 rose plants.
- **Ángel Caído:** the world's only public sculpture dedicated to Lucifer.
- **Casita del Pescador and other corners of the park.**

## How it works

**Meeting point:** confirmed in email (near a Retiro entrance). Retiro metro (line 2) or Príncipe de Vergara.

**Arrive 20 minutes early** — 10-15 min prior instruction + waiver. **Duration: 1 hour** (some guides offer to extend by 30 min on request).

**Languages:** Spanish and English. **Minimum age: 14.**

**Check segway weight restrictions** at booking.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Travellers who don't cycle or don't want physical effort.
- Fit older travellers wanting to see Retiro without tiring.
- Small groups looking for something different.
- Families with teenagers from 14+.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Children under 14.
- People with balance or vestibular issues.
- Pregnant women (advised against).
- Travellers with very reduced mobility.
