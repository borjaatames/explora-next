## What you'll see

The **Valle viewpoint**, with the city above the Tagus, and a 3-hour walk through the old town: legends, hidden corners, the **cathedral**, the **Jewish quarter** and the **Santa María la Blanca** synagogue. You choose which monuments to enter.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You leave Atocha by train; in Toledo the guide meets you and you take a taxi to the viewpoint before walking down into the centre. Tickets are extra. It lasts **6 hours and 30 minutes** in total.
