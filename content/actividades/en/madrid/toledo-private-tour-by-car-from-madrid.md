## What you'll see

The **Primate Cathedral**, the **Santa María la Blanca** and **El Tránsito** synagogues, the **Monastery of San Juan de los Reyes** and **The Burial of the Count of Orgaz** in Santo Tomé. If you love El Greco, the guide can add his museum.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You leave Madrid in a private car or van with a driver and official guide. Skip-the-line tickets are included; lunch is not. It lasts **8 hours** in total.
