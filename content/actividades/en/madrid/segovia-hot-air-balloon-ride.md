## What you'll see

A **1-hour hot air balloon flight** over **Segovia**, a UNESCO World Heritage city. From the air you can take in the **Roman Aqueduct**, the **Gothic Cathedral** and the **Alcázar** all at once, along with the plateau landscape around the city.

## How it works

The whole activity takes about **4 hours**. You meet at the operator's base in Segovia and head to the launch site. After the flight, **4x4 vehicles** take passengers from the landing spot to a **breakfast with cava**, and you receive a **flight certificate** and a **photo report**. If you don't have a car, you can add **pickup from Madrid** (Hotel Riu Plaza España, on Gran Vía) for **€40 per person**. Times depend on the **weather** and may change.

The operator is **Aerodifusión**, which says it was the first balloon company in central Spain to obtain an official quality certificate.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Couples looking for a special plan near Madrid.
- Anyone who already knows Segovia on foot and wants to see it from a new angle.
- Families with children aged 6 and over.
