## What you'll see

**Parque Warner** is one of Spain's top theme parks, located 50 km south of Madrid. Divided into **five themed areas** inspired by Warner Bros films and DC comics.

### DC Super Heroes World
Home to **Superman Ride**, the park's signature steel coaster reaching 100 km/h. Also houses the Batman Gotham City ride.

### Hollywood Boulevard and Cartoon Village
Classic movie theming, live shows, and a dedicated Looney Tunes section perfect for younger children.

### Live shows
The **Stunt Arena** show is a genuine highlight: car chases, explosions and professional stunts. Well worth planning your day around it.

## Who it's for

- Families and groups of friends who enjoy thrill rides.
- DC Comics and Looney Tunes fans.

## Who it's not for

- Visitors with limited time: the park needs a full day.
- Very young children (under 5): most big rides have height restrictions.
