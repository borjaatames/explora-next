## How the day works

You leave at 9 am from Plaza del Marqués de Salamanca by minivan for the **DO Vinos de Madrid** area, a little-known wine region under an hour from the capital. You visit **two wineries** with different styles, stories and terroirs.

You walk through the vines, see traditional and modern winemaking and taste **at least 6 wines** —reds, whites or rosés— from grapes such as **Tempranillo, Malvar and Garnacha**, with time for questions.

## Who it's for

- Wine lovers keen to discover the Madrid DO.
- Anyone with half a day who doesn't want to go far.
- Wine students (WSET) or anyone who simply wants to learn more.
