## What you will see

Toledo, the city of three cultures, on a full day with a guide and the main tickets included:

- **Mirador del Valle:** the classic view of Toledo from across the Tagus, as soon as you arrive.
- **Santo Tomé church:** *The Burial of the Count of Orgaz*, El Greco's most famous work.
- **Santa María la Blanca Synagogue:** one of the city's two surviving medieval synagogues.
- **Toledo Cathedral:** about 45 minutes inside one of Spain's great Gothic cathedrals.

## How it works

The meeting point is **Calle de San Bernardo, 7**, in central Madrid. The drive to Toledo takes about an hour, with a first stop at the Mirador del Valle.

In Toledo the guide takes you on foot to Santo Tomé, the Synagogue and the Cathedral, skipping the queues. Then you have **about 75 minutes of free time** to eat and stroll before heading back to Madrid. About 8 hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **Toledo's essentials in one day** without renting a car.
- Travellers keen on the **Cathedral** and **El Greco**: tickets are included.
- Anyone looking for good value.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: Toledo is all slopes and cobbles.
- If you prefer a **small group**: see Toledo by high-speed train (max 8).
- If you want a guide in one language only: explanations alternate English and Spanish.
