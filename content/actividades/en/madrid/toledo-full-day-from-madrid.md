## What you'll see

Toledo packs three cultures (Christian, Muslim, Jewish), a Gothic cathedral that competes for top spot in Spain, and a UNESCO-listed old town into less than a square kilometre. This excursion takes you by coach from central Madrid (about 1 hour) and spends the bulk of the day walking Toledo with an official guide.

- **Primate Cathedral (ticket included):** the centerpiece. 13th-century Gothic, monumental high chapel, sacristy with works by El Greco, Goya and Titian.
- **Sinagoga del Tránsito:** one of two surviving medieval synagogues in Toledo, in Mudejar style, hosting the Sephardic Museum.
- **Church of Santo Tomé:** home to *The Burial of the Count of Orgaz* by El Greco, one of the masterpieces of Spanish Mannerism.
- **Panoramic coach stop:** before going into the old town, the coach stops at one of the outer viewpoints for the classic Toledo skyline shot across the Tagus.

## How it works

The Fun & Tickets team meets you at **C. de San Bernardo, 7** (central Madrid, inside a commercial gallery) between **08:30 and 08:40**. The coach leaves sharp at 09:00. Travel time to Toledo is roughly 1 hour; before entering the old town the coach stops at an outer viewpoint for the classic panoramic photo.

Once in Toledo, the **bilingual walking tour** kicks in (English and Spanish). The official guide covers each landmark alternating both languages with the same group. The route covers the Primate Cathedral (with included entry), the Sinagoga del Tránsito and the Church of Santo Tomé. There's free time during the day for lunch and exploring on your own.

**Drop-off at the original meeting point** around 16:00. Total declared duration is 8 hours; minus travel and the panoramic stop, you get roughly 5 effective hours in Toledo.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If it's your first trip to Madrid and you want to add Toledo without dedicating more than one day.
- If you'd rather not rent a car or work out trains and buses on your own.
- If the Primate Cathedral and El Greco are your cultural priority for the day.
- If you're on a budget: €79 covers transport, guide and the main entry ticket.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you expected an English-only group. Here the guide alternates languages; consider a private tour if translation pauses bother you. Look at [Toledo half-day](/en/cities/madrid/activities/toledo-half-day-trip-from-madrid) if you're short on time and prefer a shorter format.
- If you have limited mobility. Toledo has steep slopes, cobbled streets and steps; this format is not accessible.
- If you want to dig deep into Toledo (museums, synagogues at leisure, country estates). One day gives you headlines; two days lets you actually enjoy the city.
- If you're traveling with very young kids and large strollers. The old town's streets don't forgive bulky strollers.
