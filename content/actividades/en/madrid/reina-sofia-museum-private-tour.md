## What you'll see

A **private 2-hour tour** of the **Reina Sofía Museum**, Spain's leading museum of contemporary art. The guide takes you to the essential works by **Picasso, Dalí, Miró, Man Ray and Magritte** and explains the social and political context they were made in. The centrepiece of the visit is **Guernica**.

## How it works

The guide can **pick you up at your hotel** and tell you about the city on the way, or you can meet at the **museum entrance, by the Spanish flag**, on Calle de Santa Isabel. **Skip-the-line entry** is included. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2**. The museum is wheelchair and stroller accessible.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to see Guernica and understand what it says.
- Anyone curious about 20th-century art but unsure where to start.
- Small groups who'd like a guide to themselves.
