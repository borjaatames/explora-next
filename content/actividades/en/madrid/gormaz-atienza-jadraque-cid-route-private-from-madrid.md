## What you'll see

The **caliphal fortress of Gormaz**, high above the Duero; **Atienza**, with its rock-top castle and medieval streets; and **Jadraque**, with its castle and views over the countryside. All told through the deeds of **El Cid**.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You leave at 8:30 from the Naval Museum in a private vehicle. Meals are on your own. It lasts **12 hours** in total.
