## How the day works

You're picked up at 8 am and head north-west to **DO Rueda**, the home of **Verdejo**. You visit **two wineries** chosen for their contrast, walking the vines and modern cellars and learning how sandy soils and cool nights shape the wines.

You taste **at least 6 wines**, from crisp steel-tank Verdejo to barrel-fermented old-vine cuvées, with a splash of Sauvignon Blanc for comparison.

## Lunch

At midday, according to the tour description, you sit down to a **multi-course seasonal menu at a Michelin-starred restaurant**, finishing with coffee and something sweet. Confirm with the operator when booking, as it isn't in the inclusions list. You're back in Madrid in time for an evening stroll.

## Who it's for

- White wine lovers who want to get to know Verdejo properly.
- Fine-dining fans looking for a special day out.
- Couples celebrating something.
