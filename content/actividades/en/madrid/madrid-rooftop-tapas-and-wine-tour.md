## What you'll see

This is a 4-hour route through three central Madrid neighbourhoods with three sit-down restaurant stops and one rooftop closing stop. The structure is more substantial than the standard 3-hour tapas crawl.

- **Las Letras**, the historic literary quarter. First restaurant stop, around 25 minutes for the first round of tapas + paired drinks.
- **La Latina**, the traditional tapas neighbourhood. Second restaurant stop, around 40 minutes for the main course.
- **Los Austrias**, the old royal neighbourhood. Visitor centre + third restaurant for more tapas, market visit included.
- **Rooftop**, on the evening tour only. One hour for cava and conversation with views over central Madrid. The morning tour replaces this with a fourth tapas stop.

## How it works

Meet your guide at Plaza de San Miguel, next to the iconic Mercado de San Miguel. The exact location is shared at booking. Arrive 10-15 minutes early.

The guide is bilingual — Spanish and English. The narration adapts to the group: fully Spanish groups get Spanish, fully English groups get English, mixed groups get both. Recent reviews suggest the mixed-language balance can vary by guide, so if you specifically want Spanish-only or English-only narration, mention it to the guide at the start.

The route is 4 hours covering walking + three sit-down stops + the rooftop close. The pace is more leisurely than a 2.5-3 hour crawl: longer stops at each restaurant, more conversation, more food per person.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Spanish and English speakers who want a bilingual tour with quality food.
- Anyone who wants the rooftop closing experience — book the evening (6 pm) for the cava ending.
- Travellers in wheelchairs or with mobility requirements — this is one of the few accessible Madrid tapas tours.
- Couples and small parties who want a 4-hour leisurely format rather than a rushed crawl.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Travellers booking the morning tour expecting a rooftop — the morning slot skips the rooftop. Book evening if the rooftop matters.
- Anyone wanting depth on a single neighbourhood. This tour covers three.
- Travellers with severe dietary restrictions where the menu can't be adapted reliably.
- Budget-sensitive travellers — €83 is mid-market. Gourmet Madrid at €77 or Vine Travel at €95 are alternatives.
