## How the visit works

You leave at 10 am from the Salamanca district for a hand-picked **family winery** 30-45 minutes from Madrid. Depending on the winery you walk through the **vineyard** or visit a **historic cave**, see how the wine is made and finish with a **guided tasting of at least 3 wines** from local grapes.

The pace is relaxed, with time for questions and to enjoy the setting before heading back.

## Who it's for

- Anyone short on time who wants a wine experience in half a day.
- Couples and solo travellers.
- Beginners: the sommelier pitches the explanation to the group.
