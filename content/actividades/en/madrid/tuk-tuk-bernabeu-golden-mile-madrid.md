## What you'll see

A **1 h 15 min route** through the Madrid of grand avenues. You start at the **Neptune Fountain**, pass **Cibeles and Plaza de Colón**, and head up the Castellana to the **AZCA** business district and **Santiago Bernabéu Stadium**. You return via **Plaza de los Delfines, El Viso and the Salamanca district**, ride along **Calle Serrano** and finish past **Puerta de Alcalá, the Retiro, the Stock Exchange and the Ritz and Palace hotels**.

## How it works

The tuk-tuk waits at **Plaza de Cánovas del Castillo**, opposite the Thyssen Museum. It's **private**, seats **up to 4 people** and the price is per tuk-tuk. Commentary plays through **headphones in 11 languages**. It doesn't include entry to the stadium: it's a panoramic ride.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Football fans who want to see the Bernabéu from outside as part of a city ride.
- Anyone who already knows the historic centre and wants another side of Madrid.
- Groups of up to 4 who want to split the price.
