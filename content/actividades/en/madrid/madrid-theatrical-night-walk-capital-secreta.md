## What the walk is like

It's an **evening walking tour** of central Madrid, one hour long and in a group. Before you set off you're given the **audio equipment** and a **period cloak**: the idea is that the characters from each period treat you as one of their own.

Along the way you discover the capital's **stories, legends and secrets**, told theatrically.

## Good to know

- Starts at 8:30 pm.
- The exact meeting point comes with your booking confirmation.
- The booking is **non-refundable**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone after a short, unusual evening plan in the centre.
- Couples and friends who enjoy urban legends.
- Visitors who've seen the landmarks and want another way to experience them.
