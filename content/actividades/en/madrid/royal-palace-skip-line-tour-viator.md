## What you'll see

2-hour tour of Madrid's Royal Palace with skip-the-line entry, offered on Viator. **It's the same Golden Tour Guide tour that appears on GetYourGuide** — same product, same quality, same structure.

- **Throne Room and Banquet Hall:** the two most representative halls of the palace, with royal canopy, lions and Tiépolo's vault.
- **Royal Apartments and Charles III's:** the rooms where the royal family lived, with original furniture.
- **Gasparini Chamber:** Italian Rococo commissioned by Charles III, one of the most photogenic halls.
- **Royal Armoury:** armours, swords and royal items (when not under refurbishment).

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Madrid Souvenirs, Calle de Carlos III, 1 — **INSIDE the souvenir shop** in front of the palace. The guide carries a **«Guía Turístico Dorado» / «Golden Tour Guide»** sign. Don't go to the palace entrance: they won't find you there.

**Arrive 15 minutes early** for check-in. **If you arrive late you lose the entry and there's no refund.**

The tour lasts **exactly 2 hours**, in a group of up to **30 people** with included headphones. Languages: Spanish or English.

**Warning:** per recent reviews, the palace can temporarily close access due to overcrowding (palace decision, not the operator's). On peak days (Saturdays, Sundays and holidays), expect extra waits even with priority entry.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who prefers booking on Viator by habit or because they already have an account.
- Anyone valuing the Viator-Tripadvisor integration (reviews consolidate from both platforms).
- Anyone who wants the Golden Tour Guide operator without preferring the booking platform.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone needing wheelchair accessibility: Viator marks this product as NOT adapted (GYG does). Better book the GYG version.
- Anyone wanting a small group: here it's up to 30 people. Look at the palace's small-group versions.
- Anyone who suspects they might arrive late: here you lose the entry without refund.
- Anyone needing the Royal Collections Gallery in the same ticket: it's not included here.
