## What you'll see

Ávila and Segovia are just over an hour from Madrid and are two of the best-preserved historic cities in Castile, both UNESCO World Heritage Sites. This full-day trip covers them with an official guide and, unlike the cheaper versions, actually goes inside the key monuments rather than just seeing them from the outside.

- **Walls of Ávila:** the most complete medieval fortifications in Spain, raised in the 11th century, encircling the entire old town.
- **Basilica of San Vicente (entry included):** one of the finest examples of Romanesque architecture in Spain, just outside the walls.
- **Church of Saint Teresa of Jesus:** built in the 17th century by the Discalced Carmelites on the spot where, by tradition, the saint was born.
- **Aqueduct of Segovia:** the city's great Roman engineering work, with its double row of granite arches raised without mortar.
- **Cathedral and Alcázar of Segovia (entries included):** the Gothic cathedral dedicated to Our Lady of the Assumption and the Alcázar fortress, on the crag where the Eresma and Clamores rivers meet.

## How it works

You are met by the **Fun and Tickets team at calle San Bernardo, 7**, in central Madrid. Arrive about 15 minutes early for check-in and don't confuse the spot with other operators on the same street. Departure is by air-conditioned coach with WiFi on board.

The first stop is **Ávila**, just over an hour from Madrid. There you walk the walls, the Basilica of San Vicente — with entry included — the Plaza Mayor, the Cathedral and the church of Saint Teresa. Then there is a **break with free time** during which a snack and a drink are included.

Next, the coach continues to **Segovia**. The guided visit starts at the Roman Aqueduct and continues to the Cathedral and the Alcázar, both with **entry included**. The visit uses an **audio system with headsets**, so you can follow the guide easily even in a large group. The full day lasts around 9 hours including travel, returning to the departure point in Madrid.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If it's your first time in Ávila and Segovia and you want to go inside the monuments, not just see them from outside.
- If you'd rather not drive or organise trains and buses to link the two cities.
- If you want the tickets to Segovia Cathedral, the Alcázar and the Basilica of San Vicente already sorted.
- If you value having an official guide to put what you see into context at each stop.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you want to wander calmly and without a clock. It's two cities in 9 hours; if you're after a relaxed visit, give each one its own day.
- If mobility is an issue. Ávila and Segovia have slopes, steps and cobblestones, and this format is not accessible.
- If you only care about the exteriors and budget is the priority. For that there is the [Ávila and Segovia budget day trip](/en/cities/madrid/activities/avila-segovia-budget-day-trip), cheaper and without entries.
