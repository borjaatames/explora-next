## What you'll see

Ávila, Segovia and El Escorial are three of the great names around Madrid, and this private tour links them in a single day with an exclusive guide and vehicle for your group.

- **Ávila:** a stop at the Cuatro Postes viewpoint, with the classic view of the walled city, and a walking route through the old town, the cathedral and the walls.
- **Segovia:** the Roman aqueduct, the cathedral area and the Alcázar, with the guide's explanations and free time to wander.
- **Monastery of El Escorial (entry included):** Philip II's vast complex — palace, monastery and royal pantheon of the Habsburgs — visited inside with the guide.
- **A route at your pace:** being private, the guide adapts the timing to the group within the day.

## How it works

This is a **private tour with hotel pickup**. The operator confirms the time and exact spot after booking, depending on where you stay; pickup is included for hotels in central Madrid. Being punctual matters: with three destinations to link, the schedule is tight and latecomers can't be waited for without throwing off the day.

The day starts early towards **Ávila**, with a stop at the Cuatro Postes viewpoint before walking the city. Then the vehicle heads to **Segovia**, where the guide explains the aqueduct, the cathedral and the Alcázar, with some free time. The last stop is the **Monastery of El Escorial**, visited inside with the included entry.

The guide is dedicated **to your group alone** throughout the day, without alternating languages with other travellers. The total duration is around **11 hours**, returning to Madrid at the end of the day.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If you're travelling as a group or family and want a guide and vehicle exclusively for you.
- If you'd rather set your own pace than go with a large group.
- If you value the convenience of hotel pickup and not having to find a meeting point.
- If you want to see El Escorial inside on the same day as Ávila and Segovia.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you're travelling as a pair on a tight budget. The per-person price of a private tour is high in small groups; a [shared day trip](/en/cities/madrid/activities/avila-segovia-day-trip-with-tickets) delivers for much less.
- If mobility is an issue. The route combines historic centres with slopes and cobblestones and the visit to El Escorial.
- If you were expecting entries to every monument. The included one is El Escorial; in Ávila and Segovia you walk the exteriors and historic centres.
- If you'd prefer a relaxed day. Three destinations in one day is still an intense day, even private.
