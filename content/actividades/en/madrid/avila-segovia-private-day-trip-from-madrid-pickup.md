## What you'll see

In **Segovia**, the **Roman aqueduct**, the **cathedral** and the **Alcázar**. In **Ávila**, a walk along its **medieval walls**, the best preserved in Europe, and the old town with its Romanesque churches.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. The driver and guide pick you up at your Madrid hotel. Tickets are not included: the guide tells you which ones are worth it. It lasts **8 hours and 30 minutes** in total.
