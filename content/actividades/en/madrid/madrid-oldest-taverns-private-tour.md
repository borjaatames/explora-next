## What you'll see

A **private 2 h 10 min walk** through Madrid's most popular history: **its taverns**. Bars **over 100 and 200 years old** where kings, writers and ordinary people have eaten and drunk. The route takes in an 1839 restaurant frequented by the aristocracy, an 1860 tavern, a shop from 1610, a bullfighting tavern from 1895, another known as the tavern of three centuries and **the world's oldest restaurant according to Guinness**.

## How it works

The guide picks you up at your hotel or meets you at a central spot confirmed after booking. It includes **one drink and one tapa at the first and last tavern**: 2 drinks and 2 tapas per person in total. The route may vary and you won't always go inside every venue. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2**. Minimum age 12.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who likes getting to know a city through its old-school bars.
- Couples or small groups interested in history.
- Anyone after something different from museums, with a drink along the way.
