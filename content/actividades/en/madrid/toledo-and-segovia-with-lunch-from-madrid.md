## What you'll see

Two (optionally three) UNESCO World Heritage sites in one day from Madrid, with the cheapest entry-level price among combined Toledo + Segovia tours. The base ticket gets you transport, official guide and walking tours. Monument entries and lunch are paid upgrades, so you can flex the cost to your budget.

- **Toledo's old town:** walking tour through the cobbled streets of the UNESCO city, with the Jewish quarter and key viewpoints.
- **Damascene workshop:** an artisan demonstration of "damasquinado" — Toledo's thousand-year-old technique of inlaying gold and silver into black steel. Included in the base tour.
- **Toledo Cathedral interior (optional upgrade):** the Primate Cathedral entry is part of the All Inclusive package.
- **Alcázar of Segovia (optional upgrade):** the iconic castle entry is included with the upgrade (free visit inside, no internal guided tour).
- **Walking tour of Segovia:** Aqueduct, Jewish quarter, Plaza Mayor.
- **Mirador del Valle:** panoramic stop overlooking Toledo across the river Tagus.
- **Ávila walls (optional add-on):** an extra upgrade adds a stop at Ávila to see the medieval walls.

## How it works

The coach leaves from **C. de Julio Camba** (next to Plaza de las Ventas, Metro Las Ventas L2/L5). Two departure times: **07:45** (return 18:45) or **09:00** (return 20:00). You must be there **15 minutes early** with check-in done.

The order varies by day, but typically the route covers **Toledo first** (about 3 hours, including the walking tour, the damascene workshop and free time) and **Segovia after** (about 3 hours, with 1 hour of guided walk plus 2 hours free). Total duration with travel: **11 hours**.

If you booked the **All Inclusive upgrade**, lunch happens between cities and includes Toledo Cathedral entry plus Alcázar of Segovia entry. If you didn't, you'll have free time to eat on your own and skip the monument interiors.

For travelers with **children under 11**, you must bring your own approved car seat — the operator does not provide one.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If your priority is value for money: €38 is the lowest entry price for Toledo + Segovia in a day.
- If you'd rather customise (eat on your own, skip Cathedral entry, go light) — the modular pricing is friendly to that.
- If the damascene workshop sounds interesting (it's a genuine cultural detail, not a shopping trap).
- If you have flexibility on departure time and prefer the 09:00 slot (more relaxed than 07:45).

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you want everything bundled. The base price hides upgrades; if you want the All Inclusive, you'll end up around €70-80 anyway.
- If you have limited mobility. Two old towns with hills and cobbles in a day is demanding.
- If your kids are under 11 and you don't have a car seat to bring along.
- If your priority is just Toledo. The [Toledo full-day](/en/cities/madrid/activities/toledo-full-day-from-madrid) at €79 with Cathedral included is more efficient if you don't care about Segovia.
