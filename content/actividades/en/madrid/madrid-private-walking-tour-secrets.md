## What you'll see

A **private 2 h 15 min walking tour** of central Madrid's essential sights: **Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, Plaza de la Villa, the Royal Palace, Almudena Cathedral and the Royal Theatre**. Between stops the guide shows you **little-known corners and details** you'd walk straight past on your own.

## How it works

The guide **picks you up at your hotel** or meets you at **Puerta del Sol, by the Bear and Strawberry Tree**. The whole route is **on foot** and no tickets are needed. At the end you get tips on tapas bars, restaurants and things to do for the rest of your trip. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2**.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone arriving in Madrid who wants to get their bearings on day one.
- Families or groups who'd like their own guide, at their own pace.
- Anyone who wants time for questions and a list of places to eat.
