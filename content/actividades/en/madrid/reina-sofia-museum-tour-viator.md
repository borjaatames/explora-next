## What you'll see

1h15 guided tour of the Reina Sofía with Amigo Tours Spain in a group of max 15. Simultaneous bilingual ES + EN tour.

- **Picasso's Guernica:** the 1937 anti-war work, main hall.
- **Dalí and Miró halls:** Spanish surrealism and abstraction.
- **Diego Rivera:** Mexican muralism within the collection.
- **Cubism and modern art:** contextualised with 20th-century Europe.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Calle Santa Isabel, 52 — museum main entrance, next to the tall statue. **«Amigo Tours» sign**. Arrive 10-15 minutes early.

**1h15** tour, max 15 group, simultaneous bilingual ES + EN. After the tour you can stay in the museum freely.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Mixed ES + EN groups preferring a simultaneous tour.
- Anyone wanting a smaller group (15) than the standard bilingual on GYG.
- Anyone who habitually books on Viator.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Anyone looking for max 6-person groups (better MAD-05 on GYG).
- Anyone needing maximum operational reliability (there are delay/no-show cases).
- Anyone preferring 100% tour in their language without bilingual mixing.
