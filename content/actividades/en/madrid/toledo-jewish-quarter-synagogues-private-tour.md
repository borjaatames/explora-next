## What you'll see

Toledo's **Jewish quarter**: the **Santa María la Blanca** synagogue, with its horseshoe arches, and **El Tránsito**, now the Sephardic Museum; sites linked to the **Jewish and converso** communities; and, along the way, the **cathedral** and **Plaza de Zocodover**.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. You meet the guide at Plaza de Zocodover. Monument tickets are included. It lasts **3 hours** in total.
