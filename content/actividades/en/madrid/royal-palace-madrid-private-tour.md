## What you'll see

A **private 2 h 30 min tour** of the **Royal Palace of Madrid**, the largest royal palace in Western Europe, with over 3,000 rooms. You visit the **state apartments**: the **Throne Room**, the gala halls with their painted ceilings and chandeliers, and the rooms displaying pieces such as the **crown and sceptre**. The guide explains how it was built, what each room was used for and which artists decorated it.

## How it works

The guide **picks you up at your hotel** and walks you to the palace, or you meet in front of the entrance on **Plaza de la Armería**. **You choose the start time**: 10:00-15:00 in winter and 10:00-17:00 in summer. **Skip-the-line entry** is included. The price is **per person, with a minimum of 2**. It isn't wheelchair accessible.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to understand the palace, not just walk through it.
- Families or small groups who'd like their own guide and schedule.
- Visitors combining it with Almudena Cathedral, right across the square.
