## What you'll see

A **3-hour midday walk** through the streets of 18th-century Madrid and the **La Latina** quarter, stopping at **three taverns**. You start with homemade croquettes and a Toledo red aged in clay jars, pass **Puerta del Sol** and head into La Latina's tavern district to try **cured meats, cheeses and wines from Rioja, Ribera del Duero and Rías Baixas**.

## How it works

The meeting point is the **statue in the middle of Plaza de Isabel II** (Ópera). The tour ends nearby with a **traditional lunch** of tapas such as **paella, tortilla, calamari, patatas bravas or morcilla**, plus the area's signature tostas. The guide explains the history of Spanish wine and of Madrid along the way. A vegetarian option is available if requested when booking.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Wine lovers who want to get to know Spain's main wine regions.
- Anyone who prefers a lunchtime plan to a late dinner.
- Couples or groups of friends who want to eat well in the centre.
