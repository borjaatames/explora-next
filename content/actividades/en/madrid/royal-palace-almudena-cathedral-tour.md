## What you'll see

2-hour tour combining two key Madrid landmarks: the Royal Palace and the Almudena Cathedral, connected by a walking tour through the old town.

- **Royal Palace:** the main halls (Throne Room, royal apartments, furniture and artworks) with an official guide and early skip-the-line entry.
- **Almudena Cathedral:** Madrid's metropolitan cathedral, consecrated by John Paul II in 1993. Exterior visit; see FAQ about interior access.
- **Plaza Mayor:** the 17th-century arcaded square with the equestrian statue of Philip III.
- **San Miguel Market:** the iron-and-glass gastronomic market, right next to Plaza Mayor.
- **Plaza de la Villa:** one of the oldest urban ensembles in the centre, with the Casa de la Villa, Casa de Cisneros and Torre de los Lujanes.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Calle Mayor 43, **Fun and Tickets** office. **Arrive 10 minutes early**.

The tour starts with an 800-900 metre walk through Plaza Mayor, San Miguel Market and Plaza de la Villa before reaching the Almudena Cathedral and the Royal Palace. The **order may vary** depending on palace schedules.

Palace entry is **early and skip-the-line** (express security). Although this avoids the main queue, security itself can be delayed at times by capacity or events — outside the operator's control.

**Gratuities are included** in the price (unusual on GetYourGuide).

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone wanting Royal Palace and Almudena in a single tour without organising two.
- Anyone who values the old-town walk (Plaza Mayor, San Miguel, Plaza de la Villa) integrated into the visit.

- Anyone needing the reassurance of a product with 1,000+ reviews: here it's just 9.
- Anyone after the lowest Royal Palace price (the standalone option is €38).
- Anyone travelling in a large group in a hurry: the tour's pace combines walking + two visits.
