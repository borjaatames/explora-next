## What it is exactly

This is a **private transport service**, not a guided tour. A driver picks you up at the door of your Madrid hotel, takes you to **Toledo** (about an hour's drive) and brings you back the same day. In Toledo **you are on your own**: no guide and no tickets are included.

## How it works

Departure is at **9:00 am**. The driver drops you in Toledo and collects you about **8 hours after departure**; if you would rather head back earlier, tell him and he adjusts the time. The vehicle is **just for your group**, from 1 to 6 people, and the price is the same whatever the number: **€265.20 in total**.

## Who it is worth it for

Families and groups of 4 to 6, anyone travelling with older relatives or small children, and anyone who wants their own timetable without depending on trains. If there are two of you and you want to save money, the AVE from Atocha station costs a good deal less.
