## What you'll see

Ávila and Segovia are just over an hour from Madrid and can be linked in a single day. This trip covers both with a bilingual guide and walking tours, focused on the heritage you can see from the street.

- **Walls of Ávila:** the best-preserved medieval fortifications in Europe, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, walked from the outside during the walking tour. The visit also includes the places linked to Saint Teresa of Jesus.
- **Aqueduct of Segovia:** the great Roman engineering work, raised without mortar, the city's symbol.
- **Segovia's old town:** the walking tour passes the Gothic Cathedral and the area around the Alcázar, seen from the outside.
- **Alcázar of Segovia (optional):** there is an option that adds the guided visit inside the castle that inspired Disney's Snow White castle, at an extra cost.

## How it works

The meeting point is **LOCAL 13 of the Gran Galería shopping centre**, at calle San Bernardo, 5, in central Madrid. Departure is at **09:00**; it is worth arriving 15 minutes early.

The route links **Ávila** and **Segovia** with a walking tour in each and free time. The narration is **bilingual Spanish-English**. It's important to be clear on the format: **the monuments are seen from outside**; neither the Cathedral nor the Alcázar of Segovia is included, though there is a paid option that adds the visit inside the Alcázar.

The full day lasts around **11.5 hours** including travel, returning to the departure point in Madrid. The group can be large, up to 50 people.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If you want to see Ávila and Segovia in one day and you're happy to see them from outside with a guide explaining them.
- If you'd rather not drive or organise transport to link the two cities.
- If you value a very well-rated operator: it has one of the highest scores in the cluster.
- If you are travelling with young children: under 4s go free.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you were expecting to enter the monuments. This trip is exteriors only; for the entries there is the [version with tickets](/en/cities/madrid/activities/avila-segovia-day-trip-with-tickets) or the [Segovia tour with the Cathedral and the Alcázar](/en/cities/madrid/activities/segovia-cathedral-alcazar-day-trip).
- If large groups bother you: it can reach 50 people.
- If mobility is an issue. The walking tours go through historic centres with slopes and cobblestones.
- If you want to wander without rushing: it's two cities in a long day, with time tightly measured.
