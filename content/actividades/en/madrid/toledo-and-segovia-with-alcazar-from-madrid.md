## What you will see

Two UNESCO World Heritage cities in a single day:

- **Segovia:** the **Roman aqueduct**, the old town and the **Alcázar**, with entry included.
- **Toledo:** a walk through the old town, a panoramic photo stop and free time.
- **Toledo Cathedral:** a guided visit inside if you choose the option that includes it.

## How it works

Departure is from **Calle de Julio Camba, 13**, next to Las Ventas (metro Ventas). Look for the **IBE Tours** coach and sign.

The drive to Segovia takes an hour. There you get a guided walk, free time and Alcázar entry. Then another hour to Toledo, where you spend about 4 hours between the guided walk, photo stop and free time. About 12 hours in total. The order of the cities may change.

## Who I recommend it for

- Travellers with **one day** who want to see **Segovia and Toledo**.
- Anyone looking for the **best price**.
- People happy with a big-coach group.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- If you want time to enjoy each city slowly: it is two cities in one day.
- People with **reduced mobility**: both cities are hilly.
- If you want a guide in one language only: explanations alternate English and Spanish.
