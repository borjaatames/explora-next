## What you will see

The essentials of Toledo's UNESCO-listed old town in a small group:

- **Plaza de Zocodover**, the heart of the city.
- **The Jewish Quarter and the convent district**, with the story of the three cultures.
- **The outside of Toledo Cathedral** and the medieval streets of the centre.
- **One hour of free time** to go inside wherever you like or grab a drink.

## How it works

You meet at **Calle de Atocha, 118**, opposite Atocha station, 20 minutes before departure. The high-speed train takes about 30 minutes to Toledo.

There, the official guide walks you through the old town with a group of **up to 8 people**, followed by an hour of free time. You return by train too. About 5 hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- Travellers **short on time** who want to be back in Madrid by mid-afternoon.
- Anyone who prefers a **small group** to a big coach.
- People who prefer the **train** to the road.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- If you want to **go inside the Cathedral and Santo Tomé** with the guide: see the full-day tour.
- People with **reduced mobility**: there are slopes and steps.
- If you tend to run late: the train won't wait and there is no refund.
