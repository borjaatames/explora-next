## What you'll see

In the morning, the **Royal Palace**, **Almudena Cathedral** and **San Miguel Basilica**, with a stop to see the gardens of the Zarzuela Palace. After lunch (on your own), the tour continues to the **Royal Monastery of San Lorenzo de El Escorial**, Philip II's great complex: basilica, library and royal pantheons.

## How it works

It is a **private tour just for your group**: nobody else joins and you set the pace. The guide picks you up at your Madrid accommodation and you travel in a private vehicle. Monument tickets are included; lunch is not. It lasts **10 hours** in total.
