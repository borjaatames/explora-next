## What you'll experience

A **five-hour night out through four venues in central Madrid**: three bars or pubs, plus one big nightclub to close the night. Every stop comes with a free welcome shot, and tips are already included in the price, so there are no last-minute surprises. The exact route varies by day of the week, and as part of the group you get drink discounts at some of the centre's best nightclubs.

## How it works

The meeting point is **Enbabia Infuse Bar**, on Calle de Núñez de Arce 9, just off Plaza Santa Ana. That's where you swap your ticket for a wristband before the route starts. There's a **dress code**: no sportswear, and men can't wear sandals or shorts. The activity is 18+ only, and it isn't suitable for wheelchairs, strollers or pets; it's also not recommended if you have back or heart problems, or are pregnant. If you have questions before booking, you can email the operator directly at info@pubcrawl.madrid.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- International groups, thanks to the guide speaking six languages.
- Anyone who prefers a flat price with tips already covered.
- Anyone who wants flexibility to change plans, with free cancellation up to 24h before.
