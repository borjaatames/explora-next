## What you'll see

A **2-hour tuk-tuk ride** past Madrid's most iconic landmarks, at a brisk pace and without the waits of a tourist bus. You'll pass **Gran Vía, the Royal Palace and the Salamanca district**, plus corners that bigger vehicles can't reach, like the **cobbled streets of La Latina**. The driver shares curious stories about the city along the way, and there's plenty of time to take photos as you go.

## How it works

The tour departs from **Plaza de la Villa**, right in the historic center, and it's priced **per tuk-tuk, not per person** — good value if there are several of you. Each tuk-tuk seats multiple people, making it a solid option for families or groups of friends. Child seats are available if requested when booking, and the vehicle is stroller accessible, though **not wheelchair accessible**. If the meeting point is hard to reach on foot, pickup is arranged at the nearest accessible spot.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone short on time in Madrid who wants a general feel for the city without walking for miles.
- Families or groups who want to split the cost of a private activity.
- Anyone hoping to see corners like La Latina that tourist bus routes usually miss.
