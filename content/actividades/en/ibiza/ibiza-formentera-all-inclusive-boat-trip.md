## The three versions

You pick your version when you book. All of them are all-inclusive on board — buffet breakfast, paella, open bar, water sports and music.

- **Essential (about 5 hours):** the morning at sea. You sail from Ibiza to the turquoise waters of Espalmador and Ses Illetes, with stops to swim, music and food on board.
- **Explorer (about 10 hours):** everything in the Essential, plus disembarking in Formentera with **4–6 hours of free time** to explore its beaches, villages and beach bars, and a flexible ferry back (17:00, 18:00, 19:00 or 20:00).
- **Full Experience – Sunset Party (about 12 hours):** everything in the Essential, plus around **6 hours free in Formentera** and the **Sunset Boat Party** on the way back — two DJ stages (Latin rhythms on the lower deck, Ibiza electronic on the upper), dinner on board (Hot Dog Party & Popcorn) and a shot corner with Ibizan herbal liquors and limoncello.

## What's included

- Boat crossing Ibiza–Formentera with swim stops
- Buffet breakfast: cold cuts, fresh fruit, bread, jams, pastries, coffee, teas and juices
- Ibizan paella for lunch, with vegetarian and vegan options
- Open bar all trip: beer, sangria, soft drinks, mojitos and the Blue Formentera cocktail
- Water sports: paddle surf, snorkelling, water slide and floats
- Music on board throughout

Not included: hotel transfers, personal expenses on land in Formentera, and tips.

## How it works

You make your own way to the **Port of Ibiza** (the exact boarding point is confirmed when you book) and should **arrive at least 60 minutes before departure** — bring your ticket, printed or digital, as you can't board without it. Departure times can shift slightly with the weather. Bring a towel, swimwear, sun cream and some cash for extras.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone after the classic Ibiza summer day: Formentera's water, open bar and water sports all sorted.
- Groups and friends who want a full beach day with food, drinks and music included.
- If you want the party finale, go for the Full Experience with the sunset boat party; if you just want the morning swim, the Essential is enough.
