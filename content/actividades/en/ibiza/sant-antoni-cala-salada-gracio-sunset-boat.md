## What you will see

- **Cala Salada** and **Cala Gració**, north of Sant Antoni.
- The rocky walls of **Punta Galera**.
- The **sunset** off Café del Mar and Mambo.

## How it works

You leave the Sant Antoni seafront heading north. The boat stops at the coves so you can swim, snorkel, try paddleboarding or jump in, with an open bar, tapas and fruit on board. In the late afternoon it returns to the bay so you can watch the sunset from the sea.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **groups of friends**.
- Anyone who wants the **Sant Antoni sunset** without the promenade crowds.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **small group**: up to 35 people can join.
- Anyone wanting an **alcohol-free** trip.
