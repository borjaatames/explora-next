## What you will see

- Arrival at **La Savina** port.
- **Ses Illetes**, **Migjorn** and other beaches, at your own pace.

## How it works

Go to the Trasmapi terminal in Ibiza port, swap the voucher and take whichever ferry suits you. In 30 minutes you're in La Savina; come back whenever you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to explore **Formentera independently**.
- Anyone **staying overnight** in Formentera.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone preferring an all-inclusive **guided trip**.
