Half a day sailing **Ibiza's** coast on a **sailboat just for you**: crystal-clear coves, swim stops, **snorkelling** and **paddle boarding**.

## Who it's for

- Families and groups of friends.
- Anyone who wants a sea day away from crowded boats.
