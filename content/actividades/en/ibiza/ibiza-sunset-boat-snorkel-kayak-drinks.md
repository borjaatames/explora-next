## What you will see

- Ibiza's **west coast**, between Sant Antoni and the coves of Cala Comte and Cala Bassa.
- Clear water for **swimming**, **snorkelling** or **kayaking**.
- The **sunset** from the sea on the way back to port.

## How it works

You board a classic wooden-decked boat at Sant Antoni port. After about 90 minutes of sailing and a safety briefing, the boat anchors in a cove and you have free time to swim or take out a kayak or paddleboard. Then it heads back towards Sant Antoni as the sun goes down.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** after a relaxed afternoon at sea.
- **Families**: children under 5 go free.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **very small group**: up to 25 people can join.
- Anyone who needs a **hotel transfer**.
