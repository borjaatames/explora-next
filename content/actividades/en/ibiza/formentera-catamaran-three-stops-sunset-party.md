## What you will see

- The turquoise water of **Espalmador** and **Ses Illetes**, from the boat.
- **Formentera** at your own pace, with around six hours for beaches and villages.
- **Es Vedrà** on the horizon during the return party.

## How it works

You leave Platja d'en Bossa at 9:15 AM with breakfast on board. The boat anchors at Espalmador and then Ses Illetes for swimming, paddleboarding, snorkelling and the waterslide, you have a Mediterranean lunch buffet and go ashore at La Savina around 1:15 PM. After your free time you reboard for about two and a half hours of DJ party and dinner back to Ibiza.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and **stag or hen parties** wanting beach and party in one day.
- Anyone who wants to see **Formentera** without arranging ferries or meals.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Families with children**: it's adults only.
- Anyone looking for a **quiet** day or prone to seasickness.
