## What you will see

- The **Sant Antoni coast** from a sailing yacht.
- A turquoise **cove** where you anchor for a swim.
- The **sunset** from the sea, if you pick the evening departure.

## How it works

You meet the crew at the entrance of Sant Antoni marina and board the yacht. You sail along the coast with cava, wine and snacks, anchor in a cove to swim, paddleboard or snorkel, and return to port after about four hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **families** looking for a relaxed time at sea.
- Groups wanting to **celebrate something** privately.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a **party boat** with a DJ.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
