## What you will see

- The west coast's **cliffs** and **sea caves**.
- **Hidden coves** only reachable by sea.
- Seabeds with **posidonia** and fish.

## How it works

After a short safety briefing at Cala Codolar, you paddle along the coast with the guide, go into caves, stop to snorkel and swim, and have a snack before returning to the beach.

## Who I recommend it for

- Active **couples** and **families**.
- Anyone who prefers the sea **without engines or music**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
