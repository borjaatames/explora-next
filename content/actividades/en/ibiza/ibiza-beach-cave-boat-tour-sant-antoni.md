## What you will see

- **Beaches** and **coves** on the Sant Antoni coast.
- Nature-reserve **islets**, **caves** and reefs.
- Celebrity villas overlooking the sea.

## How it works

You leave from the Sant Antoni seafront and the boat covers the north or south coast depending on the day, with music and commentary from the guide. At the stops you can swim, sunbathe, snorkel or go paddleboarding before heading back to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **see a lot of coastline** in little time.
- **Couples** and **friends** who prefer cruising to sitting at anchor.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **People with reduced mobility**.
- Anyone after an **open bar** on board.
