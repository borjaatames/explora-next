## What you will see

- **Coastal tracks** and cliffs.
- **Traditional villages** in Ibiza's interior.
- **Beaches** and spots off the usual circuit.

## How it works

You arrive at the Sant Josep shop 20 minutes early, the instructor shows you how to handle the quad and you set off in a small group for two and a half hours. There are several stops and, in high season, a longer one for a swim or a drink.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** who want to drive and enjoy some adventure.
- Anyone keen to see **rural** Ibiza.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Pregnant women** or people with back problems.
- Under-25s who want to drive.
