## What you will see

- **Espalmador** and **Ses Illetes** from the water, with time to swim.
- **Formentera** at your own pace for most of the day.
- The ferry ride into **Ibiza**, with Dalt Vila ahead.

## How it works

You board at Platja d'en Bossa at 9:15 AM and have breakfast on board. The catamaran anchors at Espalmador and Ses Illetes for swimming, paddleboarding or snorkelling, serves a Mediterranean lunch buffet and drops you at La Savina around 1:15 PM. From there the island is yours: you return to Ibiza on whichever regular ferry suits you, ticket included.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **families** who want Formentera without a party.
- Anyone who prefers to **choose when to head back**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **boat party**: the sunset party version is for that.
- Anyone wanting a **guided tour** in Formentera: on land you're on your own.
