## What you will see

- **Cala Salada**, **Cala Comte**, **Cala Bassa** and **Cala Gració**.
- **Caves** and hidden corners of the Sant Antoni coast.
- Cliffs and turquoise water from a traditional boat.

## How it works

You board a llaut in Sant Antoni and cruise the coast with chill-out music, sangria, beer or soft drinks, tapas and fruit. The boat stops at coves such as Cala Comte or Cala Bassa to swim, snorkel, try paddleboarding or the sea scooter, and visits hidden caves. There are morning, afternoon and sunset sessions.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **small groups** after a relaxed plan.
- Anyone who wants a boat with **local character** rather than a big one.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People **prone to seasickness**.
- Anyone looking for a **party boat**.
