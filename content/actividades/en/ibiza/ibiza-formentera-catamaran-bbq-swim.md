## What you will see

- Ibiza's south coast and **Ses Salines Natural Park**.
- The turquoise water and sandbars of **Ses Illetes**, Formentera.
- **Dalt Vila** as you leave and return to Ibiza port.

## How it works

You leave Ibiza port by catamaran and sail about 90 minutes along the south coast to Formentera. The boat anchors off Ses Illetes and you have time to swim, snorkel or sunbathe on deck. Salads, BBQ, fruit and soft drinks are served on board before you head back to Ibiza. There is a daytime and a sunset departure.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples**, **friends** and **families** who want to see Formentera from the sea.
- Anyone who prefers a **stable**, spacious boat.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **explore Formentera** on land.
- Anyone after an included **open bar**.
