## What you will see

- **Dirt tracks** and country lanes in inland Ibiza.
- **Viewpoints** over the Mediterranean and its cliffs.
- Little-known **coves** along the way.

## How it works

You start in Sant Antoni with a safety briefing and follow the guide in your own buggy. The route mixes dirt tracks, rural roads and scenic stretches, with photo stops and cold drinks included, and returns to the start after about two and a half hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** looking for an adrenaline rush.
- Anyone wanting to see **rural** Ibiza, away from the beaches.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers**: the per-buggy price adds up.
- Anyone without a **driving licence**.
