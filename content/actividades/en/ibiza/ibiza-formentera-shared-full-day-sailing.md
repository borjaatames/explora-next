## What you'll see

A full day sailing from **Ibiza** to **Formentera**, **Espalmador**, **Salinas** or **Es Vedrà**, with swim stops, **snorkelling** and **paddle boarding**.

## How it works

Depart from Cala Jondal on a 41-foot sailboat shared with other travellers (up to 7). Bring food: there's a fridge on board. From **€180 per person**.

## Who it's for

- Couples and solo travellers wanting a good-value boat day.
- Not for those who prefer a private boat.
