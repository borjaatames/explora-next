## What you will see

- **Cala Bassa** and **Cala Comte**, two of Ibiza's best beaches.
- **Es Vedrà** and Sa Conillera from the sea.
- A **secluded bay** for swimming and paddleboarding.

## How it works

You board in Sant Antoni harbour and cruise the west coast. You spend an hour ashore at Cala Bassa and another at Cala Comte, eat on board and finish in a quiet bay with paddleboarding, snorkelling and slides before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and **families**.
- Anyone wanting a **beach day** with everything sorted.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **small, quiet** boat.
