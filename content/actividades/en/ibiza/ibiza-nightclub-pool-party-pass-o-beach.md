## What you will see

- Daytime **pool parties** such as O Beach or Blue Marlin.
- Sant Antoni **nightclubs** such as Eden or Es Paradis.
- Ibiza bars with **2-for-1 drinks** and discounts.

## How it works

You buy the pass, activate it and pick three venues from the list. Each one has its own days and a cut-off time for free entry, so it's worth planning ahead. You also get a discount passport for restaurants, bars and activities that you show at each place.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** spending several nights out in Ibiza.
- Anyone who wants to **save on entry** and doesn't mind arriving early.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone heading to a **specific club late at night**: free entry ends at a set time.
- Anyone chasing **headliners** at the big clubs that aren't on the list.
