## What you will see

- **Sa Caleta**, with its red cliffs and Phoenician settlement.
- The village of **Es Cubells** and its Mediterranean views.
- The **Es Vedrà** viewpoint.
- The **Time and Space** monument near Cala Comte.

## How it works

You are picked up at an agreed point and travel in an air-conditioned vehicle to the four stops, with time at each to enjoy the view and take photos. The guide photographs you and can adjust the route if you have somewhere in mind.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and small groups who want great photos of Ibiza.
- Anyone travelling **without a car** who wants to see the south-west of the island.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who would rather spend the day on the **beach** or in the water.
- Anyone after a tour heavy on historical commentary.
