## What you will see

- **Cala Conta** and its turquoise water.
- The **Hermit's Cave** (Sa Figuera) and the **Time and Space** sculpture at Cala Llentia.
- **Mountain** tracks and the rural landscape of western Ibiza.

## How it works

You start at the Sant Antoni base with a safety talk and route briefing. Then you follow the guide in your own buggy along dirt tracks and back roads, stopping for photos, and return to the same spot about two and a half hours later.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** who enjoy driving.
- Anyone wanting to see the **west coast** away from crowded beaches.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers**: the per-buggy price adds up.
- Drivers **under 21** or without a licence.
