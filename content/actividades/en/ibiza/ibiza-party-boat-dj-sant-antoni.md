## The party

It starts with a **2-hour pre-party at Rio Ibiza** on the Sant Antoni seafront. Then **3 hours on the boat** with a DJ, the **Pukka Up** dancers, a photographer and a videographer, cruising off the coast with the music up loud.

## Day or sunset

There is a daytime departure and, on **Tuesdays and Thursdays**, a sunset one with Sant Antoni's famous sunset from the water.

## What's included

Three drinks: one welcome drink and two beers or sangrias. Food is not included; you can buy it at the pre-party or snack on board.

## Who it's for

Over-18s in Ibiza to party, especially groups of friends and stag or hen parties.
