## What you will see

- The **Margalides islands** (Ses Margalides) and their natural arch.
- Ibiza's **north coast**, with cliffs and coves such as Cala Salada.
- Spots like **Punta Galera** or the Gates of Heaven, depending on the sea.

## How it works

You arrive at the starting point on Sant Antoni bay, get a safety talk and a basic introduction to handling the jet ski, and put on your life jacket. Then you follow the instructor along the north coast to the Margalides islands, stopping for photos, and return to the start after an hour.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** looking for some adrenaline at sea.
- First-timers who want to ride a jet ski **with a guide**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone **under 16** or **over 55**.
- People with **back or heart problems**.
