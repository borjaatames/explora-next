## What you will see

- **Cala Comte** and **Cala Bassa**.
- **Cala Tarida** from the boat.
- Coastal spots **only reachable by sea**.

## How it works

You leave Sant Antoni on a small boat, stop to swim at Cala Comte and Cala Bassa, have lunch halfway and finish with paddleboarding and snorkelling before passing Cala Tarida and heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **small groups**.
- Anyone wanting a **less crowded** boat.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Pregnant women** or children under 3.
