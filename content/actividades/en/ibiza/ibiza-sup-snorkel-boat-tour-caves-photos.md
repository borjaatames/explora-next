## What you will see

- Ibiza's **west coast**, between Cala d'Hort and the north of Sant Antoni.
- **Hidden coves** and **sea caves** at water level from your board.
- The **seabed** with mask and snorkel.

## How it works

You meet at the Sant Antoni yacht club and board the support boat, which picks the route based on the sea. At the first anchorage you get a basic lesson and head out on a paddleboard with the instructor through coves and caves, with the boat always nearby. A snorkelling session follows, then fruit, tapas and drinks back on board. The crew takes photos throughout.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **learn paddleboarding** with an instructor.
- **Couples** and **small groups** after something active.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for an **open-bar party boat**.
- **Wheelchair users**.
