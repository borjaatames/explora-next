## What you will see

- Four west-coast coves: **Cala Gracioneta**, **Cala Comte**, **Cala Salada** and **Cala Bassa**.
- A **pirate cave** and spots only reachable by sea.
- Clear water for **snorkelling**.

## How it works

You board a wooden boat with loungers and a toilet in Sant Antoni. After a safety briefing the crew sails the west coast stopping at coves, where you can swim or take out a kayak, a paddleboard or the XXL board. Open bar, tapas and fruit are served throughout the 3 hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** after activities and an open bar.
- **Families** with children who can swim and want plenty of water toys.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after an **intimate group**: up to 25 people can join.
- Anyone who wants to avoid a **party atmosphere**.
