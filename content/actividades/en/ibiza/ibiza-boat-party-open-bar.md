## What you'll see

Three hours of **boat partying** along the Ibiza coast with a **DJ** on board, an **open bar of beer and sangria** and a **swim stop** at sea.

The boat leaves in the late afternoon so the **sunset** catches you on the water.

## How it works

Boarding at 18:15 at **Playa d'en Bossa** (Carrer de la Punta 2). Over-18s only, with ID. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
