## What you will see

- **Formentera** at your own pace: beaches like **Ses Illetes** or **Migjorn** and its villages.
- The crossing between islands on a **fast ferry**.

## How it works

You exchange your voucher at the Formentera Lines office in Ibiza port and take whichever ferry suits you. In La Savina you collect the e-bike at Formotor and ride around the island freely. Return the bike before 8:00 pm and sail back to Ibiza at the time you choose.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** who want to explore Formentera independently.
- Anyone who prefers a **bike** to a car or bus.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Families with children** under 14.
- Anyone after a **guided excursion**.
