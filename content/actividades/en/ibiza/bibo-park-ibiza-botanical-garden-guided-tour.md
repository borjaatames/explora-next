## What you will see

- **Native plants** from Ibiza and Formentera, including species now extinct on the island.
- The **plant piano** and other biotech inventions, such as a microalgae photobioreactor.
- A small-scale **saltworks**, traditional beehives and a collection of local **bonsai**.

## How it works

You arrive at the park in Sant Rafel with your ticket and, if a guide is free, join a tour of around 45 minutes where you can touch, smell and taste some plants. If you'd rather explore alone, the QR audio guide is included. The visit ends with a tasting of water drawn from the air.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with curious kids.
- Anyone after a **nature and science** plan in Ibiza.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting a **large botanical garden**: it's a short visit.
- Anyone who needs a **guaranteed guided tour**: it depends on availability.
