## What you will see

- **Big Putts**, with beer pong, crazy golf and pool to break the ice.
- A **beach club** with games and cocktails.
- The **O Beach** pool party, with DJs and live performers.
- A superclub: **Eden**, **Es Paradis** or **Chinois**.

## How it works

You start at Big Putts with the hosts and the rest of the group, move on to a beach club and then head together to the O Beach pool party. The route lasts about four hours and the night carries on at the club, with entry already paid.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and solo travellers who are here to party.
- Anyone wanting O Beach and a club **in one payment**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Under-18s**.
- Anyone after a **quiet** or family-friendly Ibiza.
