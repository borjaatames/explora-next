## What you will see

- The **Dalt Vila walls** and the old town.
- **Viewpoints** over Ibiza harbour.
- **Marina Botafoch** and **Talamanca** beach.

## How it works

You pick up your bamboo bike at the rental shop and set off with your private guide. On the 2-hour version you ride through Dalt Vila, the harbour, Marina Botafoch and Talamanca, stopping for stories about the island from the hippies to the jet set. If you choose 3 or 4 hours, you can add Figueretes or Platja d'en Bossa.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups** and **families** splitting the cost of a private tour.
- Anyone who wants to see **Ibiza Town** without walking in the heat.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers or couples** on a tight budget.
- Anyone after a **long-distance** ride out of town.
