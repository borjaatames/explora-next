## What you will see

- **Cala Conta**, with its islets and turquoise water.
- **Cala Bassa**, framed by pine woods.
- **Cliffs and caves** on Ibiza's south-west coast.

## How it works

You leave from the Sant Antoni seafront and sail to Cala Conta, where the boat anchors so you can swim, snorkel or take out a paddleboard. The stop is then repeated at Cala Bassa. Open bar, tapas and fruit are served throughout.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** after sea and an open bar in one afternoon.
- Anyone who wants to see the area's **best-known coves** without driving.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **go ashore**: you swim from the boat.
- Anyone after a **small group**: up to 35 people can join.
