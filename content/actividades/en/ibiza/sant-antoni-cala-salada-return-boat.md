## What you will see

- The **Sant Antoni coast** from the sea.
- **Cala Salada**, with clear water and pine trees all around.
- **Cala Saladeta**, smaller with fine sand, a short walk away.

## How it works

You board at Sant Antoni harbour and reach Cala Salada in about 30 minutes. Spend the day on the beach and come back on the 13:00, 16:00 or 17:30 boat, whichever suits you.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** after a quiet cove without a car.
- Anyone staying in **Sant Antoni** who wants a simple beach day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a **guided trip** or multiple stops.
- Anyone expecting food and drinks to be included.
