## What you will see

- The **Espalmador** natural park.
- The turquoise water of **Ses Illetes**.
- **Formentera** on your own for around six hours.

## How it works

You board at Playa d'en Bossa and sail to Espalmador for a first swim. At Ses Illetes the boat anchors for about two hours to swim, snorkel or paddleboard and have lunch on board. It then docks on Formentera, you have the afternoon free, and at 18:00 you head back to Ibiza with music and the open bar.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and couples who like a lively atmosphere.
- Anyone wanting to see **Formentera in a day** without arranging ferries.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **quiet** or small-group boat.
- **Pregnant women**, people over 70 and children under 3.
