## What you will see

- The Sant Antoni **sunset** from the sea.
- West-coast **coves** and **caves**.
- **Es Vedrà** and Sa Conillera on the horizon.

## How it works

You leave Sant Antoni in the late afternoon, stop at one or two coves to swim, paddleboard or snorkel with drinks and tapas, and watch the sunset from the boat before returning to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **groups of friends**.
- Anyone after a **relaxed sunset**, not a party.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a **party boat**.
