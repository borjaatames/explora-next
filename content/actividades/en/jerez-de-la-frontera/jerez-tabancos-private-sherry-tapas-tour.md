## The route

You meet a **wine expert** in **Plaza del Arenal** and visit three **tabancos**, Jerez's most emblematic sherry taverns. Some seem frozen in time: this is where sherry is bought and drunk with all its flavour and atmosphere.

At each tabanco you try a **tapa** with a **drink**, while the walk takes you through some of the city's most characterful corners. It's a way into Jerez's culture and food beyond the clichés.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to understand sherry in its natural setting.
- As a companion to a bodega visit.
