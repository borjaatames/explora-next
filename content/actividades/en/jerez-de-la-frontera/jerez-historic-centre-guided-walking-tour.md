## What you will see

- The **San Miguel** quarter and its Gothic church.
- **Islamic and Christian** monuments around the **Alcázar** and its walls.
- The Gothic, Baroque and Neoclassical façades of the **cathedral**.
- Squares and palaces, the old **Cabildo** and the Mudéjar church of **San Dionisio**.

## How it works

You meet in Plaza del Arenal and walk the old town for two hours. The guide traces the city's story from the Tartessians and Phoenicians to the Romans, Visigoths and Moors. Monuments are seen from outside; with the wine option you finish with a tasting of two sherries.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** who want to get their bearings.
- Anyone more interested in **history** than in museums.
- **Groups** who prefer the private option.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers**: the minimum booking is two adults.
- Anyone who wants to see the **Alcázar inside**.
