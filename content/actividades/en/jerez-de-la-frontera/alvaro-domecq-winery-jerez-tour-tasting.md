## What you will see

- The **brandy**, **oloroso** (with an audiovisual) and **fino** cellars of a historic Jerez winery.
- The **Duque de Veragua** cellar, where the **solera and criaderas** system is explained.
- The **vinegar cellar**, home to designation-of-origin sherry vinegar.
- A **guided tasting** of the house wines.

## How it works

You meet at the winery on Calle San Luis, and for an hour and a half a guide takes you through the different cellars, explaining ageing in American oak casks. You finish with the tasting and can stop at the shop. If you added the Iberian platter when booking, it is served with the tasting.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **complete winery visit** without leaving the centre.
- **Couples** and groups keen to add some food.
- Anyone curious about Jerez **vinegar and brandy**, not just wine.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**: it is not listed as accessible.
- Non-drinkers, since the tasting is the highlight.
