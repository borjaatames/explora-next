## What you will see

- **Tabancos** and traditional bars in the old town.
- Streets, squares and monuments of **central Jerez**.
- How **tapas pair** with the different styles of sherry.

## How it works

You meet your guide in Plaza del Arenal and walk around the centre for about 2 hours. Along the way you hear the city's history and legends, and stop at three tabancos or bars for a tapa and a drink at each one.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone arriving in Jerez who wants to **get their bearings** with food and drink.
- Anyone who wants to understand **sherry styles** without a full bodega visit.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **full dinner**: the tapas are small portions.
- **Solo travellers**, as a minimum of 2 adults per booking is required.
