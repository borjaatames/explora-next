## The route

With your guide you explore **Jerez's old town**, which still keeps the feel of centuries past: its squares, streets and monuments among orange trees and fountains. You'll hear its history and legends while visiting the **Alcázar** and the **Cathedral** (tickets included).

The walk follows **Calle Larga** and **Lancería**, the main street, from the **Alameda Cristina** to **Plaza del Arenal**, passing the **Santo Domingo convent**, the **Domecq palace** and the **Gallo Azul** building, the locals' favourite meeting point.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors to Jerez who want more than bodegas.
- Groups of three or four: the price per person drops a lot.
