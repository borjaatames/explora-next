## What you will see

- Horse and rider **training** in the arenas (except Saturdays).
- The octagonal **stables** and the **tack room** with the gala harnesses.
- The 19th-century **palace** and its **gardens**.
- The **Museum of Equestrian Art**, with eleven rooms on the horse in Andalusia.
- The **carriage museum**, which closes the visit.

## How it works

You enter through the reception centre, where an audiovisual runs until 11:00, and tour the grounds with a guide: gardens, palace, museum, saddlery, indoor arena, stables and carriages. It is about two hours on foot, in groups of up to 40.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see how the **school works** from the inside.
- Anyone for whom the show's timing or price does not fit.
- **Families** with horse-loving children.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who can only go on a **Saturday**: you miss the training.
- Anyone expecting the full **equestrian ballet**.
