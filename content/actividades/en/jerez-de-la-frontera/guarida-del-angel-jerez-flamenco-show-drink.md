## What you will see

- A one-hour **flamenco show** with guitar, singing and dance.
- Artists from **Jerez** and the wider flamenco scene.
- A **stately house** in the San Miguel quarter turned into a flamenco venue.

## How it works

Arrive at La Guarida del Ángel early: doors open one or two hours before, depending on the show. If you like, order tapas or food before the performance (paid separately). Then enjoy your included drink and the show, which lasts about an hour.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **flamenco in a flamenco neighbourhood** of Jerez.
- Anyone after an **affordable** ticket with a drink included.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants **dinner included** in the price.
