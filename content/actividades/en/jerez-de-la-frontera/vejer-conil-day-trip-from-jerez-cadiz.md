## What you will see

- Vejer's **walled old town**, with its steep white streets.
- **Views** over the countryside, orchards and, in the distance, the Strait.
- **Conil de la Frontera**, a coastal town known for tuna and its food.

## How it works

You are picked up from your accommodation and after a short drive reach Vejer, where the guide walks you through the walled quarter. You then continue to Conil to see the town before heading back to the pickup point. Four hours in total, in groups of up to 50.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying around the Bay of Cádiz or in Jerez **without a car**.
- Anyone who wants to see **white villages** with historical context.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: there are many slopes.
- Anyone wanting long **free time** for lunch or the beach.
