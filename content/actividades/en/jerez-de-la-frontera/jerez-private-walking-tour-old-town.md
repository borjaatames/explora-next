## What the tour looks like

You meet your guide at **Plaza del Arenal** and start with the **Alcázar**, one of the few surviving examples of Almohad architecture. You continue to the **Cathedral**, the baroque squares and palaces and the streets of the **tabancos**, while the guide explains how Jerez, Moorish Sherish, ended up giving its name to a world-famous wine.

## How it works

It is **private** and the price is **per group**: €248 for 2 people and €356 for 4. It is a walking tour; tickets and bodegas are not included.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone spending a day in Jerez who wants more than the bodegas.
- Small groups who prefer a guide just for them.
