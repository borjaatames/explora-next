## What you'll see

A **private** tour of **Jerez de la Frontera**, your group only and with a local guide. In the morning, the historic centre: the **Cathedral**, the **Alcázar** (a former Almohad palace) and the lively **Plaza del Arenal**, with the story of how flamenco was born —not in theatres, but in courtyards, markets and nameless streets.

In the afternoon, a culinary route through four historic **tabancos**, the Jerez taverns that once sold wine by the jug and now host spontaneous flamenco, with **wine tasting, lunch and a private flamenco show**.
