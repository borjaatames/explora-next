## What you will see

- An **1886 winery** in central Jerez and its architecture.
- A short **film** on Jerez, wine and the winery itself.
- The **visitor centre**: sherry history, vineyards and the ageing system.
- A collection of **historic labels**.

## How it works

You meet at the winery on Plaza de los Silos. After an introduction to the building and a short film, a guide takes you through the visitor centre. You finish with a **tasting of four wines** and can stop by the shop if you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **first winery visit** without leaving the centre.
- **Couples** and travellers short on time.
- Anyone who prefers a **family winery** to a big brand.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **food pairing**: no food is included.
- Anyone wanting to see one of Jerez's **big historic houses**.
