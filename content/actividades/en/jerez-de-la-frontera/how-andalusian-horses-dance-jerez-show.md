## What you will see

- **Doma vaquera**: the rider steers with one hand, at a trot and a gallop, with spins and sudden bursts.
- **Classical dressage** to music: pas de deux, passage and piaffe, airs above the ground.
- **Carriage driving** with period carriages and skilled coachmen.
- **Work in hand**: haute école exercises with no rider in the saddle.
- A **closing carrousel** with several horses in unison.

## How it works

You arrive at the Royal School, swap your printed voucher at the ticket office and sit in your category's area. The show lasts an hour and a half and combines six to eight routines from the school's repertoire, which can change from day to day.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Jerez.
- **Families** and **horse lovers**.
- Anyone after a very **Andalusian** cultural plan that is easy to fit in.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Keen **photographers**: no flash and strict rules.
- Anyone wanting a guided tour of the grounds: this ticket is the show only.
