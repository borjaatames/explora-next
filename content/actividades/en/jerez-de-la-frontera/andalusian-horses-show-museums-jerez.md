## What you will see

- The **"How the Andalusian Horses Dance"** ballet: classical dressage, doma vaquera, carriage driving and carrousel.
- The **Museum of Equestrian Art**, with eleven rooms on the horse in Andalusia.
- The **carriage museum**, with period harnesses and costumes.
- The 19th-century **palace**, the **gardens** and the **saddlery**.

## How it works

You enter through the reception centre and explore the gardens, palace and museums at your own pace during the morning. The show starts at 12:00 and lasts an hour and a half. There is no guide: you choose the order, but keep each area's opening hours in mind.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **the whole Royal School** in one morning.
- **Horse lovers** and families.
- Anyone who prefers to go **at their own pace** without a guided tour.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone keen on **photography**: cameras are not allowed.
- Anyone visiting on a **Saturday or holiday** expecting to see the palace.
