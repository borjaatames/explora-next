## What you will see

- An **1886 winery** right in central Jerez.
- The **casks** and the ageing system used for sherry.
- A **visitor centre** covering the house's history, grape growing and a collection of old labels.

## How it works

You arrive at the winery on Plaza Silos and start with an introduction to the building and its production. You watch a short film about Jerez and its wine, tour the visitor centre with your guide and finish with a tasting of four wines. Afterwards you can buy wine, and the monuments are just a short walk away.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **bodega visit in the centre**, no car needed.
- Anyone who wants some **sherry history** as context before the tasting.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a tasting with **food pairing**: it isn't included.
