## What you will see

- A **flamenco show** of about 60 minutes with singing, guitar and dance.
- Styles such as **martinetes, soleá and bulerías**.
- A **1739 bodega** turned into a tablao, right in central Jerez.

## How it works

You arrive at the tablao on Calle Madre de Dios and take your seat with your included drink. The five-artist group performs for about an hour. If you booked the menu option, you eat before or after the show, never during it.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **authentic flamenco** in the city where it was born.
- Anyone after an easy evening plan in **central Jerez**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **eat while watching the show**.
