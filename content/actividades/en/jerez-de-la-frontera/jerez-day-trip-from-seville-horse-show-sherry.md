## What you will see

- A **traditional Jerez bodega** from the inside, with a tasting of its wines.
- The **"How the Andalusian Horses Dance"** show at the Royal Andalusian School of Equestrian Art.
- The **vineyards** of the countryside between Seville and Jerez.

## How it works

You leave Seville in a group of no more than 8 people and drive to Jerez. First you visit a bodega, where you learn how sherry is made and taste it. Then you head to the Royal School to watch the equestrian show and, once it ends, return to Seville. It takes about 6 hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Seville** who wants to see Jerez without driving.
- Anyone who wants **sherry and horses** in a single outing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **free time** to explore Jerez or the Royal School grounds.
- Anyone who needs to **cancel at short notice**.
