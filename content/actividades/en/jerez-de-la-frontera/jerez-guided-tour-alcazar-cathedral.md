## What you will see

- The **Alcázar of Jerez**, with its Muslim and Christian architecture.
- The **Cathedral**, inside, and its façade blending Gothic, Baroque and Neoclassical.
- **Palace houses**, the old town hall and the Mudéjar church of **San Dionisio**.

## How it works

You set off from Plaza del Arenal with your guide, pass the walled Alcázar area and go inside. Then you walk through the old town to the Cathedral, which you also visit inside. Tickets are included, so there's no queueing.

## Who I recommend it for

- First-time visitors who want to see the **essentials** of Jerez.
- Anyone who prefers monuments with **context**, not just a look around.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility** or who are **pregnant**, as the provider states.
