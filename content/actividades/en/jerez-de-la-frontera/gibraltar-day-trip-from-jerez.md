## What you will see

- The **Rock's Nature Reserve** and the Pillars of Hercules.
- **St Michael's Cave**.
- The famous **Barbary apes**.
- **Africa** across the Strait.

## How it works

You leave in the morning from the Monasterio de la Cartuja car park in Jerez and travel by coach to the border. In Gibraltar, a local guide takes you around the Rock; then you head down to Main Street for free time before returning to Jerez. On Sundays there's an extra short stop in Tarifa.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone based in **Jerez** who wants to see Gibraltar without planning it themselves.
- Anyone interested in the **Rock's nature** and the apes.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone who doesn't want **many hours on a coach**.
