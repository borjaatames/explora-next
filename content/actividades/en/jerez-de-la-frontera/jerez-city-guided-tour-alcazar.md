## What you will see

- The 12th-century Almohad **Alcázar** inside: **mosque**, **Arab baths**, gardens and the **Villavicencio Palace** with its tower.
- The **cathedral**, a mix of Baroque, Renaissance and Neoclassical.
- **Plaza del Arroyo** and its palaces.
- **Plaza de la Asunción**, with **San Dionisio** and the **old Cabildo**, ending at the Town Hall.

## How it works

You meet in Plaza del Arenal and, after seeing the Alcázar's walls and gate, go inside with the guide. You then walk on to the cathedral, Plaza del Arroyo and Plaza de la Asunción, finishing at the Town Hall. About two hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** who want the essentials with context.
- Anyone who would rather not sort out **Alcázar tickets** themselves.
- **Groups** who prefer the private option.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers**: the minimum booking is two adults.
- Anyone wanting to explore the Alcázar **slowly** at their own pace.
