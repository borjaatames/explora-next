## What you'll see

- **Girona's old town** and **city walls**, with a stop to try a **xuixo** pastry.
- **Peratallada**, one of the best-preserved medieval villages in the Empordà.
- **Calella de Palafrugell**, an old fishing village with coves and two-storey fishermen's houses.

## How it works

You meet in central Barcelona and reach Girona by minivan in about an hour for a guided walk of around 75 minutes. Then you head to Peratallada to explore it with the guide, and finish in Calella de Palafrugell with about two hours free to eat, stroll or swim. The drive back to Barcelona takes just over an hour and a half.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **Girona and the Costa Brava** in one day without driving.
- Travellers who prefer **small groups** and a relaxed pace.
- **Couples** and anyone who loves charming villages.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who needs **hotel pickup**.
- Those who want to go **inside monuments**: the visit is through the streets.
