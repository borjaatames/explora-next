## What you'll see

A guided walk through the **Call**, Girona's Jewish quarter, one of the best preserved in Europe. The Jewish community lived here from the 9th century until the expulsion of **1492**.

The guide leads you through the quarter's alleys and courtyards, tells the story of **Bonastruc ça Porta** (Nahmanides) and Girona's Kabbalah school, and explains the community's daily life.

## How it works

Two and a half hours with a guide in Spanish or English, in a group of 10 at most. There are slopes and stairs. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
