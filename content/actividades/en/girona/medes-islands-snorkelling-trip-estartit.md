## What you'll see

- The underwater life of the **Medes Islands marine reserve**, with fish and seagrass meadows.
- The seven islets of the archipelago, a mile off **L'Estartit** beach.
- The **L'Estartit coastline** from the water.

## How it works

At the Medaqua centre you're given your gear and a talk on how to use it and what you'll see. You walk to the boat and reach the islands in 10 minutes. There you snorkel in groups of 8 to 10 with a guide for about 50 minutes. Afterwards there's time to swim or sunbathe on deck and, if the sea allows, a short cruise around the islands before heading back to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children aged 6 and up.
- Anyone who wants to see **marine life** without a diving licence.
- Less confident swimmers: there's a life vest, wetsuit and a guide in the water.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who just wants a **boat ride** without getting wet.
- **Children under 6**.
