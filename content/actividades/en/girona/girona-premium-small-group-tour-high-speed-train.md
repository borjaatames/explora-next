## What you will see

- The **Jewish Quarter (El Call)**, one of the best-preserved medieval Jewish districts in Europe.
- The **Arab Baths** inside and the **cathedral** from outside.
- The **Eiffel bridge**, the Onyar houses and views from the **city walls**.

## How it works

You meet your guide in Barcelona and travel together by high-speed train to Girona, about 40 minutes. There you spend close to three hours exploring the old town on foot with entry to the Arab Baths, then take the train back to Barcelona. On Sundays the Arab Baths are swapped for the Museum of Jewish History.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with **only half a day** who wants to see Girona without driving.
- Travellers who prefer **very small groups** with more attention from the guide.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone whose date is not fixed: it cannot be cancelled.
- Anyone wanting to combine Girona with the Costa Brava on the same day.
