## What you will see

- Six **cocktails**, from classics to the bar's own creations.
- A selection of local **tapas** for each pairing.
- The story and technique behind each drink, told by the bartender.

## How it works

You arrive at Seawolf bar in central Lloret and the bartender prepares the six cocktails one by one, explaining the ingredients, technique and history. Each round comes with tapas chosen to match its flavours. The experience is private, just for your group, and lasts about an hour and a half.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and groups of friends spending a few days in Lloret.
- Anyone after a **relaxed, good-value** evening plan.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Under-18s or anyone who does not drink alcohol.
- Anyone looking for a crawl around several bars.
