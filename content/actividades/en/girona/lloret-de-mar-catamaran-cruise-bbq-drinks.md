## What you will see

- The coastline of **cliffs, caves and coves** around Lloret.
- A quiet cove where the boat anchors for swimming.
- Fishing villages from the sea.

## How it works

You board straight from Lloret beach and sail for about an hour, lying on the bow net with music and drinks. Then the catamaran anchors for around two hours: jump off the trampoline, swim, use the floating mattress or snorkel. Meanwhile the BBQ is prepared on board, and the ride back to Lloret comes with a DJ and louder music.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** (on the morning trip) and groups of friends (in the afternoon).
- Anyone after an **all-inclusive** boat day at a sensible price.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a quiet boat with few passengers: it carries up to 100 people.
- Costumed stag or hen parties, which are not accepted.
