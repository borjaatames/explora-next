## What you'll see

- Over **3,000 plant species** from five continents.
- The **bamboo forest**, arid American landscapes, Australian corners and the jungle pergola.
- The **Temple of Linnaeus**, a clifftop viewpoint over the Costa Brava.
- A historic garden, listed as a Cultural Asset of National Interest, founded by **Carl Faust**.

## How it works

You book a ticket for a set day, show it on your phone at the garden entrance and explore at your own pace, without a guide. The garden sits high above Blanes, right by the sea.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Nature** and **photography** lovers.
- **Families**: children under 4 go free.
- Anyone after a **quiet** Costa Brava plan away from the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting an **explained visit**: no guide is included.
- Anyone without fixed dates: the ticket **can't be cancelled**.
