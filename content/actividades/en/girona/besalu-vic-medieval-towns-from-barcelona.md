## What you'll see

- **Vic**, with its old town, cathedral and Roman temple (from outside).
- **Santa Pau**, a medieval village with an arcaded square in the volcanic **Garrotxa** area.
- **Castellfollit de la Roca**, perched on a basalt cliff.
- **Besalú**, its old Jewish quarter and the **Romanesque bridge**, a symbol of medieval Catalonia.

## How it works

You're picked up from your Barcelona hotel between 8:00 and 9:00 am by minivan. The first stop is Vic, explored with your guide. Then you cross the Garrotxa's volcanic landscape to Santa Pau, stop for photos at Castellfollit de la Roca and finish with a guided walk in Besalú before heading back to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **inland medieval Catalonia** without driving.
- Travellers who value **hotel pickup** and **small groups**.
- **History** and Romanesque architecture fans.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for **beaches** or the Costa Brava.
- Those who don't cope well with **long hours in a van**.
