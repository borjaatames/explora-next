## What you will see

- Viewpoints over **Cala Canyelles**, **Cala Banys** and **Santa Cristina** beach.
- The **Sant Pere del Bosc** monastery and the **Santa Cristina** chapel.
- Lloret's seafront, the "Indianos" mansions and **Sant Joan castle**.

## How it works

You board a vintage Land Rover Santana at Cala Llevadó and follow the old road between Lloret and Tossa, Lloret's centre and forest tracks through cork oaks. The guide shares local history and stops at each viewpoint for photos. It ends at a secluded cove where, if the sea allows, you can have a swim.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Lloret or Tossa** who wants something different from the beach.
- **Families** with children aged 8 and over, and couples.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after intense off-roading or wanting to drive.
- Anyone without a way to reach the start point on the coast road.
