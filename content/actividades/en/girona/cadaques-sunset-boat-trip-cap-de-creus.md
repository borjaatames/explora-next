## What you'll see

- **Cadaqués** and its natural harbour from the water.
- The best-known **coves** of the **Cap de Creus Natural Park**.
- **Cap de Creus**, the easternmost tip of the Iberian Peninsula.
- **Evening light** over the rocky coastline.

## How it works

You board at 7:00 pm in Cadaqués harbour and sail north along the natural park's coves. The boat stops for about 20 minutes in a cove so you can swim and snorkel with the gear provided on board, reaches Cap de Creus and returns to Cadaqués around 9:00 pm.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** looking for a relaxed evening plan.
- Anyone staying in Cadaqués who wants to see **Cap de Creus from the sea**.
- Those who fancy a **swim** in a cove without driving or hiking.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting **drinks or snacks** on board: there's no bar.
- **Wheelchair users**.
