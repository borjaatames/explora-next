## What you will see

The boat leaves Roses and follows the coast of the **Cap de Creus Natural Park** to Cadaqués:

- **Cala Nans lighthouse** and slate cliffs that are best seen from the sea.
- The **Roca del Gat**, a rock shaped like a cat by the wind.
- **Cadaqués**, the white village that inspired Dalí, with 1.5 hours free.

## How it works

You choose a departure time, show your voucher when boarding and listen to the audio guide on your phone during the crossing of just under an hour. In Cadaqués you have free time, then return on the same boat to Roses.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **Cadaqués** without driving the winding road.
- Visitors based in **Girona** or Figueres looking for a half-day trip.
- Families: it is a short, easy outing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **spend the day** in Cadaqués: 1.5 hours is short.
- Anyone who gets seasick easily when the tramontana wind blows.
