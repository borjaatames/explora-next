## What you'll see

A day trip from **Barcelona** to the **Costa Brava**, with the main stop in **Tossa de Mar**:

- The **Vila Vella**, the only surviving medieval walled town on the Catalan coast, with its towers above the sea.
- Coves and beaches with crystal-clear water.
- Free time to swim, have lunch and stroll.

## How it works

Departure at 08:15 from **Barcelona Nord** station (platform 8). Guide in Spanish and English. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
