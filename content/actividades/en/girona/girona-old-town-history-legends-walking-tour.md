## What you'll see

- The **Onyar river** and the **Pont de les Peixateries Velles**, built by Eiffel's workshop.
- The **Basilica of Sant Feliu**, the **Lioness of Girona** and **Sant Pere de Galligants**.
- The **Arab Baths**, the **cathedral** and the **Jewish Quarter**, all from outside.
- Streets used as **Game of Thrones** locations.

## How it works

You meet your guide on Passeig de José Canalejas and walk through the old town for about two hours, stopping at each sight to hear its history and the legends attached to it. The walk ends in Plaça de la Independència, a good spot to stay on for lunch or a drink.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Girona who are comfortable in **English**.
- Anyone who enjoys **legends** and local stories.
- **Game of Thrones** fans who don't want a purely themed tour.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **go inside** the cathedral or the Arab Baths.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone who prefers a tour in **Spanish**.
