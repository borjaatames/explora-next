## What you will do

You set off from Sant Pol beach in S'Agaró and paddle along the bay for about two hours:

- **Coves and caves** you can only reach by sea, with narrow passages between the rocks.
- **Snorkel stop** of about 20 minutes to see fish and, with luck, an octopus.
- **Cliffs and islets** on the prettiest stretch of the southern Costa Brava.

## How it works

You book a time slot, arrive at the school 15 minutes early and get all the gear. After a safety briefing on the beach you head out in a group of up to 14 with an instructor. You paddle a **double kayak**, so no experience is needed.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors staying in **Girona** who want half a day at sea without an organised coach trip.
- **Families** with children over 7 who can swim.
- Anyone who wants cove photos without hunting for parking on the coast.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **cannot swim**: they will not be allowed to take part.
- Visitors without a car and short on time: the bus from Girona takes over an hour.
