## The route

1. **Montserrat** (2 h): guided visit to the monastery and the basilica of the Black Virgin.
2. **Girona** (3 h): a 2-hour guided tour of the old town and 1 hour for lunch.
3. **Tossa de Mar** (1 h 15 min): the walled Vila Vella and the beach.

## How it works

You leave Les Corts in the morning by coach and on arrival split into groups of up to 25 per guide. When booking you can choose the option with lunch included.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors in **Barcelona** with only a few days who want mountains, a city and the sea.
- Anyone who wants Montserrat and Girona without arranging transport.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone already staying in **Girona**: it departs from Barcelona.
- Anyone set on seeing the **Black Virgin**: it is not guaranteed.
