## What you will see

- Hidden **coves**, **sea caves** and cliffs of the Costa Brava from your kayak.
- Rocky Mediterranean reefs with fish, octopus and starfish.
- A stretch of the **coastal path** and a beach to relax on.

## How it works

You leave Barcelona by coach and reach the coast in just over an hour. After a safety briefing you spend 2 to 3 hours paddling with your instructor, stopping to snorkel and for a couple of jumps into the water from low rocks. Then there is a sandwich on the beach and free time before heading back to Barcelona.

## Who I recommend it for

- Strong swimmers who want an **active day** on the Costa Brava without renting a car.
- **Groups of friends** and couples after some light adventure.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who cannot swim or is not comfortable in the sea.
- Families with children under 7.
