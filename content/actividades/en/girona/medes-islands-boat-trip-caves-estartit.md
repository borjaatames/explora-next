## What you will see

The boat leaves L'Estartit and tours the Montgrí Natural Park, the Medes Islands and the Baix Ter:

- **Sea caves** and the **Roca Foradada**, a natural arch the boat sails through.
- The **seabed** through underwater windows: fish, groupers and sometimes tuna.
- A **swim stop** of about 15 minutes inside the park.

## How it works

You book a time, show your voucher at the breakwater and board the Fadrí, a 20-seat boat. The guide covers the history, marine life and birds of the area along the way.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children: it is short and very visual.
- Anyone who wants to see the reserve without diving.
- Visitors staying in Girona looking for half a day on the coast.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants serious **diving or snorkelling**: the stop is short.
- Anyone looking for a full day on a boat.
