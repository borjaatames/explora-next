## What you will see

In about 2.5 hours you walk the essentials of the Barri Vell:

- **The city walls**, with views over the rooftops and the cathedral.
- **The cathedral**, with the widest Gothic nave in the world.
- **The Call**, the old Jewish quarter, with its narrow streets and steps.
- **The Onyar river** and the iron bridge by Eiffel's firm.
- Some **Game of Thrones locations**.

## How it works

You meet the guide in Plaça de Sant Feliu and walk the route in a small group. Monuments are explained from outside; at the end the guide recommends what to visit inside and where to eat.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time** visitors to Girona.
- Anyone more interested in the city's **history** than the show.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to go inside the monuments: the tour stays outside.
- People with reduced mobility: there are many steps.
