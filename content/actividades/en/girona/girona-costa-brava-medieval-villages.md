## What you'll see

A full day through the best of inland and coastal Catalonia:

- **Girona:** the medieval old town and Jewish Quarter, the cathedral, the colourful houses along the Onyar river and the city walls (Game of Thrones fans will recognise filming locations).
- **Costa Brava (Calella de Palafrugell):** about 90 minutes in a charming seaside village to have lunch, stroll the coves or relax by the sea.
- **Medieval villages:** the cobbled streets of **Pals** and, to finish, **Peratallada**, where you toast the day with a glass of local wine.

## How it works

A **9-hour day trip departing from Barcelona** (the exact point is confirmed on booking), by air-conditioned vehicle with a professional guide. Food and drink aren't included.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants Girona plus the Costa Brava and the Empordà villages in one easy day.
- Travellers who'd rather not drive or organise the route themselves.
- Game of Thrones fans (Girona features several locations).
