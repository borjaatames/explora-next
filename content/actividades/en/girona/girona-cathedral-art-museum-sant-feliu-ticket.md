## What you'll see

A combined ticket to three monuments in Girona's **Barri Vell**:

- **The cathedral**: the widest Gothic nave in the world, the **Romanesque cloister**, the treasury and the 11th-century **Tapestry of Creation**.
- **Basilica of Sant Feliu**, with its unfinished bell tower and Roman and early Christian sarcophagi.
- **Museu d'Art**, in the former bishop's palace, with art from the Romanesque to the 20th century.

## How it works

Exchange the voucher at the Art Museum. The ticket is valid for **48 hours**. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
