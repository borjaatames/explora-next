## What you will see

Dalí designed the museum on the ruins of Figueres' old theatre as a work of art in itself. The guided tour covers the essentials:

- The **geodesic dome** and the stage of the old theatre.
- The **Mae West Room**, a portrait that only appears from one exact spot.
- The **Rainy Taxi** in the courtyard and the optical illusions in the galleries.
- The **crypt** where Dalí is buried.

## How it works

You book a time, arrive at the museum entrance with the ticket on your phone and go in with the guide, skipping the ticket office. After the one-hour tour you can stay inside at your own pace.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors going to Figueres **from Girona** by train who want to make the most of their time.
- Anyone who does not know Dalí's work well and wants to understand it.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without fixed dates: it **cannot be cancelled**.
- Anyone who prefers to explore alone with no set times.
