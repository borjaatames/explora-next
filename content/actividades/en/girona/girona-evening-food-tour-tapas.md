## What you will see

- Shops and bars in the centre, from **Carrer de Santa Clara** to **Carrer de les Ballesteries**.
- The **Peixateries Velles bridge** and the Vi, l'Oli and Catalunya squares.
- Local produce: **cured meats**, **cheeses**, wines, cannelloni, anchovies and the **xuixo**.

## How it works

You start at 5:30 pm in Plaça de la Independència with a welcome bite and stop at different places around the centre. Between tastings, the guide shares curiosities and legends about the city. There is a stop at a chocolate shop, wine and cheese tastings, tapas and dessert, and the tour ends in a tavern on Carrer de les Olles.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **get to know Girona through its food** and make the most of the evening.
- **Couples** and friends who enjoy tasting local produce.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who does not understand English.
- Anyone after a sit-down dinner in one restaurant.
