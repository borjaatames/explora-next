## What the tour is like

You meet the guide at **Plaça de la Independència**, by the river. For **3 hours** you explore the **Barri Vell**: the **cathedral** steps and the church of **Sant Feliu**, the narrow streets of the Jewish **Call**, a stretch of the **city walls** with views over the rooftops, and the **colourful Onyar houses** from its bridges. The guide tells you about the city's Roman, medieval and Jewish history, plus a few lesser-known corners.

Tickets aren't included: if you want to see inside the cathedral, you can do it afterwards.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families and groups on a day trip from Barcelona or the Costa Brava.
- Anyone who wants to understand Girona, not just take photos.
- If you're travelling solo and want to save, see the small-group walking tour.
