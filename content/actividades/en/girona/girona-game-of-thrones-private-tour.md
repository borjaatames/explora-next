## The filming locations you will see

In 2015 Girona hosted filming for **season 6** of Game of Thrones. The city stood in for Braavos, King's Landing and Oldtown:

- The **cathedral steps**, the exterior of the Great Sept of Baelor.
- The **Pujada de Sant Domènec**, where Arya's chase through Braavos was filmed.
- The **Arab Baths** and the **Call**, the old Jewish quarter.
- The monastery of **Sant Pere de Galligants**, which became the Citadel in Oldtown.

## How it works

You book a day and time, and the guide meets you in Plaça de Sant Feliu. For about 2.5 hours you walk the show's locations and the Barri Vell at whatever pace you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families and groups** of 4 or more: it costs about the same as the group tour.
- Anyone who wants to ask lots of questions and stop wherever they like.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Solo travellers or couples: the **small-group tour** is much cheaper.
- People with reduced mobility: there are many steps.
