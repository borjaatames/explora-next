## The route

1. **Girona**: guided walk through the Barri Vell, the Jewish Quarter and the Onyar river.
2. **Pals**: a stroll through one of the best-preserved medieval villages in the Empordà.
3. **Calella de Palafrugell**: the fishing village and its coastal path by the sea.

## How it works

You are picked up at your hotel between 8:00 and 9:00, spend the day in a minivan with up to 8 people and are dropped back at your hotel in the evening.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors in **Barcelona** who want Girona and the coast without a big coach.
- Anyone who wants to see **Pals and Calella**, hard to combine without a car.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone already staying in **Girona**: pickup is in Barcelona.
- Anyone looking for the Dalí Museum: it is not on this route.
