## The route

1. **Girona** (1.5 h free): the Barri Vell, the cathedral and the Onyar houses, with a phone audio guide.
2. **Figueres** (1.5 h): the Dalí Theatre-Museum, if you choose the option with a ticket.
3. **Cadaqués** (1 h 20 min): short guided walk and time for a swim.

## How it works

You leave the Eixample in the morning on a coach with a guide and return to Barcelona in the evening. Choose when booking whether you want the museum ticket included.

## Who I recommend it for

- Visitors in **Barcelona** who want to see Girona, Dalí and the coast in one day.
- Anyone who does not want to drive the Costa Brava roads.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone already staying in **Girona**: it departs from Barcelona.
- Anyone who wants time to see each place slowly.
