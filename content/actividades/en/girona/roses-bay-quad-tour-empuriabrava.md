## What you'll see

- **Empuriabrava**, home to Europe's largest marina and over 40 km of canals.
- The small coastal town of **Santa Margarida**.
- The **Bay of Roses**, the largest on the Catalan coast, from the hills above Roses.

## How it works

You meet in Roses, get your gear and a safety briefing, and set off on your quad behind the guide. Over an hour you ride through Empuriabrava and Santa Margarida and up to the viewpoints above Roses. Each quad carries up to 2 people.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** happy to share a quad.
- Anyone after a **short activity** with a bit of adrenaline on the Costa Brava.
- First-timers: no quad experience is needed.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without a **driving licence** or under 18 (to drive).
- **Pregnant women**.
- English-only speakers: the guide speaks French and Spanish.
