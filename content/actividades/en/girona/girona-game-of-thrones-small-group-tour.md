## The filming locations you will see

In 2015 Girona hosted filming for **season 6** of Game of Thrones. The city stood in for Braavos, King's Landing and Oldtown:

- The **cathedral steps**, the exterior of the Great Sept of Baelor.
- The **Pujada de Sant Domènec**, where Arya's chase through Braavos was filmed.
- The **Arab Baths** and the **Call**, the old Jewish quarter.
- The monastery of **Sant Pere de Galligants**, which became the Citadel in Oldtown.

## How it works

You meet the guide in Plaça de Sant Feliu and walk through the Barri Vell for about 2 hours. At each location the guide shows you the scene filmed there on an iPad and explains the real history of the place.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Fans of the show** who want to see the locations with the scene in front of them.
- First-time visitors to Girona who want the highlights with a guide.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **go inside** the cathedral or Arab Baths: the tour stays outside.
- People with reduced mobility: there are many steps.
