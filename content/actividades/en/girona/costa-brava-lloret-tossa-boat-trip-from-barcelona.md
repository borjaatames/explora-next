## What you'll see

- **Lloret de Mar**: the seafront promenade, the main beach, Sa Caleta cove and the modernist church of Sant Romà.
- The **coast between Lloret and Tossa** from the boat: cliffs and coves shaped by the sea.
- **Tossa de Mar** and its **Vila Vella**, one of the few walled medieval towns left on the Catalan coast.
- The old fishing quarter of **Sa Roqueta**.

## How it works

You leave Barcelona Nord by coach in the morning and reach Lloret de Mar in about 75 minutes, with an hour and a half of free time. Then you board the boat for an hour-long cruise along the coast to Tossa de Mar. There the guide shows you the old town, followed by free time for lunch, a stroll or a swim before the coach back to Barcelona.

## Who I recommend it for

- Travellers **in Barcelona without a car** who want to see the Costa Brava in a day.
- Anyone after an **affordable** day trip that's more than just a coach ride.
- People who like mixing a **historic town and the beach**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers **small groups**: there can be up to 50 people.
- Those looking for **quiet coves**: Lloret is very touristy.
- **Wheelchair users**.
