## What you will see

- **Besalú**, with its medieval bridge and the story of its Jewish community.
- **Castellfollit de la Roca**, perched on a basalt cliff.
- The **Garrotxa Volcanic Zone Natural Park**, with a chapel inside a crater.
- **Santa Pau** and, to finish, **Lake Banyoles**.

## How it works

You leave Girona by van with a group of up to six. The first long stop is Besalú with a guided visit; then a photo stop at Castellfollit, an hour's walk in the volcanic area, optional lunch in Santa Pau and a guided stroll through the village. The day ends with a walk by Lake Banyoles before heading back to Girona.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in Girona who wants to see the **inland province** without driving.
- **Nature** lovers and fans of medieval villages.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with reduced mobility.
- Anyone looking for beaches or the Costa Brava.
