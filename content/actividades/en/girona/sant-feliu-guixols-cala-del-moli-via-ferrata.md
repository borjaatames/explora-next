## What you will see

- The **cliffs** of Cala del Molí in Sant Feliu de Guíxols.
- The Mediterranean right below your feet for much of the route.
- Wide views along the coast to the horizon.

## How it works

You meet your guide in Sant Feliu de Guíxols, put on your harness and the rest of the gear, and start moving along the rock face clipped to the safety cable. Groups are eight people at most, the guide takes photos along the way and there are refreshments at the end. Between December and the end of May only the first section is open.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone after an **adrenaline** hit on the Costa Brava with no climbing experience needed.
- **Groups of friends** and families with children aged 10 and over.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone afraid of heights or with low fitness.
- Anyone without a way to reach Sant Feliu de Guíxols: transport is not included.
