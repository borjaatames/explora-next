## What you'll see

Open-date entry to the FC Barcelona Museum / Barça Immersive Tour, sold through Viator. Same museum access as the official ticket — the differences are price (higher than official), date flexibility (open date), and cancellation policy (free 24h).

- **«We are history» immersive tunnel:** Barça heritage in photographs and objects.
- **Interactive stations:** men's and women's first teams.
- **«More than Achievements»:** iconic moments and trophies with audiovisual integration.
- **Camp Nou Live 360° room:** match-day atmosphere recreation.
- **Construction Viewpoint:** observation deck for the renovation works.
- **Espai Barça:** future-stadium exhibition.

## How it works

**Book your open-date voucher.** No need to pick a specific date at booking — you have flexibility within the validity window.

**Show your Viator voucher at the museum entrance.** Mobile-only.

**Pick up your audio device + headphones** at reception — 13 languages available.

**Self-paced museum visit, ~1 hour.** No add-ons (Robokeeper, VR) — those require the Total Experience Pass instead.

**Free 24-hour cancellation** before your visit — but the official ticket non-refundable + 4-month validity is usually a better deal for €17 less.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Existing Viator users with loyalty preferences.
- Travellers specifically wanting free 24-hour cancellation.
- Visitors valuing the open-date format AND willing to pay the €17 premium for it.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Most travellers — the official `fcbarcelona.com` ticket at €28 is €17 cheaper for the same access.
- Visitors with epilepsy (the 360° room is intense).
- Travellers expecting a stadium tour (the bowl is closed).
- Budget-conscious visitors (€17 is meaningful).
