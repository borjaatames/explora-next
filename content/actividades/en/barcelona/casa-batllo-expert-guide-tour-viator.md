- **Main floor (Noble Floor):** the Batlló family's former residence at the start of the 20th century, where the licensed guide explains the family commission, Gaudí's design choices, and the Modernisme context.
- **Inner courtyard / Light well:** the «true sea» of blue tiles — gradient effect explained by the guide.
- **Attics:** the catenary arches and Gaudí's structural genius.
- **Rooftop:** dragon-back chimneys and views of Passeig de Gràcia.
- **The originality of an officially licensed guide:** real depth on Modernisme, Barcelona's reinvention as a metropolis, the rivalry between Casa Batlló and Casa Amatller (the «Manzana de la Discordia»).
