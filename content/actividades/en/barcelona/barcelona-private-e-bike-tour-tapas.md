## What you'll see

Barcelona on wheels: the medieval **Gothic Quarter**, **El Born** and **El Raval**, **Port Vell** and **Barceloneta**, finishing with tapas in **Poble Sec**.

## How it works

Meet at Plaça del Teatre, by La Rambla. E-bike and helmet included. Price **per group**: €300 for 1 or 2, €480 for 4 and €600 for 6.

## Who it's for

- Travellers who want to see a lot without getting tired.
- Couples and families with older kids.
