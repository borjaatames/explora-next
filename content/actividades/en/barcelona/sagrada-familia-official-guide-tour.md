## How the visit works

You meet your guide next to the Sagrada Familia and go in **without queuing**. For an hour and a half, an **official guide** certified by Barcelona's tourism authority explains the basilica inside and out: the **columns** that mimic a forest, the light of the **stained glass** and the symbolism of the **facades**, and how nature, geometry and faith shaped Gaudí's work. Afterwards you have **free time** inside.

## Small group or private

- **Small group:** up to 10 people, in English. From about €95 per person.
- **Private:** just your party. From about €138 per person for two.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to understand the Sagrada Familia without giving up half a day.
- Families: the guide adapts the explanations to all ages.
