## What you will see

**Teatro Flamenco Barcelona** is on a narrow street in **Ciutat Vella**, between the Gothic Quarter and El Born. Its show, "**Inspiración**", is one hour of **song, dance and guitar** in a small venue where you see and hear the artists at very close range.

## How to plan it

After the show, walk over to **Santa Maria del Mar** and the **Passeig del Born**, one of the liveliest areas for dinner in Barcelona.
