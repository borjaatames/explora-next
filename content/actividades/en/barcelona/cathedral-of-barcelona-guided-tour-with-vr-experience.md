## What you'll see

A live-guided group tour of Barcelona's Gothic cathedral combined with a virtual reality experience on the rooftop — the format that adds historical layers (off-limit medieval areas, lost chapels, original architecture) onto the present-day panorama.

- **The Gothic nave and cloister:** your guide walks you through the 13th-15th century architecture, the cloister with its 13 geese honouring Santa Eulalia, and the cathedral's role in medieval Catalan history.
- **Crypt of Santa Eulalia:** beneath the main altar — the alabaster tomb of the co-patron saint.
- **Side chapels and choir stalls:** the most significant of the 28 chapels, including the Sant Crist de Lepant.
- **Rooftop terraces:** climb to the cathedral's terraces for 360° views of the Barri Gòtic, the Sagrada Família, Montjuïc and the Mediterranean.
- **VR experience on the rooftop:** put on a virtual reality headset that overlays historical scenes onto what you're seeing — medieval cathedral states, off-limit areas, reconstructed views. Duration ~5-10 min within the broader tour.

## How it works

**Book your date and choose your guide language** (English, French or Spanish — selected at booking).

**Meet your group and guide at the designated meeting point** (varies by tour option — check your voucher 24 h before).

**Tour starts inside the cathedral** (skip-the-line) — ground level for ~45-55 min.

**Climb to the rooftop** for ~20-30 min — panoramic views + VR experience.

**The VR headset is provided by the operator** — your guide hands it out at the right moment on the rooftop.

**Mobile voucher required.** No individual access tickets — this is a group tour, you cannot enter alone.
