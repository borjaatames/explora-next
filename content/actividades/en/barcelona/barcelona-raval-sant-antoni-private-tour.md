## The route

You start on La Rambla and head into **El Raval**, Barcelona's most multicultural neighbourhood. You see **Palau Güell**, Gaudí's first major work, and stop at **one of the city's oldest bars**, once a late-night haunt of Dalí, Picasso, Hemingway and other artists. You stroll the gardens of the old **Santa Creu Hospital**, where Gaudí died, and discover the area's **street art**.

Then comes **Sant Antoni**, full of terraces, with its newly restored **market**, and **Poble-sec**, where you have a **pintxo with a glass of wine**. The walk ends on the **Rambla del Raval**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who already knows the centre and wants to see locals' Barcelona.
- Street-art fans and lovers of neighbourhoods with a life of their own.
