## How the tasting works

Your group heads up to a **central rooftop on Passeig de Gràcia** and, with a **beer guide**, tastes **6 craft beers**: comparing them and learning about beer's history and how it's brewed, all in a relaxed way.

Once the tasting's done, you can **help yourselves to your favourites**.

## Good to know

- It's **for private groups only**.
- The booking is **non-refundable**.
- The tasting is in English.

## Who it's for

- Stag and hen dos.
- Birthdays and group celebrations.
- Companies looking for an informal activity.
