## The route

At 11 am you set off by bike from the **Gothic Quarter** with your guide. The ride is **easy-paced**, taking in some of Barcelona's best-known neighbourhoods and lesser-known corners, with **photo stops**, stories and insights into the city's culture and lifestyle.

## The tapas

Once the bikes are parked, you carry on with a **traditional tapas tasting** at a lively local spot, where you can relax and chat with the rest of the group.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors who want to see a lot in a short time.
- Solo travellers keen to meet people.
- English speakers.
