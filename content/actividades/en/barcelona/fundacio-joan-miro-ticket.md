## What you'll see

The **Fundació Joan Miró** holds the most complete collection of the artist's work, in the building his friend **Josep Lluís Sert** designed on Montjuïc hill. The route follows his stages and his ties with **surrealism** and poetry.

Besides the permanent collection, the ticket covers the **temporary exhibition** and **Espai 13**, devoted to young artists. The roof terraces, with sculptures, look out over the city.

## How it works

- Exchange your voucher at reception and skip the ticket queue.
- Last entry 1 hour before closing.
- Closed on 1 January and 25 and 26 December.
