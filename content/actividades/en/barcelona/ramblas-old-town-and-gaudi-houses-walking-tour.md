## What you'll see

A "Top Rated" 2.5-hour walking tour combining TWO different Barcelona areas in a single visit — the medieval Old Town (Gothic Quarter) and the modernist Eixample "Gaudí Strip" of Passeig de Gràcia. Operated by Nostos Tours.

- **Plaça de Catalunya:** the start point — the city's central square that separates the Old Town from the modern grid.
- **La Rambla:** the iconic tree-lined boulevard, with stops to discuss its history and culture.
- **La Boqueria market:** quick walkthrough — your guide explains Catalan food culture.
- **Gothic Quarter highlights:** Plaça Reial (Gaudí's first streetlamps), Plaça Sant Jaume (political heart), Cathedral exterior, medieval squares.
- **Public transportation transfer:** the tour uses metro/bus to move from the Old Town up to Passeig de Gràcia (saving 25 min of walking).
- **Passeig de Gràcia "Gaudí Strip":** Casa Batlló (façade only, no interior) and Casa Milà / La Pedrera (façade only, no interior) — your guide explains the modernist architecture, the Catalan modernism movement, and Gaudí's vision.

## How it works

**Book your date** (English-only — no language selection needed).

**Meet your guide at the sidewalk in front of Foot Locker on Plaça de Catalunya** — look for the white umbrella, 10-15 min before start.

**Bring a public transportation ticket or buy €2 from the guide** — the tour uses metro/bus to move between zones.

**The 2.5-hour tour walks you through the Old Town first**, then transfers to Passeig de Gràcia for the Gaudí houses (exteriors).

**Optional €1 headset rental onsite** — useful in noisy areas.

**End at Passeig de Gràcia** — you're left near Casa Batlló / Casa Milà if you want to enter either with a separate ticket.
