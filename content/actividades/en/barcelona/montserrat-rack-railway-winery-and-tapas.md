## What you'll see

This is the all-inclusive version of Castlexperience: rack railway, basilica, winery and tapas or lunch in a single 7-8 hour excursion. For anyone wanting to see Montserrat and experience Catalan oenology without making two separate plans, it's the most complete option.

- **Montserrat rack railway:** historic cog-rail funicular, one of the iconic images of the visit.
- **Benedictine monastery:** 1-hour guided tour with local guide.
- **Skip-the-line basilica:** access to the church interior + tasting of traditional liqueurs and artisan pastries made by monks.
- **Oller del Mas winery (10th century):** medieval castle converted into organic winery. Walk through vineyards, guided visit to the barrel room, tasting of 3 organic wines.
- **Tapas or full lunch** (based on booked option): Catalan dishes paired with the wines.

## How it works

Meet at the **Estació del Nord** (Carrer de Nàpols, 68), Castlexperience office section B. Mandatory check-in 20 minutes early. Single departure at **9:45**.

Barcelona-Montserrat journey: 1 hour by coach. Upon arrival, **rack railway ride** to the monastery (12 minutes with views). **2-hour guided tour** of the complex + free time to taste artisan liqueurs and pastries. Skip-the-line basilica access.

Coach down to **Oller del Mas (30 minutes)**, in the Bages region. There: vineyard walk + winery visit + tasting of 3 wines. Based on booked option, **tapas brunch** or **multi-course lunch** with wine pairing.

Return to Barcelona in 1 hour. Total: 7-8 hours per option.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If you want EVERYTHING in one booking: rack railway + monastery + basilica + winery + tapas/lunch.
- If you have the full day free and want to optimize.
- If you're interested in Catalan oenology (Oller del Mas is a real winery, not a tourist set).
- If you need Spanish-only explanation WITHOUT English pauses (the only variant explicitly offering it).
- If your budget is €59-70 per person.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If your priority is the Moreneta — NOT included here. See [variants with Moreneta](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-rack-railway-black-madonna).
- If you expect a small group. Here it can be 20-22 people. See [Mont Escape](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-cable-car-and-easy-hike-small-group) or [In Out](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-small-group-hotel-pickup).
- If you want hotel pickup — not available. See [options with pickup](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-private-tour-hotel-pickup).
- If you're rushed or only have half a day.
