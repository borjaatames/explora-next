## What you'll experience

Over one hour, a professional photographer guides you through Barcelona's most iconic locations, with the Sagrada Familia as the centerpiece. You take home **45-50 professionally edited digital photos**, ready to share or print. The session can be extended if you'd like to cover more of the city's architecture.

## How it works

The meeting point is **outside the Sagrada Família**; the photographer confirms the exact spot when you book. The session is private, just for you (or your group), and available in Spanish or English. Admission to the Sagrada Familia and Park Güell is **not included** — book those separately and in advance if you want to go inside. You'll receive the edited photos by email after the session.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants a quality shoot with the Sagrada Familia in the background, without having to organize anything.
- Couples or families looking for natural photos, not just tourist snapshots.
- Anyone already planning to visit Park Güell or the Sagrada Familia's interior, who wants to fit in some outdoor photos before or after.
