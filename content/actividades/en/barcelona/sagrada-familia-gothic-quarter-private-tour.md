## What you'll see

Two thousand years of Barcelona: the **Gothic Quarter**, from the Roman wall to the cathedral, then the **Sagrada Familia** inside.

## How it works

Meet your guide at Plaça de Catalunya; after the Gothic Quarter walk, a vehicle takes you to the Sagrada Familia. Price **per group**: €420 for 1 or 2, €600 for 4 and €720 for 6.

## Who it's for

- Travellers with half a day for the essentials.
- Groups who want a guide of their own.
