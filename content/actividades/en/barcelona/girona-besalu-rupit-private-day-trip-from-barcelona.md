## How the day works

Your guide picks you up at your hotel and you drive into inland Catalonia. The first stop is **Rupit**, with lanes of 14th-century houses and a hanging bridge over the river. Next comes **Besalú**, entered over its **12th-century Romanesque bridge**. At midday, a **Catalan lunch** at a restaurant facing **Lake Banyoles**, and in the afternoon a walk through **Girona's old town**, one of the great cities of the Middle Ages. Then it's back to your Barcelona hotel.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who already knows Barcelona and wants to see medieval Catalonia.
- Families or groups of friends who can share the cost of the private car.
