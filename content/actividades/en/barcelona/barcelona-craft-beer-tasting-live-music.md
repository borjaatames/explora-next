## How it works

You start with a **guided tour of the brewery and bar** at Craft Barcelona in the Gothic Quarter, learning the story of the venue and the world of craft beer.

Then comes a **guided tasting** of **Catalan and international craft beers**, with their flavours and aromas. Afterwards you choose your favourites and **keep drinking at your own pace**. Not a beer drinker? There's a **sangria or vermouth** option.

If you stay on, the night continues with **live music** and then optional **karaoke** with free entry.

## Who it's for

- Beer fans who want to try Catalan craft beer.
- Solo travellers looking for a sociable but laid-back plan.
