## What you'll see

1-hour guided walking tour of Park Güell with skip-the-line entry, operated by Tours for Today via Viator. The route covers the monumental zone of Gaudí's hilltop park, with guide narration in English, Spanish or French depending on the time slot.

- **El Drac:** the mosaic salamander on the main staircase.
- **Hypostyle Hall:** 86 Doric columns from the original development's market (PARTIAL restoration in 2026).
- **Nature Square / Plaça de la Natura:** the serpentine bench terrace with panoramic views (PARTIAL restoration in 2026).
- **Entrance pavilions:** the two Modernista towers — the «gingerbread» buildings of Park Güell.
- **Free time after the tour:** explore the park independently, photo stops, viewpoints.

## How it works

**Pick your time slot AND language.** The booking calendar shows time slots with assigned languages (e.g., 10:00 English, 11:30 Spanish, 1:00 French). Pick the slot matching your group's language — the tour is monolingual.

**Meeting point:** Carretera del Carmel 23, Horta-Guinardó. Meet on the esplanade between the taxi stand and the gate marked «23 Carretera del Carmel — Group Entrance». Park Güell has 4 entrances; only this one works.

**Arrive 15 minutes early.** Late arrivals can't catch up.

**The tour is your ticket.** Entry depends on joining the group — there's no separate ticket pickup.

**1-hour guided portion + free time inside the park after the tour.** Most visitors stay 30-60 minutes extra on their own.

**Group size:** maximum 25 travellers. No headsets — guide projects voice directly.
