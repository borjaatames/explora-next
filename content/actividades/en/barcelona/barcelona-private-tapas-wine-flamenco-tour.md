## How the evening works

You start on La Rambla with your guide and explore the **Gothic Quarter**, Barcelona's medieval district. Then you stop at a **traditional restaurant** in the old town to taste tapas with a glass of red wine and a glass of cava. You carry on through **El Born**, where old buildings sit alongside bars and shops, and finish with a **flamenco show** in a medieval palace, with skip-the-line entry and a drink included.

## Who it's for

- Couples and groups who want a tapas-and-flamenco night without planning anything.
- First-time visitors who want the old town explained by a local.
