## What you'll see

Five stops in the **Gothic Quarter**: Iberian ham at **La Boqueria**, pintxos with cider in a hidden tavern, tapas, croquettes and cava in **Plaça del Pi**, crema catalana and churros with chocolate.

## How it works

Meet at the Liceu on La Rambla. Price **per group**: €280 for 2, €456 for 4 and €630 for 6.

## Who it's for

- Travellers who want Catalan food with context.
- Families and groups of friends.
