## What you'll see

Night-time self-guided visit to Casa Batlló with audio narration and a welcome glass of cava. The same architectural masterpiece as the daytime visit, but in a different mood — dramatic evening lighting and fewer crowds.

- **Main façade lit up at night:** the trencadís mosaics take on a different glow under the evening illumination on Passeig de Gràcia.
- **Noble floor (main level):** the Batlló family's former residence with views of Passeig de Gràcia.
- **Light well / inner courtyard:** the gradient-blue tile cascade — the «true sea» of Casa Batlló, particularly dramatic at night.
- **Attics:** Gaudí's catenary arches — the structural genius behind the building's organic shapes.
- **Rooftop:** the dragon's-back chimneys clad in trencadís, with night views of Passeig de Gràcia.
- **Gaudí Cube:** 6-sided LED immersive room, more impactful in low ambient light.

## How it works

**Book your specific date and entry slot** (8:30 PM, 8:45 PM, 9:00 PM or 9:15 PM — the last entry is 9:15 PM).

**Show your mobile voucher at the entrance** at Passeig de Gràcia 43.

**Receive your welcome glass of cava** as you enter (Catalan sparkling wine, standard 12cl pour).

**Pick up your audio device** at reception — 15 languages available.

**Self-guided tour at your own pace.** Minimum visit is 75 minutes; you can stay flexibly through closing.

**No large bags or luggage** allowed inside — no storage on-site. Plan to leave bigger items at your hotel.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Cost-conscious travellers wanting a night Casa Batlló experience.
- Couples wanting low-key Gaudí + cava at ~€31.
- Photographers — different lighting from daytime, less crowded.
- Gaudí enthusiasts adding a second night visit after a daytime tour.
- Wheelchair users — full tour accessible via the priority lift.

- Anyone needing flexible cancellation (non-refundable).
- Visitors with strollers (not allowed inside).
- Travellers arriving with large luggage (not allowed; no storage available).
- Anyone expecting a sit-down dinner experience (cava only).
