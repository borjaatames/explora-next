## What you'll do

Three hours sailing the Barcelona coast on an **Oceanis 41 yacht that's exclusively yours**. You can kick back and enjoy the drinks, help sail with the professional skipper and first mate, or jump in for a swim — at your own pace, with the city as a backdrop.

It's a **private charter for your group (up to 11 people)**, with **tapas and an open selection of drinks** (water, soft drinks, cava, wine, sangria and beer) on board.

## What's included

- Oceanis 41 yacht exclusively for your group, including fuel
- Professional skipper and first mate
- Life jackets
- Tapas and snacks, plus cava, wine, sangria, beer and soft drinks

Not included: hotel transfers, tips, and towel/swimwear.

## How it works

Meeting point at **Marina Vela, Port Vell**. The price is **per group, not per person**, so it works out better the more of you there are. Bring a towel and swimwear if you want to swim, plus loose clothing and flat-soled shoes.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Groups and celebrations who want the whole boat to themselves.
- Anyone after three relaxed hours to sail, swim and toast with no crowds.
- Not for solo travellers on a budget — the per-group price suits bigger groups.
