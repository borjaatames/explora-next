## How the day works

Your guide picks you up at your hotel and you start at **Colonia Güell**, an industrial colony that is home to **Gaudí's** visionary **crypt**, with skip-the-line entry. Then you head up to **Montserrat**: the **monastery**, the **basilica** and **La Moreneta**, tickets included, and, if seats are available, the **boys' choir** (extra cost, book in advance).

At the **farmers' market** you can taste Catalan cheeses and cured meats before walking up to the **Creu de Sant Miquel** viewpoint for panoramic views. Then it's back to your hotel.

## Who it's for

- Families who want Montserrat without trains or timetables.
- Anyone interested in Gaudí beyond the Sagrada Familia.
