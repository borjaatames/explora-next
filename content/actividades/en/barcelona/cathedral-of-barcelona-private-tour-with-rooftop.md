## What you'll see

A live-guided visit (private or small group) to Barcelona's medieval Gothic cathedral, with skip-the-line entry and access to the rooftop terraces — the format that delivers the highest panoramic payoff for the price in the Barri Gòtic.

- **The Gothic nave and cloister:** your guide walks you through the architecture, the cloister with its 13 geese honouring Santa Eulalia, and the historical context (13th-15th century construction, royal weddings, the legend of Santa Eulalia).
- **Crypt of Santa Eulalia:** the alabaster tomb of the city's co-patron saint, beneath the main altar.
- **Choir stalls:** elaborate 15th-century wooden choir with carved details.
- **Side chapels:** the most significant of the 28 chapels — guides typically highlight the chapel of the Sant Crist de Lepant (with a crucifix from the 1571 Battle of Lepanto).
- **The rooftop terraces:** the experience that justifies this product over the basic ticket. Climb to the cathedral's terraces for 360° views of the Barri Gòtic, the Sagrada Família in the distance, Montjuïc, the Mediterranean, and the surrounding medieval rooftops. Photo opportunity, not just a fly-by.

## How it works

**Book your date and choose your guide language** (English, French or Spanish — selected at booking).

**Choose private or small-group format** — private adapts the pace entirely to you; small-group is more affordable but shared with 4-8 others.

**Meet your guide on Pla de la Seu** (in front of the Cathedral) 5-10 min before start. Look for the sign.

**The tour runs 75-90 minutes:** ground level (cathedral nave + cloister + crypt + chapels) for ~45-55 min, rooftop terraces for ~20-30 min.

**Mobile voucher required.** Show it to the guide at meeting point.
