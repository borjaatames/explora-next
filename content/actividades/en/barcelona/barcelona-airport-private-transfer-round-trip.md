## How it works

**On arrival**, after collecting your luggage, head out to the arrivals hall at Barcelona-El Prat Airport. Your driver waits at the driver meeting point with a sign with your name and takes you by private car to your hotel door. They track your flight, so if it's delayed they wait.

**On departure**, they pick you up at your hotel at the time you gave when booking. They recommend leaving about 3 hours before your flight.

## Price

The price is per car and depends on the number of travellers (1 to 8). For two people it's about **€112 round trip**, taxes included.

## Who I'd recommend it to

Anyone arriving with luggage, with children or on a very early or late flight who'd rather not hunt for a taxi or drag bags onto the metro or the Aerobús.
