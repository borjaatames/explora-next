## What you'll see

A full day through the most iconic parts of Barcelona, private and at your own pace:

- **Montjuïc:** the hill with the best panoramic views over the city and the sea.
- **Gothic Quarter:** the medieval streets, the Cathedral and the historic heart, on foot with your guide.
- **Passeig de Gràcia:** the modernist avenue with Casa Batlló and La Pedrera.
- **Sagrada Família:** the finale, with skip-the-line entry and a guided visit inside Gaudí's basilica.

## How it works

It's a **full-day private tour (about 7 hours), for your group only**. You're **picked up at your hotel** (or at the agreed meeting point, depending on distance) and moved by **private transport** between the stops, with a **guide just for you** all day. **Skip-the-line entry to the Sagrada Família and the guided visit inside are included**.

The price is **per group, not per person**. Book ahead (the operator requires at least 5 days). Sagrada Família entry is nominative: keep every participant's ID document to hand. Check the guide's language when booking.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone with a single day who wants to see the essentials without organising anything.
- Families or groups who prefer a guide just for them and the comfort of hotel pickup.
- Anyone who wants the Sagrada Família explained from inside, not just from outside.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Solo travellers or couples on a tight budget: the per-group price works out better the more of you there are.
- Anyone specifically after Park Güell: this tour doesn't include it — that's what the private Sagrada Família & Park Güell tour is for.
