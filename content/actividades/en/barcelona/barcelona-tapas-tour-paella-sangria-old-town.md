## The route

You meet in **Plaça Reial** and walk through Barcelona's **historic centre**, about 2-3 km of attractive streets, hidden corners and landmarks seen through local eyes.

Between stops you try **traditional paella, sangria, freshly made Spanish tapas, local beer, coffee and sweets**. It's not one meal but a taste of the city's food culture. Your guide adds **stories, fun facts** and context on everyday life in Barcelona.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants the classics on their first afternoon or night in Barcelona.
- Solo travellers, couples and groups of friends.
- There's an afternoon (5:30 pm) and a late (10 pm) departure.
