## What you will see

**Casa Vicens** (1883-1885) was **Antoni Gaudí**'s first major commission: a summer house for the stockbroker Manuel Vicens, in what was then the village of Gràcia. It has been a World Heritage Site since 2005.

On the outside, the highlights are the **green and white tiles with carnation flowers** and the Oriental-inspired turrets. Inside, the **smoking room** with its muqarnas ceiling, the dining room with painted birds and the **roof terrace**, from which you can see Gràcia.

## Tip

Download the audio guide before you go and bring headphones. Afterwards, wander down through Gràcia to Plaça de la Virreina or Plaça del Sol.
