## How the afternoon works

You start in **Poble-sec**, by El Molino theatre, and visit **five traditional taverns and bars** in the city's most characterful neighbourhoods with your guide. You taste a selection of Spanish **tapas, pintxos and desserts** —including **Iberian ham** and **churros with chocolate**— with local wines and traditional drinks (3 per person), while your guide tells the story of Spanish and Catalan food.

## The Sagrada Familia

To finish, a **taxi** takes you to the **Sagrada Familia**. Your guide explains the story of Gaudí and the basilica and hands you **skip-the-line tickets** with an **audio guide in your language** so you can explore the interior at your own pace.

## Who it's for

- Anyone short on time who wants tapas and the Sagrada Familia in one afternoon.
- Couples or families who prefer a guide just for them.
