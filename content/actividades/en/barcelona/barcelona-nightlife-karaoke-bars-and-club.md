At 10:45 pm you meet the guides and the rest of the international group at a **karaoke bar** on Passeig de Colom. You sing, chat and get to know each other before heading out together.

Then come the **games and social challenges** to break the ice, and the route continues through **two more bars or clubs**, depending on the night. You finish at **a popular Barcelona nightclub** —a beach club or a local favourite, depending on the day— with entry organised for the group.

- Drinks aren't included.
- Bring ID and avoid sportswear or beachwear.

## Who it's for

- Solo travellers and backpackers who want company for a night out.
- Groups of friends who don't know Barcelona's nightlife.
