## What you'll see

2-hour night experience at Casa Batlló: self-guided audio visit through the illuminated house, then a 1-hour rooftop concert with a glass of cava.

- **Casa Batlló interior at night:** the same architectural masterpiece as the daytime visit, but lit dramatically — the «blue sea» courtyard becomes more intense, the catenary attics more atmospheric, the Gaudí Cube more immersive.
- **Self-guided audio tour:** typically takes ~1 hour at your own pace, covering the noble floor, the inner courtyard, the attics and the rooftop.
- **Rooftop concert (1 hour):** reserved table, live music (jazz, soul or flamenco depending on the date), professional local musicians.
- **Glass of cava:** Catalan sparkling wine, served as you settle in for the concert.
- **Gaudí Cube:** the 6-sided LED immersive room — exclusive to the visit, 360° projections.

## How it works

**Book your specific date and time slot.** Non-refundable once booked — only weather cancellations of the concert trigger refunds.

**Show your mobile voucher at the entrance** at Passeig de Gràcia 43.

**Pick up your audio device.** 15 languages available.

**Self-guided audio tour first (~1 hour).** Wander at your own pace. Aim to be on the rooftop ~10 min before the concert starts.

**Concert (~1 hour) with reserved table.** Bring something warm in winter — the rooftop is open-air.

**Glass of cava as you settle in.** Additional drinks available at the on-site bar at standard prices.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Couples on a date night in Barcelona.
- Music lovers wanting jazz, soul or flamenco in a unique architectural setting.
- Photographers — Casa Batlló rooftop at sunset is exceptional.
- Travellers wanting a non-touristy «evening cultural experience».
- Visitors who've already seen the daytime Casa Batlló and want the night version.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Travellers expecting dinner (only cava is included).
- Music-sensitive visitors (rooftop concert is amplified ~80-90 dB).
- Anyone uncertain about a specific date (non-refundable).
- Budget-conscious visitors — €64 is high; the Welcome Drink night version (t682388, ~€31) is half the price.
- Visitors wanting a live guide narration (this is audio-guided).
