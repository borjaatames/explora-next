## What you'll see

The **Montjuïc cable car** (Teleféric de Montjuïc) is Barcelona's aerial tramway, running from the Miramar viewpoint up to Montjuïc castle with **360-degree views** of the city, the port, and the Mediterranean Sea.

### The views
On a clear day you can see the entire Barcelona skyline, the Sagrada Família, the port, and the sea stretching to the horizon. Photographers rate this as one of the best city viewpoints in Spain.

### Montjuïc castle
The upper station sits next to the **18th-century Montjuïc castle**, a fortress with its own history and more panoramic views. Entry is not included in the cable car ticket.

### The gardens
Montjuïc hill has several botanical gardens. The **Jardins de Laribal** and the **Jardí Botànic** are a short walk from the upper station.

## Who it's for

- Anyone in Barcelona who wants the best city panoramic view.
- Families, couples and photographers.

## Who it's not for

- People with vertigo: the cabins pass over residential streets and steep hillsides.
- Visitors in high winds: the cable car closes when the wind is too strong.
