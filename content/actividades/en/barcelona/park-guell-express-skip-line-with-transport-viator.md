## What you'll see

Express 1h 5min guided tour of Park Güell with City Wonders. Includes skip-the-line entry, transport to the attraction and a guide solving the operational «how do I get to the park» question that other express tours leave to the traveller.

- **El Drac and main staircase:** the iconic mosaic salamander.
- **Hypostyle Hall:** the 86 Doric columns of the original market project.
- **Serpentine bench and panoramic terrace:** views over Barcelona with trencadís decoration.
- **Entrance pavilions:** the two Modernista towers.
- **Free time after the tour:** time to explore at your own pace.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Ctra. del Carmel, 23, Horta-Guinardó, 08024 Barcelona. The guide waits on the esplanade with a **City Wonders flag**. **Arrive 15 minutes early**.

**Transport to the attraction included** — the exact modality depends on the option you book. Check the confirmation email.

**Duration:** 1h 5min. **Language:** English only. **Gratuities included** in the price.

The **combined option with Sagrada Familia** adds a guided tour of the basilica the same day — useful if you want to wrap up two top monuments in one go.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- English-speaking travellers tight on time wanting Park Güell with logistic solution.
- Anyone valuing transport to the park + skip-the-line + gratuities in a single ticket.
- Anyone interested in the combined Park Güell + Sagrada Familia option.
- City Wonders fans preferring Viator over GetYourGuide for booking.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Non-fluent English speakers (the tour runs only in English here).
- Travellers wanting a deep tour (1h 5min is express — see GYG t2323 for 1.5-2h).
- Anyone who already knows how to get to Park Güell on their own (the «transport included» doesn't drive value for them).
- Anyone needing wheelchair accessibility (operator doesn't mark it).
