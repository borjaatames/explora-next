## What you'll see

1h 15min guided tour of Park Güell with skip-the-line entry, operated by Golden Tour Guide (the same team behind Madrid's best-rated Royal Palace tour). The route covers the monumental zone of the park designed by Gaudí for Count Güell.

- **El Drac:** the mosaic salamander guarding the main staircase.
- **Hypostyle Hall:** the 86 Doric columns that were meant to be the original development's market.
- **Terrace with serpentine bench:** panoramic views of Barcelona with the continuous bench decorated in trencadís.
- **Entrance pavilions:** the two Modernista towers with mosaic details.
- **Casa-Museu Gaudí:** seen in passing — interior entry not included.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Ctra. del Carmel, 23, Horta-Guinardó, 08024 Barcelona. The guide carries a **«Guía Turístico Dorado»** sign. **Critical:** Park Güell has 4 entrances — make sure you arrive only at this one. Confirm in your email and use Google Maps to navigate to the exact spot.

**Arrive 15 minutes early.** If you don't show up at the right entrance on time, you lose the tour without refund.

**How to get there:** bus 24 or 116 leaves nearby; metro Vallcarca or Lesseps (line 3) + 10-15 min walking. Calculate 35-40 min from the centre.

**Duration:** 1h 15min. **Languages:** Spanish + 4 more (English included). Tour **monolingual** — pick the language at booking.

**Tickets are managed by the guide** — there's no separate box office. Without the guide, you don't enter (recurring 1-star complaint).
