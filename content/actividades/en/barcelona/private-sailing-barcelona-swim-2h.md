## What you'll do

Two private hours on a **12 m Oceanis 41 sailboat that's exclusively yours**: you leave Port Vell, sail along the Barcelona coastline and stop for a **swim in open water**, with a very different view of the city from the sea. Relax with a drink or help sail with the skipper.

It's the **most affordable private-sailing option**, with **drinks and snacks** on board.

## What's included

- 12 m Oceanis 41 sailboat exclusively for your group, including fuel
- Professional skipper
- Life jackets
- Drinks (water, soft and alcoholic) and snacks

Not included: hotel transfers, tips, and towel/swimwear.

## How it works

Meeting point at **Marina Vela, Port Vell**. The price is **per group, not per person**. Bring a towel and swimwear — there's time for a dip.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Couples or families who want a private boat without the cost of a longer charter.
- Anyone after a relaxed couple of hours to sail and swim.
- Not for those wanting a big party boat or high speed.
