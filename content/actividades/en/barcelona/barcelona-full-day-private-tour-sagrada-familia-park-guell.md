## What you'll see

A full day in Barcelona with a **private guide and car**. Skip the lines at the **Sagrada Familia** and **Park Güell**, stroll the **Gothic Quarter**, **El Born** and **Gràcia**, and finish with a city panorama.

## How it works

Pickup at your accommodation. The driver takes you between stops for about 8 hours. The price is **per group**: €1,400 for 1 or 2 people, €1,600 for 4 and €1,800 for 6. It **cannot be cancelled** once booked.

## Who it's for

- Travellers with just one day in Barcelona who want to see it all hassle-free.
- Families and groups of 4 to 6, where the price per person makes sense.
- Not for solo travellers on a budget.
