## What you'll do

A relaxed two-hour sail on a beautiful **Oceanis 41 yacht** along the Barcelona coastline, with a very different view of the city from the Mediterranean. You can kick back and enjoy the refreshments, lend a hand sailing with the qualified skipper, or take a dip in the sea.

It's a **small group**, so it never feels crowded, and it includes **tapas and a selection of drinks** on board — good value for a couple of hours at sea.

## What's included

- Use of the yacht including fuel, with a qualified skipper and first mate
- Life jackets
- Tapas and snacks, plus a selection of drinks on board

Not included: hotel transfers, tips, and towel/swimwear.

## How it works

Meeting point at **Marina Vela, Port Vell**. Bring a **towel and swimwear** if you want to swim. **Minimum age 2**; children must be accompanied by an adult, and life jackets are provided (for ages 6+ — tell the crew if a child is younger).

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants a calm, different view of Barcelona from the water.
- Couples, families and small groups after a relaxed couple of hours with food, drinks and a swim.
- Not for you if you want speed and adrenaline — for that there's the high-speed powerboat.
