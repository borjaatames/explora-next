## What you'll see

A full day combining **Barcelona and Montserrat**. In the morning the guide shows you the city essentials: the **Sagrada Família** from outside, **Passeig de Gràcia** with Casa Milà, **Montjuïc** hill and **El Born**, the nicest part of the old town. Then you head up to **Montserrat**: natural park admission, views of its rock peaks and **skip-the-line access to the basilica** of Santa Maria de Montserrat.

## How it works

You're picked up at your **hotel or the cruise port** in an air-conditioned vehicle. The group is **12 people maximum** and the guide speaks **English**. You can keep your luggage in the vehicle, so it also works on arrival or departure day: at the end you're dropped off at your hotel, the port, the airport or the Sagrada Família. Lunch isn't included.

If you want to **go inside the Sagrada Família** at the end, email the operator after booking with each person's name and passport: the ticket costs €25 and depends on availability.

## Who I recommend it for

- Cruise passengers with a single day in Barcelona.
- Anyone short on time who wants city and mountain without arranging transport.
- Travellers who prefer a small group and are happy with an English-speaking guide.
