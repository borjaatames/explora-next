## What you'll see

**Casa Amatller** is **Josep Puig i Cadafalch**'s building on the Block of Discord, commissioned in 1898 by chocolate maker **Antoni Amatller**. Unlike its neighbours, it keeps the family's decoration and furniture.

You walk through the hallway, the main staircase and the rooms of the main floor with a **tablet video guide**: every room has commentary, old photos and films of the house in its day. The visit ends in the **Amatller chocolate** shop.

## How it works

- Timed priority entry: slots every 30 minutes from 10:00 to 19:00.
- Maximum 20 people per slot.
- High heels are not allowed; shoe covers are provided.
