## The route

1. **Figueres**: the Dalí Theatre-Museum, the building Dalí designed himself and where he is buried. Entry included.
2. **Cadaqués** (September to June): time for lunch and a walk through the white village that inspired him.
3. **Portlligat** (September to June): visit to the fishermen's huts Dalí turned into his home and studio. Entry about 15 €, in cash.
4. **Púbol** (July and August): a medieval village and the Gala Dalí Castle, instead of Cadaqués and Portlligat.

## How it works

You leave from the Palau de la Música in a minibus of up to 16 people with an English-speaking guide, and you come back to Barcelona in the evening. The guide tells you the story of Dalí and Gala on the way.

## Who I recommend it for

- Those who want to see **Dalí's house**, not just the museum.
- Those who prefer a small group to a 50-seat coach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Those who need a **Spanish-speaking** guide.
- Those who want to see Girona too: this route doesn't stop there.
