## How the tasting works

You head up to a **central rooftop** overlooking Plaça de Catalunya, Casa Batlló and Passeig de Gràcia. There you taste **six Spanish wines** —sparkling, whites, rosé and reds— while your hosts share stories about Spanish wine and culture, without the stuffiness.

The idea is to work out what you like and why, with **generous pours**, **snacks** and a sociable vibe.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to learn about wine without a formal tasting.
- Couples and groups of friends.
- Solo travellers keen to meet people.
