## The route

You start at Plaça Nova, opposite the cathedral, and step back into **Roman Barcelona**: the **Temple of Augustus** (with entry), the **walls** and the **aqueduct**. Then you enter the **Call**, one of Europe's oldest Jewish quarters: its narrow lanes, the **Great Synagogue**, with a guided visit, old Jewish baths and hidden squares, with your guide bringing each place to life.

## The Sagrada Familia

A metro ride takes you to the **Sagrada Familia**, where you skip the queue and spend about an hour inside with an **audio guide in your language**.

## Who it's for

- History lovers looking for more than the classic route.
- Anyone who wants the Sagrada Familia and the Jewish Quarter in a single morning.
