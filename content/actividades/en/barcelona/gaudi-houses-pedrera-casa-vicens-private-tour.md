## The route

With your guide, a Gaudí specialist, you spend three hours exploring some of his great works: **Palau Güell**, **Casa Batlló** and **Casa Milà**, and visit the old **medieval hospital** where the architect died. You go into **La Pedrera** with skip-the-line entry and an audio guide in your language.

Then a metro ride takes you to **Casa Vicens**, Gaudí's first major house, opened to the public only a few years ago. You spend an hour inside with skip-the-line entry and an audio guide.

## How it works

- **Outdoor walk:** with your guide, privately.
- **Inside La Pedrera and Casa Vicens:** on your own, with an audio guide.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who's already seen the Sagrada Familia and wants to see Gaudí's houses from inside.
- Art Nouveau lovers looking for a visit with context.
