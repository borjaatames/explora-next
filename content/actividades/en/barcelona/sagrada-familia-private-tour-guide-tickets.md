## What the tour is like

You meet the guide by the Sagrada Familia souvenir shop on Carrer de la Marina (or you're picked up at the port if you arrive by cruise ship). For **1 h 45 min**, with headsets, you see the **Nativity** and **Passion** façades outside and enter the basilica without queuing: the nave with its **tree-like columns**, the light from the **stained glass** and the **museum**, which shows how Gaudí worked. Afterwards you can stay inside as long as you like.

## Options

- **Private tour** meeting at the Sagrada Familia: €340 for 1 or 2 people, €480 for 4 and €570 for 6.
- **With cruise port pickup**: €390, €560 and €690. One way only.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families and groups of 4 to 6: per person it costs about the same as a group tour, with the guide just for you.
- Cruise passengers with only a few hours in Barcelona.
- If you're travelling solo or as a couple on a budget, see the small-group guided tour.
