## What you'll see

Montserrat is the most recognizable mountain range in Catalonia: a mountain with rounded, serrated peaks (hence the name, *Mont Serrat*) declared a Natural Park in 1987, with an 11th-century Benedictine monastery at the heart of the massif and, inside, the Moreneta — the Black Madonna patron saint of Catalonia since 1881. This excursion covers the cultural side and adds a Catalan winery experience at a historic 10th-century estate.

- **Monastery of Santa María de Montserrat (skip-the-line basilica entry):** 1-hour guided tour with a local guide. Renaissance atrium, basilica with its stained glass windows and the chamber where the Moreneta resides (touching her is NOT included in this variant).
- **Free time in Montserrat (1 hour):** enough to enter the basilica, walk through plaça de Santa Maria or visit the local market (cheese, honey, liqueurs).
- **Oller del Mas winery (10th century):** medieval castle in the Bages region, owned by the same family for 36 generations, converted into an organic winery. Walk through the vineyards, visit to the barrel room and tasting of 3 local wines with seasonal tapas.

## How it works

Meet at the **Estació del Nord bus station** (Carrer de Nàpols, 68) at least 20 minutes before departure. Check-in mandatory at the Castlexperience office inside the station, section B. Coach departs at 8:30 or 9:45 depending on season.

The journey to Montserrat takes about 1 hour. Once at the monastery, **1-hour guided tour** + 1 hour of free time. Then, transfer of about 30 minutes to Oller del Mas (in the Bages region), where the winery team welcomes you for the vineyard walk, the 3-wine tasting and the tapas.

Return to Barcelona in the afternoon — total declared duration between 6 and 7 hours depending on option booked. Groups are max 20 people per guide (rarely 22). Transport is shared with other operator groups.
