## What you'll see

This is the most complete Castlexperience offer on GetYourGuide: combines the icons of Montserrat (rack railway + monastery + Moreneta) with the gastronomic experience of the Oller del Mas winery, all configurable based on the option you choose when booking.

- **Montserrat rack railway (included):** historic lift climbing from the valley to the monastery with panoramic views.
- **Benedictine monastery and basilica:** walking tour + skip-the-line entry to the basilica.
- **Moreneta (optional):** chamber access to the Black Madonna if you select the option.
- **Oller del Mas winery (10th century, optional):** medieval castle converted into organic winery, with 3-wine tasting and tapas or multi-course lunch depending on the option.
- **1-2 hours of free time** at Montserrat depending on the chosen option.

## How it works

Meet at the **Estació del Nord bus station** (Carrer de Nàpols, 68), Castlexperience office section B. Mandatory check-in 20 minutes before departure.

The day has **4 booking options**:
- **Option 1 (~€47, half-day):** rack railway + monastery + free time + return.
- **Option 2 (~€57, 7 hours):** all of the above + winery + tapas.
- **Option 3 (~€70, 9 hours):** all of the above + multi-course lunch with wine pairing.
- **Option 4 (~€5 extra):** add Moreneta access to any of the above.

Barcelona-Montserrat (45 min) → rack railway (12 min) → guided monastery tour (2.5 hours with free time) → Oller del Mas if applicable (30 min by coach + 2 hours at the winery) → return to Barcelona (1 hour). Total between 6 and 10 hours depending on option.
