## What you'll see

A **professional 1-hour flamenco show at Barcelona's City Hall Theatre** — the MOST BOOKED flamenco product in the city, with 11,305 reviews at 4.5/5. GetYourGuide Top Pick. Central location next to Plaça de Catalunya.

- **Full flamenco cuadro with recognized artists:**
  - Dancers: Ivan Alcalà, Raúl Ortega, Patricia Domínguez, Yolanda Cortés.
  - Singers: Antonio el Mantecao, Miguel de la Tolea, La Tana, Josefa G. Contreras.
  - Guitarists: Oliver Haldon, Eduardo Cortés.
  - Percussion: Joni C. Sánchez and Jacobo C. Sánchez.
- **Traditional theater format:** large capacity (~300-400 seats), tiered seating (guaranteed view from any zone). NOT an intimate tablao — it's a theater with a large stage and microphones for amplification.
- **Structured 1-hour show:** alegrías, soleá, bulerías, tangos, fandangos — the classic flamenco palos, interspersed with brief breaks between pieces.
- **Bar inside the theater:** order drinks before and during the show (Standard no drink, Premium 1 included, VIP 2 drinks or bottle).
- **4 daily sessions:** 5, 6:45, 8:30 and 10 PM — maximum schedule flexibility.

## How it works

**Book your session and zone** (Standard €25 / Premium €40 / VIP €55).

**Arrive 20-30 min early** at the City Hall Theatre, next to Plaça de Catalunya. Metro: Catalunya (L1/L3), Universitat (L1/L2) or Passeig de Gràcia (L3/L4/L2). Exact address confirmed in the voucher.

**Show your mobile voucher at the entrance.** Staff will indicate your zone. Within each zone, seats are NOT numbered — first come, first choice (from best to worst within the booked zone).

**You can order drinks at the bar** before the show. If your option includes drinks, the ticket is also valid for redemption at the bar before or at the start of the show.

**Enjoy the 1-hour show.** Photos and video NOT allowed. Applaud between pieces as a sign of respect. The show starts on time — past 10 min from start, entry may not be allowed (at staff discretion).

**After the show**, Plaça de Catalunya has hundreds of restaurants and bars 2-5 min walking to continue the evening.

## Who I recommend it to

- Travellers on a **tight budget** wanting to see professional flamenco in Barcelona (from €25).
- Visitors with **rush or tight schedule** needing flexibility (4 daily sessions).
- **Large groups** (families, hen/stag parties, group travel) who don't fit in an intimate tablao.
- **Flamenco first-timers** wanting an accessible experience without dinner commitment.
- Visitors staying near Plaça de Catalunya with minimal commuting.

## Who I do NOT recommend it to

- Flamenco purists valuing intimate format without microphones (Cordobés is the reference).
- Anyone wanting dinner during or before the show (Tablao de Carmen or Palau Dalmases with add-on).
- Anyone prioritizing architecturally unique venue (Palau Dalmases — 17th-c. palace).
- **Wheelchair users** (this venue is NOT accessible — Palau Dalmases, Cordobés and Tablao de Carmen ARE).
- Anyone seeking TRADITIONAL TABLAO format (the previous 3 fulfill that; this is theater format).
