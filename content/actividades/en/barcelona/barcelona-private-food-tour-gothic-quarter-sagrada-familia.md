## What you'll see

A tasting walk through the **Gothic Quarter**: Iberian ham at **La Boqueria**, pintxos with cider, tapas with cava, crema catalana and churros. Then the **Sagrada Familia** inside.

## How it works

Meet at the Liceu on La Rambla. After 4 hours on foot, a taxi takes you to the Sagrada Familia, visited for an hour with an audio guide. Price **per group**: €388 for 2, €680 for 4 and €966 for 6.

## Who it's for

- Travellers who want great food and the Sagrada Familia on the same day.
- Families: there are kid-friendly options.
