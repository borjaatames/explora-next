## What you'll see

The #1 selling guided tour of the Museu Picasso de Barcelona — a 1.5-hour expert-led visit through 5 connected medieval Gothic palaces on Carrer de Montcada, housing the world's most complete collection of Picasso's early years and Barcelona period.

- **The 5 connected palaces (13th-14th century):** the Museu Picasso spans Palau Aguilar, Palau del Baró de Castellet, Palau Meca, Casa Mauri and Palau Finestres — Gothic palaces interlinked across the medieval street.
- **Picasso's Blue Period (1901-1904):** the museum's strongest collection — works from his depressed Barcelona-Paris years, the muted blue tones, the "Science and Charity" canvas.
- **The Las Meninas series (1957):** 58 canvases reinterpreting Velázquez's masterpiece — a unique room dedicated entirely to this series, found nowhere else in the world.
- **Youth and Academy works:** Picasso's earliest training pieces (some painted at age 13-15) showing the technical mastery that preceded his stylistic revolutions.
- **The 5 stylistic periods narrated:** Blue, Pink, Cubist, Neoclassical, Surrealist — your guide walks you through the evolution chronologically.
