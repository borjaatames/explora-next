## How the route works

You meet at the door of **El Molino** theatre in Poble-sec, and from there your guide takes you to **five traditional bars and taverns** in neighbourhoods with a local feel. At each stop you taste tapas and pintxos with local wine, adding up to **6 tapas and 5 drinks** per person (4 glasses of wine and 1 of cava), while your guide explains Barcelona's food and wine traditions and shares tips for the rest of your trip.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to eat like a local rather than in the busiest tourist areas.
- Couples, friends or families who prefer a guide just for them.
