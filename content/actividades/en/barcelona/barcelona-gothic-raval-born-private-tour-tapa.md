## The route

You start on La Rambla with your guide. The first stop is **Palau Güell**, Gaudí's first major work, followed by **Plaça Reial**, with its palm trees and arcades. You pass **La Boqueria market** before heading into the heart of the **Gothic Quarter**: the Roman walls, the **cathedral**, the **Call** (the old Jewish quarter) and **Plaça de Sant Felip Neri**, among other corners.

Halfway through, you stop at a **local tavern** for a tapa with a traditional drink. Then it's on to **El Born**: the basilica of **Santa Maria del Mar** and, to finish, the **Born market**, where you can see the medieval ruins found beneath the city.

## Who it's for

- Anyone arriving in Barcelona who wants to understand the old town from day one.
- Families: the operator adapts the route for children.
- Groups of 3 or 4, where the price per person drops a lot.
