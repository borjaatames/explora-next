## What you'll see

This variant combines monastery + Sant Joan funicular + easy hike in small-group format. Compared to large coach options, the difference is the number of people: max 8 with single guide.

- **Monastery of Montserrat (1-hour guided visit):** atrium, basilica and iconic points of the complex.
- **Sant Joan funicular (included):** traditional funicular climbing from the monastery (725 m) to the highest viewpoint (1,000 m). 7-minute climb with views of all Montserrat peaks.
- **Easy hike through the natural park (2 hours):** gentle routes with photo stops. The guide explains Mediterranean flora, native fauna and Montserrat's unique geology (rounded rock formations from millions of years of erosion).
- **45 minutes of free time** at the local market.

## How it works

Meet at **8:00 at Passeig de Gràcia 29** (Eixample). The operator will be at the benches identified; arrive on time because the tour leaves punctually.

Van transfer to Montserrat (55 minutes). Upon arrival: **guided monastery visit (1 hour)** → **Sant Joan funicular to the upper viewpoint** → **easy 2-hour hike** through park trails with provided hiking poles → **45 minutes of free time** at the market.

Return to Barcelona in 55 minutes. Total: 6 hours. Max 8-person group with single guide in the language chosen at booking.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If traveling with partner, family or small group and want intimate 8-per-guide ratio.
- If you're interested in the monastery + funicular + hike combination.
- If you need a guide in German, Catalan or Basque (this operator is one of the few offering them).
- If you require wheelchair accessibility (confirm at booking).

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If your priority is winery and oenology — this variant doesn't include it. See [Castlexperience with winery](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-winery-and-tapas-castlexperience).
- If you expect the iconic rack railway — they use van and funicular. See [variants with rack railway](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-rack-railway-black-madonna).
- If your goal is just the monastery (no hiking, no funicular). The price (€68) reflects the complete package; if you only want the cultural side, [the cheapest option](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-basilica-and-transfers) is €41.
- If you have severe vertigo. The funicular can feel intense.
