## How the party works

You check in at **Ice Bar** on the Barceloneta seafront and board a **double-decker catamaran** with surround sound. For **two hours** you sail off Barcelona's coast with a **DJ and dance floor**.

Everyone gets **tokens for 4 drinks**. Afterwards you have **free guest-list entry to a nightclub** in the city that same night (before 1 am).

## Good to know

- It's **non-refundable**.
- There are no winter sailings.
- Turn up drunk and you won't be allowed on board.

## Who it's for

- Hen and stag dos, birthdays and groups of friends.
- Anyone after a daytime party that rolls into the night.
