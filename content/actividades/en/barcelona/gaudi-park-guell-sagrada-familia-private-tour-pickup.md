## What you'll see

**Gaudí's** two masterpieces: **Park Güell**, with its mosaics and views, and the **Sagrada Familia**, inside and skip-the-line.

## How it works

The guide picks you up at your hotel and a vehicle takes you between both sites. The tour ends at the Sagrada Familia. Price **per group**: €520 for 1 or 2, €800 for 4 and €1,080 for 6.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors to Barcelona.
- Families who prefer to skip public transport.
- Not for those who also want inside Gaudí's houses.
