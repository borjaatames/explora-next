## The route

In two hours you walk the streets of the **Gothic Quarter** with a local guide who tells you the stories behind each corner. You stop in elegant **Plaça Reial**, see **Santa Maria del Pi**, a 14th-century church, and pass through **La Boqueria market**.

Then you reach **El Born**, where old and modern buildings sit side by side, visit the basilica of **Santa Maria del Mar** and finish at the **medieval ruins** of old Barcelona uncovered beneath the **Born market**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who's just arrived and wants to get oriented fast.
- Families: children go free and the route adapts.
- Anyone short on time in the city.
