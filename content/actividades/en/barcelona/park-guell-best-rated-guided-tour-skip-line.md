## What you'll see

75-minute (basic) to 3-hour (private premium) guided tour of Park Güell with skip-the-line entry, operated by Golden Tour Guide and certified by the Barcelona Tourism Authority. The route covers the full monumental zone designed by Antoni Gaudí for Count Eusebi Güell.

- **El Drac:** the mosaic salamander on the main staircase — Gaudí's most photographed Barcelona icon.
- **Hypostyle Hall:** 86 Doric columns originally meant to be the development's market (PARTIAL restoration in 2026).
- **Nature Square:** the elevated terrace with the continuous serpentine bench in trencadís mosaic (PARTIAL restoration in 2026).
- **Entrance pavilions:** the two Modernista towers with mosaic-tile decoration.
- **Carretera del Carmel viewpoints:** panoramic views of Barcelona and the Mediterranean.

## How it works

**Meeting point:** Carretera del Carmel 23, 08024 Barcelona — main entrance on the upper side of the park. The guide carries a **«Golden Tour Guide»** sign on the esplanade between the taxi stand and the gate.

**CRITICAL:** Park Güell has 4 entrances — only this one is valid. If you arrive at another, you miss the tour.

**Arrive 15 minutes early.** Late arrivals can't catch up or enter independently.

**The tour is your ticket.** You only enter the park with the guide — there's no separate ticket pickup. If you don't show up at the meeting point on time, you don't enter.

**How to get there:** bus 24 from Plaça Catalunya stops 150 m away («Ctra del Carmel - Albert Llanas»). Bus V19 from Sagrada Familia area. Metro Vallcarca or Lesseps (line 3) + 15-20 min uphill walking. Taxi: specify «Carretera del Carmel 23», not just «Park Güell».

**Duration:** 75 min (basic) up to 3 h (premium with audio guide and broader park coverage).

**Headsets included** — critical in groups of 25+ people.
