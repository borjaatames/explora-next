## How the day works

Your guide picks you up by car and you head to **Colonia Güell**, a 19th-century industrial colony with its **crypt**, whose design Gaudí used as a trial run for the Sagrada Familia. You have a **tapas lunch** there at a traditional restaurant.

Next comes **Bellesguard Tower**, a mysterious work blending neo-Gothic forms with Catalan Art Nouveau, and the last stop is **Casa Vicens**, the first house Gaudí designed, which you explore with an audio guide in your language.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who knows the famous Gaudí and wants to discover the less-visited one.
- Architecture lovers who value going with a specialist.
