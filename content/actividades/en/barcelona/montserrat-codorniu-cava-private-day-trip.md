## How the day works

Your guide picks you up at your hotel and you drive to Penedès, to the **Codorníu cellars**, an Art Nouveau landmark. There you ride a **train** through the huge underground galleries with an expert guide and finish with a **tasting of two premium cavas**.

Then you head up to **Montserrat**, with about three and a half hours for the **monastery**, the **basilica** and **La Moreneta** (with skip-the-line entry) and an easy walk towards the **Creu de Sant Miquel** viewpoints. Then it's back to your hotel.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants Montserrat and cava in a single day, without driving.
- Families or groups of 3-4 who can share the cost of the private car.
