## What you'll see

Express 1-hour guided tour of Park Güell's monumental zone with City Wonders Ltd. Condensed format for travellers tight on time.

- **El Drac:** the mosaic salamander guarding the main staircase.
- **Hypostyle Hall:** the 86 Doric columns meant to be the development's market.
- **Terrace with serpentine bench:** panoramic views and the continuous bench decorated in trencadís.
- **Entrance pavilions:** the two Modernista towers opening the park access.

**Meeting point:** Park Güell entrance confirmed by City Wonders in email. Metro Vallcarca or Lesseps (line 3) + 10-15 min uphill. **Arrive 15 min early** — the operator is professional and usually punctual.

**Duration:** exactly 1 hour. **Main language:** English (others subject to availability — check at booking).

**Skip-the-line** via express security check. **Strollers NOT allowed**. Tour marked physically moderate — involves walking and stairs.
