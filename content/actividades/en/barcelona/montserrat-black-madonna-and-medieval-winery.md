## What you'll see

Castlexperience's "premium" variant: full day with rack railway, Moreneta, medieval winery and formal lunch in a 9.5-hour excursion. It's the operator's longest and most complete option.

- **Rack railway** (optional): historic climb to the monastery.
- **Basilica and monastery:** extended guided tour with free time.
- **Moreneta** (optional): chamber access to the Black Madonna. **Only Castlexperience product that includes it.**
- **Boys' Choir / Escolanía** (optional, school day): 13:00 performance.
- **Oller del Mas winery (10th century):** 1,500-acre estate with public golf course, restaurant, barrel rooms. Tasting of 3 organic wines.
- **Formal multi-course lunch** with wine pairing in the winery's restaurant.

## How it works

Meet at the **Estació del Nord** (Carrer de Nàpols, 68), Castlexperience office. Check-in 20 minutes early. Single departure at **8:30**.

Barcelona-Montserrat journey: 1 hour. **4 hours at Montserrat** (guided tour + extensive free time for Moreneta, Escolanía, funicular or museum).

Coach to **Oller del Mas (30 min)**. There: **multi-course lunch with pairing** in the restaurant + vineyard walk + guided winery visit + 3-wine tasting. Total: 4.5 hours at the estate.

Return to Barcelona. Total declared duration: **9 hours 30 minutes**.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If you have the full day free and want to maximize the experience (culture + Moreneta + oenology + meal).
- If your goal is to touch the Black Madonna AND have a real winery experience — this is the only Castlexperience variant that combines them.
- If you value a formal sit-down meal (not just tapas).
- If you're interested in the Escolanía and land on a school day during school term.

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you don't have 9.5 hours or tire quickly in groups. See [shorter variants](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-winery-and-tapas-castlexperience).
- If your plan is a small group. Here it can be 20-22 people.
- If you want hotel pickup — not included. See [In Out with hotel pickup](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-private-tour-hotel-pickup).
- If you don't eat meat or don't want a formal lunch (although dietary restrictions are accommodated if communicated).
