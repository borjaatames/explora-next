## What you'll experience

A social painting workshop in a **private space in central Barcelona**. Tables are set, canvases out and music on; a host guides the painting step by step, so you don’t need any experience. There’s **unlimited sangria** throughout (non-alcoholic too), and you take your painting home. Sharing a table and a canvas makes it easy to start chatting with the rest of the international group.

## How it works

It runs **on Fridays from 20:00 to 22:00** at Carrer de la Unió 10-12, just off La Rambla. 18+ only. It’s €19.60 per person; choose the **Social Night Out** option (€29.40) to carry on afterwards with three bars and skip-the-line club entry.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Solo travellers who want a relaxed way to meet people.
- Couples or friends looking for something different on a Friday night.
- Anyone who wants to warm up before a night out.
