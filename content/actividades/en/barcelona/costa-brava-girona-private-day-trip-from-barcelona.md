## What you'll see

Three Costa Brava highlights in one day: the fishing village of **Calella de Palafrugell**, the medieval village of **Pals** and **Girona's** old town, with its cathedral, walls and houses over the Onyar river.

## How it works

Pickup at your Barcelona hotel in a private vehicle. Lunch is included. The price is **per group**: €900 for 1 or 2 people, €1,280 for 4 and €1,740 for 6. **Non-refundable**.

## Who it's for

- Travellers who want the Costa Brava and Girona without driving.
- Couples and families who like to go at their own pace.
- Not for a full beach day.
