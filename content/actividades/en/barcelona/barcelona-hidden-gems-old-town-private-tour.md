## What you'll see

Barcelona's medieval heart: the **Gothic Quarter**, the **Cathedral**, **Santa Maria del Mar**, the old **Jewish Call**, hidden squares and **La Boqueria** market.

## How it works

The guide picks you up at your hotel if you stay in the centre. It's a walking, outdoor tour. Price **per group**: €280 for 1 or 2, €400 for 4 and €480 for 6.

## Who it's for

- Travellers who want to understand the old town beyond La Rambla.
- Those who've seen Gaudí and want another Barcelona.
