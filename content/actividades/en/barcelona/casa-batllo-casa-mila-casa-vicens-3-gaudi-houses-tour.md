## What you'll see

3-hour guided walk through Gaudí's three Barcelona houses, in chronological order of construction. Small group of max 12 with monolingual live guide (EN/ZH/JA/KR).

- **Casa Vicens (40 min):** Gaudí's FIRST major commission (1883-1888), inspired by Oriental and Mudéjar influences. The garden and main floor — the «hidden gem» of Barcelona's Modernisme, only open to the public since 2017.
- **Walk through Gràcia neighbourhood (20 min):** the old village atmosphere, narrow streets, modernist details on residential buildings.
- **Casa Milà / La Pedrera (45 min):** Gaudí's mature work (1906-1912), with ground-floor patios, the tenant's apartment from the early 20th century, and the famous rooftop with chimney-warrior sculptures.
- **Walk down Passeig de Gràcia (30 min):** the «Manzana de la Discordia» (Block of Discord) — the cluster of competing modernista mansions including Casa Lleó-Morera, Casa Amatller and Casa Batlló.
- **Casa Batlló (45 min):** Gaudí's fantasy masterpiece (1904-1906), main vestibule + noble floor (NOTE: rooftop NOT included).

## How it works

**Meeting point:** outside Casa Vicens (Carrer de les Carolines 20-26, Gràcia). The Touring Pandas guide holds a sign with the company logo.

**Pick your language at booking.** EN/ZH/JA/KR — the tour is monolingual in the chosen language. Spanish is NOT offered.

**Small group (max 12 people).** Pace is brisk but not exhausting — guides are known for managing the rhythm well.

**Skip-the-line at all 3 houses.** No queueing at entrance booths.

**The tour ENDS at Casa Batlló on Passeig de Gràcia** — convenient to continue exploring on your own afterwards (Casa Batlló rooftop, Sagrada Familia, El Born).

**Free cancellation 24h before** — flexible if your plans change.
