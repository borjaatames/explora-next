## How the route works

You meet at the door of **El Molino** theatre and start in **Poble-sec**, with pintxos, vermouth and croquettes. Next comes bohemian, multicultural **El Raval**, for **patatas bravas, tortilla and pan con tomate**. At **La Boqueria market** it's **Iberian ham and Manchego cheese**, and dessert is **churros with chocolate** at one of the city's oldest churrerías, in the **Gothic Quarter**.

In all, more than 11 tastings and 3 drinks per person, with a guide who explains Spanish and Catalan food and shares tips for the rest of your trip.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to taste real Spanish food in a single afternoon.
- Groups of friends or families: the tour adapts to vegetarians, vegans and coeliacs.
