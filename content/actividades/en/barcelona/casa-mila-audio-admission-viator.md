## What you'll see

Self-guided audio visit to Casa Milà / La Pedrera, sold by the official operator (Fundació Catalunya La Pedrera) through Viator. Same ticket as on `lapedrera.com` and on GetYourGuide.

- **Façade on Passeig de Gràcia:** the wave-like stone façade with wrought-iron balconies.
- **Noble floor (1911 apartment):** recreated bourgeois residence with original Gaudí details.
- **Inner courtyards:** Butterfly and Flower, with painted walls and ornate ironwork.
- **Attic / Espai Gaudí (the highlight):** 270 catenary arches + the only Gaudí biographical exhibition with scale models of Sagrada Familia, Park Güell, Casa Batlló, Casa Vicens.
- **Rooftop terrace:** the warrior-chimney sculptures + panoramic views.

## How it works

**Book your specific date and time slot.** Free 24-hour cancellation, Lowest Price Guarantee.

**Show your Viator voucher at Passeig de Gràcia 92.** Mobile-only.

**Pick up your audio device** at reception — 10 languages available.

**Walk through the visit at your own pace.** Most visitors complete the route in 1-1.5 hours.

**Rooftop opens weather permitting** — closes in rain for safety. No refund for rain closure.
