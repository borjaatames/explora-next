## What you'll see

Premium official FC Barcelona experience — everything included in the basic Barça Immersive Tour PLUS three premium add-ons (Robokeeper, Barça Virtual Dream VR, Digital Memory photo). Designed for fans, families, and gift buyers wanting the full match-day-flavoured experience during the stadium renovation.

- **Everything from the Barça Immersive Tour:** «We are history» tunnel, interactive stations, «More than Achievements» exhibit, Camp Nou Live 360° room, Construction Viewpoint, Espai Barça.
- **Robokeeper Challenge (3 shots):** advanced robotic goalkeeper — you take 3 attempts to score. Engaging for all ages, especially kids 6-14.
- **Barça Virtual Dream:** full VR experience with goggles — 5-7 minutes placing you on the pitch from a player's perspective, match-day atmosphere.
- **Digital Memory:** 1 personalised photo of your visit, delivered digitally.

## How it works

**Book your specific date and time slot.** Non-refundable; the ticket is valid only for the booked date.

**Show mobile voucher at the FC Barcelona Museum entrance** (the temporary space adjacent to the closed stadium).

**Pick up your audio device** at reception — 13 languages available.

**Plan 2-2.5 hours for the full experience.** Visit at off-peak times to minimise queues at Robokeeper and VR.

**Pick up your Digital Memory photo** at the end of the visit (delivered digitally to your email).

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Families with kids 6-14 (Robokeeper and VR are the highlights for them).
- Hardcore Barça fans wanting the full experience.
- Gift buyers (this is a more «special» experience than the basic ticket).
- Visitors curious about high-quality VR.
- Wheelchair users (fully accessible).

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Adult casual visitors (the basic ticket t449261 at €28 covers what most adults want).
- Visitors with epilepsy or photosensitivity (VR + 360° room are intense).
- Travellers in a rush (the extras add 30-45 min minimum).
- Budget-conscious travellers (the €21 premium is meaningful for many).
