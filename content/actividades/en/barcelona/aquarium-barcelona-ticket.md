## What you'll see

**L'Aquàrium de Barcelona** is one of the largest aquariums in Europe, home to **11,000 animals across 450 species**, located in the Port Vell marina.

### The shark tunnel
The star attraction: an **80-metre transparent tunnel** — the longest in Europe — surrounded by sharks, rays and open-water fish above and on all sides. The tunnel moves slowly on a travellator.

### The 35 tanks
From the Mediterranean to the tropical Atlantic, each tank recreates a different aquatic ecosystem. Highlights include the coral reef tanks and the open-ocean tank with the largest specimens.

### Explora! children's area
An interactive section designed for younger visitors: touch tanks, fish ID games and educational displays.

## Who it's for

- Families with children.
- Anyone who wants to walk through a shark tunnel.

## Who it's not for

- Visitors who've already done Valencia's Oceanogràfic (much larger collection).
- Those not interested in marine life.
