## What you will see

The **Palau de la Música Catalana** (Lluís Domènech i Montaner, 1905-1908) is the most famous concert hall of Modernisme and has been a World Heritage Site since 1997.

### The route
- **Lobby and grand staircase**, with yellow glass balustrades and columns decorated with flowers.
- **Sala Lluís Millet**, with the balcony of mosaic-covered columns overlooking the street.
- **The concert hall**, with the **central stained-glass skylight** in the shape of an inverted sun, the muses on the stage and Wagner's horses above the arch.

## Tip

Go on a sunny morning: the light coming through the skylight and the large windows is what makes the hall unique. It is a 10-minute walk from the Cathedral and El Born.
