## What you'll do

A relaxed evening tapas crawl through Barcelona's old town, with **four stops at family-run bars and taverns**. At each one you taste local tapas paired with traditional drinks — wine, cava or vermouth — while a local guide tells you the story and traditions behind each dish and each spot.

It's more than dinner: it's the Spanish "tapeo" done properly, at an easy pace, across three different neighbourhoods of the Gothic and Born areas.

## How it works

You can choose the **11:00 am** tour or the **6:00 pm** one. The meeting point is in the old town (the exact address is confirmed when you book) — **arrive 10 minutes early** for check-in, and tell your guide about any allergies or dietary needs when you arrive. Vegetarian options can be accommodated.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Food lovers who want to taste a lot without ending up in a tourist trap.
- Couples and groups of friends after a relaxed evening with local flavour.
- Anyone who'd rather have a guide pick the best bars than search on their own.
