## What you'll experience

It's not about posing — it's about enjoying Barcelona while a professional photographer captures real, natural moments, at your own pace. In 20 minutes you take home **10 fully edited digital photos** that tell the story of your time in one of Europe's most magical cities.

## How it works

The meeting point is near the **Sagrada Família** (the photographer confirms the exact spot when you book). The session is private and personalized, available in Spanish or English. You'll receive the 10 edited photos by email within **24-48 hours**. Admission to the Sagrada Familia is not included.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants a quality keepsake without giving up a full hour.
- Couples or solo travelers who prefer candid shots over posed ones.
- Anyone with a packed schedule looking for something quick and simple.
