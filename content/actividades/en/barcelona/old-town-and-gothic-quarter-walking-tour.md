## What you'll see

A 2.5-hour live-guided walking tour through Barcelona's Old Town and Gothic Quarter — the top-volume product on GetYourGuide for this area, with 1,582 reviews at 4.8/5 (Guide rating 4.9/5).

- **Plaça de Catalunya:** the city's central square — the meeting point and gateway between modern Barcelona and the medieval Old Town.
- **La Rambla:** the famous tree-lined boulevard running from Plaça de Catalunya to the harbour — stops to discuss its history, the human statues, and the controversial 2017 attack memorial.
- **La Boqueria market:** the iconic 19th-century food market — your guide walks you through the stalls (no purchases required) and discusses Catalan food culture.
- **Plaça Reial:** the palm-tree-lined square just off La Rambla, with Gaudí's first commissioned streetlamps (1879).
- **Gothic Quarter medieval streets:** narrow lanes, Plaça Sant Jaume (political heart of the city), Plaça del Rei (medieval royal palace), Plaça de Sant Felip Neri (with its Civil War scars).
- **Cathedral of Barcelona exterior:** the tour ends at Pla de la Seu in front of the Cathedral — exterior visit only (interior is not included).

## How it works

**Book your date and choose your guide language** (English or Spanish — selected at booking).

**Choose tour option:** small-group (max 15), private (just your party), or the harbour boat trip variant (longer).

**Meet your guide at the designated point** — typically Plaça de Catalunya or top of La Rambla — 10-15 min before start.

**The 2.5-hour tour walks you through the Old Town at a manageable pace.**

**Optional €1 headset rental onsite** — useful at La Rambla and La Boqueria where ambient noise is high.

**End at the Cathedral exterior** on Pla de la Seu. From here, you can continue to visit the Cathedral interior separately if you booked the entry ticket.
