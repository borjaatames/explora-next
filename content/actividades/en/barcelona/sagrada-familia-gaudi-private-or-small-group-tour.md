## What you'll see

The tour starts on La Rambla and spends about three hours exploring Antoni Gaudí's Barcelona with a local guide: his early works, Passeig de Gràcia with **La Pedrera** and **Casa Batlló** (from outside) and the architect's story told on the spot. Then a metro ride takes you to the **Sagrada Familia**, where you go in without queuing.

## How the basilica visit works

It depends on the option you choose:

- **With audio guide:** explore the interior at your own pace, with the audio guide in your language.
- **With official guide:** a shared guided visit inside the basilica, in English and with a maximum of 10 people. It lasts about 90 minutes.

Both can be booked as a **private tour** (just your party) or a **small group**.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors who want to understand Gaudí before going in.
- Families: the operator lists it as child-friendly, and it's wheelchair and stroller accessible.
- Groups of 3 or more who'd like a guide just for them.
