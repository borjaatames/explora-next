## What you'll see

1.5-hour semi-guided night experience at Casa Milà, ending with a glass of cava. The route moves through the building level by level, with multimedia at each stop.

- **Inner courtyards (Butterfly + Flower):** light projections on the painted walls — Gaudí's organic shapes brought to life.
- **Period apartment (1911):** brief tour with a guide intro, dramatically lit.
- **Penthouse:** view of the attic catenary arches at night.
- **Rooftop:** the showpiece — video mapping projected onto the warrior-chimney sculptures, ambient music, panoramic night views over Passeig de Gràcia.
- **Cava at the end:** Catalan sparkling wine, served as you wind down.

## How it works

**Book your specific date and time slot online.** Free 24-hour cancellation, flexible until the last day.

**Show mobile voucher at Passeig de Gràcia 92.** No print needed.

**Pick your language (English or Spanish) at booking.** Live intros are monolingual.

**Walk through the building with short live intros at each level.** Multimedia plays at each stop; the rooftop show is ~10-15 minutes.

**Glass of cava at the end.** Catalan sparkling wine, standard pour.

**Total: 1.5 hours.** Operate evening slots — summer 8:40 PM-10:20 PM, winter 9:00 PM-11:00 PM.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Couples wanting an atmospheric Barcelona date night.
- Photographers (dramatic night light + rooftop projections).
- Gaudí enthusiasts doing a second Casa Milà visit after the daytime audio.
- Travellers comparing Casa Milà vs Casa Batlló night experiences (this is the architectural one; Casa Batlló is the live-music one).

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- Travellers expecting full live-guided tour (book t913306 Early-Morning Access).
- Music-sensitive visitors (audiovisual shows are amplified).
- Anyone wanting dinner (only cava is included).
- Kids under 8 (operator's recommended minimum age).
- Budget travellers (the daytime audio ticket at €25 covers the building for half the price).
