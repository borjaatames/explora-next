## What you'll experience

A private one-hour photoshoot in Montserrat, with the mountain scenery and the monastery as your backdrop. You take home **30 edited digital photos**, delivered within 24-48 hours — a keepsake unlike the rest of your Barcelona trip photos.

## How it works

Montserrat is about **an hour from Barcelona** by train or car, so this session works well as a day trip: transport up the mountain is on you. The meeting point is at the Montserrat Monastery (the photographer confirms the exact spot when you book). Admission to the church and cloister is not included.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone already planning a Montserrat day trip from Barcelona who wants a great keepsake from it.
- Anyone after mountain scenery, not just city shots.
- Anyone able to arrange their own round-trip transport without hassle.
