## What you'll see

A **live 1h 20min flamenco show without microphones** at Barcelona's most historic tablao — founded in 1970 on Las Ramblas, with 55 years welcoming the great legends of Spanish flamenco. 4,617 reviews at 4.6/5 and «Top Awarded» on GetYourGuide.

- **Unamplified show:** voice, guitar and footwork project acoustically. The typical flamenco cuadro is dancer + singer + guitarist + handclaps, on an intimate stage that lets you see the artists' facial expressions and sweat.
- **Authentic Nasrid décor:** the interior is decorated with plasterwork, muqarnas and elements from the Alhambra of Granada from the late 15th century, hand-restored by Napoleón Morillas and Antonio Zubeldia — the **official Alhambra restorers**.
- **Top-tier artists:** Tablao Cordobés presents award-winning artists like Jesús Carmona, José Maya, Alfonso Losa, Belén López and other recognized names from the current flamenco circuit.
- **Dinner options BEFORE the show:** Alhambra-style restaurant with Las Ramblas views — Spanish Tasting Buffet with 40+ tapas and unlimited drinks, or restaurant menu with premium tapas served at the table with dessert and coffee.
- **One drink during the show:** wine, beer, sangria, cava, soft drink or water included in all options (not just the dinner ones).

## How it works

**Book your date and choose the option** that suits you best: show + drink only (€48), tapas + show (€69), Spanish Tasting buffet + show (€84) or restaurant menu + show (€89).

**Arrive 15 minutes before your booked time** at Tablao Cordobés on Las Ramblas 33-35 (between Liceu and Drassanes metros). The ticket is valid up to 15 min after the booked time — past that, it's considered a non-refundable no-show.

**Show your mobile voucher** at the entrance. Staff will assign you a table: dinner reservations get preferred seating; the rest is assigned in arrival order.

**If you chose dinner, you'll be served before the show** in the Alhambra restaurant (separate from the tablao, without show distractions during the meal). The buffet or menu typically lasts 60-90 min.

**Move to the tablao for the 1h 20min show.** One drink is included during the show. Smoking and photos during the performance are prohibited. Applaud between pieces — silence is a sign of respect during the singing.

## Who I recommend it to

- Visitors who want the classic experience of a **traditional historic tablao** (1970, 55 years on Las Ramblas).
- Travellers who value the **no microphones** format as a sign of acoustic authenticity.
- Diners seeking a quality meal BEFORE the show (the Spanish Tasting Buffet is exceptional).
- Couples and small groups looking for a formal yet intimate atmosphere.
- Visitors with a central location (Las Ramblas is accessible from almost anywhere in the centre).

## Who I do NOT recommend it to

- Travellers on a very tight budget (Flamenco at the Theater from €25 is more affordable).
- Those preferring smaller intimate venues of 50-60 seats (Palau Dalmases or Tablao de Carmen are more compact).
- Families with children under 4 (not admitted by minimum age).
- People with electric wheelchairs (only manual wheelchairs allowed).
- Those looking for a relaxed cocktail-bar atmosphere (in that case, El Duende by Tablao Cordobes, next door, is the right choice).
