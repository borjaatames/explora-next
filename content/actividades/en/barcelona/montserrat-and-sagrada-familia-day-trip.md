## How the day works

You leave Barcelona by car with your guide for **Montserrat**, where rocky pinnacles surround the Benedictine abbey. You visit the **basilica** and **La Moreneta**, tickets included, take in the **viewpoints** and taste local products at the **farmers' market**.

Back in the city, you head to the **Sagrada Familia** with **skip-the-line entry** to see its forest-like columns and stained glass.

## The options

- **With audio guide:** explore the Sagrada Familia interior at your own pace.
- **With official guide:** a 1.5-hour guided visit inside the basilica.

## Who it's for

- Anyone short on time who wants Montserrat and the Sagrada Familia in one day.
- Families who prefer a private car to trains.
