## How the day works

You drive up to Montserrat with your guide, through the mountain's rock pinnacles to the **Benedictine monastery**. There you tour the complex, enter the **basilica** and see **La Moreneta**, the Black Madonna, with the ticket included. There's time for the **farmers' market**, where you can taste local cheeses and sweets, and an easy walk to the **Creu de Sant Miquel** viewpoint.

## Private or semi-private

- **Private:** just your party, with pickup and drop-off at your Barcelona hotel.
- **Semi-private:** a small group of up to 8, meeting at Plaça d'Espanya.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to see Montserrat without relying on the train, and with context.
- Families: it's child-friendly (car seat on request).
- Couples on a tighter budget: the semi-private option.
