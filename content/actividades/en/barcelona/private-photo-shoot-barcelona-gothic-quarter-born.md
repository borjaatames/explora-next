## What you'll do

A local photographer walks with you through the **Gothic Quarter and El Born**, the most atmospheric parts of Barcelona's old town: narrow lanes, sunny squares and medieval facades as your backdrop. They tell you where to stand and how to move so the photos look **natural rather than stiff**. It works just as well solo as for couples, friends or families.

## How it works

You choose the length when you book:

| Option | Length | Edited photos | Price per session |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | 20 min | 15 | €59.99 |
| Plus | 40 min | 30 | €89.99 |
| Elite | 60 min | 50 | €109.99 |
| Ultimate | 90 min | 75 | €139.99 |

The price is **per session**, for groups of up to 10. You meet at **Barcelona Cathedral**, and the photographer confirms the exact spot after booking. Within 24 hours you receive the edited photos in a **private online gallery**.

## Who it's for

- Couples, families and friends who want photos together instead of selfies.
- Anyone celebrating in Barcelona: an anniversary, honeymoon, birthday or hen party.
- Solo travellers who want to come home with photos of themselves, not just the sights.
