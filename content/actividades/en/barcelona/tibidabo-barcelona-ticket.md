## What you'll see

**Tibidabo** is Barcelona's historic hilltop amusement park, open since 1901, perched at **512 metres** above sea level. The view of Barcelona from the top is the park's greatest attraction.

### The views
From Tibidabo you can see the entire city in a single panorama: from Montjuïc to the Sagrada Família, the port, and on clear days, the Pyrenees to the north and Mallorca to the east. No other viewpoint in Barcelona compares.

### The historic rides
**Avio** — a biplane carousel dating from 1928 — is the park's iconic image. Several other original rides from the early 20th century operate alongside modern attractions.

### The atmosphere
Tibidabo has a unique charm that modern theme parks can't replicate: the Victorian architecture, the Sacred Heart church at the very top, the fog on some mornings, the scenic journey up by tram and funicular.

## Who it's for

- Visitors who want Barcelona's best panoramic view.
- Families with children and anyone who appreciates heritage and atmosphere.

## Who it's not for

- Those who want a full programme of intense rides (the park is small by theme park standards).
- Visitors on days with poor visibility (the views are the main draw).
