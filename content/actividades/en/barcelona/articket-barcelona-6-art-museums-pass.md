## What the pass covers

**Articket** gets you into six Barcelona art museums, with priority access and **12 months** of validity:

- **Museu Picasso** (Carrer de Montcada 15-23)
- **MNAC**, in the Palau Nacional on Montjuïc, with the finest collection of Romanesque mural painting
- **Fundació Joan Miró** (Montjuïc)
- **Fundació Antoni Tàpies** (Carrer d'Aragó 255)
- **MACBA** (Plaça dels Àngels 1)
- **CCCB** (Carrer de Montalegre 5)

## How it works

Go to the priority entrance of the first museum and swap the voucher for the Articket passport. All close on Mondays except the MACBA, which closes on Tuesdays. Book a time slot for the Museu Picasso.
