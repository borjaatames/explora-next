## How the walk works

You start on **La Rambla** and **Plaça Reial**, with some Gaudí details, and pass through **La Boqueria market**, where the adults taste ham while the kids play. Then on through the **Gothic Quarter**: the **cathedral**, **Plaça de Sant Felip Neri** and the Roman ruins. Time for **churros with chocolate**.

Next comes **El Born**, with the church of **Santa Maria del Mar** and the medieval ruins under the old market, and to finish, **Ciutadella Park**, with paths, lakes with boats and ducks, and play areas.

## Games for the kids

Throughout the tour there's a **treasure hunt**, games with prizes, play breaks in parks and squares and surprises for the little ones.

## Who it's for

- Families with children who want to see the city without the kids getting bored.
- Anyone who prefers a guide just for their family.
