## The route

You start on La Rambla with your private guide and walk through the **Gothic Quarter**, **La Boqueria market** and **Palau Güell**, stopping for coffee at **Els 4 Gats**, Picasso's old haunt. Depending on the option, you have a tapas lunch on Rambla de Catalunya.

In the afternoon a taxi takes you to **Park Güell**, which you visit on your own with the included ticket, and another to the **Sagrada Familia**, with skip-the-line entry. You spend about 90 minutes there, with an official guide or an audio guide depending on the option.

## The four options

| Option | Sagrada Familia | Lunch |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Audio guide | No |
| Exclusive | Audio guide | Tapas |
| Premium | Official guide | No |
| VIP | Official guide | Tapas |

## Who it's for

- Anyone with just one day in Barcelona who wants to see it all without planning.
- Groups of 3 or 4, where the price per person drops a lot.
- Cruise passengers who want both key visits in a day.
