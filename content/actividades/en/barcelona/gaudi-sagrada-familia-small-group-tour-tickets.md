## What you'll see

A walk through modernist Barcelona past **Casa Batlló** and **La Pedrera**, then the **Sagrada Familia** inside with no queue.

## How it works

Meet by the Pitarra statue on La Rambla. After 3 hours on foot you take the metro to the Sagrada Familia, where you go in with an audio guide or official guide, depending on the option. Price **per person**, from €91. **Non-refundable**.

## Who it's for

- Travellers who want a small group.
- Not for those who want to go inside Gaudí's houses.
