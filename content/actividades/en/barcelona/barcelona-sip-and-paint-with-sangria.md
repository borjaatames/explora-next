## How the evening works

At 5:30 pm you arrive, grab your **first glass of sangria** and start a **guided painting session**, step by step, with music playing. No experience needed: the point is to laugh at your masterpiece (or your neighbour's).

For **two hours the sangria keeps flowing**. Then the mood shifts: the music goes up and the group ends up **dancing**.

## Who it's for

- Solo travellers who want to meet people.
- Groups of friends or hen parties after something different.
- Not anyone looking for a serious painting class.
