## What you'll see

A day around the **Dalí triangle**: the **Figueres Theatre-Museum**, the white village of **Cadaqués**, the **Portlligat House** where he lived and **Púbol Castle**, his gift to Gala.

## How it works

Pickup at your Barcelona accommodation and private transport all day. The price is **per group**: €548 for 2, €764 for 4 and €978 for 6. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who it's for

- Dalí and surrealism fans.
- Travellers who want Cadaqués and the northern Costa Brava without driving.
- Not for anyone who dislikes long drives.
