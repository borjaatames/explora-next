## What you'll see

This variant is identical in content to [EXB-03 (Mont Escape on GetYourGuide)](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-cable-car-and-easy-hike-small-group), but bookable on Viator. Same operator, same format, same price.

- **Monastery of Montserrat (atrium + basilica):** guided tour with expert local guide.
- **Aeri de Montserrat cable car (included):** historic ride from the valley to the upper viewpoint.
- **Easy hike from the top to the monastery:** gentle route with panoramic views, photo stops and **San Miguel Cross** as a viewpoint.
- **45 minutes of free time** at the monastery's local market.

## How it works

Meet at **8:00 at Passeig de Gràcia 69** (in front of Benetton store). The excursion uses two vans with capacity for 8 travelers each.

Barcelona-Montserrat journey: about 1 hour. Upon arrival, first **Aeri cable car** to the mountain top (5-10 minutes climb with spectacular views). Then, **easy downhill hike** toward the monastery (1h 55min) with photo stops and the San Miguel Cross.

Upon reaching the monastery: **guided atrium and basilica visit** with entry included. Then, **45 minutes of free time** for the market, cafés (vegan and gluten-free options available) or entering the basilica.

Return to Passeig de Gràcia. Total: 6 hours.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- If your preferred booking platform is Viator (rather than GetYourGuide).
- If you value small group and expert guide over large coach.
- If you're interested in the nature + monastery combination without spending the full day.
- If you want easy downhill hike (more comfortable than uphill).

## Who I would NOT recommend it to

- If you have walking difficulties. Although the hike is easy, it's on natural terrain.
- If traveling with children under 5 (not accepted).
- If you have serious heart problems or severe vertigo.
- If your priority is the Moreneta (not explicitly included) or winery (not here).
