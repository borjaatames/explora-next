## The route

You set off by bike from the shop next to the Sagrada Familia to explore **Barcelona's Art Nouveau**: **Casa de les Punxes**, **Casa Milà** and **Casa Batlló**. Then you ride through the **medieval quarters** and peaceful **Ciutadella Park**, pass under the **Arc de Triomf** and see the old **Monumental** bullring. There are breaks to rest along the way.

## The Sagrada Familia

The tour ends at the **Sagrada Familia**, with **skip-the-line entry** and an **audio guide in your language** for about an hour inside.

## Who it's for

- Families: a child bike seat is included.
- Couples who want to see a lot in little time.
- Anyone who'd rather cycle than walk.
