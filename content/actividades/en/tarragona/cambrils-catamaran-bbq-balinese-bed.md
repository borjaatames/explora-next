## What you will see

- The **Cap de Salou** coastline, with its coves and cliffs.
- The beaches of **La Pineda** from the sea.
- **Cambrils port** on the way out and back.

## How it works

You board in Cambrils, take your shoes off and settle onto your Balinese sunbed. The catamaran heads towards Cap de Salou and La Pineda, stops in clear water for a swim, and the barbecue and open-bar drinks are served along the way.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **groups of friends** after a relaxed day at sea.
- **Families** who'd rather eat on board than hunt for a restaurant.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **small, intimate boat**: up to 50 people go aboard.
- **Wheelchair users**.
