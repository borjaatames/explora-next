## Spain's biggest theme park resort

**PortAventura World** is the largest theme park resort in Spain and one of the five most visited in Europe. The three-park combo ticket gives you access to the full resort: the main **PortAventura World** theme park, **Ferrari Land**, and **Caribe Aquatic Park** water park.

---

### PortAventura World

The main park is divided into six themed areas — **Mediterranean, Polynesia, Far West, México, China**, and **SésamoAventura** (for younger children).

**The roller coasters:**
- **Shambhala** — Europe's tallest hypercoaster, 76 metres, 134 km/h
- **Dragon Khan** — 8 inversions; the most iconic coaster in Spain
- **Furius Baco** — hydraulic launch to 135 km/h in 3 seconds
- **Stampida** — wooden twin coaster through the Far West area

**Shows:** The park runs large theatrical productions throughout the day. The evening show and parade are highlights for families.

---

### Ferrari Land

Adjacent to PortAventura, **Ferrari Land** is the only Ferrari-branded theme park outside Italy.

**Red Force** is the centrepiece: a **vertical launch coaster** that goes from 0 to 180 km/h in 5 seconds and reaches 112 metres. It is the tallest and fastest launch coaster in Europe. The rest of the park is more family-oriented — Ferrari simulators, junior driving experiences, and Ferrari-themed rides.

---

### Caribe Aquatic Park

One of Spain's largest water parks, themed around the Caribbean. **Open seasonally (May–September)**. Highlights include:
- **King Khajuna** — one of Europe's tallest water slides
- The **wave pool** and the **lazy river**
- Multiple slides for all ages and thrill levels

---

## Getting to PortAventura

The easiest route from Barcelona is the **direct train from Barcelona Sants** to PortAventura station — the journey takes about 1 hour and the station is inside the resort. By car, the AP-7 motorway from Barcelona takes about 55 minutes.
