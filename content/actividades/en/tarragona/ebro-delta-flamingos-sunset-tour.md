## What you will see

- The **Riet Vell Nature Reserve** and its rice fields.
- The **flamingos** of the Tancada lagoon.
- **Sunset** at the Trabucador.

## How it works

You meet at Riet Vell, walk with the guide to a hidden lagoon to see birds, drive to the Tancada for flamingos and finish at the Trabucador at sunset.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Nature lovers** and photographers.
- Anyone wanting to discover the **Ebro Delta** with an expert.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone **without a car**.
