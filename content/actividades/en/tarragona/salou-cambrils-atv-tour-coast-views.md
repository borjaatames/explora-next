## What you will see

- **Country roads**, dirt tracks and mountain trails inland from the Costa Dorada.
- **Viewpoints** with panoramic views over the area.
- Quiet corners you never see from the beach.

## How it works

You are picked up in Salou, Cambrils or an agreed point and, after a short van ride, you get a handling and safety briefing. Then you ride behind the guide for a little over an hour, with a stop for photos and free time before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants an **adventure** with no previous experience.
- **Groups of friends** and couples looking for something other than the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone **without a driving licence** or under 18.
- Pregnant women, or anyone who would rather not end up covered in dust.
