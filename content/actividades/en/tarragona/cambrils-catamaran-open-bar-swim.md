## What you will see

- The **Cambrils** and Salou coast from the sea.
- **Cap de Salou** and its coves.
- A **swim** stop in the Mediterranean.

## How it works

You board in Cambrils port and cruise for about an hour along the coast before anchoring for a swim. Open bar throughout and food on midday departures before returning to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and **families**.
- Anyone wanting a simple, complete **sea trip**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**.
