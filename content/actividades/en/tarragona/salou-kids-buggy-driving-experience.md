## What you will see

- A **buggy circuit** built for children, a few minutes from Salou.
- Your child **behind the wheel** for the first time.

## How it works

You're picked up from your hotel and reach the circuit in about 10 minutes. After a short safety briefing, the child gets into the buggy with an adult beside them for the first laps; once they've got the hang of it, they carry on driving alone. The van then takes you back to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children aged 8 and up who are keen to drive.
- Anyone staying in **Salou, Cambrils or La Pineda** who wants a short activity.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Children **under 8** or under 120 cm.
- Anyone wanting a **long outing**: it's 20 minutes of driving.
