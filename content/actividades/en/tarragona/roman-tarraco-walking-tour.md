## What you will see

- The **Roman walls**, the oldest outside Italy.
- The **model of Tarraco**.
- The Roman **circus vaults**.
- The **amphitheatre** by the sea.

## How it works

You meet at the Itinere office by the walls and walk the old town with the guide, seeing how Roman remains blend with today's houses, go into the circus vaults and finish at the amphitheatre viewpoint.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Tarragona.
- **Roman history** fans.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't speak Spanish or Catalan**.
