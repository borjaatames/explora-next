## What you will see

- **Siurana**, a medieval village perched on a rocky outcrop.
- Its stone **Romanesque church**.
- Views over the **reservoir** and the surrounding valleys.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel and driven inland by van or SUV, with commentary from the guide along the way. In Siurana you get about an hour of free time to explore the streets, take in the viewpoints or have a drink, then head back to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone holidaying in **Salou or Cambrils** without a car who wants to see the interior.
- **Couples** and **families** with children over 5.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: there are slopes and a bit of climbing.
- Anyone wanting a **full-day excursion**.
