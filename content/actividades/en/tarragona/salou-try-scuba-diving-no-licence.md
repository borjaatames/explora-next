## What you will see

- The **seabed** off the Salou coast.
- Shallow-water marine life, with the instructor by your side.

## How it works

You meet the instructor at the dive centre, get a theory session on the equipment and basic techniques, and kit up. Then you get in the water for a personalised, guided shallow dive, with underwater photos included.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **try diving** for the first time.
- **Families** with children aged 8 and up.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **heart or breathing problems**, or pregnant women.
- Anyone looking to get **certified**: this is just a taster dive.
