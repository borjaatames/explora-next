## What you will see

- The **Mussara mountains** from the air.
- Inland Tarragona from **up to 900 metres** high.

## How it works

You are picked up at your Salou hotel and the pilot briefs you on the flight and hands you the gear. Then you head to whichever take-off point suits the day's weather and fly tandem for about 20 minutes before returning to Salou.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who has always wanted to **fly** and fancies something different.
- **Teenagers** and adults looking for an adrenaline rush (ages 9 and up).

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with a **fear of heights** or prone to motion sickness.
- Anyone on a short stay who cannot move the date if the weather turns.
