## What you will see

- A **beer-themed spa** inside a Salou hotel.
- A **wooden barrel** turned into a jacuzzi with beer extracts.

## How it works

You arrive at the hotel, are welcomed at the spa and start with a hot soak in the barrel while sipping a cold beer with snacks. Then you move on to the beer sauna and finish lying on a barley bed. You can add a massage, paid on site, if you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **groups of friends** looking for something original.
- Anyone after a **relaxing** plan for a non-beach day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Families with children**: it is adults only.
- Anyone who doesn't drink alcohol or doesn't like beer.
