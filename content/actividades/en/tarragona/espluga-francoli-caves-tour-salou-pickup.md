## What you will see

- The **caves of l'Espluga de Francolí**, around 600 metres long.
- A journey through **37,000 years of prehistory** and the communities that lived there.
- Inland scenery on the way, at the foot of the Prades mountains.

## How it works

You are picked up at your hotel or address in Salou, Cambrils or La Pineda and reach l'Espluga de Francolí in about 45 minutes. You explore the cave with an audio guide for just over an hour, then head back to the coast.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and anyone curious about **prehistory**.
- Anyone looking for a plan for a **non-beach day**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone who needs a fixed schedule confirmed well in advance.
