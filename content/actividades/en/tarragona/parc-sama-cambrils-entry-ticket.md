## What you will see

- A 19th-century **Romantic garden** with a lake, grottoes and paths among rare trees.
- **Deer**, **pheasants** and **peacocks** roaming freely.
- The **aviary** and the former summer residence from 1881.
- The **Oil Museum** and the **Vermouth Museum**, with a tasting.

## How it works

You buy the ticket for your chosen day, show it on your phone and explore the park at your own pace. It's worth climbing to the viewpoints and finishing at the oil and vermouth museums to try local products.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**, for the free-roaming animals.
- Anyone after a **calm, shady** plan away from the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for **rides** or a theme park: it's a historic garden.
