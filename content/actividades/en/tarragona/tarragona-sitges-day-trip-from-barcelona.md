## What you will see

- The **Pont del Diable**, the Roman aqueduct of Les Ferreres.
- The **amphitheatre**, **circus** and **forum** of Tarraco, along with the Roman walls.
- **Sitges** Old Town, the church of Sant Bartomeu i Santa Tecla and the Palau de Maricel.
- **Sant Sebastià beach**.

## How it works

You are picked up at your Barcelona hotel in the morning. The first stop is the Les Ferreres aqueduct, followed by a guided walk through Roman Tarragona with entry to the amphitheatre and circus. In the afternoon the guide shows you Sitges, and the day ends with free time on the beach before returning to Barcelona.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Barcelona** who wants to see Tarragona without arranging transport.
- **Couples** and **small groups** who prefer a small-group format.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **Tarraco in depth**: time in Tarragona is limited.
- **Cruise passengers**: there is no pickup at the terminal.
