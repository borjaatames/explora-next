## What you will see

- **PortAventura Park** and its six themed areas.
- **Shambhala** and **Dragon Khan**.
- **Ferrari Land** and **Red Force**.

## How it works

You pick the date and number of days, get the ticket on your phone and walk straight into the park. Ferrari Land is a single visit on the same day as your first PortAventura day.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and **groups of friends**.
- **Roller-coaster fans**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with only **a few hours** free.
