## What you will see

- The **Priorat mountains**, one of Catalonia's best-known wine regions.
- The inside of a **cellar in Cornudella de Montsant**.
- Inland scenery that contrasts with the coast.

## How it works

You are picked up at your hotel in Salou, Cambrils or La Pineda and driven by van or SUV up into Priorat. At the cellar, an oenologist explains how the local wines are made during a tasting of three or four glasses, and then you head back to the coast.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Wine lovers** staying on the coast without a car.
- Anyone after a relaxed **half-day** plan away from the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a full meal or a wine-pairing lunch.
- Travellers who need fixed timings well in advance.
