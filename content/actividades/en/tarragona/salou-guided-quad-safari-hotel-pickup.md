## What you will see

- **Tracks** and country lanes around Salou.
- A **mountain** with Costa Daurada views (2-hour route).

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel, the guide explains how to drive the quad and you set off on dirt tracks and forest trails, with a photo stop and a driver swap.

## Who I recommend it for

- Adventure-loving **couples** and **groups of friends**.
- **Families** with children aged 8 and over.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone **without a driving licence**.
