## What you will see

- The **Ebro river** and its islands.
- **Miravet's Templar castle**.
- The traditional **river ferry**.
- **Riverside woodland** and its birds.

## How it works

You get a technical briefing in Móra la Nova and paddle down the Ebro with the instructors, stopping on an island for a swim, until you reach Miravet below the castle.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and **nature** lovers.
- Kayak beginners.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
