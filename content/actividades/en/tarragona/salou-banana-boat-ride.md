## What you will see

- **Salou beach** from the water.
- Plenty of spray and the odd tumble into the sea.

## How it works

You meet the instructor on the beach, get a safety briefing and put on your life jacket. Then you climb onto the banana boat with your group and a speedboat tows you at full speed for about 15 minutes before returning to shore.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** aged 5 and up.
- **Groups of friends** looking for a fun moment at the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **back problems** or who dislikes bumps.
- Anyone looking for a long or relaxing activity.
