## What you will see

- The six areas of **PortAventura Park**: Mediterrània, Far West, México, China, Polynesia and SésamoAventura.
- The park's **rides** in the evening atmosphere.
- The **night shows** with lights, music and pyrotechnics.

## How it works

Pick your date, get the ticket on your phone and enter the park from 5 pm. You can stay until closing that day. Download the official app to check wait times and show schedules.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone in **Salou or Cambrils** who wants to see the park without spending a whole day.
- Anyone who'd rather avoid the midday **heat** in summer.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **ride everything**: one evening isn't enough.
- Anyone visiting on a day the park **closes early**.
