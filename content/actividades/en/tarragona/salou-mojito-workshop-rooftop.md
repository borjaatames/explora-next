## What you will see

- **Views over Salou** from the Summit rooftop of the Olympus Palace hotel.
- A **tasting of three rums** before you make the cocktail.

## How it works

You head up to the rooftop, taste three different rums and pick your favourite. The bartender then walks you step by step through making your own mojito with it. Afterwards you get a snack and can stay in the chill-out area with your drink and the views.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **groups of friends** looking for an evening plan.
- Anyone who wants to see Salou **from above**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with **under-18s**.
- Anyone after a full **mixology course**.
