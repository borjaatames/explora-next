## What you will see

- The **lava fields** of the Timanfaya eruptions, on foot.
- Volcanic **craters** up close.
- **Panoramic views** over the volcanic heart of the island.

## How it works

The operator contacts you the day before to confirm the time and meeting point, or your hotel pickup if you chose that option. The hike lasts about three hours and covers around 9 km at an easy pace, with a guide explaining how the landscape formed and Lanzarote's volcanic history. Snacks are provided along the way; bring your own water.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **walk on the lava**, not just see it from a coach.
- Anyone who has already done the **classic Fire Mountains visit** and wants more.
- Anyone who prefers **small groups**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after the **geothermal demonstrations** at Islote de Hilario: they aren't included.
- Anyone with **mobility issues** or low fitness.
