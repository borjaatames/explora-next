## What you'll see

A half-day trip focused only on **Timanfaya National Park**, the landscape left by the eruptions of 1730 to 1736. It's the short option if you don't want a full day on a coach.

- The **Volcano Route**, the coach circuit through craters and lava fields that you can only access on official transport.
- The **geothermal demonstrations** at Islote de Hilario: water that shoots back out as steam and brushwood that catches fire in a hole in the ground.
- Views of the **craters** and the sea of lava from the El Diablo restaurant area.
- An optional **camel ride** of about 30 minutes, paid separately in cash.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel or the point you choose when booking (there are more than forty across the island, from Playa Blanca to Costa Teguise). Depending on the area, the whole trip takes **4 to 6 hours**, and there may be a logistics stop in Yaiza to reorganise groups.

In the park, entry is included and **you don't have to fight for time slots**: Fire Mountains tickets are now sold online only, for a set time. The Volcano Route is done seated on the coach; the only walking is around Islote de Hilario, where the ground is uneven and slippery in places.

If the park closes for reasons beyond the operator's control, you visit the visitor centre instead. The camel ride and anything you book yourself are not the operator's responsibility.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone **not hiring a car** who wants to see Timanfaya without hassle.
- Anyone **short on time** who'd rather keep the afternoon free.
- Anyone who couldn't get a **timed ticket** on the official website.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone with a **car**: going early on your own, you only pay for the ticket. Read [how to visit Timanfaya](/en/guides/lanzarote/visiting-timanfaya).
- Anyone who wants to **hike** among the volcanoes: the [guided volcano hike](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/timanfaya-volcano-hike) is better.
- Anyone who also wants **Jameos and Cueva de los Verdes**: the [full-day combined tour](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/timanfaya-jameos-cueva-de-los-verdes-tour) is better value.
