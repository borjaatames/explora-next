## What you will see

- Lanzarote's southern **volcanic coast** from the sea.
- The **Papagayo beaches** and their clear water.
- With luck, **dolphins**, whales or flying fish along the way.

## How it works

You board in Puerto Calero and sail for about an hour towards Papagayo, with optional yoga on deck. There you anchor for around two hours to swim, snorkel, kayak or paddleboard and ride the slide, and lunch is served. Drinks are included throughout, all the way back to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and **groups of friends** after a full boat day.
- Anyone wanting **water activities** with no extras to pay.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **small, quiet boat**: it takes up to 55 people.
- Anyone staying in **Playa Blanca** who doesn't want to travel to Puerto Calero.
