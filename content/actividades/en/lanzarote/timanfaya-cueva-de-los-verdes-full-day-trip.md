## What you will see

- **Cueva de los Verdes**, a walk-through lava tube.
- The **cliffs by Mirador del Río**, facing La Graciosa.
- **Haría**, the valley of palms.
- A **La Geria winery**, with a glass of wine.
- **Timanfaya**: geothermal demonstrations and the Volcano Route.
- **Charco de los Clicos**, El Golfo's green lagoon.

## How it works

You're picked up in the morning and start in the north: the cave, the cliffs by Mirador del Río and Haría. Then you head through the centre of the island, past the Monument to the Peasant, stop at a La Geria winery and enter Timanfaya, with an optional camel ride. You finish at the green lagoon before returning to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with **just one day** who wants to see north and south without driving.
- Those who prefer **Cueva de los Verdes** to Jameos.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone who can't handle **long coach days**.
