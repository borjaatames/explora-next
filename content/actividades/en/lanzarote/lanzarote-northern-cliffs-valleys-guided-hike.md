## What you will do

A moderate guided walk of about **3 to 4 hours** in the **north of Lanzarote**, the greenest and least touristy part of the island. You enjoy **cliff views** over Famara and La Graciosa, walk through **ravines** and down into the **Valley of a Thousand Palms**, and pass charming villages, local flora and fauna and the island's only **handicraft market**, in Haría.

## How it works

You are **picked up at your accommodation** in an air-conditioned minivan (except in the Playa Blanca area) and walk in a **small group** with a local guide. The whole outing lasts about **4.5 hours** and costs **€42 per person**, with snacks and water included; walking poles are available on request. The walk runs in all weather, so bring warm clothes and sun protection. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Who I recommend it to

- If you want to see the green north and not just the volcanoes.
- Walkers who like viewpoints, villages and a gentle pace.
- If you want a guide in English, Spanish or French.

## Who I do not recommend it to

- If you are staying in Playa Blanca and need pickup: check with the operator first.
- Children under 11 or people with heart conditions.
