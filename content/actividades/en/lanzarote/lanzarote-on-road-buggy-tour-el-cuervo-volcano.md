## What you will see

- The crater of **El Cuervo volcano** in Los Volcanes Natural Park.
- The pit-planted vineyards of **La Geria**.
- **Las Grietas**, a highly photogenic volcanic formation.

## How it works

You meet at the back of a petrol station in Puerto del Carmen, get a windbreaker and goggles, and set off in a convoy of up to five buggies behind the guide. The first stop is El Cuervo volcano, reached on a short walk. Then you cross La Geria towards Tías and stop at Las Grietas for photos before returning to Puerto del Carmen. It takes about three hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of four** or families sharing a buggy.
- Anyone who wants to **drive** through the volcanic landscape with a guide.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after **off-road** dirt tracks.
- Anyone who wants to drive without a **licence held for two years**.
