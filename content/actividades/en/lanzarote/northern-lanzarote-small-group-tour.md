## What you will see

- **La Geria** from the road on the way north.
- **Mirador de Las Nieves** and other viewpoints off the usual circuits.
- Haría's **Valley of a Thousand Palms** from above, and El Bosquecillo.
- **Lava fields** and volcanic bombs near **Órzola**.

## How it works

You are picked up at your hotel and travel in a small vehicle along secondary roads to the north. There are viewpoint stops, a short walk among vegetation, a walk across the lava and free time by the sea in Órzola before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the north **without big coaches** or paid tourist centres.
- **Amateur photographers** and landscape lovers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to go inside **Jameos del Agua** or **Cueva de los Verdes**.
- Anyone who prefers a day with **lunch included**.
