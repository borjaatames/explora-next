## What you'll see

A nine-hour loop of the island that brings together both sides of Lanzarote: the volcanic south and Manrique's north. It's the way to see almost everything in one day without a car.

- **Timanfaya National Park**, with the geothermal demonstrations at the Fire Mountains.
- **Cueva de los Verdes** and **Jameos del Agua**, in the northern lava tube.
- A stop in **Haría**, the valley of a thousand palms, and views of **La Graciosa** from the cliffs.
- **La Geria**, with its vines planted in pits, and El Golfo's **green lagoon**.

## How it works

Pickup is from five areas: **Playa Honda, Arrecife, Playa Blanca, Costa Teguise and Puerto del Carmen**. The day before, around 15:00, the operator sends you a WhatsApp with the time and place, which isn't always your hotel door.

When booking you choose from three options: **no tickets, two tickets or all three** (Timanfaya, Jameos del Agua and Cueva de los Verdes). With tickets, the group uses a separate entrance and skips the general queue. Bought yourself in 2026, the Fire Mountains ticket costs €30, Cueva de los Verdes €17 and Jameos from €17; the first two must be bought online for a time slot.

**Lunch is not included**: there's a stop after La Geria, where according to the operator's description you taste wine from a local producer. The camel ride in Timanfaya is optional and paid separately. Wear comfortable shoes: in Cueva de los Verdes you walk about a kilometre with stairs and low ceilings.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone **not hiring a car** who wants the essentials in a day.
- Anyone with **only a few days** on the island.
- Anyone who'd rather **let someone else handle** the timed tickets.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone with a **car**: with a little planning you can do the same route at your own pace. Read [what to see in Lanzarote](/en/guides/lanzarote/what-to-see-in-lanzarote).
- Anyone who dreads **long coach days**: the [half day in Timanfaya](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/timanfaya-national-park-half-day-tour) is better.
- Anyone with **reduced mobility** or claustrophobia, because of Cueva de los Verdes.
