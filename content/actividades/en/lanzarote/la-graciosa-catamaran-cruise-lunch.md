## What you'll see

A full-day trip to **La Graciosa**, the eighth inhabited Canary Island, across the water from the Famara cliffs. It combines the scheduled ferry with a sailing catamaran along the island's south coast.

- **Caleta de Sebo**, the village of sand streets where the ferry docks, with about 45 minutes of free time.
- The island's south coast by **sailing catamaran**, inside the marine reserve.
- A stop off **Playa Francesa** to swim, take out a kayak or snorkel.
- **Paella** cooked on board, fruit and an open bar.

## How it works

You're picked up at the point nearest your accommodation (there are around twenty across the island) and taken to **Órzola** harbour. If you have a car you can go straight there: the guide and group wait at the port. Note that **there's no pickup in Playa Blanca on Sundays, Mondays and Wednesdays**, and it isn't guaranteed if you book less than 24 hours ahead.

The fast ferry takes **about 30 minutes** to Caleta de Sebo. After your free time in the village you board the catamaran with a welcome mojito and spend **almost four hours** sailing, swimming and eating. Swimming is **from the boat**: you don't land on the beach. Then it's the ferry back and the transfer to your area.

If the weather forces a cancellation, you can choose another date or ask for a full refund. The operator notes that it sells out easily in high season.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- **Families** and anyone who wants a day at sea without planning anything.
- Anyone without a **car** to get to Órzola.
- Anyone who prefers **swimming and sailing** to walking sandy tracks in the sun.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to **explore La Graciosa**: Las Conchas and Montaña Amarilla are not included. Read [how to get to La Graciosa on your own](/en/guides/lanzarote/how-to-get-to-la-graciosa).
- Anyone prone to **seasickness**: the El Río strait can be choppy.
- Anyone looking for a **party** atmosphere: party groups are not allowed.
