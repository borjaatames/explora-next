## What you will see

- The lava coast with views of the **Janubio salt flats**.
- The **Green Lagoon** at El Golfo.
- The Timanfaya **Volcano Route**.
- The vineyards of **La Geria**.
- **Jameos del Agua**, the cave reshaped by César Manrique.

## How it works

After pickup, the coach runs down the south coast with a panoramic view of the Janubio salt flats and stops at El Golfo. Next comes Timanfaya and the Volcano Route, lunch along the way and a stop in La Geria. The day ends in the north at Jameos del Agua before returning to the hotels. It's about nine hours with an official guide.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants a full day **without worrying about lunch**.
- Anyone **without a car** who wants to see the south and north in one go.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who dislikes **large groups**.
- **Wheelchair users**: the coaches aren't adapted.
