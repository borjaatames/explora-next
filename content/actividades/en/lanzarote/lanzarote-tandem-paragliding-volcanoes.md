## What you will see

- The **volcanoes** and reddish rock formations from the air.
- **Cliffs** and beaches along the Atlantic coast.
- Lanzarote's sea and landscape on a **silent flight**.

## How it works

You meet directly at the launch site confirmed a day or two before. After the safety briefing and kitting up, you take off with the pilot and fly for 20 to 40 minutes depending on the option. If the wind isn't right, another day is agreed or you get your money back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **fly for the first time** with no experience.
- **Families** with children aged 5 and up.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Pregnant women** or people over **120 kg**.
- Anyone with a **fixed day** and no flexibility: it's weather-dependent.
