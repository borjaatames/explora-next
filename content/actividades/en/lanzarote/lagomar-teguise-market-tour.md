## What you will see

- **LagOmar**, with its caves, gardens and passageways carved into volcanic rock.
- **Teguise**, the old capital, with its cobbled streets.
- The **Church of Our Lady of Guadalupe**.
- The **Teguise market**, if you go on a Sunday.

## How it works

You're picked up in your area and taken by coach to Teguise, where you get nearly two hours of free time to stroll or browse the market. Then you visit LagOmar with the guide, who tells the story of the house and the Omar Sharif legend, before returning to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to visit the **Sunday market** without a car.
- Fans of **architecture** blended into volcanic landscape.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**.
- Anyone after a **full island tour**: this is a half day.
