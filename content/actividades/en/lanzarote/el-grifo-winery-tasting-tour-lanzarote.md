## What you will see

- The **Wine Museum**, in the old 1775 winery.
- Winemaking tools and utensils from the 19th and 20th centuries.
- **Malvasia**, **listán negro** and **syrah** wines from the family estate.

## How it works

You arrive at the winery, tour the museum with the head sommelier and move on to a professional tasting of six wines with island cheeses, while learning how the trade winds and volcanic soil shape the grapes.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Wine lovers** who want a tasting with substance.
- **Couples** and small groups of adults.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with **under-18s**.
- Anyone without **transport** or who has to drive afterwards.
