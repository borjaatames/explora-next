## What you'll see

A real dive on the **Sub Fun Tres** submarine, with 44 seats and 22 large windows, which goes down to **30 metres** off Puerto Calero. According to the operator, it's one of only a handful of tourist submarines in the world.

- **Three shipwrecks** on the sandy seabed.
- Atlantic **fish** swimming freely around the hull.
- An **audio guide in 10 languages** about marine ecosystems and why they need protecting.
- Afterwards, a **personalised certificate** and free time at the marina.

## How it works

The meeting point is the **Submarine Safaris office at Marina Puerto Calero** (Module C, Unit 2), **30 minutes before** your dive. There are several departures a day; the booking page mentions 11:00, 12:00, 13:00 and 14:00, among others.

If you choose the **pickup option**, it depends on the time: the **11:00 and 13:00 dives only collect from Playa Blanca**, and the **12:00 and 14:00 dives only from Costa Teguise and Puerto del Carmen**. Outside those areas, you'll need to get there yourself.

Once aboard you sit by a window, put on the headphones and the submarine descends. The cabin stays at **normal atmospheric pressure**, so you won't feel it in your ears. The dive lasts about an hour; food and drink aren't included, but there are bars and restaurants at the marina.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- **Families with children** aged 2 and over.
- Anyone who wants to see the **seabed without diving** or getting wet.
- Anyone staying in **Puerto del Carmen, Playa Blanca or Costa Teguise** who can use the pickup.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone who already **scuba dives**: a tank dive in Lanzarote will give you much more.
- Anyone after **a day of sailing and swimming**: the [Papagayo mini-cruise](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/papagayo-beach-cruise-playa-blanca) is better.
- Anyone with **babies under 2**: they can't board.
