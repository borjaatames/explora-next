## What you will see

- The **Fire Mountains** in evening light.
- The **geothermal demonstrations** at Islote de Hilario.
- The **Volcano Route**, with stops regular visits skip.
- The **sunset** over Timanfaya's skyline.

## How it works

You're picked up in the late afternoon and reach the park after the day's visitors have left. After the demonstrations and the guided route, you have a three-course dinner cooked over volcanic heat, with drinks and live music, as the sun goes down, then return to your hotel by coach.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and travellers who know Timanfaya by day or want a **different** visit.
- Anyone after **sunset photos** in the park.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting an **intimate dinner**: tables are shared.
- **Pregnant women** or people with **heart or breathing** conditions.
