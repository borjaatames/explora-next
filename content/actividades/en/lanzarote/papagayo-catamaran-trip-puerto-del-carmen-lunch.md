## What you will see

- Lanzarote's **volcanic south coast** from the sea.
- The golden sandy beaches of **Papagayo**.
- **Puerto del Carmen** harbour on the way out and back.

## How it works

You board in Puerto del Carmen, where you are welcomed with a mojito and papas with mojo. You sail for an hour to Papagayo, anchor for about two hours to swim, use the kayaks and have lunch on board, and return to port at around 3 pm.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and **groups** wanting an all-inclusive day on a boat.
- Anyone staying in **Puerto del Carmen** or Costa Teguise.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **go ashore** at Papagayo.
- Anyone who gets **seasick** easily.
