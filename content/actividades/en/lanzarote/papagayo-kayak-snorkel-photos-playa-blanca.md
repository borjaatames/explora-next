## What you will see

- The Papagayo **cliffs** from the water.
- **Papagayo beach** and its turquoise water.
- The local **sea life** with mask and snorkel.

## How it works

After a safety briefing at the base behind the LIVVO Mirador Papagayo hotel, you paddle for about an hour to Papagayo with photo stops while the guide explains the area's history and geography. On the beach there's a picnic, snorkelling and free time, then you paddle back to the start.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children aged 5 and up.
- **Beginners** wanting to try sea kayaking.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone not comfortable **paddling for around two hours**.
