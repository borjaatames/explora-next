## What you will see

- **Vines in black ash pits** sheltered by stone walls.
- **Volcanic craters** and slopes covered in lapilli.
- The contrast between the **black ground** and the green vines.

## How it works

You meet at 9:30 at the Bodega Rubicón car park (or get picked up at your hotel if you choose that option) and walk about 8.5 km with a licensed guide who explains how eruptions and farmers' work created this landscape. The hike ends with a tasting of volcanic Malvasía and local cheese.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who enjoys **walking** and wants to see Lanzarote beyond the viewpoints.
- **Wine** lovers and fans of farming landscapes.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Children under 12**.
- People with **low fitness** or heart or breathing conditions.
