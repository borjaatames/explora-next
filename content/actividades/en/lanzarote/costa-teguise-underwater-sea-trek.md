## What you'll experience

You put on an **air-fed helmet** and walk on the **seabed** off Costa Teguise. You breathe normally, with no mouthpiece or tank, and get that moon-walk feeling while fish swim around you.

## How it works

Arrive 15 minutes early, the guide explains the gear and safety, and you go down in groups of up to 8. The walk lasts about 30 minutes, and you get photos and videos.

## Who I recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to see the seabed **without diving**.
- Families with children **aged 8 and up**.

## Who I don't recommend it to

- Pregnant women or people with reduced mobility.
