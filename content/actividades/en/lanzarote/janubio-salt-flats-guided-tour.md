## What you will see

- The **evaporation pans** where the salt crystallises.
- The **windmills** and water system that move the seawater.
- The Janubio lagoon and the Yaiza coastline.

## How it works

You arrive at the salt flats and walk around the pans with a guide who explains the traditional process, the family's history and the site's architecture. You finish by tasting flor de sal and flavoured salts, with an optional shop and coffee.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone touring the south **by car** who wants a different kind of stop.
- Travellers **curious** about traditional crafts and local food.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without **their own transport**.
- Anyone looking for an **adventure** activity.
