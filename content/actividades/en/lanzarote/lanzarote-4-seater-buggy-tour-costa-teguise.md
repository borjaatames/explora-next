## What you will see

- The villages and fields of **Guatiza**, **Charco del Palo** and **Mala**.
- **Mirador de las Nieves**, at 610 metres.
- The old **lime kilns** of Teguise.
- Tracks by the old **dam** and the volcanic landscape of the north.

## How it works

You leave Costa Teguise in convoy behind the guide, with a maximum of five buggies, and head north on roads and tracks up to the highest point of the route. You stop at the viewpoint for photos and return along a bumpier dirt stretch.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and **groups of four** who want to share one vehicle.
- Anyone after some **adrenaline** with no experience needed.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Pregnant women** or people with **back problems**.
- Anyone wanting to see **Timanfaya**: this route covers the north.
