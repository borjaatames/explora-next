## What you'll see

A full day at Lanzarote's **three most visited centres** with all three tickets included: the volcanic south in the morning and César Manrique's north in the afternoon.

- **Timanfaya**: the Volcano Route, only accessible on official transport, and the geothermal demonstrations at Islote de Hilario.
- **Cueva de los Verdes**: a guided visit through the La Corona volcano's lava tube, with its final surprise.
- **Jameos del Agua**: free time in the cave Manrique turned into a lake, garden, pool and auditorium.

## How it works

You're picked up at one of almost **60 points** on the island; the evening before, the operator confirms the time on WhatsApp. According to its itinerary, you spend about **100 minutes in Timanfaya**, an hour at lunch, **55 minutes in Cueva de los Verdes** and **55 at Jameos del Agua**. There may be a logistics stop in Yaiza to reorganise the coaches.

The big advantage is that **you don't have to book time slots**: at the Fire Mountains and Cueva de los Verdes tickets are sold online only for a set time, and in high season they sell out. Lunch isn't included: there's an optional buffet at a local restaurant, paid separately, or you can eat on your own.

In Cueva de los Verdes you walk about a kilometre with stairs, narrow passages and low ceilings; at Jameos swimming in the lake and pool is forbidden. Wear **closed shoes**: the ground at Islote de Hilario is uneven.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone **not hiring a car** who wants the must-sees in one go.
- Anyone who couldn't find **timed tickets** for Timanfaya or the Cueva.
- Anyone who prefers **caves and volcanoes** to the scenic stops of the general island tour.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone with a **car** and some flexibility: on your own you only pay for the tickets. Read [how to visit Timanfaya](/en/guides/lanzarote/visiting-timanfaya).
- Anyone with **reduced mobility** or claustrophobia, because of the Cueva.
- Anyone who also wants **La Geria and El Golfo**: the [full-day island tour](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/lanzarote-full-day-island-tour) includes them.
