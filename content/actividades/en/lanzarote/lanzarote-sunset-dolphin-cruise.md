## What you'll see

Two hours on a catamaran along the **Puerto del Carmen** coast, with the sunset as the main event and a chance of meeting wild dolphins.

- Lanzarote's **south coast** from the sea, in sunset light.
- The areas where **dolphins** often move, with no guarantee of seeing them.
- A **swim stop**, if the sea allows.
- A **welcome mojito**, another drink of your choice and a snack box each.

## How it works

The transfer is included: you're picked up at one of **17 points** around the island (hotels, the Arrecife fairground or the C.C. Centro Atlántico bus stop, among others) and taken to **Puerto del Carmen harbour**. There are exceptions: **Puerto Calero pickup only runs on Thursdays** and **there's no Playa Blanca pickup on Sundays, Mondays or Wednesdays**. If you book less than 24 hours ahead, pickup isn't guaranteed and you'll need to get to the harbour yourself; parking there is free but limited.

The **departure time changes** with the season to match the sunset. On board there's music, a deck to lie out on and your included drink; anything else is extra. If you're vegetarian or vegan, say so when booking; the operator can't guarantee gluten-free options.

The key thing to know: **dolphin sightings are not guaranteed** and if none appear there's no refund or new date. If bad weather forces a cancellation, you can choose another date or get your money back.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- **Couples** looking for a relaxed end to the day.
- Anyone staying in **Puerto del Carmen** or Costa Teguise who doesn't want to drive.
- Anyone who wants to see the coast from the sea **without spending a whole day** on a boat.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone coming **just for the dolphins**: there's no guarantee and no compensation.
- Anyone after **swimming and coves**: the [Papagayo mini-cruise](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/papagayo-beach-cruise-playa-blanca) is better.
- Anyone prone to **seasickness**, especially on windy days.
