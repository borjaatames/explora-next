## What you will see

- **LagOmar**, with its lagoon, tunnels and caves carved into the mountain.
- The **Cactus Garden**, with hundreds of species and a traditional windmill.
- **La Graciosa**, its fishing village and church.

## How it works

You are picked up in the morning and travel by coach to LagOmar and the Cactus Garden. From Órzola you take the ferry to La Graciosa, have a short visit, eat paella with drinks and enjoy free time before returning by ferry and coach to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** who want to see the north and La Graciosa without driving.
- Anyone with **only a few days** who wants to combine art and nature.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone who would rather spend **the whole day** on La Graciosa.
