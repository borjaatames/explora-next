## What you will see

- The **volcanic coastline** between Puerto del Carmen and Puerto Calero.
- The unspoilt beaches of **Playa Quemada**.
- On the long route, the **Papagayo** beaches and Las Coloradas.

## How it works

You suit up with a wetsuit and life jacket at the harbour, get a safety briefing and head out behind the guide. You pick the route when booking: the short one reaches Playa Quemada, while the longer ones continue towards Papagayo before returning to Puerto del Carmen.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** who want to share a jet ski and split the cost.
- Anyone after an **adrenaline** hit and a view of the coast from the water.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with **children under 8**.
- Anyone with **back problems** or prone to seasickness.
