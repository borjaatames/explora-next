## What you will see

- **Cueva de los Verdes**, a lava tube from the La Corona volcano.
- **Jameos del Agua**, the part of the same tube transformed by César Manrique.
- The view over **La Graciosa** from the Guinate viewpoint.

## How it works

You're picked up at a stop near your hotel and the coach heads north. First there's a short stop at the Guinate viewpoint, then an almost hour-long guided visit of Cueva de los Verdes, followed by another hour to explore Jameos del Agua at your own pace. It takes about five hours in total, with no lunch stop.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **both northern caves** without a car.
- Anyone with **just a morning** free.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **reduced mobility** or **claustrophobia**.
- Anyone staying in **Playa Blanca**: there's no pickup there.
