## What you'll see

A guided walk of **about 8 km in a loop** through the volcanic west of Lanzarote, next to Timanfaya, with a local guide from **Lava Trekking** and a group of **nine people at most**.

- **Several volcanoes** and lava flows, walking across the lava field.
- Explanations of the **geology, the plants** colonising the lava and the history of the eruption.
- A halfway stop with **Canarian snacks and Canary Islands white wine**.
- A minute of silence in the middle of the lava field, which the operator offers as a pause to meditate.

## How it works

The meeting point is a **car park on the left of the Parque Natural de los Volcanes road, before Tinajo**: it sits between two hills and is marked by a palm tree. The operator sends the Google Maps location. If you don't have a car, you can request a **transfer** when booking, possibly at extra cost; it doesn't reach Playa Blanca, though pickup in Yaiza is possible.

The start time follows the **sunset**: around 17:00 in June and 14:30 in December. It's about **three hours** of walking on volcanic ground, which participants describe as easy to moderate, with some uphill. Wear **trainers or walking shoes** and bring water and ID; the guide provides sun cream.

One important point: the listing mentions Timanfaya, but you can't walk freely inside the National Park. The route runs through the **Parque Natural de los Volcanes**, which surrounds it, and doesn't include Islote de Hilario or the Volcano Route coach.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to **walk on lava** rather than just see it from a coach.
- Anyone who has already done the **official Fire Mountains visit** and wants more.
- Anyone looking for a **small group** and a local guide.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone who wants the **geothermal demonstrations**: those are on the [half-day Timanfaya tour](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/timanfaya-national-park-half-day-tour).
- Anyone with **mobility problems** or over 70, under the operator's conditions.
- Anyone staying in **Playa Blanca** without a car: there's no pickup there.
