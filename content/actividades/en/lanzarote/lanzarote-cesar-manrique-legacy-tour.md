## What you will see

- The **Monument to the Peasant** in the centre of the island.
- The **Cactus Garden** in Guatiza.
- **Mirador del Río**, looking across to La Graciosa.
- **Jameos del Agua**, a lava tube turned into an art space.
- The **César Manrique Foundation**, his former home built over lava bubbles.
- Views of **Haría**, the **Famara cliffs** and **La Corona volcano**.

## How it works

You're picked up in your area and spend the day touring the north by coach with an official guide, who explains how Manrique blended art, architecture and volcanic landscape. Tickets to all five sites are included; lunch is on you.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **Manrique's essentials** without hiring a car.
- Travellers into **art, architecture and landscape**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone who prefers **small groups** and free time at each stop.
