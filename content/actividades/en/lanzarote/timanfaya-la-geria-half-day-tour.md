## What you will see

- The Timanfaya **Volcano Route**, with craters and lava rivers.
- The **geothermal demonstrations** at Islote de Hilario.
- The **Green Lagoon** at El Golfo.
- The pit-planted vineyards of **La Geria**.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel or a nearby stop and the coach heads straight to Timanfaya, where you spend around an hour and a quarter on the guided route and the underground heat demonstrations. Then there's a stop at a viewpoint and at El Golfo to see the green lagoon, and a final one in La Geria before heading back. It takes about five and a half hours in total and doesn't include lunch.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants **Timanfaya without giving up a full day**.
- Anyone without a car who wants to add **El Golfo and La Geria**.
- **Families** with children who tire on long tours.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone hoping to **taste wine** at a winery: there's no tasting.
- **Wheelchair users**: the coaches aren't adapted.
