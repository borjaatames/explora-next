## What you will see

- **Volcanic cones** and craters from the 1730-1736 and 1824 eruptions.
- **Lava flows**, solidified lava lakes and the remains of lava tubes.
- How **life slowly returns** to the lava.

## How it works

You meet at Camino Testeina (or get picked up if you choose that option) and walk about 8 km with the guide through Los Volcanes Natural Park, climbing some cones and crossing lava flows while learning how the landscape formed.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **walk on the volcanoes**, not just see them from a coach.
- **Families** with children aged 7 and up who are used to walking.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility** or low fitness.
- Anyone wanting to see the **geysers** at Islote de Hilario: those are in the National Park.
