## What you will see

- A **vineyard** at the foot of the Testeina volcano in La Geria.
- The estate's **manor house** and farmhouse, with centuries of history.
- Views of **Masdache** and Los Volcanes Natural Park.

## How it works

You arrive at the estate, take a roughly 50-minute walk through the vines while the guide explains growing in volcanic ash and the island's water culture, and finish with a tasting of two wines and chocolates facing the volcano.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone **driving** through La Geria who wants a stop with substance.
- **Wine lovers** curious about Malvasía Volcánica.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without **their own transport**.
- Anyone looking for a **meal** or a long tasting.
