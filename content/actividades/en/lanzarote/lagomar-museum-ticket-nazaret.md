## What you will see

- **Caves and tunnels** carved into a former lava quarry.
- **Terraces, staircases and gardens** built into the rock.
- The **Omar Sharif house**, the best-known part of the complex.
- **Art galleries** and shops with Lanzarote products.

## How it works

Make your own way to the museum in Nazaret, show your ticket at the box office and explore the complex at your own pace. A typical visit takes around 45 minutes. Last entry is at 5:30 pm.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone into **architecture** and **photography**.
- Anyone **driving** around the centre and north of the island who wants a short stop.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**.
- Anyone after a **guided tour**: it's self-guided.
