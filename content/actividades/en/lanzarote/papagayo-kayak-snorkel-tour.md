## What you'll see

A morning in a kayak around **Papagayo bay**, the golden-sand coves of the Los Ajaches Natural Monument, with a beach stop for snorkelling.

- **Papagayo bay** from the water, with stops for explanations and photos.
- A **protected area full of fish** for snorkelling.
- The cliffs of the **Los Ajaches park**.
- **Sea caves** on the way back, if sea conditions allow.

## How it works

You meet **in front of the reception of Hotel Sandos Papagayo**, east of Playa Blanca. A guide in **Papagayo Watersports** uniform walks you to the centre by the beach. According to the description, the activity starts at **9:45**: you're given a dry bag, mask, snorkel, water and a short briefing on paddling and safety.

Then you paddle around the bay for **about 45 minutes** with stops. On the beach there's a chocolate bar and fruit, and **an hour and a quarter** to snorkel or walk up to the viewpoints. On the way back, if the sea allows, you go into sea caves. You're back around **13:00**, and the **photos** arrive by WeTransfer that same afternoon.

Transport to the hotel isn't included, so you'll need to get there yourself. Bring a towel and beachwear; the rest of the gear is provided.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone staying in or near **Playa Blanca**.
- **Families** with children aged 3 and over, and beginners.
- Anyone who wants to see Papagayo **with some activity**, not just from a boat.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone staying **far from the south** who doesn't want an early start.
- Anyone who'd rather **not paddle**: the [Papagayo mini-cruise](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/papagayo-beach-cruise-playa-blanca) is more relaxed.
- Anyone **over 130 kg**: the operator doesn't accept them.
