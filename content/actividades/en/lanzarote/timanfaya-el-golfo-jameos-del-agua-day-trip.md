## What you will see

- The **Green Lagoon** at El Golfo, a lagoon inside a crater by the sea.
- The **Fire Mountains** and the Volcano Route in Timanfaya.
- The **geothermal demonstrations** at Islote de Hilario.
- **Jameos del Agua**, the lava tube transformed by César Manrique.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel or a nearby stop and the coach heads south first: El Golfo, Timanfaya with its guided route and Islote de Hilario, and an optional camel ride. Then there's a lunch break (paid separately) before the coach crosses to the north, with an optional photo stop at Monte Corona, ending with almost an hour at Jameos del Agua. In total it takes 7 to 8 hours depending on your pickup point.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone **without a car** who wants the essentials in one day.
- Anyone on a **short stay** on the island.
- **Families** who'd rather someone else handled tickets and transport.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **go at their own pace**: stops are short.
- Anyone with **reduced mobility**.
