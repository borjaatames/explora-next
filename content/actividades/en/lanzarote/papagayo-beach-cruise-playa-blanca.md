## What you'll see

A short **Líneas Romero** catamaran trip from Playa Blanca to the **Papagayo coves**, the golden-sand beaches of the Los Ajaches Natural Monument. It's more a swimming trip than a sailing one.

- Lanzarote's **south coast**, with the Los Ajaches cliffs.
- An **hour at anchor** in Papagayo bay to swim or snorkel.
- A **deck** with areas to sit and lie in the sun.
- With the early departure, free time at **Marina Rubicón**, which has a market on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

## How it works

There's no hotel pickup: you leave from **Playa Blanca**, and the boarding and return point depends on the option you book. According to the published itinerary, there are about twenty minutes of sailing with a short stop, another leg to the bay, **an hour of swimming** and the return. In total, between **an hour and a half and three hours**, depending on the option.

The boat **anchors in the bay**: you don't land on the beach. **Snorkelling gear** is included, though you can bring your own. Beer, sangria, water, soft drinks and snacks are sold on board; no food or drink is included.

Bring a jacket even if it's sunny: on the way back, with wet hair and the wind, it gets cool. If you get seasick easily, think twice; the operator advises against it.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Anyone staying in **Playa Blanca** who wants to see Papagayo without a car.
- **Families** looking for some time at sea without a full day on a boat.
- Anyone who wants to **swim** in calm water on a tight budget.

## Who I wouldn't recommend it to

- Anyone who wants to **lie on the sand**: with a car, the Papagayo track costs €3 and drops you at the coves.
- Anyone who'd rather **paddle**: the [Papagayo kayak and snorkel tour](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/papagayo-kayak-snorkel-tour) is more active.
- Anyone after a **party on board**: party groups and stag or hen dos are not allowed.
