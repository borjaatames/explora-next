## What you will see

- A three-part **comedy drag** show with stand-up and mime routines.
- **Live vocals**, harmonies and full-cast choreography.
- Routines with **LED and fire**.

## How it works

You arrive at the venue in the La Peñita shopping centre in Puerto del Carmen, on your own or with the optional transfer. The cast welcomes you, your souvenir photo is taken and you have a three-course dinner with table service as the show begins, split into three parts with intervals. It takes about four hours in total; drinks are ordered separately at the bar.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and hen or stag parties after a fun night.
- **Families** with children who understand English.
- Anyone celebrating a **birthday** or special occasion.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone **not comfortable with English**: much of the humour is lost.
- Anyone looking for a **gourmet dinner**.
