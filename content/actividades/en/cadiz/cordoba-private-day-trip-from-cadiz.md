## How the day works

Your driver picks you up in Cádiz and in about two and a half hours you reach **Córdoba**, where a local guide is waiting. Together you do a **two-and-a-half-hour private tour** of the essentials: the **Mosque-Cathedral** (ticket included) and the **Jewish Quarter**, where Roman, Islamic, Jewish and Christian cultures met.

Then you have free time for lunch or a stroll before heading back to Cádiz.

## The two options

- **8 hours:** guided tour plus an hour and a half free.
- **10 hours:** the same tour plus two and a half hours free.

## Who it's for

- Visitors staying in Cádiz or arriving by cruise who don't want to miss the Mosque.
- Families and groups: the price is per group.
