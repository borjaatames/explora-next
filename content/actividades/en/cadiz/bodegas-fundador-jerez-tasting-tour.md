## What you will see

- The **La Mezquita** hall, with its arches and casks.
- The winery's historic **gardens**.
- The Fundador and Harveys **museum**.
- The sherry and brandy **tasting**.

## How it works

You arrive at the winery, the guide takes you through the halls, gardens and museum explaining how sherry and brandy are made, and you finish with the tasting.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Wine lovers**.
- Anyone on a **day trip to Jerez** from Cádiz.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't drink alcohol**.
