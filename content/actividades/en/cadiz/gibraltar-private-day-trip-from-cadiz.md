## How the day works

Your driver collects you from your hotel in Cádiz and in an hour and a half you reach the **Gibraltar border**, where your guide is waiting. For about two hours you tour the Rock in a private vehicle:

- The **macaque area**, the only place in Europe where they roam free.
- **Europa Point**, the mythical 'end of the world' linked to the labours of Hercules.
- **St Michael's Cave**, steeped in history and legend.
- The **Great Siege Tunnels**, carved into the rock.
- The **Skywalk**: three countries and two continents from one viewpoint.

Finally your guide drops you on **Main Street** with an hour and a half to two hours free for lunch, a stroll or shopping, before heading back to Cádiz.

## Who it's for

- Visitors in Cádiz who want to see Gibraltar without driving.
- Families and groups: the price is per group.
