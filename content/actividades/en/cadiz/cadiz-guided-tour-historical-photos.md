## What you will see

- **El Pópulo** and **Cathedral Square**.
- **La Viña**, home of the Carnival, and **La Caleta beach**.
- **Gran Teatro Falla**, **Plaza de San Antonio** and the **Oratory of San Felipe Neri**.
- **Torre Tavira** and the **Central Market**.

## How it works

You leave from the Town Hall with your guide and walk the old town for two hours. At every stop you see old photographs of the same spot to compare then and now. You finish at the Central Market with recommendations on where to eat.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone after a **budget-friendly**, entertaining tour.
- **History** and **photography** lovers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to go inside the cathedral or Torre Tavira: no tickets are included.
