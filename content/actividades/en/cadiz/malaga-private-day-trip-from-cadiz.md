## How the day works

Your driver picks you up in Cádiz and you travel along the **Andalusian coast** to **Málaga**, about two and a half hours away. There your guide meets you for a **walk of about two hours through the historic centre**: around the **Cathedral**, the **Alcazaba** and the city's Phoenician, Roman and Moorish traces.

Then you have **two hours free** for lunch, museums, a stroll or shopping, before heading back to Cádiz.

## Who it's for

- Visitors in Cádiz whose trip won't otherwise take in Málaga.
- Groups of four or more: the group price rises little.
