## What you will see

- The **cathedral** and Campo del Sur from the water.
- **San Sebastián** and **Santa Catalina** castles.
- **La Caleta** and sunset over the Atlantic.

## How it works

You board at the Estación Marítima, the catamaran cruises the bay while you listen to the audio guide and enjoy your cava, and you return to port after sunset.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and anyone after a relaxed evening plan.
- **Sunset photographers**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone prone to **seasickness**.
