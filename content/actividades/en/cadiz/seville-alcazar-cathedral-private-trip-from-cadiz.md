## How the day works

You're picked up at your Cádiz hotel or the cruise pier and in just under an hour and a half you reach **Seville**. There a **three-hour visit with an official guide** begins:

- The **Royal Alcázar**, one of Spain's most extraordinary palaces: Mudéjar architecture, courtyards, tilework and gardens still used by the Royal Family.
- The **Cathedral**, the largest Gothic church in the world, with its chapels, the **tomb of Christopher Columbus** and the **Giralda**.

Tickets are included. Then you have **two hours free** for tapas, shopping or getting lost in the lanes of **Santa Cruz** before heading back.

## Good to know

This trip is **non-refundable**.

## Who it's for

- Cruise passengers who want to see Seville's two great monuments.
- Anyone who'd rather not queue or worry about tickets.
