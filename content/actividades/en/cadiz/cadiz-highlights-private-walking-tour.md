## What the tour looks like

You start from your hotel or the port and walk into the **Pópulo**, the oldest quarter of Cádiz, with its medieval alleys and arches. Next comes a private visit inside the **Cathedral**, whose golden dome dominates the skyline. You continue to **La Caleta**, between the castles of San Sebastián and Santa Catalina, and finish in **La Viña**, the neighbourhood of carnival and local food.

## How it works

It is **private** and the guide speaks **English**. The Cathedral ticket is included; transfers and lunch are not.

## Who I recommend it to

- Cruise passengers with a few hours in Cádiz.
- Couples and families who want the essentials of the city with context.
