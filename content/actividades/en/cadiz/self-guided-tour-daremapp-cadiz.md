## What you'll see and discover

Explore Cádiz on your own terms with the **DareMapp** app: a self-guided audio tour covering +25 points of interest (2 different routes), including the Parroquia de la Santa Cruz, the Gran Teatro Falla, the Museo de Cádiz and the Cathedral. No group schedule and no waiting around: you choose which stops to make, how long to spend at each one, and when to pause or pick the route back up, even on a different day.

## How it works

There's no physical meeting point. After purchase, your ticket includes a **reference code** that you activate from your phone, whenever and wherever you choose in Cádiz. The app runs in 6 languages (Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian and German) and is available **24 hours a day, 365 days a year** — the access never expires. Inside the app you'll also find food and family-activity recommendations to round out your visit.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Independent travellers, couples or families who'd rather not depend on set times or a group.
- Anyone who likes exploring at a relaxed pace and deciding what to see as they go.
- Anyone after a flexible, affordable option — though it won't give you the interaction or stories of a live guide.
