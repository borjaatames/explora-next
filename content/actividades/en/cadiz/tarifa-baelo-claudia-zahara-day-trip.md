## What you'll see

A full day along the wildest stretch of the **Costa de la Luz**:

- **Tarifa**: the southernmost point of mainland Europe, with Guzmán el Bueno castle and the Moroccan coast across the strait.
- **Baelo Claudia**: a Roman town by **Bolonia** beach and its huge dune, with forum, temples, theatre and fish-salting factories.
- **Zahara de los Atunes**: a fishing village with one of the best beaches in Cádiz.

## How it works

Transport and a guide in Spanish or English. The departure point and length depend on the option booked. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
