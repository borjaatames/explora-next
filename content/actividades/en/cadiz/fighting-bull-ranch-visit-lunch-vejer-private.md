## How the day works

You're picked up at your hotel and driven straight to a **fighting bull ranch** in the **La Janda** area of Cádiz. The bull's habitat is the **dehesa**, open wooded pasture where it feeds and develops its defining traits: bravery, presence, strength and nobility. There you discover that ecosystem and see how the bulls live in herds.

Then you visit the part of the ranch where they explain the **selection process** and the care each bull receives throughout its life.

After the visit you head to **Vejer de la Frontera** for a **traditional lunch** (included). You're back at your hotel by mid-afternoon.

## Who it's for

- Families with children who want to see animals in the countryside.
- Anyone with an interest in Spain's bull-breeding tradition.
- Visitors looking for something other than monuments in Cádiz province.
