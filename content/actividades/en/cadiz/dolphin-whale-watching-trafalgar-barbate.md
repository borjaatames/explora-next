## What you will see

- Common and bottlenose **dolphins**.
- **Pilot whales** and, depending on the season, **fin whales** or orcas.
- The **Trafalgar lighthouse** from the sea.

## How it works

You board at Barbate marina and head out off Cape Trafalgar. The guides track down the cetaceans and explain their behaviour before returning to port.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Nature lovers**.
- **Families** with kids who don't get seasick.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone prone to **seasickness**.
