## What you'll see

A guided walk around **Cádiz** old town with two entries included:

- **Torre Tavira**: the city's tallest watchtower, where merchants watched for their ships. At the top is the **camera obscura**, which projects Cádiz live, and a terrace with views.
- **The cathedral**: the one with the golden dome, built in the 18th and 19th centuries with money from trade with the Americas.

## How it works

Two hours with a guide in Spanish or English. The tower has 173 steps. Minimum 2 adults per booking.
