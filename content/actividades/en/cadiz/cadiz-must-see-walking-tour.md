## What you'll see

- The **Campo del Sur**, the seafront walk with the cathedral behind it.
- **El Pópulo**, the oldest neighbourhood in Cádiz.
- **Calle Ancha** and its 18th-century palace houses.
- The **cathedral**, from outside.

## How it works

You meet the guide in the centre and walk the old town for about two hours. At the end, the guide shares tips for the rest of your stay.

## Who I recommend it to

- First-time visitors to Cádiz.
- Families or groups who prefer a **private tour**.
