## What you will see

- The **Cádiz skyline** from the bay.
- The coast towards **El Puerto de Santa María**.
- **La Puntilla beach**, where the boat anchors for swimming.

## How it works

You board at the port of Cádiz and sail for about 45 minutes to La Puntilla. The catamaran anchors there for an hour so you can swim and use the giant mats and slide. Then you sail back, with chill-out music on weekdays and a livelier vibe at weekends.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** looking for a sea-and-swim day.
- Anyone wanting to **cool off** in summer without going to the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a guided heritage commentary.
- **Stag and hen parties** and party groups, which are not allowed.
