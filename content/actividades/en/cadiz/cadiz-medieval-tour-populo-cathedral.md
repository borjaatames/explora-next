## What you will see

- **El Pópulo**, the oldest quarter of Cádiz, and its medieval gates.
- The **Roman theatre** and Phoenician-era remains.
- The **New Cathedral** inside and the view from the **Poniente Tower**.
- The **Puerta de Tierra** city wall.

## How it works

You meet your guide in Plaza de San Juan de Dios, by the Town Hall. Over two hours you walk the old town, enter the cathedral with the ticket already included and climb the tower. Choose a standard group (up to 30 people) or a private option.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** who want to see inside the cathedral without queuing or sorting tickets.
- **Cruise passengers** or anyone with just a morning in town.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who has already visited the cathedral and just wants a city stroll.
- Groups of **minors without two adults**.
