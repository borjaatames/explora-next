## What you'll see

Bodegas are the great draw of a city that grew up around wine, and visiting one is the best way to understand Jerez. On this **private** visit your guide takes you round one of the **largest bodegas in Jerez**: its cellars, its casks and how the local wines are made.

Inside the bodega you'll also see its **private collection of works by Picasso and Botero**. The visit ends with a **wine tasting**.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants a bodega visit without a group, in their own language (Spanish, English or French).
- Groups of three or four: the price per person drops a lot.
