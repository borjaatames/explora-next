## What you will see

- The **Fuente del Suero** estate and its facilities.
- This year's **mares and foals** roaming free.
- A **display** of dressage, mare teams and carriages.

## How it works

You arrive at the estate, the guide shows you the facilities and the horses in the fields, and you finish in the main arena with the show.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Horse lovers**.
- **Families with children**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone travelling **without a car**.
