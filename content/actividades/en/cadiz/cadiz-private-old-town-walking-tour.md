## The route

You start in **Plaza de San Juan de Dios**, outside the City Hall, the historic heart of Cádiz. From there you head into the **Pópulo**, the city's oldest quarter, with cobbled lanes and hidden corners. Then on to **Cathedral Square**, the colourful flower stalls of **Plaza de las Flores**, elegant **Calle Ancha** and peaceful **Plaza de Mina**. The walk ends at the **Central Market**, with its tuna, shellfish and buzz.

## How it works

- It's **private** and the **price is per group**.
- Choose **2 or 3 hours**.
- The route is flexible: tell your guide if you want to see something in particular.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors who want the old town explained.
- Families: strollers are fine.
