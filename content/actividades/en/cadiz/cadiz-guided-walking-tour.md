## What you will see

- The **Pópulo quarter** and the Arco de los Blanco.
- The **old** and **new cathedrals**.
- The **Constitution of 1812 monument** in Plaza de España.
- Old-town squares and streets.

## How it works

You meet the guide in the centre and walk the old town for two hours, hearing about Phoenicians, Romans, the Indies trade and the 1812 Cortes.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** to Cádiz.
- **History lovers**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers**: a minimum of 2 adults is required.
