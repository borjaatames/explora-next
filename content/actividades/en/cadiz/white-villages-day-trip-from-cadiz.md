## What you will see

- **Zahara de la Sierra**, its castle and turquoise reservoir.
- **Grazalema** and its whitewashed streets.
- The **Palacio de los Ribera** in Bornos.
- Scenery of the **Sierra de Grazalema Natural Park**.

## How it works

The coach picks you up at your chosen point and stops first in Bornos. It then climbs to Zahara for a guided walk and ends in Grazalema, with a visit and free time for lunch, before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see the **white villages without driving**.
- Anyone after **mountains and villages** after the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: lots of steep streets.
