## How the day works

Your driver picks you up in Cádiz and you head first to **Arcos de la Frontera**, perched on a crag with narrow streets and big views: an hour to wander or have a coffee. In under an hour you reach **Zahara de la Sierra**, with its castle and turquoise reservoir: another free hour.

About 30 km on is **Grazalema**, deep in a natural park. This stop is longer, about two hours, for lunch, a stroll or some shopping. Then it's an hour and a half back to Cádiz.

## The two options

- **Driver only:** private transport and free time in each village.
- **With guide:** adds a private guide for the trip.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to see the white villages without driving, at their own pace.
- Families and groups: the price is per group.
