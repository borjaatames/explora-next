## What you will see

- Cádiz **Central Market**.
- The area around **Torre Tavira**, **Plaza de San Antonio** and **Plaza de Mina**.
- The finish at the **Arco del Pópulo**.

## How it works

You set off from the City Hall with a local guide and stop at bars and stalls in the centre. You try chicharrón, papas aliñás, fried fish, shrimp fritters and a traditional dessert, with sherry and other drinks included. Groups are capped at 12 people.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **eat well** and understand Cádiz cooking in one afternoon.
- **Couples** and solo travellers looking for a social outing.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
- Anyone happy to go tapas-hopping alone on a tight budget.
