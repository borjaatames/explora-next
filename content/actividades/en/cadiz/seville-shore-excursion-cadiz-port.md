## What you'll see

A day in **Seville** from **Cádiz port**, designed for cruise passengers. With a guide you walk through the **Santa Cruz** quarter, pass the **General Archive of the Indies** and see the **Cathedral** and the **Royal Alcázar**, two World Heritage sites. There's free time at the end to explore on your own.

## How it works

When you leave the passenger terminal, cross the street: they wait by the gate with a **Spain Day Tours / Top Day Tours** sign. The drive to Seville takes about 1 h 30 min on the motorway, in a **large coach with other groups** and one stop at a service area. In Seville the visit is in **groups of 10 to 18** with an **English-speaking** guide. The operator adapts the duration to your time in port and includes a back-to-ship guarantee.

**The Royal Alcázar ticket isn't included.** As soon as you book, email the operator each person's full name, passport number and date of birth so they can get your skip-the-line ticket.

## Who I recommend it for

- Cruise passengers calling at Cádiz who want to use the day to see Seville.
- Anyone who doesn't want to gamble on trains and timetables on a port day.
- Travellers happy with a coach leg in exchange for a fair price.
