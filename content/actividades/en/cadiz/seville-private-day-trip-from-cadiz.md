## How the day works

You're picked up at your Cádiz hotel or the cruise pier and in just under an hour and a half you're in **Seville**. You have up to five hours there, starting with a **private guided walk of about two hours** through the **Santa Cruz quarter**, **Plaza de España**, the **historic centre** and the banks of the **Guadalquivir**.

Then you have about two and a half hours free for lunch, a stroll or a monument on your own, before heading back to Cádiz.

## Good to know

It's an outdoor tour: **no tickets** to the Royal Alcázar or the Cathedral. If you'd like them added, contact the operator before booking.

## Who it's for

- Cruise passengers calling at Cádiz.
- Anyone wanting a first taste of Seville without changing hotel.
