## What you will see

- The **Torres Miradores** and the San Carlos walls.
- The **cathedral** from the sea.
- The castles and **La Caleta**, or the **1812 bridge** on the south route.

## How it works

You check in with the host at the Estación Marítima, board, and the catamaran cruises the bay for 75 minutes with the audio guide before returning to the same pier.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**.
- Anyone wanting a **first look** at Cádiz from the water.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after **sunset**: the sunset tour is better.
