## What you will see

- The **Explanada** and **City Hall square**.
- The **Basilica of Santa María** and the Moor's Face on Mount Benacantil.
- The **Ereta Park** viewpoint and the **Santa Cruz** quarter.
- **St Nicholas Co-Cathedral**, **Mushroom Street** and Plaza Gabriel Miró.

## How it works

You meet at the tourist office by the Explanada and walk through the old town with a local guide for two hours, with stops for stories and photos, finishing in Plaza Gabriel Miró.

## Who I recommend it for

- Travellers who speak **English, French or Italian**.
- Anyone just arrived who wants to **get their bearings**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants the tour **in Spanish**.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
