## What you will see

- **Altea old town**, with its white houses and blue-domed church.
- **Calpe historic centre**.
- The **Peñón de Ifach** from the beach and promenade.

## How it works

The coach leaves Puerta del Mar and reaches Altea in about 75 minutes, where the guide leads a short walk before an hour and a half of free time. You then continue to Calpe for a brief old-town walk, some free time and two hours by the beach before heading back to Alicante.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in Alicante **without a car** who wants to see two coastal towns in a day.
- Travellers who like plenty of **free time** to stroll, eat and swim.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after an **in-depth guided visit**: the explanations are short.
- Anyone who dislikes **large coach groups**.
