## What you'll see

A kayak trip from **Granadella cove** in Jávea, following the cliffs to **Cala en Caló**, a cove you can only reach by sea.

Along the way you paddle into the **Cova del Llop Marí** sea cave and stop to **snorkel** in very clear water, with gear included.

## How it works

Two and a half hours with a guide in Spanish or English. Maximum weight 100 kg; children aged 4 to 12 with an adult. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
