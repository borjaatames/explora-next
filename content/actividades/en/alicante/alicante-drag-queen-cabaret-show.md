## What you will see

- A **cabaret show** with drag queens and female impersonators.
- **Comedy, dance and music** numbers from the 80s and 90s.
- Themed productions that change with the **season**.

## How it works

You arrive at La Casa delle Follie at least 20 minutes early, take your table and order your two drinks. The show lasts around two hours and ends with dancing.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and stag or hen parties looking for a fun night.
- Fans of **cabaret** and 80s and 90s music.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **quiet or classic** show.
