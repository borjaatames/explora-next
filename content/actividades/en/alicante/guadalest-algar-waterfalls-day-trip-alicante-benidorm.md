## What you will see

- **Guadalest**, a village perched on a rock, with its castle and cobbled lanes.
- Panoramic views over the inland mountains.
- The **Algar Waterfalls**, with cascades, pools and wooden bridges.

## How it works

You travel by coach from Alicante or Benidorm to Guadalest, where you have around two hours to explore. Then you continue to the Algar Waterfalls for another two hours of walking and swimming before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone on the coast who wants an **inland day** without a car.
- Families and couples after **nature and a swim** in summer.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: there are stairs and uneven ground.
