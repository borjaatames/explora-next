## What you will see

- A full **flamenco ensemble**: dance, singing and guitar.
- The old **stables of the Marbeuf Palace**, turned into a performance space.
- The **Santa Cruz** quarter, with whitewashed houses below the castle.

## How it works

You arrive at the venue with your ticket and take your seat facing the stage. The show lasts around an hour and a half, with dancers, singers and a guitarist performing live.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone seeing **flamenco for the first time** on a budget.
- Couples looking for an **evening plan** in the old town.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting **dinner with the show**: no food is included.
