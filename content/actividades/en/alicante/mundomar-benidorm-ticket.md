## Benidorm's marine park

**Mundomar** is a marine and exotic animal park next to Terra Mítica, focused on **shows, interaction with animals, and a tropical garden**.

### The shows

**Dolphin show** — the highlight of any visit. A 20-minute performance showcasing the intelligence and agility of bottlenose dolphins. The trainers provide commentary on dolphin biology and behaviour alongside the performance.

**Sea lion show** — similarly entertaining, with trained California sea lions demonstrating natural behaviours and tricks.

Both shows are included in the entry ticket. Timetables are posted at the entrance; plan your day around the show times.

### The animal enclosures

Beyond the shows, Mundomar has:
- **Tropical bird garden** with free-flying parrots, flamingos, and toucans
- **Ring-tailed lemurs** in a walk-through enclosure
- **Capybaras, meerkats**, and other small exotic mammals
- **Reptile house** with snakes, lizards, and tortoises

### Swim with dolphins

A separate, paid "swim with dolphins" experience is available (book at the ticket office). Not included in the standard entry, but popular with families wanting a closer interaction.

## Combining with Terra Mítica

Mundomar and Terra Mítica share the same access road and parking. Many families visit both parks on the same day — Mundomar in the morning (calmer, fewer crowds, good for young children) and Terra Mítica in the afternoon (for older children and teenagers).
