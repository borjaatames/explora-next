## What you will see

- **City Hall square**, Plaza Gabriel Miró and **Mushroom Street**.
- The **Santa Cruz** quarter and the **Basilica of Santa María**.
- The views from **Santa Bárbara Castle**.
- **Plaza de los Luceros** and **Postiguet** beach.

## How it works

You leave Puerta del Mar in an electric tuk tuk just for your group of up to 4. The driver shares stories and local facts as you tour the old town, ride up to the castle and come down via Luceros to finish by Postiguet beach.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children and anyone who wants to skip the hills.
- Anyone **short on time** in Alicante.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **go inside** the castle or monuments.
- Anyone after a longer route with more stops.
