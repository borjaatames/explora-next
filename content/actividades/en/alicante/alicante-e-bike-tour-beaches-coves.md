## What you will see

- **Beaches and coves** along the Alicante coast and neighbouring towns.
- Local spots for **swimming and sunbathing**.
- The **Mediterranean** from coastal paths and promenades.

## How it works

You pick up your e-bike and helmet on Plaza de Quijano and set off with the guide towards the coast. For two hours you ride by the sea, stopping at beaches and coves while the guide shares local stories and suggests places to come back to.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **find beaches** beyond the city centre.
- People after something active **without much physical effort**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **can't ride a bike**.
- Families with **children under 14**.
