## What you will see

- The **Santa Cruz** quarter and the historic centre.
- The **Basilica of Santa María**, Plaza de los Luceros and the **Explanada**.
- **Postiguet** beach, the seafront and the port, with Santa Bárbara castle above.
- The **Central Market**, where the ride ends with a tasting.

## How it works

You pick up your bike on Calle Cienfuegos and ride with the guide through the centre, the old town and the seafront, stopping to hear the story of each place. The tour ends at the Central Market with a surprise tasting.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone **short on time** who wants to see a lot of Alicante in one go.
- Families with kids who already **ride confidently**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who does not speak **English or Dutch**.
