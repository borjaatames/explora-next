## What you'll see

The **Huerto del Cura** is a botanical garden inside the **Elche Palm Grove**, the largest in Europe and a World Heritage site since 2000.

Its most famous tree is the **Imperial Palm**, with seven arms growing out of the trunk. There are also ponds, a **cactus** collection and tropical plants in the shade of the date palms.

## How it works

One-day ticket. Wheelchair accessible. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
