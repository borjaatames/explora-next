## What you will see

- The waterfalls and pools of the **Fuentes del Algar**.
- **Guadalest** and its castle on the rock.
- **Altea's** old town and blue-domed church.

## How it works

You leave Alicante by coach with a short stop in Benidorm. First you swim at the Fuentes del Algar, then visit Guadalest with a guide and time for lunch, and finish strolling through Altea before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see the **inland Costa Blanca** without a car.
- Anyone after **nature and a swim** in summer.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**.
