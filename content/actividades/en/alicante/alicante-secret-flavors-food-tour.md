## What you will see

- Alicante **Central Market**, with its fruit, meat, fish and charcuterie stalls.
- Old-school shops and bars, some over **a hundred years old**.
- Local specialities: **cheeses**, handmade sweets, Mediterranean fish and traditional drinks.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the Central Market and visit around ten stops in and around the market. At each one you taste something while the guide explains where it comes from and who makes it.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone arriving in Alicante who wants to **know what to order** for the rest of the trip.
- People who would rather **eat well and learn** than tick off monuments.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Vegans**: there is no adapted option.
