## What you will see

- The **Alicante coast** and Santa Bárbara Castle from the sea.
- **Cabo de las Huertas** and its lighthouse.
- Waters for **snorkelling**.

## How it works

You leave Alicante port and sail for about 45 minutes to Cabo de las Huertas, where you anchor to swim and snorkel. Then you head back with a drink on the catamaran nets.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **families**.
- Anyone wanting an **offshore swim**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**.
