## What you will see

- **Altea** old town, with whitewashed houses, flowers and cobbled lanes.
- The Church of **Our Lady of Consolation** and its blue domes.
- **Guadalest**, a village clinging to a rock, with views over the valley.

## How it works

You travel by coach from Alicante or Benidorm to Altea for a short guided walk and free time. Then you continue to Guadalest for another guided walk and more free time before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **charming Costa Blanca towns** without a car.
- People who prefer **strolling and taking photos** to swimming.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**: there are slopes and stairs.
