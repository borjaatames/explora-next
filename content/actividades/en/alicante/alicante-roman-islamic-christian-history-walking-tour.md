## What you will see

- The **Explanada** and **Mushroom Street**.
- **St Nicholas Co-Cathedral**, **City Hall** and its ancient walls.
- The **Basilica of Santa María**, the **Powder Tower** and the chapel of San Roque.
- The **Santa Cruz** quarter and the **Central Market**.

## How it works

You meet at the Explanada bandstand shell and spend two hours exploring the centre with a certified guide who explains how Alicante passed through Roman, Muslim and Christian hands, with 10 to 20-minute stops at each point, ending at the Central Market.

## Who I recommend it for

- **History** lovers.
- Anyone who prefers **very small groups**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who needs the tour **in a language other than English**.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
