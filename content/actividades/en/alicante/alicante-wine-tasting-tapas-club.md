## What you will see

- **Five Alicante wines**, from full-bodied reds to fresh whites.
- **Five artisan tapas** designed for each wine.

## How it works

You arrive at the club on Calle Santo Tomás and take your seat. A sommelier introduces each wine, its history and how it's made, while the kitchen sends out the matching tapa, with lighting, music and aromas setting the mood.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone keen to discover **Alicante wines** without leaving the city.
- **Couples** and groups after an evening plan.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to visit **vineyards or a winery**.
