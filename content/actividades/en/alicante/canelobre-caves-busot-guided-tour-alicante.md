## What you will see

- The **Canelobre Caves**, a huge cavern over 80 metres long and up to 30 metres high.
- Illuminated **stalactites and stalagmites**.
- Mountain and sea views from the entrance.
- **Busot**, a hilltop village of narrow lanes.

## How it works

You leave central Alicante in a 7-seat hybrid vehicle and reach the caves in about half an hour, where your guide takes you through for 45 minutes to an hour. Then you head down to Busot for a guided stroll and a drink at a café before returning.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **get away from the coast** for a few hours without a car.
- People who value **very small groups** and a local guide.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: there are lots of stairs.
