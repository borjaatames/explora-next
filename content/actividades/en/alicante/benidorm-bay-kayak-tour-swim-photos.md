## What you will see

- The **Benidorm coastline** from the water, away from the beach crowds.
- Hidden **coves** and corners of the bay.
- Clear water for **swimming and snorkelling**.

## How it works

You meet on the Yacht Club's private beach, get a briefing and safety talk, and set off by kayak with the guide. For two and a half hours you paddle along the coast with swim stops, and back on shore there are hot showers and lockers.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **another side of Benidorm**.
- **Beginners** looking for a small, well-looked-after group.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with **children under 14**.
- **Non-swimmers** or anyone prone to seasickness.
