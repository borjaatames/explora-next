## What you will see

- The bay of **Alicante** from the water, at speed.
- The **port** and Alicante Marina.

## How it works

You put on your life jacket and helmet at Pier 8 in Alicante Marina, climb onto the inflatable sofa with your group (up to 6 people) and a speedboat tows you at full throttle. On turns and when crossing the wake the sofa bounces and it's easy to end up in the water.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and stag or hen parties after adrenaline and laughs.
- Families with older kids who are **comfortable in the water**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Pregnant women**.
- Anyone after a **relaxed trip** along the coast.
