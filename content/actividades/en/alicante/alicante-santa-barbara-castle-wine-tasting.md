## What you will see

- **Santa Bárbara Castle**, on top of Mount Benacantil.
- Views over the **sea**, the port and the whole city from the Contramina Bastion.
- **DO Alicante** wines, including the area's traditional sweet wine.

## How it works

You go up to the castle and head to the Contramina Bastion, at the second lift stop. There a specialist talks you through three wines, depending on the option chosen, while you pair them with Alicante tapas and cured sausage.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone visiting the castle who wants to **round off the visit** with something different.
- Couples after a **short plan** with views.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **long winery tasting**: a countryside wine tour is a better fit.
