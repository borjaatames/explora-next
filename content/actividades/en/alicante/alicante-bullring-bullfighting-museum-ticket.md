## What you will see

- Alicante's **bullring**, a late 19th-century building.
- Areas usually closed to the public: the **chapel**, the **pens** and the **infirmary**.
- The **ring** itself, which you can step into, and castle views from the stands.
- The **Bullfighting Museum**, with suits of lights and works on Alicante's matadors.

## How it works

You enter with your ticket, pick up the audio guide and explore the bullring and museum at your own pace. Videos on bullfighting history and culture play along the way.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone interested in the **history and culture** around bullfighting.
- Anyone after a **short, cheap visit** in the centre.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone opposed to **bullfighting**: the museum presents it from the inside.
