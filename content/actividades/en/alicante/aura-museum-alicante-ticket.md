## What you will see

- **Themed rooms** where light, sound and projections change the mood.
- **Interactive** spaces designed for play and photos.

## How it works

You book a time slot, arrive on time at the museum on Avenida Benito Pérez Galdós and walk through the rooms at your own pace for about 45-60 minutes. You get a bottle of water on entry.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** and groups of friends.
- Anyone after an **indoor plan** on a hot or rainy day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a traditional **history or art museum**.
- Families who need to bring a **baby stroller**.
