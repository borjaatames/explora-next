## What you will see

- Live **song**, **guitar** and **dance**.
- An Andalusian-style **tablao** in the port.

## How it works

You arrive at the port tablao, take your table and, if you chose dinner, the tapas menu is served before or during the live flamenco show.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** looking for an evening plan.
- Anyone wanting **dinner and flamenco** in one place.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting **drinks included**.
