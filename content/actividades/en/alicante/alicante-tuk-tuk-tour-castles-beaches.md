## What you will see

- **Santa Bárbara** and **San Fernando** castles.
- The **old town** and Santa María basilica.
- The **Explanada**, the port and the beaches.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel or the Explanada and the guide drives you through the old town, up to the castles and down to the port and beaches, with photo stops. You're dropped wherever you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and people who find **walking** hard.
- Anyone wanting to see Alicante **quickly**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers to **walk** and go inside monuments.
