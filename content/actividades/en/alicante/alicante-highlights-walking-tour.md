## What you will see

- The **Explanada de España**.
- The **Santa Cruz quarter** below the castle.
- **San Nicolás** co-cathedral.
- **City Hall** and the old town.

## How it works

You meet in front of City Hall and walk with the guide through the old town, the co-cathedral and the Santa Cruz quarter, finishing on the Explanada after a stop at a recommended restaurant.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone arriving **on day one**.
- Anyone wanting **local tips**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to go inside the **castle**.
