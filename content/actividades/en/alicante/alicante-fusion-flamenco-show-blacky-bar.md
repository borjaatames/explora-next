## What you will see

- **Live fusion flamenco**, with guitar, singing and dance depending on the day's artist.
- The atmosphere of an old-town **music bar**.

## How it works

You arrive at Blacky Music Bar on Villavieja Street and watch the 90-minute show from your table. If you want dinner or a drink, you order from the menu and pay separately.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants **cheap live flamenco** in a casual setting.
- People out **tapas hopping** in the old town looking for live music.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a **classic tablao** with dinner included.
- Anyone who wants a fixed price covering **food and drinks**.
