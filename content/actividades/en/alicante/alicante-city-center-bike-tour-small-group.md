## What you will see

- **Central Alicante** and corners with a story.
- Streets and squares where **locals** go about their day.
- Recommended spots to **eat, shop** and keep exploring.

## How it works

You meet on Calle Bailén, have a welcome coffee, pick up your bike and helmet and set off with the guide. Over two and a half hours you ride through the centre at an easy pace, stopping wherever there is something to see or tell.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families**, including grandparents and kids.
- Anyone who prefers **small groups** and a personal touch.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who does not speak **Dutch, French or English**.
- Anyone after a **sporty ride**.
