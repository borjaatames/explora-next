## What you will see

- The **walled village** of Nueva Tabarca.
- The island's **lighthouse** and countryside.
- The **beaches** and marine reserve.

## How it works

You board in Alicante port and reach Tabarca in an hour, watching the seabed through the glass hull. You spend the day freely and return on the 16:00 or 17:30 boat, or any other day.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and anyone after a **different beach day**.
- Anyone wanting to eat **caldero** on the island.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a **quiet, exclusive boat**: it's busy in summer.
