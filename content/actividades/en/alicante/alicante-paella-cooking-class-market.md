## What you will see

- A local Alicante **market**.
- A **kitchen** where you make paella step by step.
- Local **tapas** and wines.

## How it works

You meet the chef, buy ingredients at the market and head to the kitchen to make paella while tasting tapas and wine. At the end you eat it together.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Cooking lovers**.
- **Couples** and **groups of friends**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who just wants to **eat** without cooking.
