## What you'll see

- The **Alicante coastline**, Santa Bárbara Castle and the beaches from the sea.
- The city and mountains from about **150 metres up**.

## How it works

You board at Marina de Alicante and the speedboat takes you along the coast, with music, to the flying area. The instructor gets you ready and you take off from the boat itself, in groups of up to 5 people per parasail. You fly for about 15 minutes and land back on the boat without getting wet. Then it's back to the marina.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and **couples** looking for something different.
- **Families** with children aged 4 and over.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to **swim or spend the day** at sea.
- Pregnant women or people with back or heart problems.
