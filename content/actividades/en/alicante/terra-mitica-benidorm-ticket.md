## Mediterranean ancient history meets theme park thrills

**Terra Mítica** — "Mythical Land" — is themed around five ancient Mediterranean civilisations: **Egypt, Greece, Rome, Iberia**, and **Magna Grecia**. Each zone has its own architecture, rides, and character.

### The rides

**Roller coasters:**
- **Magnus** — a classic wooden coaster, rougher and faster than it looks
- **Titanes** — a large steel coaster with inversions
- **El Vuelo de Fénix** — a hang-glider style ride over the park

**Water rides:**
- **La Furia de Tritón** — rapids ride; you will get wet
- **El Naufragio** — flume ride with a steep drop
- **Chaos** — spinning boat ride

### The shows

Live theatrical shows are included with the ticket. The schedule changes seasonally — pick up the timetable at the entrance and plan your day around the show times.

### The themed zones

Each zone feels different from the last. The **Egypt zone** has the most elaborate architecture; the **Greece zone** has the most intense coasters; the **Iberia zone** is where the water attractions are concentrated.

## Practical notes

The park is open daily from mid-May to late September, with varying schedules in spring and autumn. Check the park calendar before booking. In July and August, **arriving at opening time** is the only way to do the popular rides before queues exceed 45 minutes.
