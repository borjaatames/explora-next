## What you will see

- The old **shipyards and factories** along the estuary and the regenerated neighbourhoods.
- The **Bizkaia Bridge**, which you cross on its gondola.
- The **villas of Getxo** and **Algorta Old Port**.

## How it works

You leave the shop in central Bilbao and ride with your guide along the estuary for about four hours, stopping to explain how the city changed. You cross the Bizkaia Bridge, ride along the Getxo coast and the guided part ends in Algorta. From there the bike is yours: beach, cafés and the ride back to Bilbao on the bike path before closing time.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone interested in Bilbao's **industrial history**.
- Anyone who wants a **day on the coast** without a car.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who would rather not **ride back alone**.
