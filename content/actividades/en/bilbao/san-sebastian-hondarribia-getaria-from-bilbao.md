## What you will see

- **La Concha** and San Sebastián's **Old Town**.
- Hondarribia's **Marina** quarter.
- **Hendaye**, already in France.
- **Getaria** harbour.

## How it works

You leave Bilbao by coach, stop in Hendaye and Hondarribia, spend about 3 hours in San Sebastián with time for lunch and finish in Getaria before heading back along the coast.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **San Sebastián** and the coast without a car.
- Anyone after a **cheap** trip covering several towns.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who'd rather spend the whole day in San Sebastián.
