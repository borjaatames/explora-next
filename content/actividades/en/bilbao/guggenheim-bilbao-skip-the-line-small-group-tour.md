## What you will see

- **Frank Gehry**'s building, explained from the outside.
- The museum's **permanent and temporary exhibitions**.
- Key works of modern and contemporary art, with their context and stories.

## How it works

You meet the guide in front of the museum, tickets already in hand, and go in without queuing. For an hour and a half you tour the galleries in a group of up to 7, or just your group if you choose the private option.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time** Guggenheim visitors who want context.
- Anyone after a **very small group** or a private visit.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers** who don't want to risk cancellation if the minimum isn't reached.
