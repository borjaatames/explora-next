## What you will see

- The **Arriaga Theatre** and the **Arenal** gardens by the river.
- **San Nicolás**, the Yohn Palace and **Plaza Nueva**.
- The **Seven Streets** and **Santiago Cathedral**.
- **La Ribera market**.

## How it works

You meet in front of the Arriaga Theatre, pick up your radio headset and walk through the Old Town with short stops at each sight. The guide tells the story of the city and finishes at La Ribera market with tips on where to eat and what to do next. At the end you pay what you like.

## Who I recommend it for

- Travellers on a **tight budget**.
- Anyone wanting a **first loop** of the Old Town.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers a **small group**.
