## What you will see

- **Haro**, the capital of Rioja wine, with a century-old winery and Plaza de la Paz.
- **Laguardia**, a walled town among vineyards, and a small family winery.
- The **Marqués de Riscal** building in Elciego on the way back.

## How it works

You leave central Bilbao by coach and reach Haro in about an hour for a winery visit with a tasting and a guided walk through the old town. Then it is on to Laguardia: a stroll through its streets, a visit to a traditional winery with two wines and an hour and a half of free time for lunch. On the way home you pass through Elciego before returning to Bilbao.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **La Rioja without driving** (and enjoy the tastings).
- Anyone after **two very different wineries** in one day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **small group**: there can be up to 50 people.
- People with **limited mobility**.
