## What you will see

- The **Guggenheim** from the estuary.
- The central bridges, such as the **Zubizuri**.
- The **Bizkaia Bridge**, on the 2-hour option.

## How it works

You board at the Pío Baroja pier, listen to the audio guide on your phone as you sail along the estuary, and return to the same pier.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with kids.
- Anyone after a **short, cheap** plan.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a live guide.
