## What you will see

- Rooms full of **light, colour and sound** where you can touch, dance and play.
- Spaces made for **fun photos and videos**.
- A visit **for all ages**.

## How it works

You arrive in your time slot, check in, store your belongings and explore the rooms on your own for about an hour. Museum staff are on hand to point you in the right direction and answer questions.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**.
- Anyone looking for an **indoor plan** on a rainy day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after **art or history**: it is a sensory experience, not a classic museum.
- Anyone short on time who wants to stay in the centre.
