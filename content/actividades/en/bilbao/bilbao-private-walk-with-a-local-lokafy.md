## What you will see

- Whatever **you choose**: neighbourhoods, street art, history or food.
- **Hidden corners** that locals frequent.
- Tips on **where to eat, wander or shop** for the rest of your trip.

## How it works

The guide, a Bilbao resident, picks you up in Plaza Moyúa at the time you agree. There is no fixed route: for two hours you walk wherever interests you most, just your group, chatting as you would with a friend who lives in the city.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Repeat visitors** or anyone wanting to go beyond the obvious.
- **Families or couples** who prefer a guide to themselves.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a structured **monuments tour** with tickets included.
