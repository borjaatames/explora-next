## What you will see

- **San Juan de Gaztelugatxe**, Game of Thrones' Dragonstone.
- The harbours of **Bermeo** and **Mundaka**.
- **Guernica**, with the Tree and the Assembly House.
- A **txakoli winery**.

## How it works

You leave by coach from the Guggenheim's Puppy, walk down to and up the Gaztelugatxe hermitage with the guide, stroll round Bermeo, have lunch in Mundaka, visit Guernica and finish with the txakoli tasting before heading back to Bilbao.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **Gaztelugatxe** without worrying about the booking.
- **Game of Thrones fans**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: there are lots of steps.
