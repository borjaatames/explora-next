## What you will see

- The bars of the **Old Town**, from Plaza Nueva to Calle Ribera.
- Bar classics: **anchovies, croquetas, salt cod and tortilla**.
- A sweet finish with **pastel vasco** or a **carolina**.

## How it works

You meet your guide by San Nicolás church and hop from bar to bar through the Old Town for three and a half hours. At each stop you try pintxos with a local drink while the guide explains the history and customs behind them. It ends with dessert on Calle Jardines.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **eat well without planning** where.
- New arrivals who want to learn **how pintxos work**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **limited mobility**.
- Anyone after **gourmet cooking** or award-winning pintxos.
