## What you will see

- **Frank Gehry's architecture**, outside and in.
- **Puppy** and **Maman**.
- The **permanent collection** and **temporary exhibitions**.

## How it works

You meet the guide by Puppy, enter without queuing with the group and spend 2 hours on the outside and the galleries. Then you can stay on your own.

## Who I recommend it for

- Spanish speakers wanting to **understand contemporary art**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't speak Spanish.
- Families with children under 7.
