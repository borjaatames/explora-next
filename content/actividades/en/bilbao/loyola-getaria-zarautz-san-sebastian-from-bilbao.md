## What you will see

- The **Sanctuary of Loyola**, with the basilica and the birthplace of St Ignatius.
- **Getaria**, the fishing town of Juan Sebastián Elcano and Cristóbal Balenciaga.
- **Zarautz** beach, from the coach.
- San Sebastián's old town and La Concha from **Mount Igueldo**.

## How it works

You leave Bilbao by coach and reach Loyola in about an hour for the guided visit. Next comes a short walk in Getaria, a drive past Zarautz and then San Sebastián, with a tour of the old town and two hours of free time for lunch. In the afternoon you go up Igueldo to see the bay and head back to Bilbao.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see **several Gipuzkoa towns** in a day without a car.
- Anyone interested in **St Ignatius** or the Basque coast.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to spend **the whole day in San Sebastián**.
