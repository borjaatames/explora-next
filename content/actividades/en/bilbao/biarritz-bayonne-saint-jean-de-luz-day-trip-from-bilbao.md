## What you will see

- **Biarritz**: the Grande Plage, the Casino and the Sainte-Eugénie church.
- **Bayonne**: the Town Hall, St Andrew's church and its **Gothic cathedral**.
- **Saint-Jean-de-Luz**: the fishing harbour and the Basque houses of the centre.

## How it works

You leave by coach from Bilbao or San Sebastián. In Biarritz there is a guided visit and some free time; in Bayonne, the longest stop, there is a guided visit and time for lunch. The day ends with a guided walk around Saint-Jean-de-Luz before returning to the departure point.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **French Basque Country** in a day without driving.
- Travellers as interested in **old towns** as in the coast.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without a valid **ID card or passport**.
- People who find long **coach** journeys tiring.
