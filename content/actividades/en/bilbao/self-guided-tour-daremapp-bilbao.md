## What you'll see and discover

Explore Bilbao on your own terms with the **DareMapp** app: a self-guided audio tour covering +15 points of interest, including the Guggenheim Museum, the Arriaga Theatre, the Palacio de Arana and Santiago Cathedral. No group schedule and no waiting around: you choose which stops to make, how long to spend at each one, and when to pause or pick the route back up, even on a different day.

## How it works

There's no physical meeting point. After purchase, your ticket includes a **reference code** that you activate from your phone, whenever and wherever you choose in Bilbao. The app runs in 6 languages (Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian and German) and is available **24 hours a day, 365 days a year** — the access never expires. Inside the app you'll also find food and family-activity recommendations to round out your visit.

## Who I'd recommend it to

- Independent travellers, couples or families who'd rather not depend on set times or a group.
- Anyone who likes exploring at a relaxed pace and deciding what to see as they go.
- Anyone after a flexible, affordable option — though it won't give you the interaction or stories of a live guide.
