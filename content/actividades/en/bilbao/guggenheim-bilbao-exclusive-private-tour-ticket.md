## What you will see

- The **ArcelorMittal gallery**, with **Richard Serra**'s eight sculptures.
- Frank Gehry's **atrium**, with its curved volumes and huge skylight.
- Works from the **permanent and temporary exhibitions**, by artists such as Louise Bourgeois, Chillida, Yves Klein and Jeff Koons.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the information point inside the museum. For an hour and a half, just your group tours the main galleries, with explanations about the works and the building itself. Entry is included, so there is no ticket to buy separately.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families or groups** who want a guide to themselves.
- Anyone who needs the tour in **French, German or Italian**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Solo travellers or couples** on a tight budget: the price is per group.
