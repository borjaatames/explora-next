## What you will see

- The two wines of the Basque Country: **txakoli** and the reds and whites of **Rioja Alavesa**.
- A tasting room inside a venue with a **wine bar and shop**.

## How it works

You arrive at Gastronomía Bilbao, opposite the Guggenheim, and a sommelier takes you through five wines over just over an hour, explaining the traditions of each area. Each wine comes with local cheeses and charcuterie.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **understand Basque wine** quickly.
- Anyone visiting the **Guggenheim** and looking for something nearby.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone hoping to visit **vineyards or a winery**.
