## What you will see

- The **Seven Streets** and **Santiago Cathedral**.
- **La Ribera market**.
- The **Ensanche**, the **Zubizuri** and the **Guggenheim**.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the Teatro Arriaga, walk through the Old Town and the Ensanche and finish on the modern riverside by the Guggenheim.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone **short on time** in Bilbao.
- Anyone who prefers **small groups**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to go inside the museums.
