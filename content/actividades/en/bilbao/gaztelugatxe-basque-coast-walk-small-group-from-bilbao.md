## What you will see

- **San Juan de Gaztelugatxe**, the islet linked to the mainland by a stone bridge.
- A **hidden viewpoint** among the vegetation, weather permitting.
- The **Bizkaia coast**, with explanations about its plants and wildlife.

## How it works

You leave by van from Plaza Ernesto Erkoreka in Bilbao, about a 40-minute drive. On arrival the guide first takes you to a viewpoint, then you walk for an hour and a half up to the top of the islet. Afterwards you return to the same spot in Bilbao.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see Gaztelugatxe **unhurried** and in a **small group**.
- People who enjoy **walking** and learning about nature.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with **children under 12**.
- Anyone hoping to see **several villages** on the same trip.
