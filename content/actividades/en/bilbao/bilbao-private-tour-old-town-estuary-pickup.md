## What you'll see

Bilbao's **Old Town**, with **Plaza Nueva** and **Santiago Cathedral**, and the **estuary**, symbol of the city's transformation, to the **Zubizuri** bridge and the **Guggenheim**.

## How it works

The guide picks you up at your hotel and adapts the route to your interests. Price **per group**: €540 for 2, €640 for 4 and €780 for 6.

## Who it's for

- First-time visitors to Bilbao.
- Those who want Basque history beyond the museum.
