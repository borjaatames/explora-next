## What you will see

- **San Juan de Gaztelugatxe**, the hermitage on an islet facing the cliffs.
- Bilbao's **Old Town** and estuary.
- The **Guggenheim Museum**, inside if your option includes it.

## How it works

You are picked up in San Sebastián and drive along the coast to Gaztelugatxe, with time to walk down to the islet and, if you like, climb to the hermitage. Then it is on to Bilbao for a stroll through the Old Town and along the river, with a pintxo lunch stop. The afternoon ends at the Guggenheim with a ticket and audio guide, or free time in the city if your option does not include it, before returning to San Sebastián.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **San Sebastián** with a day for Bizkaia.
- Anyone wanting to combine **nature and art** without driving.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone already staying in **Bilbao**.
- People with **limited mobility**.
