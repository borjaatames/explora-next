## What the day is like

The guide picks you up at your Bilbao hotel and you drive in a private car to **San Juan de Gaztelugatxe**: the stone path and the roughly 240 steps up to the hermitage above the sea (the islet appeared in *Game of Thrones* as Dragonstone). Access is already booked. Then **Mundaka**, a fishing village famous for its wave, and **Gernika**, with the **Tree** and the **Assembly House**, symbols of the Basque Country, and the *Guernica* mural. In the afternoon, back to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families and groups who prefer a private car and their own pace.
- Anyone who doesn't want to worry about the Gaztelugatxe booking in high season.
- If you're travelling solo or as a couple, the group trip covers a similar route for much less.
