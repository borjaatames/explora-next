## What you will see

- The **Bizkaia Bridge**, the UNESCO-listed transporter bridge.
- **Algorta Old Port** and Getxo's seafaring neighbourhoods.
- The **cliffs** over the Bay of Biscay.

## How it works

You take the metro to Algorta and pick up your bike and helmet there. With your guide you ride along the Getxo coast at an easy pace, stopping often to talk about what you see, and take a break at a bar for a pintxo and a drink.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **coast near Bilbao** without a car.
- Anyone who prefers a **very small group**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone not comfortable **on a bike**.
