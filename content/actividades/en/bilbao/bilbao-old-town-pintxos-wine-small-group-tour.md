## What you will see

- The streets of Bilbao's **Old Town** (Casco Viejo).
- **Classic bars** and others only locals know.
- **Traditional and modern pintxos**, with txakoli and Rioja wines.

## How it works

You meet at Tourné Bilbao and spend three hours hopping between bars with a local guide and a group of up to six. In total you taste six pintxos and four drinks, and the guide gives you tips for ordering on your own for the rest of your trip.

## Who I recommend it for

- New arrivals who want to **learn how to order pintxos**.
- Anyone who prefers **very small groups**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **Spanish-language** tour: it runs in English only.
