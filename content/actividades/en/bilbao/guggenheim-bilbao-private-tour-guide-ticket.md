## What the tour is like

You meet the guide at the museum entrance. For **1.5 hours** you explore **Frank Gehry's** building, works from the collection (such as Richard Serra's *The Matter of Time*) and the current temporary exhibitions, with explanations tailored to your interests. The **ticket is included** and afterwards you can stay in the museum as long as you like.

There's a second **4-hour** option that adds a walk around Bilbao with the same guide.

## Who I recommend it to

- Families and groups: per person it costs about the same as a group tour.
- Anyone who isn't into contemporary art but wants to understand what they're seeing.
- If you're travelling solo or as a couple on a budget, see the group guided tour with ticket.
