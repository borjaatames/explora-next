## What you will see

- **La Ribera market** and **Plaza Nueva** in the Old Town.
- The **Guggenheim** from two different angles.
- The **Maritime Museum**, **Doña Casilda** park and the **Zubizuri** bridge.
- The **Arenal** promenade on the way back.

## How it works

You meet at La Bicicleta bike shop next to the Old Town, where they fit your classic bike (or e-bike, if you pay the extra). For two hours you ride around the city in a group of up to eight, with short stops at each spot, and return to the shop.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants Bilbao's highlights in **little time** and at a good price.
- People who prefer **small groups**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with **children under 14**.
- Anyone who doesn't speak **English**.
