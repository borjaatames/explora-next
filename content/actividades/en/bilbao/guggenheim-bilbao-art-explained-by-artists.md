## What you will see

- **Frank Gehry's building** from outside, with its titanium curves.
- The outdoor sculptures: Louise Bourgeois's **Maman** and Jeff Koons's **Puppy**.
- **The Matter of Time**, **Richard Serra**'s huge steel pieces.
- The permanent collection, with works by **Rothko, De Kooning and Warhol**.

## How it works

You meet in front of Puppy. The tour starts outside the building and continues inside for an hour and a half, led by a guide who is an artist. Afterwards you can stay in the museum for the rest of the day on the same ticket.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **understand contemporary art** without an academic lecture.
- People who prefer **small groups**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who just wants to see the museum **on their own**: a ticket is enough.
