## What you will see

- The Bilbao **must-sees** you choose, from outside.
- **Corners and venues** your guide knows that are not in the guidebooks.
- Tips for **eating and getting around** for the rest of your stay.

## How it works

Before the tour your guide gets in touch to find out what interests you. On the day they pick you up at your accommodation and you walk the city for two hours, just your group, following the route you agreed. If you want to go inside a museum, say so in advance.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** and groups who want to set their own pace.
- Anyone with **specific interests** (architecture, history, food).

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a tour with **tickets included**.
