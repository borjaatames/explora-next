## What you will see

- **Plaza Moyúa** and the **Three Lions fountain**.
- The **Old Town** (Casco Viejo), with its historic streets and squares.
- **La Ribera market**, by the estuary.
- **Lesser-known corners** suggested by the guide.

## How it works

You meet the guide in Plaza Jado and spend two hours exploring the centre and Old Town with just your group. The itinerary and pace adapt to your interests, whether history, architecture, food or everyday life.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples, families or small groups** who want their own guide without spending much.
- **First-time** visitors to Bilbao.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a tour with **museum tickets** included.
