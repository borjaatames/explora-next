## What you will see

- **Five Bilbao bars**, from some of the oldest to very modern ones.
- **Hot pintxos** cooked to order that are not on display.
- **Award-winning** pintxos alongside traditional ones.

## How it works

You meet the guide outside the KFC on Calle Viuda de Epalza and spend three hours visiting five bars in a group of up to 12. At each stop the guide orders the pintxos and a paired drink, and between bars shares stories about Bilbao and Basque culture.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants a **pintxos dinner** with someone who knows what to order.
- Travellers doing it **early in their trip** so they can return to their favourite bars.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Vegetarians and vegans**.
- Anyone who doesn't speak **English**.
