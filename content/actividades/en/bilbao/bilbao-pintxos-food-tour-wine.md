## What you will see

- **Pintxos bars** in several Bilbao neighbourhoods.
- Classic and creative pintxos.
- **Local wine** to go with them.

## How it works

You meet the guide on Gran Vía and walk from bar to bar. At each one you try pintxos and a drink while the guide tells you about Bilbao.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone arriving in Bilbao who wants to **get pintxos right**.
- **Solo travellers**: it's very social.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who'd rather go pintxos-hopping alone at their own pace.
