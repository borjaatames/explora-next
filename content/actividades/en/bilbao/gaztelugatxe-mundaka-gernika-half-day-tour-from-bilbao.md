## What you will see

- **San Juan de Gaztelugatxe**, the islet hermitage made famous as Dragonstone.
- **Mundaka**, a seafaring village inside the Urdaibai biosphere reserve.
- **Gernika**, with its tree and the memory of the bombing that inspired Picasso.

## How it works

You meet the guide by the Guggenheim's Puppy (or get picked up at your hotel on some options). The first stop is Gaztelugatxe, around two hours to cross the bridge and climb to the hermitage. Then come an hour and a half in Mundaka and another hour and a half in Gernika before heading back to Bilbao.

## Who I recommend it for

- Travellers **short on time** who want the essentials of the Bizkaia coast.
- Anyone who prefers a **small or private group** with comfortable transport.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**: there are many stairs and uneven ground.
- Anyone who also wants **Bermeo** or a wine tasting: full-day trips cover that.
