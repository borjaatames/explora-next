## What you will see

- **San Nicolás** church and **Plaza Nueva**.
- **La Ribera market**, with a stop inside.
- Plaza de Santiago, the **La Bolsa** building and the **Dog Fountain**.
- **Bidebarrieta library**.

## How it works

You meet your guide at Plaza Jado and spend two hours walking through the Old Town, with short stops at each sight and a little longer at the market and the library. Along the way the guide covers the city's history and shares tips on where to eat.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** who want their bearings.
- Travellers who prefer a **small group** in English or German.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **modern Bilbao** along the river.
