## What you will see

- The spot where **Bilbao began** and its oldest streets.
- The **Nervión estuary**, Calatrava's **Zubizuri** bridge and the **Guggenheim**.
- **San Mamés** stadium and the area around the **Maritime Museum**.
- The parks and green spaces that transformed the city.

## How it works

You pick up your e-bike at the meeting point and set off with the guide. The route starts where the city was founded and moves forward in time along the estuary to modern Bilbao, on bike lanes and pedestrian streets. In three hours you get a full overview of the city plus tips for the rest of your stay.

## Who I recommend it for

- **First-time visitors** who want an overview of Bilbao.
- Anyone who would rather **ride** than walk for hours.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't speak **English or Dutch**.
- Children **under 140 cm** who want to ride their own bike.
