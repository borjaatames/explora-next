## What you will see

- Bilbao's **best-known sights**, from the Old Town to the estuary.
- **Lesser-known corners** the guide points out along the way.
- Short stops at each spot for **photos and commentary**.

## How it works

You meet the guide in front of the Arriaga Theatre, where you get your e-bike and helmet. For two and a half hours you ride around the city in a group of up to ten, stopping at each spot to hear the story and take photos, before returning to the start.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families**, since there are non-electric bikes and child seats.
- Anyone **short on time** who wants to see a lot without tiring.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility** or **visual impairments**.
