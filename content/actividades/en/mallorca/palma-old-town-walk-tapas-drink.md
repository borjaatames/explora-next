## What you will see

- The **streets and squares** of Palma's old town.
- **Gothic, Renaissance, Baroque and Modernisme** buildings.
- A central **tapas bar** to finish.

## How it works

You walk for about 75 minutes with the guide, who covers Palma's history from the Romans through the Moors and the Aragonese conquest to today. Then you sit down at a bar to share tapas and a drink while the guide talks about life in Mallorca.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants **history and tapas** in one afternoon.
- Couples and solo travellers who speak **English or German**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a food tour with many stops.
- Anyone who wants a tour in Spanish.
