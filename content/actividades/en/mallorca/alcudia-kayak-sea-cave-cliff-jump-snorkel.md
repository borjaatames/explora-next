## What you will see

- **Mallorca's north coast** near Alcúdia, from a kayak.
- A **sea cave** with crystal-clear water.
- Rocks for **cliff jumping** and a seabed for **snorkelling**.

## How it works

You're picked up in the north (or meet near Alcúdia), get your gear and paddle to the cave, where the hunt for the treasure key begins. A short coastal walk leads to the jumping spot, then a snorkelling session to find the treasure and a picnic on the beach. Photos are emailed to you for free.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with kids** aged 5 and up.
- Anyone who wants **several activities in one morning** with transport sorted.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People who aren't comfortable in the water or have **claustrophobia**.
- Anyone looking for a quiet small-group outing.
