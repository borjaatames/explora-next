## What you will see

- An **old flour windmill** in Palma turned into a kitchen.
- How a **paella** is made from scratch.
- Home-style classics: **tortilla**, **sobrasada** and **crema catalana**.

## How it works

You arrive at the windmill, put on an apron and cook a five-course menu as a group with your host. Afterwards everyone sits at a big table to eat what you made, with wine, beer and soft drinks, and you take the recipes home.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **friends** who enjoy cooking.
- Anyone wanting a **full meal** with a difference in Palma.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Vegans**, as there is no adapted menu.
- Anyone looking for a **budget** activity.
