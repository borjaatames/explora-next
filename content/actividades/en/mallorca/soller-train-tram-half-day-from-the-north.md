## What you will see

- The **wooden train** from Palma to Sóller, about 60 minutes through the mountains.
- The Sóller **tram** to Port de Sóller.
- **Sóller's main square** and the harbour bay.

## How it works

You are picked up in northern Mallorca, the coach takes you to Palma, you ride the train to Sóller, the tram to the port and return by coach. A guide stays with you throughout.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families staying in the north**, as we recommend.
- Anyone who wants the Sóller train in **half a day**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Visitors staying in Palma or the south: there are more direct options.
- Anyone who also wants Sa Calobra: see the island tour.
