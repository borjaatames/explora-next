## What you will see

- Slides for **all ages**, from gentle to extreme.
- The **kids' area** with shallow pools.
- **The Beast**, over 30 metres tall.

## How it works

You buy a ticket for one day, activate it at the park portal and use all rides without limit.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** looking for a day of pools and slides.
- Anyone staying in Palma or the southwest.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the north: Hidropark is closer, although rated lower.
- Anyone who does not want to pay extra for loungers and lockers.
