## What you will see

- The dolphin, sea lion and parrot **shows**.
- The park's **aquarium**.
- The **children's pool** for the little ones.

## How it works

You buy a ticket for one day, show it at the box office and explore the park at your own pace, following the show schedule.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with young children**.
- Anyone staying in **Palma** or the southwest.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers not to watch **animal shows**: Palma Aquarium is better.
- Anyone staying in the north without a car.
