## What you will see

- A Mallorcan **manor house** with over five centuries of history and traces of many styles.
- **Gardens** with Arab roots and English landscaping influence.
- A grand **plane-tree avenue**, fountains, hydrangeas and the traditional courtyard.

## How it works

You make your own way to Alfabia, show your ticket and explore the gardens and house at your own pace. You'll be given an ID wristband for the visit. Last entry is one hour before closing.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone visiting **Sóller** or the Tramuntana who wants a quiet stop.
- Lovers of **gardens** and historic houses.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **guided** visit, as it's self-guided entry.
- Anyone short on time who'd rather prioritise **villages and coast**.
