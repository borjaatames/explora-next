## The tour

Five hours to see **Formentor** without driving. A panoramic minibus with a local guide picks you up in north Mallorca and climbs the cape road to the **Es Colomer viewpoint**, with cliffs dropping into the sea.

## Beach and boat

Then there is free time at **Formentor beach**, turquoise water among pines. You return by **boat across Pollença bay** to the port.

## Why a tour

From 15 May to 18 October private car access to Formentor is restricted and the beach car park fills up. This tour solves that.
