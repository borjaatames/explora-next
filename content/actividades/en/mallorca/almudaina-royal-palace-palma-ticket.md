## What you will see

- The palace **rooms**, with tapestries, furniture and pieces from the 14th century on.
- The **Royal Staircase** and royal apartments.
- **Saint Anne's Chapel**, with its Romanesque pink marble portal.
- The **gardens** and views over the Bay of Palma.

## How it works

You buy a one-day ticket and explore the palace at your own pace, without a guide or audio guide. It's next to the Cathedral, so both visits are easy to do back to back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone visiting the **Cathedral** who wants to complete the set.
- Anyone interested in Palma's **history** and the monarchy.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who needs the flexibility to cancel.
- Anyone who prefers a guided visit.
