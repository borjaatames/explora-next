## What you will see

- **Woods and farmland** in inland Mallorca near Palma.
- **Views** on the climb towards Randa mountain.
- A **cove** for a swim in summer.

## How it works

After a briefing on handling the quad, you ride in a group behind the guide along winding roads and quiet tracks. In summer there's a stop at a cove to swim, snorkel and, if you dare, jump from 6 to 10-metre rocks.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with a **car licence** who wants a bit of adrenaline.
- **Couples and friends** who want a few hours away from the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without their **original licence** or with a provisional one.
- Families with young children.
