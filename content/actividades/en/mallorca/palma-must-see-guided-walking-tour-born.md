## What you will see

- **Plaça de Cort** and the church of **Santa Eulàlia**.
- The **Arab Baths** and Guillem Sagrera's **Llotja**.
- The **Basilica of Sant Francesc** and the **Santa Clara** convent.
- The **Almudaina Palace** and **Parc de la Mar**, beside the cathedral.

## How it works

You meet the guide on Passeig del Born and explore the old town on foot for about two hours, stopping to explain the history of each place. The route may vary with the guide and ends back at the start.

## Who I recommend it for

- First-time visitors to **Palma**.
- Anyone wanting to **get their bearings** before exploring alone.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to go **inside** the cathedral or the Almudaina.
- Anyone who already knows **central Palma** well.
