## What you will see

- The **inside of Palma Cathedral**, without queuing.
- Streets and corners of the **historic centre**, from Plaza Mayor to the waterfront.
- A **traditional café** to try the ensaïmada.

## How it works

You meet outside CaixaForum and walk the centre with a local guide in a small group. Along the way you stop to taste an ensaïmada, then enter the Cathedral without queuing. At the end, the guide shares tips for eating and getting around during the rest of your stay.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **Cathedral inside** with explanations.
- First-time visitors to Palma who want their bearings.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to go up to the Cathedral **terraces**.
- Anyone looking for a full food tour.
