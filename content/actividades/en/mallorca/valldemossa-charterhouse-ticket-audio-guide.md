## What you'll see

The **Royal Charterhouse of Valldemossa** was a Carthusian monastery from 1399 until the 1835 confiscations. Shortly after, **Frédéric Chopin** and **George Sand** spent the winter of 1838-1839 here, which she described in *A Winter in Majorca*.

The route covers the **church**, the **cloister**, the monks' **pharmacy**, **Chopin's cells** (with his piano) and **King Sancho's palace**. Several times a day there is a short **piano recital**.

## How it works

Ticket with an audio guide on your phone. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
