## What you'll see

**Es Fangar** is an **organic** wine estate in Felanitx. Your host shows you the winery and explains the estate's history and how the wine is made, from vineyard to ageing.

## How it works

The visit ends with a **seated tasting of five premium wines** with artisan cold cuts and cheeses. You get there by car; the price is **per person** (€62.50).

## Who it's for

- Wine lovers who want to understand it.
- Anyone looking for a calm plan inland in Mallorca.
