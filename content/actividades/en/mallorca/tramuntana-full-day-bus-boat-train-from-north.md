## What you will see

- The **Serra de Tramuntana** mountain road down to **Sa Calobra**.
- The mouth of the **Torrent de Pareis**, through tunnels cut in the rock.
- The cliff coast between Sa Calobra and **Port de Sóller**, from the boat.
- The Sóller **tram** and the **wooden train** through orange and olive groves.

## How it works

You leave Alcúdia by coach for the Tramuntana and drive down to Sa Calobra for free time. From there you take a boat to Port de Sóller, ride the tram up to Sóller and finish aboard the historic train. The guide handles all the connections.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the **north** who doesn't want to drive the mountain roads.
- Anyone wanting the **Tramuntana essentials** in a day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets sick on **winding roads** or boats.
- Anyone who prefers **small groups** or their own pace.
