## What you will see

- The **Calvià** coast and **Malgrats** island.
- **Palma**, passing the Nautical Club and the **cathedral**.
- The coastal viewpoints of **Banyalbufar** and **Estellencs**.
- **Port d'Andratx** and views over Camp de Mar on the way back.

## How it works

After a briefing from the guide, you leave Paguera in convoy, each at the wheel of your own car. You stop at Malgrats, cross Palma along the seafront, head up the north coast with a coffee stop in Estellencs and return over the mountains via Port d'Andratx to Paguera.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who loves **driving** mountain roads.
- **Couples** or friends who want to take turns at the wheel.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who can't drive a **manual car** or has held a licence for under 2 years.
- Anyone who gets sick on **winding roads**.
