## What you will see

- A Mallorcan **weekly market**, in Sineu or Alcúdia.
- **Pollença Bay** from the boat.
- **Formentor Beach**, with time for a swim.
- The Formentor cliffs from the **Es Colomer viewpoint**.

## How it works

You're picked up in the morning and taken by coach to the market for just over an hour of free time. Then you continue to Port de Pollença, cross by boat to Formentor Beach and spend about two hours there. Before heading back there's a half-hour stop at the Es Colomer viewpoint.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the **north** without a car.
- Anyone wanting **beach, market and scenery** in one day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers **small groups**.
- Anyone who only wants the beach and could skip the **market**.
