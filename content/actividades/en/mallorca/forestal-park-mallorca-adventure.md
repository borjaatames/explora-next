## What you will see

- **Courses** in the trees with bridges, nets and swings.
- The longest **zip line** in Mallorca.
- **Children's** routes and adult routes.

## How it works

You book a course to suit your children's age, get a harness and safety briefing, and do the routes with instructors nearby.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with active children** aged around 6 and up.
- Anyone looking for something other than beaches and water parks.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone afraid of **heights**.
- Families without a car: it is outside Palma.
