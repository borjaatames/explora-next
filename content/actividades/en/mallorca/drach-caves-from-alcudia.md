## What you will see

- The **stalactite chambers** of the Drach Caves.
- **Lake Martel** and the **concert** from lit boats.
- The option to **cross the lake** by boat.

## How it works

You leave Port d'Alcúdia by coach, enter the caves without queuing, watch the concert and return north at midday or in the afternoon depending on the schedule.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families staying in the north**.
- Anyone who does not want to drive to Porto Cristo.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with a **pushchair**: there are about 300 steps.
- Anyone who dislikes shop stops.
