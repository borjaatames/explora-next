## What you will see

- The **seabed** through the glass hull.
- Wild bottlenose **dolphins** and pilot whales.
- Mallorca's **southwest coast** from the sea.

## How it works

You arrive at the Tora beach jetty 20 minutes early, board and sail for about 2 hours looking for dolphins. A light breakfast is available at the boat's bar.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children of any age.
- Anyone who wants to see **wild dolphins**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the north: Paguera is far.
- Anyone who gets seasick easily.
