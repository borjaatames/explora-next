## What you will see

- The ranch **farm**, with animals you can feed.
- The **woods** on the mountain slope.
- **Alcúdia Bay** from above on the 2-hour ride.

## How it works

You start at the farm, get a helmet and are matched with a horse for your level. The ride winds through the trees on the hillside, at a gentle pace for beginners and with trotting or cantering for experienced riders. If you chose brunch, lunch or dinner, you eat at the ranch afterwards.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Beginners** and **families** staying in Alcúdia or Playa de Muro.
- Anyone who wants a countryside outing with some Mallorcan food.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Expert riders looking for a long, demanding route.
- Anyone over 110 kg or 70 years old.
