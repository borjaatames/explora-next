## What you will see

- The mountain and countryside scenery of **Calvià**.
- Views from the **Malgrats** and **El Toro** viewpoints.
- The **Portals Vells cave** and its clear-water cove.

## How it works

You leave Torrenova on an automatic quad behind the guide. You stop at the Malgrats and El Toro viewpoints for photos and finish at Portals Vells, with about 35 minutes to swim, snorkel and see the cave before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Magaluf, Palmanova or Santa Ponça**.
- **Groups of friends** and families with kids aged 6+ as passengers.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without a physical **category B licence**.
- Anyone after a hardcore **off-road** route.
