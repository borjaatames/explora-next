## What you'll see

A boat trip from **Cala Figuera** harbour north along Mallorca's south-east coast, past cliffs and turquoise coves, to **Caló des Moro**.

Along the way there are **swimming and snorkeling stops**, with gear included, and **one drink** is served on board.

## How it works

Two and a half hours at sea with a Spanish- and English-speaking crew. In rough sea the trip may be cancelled or rerouted. You need to be able to swim to go in the water.
