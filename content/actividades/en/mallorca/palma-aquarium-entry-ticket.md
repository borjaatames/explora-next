## What you will see

- The **Big Blue**, one of Europe's deepest shark tanks.
- One of the world's largest collections of **living corals**.
- The **tropical jungle**, with a waterfall and plants.
- **Feeding sessions** for sharks, rays and turtles.

## How it works

You buy a ticket for one day and show it on your phone at the entrance. The route is self-guided and takes about 3 hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children of any age.
- Anyone looking for a **cloudy-day** plan.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Visitors with only one day in Mallorca: the island has more unique experiences.
