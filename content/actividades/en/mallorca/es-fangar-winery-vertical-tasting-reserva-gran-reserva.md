## What you'll see

A **vertical tasting** serves several **vintages of the same wine**: same vineyard, same hands, different years. Choose five vintages of **Reserva Fangar · Elements** or four of **Gran Reserva N’Amarat**.

## How it works

Your host serves them in order and explains each year, with cold cuts and cheeses. Price **per person**: €87.50.

## Who it's for

- Wine enthusiasts with some experience.
- Anyone wanting a different kind of tasting.
