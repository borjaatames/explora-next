## What you will see

- A **flamenco group** with live dance, singing and guitar.
- Palma's **first tablao**, in an intimate setting.

## How it works

You head to the tablao on Avinguda de Portugal and watch a show of around an hour, with artists who improvise every night. Depending on the option, you get a drink or a tapas selection with a drink while you watch.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **live flamenco** on holiday in Mallorca.
- **Couples** looking for an evening out in central Palma.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a full dinner, as the food option is tapas.
