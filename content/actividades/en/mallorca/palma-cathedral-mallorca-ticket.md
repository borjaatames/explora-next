## What you'll see

**Palma Cathedral** (La Seu) is one of Europe's finest Gothic cathedrals, built over four centuries from 1229 to 1601, sitting above the Parc de la Mar with its reflection pool.

### The exterior
The cathedral's south facade faces the sea — the image of Mallorca. It is best photographed in the late afternoon with the sun behind you.

### Gaudí's interior
Between 1904 and 1914, Antoni Gaudí was commissioned to redesign the interior. His additions — a great **canopy of wrought iron and lamps** over the main altar, repositioned choir stalls, and ceramic work — create a uniquely Modernista atmosphere inside a medieval Gothic shell.

### The rose window
The great **rose window** over the main portal is one of the largest in Gothic architecture. When sunlight shines through it in early afternoon, the effect is extraordinary.

### The museum
The ticket includes the cathedral museum with medieval religious artefacts, gold and silverwork, and vestments.

## Who it's for

- Anyone visiting Palma — La Seu is non-negotiable.
- Architecture lovers interested in Gothic and Modernisme.

## Who it's not for

- Those uncomfortable with strict dress codes (covered shoulders and knees are required).
