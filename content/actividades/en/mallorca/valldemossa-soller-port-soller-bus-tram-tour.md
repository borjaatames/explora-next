## What you will see

- **Sóller**, its square and Modernisme buildings among orange groves.
- **Port de Sóller** after riding down on the historic tram.
- The coastal road via **Deià**, Llucalcari and Sa Foradada.
- **Valldemossa** and, if you like, the Charterhouse.

## How it works

The coach takes you to Sóller, where you look around before catching the tram to the port. There you have free time to stroll or eat. The coach then follows the Tramuntana coast to Valldemossa, with about 90 minutes free before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **Tramuntana without a car**.
- Anyone looking for a **budget** day trip with the tram included.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People who dislike large groups.
- Anyone who wants a Spanish-speaking guide.
