## What you will see

- The estate's **mill** and each step of oil production.
- The **olive grove**, with explanations of the work in the field.
- How to tell an **extra virgin** from other oils, with a tasting.

## How it works

You're welcomed at the estate with a short intro to the project, visit the mill and walk through the olive grove. Then comes the tasting class and, to finish, a snack with the house oil, local produce and a glass of wine. Two hours in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Food lovers** and fans of good olive oil.
- Anyone wanting to see **rural inland** Mallorca.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without a **car**, as the estate is in the countryside.
- Anyone after a **beach or adventure** activity.
