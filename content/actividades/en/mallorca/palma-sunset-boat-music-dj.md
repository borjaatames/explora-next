## What you will see

- **Palma harbour** with the Serra de Tramuntana behind.
- The **sunset** over the bay.
- Palma's coastline from the sea.

## How it works

You board on Palma's seafront and the boat cruises the bay to a DJ set. There's homemade pizza and water for everyone and a bar for other drinks. In summer it stops for a swim before heading back to port after dark.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and people who like meeting other travellers.
- Anyone after an **evening with music** at a good price.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Families with children**, as it's adults only.
- Anyone wanting a quiet sunset on a small boat.
