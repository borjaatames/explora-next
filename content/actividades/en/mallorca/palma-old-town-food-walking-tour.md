## What you'll see

A three-hour food walk through **Palma** old town, in a group of 15 at most:

- One of Mallorca's oldest **convents**, where nuns have made sweets for centuries.
- **Panades**, rustic bread with **sobrasada** and three **seasonal tapas** with a glass of Mallorcan wine.
- **Mercat de l'Olivar**, Palma's main market.
- Finish with **ensaimada**, **almond ice cream** and a traditional liqueur at a long-standing shop.

## How it works

Starts at the Parc de la Mar tourist office and ends at Plaça de Cort. In English or German. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
