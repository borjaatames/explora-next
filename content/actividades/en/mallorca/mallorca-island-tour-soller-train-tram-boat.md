## What you will see

- The **1912 wooden train** across the Serra de Tramuntana.
- The **tram** between Sóller and the port.
- The **boat** past the cliffs between Sa Calobra and Port de Sóller.
- **Lluc** monastery, high in the mountains.

## How it works

You are picked up in the morning in southern Mallorca and spend the day combining coach, boat, tram and train with a guide. You return to the same point in the evening.

## Who I recommend it for

- Families with children who can handle a **long day**.
- Anyone who does not want to **drive** in the Tramuntana.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone staying in the **north**: pickup is in the south.
- Children who get car sick: there are many bends.
