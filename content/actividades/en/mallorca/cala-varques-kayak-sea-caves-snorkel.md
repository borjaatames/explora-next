## What you will see

- The wild **east coast of Mallorca** between Cala Romántica and Cala Varques.
- Inside several **sea caves**, swimming with helmet and torch.
- The **natural arch** of Cala Varques and Cala Falcó.
- **Posidonia** meadows underwater.

## How it works

After a safety briefing in Cala Romántica, you paddle along the coast with the guides to Cala Falcó and Cala Varques, stopping to swim, enter the caves and snorkel. At the end you walk about 15 minutes through the reserve and ride back by 4x4. The order may change on the day.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **unspoilt coves** without crowds.
- **Families** with kids aged 6+ and groups of friends.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone not comfortable **swimming** in the sea.
- Anyone after a plan with **no physical effort**.
