## What you'll see

A **self-guided** route through five restaurants in **Palma** old town, near Plaça Major and Plaça de la Reina: **Fervor, Casa S'Oli, Bodega Mayor, Bocalto and Maura**.

At each one you pick a **gourmet tapa** from two options and a drink: local wine, homemade sangria, beer, a soft drink, water or coffee.

## How it works

The day before you get a PDF with the route and a **QR code** to show at each stop. You start at your booked time (with 30 minutes' leeway) and have until 22:00 to finish. WhatsApp support. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
