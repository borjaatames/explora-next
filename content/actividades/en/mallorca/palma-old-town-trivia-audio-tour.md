## What you will see

The tour starts at the **cathedral** and leaves the tourist trail for the medieval alleys of the **upper town**: the centre of Roman Palmaria, traces of the **Knights Templar**, the **Jewish Quarter** and the Royal Palace of the Almudaina. Trivia questions make it more fun, and the app suggests where to stop for a sweet treat.

## How it works

After booking you receive a link to download the free **WeGoTrip** app and your tour. Download it before you go (it works **offline**), put your earphones in and press play at the first stop. The app shows the route on a live map and each stop has audio and written text, so you can **pause, skip or repeat** whenever you like. There is no live guide and no group.

## Who I recommend it to

- To families with children and travellers who enjoy games.
- To travellers who like to go at their own pace, without a group or a fixed schedule.
- Not for those who want to ask a live guide questions: in that case a guided tour is a better fit.
