## What you will see

- Rafa Nadal's most iconic **trophies** and rackets from historic matches.
- A **gallery of his 22 Grand Slams** and an area devoted to his clay-court dominance.
- An audiovisual on his rivalry with **Federer and Djokovic**.
- The **Sports Legends** room, with items from athletes such as Michael Jordan and Usain Bolt.

## How it works

You arrive at the Rafa Nadal Academy in Manacor, show your ticket and explore the museum on your own: an immersive tunnel on his early years, themed rooms on his career and an audiovisual finale. The visit takes about an hour.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Tennis** and sports fans.
- **Families** with sporty kids.
- Anyone visiting the **Manacor and Porto Cristo** area.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with no interest in **sport**.
- Anyone expecting to see the **academy facilities**, which aren't included.
