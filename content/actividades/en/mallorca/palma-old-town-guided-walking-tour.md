## What you will see

- The **Cathedral**, **La Lonja**, the **Consolat de Mar** and the **Almudaina**, from outside.
- The **courtyards** of old-town palaces.
- The **Jewish quarter**, the bishop's palace, the city walls and the **Born**.

## How it works

You walk for almost two hours through the historic centre with a guide who explains Palma's history from its heyday as a Mediterranean trading hub, the Cathedral's builders and the Modernisme palaces. You don't go inside the monuments.

## Who I recommend it for

- **German speakers**, or groups who want a **private tour in English**.
- Anyone arriving in Palma who wants context before exploring alone.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a shared tour in English or Spanish.
- Anyone who wants to see inside the Cathedral.
