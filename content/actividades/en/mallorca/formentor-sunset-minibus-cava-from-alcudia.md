## What you will see

- The **Formentor peninsula** road in the evening light.
- The cliffs from the **Es Colomer viewpoint**.
- **Formentor Beach**, on a photo stop.
- The sunset from around the **Albercutx watchtower**.

## How it works

You leave Alcúdia by minibus for Formentor and stop for half an hour at Es Colomer. After a short stop at the beach, you head up to the Albercutx watchtower, where you have about an hour to watch the sunset with cava and strawberries before returning to Alcúdia.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and anyone after a relaxed end to the day.
- Anyone staying in **Alcúdia** or the north who doesn't want to drive Formentor.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets sick on **winding roads**.
- Anyone wanting **beach time or a swim**; there's no time for that.
