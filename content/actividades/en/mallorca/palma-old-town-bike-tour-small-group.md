## What you will see

- The **Cathedral** and the **Almudaina Palace**.
- What remains of the **city walls** and their viewpoints.
- **La Lonja**, the **Town Hall** and the **Arab Baths**.
- The **Born** promenade to finish.

## How it works

You meet near the Cathedral, pick up a bike and helmet and ride the old-town streets with the guide for about three hours, stopping to explain each monument and take photos. The route can be adjusted to what interests the group most.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see **a lot of Palma in little time**.
- Anyone comfortable in **English** and cycling in a city.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to go inside the monuments.
- Anyone who wants a Spanish-speaking guide.
