## The trip

Three hours on a **semi-private boat** from Alcúdia, 12 people at most. You leave at sunrise, when the **dolphin** pods are most active, and the operator guarantees at least **30 minutes with them**.

## The coast

Then you cruise the **cliffs and caves of Cap de Formentor** and stop for a swim at **Coll Baix**, a pebble beach with crystal water that is very hard to reach by land.

## Who it's for

Families with children from age 3 and anyone who wants to see wild dolphins without a crowded boat. There is a hotel pickup option.
