## What you will see

- The **Bay of Palma** from the sea.
- From Can Pastilla, the **marine reserve** and coves such as **Cala Vella**.
- From Palma, the coast of **Illetas** and **Cala Major** on the way to Palmanova.

## How it works

You board at the port for your chosen option and the boat cruises the bay with music. Weather permitting, it stops for a swim and snorkel with the gear on board, then returns to the start. One drink is included and there's a bar for the rest.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Palma, Can Pastilla or Playa de Palma**.
- Anyone after a **short, cheap** plan at sea.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a **small**, quiet boat.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
