## What you will see

- The **cliffs** between Cala Romàntica and Cala Falcó.
- **Cova de Coloms**, with chambers, tunnels and natural pools.
- Stalactites, stalagmites and **reddish tones** on the walls.

## How it works

After the safety briefing you hike about 30-40 minutes along the cliffs to Cala Falcó. There you choose to abseil down to the water or swim about 150 metres to the cave entrance. Inside you spend around 45 minutes exploring with the guide, then return to the beach and the starting point.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Strong swimmers** looking for adrenaline.
- Teenagers aged 12 and up with their parents.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **vertigo or claustrophobia**.
- Anyone without a car to reach Cala Romàntica.
