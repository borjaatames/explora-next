## What you will see

- The **stalactite chambers** of the Drach Caves in Porto Cristo.
- **Lake Martel**, one of Europe's largest underground lakes.
- The classical **concert** from lit boats.
- The **boat ride** across the lake at the end.

## How it works

You leave Palma at 9:30 on a direct coach, enter the caves without queuing and have over 2 hours there. You are back in Palma in the early afternoon.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children who walk on their own.
- Anyone staying in or near **Palma**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with a **pushchair**: there are about 300 steps.
- Anyone staying in the north: take the departure from Alcúdia.
