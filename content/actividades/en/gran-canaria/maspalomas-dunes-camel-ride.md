## What you will see

- The **Maspalomas Dunes** nature reserve.
- The **desert** landscape facing the Atlantic.
- The **camels** and their Sahrawi handlers.

## How it works

You arrive at the site in Maspalomas, wait your turn and climb onto a two-seat saddle. The guides lead the camels on a half-hour loop through the dunes.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with young children.
- Anyone wanting an **iconic photo** in the dunes.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a **long** or adventurous activity.
- People **over 100 kg**.
