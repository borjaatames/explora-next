## What you will see

- **Calle Triana** and its Modernist architecture.
- The **Vegueta** district, the oldest part of the city.
- Buildings tied to Las Palmas' **colonial past** and lesser-known corners.

## How it works

You meet the guide at the San Telmo kiosk and walk through Triana and Vegueta for about two and a half hours at a relaxed pace. Halfway through you stop at a local spot for one tapa per person.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **understand the history** of Las Palmas with a guide.
- **Families** and groups who prefer a **private** tour.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a full **food tour**: only one tapa is included.
