## What you will see

- **Bandama Caldera**, with views from the top of the volcano.
- **Historic villages** of the interior on the way to the winery.
- A **family winery** and its vineyards, shown by the winemaker.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel and driven by van to Bandama for the first viewpoint stop. About 20 minutes later you reach the winery, where you spend around two hours: a walk through the vineyards and cellar with the winemaker and an outdoor tasting of artisan wines paired with local products. Afterwards you're taken back to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Wine lovers** keen to discover local production.
- **Couples** and small groups of adults.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Families with **under-18s**.
- Anyone after a quick, cheap tasting.
