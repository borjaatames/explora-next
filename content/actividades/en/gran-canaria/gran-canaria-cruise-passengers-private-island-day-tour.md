## What you will see

- The northern villages of **Arucas**, **Firgas** and **Teror**.
- **Cruz de Tejeda**, with views over the island's ancient central crater.
- **Pico de las Nieves**, the highest point of Gran Canaria.
- The village of **Fataga** and the **Maspalomas dunes** on the way back.

## How it works

A private driver picks you up next to the cruise terminal and drives the classic island loop: the green north, up to the central crater and the summit, then down south past Fataga to the Maspalomas dunes, and back to the ship. The route is fixed, so it's the full island in one day rather than a tailored tour.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Cruise passengers** who want to see the whole island in one port day.
- **Small groups of up to 4**, since the price is per vehicle.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Wheelchair users**, as stated by the provider.
- Anyone wanting a bespoke route or specific stops — for that, a tailored private tour fits better.
