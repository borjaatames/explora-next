## What you will see

**Aqualand Maspalomas** is the largest water park in Gran Canaria, inland from Maspalomas and a few kilometres from the southern beaches.

### Slides

More than **40 slides** for all ages. The best known is the **Anaconda**, and there are multi-lane racing slides and a **water maze**.

### For little ones

The **Children's Paradise** is an area with slides and water games on their scale, designed for children who are still too small for the big rides.

### Out of the water

The **Polynesia** area and gardens offer dry-land activities, and there's an optional **sea lion experience**, paid separately and subject to availability.

## How it works

Pick your date and go straight in through the **ticket control gate** with your booking on your phone. The ticket is valid for the whole day within park opening hours.

## Opening hours and practical tips

- **September to June**: 10:00 to 17:00, last entry 15:30.
- **July and August**: 10:00 to 18:00, last entry 16:30.
- Rides close **30 minutes before** the park.
- **Parking** costs €5 per day. Without a car, you can get there by taxi or bus from Playa del Inglés and Maspalomas.
- **Sun loungers, parasols, lockers and fast passes** cost extra. If you want a spot in the shade, arrive at opening time.
- Some rides have **height limits** and you can't go down with a baby on your lap.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** and teenagers wanting a day on the slides.
- Groups of friends after some adrenaline without leaving the south.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting a cheap day out: the extras add up.
- Families with babies who could only use the kids' area.
