## What you will see

- The **organic vineyards** of a family winery on volcanic soil.
- A **wine museum** with vintage tools and machinery.
- The **old cellar** and today's production area.

## How it works

You make your own way to the winery in Santa Brígida, where you're welcomed with the family's story. You walk through the vineyards, visit the museum and the cellar, and finish in the tasting room with two wines, cheese and toasted bread. About an hour and a half in total.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone with a **car** wanting a cultural stop in the centre of the island.
- **Wine lovers** after something small and family-run.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't speak **English**.
- Anyone who needs **transport** from their hotel.
