## What you'll see

A **private full-day** tour of **eastern Gran Canaria**, your group only, with your own transport and guide. It starts in **Agüimes**, with its cobbled streets and colonial feel; continues through the lush **Guayadeque ravine** and its traditional cave houses; passes the coastal village of **Tufia**, with white houses and a volcanic beach; and ends at the **Bandama crater**, with panoramic island views.

## How it works

A **private** tour (your group only), around **7 hours**, with **private transport and guide** included (meals not included). The guide picks you up at your accommodation. **Price is per group.**

> The tour runs in **English and French** (not Spanish).
