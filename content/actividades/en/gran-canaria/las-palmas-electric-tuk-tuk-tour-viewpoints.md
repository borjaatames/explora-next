## What you will see

- The **Triana** district and **San Telmo Park**.
- **Castillo de Mata**, the **Museo Canario** and **Santa Ana Cathedral** in Vegueta.
- The city and the Atlantic from the **Schamann viewpoint**.
- **Doramas Park** and Ciudad Jardín.

## How it works

You set off from Triana in an electric tuk tuk just for your group. The driver takes you through the historic districts with photo stops, climbs to the Schamann viewpoint and finishes at the Hotel Santa Catalina after about 90 minutes.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone **short on time** in Las Palmas, such as cruise passengers.
- **Couples** and **families** who'd rather not walk much.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone wanting museum visits or an **in-depth** look at Vegueta.
