## What you will see

- The **Mogán** coast and **Cala del Perchel**, reachable only by sea.
- Mogán bay and **Tauro** beach.
- The **Tiritaña** cliffs on the way back.

## How it works

You're picked up from your accommodation in the south or go straight to Puerto Rico harbour. You set sail with a glass of cava, head towards Mogán and anchor in a cove to swim, snorkel and paddleboard while the crew prepares the snacks. You return to Puerto Rico along the coast.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **groups of friends** after a morning in the water.
- Anyone who prefers a **small boat** to a big catamaran.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets **seasick** easily.
- Anyone mainly hoping to **see dolphins**.
