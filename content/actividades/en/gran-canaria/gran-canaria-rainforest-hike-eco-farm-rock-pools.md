## What you will see

- **Firgas**, known as the "city of water", with its old mills and Atlantic views.
- The **Azuaje ravine**, a remnant of the ancient **Doramas forest**, with laurel woodland and streams.
- The ruins of the old **Azuaje spa**, hidden in the ravine.
- **Ecofinca Ventana Verde**, a sustainable farm.
- The **El Puertillo natural pools** on the north coast.

## How it works

You're picked up in your area and head north. After half an hour in Firgas, you hike the Azuaje ravine (about 3 km and 2 hours, crossing streams) and stop at the organic farm to taste its produce. You finish with a swim in the El Puertillo rock pools before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the island's **green north** and fancies something other than the beach.
- Families with **children aged 5+** who are used to walking.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't want **wet feet** or rocky ground.
- Anyone prone to **carsickness** on winding roads.
