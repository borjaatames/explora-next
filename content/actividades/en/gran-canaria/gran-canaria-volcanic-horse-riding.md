## What you will see

- The **volcanic dirt tracks** and mountain trails around El Salobre.
- The arid landscape of the **southern interior** of Gran Canaria, a world away from the coast.
- Ravines and slopes you can't reach in a hire car.

## How it works

The ride starts from the **El Salobre Horse Riding** ranch, inland in the south of the island, between Maspalomas and Puerto Rico. You can make your own way there or book the option with **hotel pickup**, available from some areas.

On arrival you're introduced to the horses and given a short **safety briefing**. The team matches each rider to a horse based on experience and hands out helmets (and vests for younger riders). Then you head out in a small group with the guide along mountain paths and volcanic tracks.

There are two options:

- **1 hour**: designed for beginners, at a walk.
- **2 hours**: for all levels; experienced riders can **trot and gallop** on some stretches.

## Practical tips

- Clothing matters: **long trousers and closed shoes**. You won't be allowed to ride in shorts, a skirt, a bikini or sandals.
- If you don't have boots, you can **rent them at the ranch**.
- The **maximum weight** is 110 kg and children under 3 cannot ride.
- In summer the sun is strong inland: book early in the morning or late afternoon and bring water and sun protection.
- The tracks are dusty; a scarf for your face helps.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children wanting a first experience with horses.
- Experienced riders looking to gallop on the **2-hour ride**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People over **110 kg**.
- Anyone after a beach ride: the route is inland and in the hills.
