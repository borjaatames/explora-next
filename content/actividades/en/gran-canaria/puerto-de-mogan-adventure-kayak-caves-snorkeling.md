## What you will see

- Mogán's volcanic **sea cave system**.
- The coastal **cliffs** seen from the water.
- The **seabed** at a beach where you snorkel.

## How it works

You get your gear plus a technique and safety briefing, carry the kayaks to the beach together and paddle a double kayak to the caves. If the sea allows, you swim inside them, and there are optional jumps from the rocks. The second stop is a beach for snorkeling with the guide.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Sporty** sea lovers with good fitness.
- Anyone after an **adrenaline** hit from the cliff jumps.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who isn't a **confident sea swimmer**.
- Families with **children** or anyone over 55.
