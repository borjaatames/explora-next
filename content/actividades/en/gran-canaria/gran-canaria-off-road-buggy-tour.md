## What you will see

- The **Ayagaures valley** and reservoir.
- The **Pedro González viewpoint**.
- The **Fataga ravine** and its villages.
- Dirt tracks between **mountains and canyons**.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel and, after a safety briefing, drive the buggy behind the guide for about two hours on southern tracks, stopping at viewpoints.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone after **adrenaline** and driving.
- Anyone wanting to see the southern **interior** without a car.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **back problems**.
- Anyone **without a licence** who isn't travelling with someone who has one.
