## What you will see

- **Volcanic caves** reachable only from the sea.
- The white **cliffs** of the Mogán coast.
- Fish and marine life at the **snorkel stop**.
- **Mogán** from the water.

## How it works

You gear up at the Taurito meeting point, paddle with the guide along cliffs and into caves, stop to snorkel (and jump if you like) and paddle back to the start.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone after active **adventure** at sea.
- Sporty **groups of friends** and couples.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Non-swimmers**.
- People with **low fitness**.
