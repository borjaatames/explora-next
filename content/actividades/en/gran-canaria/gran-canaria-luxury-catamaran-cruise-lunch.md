## What you will see

- Gran Canaria's **southwest coast**, with volcanic cliffs and coves.
- **El Perchel Bay**, the first swim stop.
- The quiet bay of **Tasarte**, where the boat anchors for lunch.

## How it works

You're picked up in the south and leave Puerto Rico at 10:30 heading west. At El Perchel there's time to swim, paddleboard, kayak or snorkel. The boat then anchors in Tasarte, where lunch is served while you can keep swimming. You cruise slowly back along the coast and reach Puerto Rico around 15:00.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples**, **families** and **groups of friends** who want an unhurried day at sea.
- Anyone who prefers a **small boat** to the mass-market catamarans.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone mainly after **dolphin sightings**.
- Anyone prone to seasickness.
