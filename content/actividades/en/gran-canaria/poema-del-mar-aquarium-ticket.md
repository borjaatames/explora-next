## What you will see

**Poema del Mar** is the aquarium of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, run by the Loro Parque group. It's laid out as **35 ecosystems** running from freshwater to the deep ocean, split into three main areas.

### The Jungle

Rivers, deltas, mangroves and the **Amazon** rainforest, with freshwater species.

### Arrecife

A recreated **coral reef** and the most striking piece of the visit: a **400,000-litre** water cylinder you can walk all the way around, with tropical fish swimming around you.

### The deep sea

The final area, with the **huge curved window** onto the main tank, where you'll see sharks and rays, plus a deep-sea exhibit with around 3,000 specimens of up to 40 species. **El Veril** shows the species of Canary Islands waters, the same ones you'd see diving around the island.

## How it works

Pick your date, show your booking at the **ticket office** and go straight in without queuing to buy. The ticket is valid all day and the route is self-guided: there's no guide and no set visiting time.

## Practical tips

- From Maspalomas or Playa del Inglés it's about 50 km to the port of Las Palmas, by car or by bus. Spend the rest of the day in the city: the **Vegueta** old quarter or **Las Canteras** beach.
- It's the perfect plan for **cloudy or calima** days, when the beach doesn't appeal.
- **Canary Islands residents** get a special price at the ticket office, so this ticket isn't worth it for them.
- Under-3s go free.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**, especially on non-beach days.
- Anyone staying in **Las Palmas** or spending the day in the capital.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't want to see animals in captivity.
- Visitors based in the south with only half a day: the journey eats up much of the time.
