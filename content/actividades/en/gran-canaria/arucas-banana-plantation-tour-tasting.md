## What you will see

- More than **50 banana varieties**.
- Other crops on the farm, such as **avocado**.
- Views over the plantations to the **Atlantic**.
- A **tasting** of banana products.

## How it works

You arrive at the Arucas farm, a guide shows you round the plantation and explains how Canary bananas are grown, and you finish with a tasting. Afterwards you can wander the gardens.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children.
- Anyone curious about the north's **farming** Gran Canaria.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without a **car** to reach Arucas.
- Anyone looking for a **long** activity.
