## What you will see

**Sioux City Park** is a **Wild West** theme park in southern Gran Canaria, billed as the first of its kind in Spain. What makes it different is that the town isn't a cardboard set: the **buildings are real** and you can walk around inside them.

### The shows

The best part of the visit is the **live shows**: gunfighter duels on the main street and the **bank robbery**, complete with a showdown with the outlaws.

### The farm

An area with **Canarian goats, cows, ponies, donkeys and hens** that younger children love.

### Dressing up

In the themed shop you can dress up as a **cowboy or a Native American** and step right into the film.

## How it works

Pick your date and go in with the booking on your phone, **without queuing at the ticket office**. The ticket is valid for one day. Show times aren't listed in the booking, so check them when you arrive and plan your visit around them.

## Practical tips

- Tickets **sell out easily**: book ahead.
- The park is in the **Barranco del Águila**, near San Agustín and a few minutes from Playa del Inglés. Pickup isn't included; some travellers mention a free bus, but confirm it with the park.
- The ravine is in **full sun**: bring a cap, water and sunscreen.
- Food and drink are extra.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children**, who are the ideal audience.
- Anyone after a fun, different plan away from the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting a big theme park with mechanical rides.
- Anyone who can't handle the midday heat outdoors.
