## What you will see

- **Bandama Caldera** from the peak, with views over much of the island.
- The **Cruz de Tejeda** market, with local produce.
- **Roque Nublo**, reached on foot, in a UNESCO World Heritage landscape.
- The **Tirajana caldera** and the central summits.
- **Sunset** from **Pico de las Nieves**, the highest point in Gran Canaria.

## How it works

You're picked up in the morning and start at Bandama. Next comes Cruz de Tejeda, with time for the market and a tasting lunch at a local restaurant. In the afternoon you hike to Roque Nublo (around three hours with stops) and finish on Pico de las Nieves to watch the sunset before heading back to your hotel.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **volcanic interior** without driving.
- Anyone who wants to **hike Roque Nublo** and catch the **sunset** in the summits on the same day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone without **good fitness** or with knee problems.
- Anyone who wants to be back for **hotel dinner**.
