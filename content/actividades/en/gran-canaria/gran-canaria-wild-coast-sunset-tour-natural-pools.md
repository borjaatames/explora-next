## What you will see

- Lava **natural pools** on the north coast, mostly used by locals.
- The **Sardina lighthouse** and Sardina beach, with its cave houses.
- The **west coast** cliffs and Agaete.
- The **sunset** from the Mirador del Balcón.

## How it works

You're picked up at midday from your accommodation and head north by van. You stop at several natural pools (with an optional swim at one), have a picnic and cocktail by the sea, continue through Gáldar and Agaete, and finish watching the sunset from the Mirador del Balcón before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Photography** lovers.
- Anyone who wants to see the **less touristy** side of Gran Canaria.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets **carsick** on winding roads.
- Families with children **under 5**.
