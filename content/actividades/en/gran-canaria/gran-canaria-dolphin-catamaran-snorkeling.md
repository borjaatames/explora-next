## What you will see

- **Dolphins and pilot whales** in the wild off the south coast.
- The sea below through the **glass panels**.
- The south-west **coves and cliffs** from the water.
- A **swim stop** with snorkeling and a slide.

## How it works

You board at Puerto Base (Puerto Rico), with hotel pickup if you book it. The catamaran searches for cetaceans, then anchors in a small bay for swimming and snorkeling before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families** with children.
- Anyone wanting to see **dolphins** on a budget.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone prone to **seasickness**.
- Anyone looking for **food or a party** on board.
