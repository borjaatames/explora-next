## What you will see

- Southern Gran Canaria's **cliffs and ravines** from above.
- A **suspension bridge** and a **swing hidden inside a cave**.
- A volcanic landscape that feels far from the beaches.

## How it works

You meet at the activity car park in Bahía Feliz, get your gear and a safety briefing, and set off with the guide on a roughly 4-hour route that mixes hiking with vertical sections of cable and rungs. There are photo breaks along the way and you finish where you started.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **try climbing** with no experience.
- **Couples** and **groups of friends** after something active.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone with **vertigo** or low fitness.
- Families with **children under 12**.
