## What you will see

- Gran Canaria's **southwest coast**, with beaches, cliffs and coves.
- A clear-water **bay** in the **Mogán** area, where the boat anchors for swimming.

## How it works

You're picked up from a point in your area and taken to Puerto Rico. After the safety briefing, the catamaran sails for about an hour to a bay near Mogán, where you spend around two and a half hours swimming, snorkelling and having lunch; jet ski, parasailing or donut rides are available at extra cost. You then sail back to port and are returned to your pickup point.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends** and **families** after a lively day at sea.
- Anyone keen to try **water sports** at a discount.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone looking for a quiet **small boat**.
- Anyone staying in **Puerto Rico or Mogán** who needs pickup.
