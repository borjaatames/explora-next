## What you will see

- The mountains of the island's centre, near **Pico de las Nieves** (1,949 m).
- **Canary Island pine** forests.
- The silhouette of **Roque Nublo**.
- In clear weather, the east and south coasts and **Mount Teide** on Tenerife.

## How it works

You're picked up at your hotel in a nine-seat minibus and driven up to the summits. There you hike with an international mountain guide: 7 km on Sundays and Wednesdays, 10 km on Mondays (about 7 km in summer). There's a lunch stop before heading back to the hotel. The whole day takes around 8 to 9 hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- Keen **walkers** who want a **professional guide** and a small group.
- Anyone who'd rather not **drive** up to the summits.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Las Palmas** or the **Mogán and Puerto Rico** area (no pickup).
- Anyone with **respiratory issues**.
