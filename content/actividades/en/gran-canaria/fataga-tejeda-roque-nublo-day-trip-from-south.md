## What you will see

- The Fataga valley from the **Degollada de las Yeguas** viewpoint.
- An **aloe vera farm** and how its products are made.
- The village of **Fataga**, with white houses and bougainvillea.
- **Tejeda** and **Roque Nublo** from the Degollada de Becerra.

## How it works

You're picked up in the south and climb by coach into the interior. You stop at the Degollada de las Yeguas viewpoint, visit the aloe vera farm, take a short walk through Fataga and get almost two free hours in Tejeda before the Roque Nublo viewpoint stop and the drive back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see the **interior** without renting a car, at a good price.
- Groups that speak **different languages**.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **hike up** to Roque Nublo.
- Anyone who gets **carsick** on mountain roads.
