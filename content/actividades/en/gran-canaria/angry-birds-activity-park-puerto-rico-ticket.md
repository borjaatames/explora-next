## What you will see

- **Red, Chuck, Bomb** and other Angry Birds characters.
- **Chuck's Flight Bridge** and the **King Pig Fortress**, with tunnels and slides.
- **Trampolines**, zip lines, pedal cars and a scooter ramp.
- **Mini golf** and the **splash zone**, with daily foam parties.

## How it works

Buy a ticket for the day you want and, before going, sign the safety form online and watch the video (otherwise you'll do it at the entrance). At the park in Puerto Rico you use the activities at your own pace and can come and go. There's a snack bar with kids' and adults' menus, and the 10D cinema costs extra.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** staying in Puerto Rico, Mogán or nearby.
- Anyone after a **cheap, active** plan away from the beach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting a **theme park** with rollercoasters.
- Teenagers looking for thrills.
