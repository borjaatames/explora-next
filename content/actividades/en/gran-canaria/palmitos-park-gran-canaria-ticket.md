## Gran Canaria's finest nature park

**Palmitos Park** is set in a dramatic natural **barranco** (ravine) in the interior of Gran Canaria, 15 km north of Maspalomas. It combines a zoological park, botanical garden, dolphin and parrot shows, and a remarkable butterfly house in a single day out.

### Dolphin show

The park's **dolphin auditorium** seats 4,000 people — the largest in the Canary Islands. The show features bottlenose dolphins performing natural behaviours and acrobatics. Arrival at the park early allows you to claim good seats.

### Parrot show

The **parrot show** is one of the most entertaining in any Spanish theme park — trained macaws, cockatoos, and African grey parrots perform with their handlers in a naturalistic outdoor theatre. The grey parrots' ability to mimic human speech is always a crowd favourite.

### The butterfly house

One of the park's stars: a large **tropical greenhouse** with hundreds of butterflies from South America, Asia, and Africa flying freely around visitors. The best time to visit is mid-morning when warmth and light make the butterflies most active.

### The gardens

The park is set within a botanical garden featuring **native Canarian plants**, cacti, tropical palms, and flowering succulents. Walking between attractions through the barranco is a pleasure in itself.

### The bird garden

**Flamingos, toucans, hornbills, crowned cranes**, and dozens of other tropical species in large aviaries. The free-flying parrot zones allow close encounters.

## Getting there

The drive along the **GC-503** from Maspalomas is scenic — a winding mountain road through a stark volcanic landscape. Allow 30 minutes from the south coast resorts.
