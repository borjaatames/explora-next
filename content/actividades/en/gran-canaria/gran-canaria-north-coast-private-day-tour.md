## What you'll see

A **private full-day** tour of **northern Gran Canaria**, your group only, with your own transport and guide. It starts on the **Tufia** coast; continues to **Arucas**, with its striking neo-Gothic church; lunch in **Firgas**, famous for its watercress dishes; and ends in **Agaete**, to stroll the seafront, see the cliffs and swim in its natural pools if you like.

## How it works

A **private** tour (your group only), around **9 hours**, with **private transport and guide** included (lunch not included). The guide picks you up at your accommodation. **Price is per group.**

> The tour runs in **English and French** (not Spanish).
