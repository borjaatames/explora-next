## What you will see

- The **Degollada** viewpoint, overlooking the south of the island.
- **Fataga** and the **Valley of a Thousand Palms**.
- **San Bartolomé de Tirajana** and the almond groves of **Ayacata**.
- **Tejeda**, up in the summits.
- The **Artenara cave houses** and the **Monte Pavón** viewpoint.
- **Fontanales**, **Moya** and the coastal village of **San Felipe** in the north.

## How it works

You're picked up in the south and the minivan climbs the Fataga ravine towards the summits, with short stops at each village and viewpoint. After Tejeda and Artenara you head down through the midlands to Fontanales, where there's free time for lunch. You then pass through Moya and the Los Tilos valley to San Felipe, returning south around Las Palmas.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **interior and north** in a day without driving.
- Anyone who prefers a **small vehicle** to a coach.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to **hike** or spend a long time in each village.
- Anyone prone to **carsickness** on mountain roads.
