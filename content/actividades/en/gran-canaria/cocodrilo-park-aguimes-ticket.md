## What you will see

- **Europe's largest alligator exhibit**.
- **Tigers**, **chimpanzees** and other monkeys.
- **Meerkats**, **turtles**, lizards, raccoons and birds.
- Farm animals such as **llamas**, **pygmy goats** and pigs.

## How it works

Buy the ticket, choose any day within a year and show it on your phone at the park, on the Los Corralillos road in Agüimes. Explore the 20,000+ m² of enclosures at your own pace; there's a snack bar inside for food.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Families with children** looking for a cheap day out.
- Anyone staying in the **east** or **south** of the island with a car.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone expecting a **big zoo** like Palmitos Park or Loro Parque.
- Anyone without their own transport.
