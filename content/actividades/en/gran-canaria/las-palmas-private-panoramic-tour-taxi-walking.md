## What you will see

- **Viewpoints** over the city, the port and the coast.
- **Las Canteras** beach and the **Santa Catalina** district.
- The historic streets of **Vegueta** and the cathedral area.

## How it works

You meet the guide in the port area and tour the city combining short taxi rides with walks. You stop at several viewpoints for photos, stroll through Santa Catalina, Las Canteras and Vegueta, and have a tapa and a drink along the way.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Cruise passengers** with only a few hours in the city.
- **Couples** and **families** who want the highlights without tiring out.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**, as stated by the provider.
