## What you will see

- The **Guayadeque ravine** and its cave houses.
- The **Maspalomas Dunes** viewpoint.
- **Puerto Rico** marina.
- **Puerto de Mogán**, the 'Venice of the south'.

## How it works

You're picked up in Las Palmas and the coach climbs to the Guayadeque ravine, drops down to the Maspalomas viewpoint, stops in Puerto Rico (optional boat) and ends with free time in Puerto de Mogán before heading back north.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone staying in **Las Palmas** without a car.
- Anyone wanting to see **several areas** of the island in a day.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who prefers **small groups**.
- Anyone who already has a **hire car**.
