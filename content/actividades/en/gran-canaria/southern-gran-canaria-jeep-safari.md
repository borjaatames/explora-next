## What you will see

- The **Chira reservoir** and the Nublo Rural Park.
- Views of **Roque Nublo**.
- **San Bartolomé de Tirajana** and its caldera.
- The **Valley of a Thousand Palms** and Degollada de las Yeguas.

## How it works

You're picked up at your southern hotel and the jeep climbs dirt tracks to the Chira reservoir, stops in San Bartolomé de Tirajana for coffee and returns via the Fataga valley and Degollada de las Yeguas.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone wanting to see the **interior** without driving.
- **Families** and mixed-age groups.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after **adrenaline**: choose the buggy.
- Anyone staying in **Las Palmas**.
