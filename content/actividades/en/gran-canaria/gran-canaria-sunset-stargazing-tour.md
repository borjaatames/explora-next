## What you will see

- The **Grand Canyon** from Degollada de las Yeguas.
- **Sunset above the clouds** at Pico de las Nieves, with Teide.
- **Planets, galaxies and nebulae** through telescopes.
- **Constellations** pointed out by laser.

## How it works

You head up to the summits at sunset via Fataga and Degollada de las Yeguas, watch the sunset with cava at Pico de las Nieves and finish stargazing with telescopes at Llanos de la Pez.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and **photography** lovers.
- Anyone wanting to see the island's **interior** differently.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who **doesn't understand English**.
- Anyone who struggles with **cold**.
