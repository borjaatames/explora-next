## What you will see

**NOMAD, Journey to the Origins** is the dinner show at **Sala Scala**, a venue in southern Gran Canaria with more than 35 years of history. The show travels across **three continents** through music, dance and acrobatics, with more than **30 international artists** on stage.

- **Live music and dance** from the cultures the journey visits.
- **Acrobatic acts** woven between the choreographies.
- **20 metres of screens**, lighting and special effects for each part.
- A **tasting menu** of local produce, inspired by the zero-mile idea and designed to accompany each moment of the show.

## How it works

Make your own way to Sala Scala, in San Bartolomé de Tirajana, before **20:00**, when the show begins. You're seated at your table and dinner is served in courses as the show unfolds over about **three hours**. Allowing for arrival and departure, plan on three to five hours for the evening.

The price and the dishes on the menu depend on the **ticket category** you choose when booking. Drinks are not included in any of them and are ordered in the venue.

## Practical tips

- Shows run only **Thursday to Sunday**: bear it in mind when planning your week.
- Hotel transfer is **not included**; according to the provider it can only be added by booking ahead, if there's space. Otherwise, take a taxi.
- **Drinks are extra**: factor them into your budget.
- The venue is **wheelchair accessible** and smoking is not allowed inside.
- You don't need Spanish: almost the whole show is music, dance and acrobatics.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and groups looking for a different night out in the south.
- Anyone celebrating something with dinner and a show without leaving the Maspalomas area.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone after a purely **gastronomic dinner**: the show is the star.
- Early sleepers: it finishes late.
