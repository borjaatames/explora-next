## What you will see

- A show with professional **drag performers**, **singers** and **dancers**.
- **Comedy sketches**, musical numbers and audience interaction.
- A **meet and greet** with the cast and a souvenir photo.

## How it works

You arrive at Garbos by Captain 13 in Bahía Feliz (or get a lift from your area if you choose the transfer option) and take your seat. The show starts while the three-course dinner is served, mixing live vocals, comedy and big production numbers. The evening lasts about four hours.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Groups of friends**, **hen parties** and **birthdays**.
- Anyone after a **fun night out** with dinner included.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who doesn't speak **English**.
- Anyone who'd rather avoid **audience participation**.
