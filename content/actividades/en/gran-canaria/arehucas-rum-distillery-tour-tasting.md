## What you'll see

**Destilerías Arehucas** is the best-known rum distillery in the Canary Islands. The tour takes you through its **ageing cellar**, one of the oldest in Europe, with **4,308 American oak barrels**.

Then you see how the rum is made: the **mill**, the **fermentation and distillation** rooms and the **bottling plant**. It ends with a **tasting of the house rums and liqueurs**, and you can buy in the shop.

## How it works

One hour with a guide in Spanish, English or German. The distillery is at the entrance to Arucas, about 20 minutes from Las Palmas. It's wheelchair accessible.

## Who it's for

For anyone after a short, inexpensive plan in the north of the island, and for rum fans. It combines well with the old town of **Arucas** and its volcanic-stone church, or with the banana plantation visit.
