## What you will see

- The **Tirajana caldera** from the El Guriete viewpoint.
- The **Fortaleza de Ansite**, with caves and sacred sites of the ancient Canarians.
- The village of **Santa Lucía de Tirajana**.
- The reddish walls of the **Barranco de las Vacas**.
- The beach in the fishing village of **Arinaga**.

## How it works

You're picked up from your hotel and taken by coach to the El Guriete viewpoint. You cross the caldera, visit the Fortaleza with the guide, have lunch and taste local products in Santa Lucía, walk the Barranco de las Vacas and finish with a swim in Arinaga before heading back.

## Who I recommend it for

- Anyone who wants to see the **interior** and **aboriginal history** in one day.
- **Families** with children aged 5 and over.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- Anyone who gets **carsick** on mountain roads.
- People with **reduced mobility**.
