## What you will see

- The **south-west coast** of Gran Canaria from the water, with its cliffs and resorts perched above the sea.
- The harbour of **Puerto Rico**, one of the big marinas of the south.
- With luck, **turtles, dolphins or flying fish** along the way.

## How it works

Meet the **Gran Canaria Water Sport** team at Puerto Base in Puerto Rico, opposite the Costa Mar restaurant at the corner of the commercial dock: look for the **yellow flag** with their logo and the words "moto de agua". Arrive 15 minutes early.

Once you've put on your life jacket and had your briefing, a boat takes you from the harbour out to the jet skis. You ride your own while the **instructor follows in a safety boat**, alternating fast and gentle stretches along the coast. The tour lasts between **30 minutes and 1 hour**, depending on the option you book.

## The price, explained

The "from" price is **per jet ski, not per person**. Two people can ride on each jet ski:

- **Two people** on one jet ski: book 1 participant.
- **Three people**: you need 2 jet skis, so book 2 participants.

## Practical tips

- You must be **able to swim**, and minors can only take part with an adult.
- **Pregnant women** and people with heart problems cannot take part.
- **Cameras are not allowed** and only small bags are permitted: leave valuables at the hotel.
- Bring **swimwear, a towel and sun protection**; you will get wet.
- Departures depend on the sea and the weather.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Couples** and friends who want to share a jet ski and cut the cost.
- First-timers who want to try a jet ski with supervision.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- **Non-swimmers**.
- Anyone after a relaxed wildlife trip: a catamaran is better for that.
