## What you will see

- The **old town of Las Palmas**: Vegueta, Santa Ana Square and the cathedral area.
- **Las Canteras** seafront and the **Santa Catalina** district.
- The northern villages of **Arucas**, **Firgas** and **Teror**, with their squares, balconies and promenades.
- A stop for a **tapa and a drink** at a traditional spot.

## How it works

A local guide picks you up next to the cruise terminal (or at your hotel in Las Palmas) and you tour the city and the green north with private transport. Because it's a private tour, the route is flexible: you can arrange the stops with the guide in advance to focus on what interests you most.

## Who I recommend it for

- **Cruise passengers** who want the island's highlights and still be back at the ship in time.
- **Couples** and **families** who prefer their own guide and pace over a coach tour.

## Who I don't recommend it for

- People with **reduced mobility**, as stated by the provider.
