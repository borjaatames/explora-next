**Girona was a filming location for season 6 of Game of Thrones.** Its old town played Braavos and King's Landing, and all the locations are within five minutes of each other: you can walk the route in a morning.

## The locations

- **Cathedral steps:** the Great Sept of Baelor in King's Landing.
- **The Call (Jewish quarter):** the Braavos alleys where Arya runs.
- **Pujada de Sant Domènec:** the steps from Arya's chases.
- **Sant Pere de Galligants:** the monastery that became the Citadel in Oldtown.
- **Pont de Pedra and the Onyar houses:** Braavos scenes.
- **Passeig Arqueològic:** the city walls, backdrop to several sequences.

## Tips

1. **Go early:** the cathedral steps get crowded by midday.
2. **On your own, bring a locations map:** without it, scenes are hard to place.
3. **The Call is a lived-in neighbourhood:** keep the noise down.
