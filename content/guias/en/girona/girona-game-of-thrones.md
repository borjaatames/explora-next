**Girona was a filming location for season 6 of Game of Thrones, shot in 2015.** Its old town played Braavos, King's Landing and Oldtown, and all the locations are within five minutes of each other: you can walk the route in a morning, for free.

## Which tour to choose

- **You're a fan and travelling as one or two:** the small-group tour. Two hours, up to 10 people, and the guide shows each scene on the iPad on the spot. That matters, because many corners were digitally altered for the show and are hard to recognise alone.
- **You're a family or group of four or more:** the private tour. €260 is the price for the whole group (up to 8), so per person it comes close to the group tour. Same company and route, 2 h 30 min, at your own pace. It's new and has no reviews of its own yet.
- **You care more about Girona than the show:** the Barri Vell walking tour. It's 2 h 30 min of history (the walls, the cathedral, the Call, the Eiffel bridge) and passes several locations along the way.

All three meet in Plaça de Sant Feliu, 15 minutes on foot from the train station, and stay outdoors: monument tickets aren't included.

## The locations

- **Cathedral steps:** the exterior of the Great Sept of Baelor in King's Landing. Jaime Lannister rides up them with the Tyrell army.
- **The Call (Jewish quarter):** the Braavos alleys where Arya runs.
- **Pujada de Sant Domènec:** the steps where Arya, chased by the Waif, tumbles into a crate of oranges.
- **Sant Pere de Galligants:** the monastery that became the Citadel in Oldtown, where Sam and Gilly arrive.
- **Pont de Pedra and the Onyar houses:** Braavos scenes.
- **Passeig Arqueològic:** the city walls, backdrop to several sequences.

## What to know before you go

- **The locations are open streets:** you pay nothing to see them, unless you go inside the Cathedral (joint ticket with Sant Feliu, €7.50 as of 2026, audio guide included), the Arab Baths (a few euros) or Sant Pere de Galligants, now the Archaeology Museum of Catalonia (also a few euros).
- **The whole route on foot takes 2 hours** at a relaxed pace, within a radius of 500 metres. The Pujada de Sant Domènec steps and the climb onto the walls are steep: with a pushchair or reduced mobility, skip those two.
- **The Cathedral steps** (90 of them, the Great Sept) fill with groups from 11:00. For a photo without people, go before 9:30 or after 19:00.
- **Not everything was filmed here:** the Braavos scenes mix Girona with Šibenik in Croatia. If you can't find a location, it probably isn't in Girona.
- **If you're staying in Barcelona,** the high-speed train gets you here in 38 minutes and the station is a 15-minute walk from the first location. You can do the route in a morning and keep the afternoon for the city.

If you start at 9:00, you'll reach the Cathedral steps before the groups and finish on the walls by late morning.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Going at midday:** the Cathedral steps get crowded and your photos come out with a hundred people.
2. **Going on your own without a locations map:** without screenshots from the show beside you, scenes are hard to place. The small-group tour brings an iPad with every shot.
3. **Looking for scenes that weren't filmed here:** Braavos is half Girona, half Croatia.
4. **Shouting or posing in the Call:** it's a lived-in neighbourhood with narrow alleys. Keep the noise down.
5. **Only seeing the locations:** the cathedral interior, the Call and the walls are worth as much as the show.
