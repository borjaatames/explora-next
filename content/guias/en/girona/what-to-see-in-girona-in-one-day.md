**Girona is easy to see in a day.** Its old town is compact and walkable: the cathedral, the Call, the colourful Onyar houses and the city walls. It's 38 minutes from Barcelona by high-speed train.

## One-day route

| Time | Stop |
| --- | --- |
| 9:30 | Onyar houses and the Eiffel iron bridge |
| 10:00 | Cathedral and steps |
| 11:30 | The Call (Jewish quarter) |
| 13:00 | Arab Baths and Sant Pere de Galligants |
| 14:00 | Lunch in the Barri Vell |
| 16:30 | Walk along the city walls |

- **Cathedral:** the widest Gothic nave in the world and the Tapestry of Creation. Its steps were the Great Sept of Baelor in Game of Thrones.
- **The Call:** one of Europe's best-preserved medieval Jewish quarters.
- **City walls:** the best viewpoint in town, and few people climb up. Best at sunset.

## Eating

Look for Empordà cooking in the Barri Vell, one street back from the Rambla (the Rambla terraces cost more). Try a **xuixo**, the local pastry, and an ice cream at **Rocambolesc**.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Seeing it in half an hour:** Girona deserves a full day.
2. **Skipping the cathedral interior:** the nave and tapestry are worth the ticket.
3. **Leaving before sunset:** the light on the Onyar and the walls is the best part of the day.
4. **Coming during Temps de Flors (mid-May) without booking:** the city fills up.
