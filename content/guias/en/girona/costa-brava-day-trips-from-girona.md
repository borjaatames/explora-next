**Girona is the best base for the Costa Brava.** In under an hour you can reach Dalí's Figueres and Cadaqués, medieval villages like Besalú and the prettiest coves on the coast. Only Figueres is easy by train.

## Day trips on your own

| Besalú | 45 min (car) | Medieval village and Romanesque bridge |

- **Dalí:** the Figueres Theatre-Museum, Cadaqués and the Portlligat house complement each other.
- **Medieval villages:** Besalú, Pals and Peratallada.
- **Coves:** Calella de Palafrugell, Llafranc, Tamariu and Begur's coves (Sa Riera, Aiguablava).

## Which trip to choose

- **No car, one day:** Figueres. Regional trains leave Girona about every half hour and take around 35 minutes; the museum is a 10-minute walk from Figueres station. The AVE takes 15 minutes but stops at Figueres-Vilafant, a 20-minute walk out.
- **You want to swim and see the coast from the water:** the [kayak and snorkel tour in S'Agaró](/en/cities/girona/activities/costa-brava-kayak-snorkel-sea-caves-sagaro), 35 minutes from Girona by car. Two hours, no experience needed, but you must be able to swim (minimum age 7).
- **With children:** the Medes Islands boat from L'Estartit. It lasts 1 h 30 min, the boat has underwater windows and there's a 15-minute swim stop.
- **Cadaqués without the hairpin road:** drive or take the direct Sarfa bus to Roses (just over an hour) and do the boat along Cap de Creus. You get 1 h 30 min in Cadaqués, enough for the old town and the seafront, not for Portlligat.

- **Villages and history by car:** Besalú in the morning and Figueres in the afternoon combine well. Pals and Peratallada pair with Calella or Begur.

## What to know before you go

- **The Dalí Theatre-Museum** costs from €18.50 (as of 2026; free under 9) with timed entry that **sells out in high season**: book on the official website the week before. Allow 2 hours. It **closes on Mondays** except in July and August, when it opens daily from 9:00 to 20:00; the rest of the year it opens at 9:30 or 10:30 and closes at 18:00.
- **Dalí's house in Portlligat** (Cadaqués) can only be visited with advance booking, in small groups entering every 10 minutes. Collect your ticket 30 minutes before your slot or you can lose it. It closes from early January to mid-February, and backpacks and strollers aren't allowed inside.
- **Cadaqués in summer:** the only road in is a winding one and the village closes its centre to traffic. Arrive before 10:00 or leave the car in the car park at the entrance and walk.
- **The coves of Begur and Palafrugell** have small car parks that fill by 10:30 in July and August. Arrive by 9:00, use the shuttle buses Begur runs in summer, or go late in the afternoon, when the light is best.
- **Empúries:** €6 (free on the first Sunday of the month and for under-16s). Bring a swimsuit: the beach is right next to the ruins.
- **Besalú:** the bridge and the old town are free; the mikveh (medieval Jewish bath) can only be seen on the tourist office's guided tour, which is worth booking.

## Getting around

Figueres is easy by train. For Cadaqués, the villages and the coves you need a car or an organised trip: Sarfa buses leave Girona for the coast, but they take over an hour and run less often outside summer.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Not booking the Dalí Museum or the Portlligat house:** both sell out in season, and the second doesn't admit visitors without a booking.
2. **Going to Figueres on a Monday outside July and August:** the museum is closed.
3. **Cramming two long trips into one day:** Cadaqués or the coves fill a whole day; Figueres and Besalú can be combined.
4. **Heading to the coves at midday in August:** there's no parking; go early or late.
5. **Relying on public transport for the coast:** the train reaches Figueres, but Cadaqués, Begur and Besalú need a car or an organised trip.
6. **Driving to Cadaqués with children who get carsick:** it's 20 minutes of bends from Roses. The boat from Roses avoids the road and passes Cap de Creus.
