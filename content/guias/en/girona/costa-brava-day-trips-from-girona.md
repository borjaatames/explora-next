## Figueres and the Dalí Theatre-Museum

It's the easiest trip and the only one that works well without a car. The **Dalí Theatre-Museum** is a 15-minute walk from the station.

- **Ticket:** from **€18.50** (2026; free up to age 8), with a time slot that **sells out in season**. Book on the official website the week before.
- **Guided tour:** from **€31**, 1 hour, with skip-the-line entry.
- **Time:** about **2 hours** to see it all. The Lincoln painting and the Mae West Room are what everyone looks for.

## Cadaqués and Dalí's house in Portlligat

**Cadaqués**, the white village of Cap de Creus, completes the Dalí route together with Figueres and the **Portlligat House-Museum**.

- **The Portlligat house** can **only be visited with advance booking**, in small groups entering every 10 minutes. Without a booking you won't get in even in low season, and it isn't included in other plans.
- **By car:** the only access is a winding road (20 minutes from Roses), and in summer the village closes its centre to traffic. Arrive before 10:00 or leave the car in the car park at the entrance and walk.
- **By boat:** from Roses via Cap de Creus, about 3 h 30 in total with 1 h 30 free in Cadaqués. It avoids the road, ideal if anyone gets carsick.

## Besalú and the medieval villages

**Besalú**, 45 minutes away, is the area's great medieval village, with its Romanesque bridge. The bridge and the quarter are free; the **mikveh** (medieval Jewish bath) can only be seen on the tourist office's guided tour, which is worth booking.

In the same vein, **Pals** and **Peratallada** are stone villages that fit nicely on the way to the coast. Figueres and Besalú can be combined in one day.

## The Costa Brava coves, Tossa de Mar and Empúries

- **Coves:** Calella de Palafrugell, Llafranc, Tamariu and those of Begur (Sa Riera, Aiguablava). Their car parks are small and fill up by around **10:30** in July and August: arrive before 9:00, use the shuttle buses Begur runs in summer or go late in the afternoon, when the light is best.
- **From the sea:** kayaking and snorkelling through the S'Agaró caves, or a boat to the **Medes Islands** from L'Estartit.
- **Tossa de Mar:** the walled Vila Vella above the beach, an hour away by car.
- **Empúries (L'Escala):** Greek and Roman ruins for **€7** (free for under-16s and on the first Sunday of the month, except in July, August and September). Bring a swimsuit: the beach is right next to the ruins.

## Tips for planning your trips

- **Book the Dalí Museum and the Portlligat house:** both sell out in season and the latter doesn't admit visitors without a booking.
- **Don't squeeze two distant trips into one day:** Cadaqués or the coves already fill a day.
- **Avoid the coves at midday in August:** there's no parking.
- **Don't rely on public transport for the coast:** the train reaches Figueres, but Cadaqués, Begur or Besalú need a car or a tour.
- **If you're staying in Barcelona,** there are day trips that combine Girona, Figueres and Cadaqués, or Pals and Calella de Palafrugell.
