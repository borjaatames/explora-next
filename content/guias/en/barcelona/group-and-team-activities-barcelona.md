## The Sagrada Familia, privately

An **official guide just for your group**, with headsets, explains the Nativity and Passion façades outside and the nave, tree-like columns and stained glass inside; afterwards you can stay in the basilica. Skip-the-line tickets are included. Shoulders and knees must be covered to go in. The start time may shift depending on ticket availability: the provider confirms it.

## Team dinner: private tapas and wine

Three hours through the Gothic Quarter and old town with a food guide just for you: **four venues**, with tapas paired with regional wines at each. Departures are usually around 6 pm, and allergies and vegetarian options are catered for if you mention them on arrival. If the group wants a show, the **private tapas, wine and flamenco tour** ends with flamenco in a medieval palace and works out at about €88 per person from 4 people.

## Private trip to Montserrat

A vehicle and guide just for your group, with **hotel pick-up and drop-off**, monastery entry and a walk in the natural park, stopping where you like (Holy Cave, Sant Miquel Cross or viewpoints). It takes five hours. Book **at least three days ahead**. If the group would prefer a full day with cava, the private **Montserrat and Codorníu cava** trip lasts eight hours, with a train through the cellars and a tasting of two cavas. We compare every option in [which Montserrat trip to choose](/en/guides/barcelona/montserrat-which-excursion-to-choose).

## Team building: a beer tasting just for your group

This is the plan most geared to companies: a **tasting of six craft beers** with a beer guide on a rooftop on Passeig de Gràcia, for private groups only, followed by your favourites from the bar. Sessions start every half hour between 2 pm and 4 pm, the tasting is in English and it is **non-refundable**. For something livelier, the **salsa class with sangria** on another rooftop on the same avenue (€48.60) is a shared group.

## A whole day sorted

The **private best-of-Barcelona tour** lasts about seven hours, with hotel pick-up, private transport and a dedicated guide through Montjuïc, the Gothic Quarter and Passeig de Gràcia, plus a guided visit inside the Sagrada Família. It takes **6 people at most**: for six, it comes to €169 each.

## Practical tips

- **Book ahead:** two or three weeks for groups of more than 6, and at least three days for private Montserrat.
- **One person coordinates:** they book and share each activity's meeting point with the group.
- **Combine:** the Sagrada Familia in the morning and private tapas in the evening make an unhurried full day.
- **City rules:** since 29 October 2025, tours of several bars whose main purpose is drinking have been banned in Barcelona. A guided food tour or a tasting at one venue is a different kind of plan, but do not present your outing as a drinking crawl.

## Common mistakes

- **Comparing a private tour's "from" price with a group tour's per-person price:** for 4 to 6 people, the private Sagrada Familia visit costs about the same per head as a group visit.
- **Booking the beer tasting before the guest list is final:** there are no refunds.
- **Turning up at the Sagrada Familia in a vest top or shorts:** you may be refused entry to the basilica.
