## Sunset sailing

Two hours on a **Beneteau Oceanis 41** from Marina Vela in Port Vell: you motor out past cruise ships and superyachts, the skipper raises the sails and you cruise along the coast as the city lights come on. On board there is an **open bar of cava, wine and beer** and snacks; you can help sail or do nothing at all. It is a small group, **12 people at most**.

- €55 per person; minimum age 8.
- Bring a light layer for when the sun goes down.
- Weather dependent: if cancelled, you get another date or a full refund.

## A full evening: private tapas, wine and flamenco

A guide just for you spends **two hours in the Gothic Quarter and El Born** and seats you at a traditional restaurant for tapas, a glass of wine (or beer or vermouth) and a glass of cava each. It ends with **skip-the-line entry to a flamenco show in a medieval palace**, with a drink. Vegetarian option available. For two, it works out at about €114 each. You meet on La Rambla by the Frederic Soler "Pitarra" monument.

## Flamenco in a 17th-century palace

**Palau Dalmases** is on Carrer Montcada in El Born, a few steps from the Picasso Museum. The show lasts 50 to 55 minutes, with performances at **5.30 pm, 6.45 pm, 8 pm and 9.15 pm**, so it fits before or after dinner in the neighbourhood. Zone B costs €30; zone A (€40) and VIP (€50) include a drink and are closer to the stage. Capacity is limited: book ahead for weekends and summer.

If you would rather eat during the show, **Tablao de Carmen**, inside Poble Espanyol, serves dinner while the dancing happens, from €88 per person. We compare them in [flamenco in Barcelona: which venue to choose](/en/guides/barcelona/flamenco-barcelona-which-tablao-to-choose).

## Gaudí by night

- **Casa Batlló by night (€59):** an audio-guided visit plus a **one-hour live concert on the rooftop**, with a reserved table and a glass of cava. The style changes by date (jazz, soul or flamenco). It is **non-refundable** and in summer sells out one or two weeks ahead.
- **La Pedrera by night (€39.50):** a semi-guided visit with projections in the courtyards, an audiovisual show on the rooftop among the chimneys and a glass of cava at the end. In heavy rain the rooftop may close; if so, they reschedule or refund.

## By day: wine with a view and photos

- **Rooftop wine tasting:** six Spanish wines (sparkling, whites, rosé and reds) with snacks, on a rooftop overlooking Plaça de Catalunya and Passeig de Gràcia. Sessions between noon and 6 pm, €48 per person.
- **VIP private photo shoot:** an hour with a professional photographer around the city's best-known spots, including the outside of the Sagrada Familia, and 45 to 50 edited photos for €125 per session, not per person.

## A perfect day for two

1. **Morning:** photo shoot with the Sagrada Familia as a backdrop.
2. **Midday:** rooftop wine tasting.
3. **Sunset:** sailing from Port Vell.
4. **Night:** flamenco at Palau Dalmases and dinner in El Born; ideas in [eating in Barcelona with criteria](/en/guides/barcelona/eating-in-barcelona-with-criteria).

## Practical tips

- **Book ahead** for the sailing trip and Casa Batlló by night between May and September.
- **Outside summer,** it is cooler on the water: bring a light jacket even on a nice day.
- **Proposals:** the photo shoot and the tapas tour are private. If you have something planned, tell the operator when you book.
- **Cancellation:** the sailing trip, private tour, Palau Dalmases, La Pedrera, wine tasting and photo shoot all offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before; Casa Batlló by night cannot be cancelled.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking the sail and flamenco back to back:** the boat returns to Marina Vela and El Born is a walk away. Leave some margin.
- **Buying Casa Batlló by night without checking that day's concert style.**
- **Rushing Tablao de Carmen:** your ticket includes Poble Espanyol from 4 pm, so make use of it before dinner.
