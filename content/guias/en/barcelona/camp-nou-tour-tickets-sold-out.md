## What each Camp Nou tour ticket includes

The stadium is still under construction, and that changes what you see. This is what's on offer in November 2026:

- **Barça Immersive Tour (from €28):** the interactive museum, with an audio guide in 14 languages; the **viewpoint over the works**; the "Spotify Camp Nou Live" 360º immersive room; and the Espai Barça project exhibition. **It doesn't include the pitch, dressing rooms, press room or tunnel.**
- **Total Experience (from €54):** the museum with virtual reality and Robokeeper.
- **Spotify Camp Nou Experience (from €69):** a guided visit with a panoramic view of the stadium and access to the **first tier**, normally closed because of the works. It includes the museum. There are sessions in Catalan, Spanish and English at 10:00, 12:00 and 14:00; the rest are in English. Be at **Access 15** 15 minutes before: if you're late, you miss the guided visit, though you can still enter the museum.
- **Match Day Tour (from €149):** match days only, a few hours before kick-off. You go onto the **pitch**, the competition areas and the directors' box. **It doesn't include a match ticket** and isn't wheelchair accessible.

The Experience and the Match Day Tour are sold on the club's website.

## If the Camp Nou tour is sold out

1. **Buy the museum's flexible ticket** (€38): it has no fixed time and is valid until 28 February 2027.
2. **Try another slot.** The quietest is usually **Saturday from 15:00 to 17:00**, unless there's a match.
3. **Avoid match days:** the museum opens with reduced hours. On 8 December, for example, it closes at 15:00.
4. **Check an authorised reseller:** they sell the same official ticket, at a slightly higher price, with cancellation according to their terms.

## FC Barcelona Museum opening hours and getting there

- **Opening hours:** from 19 October to 19 March, **10:00 to 18:00**; from 20 March to 17 October, **9:30 to 19:00**. Closed on 1 January and 25 December. Last entry 45 minutes before closing.
- **Entrances:** Carrer Arístides Maillol (Access 15) and Avinguda Joan XXIII (Access 9). There's no parking for the tour.
- **Metro:** L5 Collblanc or Badal (about 5 minutes' walk) and L3 Palau Reial or Les Corts (about 10 minutes).
- **Accessibility:** the museum is wheelchair accessible. On the Experience, wheelchairs can reach the panoramic viewpoint but not the stands, which have steps. The 360º room isn't recommended for people with epilepsy or photosensitivity.

## Tips

- **Don't expect to see the dressing rooms:** with the works they aren't included in any ticket, except partly on the Match Day Tour.
- **Check the fixture list** before booking: the museum closes earlier and it's much busier.
- **Be on time for the Experience:** the guided visit doesn't wait.
- **Don't trust old blogs:** many describe the pre-works tour, with the pitch and press room.
