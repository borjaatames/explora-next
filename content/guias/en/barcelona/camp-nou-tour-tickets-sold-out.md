Barça's stadium is still under construction, and that changes what you see on the tour. Since the team returned to the Spotify Camp Nou, the basic visit is the renovated museum, with a viewpoint over the works; to get into the stands or onto the pitch there are separate products. This is what's on offer in November 2026 and what to do if your time slot sells out.

## Which ticket should you choose?

## 1. What each ticket includes

- **Barça Immersive Tour (from €28):** the interactive museum, with an audio guide in 14 languages; the **viewpoint over the works**; the "Spotify Camp Nou Live" 360º immersive room; and the Espai Barça project exhibition. It does not include the pitch, dressing rooms, press room or tunnel.
- **Spotify Camp Nou Experience (from €69):** a guided visit with a panoramic view of the stadium and access to the lower tier, normally closed because of the works. Includes the museum. There are tours in Catalan, Spanish and English at 10:00, 12:00 and 14:00, and the rest are in English. You need to be at Access 15 15 minutes beforehand; if you're late, you miss the guided visit but can still enter the museum.
- **Match Day Tour (from €149):** match days only, a few hours before kick-off. You go onto the pitch, through the competition areas and into the directors' box. **It does not include a match ticket** and it is not wheelchair accessible.

**Museum prices:** general (ages 11 to 64) from €28; over-65s, students and children aged 4 to 10, from €21; ages 0 to 3, free.

## 2. If your time slot is sold out

- **Buy the museum's flexible ticket** (€38): it has no fixed time and is valid until 28 February 2027.
- **Look at another slot.** The quietest is usually Saturday from 15:00 to 17:00, unless there's a match.
- **Avoid match days.** The museum opens with reduced hours: on 8 December, for example, it closes at 15:00.

## 3. Opening hours and getting there

- **Opening hours:** from 19 October to 19 March, 10:00 to 18:00; from 20 March to 17 October, 9:30 to 19:00. Closed on 1 January and 25 December. Last entry 45 minutes before closing.
- **Entrances:** Carrer d'Arístides Maillol (Access 15) and Avinguda de Joan XXIII (Access 9). There is no parking for the tour.
- **Metro:** L5 Collblanc or Badal (about a 5-minute walk) and L3 Palau Reial or Les Corts (about 10 minutes).

The museum is wheelchair accessible. On the Experience, wheelchairs can reach the panoramic viewpoint but not the stands, which have steps. The 360º room is not recommended for people with epilepsy or photosensitivity.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Expecting to see the dressing rooms.** With the works, no ticket includes them, except the Match Day Tour, and only partly.
2. **Going on a match day without checking the hours.** The museum closes earlier and it's much busier.
3. **Arriving late for the Experience.** The guided visit doesn't wait.
4. **Trusting old blog posts.** Many describe the tour from before the works, with the pitch and press room.
