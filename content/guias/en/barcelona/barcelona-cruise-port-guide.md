## Barcelona cruise terminals

- **Moll Adossat:** terminals A, B and C; D and E (Carnival) and MSC's new terminal, open since 2025. Almost all ships dock here, a **25-45 minute walk** from Columbus depending on the terminal.
- **World Trade Center:** the Sud terminal, for smaller ships, **about 15 minutes' walk** from La Rambla. It stops taking cruise ships at the end of 2026.

The port is cutting back on terminals: WTC Nord closed in 2023 and Royal Caribbean is building a new one on the Moll Adossat for 2027. Your daily programme tells you which one is yours.

## From the cruise terminal to central Barcelona

- **Cruise Bus (formerly Portbus or T3):** links the Moll Adossat terminals with the Columbus monument area in about 5 minutes. **€3.50 one way or €5 for a day ticket** with unlimited rides: worth it if you'll go back to drop shopping on the ship. You pay by card and **TMB tickets aren't valid**.
- **On foot:** 25-45 minutes along a quay with no shade. Only with time to spare and good weather.
- **Taxi:** to Columbus, about 10 minutes and €12-15. Metropolitan tariff: €2.80 flag fall, €1.35 per km on weekdays from 8am to 8pm and €1.66 at night, on Saturdays and holidays, plus a **€4.60 supplement** for pickups at the Moll Adossat.
- **Metro:** from Drassanes (L3), at the bottom of La Rambla. A single ticket is €2.90; the T-casual costs €13 for 10 rides but it is personal: each traveller needs their own (groups can share a T-familiar).
- **Cruise line shuttle:** check where it stops and whether it charges.

**To the Sagrada Família from the cruise port:** it's about 3 km from Columbus. By metro, L3 from Drassanes to Diagonal and L5 to Sagrada Família, about 20-25 minutes. A direct taxi from the ship (15-25 minutes, about €20-25) saves you the change.

## What to see in Barcelona on a cruise stop

**With 4 hours.** Two options: La Rambla, La Boqueria, the Gothic Quarter, the Cathedral and El Born, all on foot from Columbus; or just the Sagrada Família, by taxi there and back.

**With 6 hours.** The Sagrada Família with a booked ticket, Passeig de Gràcia (Casa Batlló and La Pedrera from outside) and a walk back down through the Gothic Quarter to Columbus.

**With 8 hours.** The Sagrada Família and Park Güell with tickets, plus the Gothic Quarter; or Montserrat, if the ship is in port for 8-9 hours.

The **Sagrada Família only sells online** and in summer sells out weeks ahead. Allow 1 h 30 to 2 hours inside and choose a slot that leaves plenty of time to get back to the ship. The official ticket costs €26 on sagradafamilia.org (with the audio guide app); resellers charge from €34.

## Ship excursion, private or on your own

- **Cruise line excursion:** the price is set by the cruise line, but **the ship waits for its own excursions**.
- **Excursion with port pickup:** the operator plans around your departure time, but the ship won't wait. Always tell them your all-aboard time when booking.
- **Private:** the private Sagrada Família tour with an official guide costs **€340 per group** of 1-2 people (€480 for 4), tickets included; with **cruise port pickup** it's €390 (€560 for 4) and **only covers the outward trip**. The private full day with the Sagrada Família and Park Güell (6 h 30, from about €196 per person for two) starts on La Rambla, near the Cruise Bus, and recommends the earliest start for cruise passengers.
- **On your own:** Cruise Bus, metro and tickets; all the risk is yours.

## Getting back to the ship on time

- **All aboard** is usually 30 to 60 minutes before departure, in **ship's time**.
- **Rule of thumb:** be at the terminal **1 hour before all aboard** and leave the centre with **1 h 30 to spare**: between traffic and the late-afternoon Cruise Bus queue, the margin goes fast.
- Carry your cruise card and ID. Watch out for pickpockets on La Rambla and the metro.

## Sundays, holidays and common mistakes

- **Sunday:** the Sagrada Família opens at 10:30, not 9:00; La Boqueria is closed; the Picasso Museum is free on the first Sunday of every month, with advance booking on its website.
- **Shops:** in Ciutat Vella and the Eixample they can open on Sundays and holidays **from 15 May to 15 September, 12:00 to 20:00**; outside those dates most close except on authorised Sundays.

Mistakes we see often:

1. **Turning up at the Sagrada Família without a ticket.** There's no ticket office: it's online or nothing.
2. **Walking from the Moll Adossat at midday in summer.** Up to 45 minutes with no shade.
3. **Trying Montserrat with 6 hours.** There isn't time with a safe margin.
4. **Paying for the Cruise Bus with a TMB ticket.** It doesn't work.
5. **Booking the private tour with pickup thinking it includes the return.** It only covers the outward trip.
