Barcelona is the Mediterranean's biggest cruise port and, unlike Málaga or Cádiz, **the ship doesn't drop you in the centre**: the big terminals sit at the end of a long breakwater. What you manage to see comes down to two things: how you get out of the port, and whether you've already got your Sagrada Família ticket.

## Which terminal will you dock at?

| Area | Terminals | Distance to Columbus and La Rambla |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moll Adossat | A, B and C; D and E (Carnival); MSC's new terminal, open since 2025 | 25-45 minutes' walk depending on the terminal |
| World Trade Center | Terminal Sud, for smaller ships, until the end of 2026 | About 15 minutes' walk |

The port is cutting back on terminals: WTC Nord closed in 2023, WTC Sud stops taking cruise ships at the end of 2026, and Royal Caribbean is building a new one on the Moll Adossat for 2027. Your daily programme tells you which one is yours.

## Getting into town

| Option | Time | Cost | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cruise Bus | About 5 min to La Rambla | €3.50 one way, €5 day ticket | Card payment; TMB tickets not valid |
| Walking | 25-45 min | Free | No shade on the quay; only with time and good weather |
| Taxi to Columbus | About 10 min | About €12-15 | €4.60 supplement for pickups at the Moll Adossat |
| Taxi to the Sagrada Família | 15-25 min | About €20-25 | Dearer at night, on Saturdays and holidays |
| Cruise line shuttle | Varies | Set by your cruise line | Check where it stops and what it costs |

- **The Cruise Bus** (formerly the Portbus or T3) links the Moll Adossat terminals with the area around the Columbus Monument. The day ticket lets you go back and forth, so it's the one to buy if you want to drop shopping off on board.
- **Taxis** use the metropolitan tariff: €2.80 flag fall, €1.35 per km on weekdays from 8am to 8pm and €1.66 at night, on Saturdays and holidays, plus the **€4.60 Moll Adossat supplement**.
- **The metro** leaves from Drassanes (L3) at the bottom of La Rambla. A single costs €2.90 and a T-casual €13 for 10 journeys, which a group can share.

**To the Sagrada Família:** it's about 3 km from Columbus as the crow flies. By metro, take L3 from Drassanes to Diagonal and L5 to Sagrada Família, about 20-25 minutes. From the ship, a direct taxi saves you the change.

## What to see in 4, 6 or 8 hours

| Time ashore | Plan | What to book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 hours | Option A: La Rambla, La Boqueria, the Gothic Quarter, the Cathedral and El Born, all on foot from Columbus. Option B: just the Sagrada Família, by taxi there and back | Sagrada Família ticket, from €34 |
| 6 hours | Sagrada Família with a pre-booked ticket, Passeig de Gràcia (Casa Batlló and La Pedrera from outside) and a walk back down through the Gothic Quarter | 2-hour private Gothic and Born tour, from €54 |
| 8 hours | Sagrada Família and Park Güell with tickets plus the Gothic Quarter; or Montserrat with port pickup if the ship is in for 8-9 hours | Barcelona and Montserrat from the port, from €149 |

**The Sagrada Família only sells tickets online** and in summer they go weeks ahead. Allow 1 h 30 to 2 hours inside and choose a slot that leaves plenty of time to get back to the ship.

If you'd like an official guide and a pickup from the ship, the **private Sagrada Família tour** costs **€340 per group** for 1-2 people (€480 for 4), tickets included. The option with **cruise port pickup** goes up to €390 (€560 for 4) and **only covers the outward trip**: getting back to the ship is up to you.

## Montserrat from the ship?

Montserrat is **an hour by road**. There and back plus the monastery adds up to at least 5 hours, so it only makes sense with **8-9 hours in port**. The **Barcelona and Montserrat small-group tour** (maximum 12, English-speaking guide) picks up at the cruise port, lasts 8 hours and costs **from €149 per person**. You see the Sagrada Família from outside; going in costs €25 extra and has to be requested from the operator.

## Ship's tour or private?

| Option | Guide price | If something runs late |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cruise line excursion | Whatever your cruise line charges | The ship waits for its own tours |
| Tour with port pickup | From €149 per person (Montserrat) | The operator plans around your sailing time, but the ship won't wait |
| Private with pickup | From €390 per group (Sagrada Família) | You set the timetable; the return may be up to you |
| Independent | Cruise Bus, metro and tickets | All the risk is yours |

The ship only waits if the tour is its own. With an independent operator, timing is their responsibility, so always give them your all-aboard time when booking.

## What time should you be back on board?

- **All aboard** is usually 30-60 minutes before sailing, in **ship's time**.
- **Rule of thumb:** be at the terminal **an hour before all aboard** and leave the centre with **1 h 30 to spare**: between traffic and the late-afternoon queue for the Cruise Bus, your margin goes fast.
- Take your cruise card and photo ID. Watch out for pickpockets on La Rambla and the metro, and carry only what you need.

## Sundays and public holidays

- **Sagrada Família:** opens at 10.30am on Sundays, not 9am.
- **La Boqueria:** closed on Sundays.
- **Picasso Museum:** free on the first Sunday of the month, booked in advance on its website.
- **Shops:** in Ciutat Vella and the Eixample they can open on Sundays and holidays **from 15 May to 15 September, noon to 8pm**. Outside those dates most close, apart from the authorised Sundays in the calendar.

## Common mistakes

1. **Turning up at the Sagrada Família without a ticket.** There's no ticket office: online or nothing.
2. **Walking from the Moll Adossat at midday in summer.** Up to 45 minutes with no shade.
3. **Trying Montserrat with 6 hours.** There's no safe margin.
4. **Trying to use a TMB ticket on the Cruise Bus.** It isn't valid: buy its own ticket.
5. **Booking the private tour with pickup and assuming it includes the return.** It's outward only.
