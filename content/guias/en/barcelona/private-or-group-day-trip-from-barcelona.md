**From Barcelona, a group trip to Montserrat costs €47-149 per person and a private one €149-419, depending on whether you are 2, 4 or 6.** What makes Barcelona interesting is that the private Montserrat tour is priced almost flat per group: going from 2 to 6 people only adds €56 to the total. So here, unlike Madrid or Seville, there is a point where private catches up with the group tour.

## Quick answer by who's travelling

- **Couple:** group tour. Montserrat on a [half-day small-group tour](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-half-day-small-group) from €59 or with [CastlExperience](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-rack-railway-winery-castlexperience) from €47. Private would cost you €838.
- **Family of 4:** depends how much you value being on your own. A guided private trip works out at €186-219 each; group tours at €47-109.
- **Party of 6:** private. The [private tour with pickup](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/montserrat-private-tour-hotel-pickup) comes to €149 each, the same as the 12-person small group.
- **First time:** half day in a small group. It reaches the monastery before the big coaches and leaves your afternoon free in Barcelona.
- **Tight budget:** CastlExperience (€47) or on your own with the Trans Montserrat ticket (from €50).

## How many people before private costs the same?

**For Montserrat, 6.** The private tour with guide and pickup (€149 each) matches the Barcelona and Montserrat small group (€149) and is €40 more than the farmhouse lunch trip (€109). Against the cheapest option (€47), it's still €102 more per person.

Mind what you're comparing: the 12-person tour also includes a drive round Barcelona (Sagrada Família from outside, Montjuïc, El Born), and the farmhouse trip a lunch with wine pairing. The private tour is 5 hours with guide, vehicle and monastery entry.

## Pros and cons

### Group tours

- **For:** price and review volume (the half-day small group has over 6,000). Some include the rack railway, a winery or lunch.
- **Against:** a fixed early start (8:30 on the half day), groups of 20-22 per guide on the cheapest formats and set free time at the monastery.

### Private trips

- **For:** hotel pickup, your own schedule, a guide just for you and combinations you won't find in a group: Codorníu cava, Gaudí's crypt, Girona with Besalú and Rupit.
- **Against:** expensive for a couple. And not all use the rack railway: the private tour drives you up to the monastery in its own vehicle.

## Who each one suits

- **Group:** couples, solo travellers, first visits with little time.
- **Semi-private or 12-person group:** those who want a small group without paying for private.
- **Private:** families of 4 or more, groups of 5-6 friends, older travellers, and anyone wanting Montserrat plus wine or Gaudí in one day.

## Common mistakes

1. **Looking only at the private "from" price.** €838 looks prohibitive; for 6 people it's €149 each.
2. **Assuming private means the rack railway.** It's a car or van; if you want the train ride, pick a tour that includes it.
3. **Going up on a foggy day with no plan B.** It's several degrees colder on the mountain than in Barcelona.
4. **Booking the 12-person tour expecting to go inside the Sagrada Família.** You see it from outside; entry is requested separately from the operator.
5. **Forgetting the child seat.** On private and semi-private car tours you must request it when booking (€8 supplement).
