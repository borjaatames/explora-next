## Private or group day trip: quick answer by who's travelling

- **Couple:** group. Montserrat on a half-day small-group tour from €59 or with CastlExperience from €47. Private would cost you €838.
- **Family of 4:** depends on how much you value being on your own. A private tour with a guide works out at **€186-219 per person**; group tours, €47-109.
- **Group of 6:** private. The private tour with hotel pickup comes to **€149 per person**, the same as the small group of 12.
- **First time:** a half-day small-group tour. It reaches the monastery before the big coaches and leaves your afternoon free in Barcelona.
- **Tight budget:** CastlExperience (€47) or on your own with the Trans Montserrat ticket (from €50).

## Pros and cons, and who each one suits

**Group tour**

- **Pros:** price and volume of reviews (the half-day small group has over 6,000). Some include the rack railway, a winery or lunch.
- **Cons:** fixed early departure (8:30 AM on the half day), groups of 20-22 people per guide on the cheapest formats and set free time at the monastery.
- **Suits:** couples, solo travellers and first visits with little time.

**Semi-private or group of 12**

- The semi-private Montserrat tour is a group of **up to 8 people** leaving from Plaça d'Espanya, by car with a guide and tickets for the basilica and the Black Madonna, from about €121. For anyone who wants a small group without paying for private.

**Private**

- **Pros:** hotel pickup, your own schedule, a guide just for you and combinations that don't exist as group tours: Codorníu cava cellars, Gaudí's crypt, **Girona with Besalú and Rupit** (groups of up to 8).
- **Cons:** expensive for a couple. And not all include the rack railway: private tours drive their own vehicle up to the monastery.
- **Suits:** families of 4 or more, groups of 5-6 friends, older travellers and anyone wanting to combine Montserrat with wine or Gaudí on the same day.

## Common mistakes

1. **Looking only at the private "from" price.** €838 looks prohibitive; with 6 people it's €149 each.
2. **Assuming the private tour goes up by rack railway.** It goes by car or van; if you're keen on the rack railway, pick a tour that includes it.
3. **Going to Montserrat on a foggy day without a plan B.** It's several degrees cooler on the mountain than in Barcelona.
4. **Booking the group of 12 expecting to go inside the Sagrada Família.** You see it from outside; entry is requested separately from the operator.
5. **Forgetting the child seat.** On private and semi-private car tours you have to request it when booking (€8 supplement).
