Three days is the right number for a first visit to Barcelona: Gaudí properly, two neighbourhoods with character, Montjuïc and the sea. With two you must choose between Gaudí and the neighbourhoods; with four, add Montserrat. Only one day? See [Barcelona in 1 day](/en/guides/barcelona/barcelona-in-1-day).

**2026 is Gaudí Year** (centenary of his death) and the Tower of Jesus is being completed: Sagrada Familia tickets sell out earlier than ever.

## The plan at a glance

| Day | Morning | Afternoon | Evening |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sagrada Familia | Casa Batlló, Passeig de Gràcia | Dinner around Aribau |
| 2 | Park Güell | Gràcia | Sunset at the Bunkers del Carmel |
| 3 | Gothic Quarter, El Born | Barceloneta, Montjuïc | Sunset at MNAC, Magic Fountain |

## When to go, how to get there and where to stay

| Season | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| May, June, September, first half of October | Ideal: 20–28°C, warm sea in September |
| November | Underrated: good light, empty museums |
| July and August | Avoid: sticky heat and queues |
| Mobile World Congress (late February or March) | Avoid: hotels triple their prices |

- **Airport:** R2 Nord train to Sants or Passeig de Gràcia (€5, 25 minutes), Aerobús (€7.25) or taxi (€30–35). **AVE:** 2h 30min from Madrid to Sants (€50–90 booked ahead). **Car:** don't; parking is €25–35 a night and the centre is a low-emission zone.
- **Getting around:** the T-casual card gives 10 journeys for €13 (as of 2026; a single ticket is €2.90) and covers the Montjuïc funicular. The metro closes at midnight on weekdays, at 2 AM on Fridays and runs all night on Saturdays.
- **Where to stay:** Eixample, El Born, Sant Antoni or Gràcia. **Avoid** Las Ramblas and Plaça de Catalunya (expensive, noisy, pickpockets), Barceloneta and the northern Raval at night.

## Day 1: Sagrada Familia, Eixample and Casa Batlló

**9:30 AM — Sagrada Familia.** Book weeks ahead on the official site (release is 60 days ahead, and it sells out in high season): €26 with audio guide, €36 with a tower, €30 guided. The **Hour of Silence** (9–10 AM) is the quietest slot. With the Carmel and Montjuïc views coming, the towers are optional: the stained glass is the reason to go in. Cover your shoulders. Details in [how to visit Sagrada Familia](/en/guides/barcelona/sagrada-familia-how-to-visit).

[actividad: sagrada-familia-ticket-audio-guide]

**Noon — Lunch:** a *menú del día* (€15–18) on a side street off Provença.

**2 PM — Block of Discord** (Passeig de Gràcia at Aragó): Casa Batlló, Casa Amatller and, two streets up, La Pedrera. Enter one: **Casa Batlló** (€29; €35 skip-the-line) is the better-produced visit; La Pedrera (€28) is more austere and, for architecture lovers, more interesting.

**8:30 PM — Dinner** around Aribau and Enric Granados, where locals eat.

## Day 2: Park Güell, Gràcia and sunset at the Carmel

**10 AM — Park Güell.** Book online: the Monumental Zone costs €18 (€7 ages 7–12, under-7s free) with a 30-minute entry slot; the rest of the park is free. Metro L3 to Lesseps or Vallcarca and a 15–20 minute climb, or bus 24 to the gate (from Sagrada Familia it's 45 minutes uphill on foot). Allow 1h 30min plus half an hour for the **Turó de les Tres Creus**, a free 360° viewpoint at the top. Short of time? Cut this first.

**1 PM — Lunch in Gràcia**, 20 minutes downhill, where residents eat: around Plaça del Sol, Plaça de la Vila and Carrer de Verdi and Torrijos, in small places rather than big terraces.

**7 PM — Bunkers del Carmel** (Turó de la Rovira): Civil War anti-aircraft batteries, free, with the best panorama of the city. Metro L5 to El Carmel and 20–25 minutes uphill, or bus V17. Arrive 30 minutes before sunset (9–9:30 PM in summer). It's residential: keep the noise down and take your rubbish. Dinner back in Gràcia.

## Day 3: Gothic Quarter, El Born, the sea and Montjuïc

**9:30 AM — Gothic Quarter**, before the crowds. **Barcelona Cathedral** (Plaça Nova; the paid cultural visit adds choir, cloister and rooftop), medieval **Plaça del Rei** and **Plaça Sant Felip Neri**, with Civil War shrapnel marks. The Carrer del Bisbe bridge is from 1928: skip the photo queue.

**11:30 AM — El Born.** The Mercat del Born (ruins from 1714 under the 19th-century market, free), **Santa Maria del Mar** (the purest Catalan Gothic; free during worship hours, €5 with the rooftops) and, two streets away, the Picasso Museum (€14, free Thursday afternoons, closed Mondays).

**2 PM — Lunch:** Catalan cooking in El Born or Sant Antoni. Not paella in Barceloneta: pre-cooked rice and frozen seafood at high prices.

**5 PM — Montjuïc**, after a short beach walk. Funicular from Paral·lel (T-casual) or the port cable car (€12–13 return).

| Montjuïc | Price | For whom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Castell de Montjuïc | €12 | Port views, quick visit |
| MNAC | €12 (free Saturdays from 3 PM) | Essential if you like Romanesque art |
| Fundació Joan Miró | €14 | If you care about Miró |

The MNAC esplanade at sunset has the best horizontal view of the city. The **Magic Fountain** runs Thursday to Saturday nights (Friday to Sunday in summer) but closes for whole months for drought or maintenance: check first. Dinner at 10 PM in Sant Antoni.

## What we don't recommend

- **Las Ramblas** for walking or eating: cross once and move on.
- **La Boqueria at midday.** Go before 10 AM or after 7 PM; eating inside costs double a neighbourhood market.
- **Fixed-price tapas tours** (€50–60, commission bars, tiny portions).
- **Casa Vicens** (€22) on a first visit, **"flamenco experiences"** (flamenco isn't Catalan) and the **Bus Turístic** (€30–35 a day to move slower than the metro).

## Common mistakes

1. **The wrong shoes:** expect 12–15 km a day on cobbles and slopes.
2. **Eating too early:** lunch is 2–4 PM, dinner 9–11 PM.
3. **Pickpockets** on Las Ramblas and the metro: backpack on your front, phone never in a back pocket.
4. **Montserrat on day one.** Save it for a 4–5 day visit.

## Dates to plan around

**Sant Jordi** (April 23, books and roses), **Festes de Gràcia** (mid-August) and **La Mercè** (September 24, castellers, free concerts, fireworks).
