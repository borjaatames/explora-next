## What you'll see in the Monumental Zone

You only need a ticket for the **Monumental Zone**, the part Gaudí actually finished:

- **The two entrance pavilions** and the **staircase with the trencadís salamander**, the park's most photographed spot.
- **The Hypostyle Room**, a forest of Doric columns designed as a market hall.
- **The great square** above it, edged by the **wavy bench** covered in broken ceramic tiles, with views over the city.
- **The stone porticoes and viaducts**, which imitate natural shapes and follow the slope.

Allow **about 1 h 30** for this area. The Gaudí House Museum, where the architect lived, is not included.

## Prices and time slots on the official website

Capacity is **700 people every half hour**, and in summer, Easter and long weekends the best slots sell out days or weeks ahead. The official website is parkguell.barcelona:

- **General:** €18.
- **Ages 7 to 12 and over 65:** €13.50.
- **Ages 0 to 6:** free, but they need a ticket because they count towards capacity.
- **Tourist time slots:** 9:30 to 19:30. Tickets go on sale **up to three months ahead** and **can't be cancelled**.

If it says "sold out", don't give up at the first try:

- **Check several days in a row.** Individual tickets can be moved to another date up to the day before, so slots free up.
- **Be flexible with the time.** Early-morning and late-afternoon slots are the most popular; midday ones last longer, although it's hotter and there's less shade.
- **If you're a group, split the purchase.** There may be room for two in one slot and two more in the next.

## Last-minute Park Güell tickets: resale and tours with entry

**Authorised resellers** buy allocations in advance, so they may still have places when the official website doesn't. They cost more: the Monumental Zone starts at **€21.50** and the combo with the **Gaudí House Museum** at **€28**. They can't be cancelled either, so only buy once your day is fixed.

If you'd also like someone to explain the park, a **tour with entry included** comes with the time slot already booked. The **Sagrada Familia and Park Güell** tour (from €104, 4 hours) includes both tickets, the guide and the transfer between the two, with **free cancellation up to 24 hours before**. Before paying, check that it says **"Park Güell ticket included"** and that the visit covers the Monumental Zone: some walks only go round the free part.

The park's official guided visits (from €17) won't help here: they're paid separately and you still need an entry ticket.

## Park Güell for free: the area without a ticket

The Monumental Zone is a small part of the park. The rest is **free to enter** and worth the climb:

- The **woodland paths** around the paid area, with shade and far fewer people.
- The **Turó de les Tres Creus viewpoint**, the highest point, with the best view of Barcelona and the sea.
- The **entrance houses**, which you can see from Carrer d'Olot without going in.

Note: the old trick of getting in free early no longer works. From **7:00 to 9:30 and 20:00 to 22:00** the park is only for residents and Passi Verd holders.

If what you want is Gaudí indoors, **Casa Batlló** and **La Pedrera** sell tickets with more notice, and the Sagrada Familia has its own options: we explain them in [what to do if Sagrada Familia tickets are sold out](/en/guides/barcelona/sagrada-familia-tickets-sold-out).

## How to get there

- **Buses H6 and D40.** The easiest entrance is **Carretera del Carmel 23**, with little climbing and a taxi rank; it's the one to use with a pushchair or wheelchair.
- **Lesseps metro (L3):** 15-20 minutes uphill. The Avinguda del Santuari gate has escalators.
- **Taxi from the centre:** €12-15.

Your ticket is valid at any of the four gates.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Buying on websites that imitate the official one.** They add surcharges, and the park doesn't stand behind tickets from unofficial channels.
2. **Booking a tour without checking it enters the Monumental Zone.** Walks around the free part are cheaper for a reason.
3. **Arriving late for your slot.** You can only enter within **30 minutes** of your time; after that you lose the ticket.
4. **Leaving to "come back later".** Once inside, you can't go out and back in.
