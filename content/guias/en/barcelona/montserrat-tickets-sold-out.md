The monastery of Montserrat is an hour from Barcelona, perched on the mountain, and the basilica is free to enter. The problem usually isn't the entry but **getting up there**: the rack railway and cable car have limited capacity, the car park at the top fills, and the Trans Montserrat and Tot Montserrat combined tickets sell out on weekends. No spaces left doesn't mean no Montserrat; here are the routes, in order.

## 1. How you get up and what sells out

From Barcelona you arrive on the **R5 FGC train** (Plaça Espanya) and then go up the mountain in one of two ways:

- **The rack railway**, from Monistrol de Montserrat, which drops you next to the monastery.
- **The cable car (Aeri)**, from the Montserrat-Aeri station, faster and with views.

What sells out on weekends is the capacity of both, the car park at the top and the combined tickets. If you go on your own, buy the pass in advance at FGC and go up early.

## 2. The Trans and Tot Montserrat passes

- **Trans Montserrat:** R5 train + rack railway or cable car + both funiculars (Sant Joan and Santa Cova).
- **Tot Montserrat:** all of the above + the museum and a self-service lunch.

They are sold at FGC (Plaça Espanya) and on the FGC website. On long weekends they can easily sell out, so don't leave them for the same day.

## 3. A trip with transport and transfers included

This is the safest option when there are no rack railway spaces or room in the car park: transport from Barcelona and the ascent are included and you don't depend on the passes. The rack railway and Black Madonna trip is the most chosen, with almost 10,000 reviews.

## 4. What to see once you're up

- **The basilica and the Black Madonna (La Moreneta):** entry is free; to touch the image there's a queue, especially at midday.
- **The Escolania**, the boys' choir, sings at set times almost every day (not in August or during the choir's holidays); check the schedule before going up.
- **The Sant Joan and Santa Cova funiculars**, for the views and the hermitages; they're included in the combined passes.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Driving up on a Saturday without an early start.** The car park at the top fills by mid-morning.
2. **Buying the pass on the day.** On busy days they're gone; buy it in advance at FGC.
3. **Going only for the Black Madonna.** The queue to touch it is long; plan the funiculars and the Escolania too.
4. **Not checking the Escolania schedule.** If you go for the choir and it doesn't sing that day, you miss it.
