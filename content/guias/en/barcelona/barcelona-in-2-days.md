## Barcelona 2-day itinerary at a glance

| Time | Day 1: Gaudí | Day 2: old city and the sea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | Sagrada Família (9:00) and Passeig de Gràcia | Gothic Quarter and Born |
| Lunch | Eixample | Born or Barceloneta |
| Afternoon | Gràcia and Park Güell (17:00) | Barceloneta and Montjuïc |
| Evening | Dinner in Gràcia | Tapas in Poble-sec or flamenco in the Born |

## Day 1: Gaudí from top to bottom

**9:00 — Sagrada Família** (1 h 30 min). First slot, with the morning light in the stained glass. Open 9:00 to 18:00 from November to February, until 19:00 in March and October and until 20:00 from April to September; on Saturdays it always closes at 18:00 and on Sundays it opens at 10:30. No vest tops, swimwear or shorts above mid-thigh. The towers (€10 extra) add 30 minutes; in two days, skip them. More in [how to visit the Sagrada Família](/en/guides/barcelona/sagrada-familia-how-to-visit).

[actividad: entrada-sagrada-familia-audioguia]

**11:00 — Passeig de Gràcia.** Metro L2 to Passeig de Gràcia (10 minutes) or a 25-minute walk. **Choose one house to go inside**: Casa Batlló (from €29, 1 h 15 min) or La Pedrera, 500 metres further up (from €29, with the chimney rooftop). See the other from outside.

**13:30 — Lunch in the Eixample.** Avoid Passeig de Gràcia itself: two streets either side there are more honest lunch menus.

**15:30 — Gràcia.** From La Pedrera, a 15-minute walk up Gran de Gràcia. A former village with its own squares: Plaça del Sol, Plaça de la Vila and the façade of **Casa Vicens**, Gaudí's first house.

**17:00 — Park Güell** (1 h 15 min). From Gràcia, a 20-25-minute uphill walk or a bus. Monumental zone **€18**, online only, with a 30-minute slot: the dragon, the Hypostyle Room and the wavy bench. From 25 October to 6 February the last entry is at 17:30, so don't be late. From April to October, if there's still light, go on up to the **Bunkers del Carmel** (20 minutes), which close at 19:30 in summer and 17:30 in winter. Ticket comparison in [which Park Güell ticket to choose](/en/guides/barcelona/park-guell-which-ticket-to-choose).

**20:30 — Dinner in Gràcia**, then metro L3 from Lesseps (15 minutes' walk from the park) back to the centre.

## Day 2: Gothic Quarter, Born, the sea and Montjuïc

**10:00 — Gothic Quarter.** The **Cathedral** (paid tourist visit with cloister and rooftops; the 13 geese of Santa Eulàlia live in the cloister), **Plaça del Rei** and **Plaça Sant Felip Neri**, with Civil War shrapnel marks on the façade.

**12:00 — The Born**, 10 minutes away across Via Laietana: **Santa Maria del Mar**, the purest Catalan Gothic, and Carrer Montcada. The Picasso Museum is here; if you want it, book a time slot and allow another 1 h 30 min.

**14:00 — Lunch in the Born**: Bar del Pla (book ahead) or Bodega La Puntual, on Montcada. Not at the Boqueria or on La Rambla.

**16:00 — Barceloneta**, a 15-minute walk: seafront promenade to the beach and Port Vell. In summer, a swim; in winter, a sunny terrace.

**18:00 — Sunset on Montjuïc.** Metro to Paral·lel and the Montjuïc funicular, included in the metro ticket. From the top, the **cable car to the castle** (€19 return) gives you the view of the port; for free, the MNAC steps look out over the whole city.

**21:00 — Evening.** Pintxos on Carrer Blai in Poble-sec, 15 minutes down from Montjuïc, or flamenco back in the Born, in a 17th-century palace (from €30).

## What to book and budget

| What | Official price | How far ahead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sagrada Família | €26 basic with audio guide; €36 with towers | 1-3 weeks |
| Park Güell (monumental zone) | €18 | 1 week; 30-minute slots |
| Casa Batlló or La Pedrera | from €29 | a few days |
| Flamenco | from €30 | 1-2 days |

**Rough budget per person, excluding accommodation: about €185-235** for two days (Sagrada Família and Park Güell, €44; one Gaudí house, from €29; T-casual, €13; cable car or flamenco, €19-30; meals, €80-120). Barcelona is the most expensive city for this kind of trip: in 2026 the average hotel price was around €195 a night in the first half of the year.

## Getting around and where to stay

- **On foot** in the centre and **by metro** for longer hops. The **10-journey T-casual (€13 in 2026)** is personal: each traveller needs their own. A single costs €2.90.
- **From Sants**, metro L3 or L5.
- **Where to stay:** the **Eixample** (near Passeig de Gràcia) is the most practical for this plan; the **Born** and **Gothic Quarter** have more atmosphere but more noise; **Gràcia** is more local and quieter.

## Tips and variations for a Barcelona weekend

- **Book the Sagrada Família and Park Güell** before you go, or you'll be left outside.
- **Don't cram Batlló, Pedrera, Sagrada Família and Park Güell** into the same day.
- **Pair Gràcia with Park Güell** so you don't go back and forth to the centre twice.
- **Watch your phone** on the metro and La Rambla: that's where pickpockets work.
- **If it rains:** swap Park Güell for La Pedrera (if you didn't do it) and day 2 for the Picasso Museum and the MNAC.
- **With kids:** Park Güell, the Montjuïc cable car and the beach work well. Ideas in [Barcelona with kids](/en/guides/barcelona/barcelona-with-kids).
- **In summer:** Barceloneta first thing or late, never at midday, and Park Güell in the latest slot.
