## Barcelona to Montserrat by train: the R5 from Plaça Espanya

The train is run by **FGC (Ferrocarrils de la Generalitat)**, not Renfe. It leaves from the FGC station at **Plaça Espanya**, under the square (metro L1 and L3). Take the **R5 towards Manresa**: it runs **every hour** and takes about an hour to Monistrol de Montserrat.

The train stops at both rides up, one after the other:

1. **Aeri de Montserrat:** get off here for the cable car.
2. **Monistrol de Montserrat:** the next stop, for the rack railway.

Buy your ticket from the FGC machines at Plaça Espanya. The Barcelona metro T-casual does not cover this trip.

## Rack railway or cable car: getting up to the monastery

**Montserrat rack railway (Cremallera).** It leaves from Monistrol de Montserrat (connecting with the R5), stops at Monistrol Vila and takes **15 minutes**.

- **2026 price (adult):** €9 one way, €15 return. Children aged 4 to 13, €4.50 / €7.50; over-65s, €8.10 / €13.50. Fares valid until 31 December 2026.
- **FGC train + rack railway combo:** €20.80 to €32.60 return, depending on the zones you start from.
- **Timetable:** from 8:35 AM, with 2 or 3 trains an hour. The last one leaves between 6:15 PM (low season) and 8:15 PM (high season); check the calendar at cremallerademontserrat.cat the day before.
- It's the easiest choice with small children, a pushchair or a fear of heights.

**Aeri de Montserrat (cable car).** It climbs 1,350 metres of cable in **5 minutes**, with departures **every 15 minutes** and the best views.

- **2026 price (adult):** €10 one way, €15 return. Children aged 4 to 13, €5.25 / €7.50; over-65s, €8.75 / €12.50. The return ticket is valid for three days from first use.
- **Free parking** next to the lower station (C-55, km 11.5).
- **Watch the wind:** it can stop in strong wind; on those days, take the rack railway.

## Combined tickets: Trans Montserrat and Tot Montserrat

- **Trans Montserrat (€50):** metro, FGC return train from Plaça Espanya, the ride up and down, the **Sant Joan funicular**, the Santa Cova funicular when running and the **Espai Audiovisual**. No discounts for children or students. Buy it online or at the FGC station in Plaça Espanya; it's valid any day until 31 December.
- **Tot Montserrat (€71.50):** the same, plus the **Montserrat Museum** and lunch at the buffet.

Is it worth it? The **Sant Joan funicular** up to the trails costs €17.50 return on its own: if you'll ride it, the Trans Montserrat pays for itself. If you only want the basilica and the Black Madonna, the train and rack railway combo is cheaper. The **Santa Cova funicular** is out of service until further notice, according to the official site.

## By car or on a Montserrat tour from Barcelona

**By car** it's about an hour on the **A-2** or **AP-7** to Martorell, then the **C-55** towards Manresa. Three options:

- **Park at Monistrol Vila** (about 1,000 spaces, free for rack railway users) and ride up. Best at weekends.
- **Park at the Aeri** (free) and take the cable car.
- **Drive up to the monastery:** a winding road and paid parking (about €6.50 a day), which can fill up on high-season weekends.

**On an organised tour** you're taken up and back without checking timetables. ## Common mistakes

1. **Going to Plaça de Catalunya or Sants.** The Montserrat train leaves from the FGC station at **Plaça Espanya**.
2. **Getting off at the wrong stop.** Aeri de Montserrat is for the cable car; Monistrol de Montserrat, the next one, is for the rack railway. Tickets are not interchangeable.
3. **Missing the train by five minutes.** The R5 runs hourly: check the timetable at fgc.cat and leave a margin.
4. **Cutting the return too fine.** The last rack railway can leave at 6:15 PM in low season; check before heading up to Sant Joan.
5. **Counting on Santa Cova:** its funicular is closed until further notice.
6. **Going in short sleeves.** It's 5-8 degrees cooler than Barcelona at the top, often with morning mist.
