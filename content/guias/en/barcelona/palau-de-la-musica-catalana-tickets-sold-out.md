## What you'll see

The visit lasts about **50 minutes** and covers:

- **The Concert Hall**, the heart of the building.
- **The grand staircase.**
- **The Foyer.**
- **The Lluís Millet Hall.**

Visits are timed and in small groups: if you arrive late, you can't join your group.

## Tour price, guided tour and visiting hours

The official website is palaumusica.cat. 2026 prices:

- **Self-guided visit with leaflet:** **€20**.
- **Guided tour:** **€24**, in several languages depending on the time. Best booked online.
- **Reduced:** €20 for over-65s and under-35s; €14 for residents of Catalonia; €13 for people with a disability below 65%.
- **Free:** accompanied under-10s and people with a disability of 65% or more.

**Visiting hours:** every day, **9 AM to 3:30 PM**.

## Palau de la Música tickets sold out: what to do

In peak months the best slots fill up. Try in this order:

1. **Another slot on the official website.**
2. **The box office**, which sells **same-day only**, at €1 more per ticket: Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM to 9 PM, and Sundays and holidays 8:30 AM to 3:30 PM. At Christmas, on 24 December it opens 8:30 AM to 7 PM, on the 25th it's closed in the morning and on the 31st it opens 8:30 AM to 6 PM.
3. **An authorised resale ticket:** the same self-guided visit for €20, without the box office. It's the quickest option once you're in Barcelona.

## Palau de la Música concerts for tourists

The visit doesn't include a concert, but the hall looks its best **full and with music**. Check the programme on the official website: there are **classical, choral, guitar and flamenco** series. Tickets are separate; always buy on palaumusica.cat or at its box office.

## Tips and what to see without a ticket

- **Buy only on the official website** (palaumusica.cat): lookalike domains resell tickets with a surcharge.
- **Don't go in the afternoon:** the last visit is at 3:30 PM; after that you only get in with a concert ticket.
- **Leave big bags behind:** large luggage isn't allowed.
- **Don't count on a refund:** it's a working venue and the tour may change route or be cancelled because of rehearsals; tickets can't be changed or refunded.
- **Accessibility:** lifts to every floor, a stair lift in the Foyer, adapted toilets and wheelchair loan.
- **Without a ticket** you can see the **façade** on Carrer Sant Pere Més Alt, with mosaic columns and the sculpture group of Catalan popular song on the corner; **Óscar Tusquets's extension**, with the square next to the church of Sant Francesc de Paula; and **Cafè Palau**, in the Foyer, open every day from 9 AM to midnight: with a coffee you see part of the modernista interior.
