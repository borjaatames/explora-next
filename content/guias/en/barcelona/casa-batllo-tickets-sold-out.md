## Casa Batlló tickets sold out: other times and ticket types on the official website

Official sales are on casabatllo.es. The general visit starts at **€29**, includes an audio guide in 15 languages and takes about **an hour and a quarter**. With **dynamic pricing**, the earlier you buy, the less you pay (the official website mentions savings of up to €15 per person). On bank holidays, at Easter and in summer the morning slots go fast. If your slot is sold out:

- **Check every slot of the day:** daytime visits have entry times from **9:00 to 19:45**. Mid-morning slots fill first; the afternoon usually holds out longer.
- **Try the Be the First visit:** entry between **8:30 and 8:45**, with fewer people and the rooftop included. It costs **€45** and is only sold online.
- **Look at the ticket types with food:** the visit with the third floor and breakfast or tapas (*Bon Appétit*, from €59) has its own allocation.
- **Change day if you can:** with dynamic pricing, the same day the following week may be cheaper.

Before paying, check what each ticket type includes: they don't all cover the same things (rooftop, immersive rooms, augmented-reality tablet) and **only Platinum lets you change date or time and cancel for free** (Gold for the Magic Nights).

## The night visit

If the day is full, the night is the easiest route. The **night visit** has entry times from **20:30 to 21:30**, costs **from €25** on the official website, includes a glass of cava or a kir royal and is sold **online only**. **It doesn't include the rooftop**, and according to the official website it runs until **15 November**.

Between March and November there are also **Magic Nights**: a visit at 20:00 and a rooftop concert at 21:00, with a drink and a table, **from €59**. Each month's programme is published two or three weeks ahead and general-visit discounts don't apply.

## The ticket office: Casa Batlló same-day tickets

Casa Batlló has a ticket office in the building itself, but it **only sells the general visit** and, according to the official website, it costs **between €4 and €15 more** than online. It's useful if there's space left in a slot that day, not as a plan for a sold-out day.

## Last-minute tickets: resale or a tour with entry included

When the official website has nothing, authorised resellers often have places because they buy allocations in advance. You usually pay a bit more:

- **Timed ticket with audio guide (from €29):** the simplest way in if you just want to see the house.
- **Night visit with a glass of cava (from €29)** or **with live music (from €59)**, the latter with the Magic Nights concert.
- **Tour of three Gaudí houses (from €119):** Casa Vicens, Casa Milà and Casa Batlló with a guide; useful if you don't have La Pedrera either.

Check that the listing says **"Casa Batlló entry included"** and the entry time: some modernisme walks only see the façades.

## What you can see without a ticket

- The **Casa Batlló façade**, at number 43, with its bone-like balconies and scaled roof. It's best seen from the opposite pavement.
- The **Block of Discord**: right next to Batlló is **Casa Amatller** (number 41, by Puig i Cadafalch), with its own guided tours, and on the same block **Casa Lleó Morera**, by Domènech i Montaner.
- **La Pedrera (Casa Milà)**, further up the same avenue, about a 10-minute walk.

## Tips

- **Only buy on casabatllo.es:** copycat websites overcharge and don't always issue a valid ticket.
- **Check what you're buying:** the *Gaudí-Miró-Gomis* exhibition (€15) and the *Street Escape* game are sold on the same website but don't include the house visit.
- **Arrive at your time:** entry is by time slot.
