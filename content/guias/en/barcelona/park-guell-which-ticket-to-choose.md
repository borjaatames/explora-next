Park Güell can be visited three different ways, each bookable separately: the basic ticket to the monumental zone, a combined ticket with the Gaudí House Museum, and the only small-group guided tour available. If you also want to pair it with the Sagrada Família, there are four more options built for that — compared in our [Sagrada Família guide](/en/guides/barcelona/sagrada-familia-which-ticket-to-choose).

## The 3 Park Güell options

| Option | Price from | Duration | Format | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Skip-the-line ticket to the monumental zone](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/park-guell-admission-ticket) | €23 | 1.5-2 h | Self-guided, no guide | Travelers who just want the cheapest ticket, at their own pace |
| [Ticket + Gaudí House Museum](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/park-guell-and-gaudi-house-museum-ticket) | €30 | 2-3 h | Self-guided + museum | Travelers who also want to see where Gaudí lived |
| [Small-group guided tour with Amigo Tours](/en/cities/barcelona/activities/park-guell-small-group-tour-amigo-tours) | €24 | 40-75 min | Guided, bilingual ES/EN | Travelers who'd rather have it explained than read panels |

## How to choose in 30 seconds

- **Tight budget, don't need a guide:** skip-the-line ticket to the monumental zone (€23).
- **Want to see where Gaudí actually lived:** ticket + Gaudí House Museum (€30).
- **Prefer context and stories over reading panels:** guided tour with Amigo Tours (€24) — the only one of the three with a guide, and not the most expensive.
- **Want to pair it with the Sagrada Família:** there are 4 options that combine both — see our [Sagrada Família guide](/en/guides/barcelona/sagrada-familia-which-ticket-to-choose).

## Things to know before booking

All three options use a fixed entry time slot — arrive 10-15 minutes before your slot, not earlier or later. The guided tour is shorter than the self-guided visits (40-75 min vs. 1.5-3 h), but it's the only one with on-site explanation. In high season (June-September) tickets sell out days or weeks ahead, so it's worth booking as soon as you have travel dates.
