## Lights, markets and the Plaza del Carmen show

In 2025 the lights went on on **Saturday 29 November**: a dance show with Granada carols at 20:00 and the switch-on at 20:30, with choirs dotted around Bib-Rambla, Puerta Real, Zacatín and the Fuente de las Batallas. The lights stayed up until 7 January.

- **Light and carol show:** every day at 19:00, 20:00 and 21:00 on Plaza del Carmen, in front of City Hall.
- **Lighting hours in the centre:** 18:30 to 1:00; until 3:00 on Fridays, Saturdays and the eve of holidays; until 5:00 on the 24th and 31st.
- **Markets:** crafts at **Puerta Real** and nativity figures on **Bib-Rambla**, which in 2025 also hosted a **convent-sweets market** for the first time.
- **Inverland:** the Christmas village along Paseo del Salón and Paseo del Violón, with an ice rink and a sledge slide; next to it, the **big wheel** on the Palacio de Congresos esplanade.

## A December evening, done right

1. **16:30, the Albaicín at sunset:** the sun sets early in December; head up in good time to the Mirador de San Nicolás to see the Alhambra light up.
2. **18:30, down the Carrera del Darro** to Plaza Nueva.
3. **19:00, Plaza del Carmen:** the first light show.
4. **19:30, Bib-Rambla and Puerta Real:** markets, nativity figures and convent sweets.
5. **20:30, tapas:** in Granada a free tapa comes with every drink.
6. **22:00, flamenco in a Sacromonte cave.**

The **guided sunset walk through the Albaicín and Sacromonte** costs from €15 and reaches San Nicolás at the right moment. There's also a private version.

- **Winter hours** (15 October to 31 March): 8:30 to 18:00. General ticket €22.27.
- **Nasrid Palaces at night:** Fridays and Saturdays, 20:00 to 21:30, €12.73. With so little daylight in December, it's the most atmospheric way to see them.
- In 2026, 24 and 31 December fall on a Thursday, and 2 January 2027 on a Saturday.

Official tickets for the December bank holidays and Christmas sell out. A **guided night visit** or the **private tour with hotel transfer** has its own allocation.

## With kids

- **Inverland**, with its ice rink and sledge slide, plus the **big wheel**.
- The **light show** on Plaza del Carmen is short and free.
- The **Royal Postman** collects letters in the City Hall courtyard.
- **Sierra Nevada:** the Mirlo Blanco area has sledging, and there are beginner slopes at Borreguiles.

## Sierra Nevada: skiing or a day in the snow

The resort is about 30 km from Granada. In 2025-26 it opened on **29 November**, with runs at Borreguiles and snow cannons, and that season the lighting on the El Río runs was renewed for **Saturday night skiing**. For 2026-27 the resort has announced new snow machines to open the beginners' area earlier, but no date. The December bank holidays and the week between Christmas and Epiphany are the busiest days.

If you don't ski, the **Alpujarra** in winter, with wood fires lit and snow on the peaks, makes a quieter day trip.

## New Year's Eve, the Toma and Three Kings

- **Midnight chimes:** on **Plaza del Carmen**, in front of City Hall.
- **2 January, Día de la Toma:** a local holiday. A civic ceremony with the waving of the royal banner at City Hall and a service at the Royal Chapel, where the Catholic Monarchs are buried. That day the **Alhambra's Alcazaba is free** from 8:30 to 18:00 so you can ring the bell of the **Torre de la Vela**: tradition says single women who ring it will marry within the year.
- **Three Kings Parade (5 January):** at 17:00 from Avenida de la Constitución along Gran Vía, Reyes Católicos and Acera del Darro; around 19:30 there's a drone show at Puente Blanco, and it ends with the Kings on the City Hall balcony. With kids, find a spot on Gran Vía near the start.

## Flamenco in the Sacromonte

The Sacromonte caves are the night out in Granada. **Cueva La Rocío** has a 90-minute show from €35; book ahead in December, and bring a coat for the walk up and back.

## Common mistakes

- **Trying the Alhambra on 25 December or 1 January:** it's closed.
- **Buying Alhambra tickets at the last minute:** at Christmas they sell out weeks ahead.
- **Heading up to the Albaicín at 18:30:** in December the sun sets around 18:00. For sunset light on the Alhambra, go up around 17:00.
- **Driving up to Sierra Nevada without chains or checking the DGT traffic site:** after snowfall the road may require them.
- **Assuming 2 January is a working day:** in Granada it's a holiday and many shops close.
