The Alhambra at night isn't a cut-down version of the day visit: it's a different ticket, with **different times, a different price and a different allocation**. That's why there can be space even when daytime tickets are gone, or the other way round. The catch is that in winter there are only **two nights a week**, and those places go quickly over bank holidays. This guide covers the night visit only; for daytime tickets, see [Alhambra tickets sold out](/en/guides/granada/alhambra-tickets-sold-out).

## 1. Official night-visit days and times

There are two separate night visits. These are the times published by the Alhambra trust (Patronato de la Alhambra):

**Nasrid Palaces at night**

| Season | Days | Visit | Box office |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 October - 31 March | Friday and Saturday | 8pm-9:30pm | 7pm-8:45pm |
| 1 April - 14 October | Tuesday to Saturday | 10pm-11:30pm | 9pm-10:45pm |

**Generalife Gardens and Palace at night**

| Season | Days | Visit | Box office |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 April - 31 May | Tuesday to Saturday | 10pm-11:30pm | 9pm-10:45pm |
| 1 September - 14 October | Tuesday to Saturday | 10pm-11:30pm | 9pm-10:45pm |
| 15 October - 14 November | Friday and Saturday | 8pm-9:30pm | 7pm-8:45pm |

In June, July and August and from 15 November to 31 March, the official site shows no Generalife night times. There are no night visits at all on Sundays or Mondays.

## 2. Official prices

Booking in advance at `tickets.alhambra-patronato.es` (the price includes an advance-sale commission of roughly 6%; at the box office you pay the base price):

| Ticket | What it covers | Adult | Reduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nasrid Palaces Night Visit | The Nasrid Palaces only, at night | €12.73 | €8.48 |
| Gardens and Generalife Night Visit | The Generalife, no Nasrid Palaces | €8.48 | €6.36 |
| Alhambra Experiences | Nasrid Palaces at night + Gardens, Generalife and Alcazaba by day, on two consecutive days | €22.27 | €14.85 |
| Dobla de Oro Night | Nasrid Palaces at night + five Albaicín monuments, over two days | €23.06 | — |

Reduced rates apply to over-65s, European Youth Card holders, large families, people with a disability of 33% or more and children aged 12 to 15. **Under-12s** go free but must be included in the booking. Tickets are **in your name**: bring your passport or ID card.

## 3. What to do if it's sold out

In this order:

1. **Check the other night.** In winter there's only Friday and Saturday; if one fails, try the other. From April to mid-October, Tuesday to Thursday has more room than Saturday.
2. **Check Alhambra Experiences and Dobla de Oro Night.** They're separate products on the same site and give you the palaces at night plus something extra for a few euros more.
3. **Look at the Granada Card 24h (€50).** It's the only Granada Card that includes the Nasrid Palaces night visit, and according to Granada's tourist board it has **its own allocation**: there may be space when single tickets are gone. Worth it if you're also visiting the cathedral, the Royal Chapel and other sights.
4. **Go to the night box office**, which opens an hour before entry. There may be a ticket or two, but there's no guarantee.
5. **Book a guided night tour** with entry included. Operators reserve their allocation well ahead.

## 5. If you can't get in: alternatives for that evening

- **The Generalife at night** (€8.48), if your date falls between April and 14 November outside summer. It's a different allocation and usually lasts longer.
- **The Mirador de San Nicolás** in the Albaicín: the floodlit Alhambra from across the valley, for free. On the Albaicín and Sacromonte sunset tour (from €15) you get there at the right time with someone explaining what you're looking at.
- **Flamenco in a Sacromonte cave**, such as Cueva del Zincalé (from €20), to round off the night.
- **The Alhambra by day:** with the [Gardens, Generalife and Alcazaba](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-gardens-generalife-skip-line-ticket) ticket (from €17.50) or a private Nasrid Palaces tour with hotel pick-up (from €139).

## When it sells out and how far ahead to book

- **Fridays and Saturdays from October to March:** the only nights of the season; book as soon as you have dates, especially over the early-December bank holidays and Easter when it falls in March.
- **Spring Saturdays:** the most sought after in the long season.
- **Tuesday to Thursday, April to October:** usually more room.

## Common mistakes

1. **Assuming the night visit includes the Alcazaba or the Generalife.** The Nasrid night visit only covers the palaces.
2. **Looking for a night visit on a Sunday or Monday.** There isn't one.
3. **Mixing up the Nasrid and Generalife night visits.** The Generalife one is cheaper for a reason: it doesn't include the palaces.
4. **Arriving late for your time.** You enter the Nasrid Palaces at the time printed on your ticket.
5. **Forgetting your ID.** Tickets are in your name and checked at the entrance.

## In a nutshell

If the Alhambra night visit is sold out, try the other night, Alhambra Experiences or the Granada Card 24h; if nothing's left, book a guided night tour with entry from €54 or head up to the Mirador de San Nicolás to see it lit up.
