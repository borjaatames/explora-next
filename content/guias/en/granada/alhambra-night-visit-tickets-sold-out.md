## Alhambra night visit: opening hours by season

There are two different night visits. These are the times published by the Alhambra Trust (Patronato):

**Nasrid Palaces at night**

| Season | Days | Visit | Ticket office |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 October - 31 March | Friday and Saturday | 20:00-21:30 | 19:00-20:45 |
| 1 April - 14 October | Tuesday to Saturday | 22:00-23:30 | 21:00-22:45 |

**Gardens and Generalife Palace at night**

| Season | Days | Visit | Ticket office |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 April - 31 May | Tuesday to Saturday | 22:00-23:30 | 21:00-22:45 |
| 1 September - 14 October | Tuesday to Saturday | 22:00-23:30 | 21:00-22:45 |
| 15 October - 14 November | Friday and Saturday | 20:00-21:30 | 19:00-20:45 |

In June, July and August and from 15 November to 31 March the official site shows no Generalife night times. **There are no night visits on Sundays or Mondays.**

## Alhambra night tickets: prices

Bought in advance at tickets.alhambra-patronato.es (the price includes an advance-sale fee of around 6%; at the ticket office you pay the base price, about €12 for the Nasrid Palaces):

| Ticket | What's included | Standard | Reduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nasrid Palaces Night Visit | Only the Nasrid Palaces, at night | €12.73 | €8.48 |
| Gardens and Generalife Night Visit | Generalife, no Nasrid Palaces | €8.48 | €6.36 |
| Alhambra Experiencias | Nasrid Palaces at night + Gardens, Generalife and Alcazaba by day, on two consecutive days | €22.27 | €14.85 |
| Dobla de Oro Night | Nasrid Palaces at night + five Albaicín monuments, over two days | €23.06 | — |

- **Reduced:** over-65s, European youth card (Carnet Joven), large families, people with a disability of 33% or more and children aged 12-15.
- **Under-12s:** free, but must be included in the booking.
- **There's no free night visit.**
- Tickets are **in your name**: bring ID or a passport.

## Nasrid Palaces night visit or Generalife at night

- **Nasrid night visit:** covers only the illuminated Nasrid Palaces, for an hour and a half. **It doesn't include the Alcazaba or the Generalife.** It's a very different, calmer experience than by day.
- **Generalife at night:** the Generalife gardens and palace, **without the Nasrid Palaces**. That's why it's cheaper.

They're separate tickets with their own quotas. If you want the Nasrid Palaces at night and the rest by day, **Alhambra Experiencias** combines both over two consecutive days.

## Alhambra night visit sold out: what to do

In this order:

1. **Try the other night.** In winter there are only Fridays and Saturdays; if one fails, try the other. From April to mid-October, Tuesday to Thursday has more room than Saturday.
2. **Check Alhambra Experiencias and Dobla de Oro Night.** They're separate products on the same site and give you the Nasrid Palaces at night plus more for a few euros extra.
3. **Try the Granada Card 24 h (€50).** It's the only Granada Card that includes the Nasrid night visit and, according to Turismo de Granada, it has **its own quota**. It's worth it if you're also visiting the cathedral, the Royal Chapel and other monuments.
4. **Go to the night ticket office**, which opens an hour before the visit. There may be a ticket left, but there's no guarantee.
5. **Book a guided night tour** with the ticket included (from €54): operators reserve their quota in advance. It includes a live guide or audio guide for an hour and a half, meeting on the Paseo del Generalife. Pushchairs aren't allowed.

## When it sells out

- **Fridays and Saturdays from October to March:** the only nights of the season. Book as soon as you have a date, especially on long weekends such as Constitution Day (December) and Easter if it falls in March.
- **Spring Saturdays:** the most sought-after of the long season.
- **Tuesday to Thursday from April to October:** usually more availability.

## If you get nothing, and common mistakes

**Alternatives for that night:**

- **The Generalife at night** (€8.48), if your date falls between April and 14 November and outside summer: it's a different quota and usually lasts longer.
- **The Mirador de San Nicolás**, in the Albaicín: the illuminated Alhambra from across the valley, for free.
- **Flamenco in a Sacromonte cave**, such as the Cueva del Zincalé, to round off the night.
- **The Alhambra by day:** if the general ticket is also sold out, see [Alhambra tickets sold out](/en/guides/granada/alhambra-tickets-sold-out).

**Common mistakes:**

1. **Thinking the night visit includes the Alcazaba or the Generalife.** The Nasrid one only covers the palaces.
2. **Looking for a night visit on a Sunday or Monday.** There isn't one.
3. **Being late for your time.** Entry to the Nasrid Palaces is at the time on your ticket.
4. **Forgetting your ID.** It's checked at the entrance.

To plan the rest of your visit, read [how to visit the Alhambra](/en/guides/granada/alhambra-how-to-visit).
