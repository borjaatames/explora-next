## The plan: Granada in one day, hour by hour

Everything hinges on something decided weeks ahead: **an Alhambra ticket with a time slot for the Nasrid Palaces**. With it, the day organises itself.

| Time | Plan | Getting around |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8:30-12:30 | Alhambra: Alcazaba, Nasrid Palaces and Generalife | C30 or C32 minibus (€1.60) |
| 12:30-13:00 | Walk down the Cuesta de Gomérez | On foot, 15 min |
| 13:30-15:15 | Tapas on Navas or Elvira | On foot |
| 15:30-16:30 | Royal Chapel and Cathedral | On foot, 5 min |
| 16:45-18:00 | Alcaicería, Carrera del Darro and Bañuelo | On foot |
| 18:00-19:30 | Up to the Albaicín, sunset at San Nicolás | C32 or 25 min uphill |
| 21:30 | Zambra in the Sacromonte | On foot; taxi back (€5-7) |

## Morning: the Alhambra

**8:30, entry to the complex.** It opens 8:30-18:00 from 15 October to 31 March and until 20:00 the rest of the year. Go up on the **C30 or C32 minibus** from Plaza Isabel la Católica or by taxi (€8-10); walking up the Cuesta de Gomérez takes 25-30 minutes.

The golden rule: your **Nasrid Palaces time is fixed** and there's only a **30-minute margin**. It takes 30-45 minutes to walk from the entrance to the palaces, so plan your route around that time:

- **Nasrid Palaces** (1 h 30): Court of the Myrtles, Hall of the Ambassadors and Court of the Lions. Look up: the domes are the best part.
- **Alcazaba** (45 min): the Torre de la Vela has the best view of Granada and Sierra Nevada.
- **Generalife** (1 hour): the summer residence, with the Patio de la Acequia.

About **4 hours** in total. Bring your **ID or passport**: tickets are named. If you want to understand what you're seeing (the "drawings" on the walls are poems), the most popular guided tour includes the ticket and has its own quota when general admission is sold out:

## Lunch and afternoon: tapas, Royal Chapel and Carrera del Darro

**13:30, free tapas.** Walk down the Cuesta de Gomérez to Plaza Nueva and carry on to **Calle Navas** or **Elvira**. In Granada **a tapa comes free with every drink**: three beers (€1.80-2.50 each) make a meal. Arrive before 14:00 or after 15:30 to find space at the bar. If you'd rather someone took you to the good bars, a private tapas tour makes three stops in 2 h 30.

**15:30, Royal Chapel** (€7, 1 hour): the tombs of the Catholic Monarchs, Joanna the Mad and Philip the Handsome, plus Isabella's crown and sceptre. Next door, the Renaissance **Cathedral** (€7; €10 until 28 November 2026 because of a temporary exhibition). Short on time? Choose the Royal Chapel.

**16:45, Alcaicería and Carrera del Darro.** Cross the Alcaicería (five minutes is enough) and go up to Plaza Nueva to take the **Carrera del Darro**, Granada's most beautiful street, with the river on one side and the Alhambra above. Stop at the **Bañuelo**, 11th-century Arab baths (€5, 15 minutes).

## Sunset and evening: Albaicín, San Nicolás and zambra

**18:00.** At the end of Paseo de los Tristes, climb to the Albaicín via the Cuesta del Chapiz (25 minutes uphill) or take the **C32 minibus** from Plaza Nueva. Wander up to the **Mirador de San Nicolás** and arrive **45 minutes before sunset**: the Alhambra turns red with Sierra Nevada behind. If it's too crowded, the Mirador de San Cristóbal, 10 minutes further up, has half the people. With a guide, the sunset walk through the Albaicín and Sacromonte starts from €15.

**21:30, zambra.** From San Nicolás it's 15-20 minutes on foot to the **Sacromonte** caves. Zambra is cave flamenco, intimate and gypsy-rooted: **€22-30 with a drink**. Book ahead, especially at weekends, and take a taxi back (€5-7). To choose a cave, see [the Sacromonte comparison](/en/guides/granada/flamenco-cave-sacromonte-which-to-choose).

## What to book and what it costs

| What | Price | How far ahead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alhambra General (with Nasrid Palaces) | €22.27 on the official website | 3-4 weeks; up to 3 months in high season |
| Guided tour with ticket | from €49 | 1-2 weeks; a lifeline if sold out |
| Zambra in the Sacromonte | €22-30 with a drink | 2-3 days; more at weekends |
| Royal Chapel and Cathedral | €7 each | same day |

**Indicative budget per person, excluding accommodation:** about **€80-105** on your own (Alhambra, Royal Chapel and Bañuelo, minibuses, tapas for lunch and dinner, and zambra) and about **€105-130** with a guided Alhambra.

## Tips: from Málaga, with kids and common mistakes

- **From Málaga:** a direct Avant train of 1 h 18 or an Alsa bus of 1 h 45 (about €13). Take an Alhambra slot from 10:30 and skip the zambra; options in [Granada and the Alhambra from Málaga](/en/guides/malaga/granada-alhambra-day-trip-from-malaga).
- **With kids:** under-12s get into the Alhambra free, but **need their own named ticket**. Use the minibuses for every climb and swap the zambra for an early dinner. More in [Granada with kids](/en/guides/granada/granada-with-kids).
- **If it rains:** the Alhambra isn't cancelled and the Nasrid Palaces are covered; wear shoes with grip, as the Albaicín cobbles get slippery.
- **Mistakes to avoid:** turning up without an Alhambra ticket hoping to buy one there; arriving more than 30 minutes late for your Nasrid slot (you won't get in); climbing to the Albaicín at midday and coming down before sunset; eating on the Plaza Nueva terraces, where the tapa isn't free; and accepting street "Alhambra tours" without accreditation.

If you can stay another day, [Granada in 2 days](/en/guides/granada/granada-in-2-days) adds the Cartuja and the Realejo.
