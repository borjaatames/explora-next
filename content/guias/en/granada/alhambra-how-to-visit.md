The Alhambra is Spain's most visited monument, and its tickets sell out weeks in advance. The safest way in is a visit that already includes your ticket. You don't depend on spaces being left, and someone explains what you're looking at.

## Why go with a guide

- **Your entry is guaranteed**, even when the official site says "sold out".
- **The guide handles the Nasrid Palaces time slot**, which is what trips up most independent visitors.
- **You understand what you see.** The poems carved into the walls, the geometry of the tiles and the story of each hall are lost without an explanation.
- **No risk of fake guides.** Unlicensed "Alhambra tours" are offered in Plaza Nueva.

## The golden rule: your Nasrid Palaces time

The Nasrid Palaces have a fixed entry time with only **30 minutes of leeway**. Miss it and you don't get in. It takes **30 to 45 minutes** on foot from the main entrance to the palaces, so arrive early. On a guided visit, the guide plans the route so you don't miss your slot.

## What to see inside

- **Nasrid Palaces:** the Court of the Myrtles, the Hall of the Ambassadors, the Court of the Lions and the Hall of the Two Sisters. Look up: the domes are the highlight.
- **Alcazaba:** the oldest part. The Torre de la Vela has the best views of Granada and the Sierra Nevada.
- **Generalife:** the sultans' summer residence, with the Patio de la Acequia.
- **Palace of Charles V:** the Renaissance palace in the middle of the complex.

A full visit takes **about 4 hours**.

## Getting there

- **C30 or C32 minibus** from Plaza Isabel la Católica, every few minutes.
- **On foot** from Plaza Nueva up the Cuesta de Gomérez: a 25-30 minute climb.
- **Taxi** from the centre.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Turning up without a ticket, expecting to buy one there.** In high season there are none left.
2. **Buying from resale sites.** They charge high fees and some tickets are not valid.
3. **Forgetting your ID or passport.** Tickets are in your name.
4. **Bringing a large backpack or pushchair.** They have to go in the left-luggage room.
5. **Rushing.** With 2 hours you only see half.
