## Alhambra tickets price and ticket types

Official Patronato prices for 2026, online fee included:

| Ticket | Price | What's included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alhambra General (daytime) | €22.27 | Nasrid Palaces, Alcazaba, Generalife and Palace of Charles V |
| Gardens and Generalife | €12.73 | Everything except the Nasrid Palaces |
| Nasrid Palaces night visit | €12.73 | Only the illuminated Nasrid Palaces, at night |
| Dobla de Oro | €30.48 | The full Alhambra plus the Andalusian monuments of the Albaicín |

- The vast majority need the **Alhambra General**.
- **Under-12s:** free, but **with a named ticket**, issued in the same purchase. Without it they can't get in.
- **Discounts:** EU over-65s, Carnet Joven (youth card) and disability.
- **There is no recurring free day:** the "free on Wednesdays" claim circulating online is false.

## How to buy official Alhambra tickets (and if they're sold out)

- **Where:** the only official site is tickets.alhambra-patronato.es. It's slow and payment sometimes fails, but any other site charges a fee or resells.
- **When:** tickets go on sale **up to three months ahead**, with about 8,000 a day. Morning slots sell out weeks in advance; at Easter, on Spanish long weekends, in July-August and at Christmas, within minutes.
- **Bring your ID or passport:** tickets are in your name. Which document works and what to do about a name mistake, in [our guide to Alhambra ticket names and ID](/en/guides/granada/alhambra-tickets-name-passport-id).

**If everything is sold out**, your real options are:

1. **A guided tour with the ticket included:** operators have their own allocation even when the official site shows sold out, and the guide handles the Nasrid Palaces time.
2. **The Nasrid Palaces night visit**, which usually has more availability.
3. **Queuing at the ticket office before 8:00** for the same-day allocation, which in high season is rarely enough.

## Nasrid Palaces: your entry time

The Nasrid Palaces have a **fixed entry time**, printed on your ticket, and there's only a **30-minute window**. After that you can't get in and there's no refund.

Walking from the site entrance to the palaces takes **30 to 45 minutes**, so arrive early. On a guided tour, the guide plans the route so you don't miss your slot.

## What to see inside

- **Nasrid Palaces:** the Court of the Myrtles, the Hall of the Ambassadors, the Court of the Lions and the Hall of the Two Sisters. Look up: the domes are the best part.
- **Alcazaba:** the oldest part. The Torre de la Vela has the best views of Granada and Sierra Nevada.
- **Generalife:** the sultans' summer residence, with the Court of the Water Channel (Patio de la Acequia).
- **Palace of Charles V:** the Renaissance palace in the middle of the complex.

The full visit takes **about 4 hours**. With 2 hours you'll only see half.

## Opening hours and how to get there

- **Summer** (1 April to 14 October): 8:30-20:00, last entry to the Nasrid Palaces at 19:00.
- **Winter:** 8:30-18:00, last entry to the Nasrid Palaces at 17:00.
- **Closed** on 25 December and 1 January.
- **Night visit:** Tuesday to Saturday in summer (22:00-23:30) and Friday and Saturday the rest of the year (20:00-21:30).

**Getting up there:**

- **Minibus C30 or C32** from Plaza Isabel la Católica: €1.60, every 8 minutes.
- **On foot** from Plaza Nueva up the Cuesta de Gomérez: a 25-30 minute climb.
- **Taxi** from the centre: €8-10.

## Tips for visiting the Alhambra

- **Best time:** the first morning slot, with side light in the courtyards and fewer people; or the last afternoon slot in summer, when the heat eases.
- **Guide or on your own?** If it's your first time, with a guide: you'll understand the poems carved on the walls, the geometry of the tiles and the story of each room, which are lost without explanation.
- **Beware of fake guides:** in Plaza Nueva people offer an "Alhambra tour" without accreditation.
- **Don't turn up without a ticket** thinking you'll buy one there: in high season there are none left.
- **Large backpacks or pushchairs:** they must be left in the cloakroom.
- If you're torn between this monument and Seville's, see [Alhambra or Alcázar of Seville?](/en/guides/granada/alhambra-or-alcazar-of-seville). And for the rest of the city, [Granada in 2 days](/en/guides/granada/granada-in-2-days).
