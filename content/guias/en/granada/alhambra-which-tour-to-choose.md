If you've searched "Alhambra tour" you'll have seen half a dozen results with different names — "skip the line", "tickets included", "small group", "skip the wait" — priced anywhere from €50 to €96. The bad news: most of them are literally the same tour. The good news: it takes two minutes to know which one you actually need.

## The "standard tour": 6 listings, 1 product

These six listings describe the same route — Alcazaba, Nasrid Palaces and Generalife, about 3 hours, with an official guide and priority access to your assigned Nasrid Palaces time slot:

| Tour | Price from | Real note |
|---|---|---|
| [Entry and guided tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-entry-and-guided-tour) | €50 | The cheapest of the six. No extra padding, free time at the end |
| [Alhambra small group tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-small-group-tour) | €65 | Spends more time at the Generalife (close to 1 hour, more than the rest) |
| [Generalife guided tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-generalife-guided-tour) | €54 | Standard split across the three areas |
| [Nasrid Palaces skip the line](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-nasrid-palaces-skip-the-line) | €75 | Same route, mid-high price |
| [Guided tour with tickets](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-guided-tour-with-tickets) | €83.78 | Same route |
| [Skip the wait, Nasrid Palaces](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-skip-the-wait-nasrid-palaces) | €96 | The most expensive of the group, with no content difference to justify it |

**Honest recommendation**: for this standard tour, what actually shapes your experience is your assigned Nasrid Palaces entry time slot (it's the same whichever operator you book through), not the operator itself. Book whichever of these is cheapest and has availability for your date — usually [Entry and guided tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-entry-and-guided-tour) (€50) or [Generalife guided tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-generalife-guided-tour) (€54). The one real difference we found is that [the small group tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-small-group-tour) spends more time at the Generalife if the gardens matter more to you than the Palaces.

## The 3 options that are genuinely different

| Option | Price from | Why it's different |
|---|---|---|
| [Night tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-night-tour) | €54 | A different time slot (evening), 90 minutes instead of 3 hours, completely different atmosphere and light |
| [Private tour](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-private-tour) | €280 | A guide just for your group, pace and focus tailored to you |
| [Generalife gardens ticket](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-gardens-generalife-skip-line-ticket) | €18 | No access to the Nasrid Palaces — the option if you couldn't get a Palaces time slot or only want the gardens |

## What actually matters: booking ahead

The real bottleneck at the Alhambra isn't which tour you pick, it's securing a Nasrid Palaces time slot: access is legally capped per time slot, and in high season (Easter week, summer, long weekends) it sells out weeks in advance. Book as soon as you have your travel dates fixed, even if the rest of your itinerary stays flexible.
