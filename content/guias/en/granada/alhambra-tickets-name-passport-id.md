**Alhambra tickets are nominative**: each one carries the name and ID number of the person who will use it, and staff can ask for your **original passport or national ID card** at the checkpoints. It's how the Patronato fights resale. This guide explains which document to bring, what to do if there's a mistake in a name and what details to give if you book with a tour.

## What a nominative ticket means

When you buy on the official site, tickets.alhambra-patronato.es, you're asked for the **first name, surnames and ID number** of every visitor. Those details are printed on each ticket.

- **It's personal and non-transferable:** you can't give it away or resell it.
- **Each visitor carries their own ticket** for the whole visit, children included.
- **Staff can ask for it together with your ID:** Patronato staff, security or police, at the gate or inside the complex.

## Which document to bring

The official rule asks for an **original identity document** issued by the Spanish Ministry of the Interior or the equivalent body in your country.

- **EU visitors:** national ID card. A passport also works.
- **Everyone else:** your passport.
- **Bring the same document whose number you gave at booking.** If you booked with your passport number, don't turn up with only a driving licence.
- **The original, not a photo on your phone.** If you have a discount (senior, youth card, large family), bring the document that proves it too, because they can check it at the ticket office or entrance.

## If there's a mistake in a name or you need to change it

This is the small print that causes most of the trouble:

- **Name changes** are only possible on orders of **5 or more tickets**, up to the day before the visit, from the private area of the purchase site, and only if the tickets haven't been printed at the ticket office or a machine.
- **The number of changes goes by bands:** 1 change for 5 to 9 tickets, 2 for 10 to 19, 3 for 20 to 30, and so on up to 6 changes for groups of 51 to 60.
- **Orders of fewer than 5 tickets have no name change option.**
- **If you made a typo** in a name or an ID number, call the **Call Center on +34 858 889 002** with your booking code. Do it before the day of the visit, not at the gate.

## Date, time and cancellations

- **Date and time can't be changed** once the purchase is confirmed. Check them carefully before paying.
- **Purchases are final:** money is only refunded if the visit can't take place for reasons attributable to the Patronato or in the cases set by Andalusian regulations.
- **Your Nasrid Palaces time is strict:** 300 people enter every half hour and, once your slot has passed, you lose the right to visit them. The rest of the complex opens 8:30 to 18:00 in winter and until 20:00 in summer.

## If you book with a tour or agency

Guided tours with entry included are nominative too: the operator buys the tickets in the name of each person in the group, so **they'll ask for everyone's full name and ID number**.

- **Write names exactly as they appear on the document**, including both surnames for Spanish and Latin American travellers.
- **Send them early:** without those details the tickets can't be issued, and in high season quotas run out.
- **If you spot a mistake later, tell the operator**, not the Patronato: the purchase is in their name.

## Tips for the day of your visit

- **Carry your ticket printed or on your phone**, one per person, with the QR code visible.
- **Keep your ID handy**, not at the bottom of your backpack: you may be asked at more than one checkpoint.
- **Allow time to reach the Nasrid Palaces.** It's a fair walk from the main entrance, and seniors or families with small children should leave extra margin.
