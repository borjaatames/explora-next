**Córdoba** is the day trip with the best ratio between what you see and how long it takes. The **Mosque-Cathedral** is unique in the world, the **Jewish Quarter** is best on foot and the **Roman bridge** at sunset rounds off the day. Here's how to plan it depending on where you start.

## What to see in Córdoba in a day

1. **Mosque-Cathedral** (1 h 30 to 2 h): the forest of columns, the mihrab and the Renaissance cathedral at its centre.
2. **The Jewish Quarter:** Calleja de las Flores, the Synagogue and Plaza de Maimónides.
3. **The Roman bridge** and the Calahorra Tower, best at sunset.
4. If there's time: the **Alcázar of the Christian Monarchs** or a **patio**; in May, the patios festival.

## Once in Córdoba: private guided tours

If you arrive on your own and want a guide just for your group: [Mosque-Cathedral and Jewish Quarter privately](/en/cities/cordoba/activities/cordoba-mosque-jewish-quarter-private-tour) (€260 for 2, €396 for 4, 2 h 30) or [the Mosque-Cathedral only](/en/cities/cordoba/activities/cordoba-mosque-cathedral-private-tour) (€210 for 2, 1 h 30).

## Tips

1. **From Seville and Málaga, the train beats the car:** you arrive in the centre with no parking to find.
2. **Go into the Mosque early:** there are fewer groups first thing.
3. **In summer Córdoba easily passes 40 °C:** visit in the morning and save the evening for the bridge at sunset.
