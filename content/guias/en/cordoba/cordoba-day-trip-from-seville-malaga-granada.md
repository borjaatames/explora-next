- **From Seville and Málaga, the train beats the car:** you arrive in the centre without hunting for parking. From the AVE station to the Mosque it's about a **20-minute walk**.
- **From Granada and Cádiz**, the road is the practical option (about 2h and 2h 30min), on your own or on a private trip.
- **Group or private?** For 4 people, the private train trip from Seville works out at about **€167 per person** with tickets and visit, compared with €109-110 for the group tour.

## What to see in Córdoba in one day

1. **Mosque-Cathedral** (1h 30min to 2h): the forest of columns, the mihrab and the Renaissance cathedral in the middle.
2. **The Jewish Quarter:** Calleja de las Flores, the Synagogue and Plaza de Maimónides, five minutes from the Mosque.
3. **The Roman bridge** and the Calahorra Tower, best at sunset.
4. **If there's time left:** the **Alcázar of the Christian Monarchs** or a **patio**; in May, the patios festival.

## Mosque-Cathedral: tickets and opening hours

- **Standard ticket:** **€15**. Reduced €12 (over-65s and students) and €8 for ages 10 to 14; under-10s free.
- **Free:** Monday to Saturday, **8:30 to 9:30**.
- **Opening hours:** Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 (until 18:00 from November to February). Sundays and religious holidays, 8:30 to 11:30 and 15:00 to 19:00. Ticket sales and entry close 30 minutes earlier.
- **Ticket office:** in the Patio de San Eulogio, Calle Torrijos.
- **Book ahead** in high season and in May, during the patios festival.

## Once in Córdoba: private guided tour

If you arrive on your own and want a guide just for your group, there's a private tour of the **Mosque-Cathedral and the Jewish Quarter** (€260 for 2, €396 for 4, 2h 30min) or of **the Mosque-Cathedral only** (€210 for 2, 1h 30min). Both include entry.

## Tips

1. **Get into the Mosque early:** there are fewer groups first thing.
2. **In summer, Córdoba easily tops 40 °C:** visit in the morning and save the afternoon for the bridge at sunset.
