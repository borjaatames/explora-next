## The best way to see Madrid in a day, by who's travelling

- **Couple on a tight budget:** free tour or small-group walking tour (€29.95, maximum 10). By tuk tuk you'd pay €39.50 each for 1 h 15 min.
- **Family of 4:** tuk tuk. At €79 for all four it works out at **€19.75 per person**, has a booster seat for children and nobody gets tired of walking.
- **Group of 6:** two tuk tuks (€158, €26.33 per person) or the bus if you want to use it all day.
- **First time in Madrid:** a walking tour in the morning to understand the centre and the 24-hour bus if you'll cover the Prado, the Retiro and the Bernabéu on the same day.
- **Reduced mobility:** the bus is listed as wheelchair accessible; the tuk tuk and walking tours are not.

## Madrid hop-on hop-off or tuk tuk: when is the tuk tuk cheaper?

**From 3 people, the 1 h 15 min tuk tuk costs less per person than the bus** (€79 ÷ 3 = €26.33, versus €29.70). With 4 it drops to €19.75, the same as a generous free-tour tip. With 5 or 6 you need two vehicles and the price per person goes up again (€31.60 with 5; €26.33 with 6).

But don't compare price alone: the bus is valid for **24 hours** and gets you to the Bernabéu or Las Ventas; the tuk tuk is a ride of just over an hour that ends where it started, at Plaza de Cánovas del Castillo (Neptune).

## Pros and cons of each option

### Free tour

- **For:** you pay what you think it's worth at the end; a live guide who tells stories and answers questions. Ideal for backpackers, solo travellers and couples.
- **Against:** groups can be large; the guide depends on tips and usually pays the platform per attendee, so there's pressure to fill up. It's all on foot.

### Hop-on hop-off bus

- **For:** 3 routes (Historic, about 85 min; Modern, about 70; Cultural, about 75), hop on and off as often as you like for 24 h, audio guide in 10 languages and an open top deck. There's also a 70-minute night tour for €23.40, with a live guide. A good choice for older travellers.
- **Against:** recorded audio guide, little shade upstairs in strong sun and the odd stop where you can't get off.

### Private tuk tuk

- **For:** just your group, no walking, with a child booster seat, water and a charger. The short route links the Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor, La Latina, Sol and Gran Vía. Perfect for families and anyone short on time.
- **Against:** **audio guide in 11 languages, not a live guide**; up to 4 people per vehicle; pricey for a couple.

### Private walking guide

The middle ground if you want a live guide just for you and time to ask questions: the private Gran Vía tour costs €164 for 2 and €300 for 6 (€50 per person).

## Common mistakes

1. **Thinking the free tour is free.** Bring cash or have a payment app ready and budget €10-20 per person.
2. **Buying the bus for a single loop.** If you won't get off, a tuk tuk shared by 3-4 or a walking tour costs the same or less.
3. **Booking a tuk tuk for 5 without checking capacity.** It fits 4; a fifth person means paying for a second vehicle.
4. **Expecting a guide in the tuk tuk.** It's an audio guide; the driver doesn't act as a guide.
5. **Sitting on the top deck at midday in summer** without a cap or water.

## At Christmas: the lights tuk tuk

While the lights are on (**21 November to 6 January**), there's a night version of the private tuk tuk that tours them: an hour and a half for up to 4 people, without queuing in the cold.
