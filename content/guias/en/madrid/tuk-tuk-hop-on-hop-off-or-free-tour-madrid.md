**In Madrid, a free walking tour is paid with a tip (Guruwalk's average is €10-20 per person), the hop-on hop-off bus costs €29.70 per person per day, and a private tuk tuk €79 per vehicle of up to 4.** They don't do the same job: the free tour explains the city on foot, the bus moves you around all day and the tuk tuk is a short, comfortable ride. What matters most is how many of you there are.

## Quick answer by who's travelling

- **Couple on a budget:** free tour or a [small-group walking tour](/en/cities/madrid/activities/madrid-habsburgs-small-group-walking-tour) (€29.95, max. 10). By tuk tuk you'd pay €39.50 each for 1 h 15 min.
- **Family of 4:** tuk tuk. At €79 for the four of you it's **€19.75 each**, there's a booster seat for children and nobody gets tired of walking.
- **Party of 6:** two tuk tuks (€158, €26.33 each) or the bus if you'll use it all day.
- **First time in Madrid:** a walking tour in the morning to understand the centre, and the [24-hour bus](/en/cities/madrid/activities/madrid-big-bus-hop-on-hop-off-tour) if you'll cover the Prado, the Retiro and the Bernabéu on the same day.
- **Limited mobility:** the bus is listed as wheelchair accessible; the tuk tuk and walking tours aren't.

## Break-even: when is the tuk tuk cheaper?

**From 3 people, the 1 h 15 min tuk tuk costs less per person than the bus** (€79 ÷ 3 = €26.33, against €29.70). With 4 it drops to €19.75, the same as a generous free-tour tip. With 5 or 6 you need two vehicles and the per-person price goes back up (€31.60 for 5; €26.33 for 6).

But don't compare price alone: the bus is valid for **24 hours** and you can use it to reach the Bernabéu or Las Ventas; the tuk tuk is a ride of just over an hour that ends where it started, at Plaza de Cánovas del Castillo (Neptune fountain).

## Pros and cons

### Free tour

- **For:** you pay what you think it's worth at the end; a live guide telling stories and answering questions.
- **Against:** groups can be large; the guide relies on tips and usually pays the platform per attendee, so there's pressure to fill the group. It's all on foot.

### Hop-on hop-off bus

- **For:** 3 routes (Historic, about 85 min; Modern, about 70; Cultural, about 75), hop on and off as often as you like for 24 h, audio guide in 10 languages and an open top deck. There's also a [70-minute night tour](/en/cities/madrid/activities/madrid-big-bus-panoramic-night-tour) for €23.40 with a live guide.
- **Against:** recorded commentary, little shade upstairs in strong sun and the odd hold-up where you can't get off, according to reviews summarised on the tour page.

### Private tuk tuk

- **For:** just your group, no walking, booster seat for children, water and a phone charger. The short route links the Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor, La Latina, Sol and Gran Vía.
- **Against:** **audio guide in 11 languages, not a live guide**; up to 4 people per vehicle; pricey for a couple.

### Private walking guide

The middle ground if you want a live guide just for you: the [private Gran Vía tour](/en/cities/madrid/activities/madrid-gran-via-private-walking-tour) costs €164 for 2 and €300 for 6 (€50 each).

## Who each one suits

- **Free tour:** backpackers, solo travellers, couples who want history without spending much.
- **Bus:** first visits with a day of scattered sights, older travellers, limited mobility.
- **Tuk tuk:** families with children, groups of 3-4, anyone tired or short on time.
- **Private walking tour:** those who want explanations tailored to them and time to ask questions.

## Common mistakes

1. **Thinking the free tour is free.** Bring cash or a payment app and budget €10-20 per person.
2. **Buying the bus for a single loop.** If you won't get off, a tuk tuk shared by 3-4 or a walking tour is as good or better value.
3. **Booking a tuk tuk for 5 without checking capacity.** It seats 4; the fifth person means paying for a second vehicle.
4. **Expecting a guide in the tuk tuk.** It's an audio guide; the driver doesn't guide.
5. **Sitting on the top deck at midday in summer** without a hat or water.

## At Christmas: the lights tuk tuk

While the lights are on (**21 November to 6 January**), there is an evening version of the private tuk tuk that tours them, with dates in the booking calendar: an hour and a half for up to 4 people, with no queuing in the cold. It's open-sided, so bring a coat. The rest of the season's plans are in [Christmas in Madrid](/en/guides/madrid/christmas-in-madrid).
