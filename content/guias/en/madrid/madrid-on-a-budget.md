## Free museums in Madrid

**Always free**

- **Temple of Debod:** a 2nd-century BC Egyptian temple, a gift from Egypt. Only at sunset; at midday it's a temple among brick buildings.
- **Faro de Moncloa:** 92 metres and 360° views with a lift, free since 2024.
- **Museum of Romanticism** (Chamberí): a 19th-century mansion with recreated rooms. One hour; closed on Mondays.
- **CaixaForum:** the Herzog & de Meuron building and its vertical garden; some temporary shows are paid.
- **Conde Duque**, **Sala Alcalá 31** and the **Geominero Museum** (minerals and fossils in a spectacular building near Puerta de Alcalá).

**With free hours**

| Museum | Free |
| --- | --- |
| Prado | Monday to Saturday 18:00-20:00; Sundays and holidays 17:00-19:00 |
| Reina Sofía | Monday and Wednesday to Saturday 19:00-21:00; Sundays 12:30-14:30 |
| Thyssen | Mondays 12:00-16:00 (permanent collection) |
| Archaeological, Sorolla, Anthropology, Costume | Saturdays from 14:00 and Sunday mornings |
| Real Academia de Bellas Artes de San Fernando and Fundación Mapfre | Wednesdays / Mondays |
| Lázaro Galdiano | Last hour of the day |

The state museums share a pattern, Saturday afternoon and Sunday morning, so you can combine two in a weekend. At the Prado the free slot saves €15, but it means **queues and packed rooms**: if you'll only see it once in your life, pay and go in at 10:00 when it's empty ([we compare them here](/en/guides/madrid/prado-vs-reina-sofia-vs-thyssen)). If there's no other option, arrive 30 minutes before the free hour.

## Free things to do in Madrid outdoors: viewpoints and parks

- **Temple of Debod and Parque del Oeste** at sunset: Royal Palace, mountains and an orange sun. If Debod is crowded, 50 metres higher up in the park you get the same view.
- **Cerro del Tío Pío** (Vallecas, metro Portazgo or Buenos Aires and a 10-minute walk): the "park of the seven hills", with the whole skyline from the south-east at sunset.
- **Retiro:** the Crystal Palace and Velázquez Palace (free Reina Sofía exhibitions), the Rose Garden in May and October, the Fallen Angel, the 11-M Forest of Remembrance and the lake, where only the boats cost money.
- **Madrid Río:** 30 km of bike lane along the Manzanares with views of the Royal Palace; it starts at the Puente del Rey (metro Príncipe Pío).
- **Casa de Campo:** 1,700 hectares of woodland with a lake and terraces (metro Lago). The cable car isn't worth it.
- **El Capricho** (metro El Capricho): the city's most beautiful and least visited park, an 18th-century garden with a pond, a temple and a Civil War bunker. **Weekends and holidays only.**
- **Sabatini and Campo del Moro:** the Royal Palace gardens, free; Campo del Moro, entered from Paseo de la Virgen del Puerto, is almost empty. The Botanical Garden (€6) isn't free.

## Recurring free events

- **Veranos de la Villa** (July and August): free concerts, open-air cinema and dance all over the city. **Conde Duque** and **Matadero** run free or nominally priced concerts and films all year.
- **Fundación Juan March:** free concerts and talks in an excellent auditorium.
- **El Rastro** (Sunday mornings, La Latina) and the **Mercado de Motores** (second weekend of each month, at the Railway Museum): more for the atmosphere than for shopping.
- **Andén 0**, Chamberí's ghost station (a 1919 metro stop): free, Friday afternoons and Saturdays.

## Neighbourhoods to walk without spending

- **Barrio de las Letras:** quotes in the pavement, Cervantes, Lope and Valle-Inclán's mirrors in the Callejón del Gato.
- **Lavapiés:** the most diverse, best on a Saturday afternoon.
- **Malasaña:** Dos de Mayo, Espíritu Santo, Pez.
- **Chamberí:** Olavide and the market; our favourite for a slow stroll.
- **Conde Duque:** Plaza de las Comendadoras at sunset.
- **Habsburg Madrid:** Plaza Mayor, Plaza de la Villa and Plaza del Cordón, without going in anywhere.

## Madrid on a budget: common mistakes

1. **Seeing the Prado only during free hours** without knowing it'll be packed.
2. **Debod at midday.**
3. **Every plan around Sol:** the free and different stuff is in Chamberí, Lavapiés and La Latina.
4. **Parks at 14:00 in July and August:** go 9:00-12:00 or after 19:00.
5. **Free isn't cheap:** if you end up with a €30 paella in Plaza Mayor, the plan has failed. A €10-12 set menu in Chamberí, a calamari sandwich in a neighbourhood bar or a picnic in the Retiro.
6. **The "free" clubs around Sol and Gran Vía:** €10 for the first drink. Better a neighbourhood bar with a €2.50 beer.

## What's worth paying for

1. **The Prado at 10:00 (€15).** The best ticket in Madrid: Velázquez with nobody in front of you.
2. **El Escorial and the Valley of Cuelgamuros** (€14 and €9): 50 km away and another world. Without a car, day trips cost €50-70 with all transport.
3. **An honest flamenco show (€30-40)**, not the Plaza Mayor ones with dinner: [which tablao to choose](/en/guides/madrid/flamenco-madrid-which-tablao-to-choose).
4. **The Círculo de Bellas Artes rooftop (€5):** better views than any trendy rooftop, at a tenth of the price.
