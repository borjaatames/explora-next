Ten days in Spain is enough for the trip most people want the first time: **Madrid, Toledo, Seville, Córdoba, Granada and Barcelona**, with no car and just the right number of nights in each. It comes down to three decisions: fly into one city and out of another, see Córdoba on the way from Seville to Granada, and fly from Granada to Barcelona instead of spending most of a day on the train.

## The route at a glance

| Day | City | What to do | Overnight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Madrid | Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor, Gran Vía, sunset at the Temple of Debod | Madrid |
| 2 | Madrid | Prado, Retiro Park, Barrio de las Letras, flamenco | Madrid |
| 3 | Toledo | Day trip by Avant train: Cathedral, Jewish quarter, Mirador del Valle | Madrid |
| 4 | Madrid → Seville | Morning high-speed train; Santa Cruz and Plaza de España | Seville |
| 5 | Seville | Real Alcázar at 9:30, Cathedral, Triana | Seville |
| 6 | Seville → Córdoba → Granada | Mosque-Cathedral and Jewish quarter; afternoon train to Granada | Granada |
| 7 | Granada | Alhambra; Albaicín and San Nicolás at sunset | Granada |
| 8 | Granada → Barcelona | Morning flight; Gothic Quarter and El Born | Barcelona |
| 9 | Barcelona | Sagrada Família at 9:00, Passeig de Gràcia, Park Güell | Barcelona |
| 10 | Barcelona | Montjuïc or Barceloneta; fly home | — |

That's 9 nights. If your flight leaves on day 11, add the night in Barcelona and use the day for [Montserrat](/en/guides/barcelona/montserrat-which-excursion-to-choose).

## Trains and flights between cities

| Leg | How | Time | Typical fare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Madrid → Toledo (return) | Avant from Atocha | 33 min | €22-26 return |
| Madrid → Seville | Renfe AVE, Iryo, Ouigo | 2 h 30-2 h 45 direct | from about €10-20 booked ahead; €60-100 last minute (approx.) |
| Seville → Córdoba | AVE, Avant, Iryo, Ouigo; about 40 a day | 40-45 min | from about €10 booked ahead |
| Córdoba → Granada | Direct train, few departures a day | 1 h 30 | from about €23 |
| Granada → Barcelona | Direct flight (Vueling, Iberia) | 1 h 25 | from about €35; average €110-120 |
| Granada → Barcelona | Direct AVE, very few departures | about 6 h 30 | €110-160 (approx.) |
| Madrid → Barcelona | AVE, Iryo, Ouigo; 20+ a day | from 2 h 30 | from €9-18 booked ahead |

Three companies now compete on Madrid-Seville and Madrid-Barcelona (Renfe, Iryo and Ouigo), so check all three before buying: two or three weeks out, under €30 is easy. The one to watch is **Córdoba-Granada**, with few departures: plan day 6 around that timetable.

## Days 1-3: Madrid and Toledo

**Day 1.** **Royal Palace at 10:00** (€18), Plaza Mayor, lunch in La Latina, Gran Vía and sunset at the **Temple of Debod**. **Day 2:** **the Prado at 10:00** (€15), Retiro Park and the Barrio de las Letras; at night, a tablao (we help you pick one in [flamenco in Madrid](/en/guides/madrid/flamenco-madrid-which-tablao-to-choose)). The hour-by-hour plan is in [Madrid in 2 days](/en/guides/madrid/madrid-in-2-days).

**Day 3: Toledo.** Avant from Atocha (33 minutes) and at the **Cathedral (€12)** by 10:00, then Santo Tomé, Santa María la Blanca and the Jewish quarter; finish at the **Mirador del Valle**. Avoid Mondays (the Sephardic Museum is closed) and Sunday mornings (the Cathedral doesn't open to visitors until 14:00). ## Days 4-5: Seville

**Day 4.** High-speed train around 9:00 and in Seville before noon. Spend the afternoon in **Santa Cruz** and at **Plaza de España** as the sun drops; flamenco at night (see [which tablao to choose in Seville](/en/guides/sevilla/flamenco-seville-which-tablao-to-choose)).

**Day 5.** **Real Alcázar at 9:30**, the first slot (2 h 30 with the gardens), then the **Cathedral and Giralda** opposite (€13 online). In the afternoon, cross the river to **Triana**. Details in [Seville in 2 days](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-in-2-days).

## Day 6: Córdoba on the way to Granada

Early train (40-45 minutes), drop your bag at a luggage storage point near the station and walk 20-25 minutes to the **Mosque-Cathedral** (€15; allow 1-1 h 30). Then the **Jewish quarter**, Calleja de las Flores and the **Roman bridge**. Salmorejo and flamenquín for lunch and, mid-afternoon, the train to Granada (1 h 30). It's a long day, but it saves a night and a hotel change. If you'd rather sleep in Córdoba, our [Andalusia 7-day itinerary](/en/guides/sevilla/andalusia-7-day-itinerary) does it that way. More in [Córdoba in one day](/en/guides/cordoba/cordoba-in-one-day).

## Day 7: Granada and the Alhambra

The whole day turns on your **Nasrid Palaces** time slot: you only get 30 minutes' leeway, and it's a 30-45 minute walk from the entrance. The Alcazaba, Nasrid Palaces and Generalife take about 4 hours. In the afternoon, the Royal Chapel and the **Albaicín**, with sunset at the San Nicolás viewpoint and a free tapa with every drink. More in [Granada in 2 days](/en/guides/granada/granada-in-2-days).

## Days 8-10: Barcelona

**Day 8.** Morning flight (1 h 25) and an afternoon in the **Gothic Quarter** and **El Born**. **Day 9:** **Sagrada Família at 9:00**, the Hour of Silence (1 h 30-2 h inside), Passeig de Gràcia and **Park Güell** in the afternoon. **Day 10:** Montjuïc or Barceloneta, depending on the weather and your flight. ## Variations

- **Andalusia and Málaga, no Barcelona.** Madrid (3 nights), Seville (2), Córdoba (1), Granada (2) and Málaga (2): Granada to Málaga is a direct 1 h 18 Avant. Fly home from Málaga, the south's busiest airport. Ideas in [Málaga in two days](/en/guides/malaga/malaga-in-two-days).
- **With Valencia, no Andalusia.** Madrid (3 nights with Toledo), Valencia (2) and Barcelona (4, with Montserrat): all in a line, with short legs, Madrid-Valencia in about 2 h and Valencia-Barcelona in about 3 h 10. More sea, less summer heat; Andalusia deserves its own trip. See [Valencia in 2 days](/en/guides/valencia/valencia-in-2-days).
- **More time around Madrid.** If central Spain interests you more than the south, do Madrid, Toledo and Segovia in 4 days ([itinerary here](/en/guides/madrid/madrid-toledo-segovia-4-days)), then Seville, Granada and Barcelona.
- **In reverse.** Flying into Barcelona and out of Madrid works just as well. In December 2025 Ouigo launched a daily direct Barcelona-Seville train of about 6 hours, stopping in Madrid and Córdoba; check it's still running on your dates.

That excludes your flights in and out. Buying trains late can quietly add €150 per person.

## Common mistakes

1. **Buying the Alhambra or Sagrada Família on arrival:** in season they're gone, and they're the two visits many people make the trip for.
2. **Taking the train from Granada to Barcelona:** about 6 h 30. By plane, 1 h 25 plus transfers.
3. **A return flight to the same city:** it forces you to backtrack and costs you almost a day.
4. **Renting a car for the whole route:** expensive parking and old towns closed to traffic.
5. **Trying to squeeze in the north, Portugal or the coast:** in ten days, five cities is already the sensible maximum.
6. **Going in July and August:** Seville and Córdoba top 40 °C. March to June or September to November is better.

## In one sentence

Fly into Madrid, take the high-speed train south, fly from Granada to Barcelona and home from there, with the Alhambra, Sagrada Família and Alcázar booked before take-off: that's how 10 days in Spain covers a lot without living in train stations.
