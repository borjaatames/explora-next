It comes down to three decisions: **fly into one city and out of another**, see **Córdoba on the way** from Seville to Granada, and **fly from Granada to Barcelona** instead of spending a whole morning on the train.

## The 10-day Spain itinerary at a glance

| Day | City | What to do | Night |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Madrid | Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor, Gran Vía, sunset at Debod | Madrid |
| 2 | Madrid | Prado, Retiro, Barrio de las Letras, flamenco | Madrid |
| 3 | Toledo | Day trip by Avant: Cathedral, Jewish Quarter, Mirador del Valle | Madrid |
| 4 | Madrid → Seville | Morning AVE; Santa Cruz and Plaza de España | Seville |
| 5 | Seville | Real Alcázar at 9:30, Cathedral, Triana | Seville |
| 6 | Seville → Córdoba → Granada | Mezquita and Jewish Quarter; afternoon train to Granada | Granada |
| 7 | Granada | Alhambra; Albaicín and San Nicolás at sunset | Granada |
| 8 | Granada → Barcelona | Morning flight; Gothic Quarter and Born | Barcelona |
| 9 | Barcelona | Sagrada Família at 9:00, Passeig de Gràcia, Park Güell | Barcelona |
| 10 | Barcelona | Montjuïc or Barceloneta; flight home | — |

That's 9 nights. If your flight leaves on day 11, add the night in Barcelona and use the day for [Montserrat](/en/guides/barcelona/montserrat-which-excursion-to-choose).

## 10 days in Spain, day by day

### Day 1: Madrid

**Royal Palace at 10:00** (€18), Plaza Mayor, lunch in La Latina, Gran Vía and sunset at the **Temple of Debod**. The hour-by-hour plan is in [Madrid in 2 days](/en/guides/madrid/madrid-in-2-days).

### Day 2: the Prado and a flamenco night

**Prado at 10:00** (€15), the Retiro and the Barrio de las Letras. In the evening, a tablao: we help you choose in [flamenco in Madrid](/en/guides/madrid/flamenco-madrid-which-tablao-to-choose).

### Day 3: Toledo

Avant from Atocha (33 minutes) and at 10:00 in the **Cathedral (€12)**, Santo Tomé, Santa María la Blanca and the Jewish Quarter; finish at the **Mirador del Valle**. Avoid Mondays (the Sephardic Museum is closed) and Sunday mornings (the Cathedral only opens to visitors at 14:00). Everything in [Toledo day trip from Madrid](/en/guides/madrid/toledo-day-trip-from-madrid); if you're torn, [we compare Toledo and Segovia here](/en/guides/madrid/toledo-or-segovia-from-madrid).

### Day 4: Madrid to Seville

AVE around 9:00, in Seville before noon. Afternoon in the **Santa Cruz quarter** and the **Plaza de España** as the sun goes down; flamenco at night (see [which tablao to choose in Seville](/en/guides/sevilla/flamenco-seville-which-tablao-to-choose)).

### Day 5: Seville

**Real Alcázar at 9:30**, the first slot (2 h 30 with the gardens), and opposite, the **Cathedral and the Giralda** (€13 online). In the afternoon, cross to **Triana**. Details in [Seville in 2 days](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-in-2-days).

### Day 6: Córdoba on the way to Granada

Early train (40-45 minutes), luggage in a locker by the station and a 20-25 minute walk to the **Mosque-Cathedral** (€15; allow 1 h 30-2 h). Then the **Jewish Quarter**, Calleja de las Flores and the **Roman bridge**. Lunch of salmorejo and flamenquín and, mid-afternoon, the train to Granada (1 h 30). It's a long day, but it saves a night and a hotel change. If you'd rather sleep in Córdoba, [Andalusia in 7 days](/en/guides/sevilla/andalusia-7-day-itinerary) does it that way. More in [Córdoba in one day](/en/guides/cordoba/cordoba-in-one-day).

### Day 7: Granada and the Alhambra

The whole day revolves around your **Nasrid Palaces** time: there are only 30 minutes' leeway, and it takes 30-45 minutes on foot from the entrance. Alcazaba, Nasrid Palaces and Generalife add up to about 4 hours. In the afternoon, the Royal Chapel and the **Albaicín**, with sunset at the San Nicolás viewpoint and free tapas with your beer. More ideas in [Granada in 2 days](/en/guides/granada/granada-in-2-days).

### Day 8: flight to Barcelona

Morning flight (1 h 25) and an afternoon in the **Gothic Quarter** and the **Born**.

### Day 9: Sagrada Família and Park Güell

**Sagrada Família at 9:00**, the Hour of Silence (1 h 30-2 h inside), Passeig de Gràcia and **Park Güell** in the afternoon.

### Day 10: Montjuïc or Barceloneta

Depending on the weather and your flight time. The full plan is in [Barcelona in 3 days](/en/guides/barcelona/barcelona-in-3-days).

## Spain by train: journeys and prices

| Journey | How | Time | Rough price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Madrid → Toledo (return) | Avant from Atocha | 33 min | €22-26 return |
| Madrid → Seville | AVE, Iryo, Ouigo | 2 h 30-2 h 45 direct | from about €10-20 booked ahead; €60-100 last minute (approx.) |
| Seville → Córdoba | AVE, Avant, Iryo, Ouigo; about 40 a day | 40-45 min | from about €10 booked ahead |
| Córdoba → Granada | Direct train, few departures | 1 h 30 | from about €23 |
| Granada → Barcelona | Direct flight (Vueling, Iberia) | 1 h 25 | from about €35; average €110-120 |
| Granada → Barcelona | Direct AVE, very few departures | about 6 h 30 | €110-160 (approx.) |

Three operators compete on Madrid-Seville and Madrid-Barcelona (Renfe, Iryo and Ouigo): check all three before buying. With two or three weeks' notice it's easy to travel for under €30. Watch the **Córdoba-Granada** leg above all, with few departures: plan day 6 around it.

## Variations

- **Andalusia and Málaga, no Barcelona.** Madrid (3 nights), Seville (2), Córdoba (1), Granada (2) and Málaga (2): there is a direct 1 h 18 Avant from Granada to Málaga, and you fly home from Málaga. Ideas in [Málaga in two days](/en/guides/malaga/malaga-in-two-days).
- **With Valencia, no Andalusia.** Madrid (3 nights with Toledo), Valencia (2) and Barcelona (4, with Montserrat): Madrid-Valencia in about 2 h and Valencia-Barcelona in about 3 h 10. More sea and less heat in summer. See [Valencia in 2 days](/en/guides/valencia/valencia-in-2-days).
- **More time in Madrid.** [Madrid, Toledo and Segovia in 4 days](/en/guides/madrid/madrid-toledo-segovia-4-days), then Seville, Granada and Barcelona.
- **The other way round.** Flying into Barcelona and out of Madrid works just as well. In December 2025 Ouigo launched a daily direct Barcelona-Seville train of about 6 hours, stopping in Madrid and Córdoba; check it is still running on your dates.

## Budget and tips

| Item (per person) | 10 days |
| --- | --- |
| Trains and Granada-Barcelona flight | €110-280 |
| Tickets (Royal Palace, Prado, Toledo Cathedral, Alcázar, Seville Cathedral, Mezquita, Alhambra, Sagrada Família, Park Güell) | about €160 |
| One flamenco show | €25-45 |
| Lunches and dinners | €350-500 |
| Mid-range hotel, double room, 9 nights | €540-990 |
| **Total, excluding flights in and out** | **about €1,150-1,950** |

- **Book the Alhambra and the Sagrada Família before you leave:** in high season there are none left on arrival.
- **Buy trains early:** leaving it late can add €150 per person.
- **Fly into one city and out of another:** returning to the same one costs you almost a day.
- **Don't rent a car for the whole route:** expensive parking and old towns closed to traffic.
- **Don't add the north, Portugal or the coast:** with ten days, five cities is already the sensible maximum.
- **Avoid July and August:** Seville and Córdoba go above 40 °C. Better March to June or September to November.
