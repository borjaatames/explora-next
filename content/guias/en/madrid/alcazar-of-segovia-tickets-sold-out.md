## What you'll see

- **The Palace and the Artillery Museum:** the core of the visit, about **40-45 minutes**.
- **The Tower of Juan II:** **152 steps** up a narrow spiral staircase with no landings. From the top you see the cathedral, the mountains and the Eresma valley. The visit lasts 25 minutes at most and is not accessible for people with reduced mobility.

There's no Tower-only ticket: it always comes with the full ticket.

## Alcázar of Segovia ticket price and opening hours

Tickets are sold on the official website, **entradas.alcazardesegovia.com**. 2026 prices:

- **Palace + Artillery Museum:** €7 general, €5 reduced.
- **Full ticket (Palace + Museum + Tower of Juan II):** €10 general, €8 reduced.
- **Reduced:** ages 6 to 16, students, large families, unemployed people, over-65s and people with a disability of 33% or more.
- **Free:** children under 6 and people with a disability of 65% or more, with a companion if needed.
- **Extras:** audio guide in 12 languages or guided tour in Spanish, €3.50 each. Ask for them at the ticket office even if you bought your ticket online.

**Opening hours:** 1 November to 31 March, **10:00 to 18:00**; 1 April to 31 October, **10:00 to 19:30**. The ticket office and entrance close 30 minutes earlier. On 24 and 31 December it opens from 10:00 to 14:30, and it's closed on 25 December.

## Alcázar of Segovia free entry: Tuesdays and what you see without a ticket

If you're an **EU citizen**, the Palace and the Museum are **free on Tuesdays from 14:00 to 16:00**, except public holidays. You get the ticket at the ticket office during that slot; it depends on capacity and doesn't include the Tower.

You can also see a lot without a ticket:

- The **esplanade, moat and entrance bridge**, free to access.
- The **gardens on Plaza de la Reina Victoria Eugenia**, next to the Alcázar, open 8:00 to 22:00.
- **The view from below**: walk down to the Eresma valley, by the Vera Cruz church, to see the whole Alcázar on its rock. At night it's lit up, best seen from the Altos de la Piedad road.

## If Alcázar of Segovia tickets are sold out

The official website itself warns that tickets sell out at weekends, on long weekends and on public holidays, especially those including the Tower. There's almost always a way in that day:

1. **Check the official website again** and, if the full ticket doesn't appear, buy **Palace + Museum**: it's the main part of the visit.
2. **Go to the ticket office**, which sells for the same day. In high season the queue grows by mid-morning: arrive at opening time or after 16:00.
3. **Book a day trip with the ticket included** if you're coming from Madrid: the operator books in advance and takes you there and back. Check it says **"Alcázar entry included"**: some trips only pass by outside.

## Getting there from Madrid

- **High-speed train (Avant)** from Chamartín to Segovia-Guiomar: under 30 minutes. The station is about 13 km from the centre; city bus 11 drops you at the Aqueduct.
- **La Sepulvedana bus** from the Moncloa interchange: just over an hour, with departures all day, dropping you 10 minutes' walk from the Aqueduct.

From the Aqueduct to the Alcázar it's about 20 minutes on foot along Calle Real, past the Plaza Mayor and the Cathedral.

## Tips

- **Buy only on the official website.** Some resellers list prices that don't exist, such as Tower-only tickets.
- **If you want the Tower, buy early:** it sells out first and has limited capacity.
- **Think twice with small children or bad knees:** the 152 spiral steps are narrow with no landings.
- **Don't go for free entry on a Tuesday that's a public holiday:** there's no free entry that day.
