## Quick answer by who's travelling

- **Couple:** group. Toledo and Segovia in a day for €41 each, or Toledo with tickets for €79.
- **Family of 4:** group, or independently by train if the kids don't do well on coaches. Private El Escorial would cost you €1,480 against €248 in a group.
- **Group of 6:** group still wins on price (€62 against €320 per person for El Escorial). Private only makes sense if someone needs door-to-door pick-up.
- **First time in Madrid:** Toledo with a guide and tickets. It's the trip people remember most.
- **Tight budget:** Segovia and Toledo from €41, or the train on your own.

## How many people does it take for a private day trip from Madrid to be worth it?

**In Madrid, never.** Private El Escorial barely drops per person because it includes an official guide and tickets for everyone: from €410 for 2 to €320 for 6. The extra cost against the group tour is **€348 per person for a couple, €308 for 4 and €258 for 6**. No group size gets close.

The cheap "private" alternative is **going independently**: the Avant to Toledo takes 33 minutes from Atocha and the one to Segovia about 30 from Chamartín (plus 15 minutes on the city bus to the aqueduct). We cover it in detail in [Toledo or Segovia from Madrid](/en/guides/madrid/toledo-or-segovia-from-madrid).

## Madrid day trips by group tour or private: pros and cons

### Group

- **For:** price, tickets included on most, and volume of reviews: Segovia and Toledo has over 10,000; Ávila and Segovia, over 4,800.
- **Against:** departure from a fixed point in the centre (Calle San Bernardo or Las Ventas, depending on the tour), a guide explaining in Spanish and English to the same group, and long days: Segovia and Toledo takes 12 hours.

### Private

- **For:** pick-up and drop-off at your hotel, an official guide just for you for 4 hours and tickets included at El Escorial. No waiting for coaches.
- **Against:** price and limited choice. We currently have no private trip to Toledo, Segovia or Ávila.

### Small group (the middle ground)

The **Toledo and Consuegra windmills** tour goes by minivan for €125, with a guide in Spanish or Italian and audio in other languages. It needs a minimum of 4 people to run.

## Who each one suits

- **Group:** almost everyone, especially couples and solo travellers.
- **Independently by train:** those who want to go at their own pace and spend little; ideal for Toledo or Segovia with older children.
- **Private El Escorial tour:** older people or those with reduced mobility, families who don't want to cross Madrid to the meeting point, or anyone wanting an exclusive guide.
- **Wine with private transport:** Chinchón with winery and lunch (€200) or Ribera del Duero (€250, groups of up to 8), for a different kind of day.

## Common mistakes

1. **Doing Segovia and Toledo in one day and expecting to see them in depth.** It's an overview; if one interests you, give it the whole day.
2. **Paying for private just to avoid the coach.** The train to Toledo or Segovia gives you that freedom for much less.
3. **Not checking the departure point.** Each tour leaves from a different place; some on the edge of the centre, like Las Ventas.
4. **Expecting a Spanish-only or English-only guide.** On big tours they explain in two languages to the same group.
5. **Booking the train at the last minute.** The first morning Avant trains fill up.
