## What you'll see in the Primate Cathedral

It's the most expensive visit in Toledo and also the one that most justifies the price. The general ticket includes:

- **The Gothic church and its cloister**, with the choir and the main chapel altarpiece.
- **The Transparente** and the **chapter house**.
- **The sacristy with the El Grecos**, *The Disrobing of Christ* among them, and a picture gallery with works by Goya, Raphael, Titian and Caravaggio.
- **The treasury**, with the **Monstrance of Enrique de Arfe**.

**The tower** only opens at certain times of year and isn't always available with the ticket: if you're interested, ask at the ticket office before buying.

## Toledo Cathedral entrance fee and when it's free

The **"Visita Catedral Primada" ticket costs €12** on the official website, **tickets.catedralprimada.es**.

- **Reduced and children:** according to the rates published by the Chapter, the reduced ticket (over-65s, students, people with disabilities and large families) costs **€8** and ages 8 to 14, **€6**. The official website no longer publishes the rate table and only sells general admission online: ask for the reduced ticket at the ticket office with ID.
- **Free:** only registered Toledo residents, with ID. There's no free day for tourists; you can go in to pray during services (for example, the 9:00 Mass in the Mozarabic rite), but only to the prayer area.
- **No refunds:** except for problems at the entrance. Buy once your day is fixed.

**Where to buy:** on the website you choose a day and pay by card, skipping the queue on long weekends and weekends. The **ticket office** is in the **Chapter's official shop, opposite the Puerta Llana**, not at the main door; on weekdays there's usually little queue. Beware of websites imitating the official one (catedralprimada.es): there are resellers with similar names and surcharges.

The daytime visit **hardly ever sells out**: the issue is usually Sunday hours and Masses, not a lack of places.

## Toledo Cathedral opening hours

- **Monday to Saturday:** **10:00 to 18:30**.
- **Sundays:** **14:00 to 18:30**. In the morning there are Masses and no visits.
- **Last entry:** half an hour before closing.
- **Closed:** 1 January, 25 December and when a celebration requires it, with prior notice.
- **Special hours:** 6 and 23 January, Palm Sunday, Holy Tuesday, Easter Sunday, 1 November and 8 December, 14:00 to 18:30. Maundy Thursday and Good Friday, 11:00 to 17:00. On Corpus Christi, only 16:30 to 18:30. On 24 and 31 December, 10:00 to 14:00.

If you go on a Sunday or at Corpus Christi, fill the morning with other monuments and leave the Cathedral for the afternoon. Our suggested order is in [things to do in Toledo](/en/guides/madrid/things-to-do-in-toledo).

## Lumina, the night-time visit to Toledo Cathedral

At night, the Cathedral offers **Lumina**, a route of about **50 minutes** with light, sound and projections. It costs **€24.90**, or **€33 combined with the daytime ticket**. This one does sell out: capacity is limited and it's best booked days ahead, especially at weekends.

## Toledo Cathedral guided tour and getting there

The general ticket includes an **audio guide on your phone**, so bring headphones. Even so, the Cathedral has so much (chapels, grilles, choir stalls, paintings) that you get far more out of it with a guide. The guided tour with entry included lasts an hour and a half to two hours and costs from €25.

From Madrid, the [Madrid-Toledo train](/en/guides/madrid/madrid-to-toledo-train) takes **33 minutes**. Take one arriving before 10:00 if you want to go in at opening time, when it's quietest.

## Tips

1. **Don't go on a Sunday morning:** until 14:00 there are only services.
2. **Don't count on the tourist wristband:** the €14 one doesn't include the Cathedral.
3. **Don't arrive right at closing time:** entry ends half an hour before, at 18:00.
4. **Don't buy Lumina on the day at weekends:** it's usually full.
5. **Dress modestly:** the Cathedral may refuse entry otherwise.
