## When the Prado is free: opening hours

| Day | Free hours | Last entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday to Saturday | 18:00 to 20:00 | 19:30 |
| Sundays and holidays | 17:00 to 19:00 | 18:30 |
| 6 January, 24 and 31 December | Closes at 14:00 | — |
| 1 January, 1 May and 25 December | Closed | — |

- **Only the permanent collection is free.** Temporary exhibitions are half price in those hours.
- **Entry closes half an hour early** and rooms are cleared 10 minutes before closing. If you get in at 18:15, you have barely an hour and a half.
- **There's no weekly closing day.** It's open on Mondays too, a good day for the free hours.

## Who gets in free at any time

If you're in one of these groups, you don't need the free hours. According to the museum's website:

- Under-18s.
- Students aged 18 to 25.
- The unemployed and working teachers.
- People with a disability of 33% or more.
- Large families as defined by the museum (one adult with three children, or two if one has a disability).
- Official tourist guides, journalists and some professional groups.

You must **show valid ID at the box office**. Over-65s, youth card holders and other large-family members pay the reduced rate, **€7.50**.

## Free days and El Prado de noche

On some days the Prado is free all day. The usual ones are **18 May** (International Museum Day), **12 October** and **19 November**, the museum's anniversary. The museum confirms them each year on its website. Those days have the longest queues of the year.

**El Prado de noche:** on the **first Saturday of every month**, from **20:30 to 23:30**, the Jerónimos building galleries open for free, entering through the **Jerónimos Gate**, last entry 23:00. It's first come, first served until capacity is reached.

## The free-entry queue: when to arrive

Free entry **can't be booked online**: the official site, entradas.museodelprado.es, only sells paid timed tickets. Free tickets are handed out **at the box office until capacity is reached**. Be wary of websites offering to "book the Prado free hour" with a booking fee.

The queue forms by the **Goya Gate**, on the north side of the building, long before the free hour. On Sundays, holidays and long weekends the wait can **exceed an hour**.

- **Weekdays:** arrive at least **30 minutes early**. Monday to Thursday, outside long weekends, is usually best.
- **Sundays and holidays:** more waiting and less museum time, as it closes at 19:00.
- **Don't arrive at 18:00 sharp:** by the time you reach the box office you'll have lost a good chunk of your time.

## What to see in the Prado in two free hours

In practice you have an hour and a quarter to an hour and a half. Grab the map at the entrance and go straight to:

1. **Velázquez** (floor 1): *Las Meninas*, *The Spinners* and the court portraits. Start here: it's the room that fills up most.
2. **Goya** (floor 1): *The Family of Charles IV* and the two *Majas*.
3. **Goya on floor 0:** *The Second of May*, *The Third of May* and the **Black Paintings**, including *Saturn*.
4. **Bosch** (floor 0): *The Garden of Earthly Delights*. Give it ten minutes.
5. **If you have time left:** Van der Weyden's *Descent from the Cross* or El Greco's *Nobleman with his Hand on his Chest*.

## Tips and alternatives

- **The free hours don't include temporary exhibitions** (they're half price).
- **On 24 and 31 December and 6 January** it closes at 14:00: no free evening hours.
- **Bring your ID, student card or certificate** if you're entering free as part of a group.
- **Other free museums nearby:** the **Reina Sofía** (Monday and Wednesday to Saturday 19:00-21:00; Sundays 12:30-14:30) and the **Thyssen** (Mondays 12:00-16:00). On a Monday you can combine the Thyssen at midday with the Prado from 18:00; the Reina Sofía overlaps with the Prado, so leave it for another day. Compare them in [Prado vs Reina Sofía vs Thyssen](/en/guides/madrid/prado-vs-reina-sofia-vs-thyssen) and find more ideas in [Madrid on a budget](/en/guides/madrid/madrid-on-a-budget).
- **If it's your only Prado visit, pay.** A general ticket costs **€15** with no time limit: at 10:00, with the museum half empty, you'll see in three hours what the free slot can't fit. If the official site has no slot, there are resale tickets from €18 and a [guided tour with ticket](/en/cities/madrid/activities/prado-museum-guided-tour) from €49; we explain it in [Prado Museum tickets](/en/guides/madrid/prado-museum-tickets-sold-out).
