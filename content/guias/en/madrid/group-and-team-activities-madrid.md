| Jewish Madrid, walking tour | €270 | €300 | €450 | €75 |

## Private tour of Madrid for a group: the walking tours

They offer the best value per group:

- **Gran Vía (2 h):** from the Centenary Model, next to Plaza de España, to Plaza de Callao, with early 20th-century architecture and historic cinemas. It is **accessible for wheelchairs and pushchairs**, useful for groups with older people.
- **Jewish Madrid (2 h):** a history of more than a thousand years, barely visible in the city, told from the Jewish Community of Madrid. A niche tour for groups interested in history.

## Tuk-tuks for groups of 6 to 10

The electric tuk-tuks leave from **Plaza de Cánovas del Castillo**, opposite the Thyssen Museum, with an audio guide in 11 languages, water and wifi.

- **Short route:** Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor, Sol, La Latina and Gran Vía.
- **Full route:** adds the Bernabéu and the Salamanca district.
- **Party tuk-tuk:** for a celebration with music, €150 per vehicle.

Book all the tuk-tuks for the same time so the group travels together. **The booking is non-refundable**: make it once your numbers are confirmed.

## Private day trip from Madrid for a group: El Escorial and Cuelgamuros

The driver and an **official guide** pick you up at your hotel at 9:00 or 10:00 and take you to the Monastery of El Escorial (basilica, library, royal halls and Pantheon of Kings) and the Valley of Cuelgamuros. Tickets are included; lunch is not. It takes about **five and a half hours** in total.

Note: **the monastery is closed on Mondays** and is not wheelchair accessible.

## Team building and corporate activities in Madrid

For a dinner, an after-work outing or a team's free morning:

- **Century-old taverns, private (€127 per person):** 2 h 10 min through taverns more than one and two hundred years old, with two drinks and two tapas per person. Minimum age 12. It works very well as an aperitif before a group dinner.
- **Wine and tapas in hidden bars (€90 per person):** four stops with at least one glass of wine at each, in a small group of up to 10 per booking.
- **Paella and sangria workshop (€64 per person, 3 h, next to Sol):** the whole group cooks and the paella is lunch or dinner. It's a **shared group**, so there may be other participants.
- **Family winery with a sommelier (€110 per person, 4 h 30 min):** for groups of up to 8.
- **Private cultural visits:** the **Royal Palace** (€141 per person, minimum 2) includes an official guide, skip-the-line entry and hotel pick-up, and you choose the start time; the **Prado Museum** (€146 per person) lasts three hours. A good option for clients or business visitors with a free morning.

## Tips and common mistakes

- **Book ahead:** two or three weeks for groups of more than 8, especially in spring and autumn.
- **One person coordinates:** they book and share each activity's meeting point with the group.
- **Mix plans:** a private tour in the morning and the taverns in the evening works better than two long activities on the same day.
- **Accessibility:** the Gran Vía tour is accessible; the tuk-tuks, El Escorial and the taverns are not.
- **Don't compare a private tour's "from" price with a group tour's per-person price:** from 4 or 6 people, private usually works out cheaper than it looks.
- **Don't book the tuk-tuks before the list is final**, and don't plan El Escorial on a Monday.
