## The five big family days out in Madrid

| Plan | Best age | Online price | Time | Getting there |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zoo Aquarium (Casa de Campo) | 4-16 | From €20; free under 3 | Full day (5-7 h) | Metro L10 Casa de Campo + 15 min walk, or bus 33; free parking |
| Parque de Atracciones (Casa de Campo) | 8-16 (kids' area 4-7) | From €24 | Full day (6-8 h) | Same as the Zoo |
| Faunia (Vicálvaro) | 4-12 | From €17 | 4-5 h | Metro L9 Valdebernardo + 15 min walk, or bus 71; paid parking |
| Parque Warner (San Martín de la Vega) | 8-16 | From €33 | Full day, 45 min each way | Car, bus from Estación Sur (Méndez Álvaro) or Cercanías C-3 to Pinto + bus 413 |
| Aquópolis Villanueva (summer) | 4-16 | From €20 | Morning plus a restful afternoon | Car (45 min) or bus 627 from Moncloa |

**Zoo Aquarium.** The most reliable plan at any age: birds of prey, sea lions, pandas and an aquarium (the dolphins were moved to another park in January 2025). Arrive at **11:00**, when it opens: after 13:00 the animals hide. Shows run at fixed times, so check the schedule when you go in. You can bring your own food and there are picnic areas by the old dolphinarium.

**Parque de Atracciones.** For kids aged 8 and up, the best memory of the trip. Little ones have **Nickelodeon Land**, good for half a day. Buy online in advance and avoid spring Saturdays (45-minute queues for the big rides). It closes for part of the winter.

**Faunia.** Recreated ecosystems instead of cages: the night forest and the penguins are a hit with small children. There's a combined ticket with the Zoo for separate days. Best in spring and autumn: in the heat, the animals hide.

**Parque Warner.** Serious roller coasters (Superman, Stunt Fall) and a large kids' area. It's pricier and closes for much of the winter. Worth it on a 4-5 day trip; with 2-3 days, the Parque de Atracciones gives the same thrill without losing a day to logistics.

**Aquópolis.** Only from June to early September (in 2026 it closed on the 6th): slides for every age, a wave pool and a lazy river.

## Museums that actually work with kids

- **Natural Sciences** (Chamberí): Madrid's best museum for children, with Triceratops and Diplodocus skeletons and interactive rooms. **€7** adults, **€3.50** ages 4-18, free under 4. Also free on Tuesdays 15:00-17:00 and Sundays 18:30-20:00 (except 1 July to 14 September). 2-3 hours; closed on Mondays that aren't public holidays.
- **Anthropology** (Atocha): mummies and models of villages around the world. Small, 1 h 30, for ages 6-14. **€3**, free on Saturdays from 14:00 and Sunday mornings.
- **CaixaForum** (Paseo del Prado): temporary shows on video games, science or photography, free or very cheap. Ages 8-16; check what's on before you go.
- **Railway Museum** (Delicias): locomotives and carriages you can climb into. **€7** adults, **€4** ages 4-12. On the second weekend of each month (except July and August) it hosts the Mercado de Motores and normal visits close.

**The ones that don't:** the Prado, Reina Sofía and Thyssen don't work with under-10s ([we explain why in our comparison](/en/guides/madrid/prado-vs-reina-sofia-vs-thyssen)). With interested teenagers, do the Prado in 1 h 30: *Las Meninas*, the Black Paintings and Velázquez. The **Royal Palace**, from age 10.

## Kids activities in Madrid outdoors, free or nearly

- **Retiro:** rowing boats on the lake (**€6** on weekdays and **€8** at weekends and on holidays per boat, 45 minutes; under-14s with an adult), free puppet shows at weekends near Puerta de Alcalá, a carousel and bike hire. The Crystal Palace is closed for works in 2026 (you can see it from outside).
- **Madrid Río:** 30 km of bike lane along the Manzanares (BiciMAD only from age 14), slides, a skate park and, in summer, a river beach with water play.
- **Casa de Campo:** 1,700 hectares with a lake, boats, easy trails from age 5-6 and picnic areas.
- **El Capricho:** an 18th-century garden with a pond, a temple and a Civil War bunker. Free, weekends and holidays only. For explorers aged 6 to 14.

## Itineraries by age

**3 days with kids aged 4 to 8**

1. Zoo Aquarium, full day.
2. Retiro in the morning (boats, puppets) and Plaza Mayor with an ice cream in the afternoon.
3. Faunia or Natural Sciences in the morning; free afternoon.

**3 days with kids aged 9 to 12**

1. Parque de Atracciones, full day.
2. Natural Sciences in the morning and Retiro in the afternoon.
3. [Toledo as a family](/en/guides/madrid/toledo-day-trip-from-madrid).

**4-5 days with teenagers aged 13 to 16**

1. Centre (Plaza Mayor, Sol, Gran Vía) and a free afternoon.
2. Parque Warner.
3. Short Prado visit (1 h 30) and a stroll in the afternoon.
4. Bernabéu and a free afternoon.
5. Aquópolis (summer) or Toledo (rest of the year).

## Tips and common mistakes

1. **One big activity a day**, plus something light. Nobody lasts four plans in a row.
2. **Don't combine the Zoo and the Parque de Atracciones** on the same day, even though they're next door.
3. **Buy online** for the Zoo, Atracciones and Warner: you save money and the ticket queue with tired kids.
4. **Watch the heat:** at 38 °C, outdoors until 13:00, an air-conditioned museum from 14:00 to 18:00 and a park at sunset.
5. **Choose where to eat:** old-style taverns rarely have high chairs or kids' menus; Chamberí and the area around Retiro have family-friendly places. Theme parks, big museums and chains have baby-changing facilities.
6. **Don't force the culture pace:** one day less of monuments and one more at the zoo or a park, and everyone goes home happy.
