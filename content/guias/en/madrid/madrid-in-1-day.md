## The route for one day in Madrid, hour by hour

| Time | Plan | Getting there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00-12:30 | Prado Museum | — |
| 12:45-14:00 | Barrio de las Letras | 5 min walk |
| 14:00-15:30 | Lunch in the side streets off Huertas | — |
| 15:30-16:15 | Puerta del Sol and Plaza Mayor | 10-15 min walk |
| 16:15-16:45 | Mercado de San Miguel and Plaza de la Villa | 3 min walk |
| 16:45-18:00 | Royal Palace and Almudena | 10 min walk |
| 18:00-19:00 | Sunset at the Temple of Debod | 15 min walk |
| 19:15-20:30 | Plaza de España and Gran Vía to Callao | 20 min walk |
| 21:00 | Dinner in La Latina | 15 min walk or metro |

The afternoon times are set for November, with sunset around 18:00. **From April to September, swap the ending:** Gran Vía first and Debod at sunset, after 21:00.

## Morning: the Prado and the Barrio de las Letras

**10:00 — Prado Museum**, at opening time. It opens Monday to Saturday from 10:00 to 20:00 and Sundays and public holidays from 10:00 to 19:00. Two and a half well-spent hours: *Las Meninas* and the Velázquez room, Goya's *Black Paintings*, Bosch's *Garden of Earthly Delights*, El Greco and Titian. Don't try to see it all. If you arrive by high-speed train, Atocha is a 10-15 minute walk away.

It's the only thing worth booking: buy your ticket online 1-3 days ahead and skip the queue.

If you'd rather have someone take you straight to the key works, a guided visit with ticket starts at €49.

**12:45 — Barrio de las Letras**, five minutes away across the Paseo del Prado: quotes from Cervantes and Lope set into the pavement of Calle Huertas, Plaza de Santa Ana and Lope de Vega's house from outside.

**14:00 — Lunch** in the streets crossing Huertas (Echegaray, Ventura de la Vega, Prado), not on Huertas itself. A set menu for **€14-18** or tapas at the bar. Madrid eats from 14:00 to 16:00.

## Afternoon: what to see between Sol and the Royal Palace

**15:30 — Puerta del Sol** (10 minutes on foot): kilometre zero and the bear statue; ten minutes and move on. **Plaza Mayor** is five minutes along Calle Mayor: enter through the Arco de Cuchilleros and don't sit at its terraces.

**16:15 — Mercado de San Miguel** (half an hour to browse; eating here is expensive) and **Plaza de la Villa**, the oldest square in the centre.

**16:45 — Royal Palace.** From October to March it opens Monday to Saturday from 10:00 to 18:00 and Sundays until 16:00, and **the ticket office closes an hour earlier**: arrive before 17:00.

- **Ticket:** €18 (plus €0.77 online booking fee), bought on the day or the day before.
- **Free** for EU and Latin American citizens **Monday to Thursday from 16:00 to 18:00** (17:00 to 19:00 from April to September), queueing at the ticket office.
- **Time:** 45-60 minutes inside. Opposite and alongside are **Almudena Cathedral** and the Sabatini Gardens.

## Sunset and evening: Debod, Gran Vía and La Latina

**18:00 — Temple of Debod**, 15 minutes on foot along Calle Ferraz. An Egyptian temple from the 2nd century BC with the Royal Palace and the mountains behind it; arrive 30-45 minutes before sunset. Free.

**19:15 — Gran Vía.** Walk down to Plaza de España and along Gran Vía to Callao (20 minutes) with the buildings lit up. If your legs give out, private tuk-tuks cover the historic centre and Gran Vía in 1 h 15.

**21:00 — Dinner in La Latina**: Cava Baja and Cava Alta, classic tapas and huevos rotos. Madrid doesn't dine before 21:00. To end with flamenco, the Tablao Las Carboneras is a stone's throw from Plaza Mayor (from €45 with a drink; book 1-2 days ahead).

## Variations on this Madrid one day itinerary, and common mistakes

- **No museums:** swap the Prado for **Retiro** (free; Crystal Palace and lake, 1 h 30) and Puerta de Alcalá; the rest stays the same.
- **With kids:** Retiro and its rowing boats instead of the Prado, the Royal Palace with its armoury, and Debod. More ideas in [Madrid with kids](/en/guides/madrid/madrid-with-kids).
- **If it rains:** the Prado in the morning, the Thyssen (€14) after lunch, the Royal Palace late in the day and an early dinner in La Latina.
- **Monday:** everything is open; the Reina Sofía closes on Tuesdays and isn't on this route.

What spoils the day most:

1. **Going to the Prado during free hours:** queues and packed rooms.
2. **Reaching the Royal Palace at 17:30** in winter: the ticket office has already closed.
3. **Eating in Plaza Mayor** or at the Mercado de San Miguel.
4. **Taking the metro for 10-minute hops:** in the centre, walking is faster.
5. **Dining at 20:00** in an empty restaurant.
