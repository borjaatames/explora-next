## What you'll see

The Reina Sofía is Madrid's museum of 20th-century art. The piece everyone comes for is Picasso's **Guernica**, in the second-floor collection of the **Sabatini Building**, alongside Dalí, Miró and the Spanish surrealists. The main site is made up of the Sabatini and Nouvel buildings.

The collection takes **two or three hours** to see well; for Guernica alone, an hour is enough. Your ticket lets you re-enter the same day.

## Reina Sofía Museum ticket prices and opening hours

Official sales are at **entradas.museoreinasofia.es**, linked from museoreinasofia.es:

- **General:** €12, collection and exhibitions at the main site.
- **Two visits:** €18, to come back a second time within a year.
- **General with audio guide:** €18.
- **Free** for under-18s, over-65s and students, but **only at the box office** with proof. 50% reduced tickets (large families and cultural volunteers) are also sold there.

**Hours:** Monday and Wednesday to Saturday **10:00 to 21:00**; Sundays **10:00 to 14:30**. Last entry 30 minutes before closing. **Closed every Tuesday**, plus 1 and 6 January, 1 May and 24, 25 and 31 December (and 15 May and 9 November, depending on the holiday calendar).

## If Reina Sofía tickets are sold out

Tickets are sold by time slot, and a paid slot at 17:00 and a free one at 19:00 **don't compete for the same places**. If yours is sold out:

1. **Check other times on the same day.** Paid slots run from 10:00 until free hours begin; going in at 16:00 instead of 11:00 won't ruin your plan.
2. **Check another day.** It's closed on Tuesdays and only open until 14:30 on Sundays, so Monday and Wednesday are usually better bets than the weekend.
3. **Go to the box office on the day.** It sells same-day tickets and closes 30 minutes before the museum (20:30 Monday to Saturday, 14:00 on Sundays). On long weekends, expect a queue.
4. **Book a guided tour with ticket included.** Operators have their own quota: a 75-minute tour with Guernica, Dalí and Miró from **€29.05**, a group of six max from **€45**, or a private tour with an official guide from **€124**.

## Reina Sofía free hours and booking

| Day | Free hours |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | 19:00-21:00 |
| Tuesday | Closed |
| Wednesday to Saturday | 19:00-21:00 |
| Sunday | 12:30-14:30 |

It's **free all day** on **18 April**, **18 and 22 May**, **12 October** and **6 December**.

- **You need a ticket even during free hours.** It's booked free online, just like a paid one.
- **It's for individual visitors only**, not groups.
- **Two hours don't go far:** Guernica and the second floor of the Sabatini.
- **The most sought-after** are Friday and Saturday evenings and Sunday: book as soon as you know your day.

**If the free tickets are sold out**, don't wait at the door: buy a paid slot before 19:00. It's a separate quota and you'll see it in peace.

## If you can't get in: nearby alternatives

- **Thyssen-Bornemisza Museum** (€14), about 15 minutes' walk along the Paseo del Prado: 20th-century art (Expressionism, Pop Art), the most logical substitute.
- **Prado Museum**, about 7 minutes' walk. If it's full too, see [Prado tickets sold out](/en/guides/madrid/prado-museum-tickets-sold-out).
- **Crystal Palace and Velázquez Palace** in the Retiro: Reina Sofía venues with temporary exhibitions and **free entry**. Open 10:00-18:00 from October to March and until 21:00 from April to September; the Crystal Palace may close in rain or extreme heat.

## Tips

- **Don't turn up at 19:00 without a free ticket booked:** without one you depend on spare capacity.
- **Don't go on a Tuesday:** it's closed every Tuesday.
- **There's no Sunday afternoon:** it closes at 14:30, last entry 14:00.
- **Buy only from the official site** or from resellers charging for an extra service (guide, flexibility); some websites imitate the official one.
- **On long weekends and holidays,** buy paid tickets a few days ahead; the rest of the year there's usually space on the day.
- **If you're also seeing the Prado and the Thyssen,** the Paseo del Arte pass (€32.80 on the official site) or the Art Walk Pass (from €38) include one entry to each museum. To choose between the three, see [Prado vs Reina Sofía vs Thyssen](/en/guides/madrid/prado-vs-reina-sofia-vs-thyssen).
