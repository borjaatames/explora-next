## The three parks of PortAventura World

**PortAventura Park** is the main one and the must-see. It has six themed areas: Mediterrània, Far West, México, China, Polynesia and **SésamoAventura**, for the little ones. This is where the star roller coasters are, **Dragon Khan** and **Shambhala**, plus Furius Baco. It needs a full day.

**Ferrari Land** is the park devoted to Ferrari, with **Red Force**, the tallest and fastest roller coaster in Europe (0 to 180 km/h). It's smaller: combine it with half a day in the main park or do it separately. For speed lovers.

**Caribe Aquatic Park** is the water park, open only in summer: ideal for the hottest days.

The golden rule: **one day per park**. Trying to see everything in a single day is a recipe for ending up exhausted without riding the best attractions.

## PortAventura tickets: prices, opening hours and Express Pass

- **Prices (online):** a 1, 2 or 3-day ticket to PortAventura Park with one Ferrari Land entry, **from €55**; with Caribe Aquatic Park, **from €76** (only worth it in summer). **Under-3s free.** None include Express, food or parking; free cancellation up to 3 days before.
- **Buy online:** you skip the ticket office queue and secure your date. For two parks, two days is better.
- **Opening hours:** opens at **10:00** and closes between 19:00 and 20:00 in mid-season; in July and August, until midnight, with a night show.
- **Express Pass:** sold separately, as one ride per attraction or unlimited, and the price varies by day. On bank holiday weekends and in August it's worth it; on weekdays in May or June, it isn't.
- **Resort hotels:** include park entry on the days of your stay and early access to Shambhala. If you're staying two days, compare with a hotel outside plus tickets.

## PortAventura with kids

The **SésamoAventura** area is designed for the youngest, with gentle rides and Sesame Street characters. Before queuing for the big rides, check the **minimum heights**:

- **1.40 m:** Shambhala, Dragon Khan, Furius Baco and Red Force.
- **1.20-1.30 m:** the intermediate rides.
- **SésamoAventura:** no minimum or 0.90 m.

Measure them before promising anything. You can bring food and drink (no glass): inside, a set meal costs €15-20 and there are queues from 13:30 to 15:00, so eat at 12:30 or after 15:30.

## How to get to PortAventura

- **By train:** the Renfe **PortAventura** station is right by the resort, about **10 minutes from Tarragona** and just over an hour from Barcelona.
- **By car:** direct exit from the AP-7/N-340, with plenty of parking.

## When to go and tips

In 2026, **Halloween** runs from 19 September to 15 November and **Christmas** from 20 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, the last stretch of the season and one of the best times to go. The 2027 opening hasn't been announced yet. Ferrari Land and Caribe have their own calendars: check the official one at portaventuraworld.com before buying.

- **Arrive at opening:** the first two hours have the shortest queues. Go in at 10:00 and head straight for Shambhala, Dragon Khan and Furius Baco.
- **Avoid weekends and bank holidays**, or go with Express Pass: queues soar.
- **Lockers:** there are paid lockers by the entrance (€6-10). You can't ride the roller coasters with bags or loose phones.
- **Don't try two parks in one day** or combine it with Roman Tarragona: each needs its own day.
- **Remember that Ferrari Land and Caribe are separate**, with different tickets and seasons.
