**PortAventura World is one of the largest theme-park resorts in Europe**, and the big family day out on the Costa Daurada, next to Salou and a few minutes from Tarragona. It's so big that it pays to plan: which park, how many days and how to dodge the queues make the difference between a great day and an exhausting one.

## PortAventura tickets

| Activity | Rating | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PortAventura and Ferrari Land 1, 2 or 3-day ticket](/en/cities/tarragona/activities/portaventura-ferrari-land-ticket) | 4.3★ (2,697) | €55 |
| [PortAventura + Ferrari Land + Caribe Aquatic Park ticket](/en/cities/tarragona/activities/portaventura-ferrari-caribe-aquatic-ticket) | 4.4★ (711) | €76 |

Which one to buy:

- **The €55 ticket** gives you 1, 2 or 3 days at PortAventura Park plus **one Ferrari Land entry**. With 2 days, Ferrari Land goes on the same day as one PortAventura day and the days must fall within 4 days; with 3 days, within 5 days. Under-3s go free, and you can cancel free up to 3 days before.
- **The €76 three-park ticket** adds a day at Caribe Aquatic Park. It's only worth it when the water park is open (summer).
- Neither ticket includes Express (ride queue) access, food or parking. You skip the ticket office, not the ride queues.

The golden rule: **one day per park**. Trying to see it all in one day is the recipe for ending up wrecked and missing the best rides.

## The three parks

| Park | What it is | For whom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PortAventura Park | The main park, 6 themed worlds | Everyone; the must |
| Ferrari Land | The Ferrari park, with Red Force | Speed lovers |
| Caribe Aquatic Park | Water park (summer) | Hot days |

**PortAventura Park.** Six themed areas (Mediterrània, Far West, México, China, Polynesia and SésamoAventura for the youngest). Here are the star roller coasters: **Shambhala**, **Dragon Khan** and **Furius Baco**. A full day.

**Ferrari Land.** The park dedicated to Ferrari, with **Red Force**, the tallest and fastest roller coaster in Europe (0 to 180 km/h). It's smaller: half a day is enough, which is why tickets pair it with a PortAventura day.

**Caribe Aquatic Park.** The water park, open in summer only: ideal for the hottest days.

## When to go (2026 season)

| Period | What's on |
| --- | --- |
| 19 September – 15 November 2026 | Halloween season |
| 20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027 | Christmas season, the last stretch of the 2026 season |
| 2027 season | Opening date not yet announced (as of October 2026) |

Opening days and hours change through the season, and Ferrari Land and Caribe have their own calendars, so check the official calendar on portaventuraworld.com for your date before you buy. Halloween and Christmas are among the best times to go. Avoid **weekends and bank-holiday weekends** if you can: the queues soar.

## Queues, heights and food

- **Arrive at opening.** The first two hours have the shortest queues: go straight to the big coasters (Shambhala, Dragon Khan, Furius Baco), then do the shows and gentler rides later.
- **Express Pass:** sold separately, either one ride per attraction or unlimited. It pays off on bank-holiday weekends and in August; on a weekday in May or June you rarely need it.
- **Minimum heights:** **1.40 m** for Shambhala, Dragon Khan, Furius Baco and Red Force; 1.20–1.30 m for the mid-level rides; SésamoAventura rides have no minimum or 0.90 m. Measure your kids before promising anything.
- **Lunch:** queues at the restaurants peak from 13:30 to 15:00. Eat at 12:30 or after 15:30.
- **Bags:** there are paid lockers by the entrance. You can't ride the coasters with bags or loose phones.
- **Resort hotels** include park entry on the days of your stay plus early access to Shambhala. For two days, compare that with an outside hotel plus tickets.

## With kids

The **SésamoAventura** area is designed for the youngest, with gentle rides and Sesame Street characters. Check the heights above before queuing: many of the big rides are off-limits for small children.

## Getting there

**By train**, the **PortAventura** station (Renfe) is right next to the resort: about 10 minutes from Tarragona and just over an hour from Barcelona. **By car**, there's a direct exit off the AP-7/N-340 with large car parks (parking is not included in the tickets). From Salou there are local buses.

## What we don't recommend

1. **Trying two parks in one day.** There's no time; one park per day.
2. **Mixing it with Tarragona's Roman old town on the same day.** Each needs its own day; see the [Roman Tarraco guide](/en/guides/tarragona/roman-tarraco-guide).
3. **Going on a bank-holiday weekend without an Express Pass.** Endless queues.
4. **Arriving late.** You lose the quietest hours.
5. **Buying the three-park ticket outside summer.** Caribe Aquatic Park is closed.
