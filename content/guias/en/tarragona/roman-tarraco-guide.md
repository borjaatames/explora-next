**Tarragona holds the largest Roman ensemble in Catalonia**, and one of the most important in Spain: the legacy of **Tarraco**, capital of Hispania Citerior and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It's spread across the whole old town (the amphitheatre by the beach, the circus beneath the streets, the cathedral over a temple) and you walk it all. This guide tells you what to see, in what order and how not to overpay.

How to choose:

- **The guided walking tour** (2 hours, official guide) covers the walls, the model of Tarraco, the circus vaults and the amphitheatre, with entry to the circus included. It's guided **in Spanish or Catalan**.

- **The cathedral** is a separate ticket with its own audio guide and a one-hour visit.

## What to see

| Monument | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Amphitheatre | By the sea; gladiator combats and Visigothic ruins |
| Roman Circus | Chariot races; vaults beneath the city |
| Provincial Forum / Praetorium | Political centre; the Praetorium Tower, a viewpoint |
| Forum of the Colony | The local forum, with its basilica |
| Walls (Passeig Arqueològic) | The walk between the Roman walls |
| Les Ferreres Aqueduct | The "Devil's Bridge", on the outskirts |

## The route order

Start at the **Amphitheatre**, the most famous image, by the beach. Continue to the **Roman Circus** and climb the **Praetorium Tower** for a panorama. Walk the **forum** and the **Passeig Arqueològic** between the walls (the oldest Roman construction outside Italy). If you have a car, finish with the **Les Ferreres aqueduct**, on the outskirts, in a park. We slot it into the [Tarragona in one day](/en/guides/tarragona/tarragona-in-one-day) route.

## Tickets, hours and timings

| Ticket (as of 2026) | Price | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single site | €5 | One monument |
| **Combined ticket** | **€15** | Passeig Arqueològic (walls), Circus and Praetorium, Forum of the Colony, Amphitheatre and Casa Canals |
| Volta del Pallol video mapping | €5 | A projection in a Roman vault |

- **Who goes free:** children under 12. Ages 12–17, students and retirees pay half. A large family pays a single ticket for everyone.
- **When it pays off:** from the third site the combined ticket is cheaper than paying one by one. Tickets are sold at the sites and online at entrades.museu.tarragona.cat.
- **Closed on Mondays.** All the municipal sites close on Mondays, and on Sundays they open in the morning only. If Monday is your only day, you're left with the walls from outside, the aqueduct and the cathedral.
- **How long:** a long half day for the urban circuit (amphitheatre, circus, Praetorium, forum and walls), plus 2 hours if you add the aqueduct.

> **Tip:** in summer, start at opening time. The amphitheatre, circus and walls have almost no shade, and by midday the heat is hard going.

## Beyond the centre

- **Les Ferreres Aqueduct (Pont del Diable).** The best-preserved Roman aqueduct in the area, 4 km from the centre, in the Pont del Diable park. Access is free and you can walk across the top. It's 10 minutes by car, with free parking, or a short city bus ride.
- **Arc de Berà** and **Torre dels Escipions**, on the old Via Augusta, on the outskirts.
- **MNAT (National Archaeology Museum).** To understand the ensemble and see the mosaics.
- **The cathedral**, built over the Roman temple area, with its cloister. It's a separate ticket and closes on Sunday mornings for worship.

## When to go

In **spring and autumn**, with good weather and without the summer heat on the shadeless monuments. And especially during **Tarraco Viva** in the **second half of May**: the Roman re-enactment festival, with gladiators in the amphitheatre and hundreds of activities, many free. Hotels fill up, so book early.

## What we don't recommend

1. **Paying for each monument separately.** The combined ticket pays off from the third site.
2. **Going on a Monday** without checking: the municipal sites are closed.
3. **Visiting at midday in summer.** Go early.
4. **Forgetting the aqueduct.** Almost no one goes, but it's spectacular and free.
5. **Skipping the MNAT.** It gives context to everything you see on the street.
6. **Allowing too little time.** The ensemble needs half a day or more.
