## What to see in Roman Tarraco

- **Tarragona Roman amphitheatre:** by the sea, the city's most famous image. A setting for gladiator fights, it also preserves Visigothic remains.
- **Roman circus:** where chariot races were held; today its vaults lie beneath the city.
- **Provincial Forum and Pretori Tower:** the political centre of Tarraco. The tower is a viewpoint over the old town.
- **Colonial Forum:** the local forum, with its basilica.
- **Walls (Passeig Arqueològic):** a walk between the Roman walls, the oldest Roman construction outside Italy.
- **Les Ferreres aqueduct:** the "Pont del Diable", the best-preserved Roman aqueduct in the area, on the outskirts.

To understand the whole site and see the mosaics, visit the **MNAT (National Archaeological Museum)**. On the old Via Augusta, on the outskirts, you'll also find the **Arc de Berà** and the **Torre dels Escipions**.

## Prices and opening hours: the combined ticket

- **Combined ticket: €15**, valid for the Amphitheatre, the Circus and Pretori Tower, the Walls and the Casa Canals, among other municipal sites. It doesn't expire within the same trip, so you can split it over two days. Each site on its own costs **€5**: from the third one, the combined ticket pays off.
- **Free:** under-12s; there are also reduced rates (check the city's price page).
- **Hours from April to late October:** Tuesday to Saturday 9:00 to 20:45, Sundays and public holidays 9:00 to 14:30.
- **Winter hours:** Tuesday to Friday 9:00 to 18:30 (the Circus-Pretori and Casa Canals until 20:00), Saturdays from 9:30 and Sundays 9:30 to 14:30.
- Last entry 30 minutes before closing. **All municipal sites close on Mondays**: if Monday is your only day, you're left with the walls from outside, the aqueduct and the cathedral.
- **The cathedral** is separate (€13 with audio guide, cloister included) and closes on Sunday mornings for services.

## The route on foot: from the amphitheatre to the walls

1. Start at the **amphitheatre**, by the beach.
2. Continue to the **Roman circus** and climb the **Pretori Tower** for a panoramic view.
3. Walk through the **forum** and along the **Passeig Arqueològic** between the walls.
4. If you have a car, finish at the **Les Ferreres aqueduct**.

**Getting to the aqueduct:** it's 4 km away, in the Pont del Diable eco-historic park. Bus 5 or 85 from the centre, or 10 minutes by car with free parking. Entry is free and you can walk across the top.

## When to go: Tarraco Viva and the best seasons

**Spring and autumn** bring good weather without the heat on monuments with little shade. The best time is **Tarraco Viva**, in the second half of May: historical re-enactments, gladiators in the amphitheatre and hundreds of activities, many free, with the city full of legionaries. Hotels fill up, so book early.

## Tips and common mistakes

- **Don't pay for each site separately:** the combined ticket pays off, and many people overpay without realising.
- **Don't visit at midday in summer:** the amphitheatre, circus and walls have little shade; go early.
- **Don't forget the aqueduct:** it's on the outskirts and hardly anyone goes, but it's spectacular and free.
- **Don't skip the MNAT:** it gives context to everything you see in the streets.
- **Don't underestimate the time** or go on a Monday without checking opening hours.
