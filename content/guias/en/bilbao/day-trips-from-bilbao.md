## San Juan de Gaztelugatxe

45 minutes away, a **hermitage on an islet** joined to the mainland by a bridge and a staircase of **241 steps**, world-famous as Dragonstone in *Game of Thrones*, with spectacular views of the coast.

- **Booking:** entry is free, but in high season it requires a **timed-slot booking**: every day from 15 June to 20 September, at Easter, on long weekends and many spring and autumn weekends (calendar on tiketa.eus). The rest of the year, access is free. In summer places sell out a week ahead.
- **The walk:** from the Eneperi car park to the hermitage it's 2.5 km and 40 minutes; the 241 steps are only the end. Allow **2 hours in total**, comfortable shoes and no rush.
- **Weather:** access closes in strong wind or storms. In season the car park fills up: go early.

If you'd rather not drive, the excursion combining Gaztelugatxe with Bermeo, Mundaka and Gernika, with txakoli, costs from €45.

## San Sebastián from Bilbao

An hour away, **Donostia** means elegance and food: **La Concha** bay, one of Europe's best city beaches, **Monte Igueldo**, the Old Town for pintxos and one of the highest concentrations of Michelin stars in the world.

- **Without a car:** Bizkaibus/PESA coach from Termibus every 30 minutes; 1 h 15 and €7-12 depending on the time.
- **In town:** everything is walkable; the Igueldo funicular costs €4.50 and the Peine del Viento is free.
- **On a tour:** some excursions add Hondarribia and Getaria, or Biarritz and Saint-Jean-de-Luz across the border.

## Mundaka, Urdaibai and Gernika

45 minutes away, the **Urdaibai biosphere reserve**: marshes, beaches and fishing villages. **Mundaka** has one of Europe's most famous **surf waves** (it has no beach as such); **Bermeo** is a charming fishing port, and **Gernika**, 30 minutes away, is a symbol of Basque history, with the **Casa de Juntas** (free) and its tree, and the memory of Picasso's *Guernica*. Gernika's market is on Mondays.

- **Without a car:** Euskotren line E4, which leaves the centre on metro line 3 from Matiko, reaches Gernika (45 minutes), Mundaka and Bermeo (1 hour), every half hour, for €3-4. It's the best day trip without a car.
- **Beaches:** **Laida** and **Laga** are the best; in summer the Laga car park is full by 11:00.
- Better to see the three villages together than Mundaka alone.

## Rioja Alavesa and Vitoria-Gasteiz

An hour away on the A-68, the vineyards of **Rioja Alavesa**: walled medieval villages like **Laguardia** and spectacular wineries, including the one at Frank Gehry's hotel in Elciego. The wineries (Marqués de Riscal, Ysios, Baigorri) can **only be visited by booking**, at fixed times, for **€20-40 with a tasting**. Without a car there's no practical way to go; if you'll be drinking, take an organised tour or a driver.

Also an hour away, **Vitoria-Gasteiz**, with its medieval old town and reputation as a green city, well connected by bus and train.

## Getting around and what weather to expect

- **Getxo and the Bizkaia Bridge:** by metro, line 1.
- **Gaztelugatxe, Urdaibai and Rioja Alavesa:** a car gives more freedom, although Urdaibai works very well by Euskotren.
- **San Sebastián and Vitoria:** comfortable buses and trains.
- **Weather:** the coast is 3-5 degrees cooler than Bilbao, with frequent morning fog. Bring a raincoat: the sirimiri turns up without warning.

## Common mistakes

1. **Going to Gaztelugatxe without a booking** in high season: you may be left outside.
2. **Doing two distant trips on the same day.** San Sebastián or Rioja is already a full day.
3. **Rushing the 241 steps or wearing the wrong shoes.**
4. **Not booking winery visits** in Rioja Alavesa.
5. **Underestimating parking at Gaztelugatxe** in season.
