**Bilbao is a perfect base for discovering the Basque Country.** Within an hour you have one of the wildest coasts in Spain, San Sebastián, a biosphere reserve, the historic Basque heartland and the vineyards of the Rioja Alavesa. This guide sorts the best day trips by how much time you have and how to get there.

The most famous is **Gaztelugatxe**, but the Urdaibai coast and San Sebastián compete head to head. Choose by the day: sea, gastronomy, nature or wine.

## The best day trips

| Destination | Distance | Ideal for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gaztelugatxe | 45 min | Hermitage above the sea (Game of Thrones) |
| San Sebastián | 1h | La Concha, pintxos and Monte Igueldo |
| Urdaibai (Mundaka, Bermeo) | 45 min | Biosphere reserve and surf |
| Gernika | 30 min | Basque history and the Casa de Juntas |
| Rioja Alavesa (Laguardia) | 1h | Wineries and medieval villages |
| Vitoria-Gasteiz | 1h | Medieval old town and a green city |

## San Juan de Gaztelugatxe

Forty-five minutes away, a **hermitage on an islet** joined to land by a bridge and a stairway of **241 steps**, made world-famous as Dragonstone in *Game of Thrones*. Access is regulated with a **free booking**; check before you go. Spectacular coastal views.

## San Sebastián

An hour away, **Donostia** is elegance and gastronomy: the bay of **La Concha** (one of the best urban beaches in Europe), Monte Igueldo, the Old Town for pintxos and one of the highest concentrations of Michelin stars in the world. A perfect day.

## Urdaibai: Mundaka, Bermeo and Gernika

Forty-five minutes away, the **Urdaibai biosphere reserve**: marshes, beaches and fishing villages. **Mundaka** has one of the most famous **surf waves** in Europe; **Bermeo**, a charming fishing port; and **Gernika**, a symbol of Basque history, with the **Casa de Juntas** and the tree (and the memory of Picasso's *Guernica*).

## The Rioja Alavesa

An hour away, the vineyards of the **Rioja Alavesa**: walled medieval villages like **Laguardia** and spectacular wineries (including Frank Gehry's hotel in Elciego). Top-tier wine tourism; book the visit.

## Getting around

**Getxo and the Bizkaia Bridge** are reached by **metro** (line 1). For Gaztelugatxe, Urdaibai or the Rioja Alavesa, a **car** gives more freedom. **San Sebastián** and **Vitoria** have comfortable buses and trains. Book Gaztelugatxe and the wineries ahead.

## What we don't recommend

1. **Going to Gaztelugatxe without a booking.** Access is controlled; without it you may be turned away.
2. **Doing two far-off trips on the same day.** San Sebastián or the Rioja are a full day each.
3. **Climbing the 241 steps in a rush or in poor footwear.** Take it slowly.
4. **Forgetting the Atlantic weather.** Bring a raincoat; the sirimiri turns up unannounced.

## Common mistakes

> **⚠️ Warning:** the slip-ups we see most.

1. **Not booking the winery visits** in the Rioja Alavesa.
2. **Seeing Mundaka without Bermeo or Gernika.** They're close and better together.
3. **Underestimating the parking at Gaztelugatxe** in season; go early.
