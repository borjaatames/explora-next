**Bilbao is a perfect base for discovering the Basque Country.** Within an hour you have one of the wildest coasts in Spain, San Sebastián, a biosphere reserve, the historic Basque heartland and the vineyards of the Rioja Alavesa. This guide sorts the best day trips by how much time you have and how to get there.

## San Juan de Gaztelugatxe

Forty-five minutes away, a **hermitage on an islet** joined to land by a bridge and a stairway of **241 steps**, made world-famous as Dragonstone in *Game of Thrones*.

- **Entry is free, but in high season you need a free timed booking:** daily from 15 June to 20 September, plus Holy Week, long weekends and many spring and autumn weekends (calendar on tiketa.eus). The rest of the year access is open. In summer, slots run out about a week ahead.
- **It's a walk, not just steps:** from the Eneperi car park to the hermitage is 2.5 km and about 40 minutes. The 241 steps are only the final stretch. Allow 2 hours in total.
- **Check the wind forecast:** access closes in storms and strong gusts.
- **Parking fills early** in season; arrive at the start of the day or take the tour above.

## San Sebastián

An hour away, **Donostia** is elegance and gastronomy: the bay of **La Concha** (one of the best urban beaches in Europe), Monte Igueldo, the Old Town for pintxos and one of the highest concentrations of Michelin stars in the world.

- **Without a car:** ALSA buses leave from the Bilbao Intermodal station (Termibus) more than 12 times a day and take about 1 h 10. Fares run around €7-12 depending on the time (as of 2026).
- **Once there, walk everywhere.** The Igueldo funicular costs €4.50 and the Peine del Viento sculptures at the end of Ondarreta beach are free.

## Urdaibai: Mundaka, Bermeo and Gernika

Forty-five minutes away, the **Urdaibai biosphere reserve**: marshes, beaches and fishing villages. **Mundaka** has one of the most famous **surf waves** in Europe; **Bermeo**, a charming fishing port; and **Gernika**, a symbol of Basque history, with the **Casa de Juntas** and the tree (and the memory of Picasso's *Guernica*).

- **The best day trip without a car.** Euskotren line E4 runs from Bilbao (it uses metro line 3 through the city and ends at Matiko) to Gernika, Mundaka and Bermeo. Trains run every 30 minutes on weekdays and hourly at weekends; tickets cost €3-4.
- **The Casa de Juntas in Gernika is free.** Gernika's market is on Mondays.
- **Beaches:** Laida and Laga are the best. In summer the Laga car park is full by 11:00. Mundaka itself has no real beach; it's a surf town.

## The Rioja Alavesa

An hour away by the A-68, the vineyards of the **Rioja Alavesa**: walled medieval villages like **Laguardia** and spectacular wineries (including Frank Gehry's hotel in Elciego).

- **You need a car or a tour:** there's no practical public transport.
- **Wineries are visited by booking only**, at fixed times. Marqués de Riscal, Ysios and Baigorri charge €20-40 with a tasting.
- **If you plan to drink**, take an organised trip or have a designated driver.

## Getting around

**Getxo and the Bizkaia Bridge** are reached by **metro** (line 1). For Gaztelugatxe or the Rioja Alavesa, a **car** gives more freedom. **San Sebastián** and **Vitoria** have comfortable buses and trains. Book Gaztelugatxe and the wineries ahead.

The coast is usually 3-5 °C cooler than Bilbao, with frequent morning mist, and the sirimiri (fine drizzle) turns up unannounced: bring a raincoat.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Going to Gaztelugatxe in high season without a booking.** Access is controlled; without it you'll be turned away.
2. **Doing two far-off trips on the same day.** San Sebastián or the Rioja are a full day each.
3. **Climbing the 241 steps in a rush or in poor footwear.** Take it slowly.
4. **Not booking the winery visits** in the Rioja Alavesa.
5. **Seeing Mundaka without Bermeo or Gernika.** They're close and better together.
