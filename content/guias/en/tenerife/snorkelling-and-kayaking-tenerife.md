**The water in southern Tenerife is clear, mild and full of life.** Paddling below volcanic cliffs and putting your head underwater to see fish, rays or a green turtle is one of the island's favourite experiences, and you don't need any experience. This guide explains where to go, which trip to choose and how to do it right.

The key: **kayaking and snorkelling from Los Cristianos** is the best-selling and cheapest option. If you'd rather not paddle, choose a **guided snorkel** in a protected area, shorter and calmer.

## Where to snorkel and kayak

- **Los Cristianos:** where most kayak trips start. Volcanic coast, caves and frequent turtles.
- **Palm-Mar:** next to the protected marine strip, with cliffs and rocky seabeds full of fish.
- **Abades:** on the east coast, a calm, sheltered bay, ideal for a first snorkel.
- **Adeje coast:** coves such as El Puertito, where turtles are often seen close to shore.

## Kayak, snorkel or both

- **Kayak + snorkel (about 3 h):** you paddle a double kayak with a guide, stop at one or two coves and snorkel. Photos or videos are often included.
- **Guided snorkel (1.5-2 h):** you go in from the shore with an instructor, no paddling. Good if you want less effort or have children.
- **Try dive + snorkel:** if you want to try breathing underwater. See our guide to diving in Tenerife.

## How to see turtles without disturbing them

- Don't touch or feed them, even if they come close.
- Keep your distance and don't chase them when they surface to breathe.
- Use reef-friendly sun cream or wear a rash vest.

## Before you book

- **Being able to swim** is required on almost every trip.
- **Minimum age** is usually 6 to 8; kayaks have weight limits.
- **Included:** kayak, paddle, life jacket, mask and snorkel. A wetsuit is sometimes optional.
- **Cancellations:** trips are cancelled in rough seas; you're normally offered a new date or a refund.

## When to go

All year. The sea is around **19 °C in winter** and **23-24 °C at the end of summer**; in winter, ask for a wetsuit. **Mornings** are usually calmer.

## What to bring

Swimwear on, towel, sun cream, a hat, water and dry clothes. Leave your phone in a locker or a waterproof case.
