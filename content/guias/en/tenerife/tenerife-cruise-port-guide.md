Santa Cruz is an easy call: the ship docks within walking distance of the centre and the tram takes you up to La Laguna in under an hour. The big question for most cruise passengers is **Mount Teide**, and the honest answer is that it's only worth it on a long call and if you accept some risk. Here are the real timings to help you decide.

## Where do cruise ships dock in Tenerife?

At the **port of Santa Cruz de Tenerife**, the island's capital, on the **Muelle Sur** or the **Muelle de Enlace**. From the ship to **Plaza de España** is a **10-20 minute walk** on a flat, signposted path, depending on your berth. You don't need a shuttle, although some lines run one if the ship is at the far end of the quay.

One thing to watch: many Tenerife hotels and tours are in the **south** (Costa Adeje, Los Cristianos), well over an hour from the port. When you book anything, check it starts in Santa Cruz or picks up in the capital.

## Getting around from the port

| Option | Where to | Time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **On foot** | Plaza de España, Calle del Castillo, Auditorium | 10-20 min | Free |
| **Tram line 1** | La Laguna (Trinidad stop) | About 40 min | €1.35 single |
| **TITSA bus** | Las Teresitas, Anaga | Depends on the line | Single fare |
| **Taxi** | La Laguna, Anaga, Teide | La Laguna about 20 min | €1.35 per km by day |

- **Tram.** Line 1 links Santa Cruz's **Intercambiador** with **Trinidad** in La Laguna: 12.5 km and 21 stops. Trams run every 5 minutes at weekday peaks and every 10-15 minutes the rest of the day. Trinidad is a few minutes' walk from the historic centre.
- **Taxi.** In Santa Cruz the meter runs at €1.35 per km by day and €1.55 at night. For Teide or Anaga, agree a fixed price for the trip, waiting time and return before you set off.
- **Buses to Anaga.** Routes leave Santa Cruz for Taganana and the coast; the one up to the **Cruz del Carmen** visitor centre (the 076) starts in La Laguna. Services are infrequent, so on a port day a taxi or a tour is better.

## What to see in Tenerife in 4, 6 or 8 hours

| Time ashore | Suggested plan |
| --- | --- |
| **4 hours** | **Santa Cruz** on foot (Plaza de España, the Auditorium, the Nuestra Señora de África market) and the **tram to La Laguna**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, for its old streets and cathedral. |
| **6 hours** | La Laguna in the morning and **Anaga** in the afternoon: a taxi to Cruz del Carmen or the guided laurel forest walk. Or a wine tasting at the Casa del Vino in El Sauzal, or Las Teresitas beach. |
| **8 hours or more** | **Mount Teide** with the cable car, or the north: **Loro Parque** in Puerto de la Cruz, about 40 minutes by motorway. |

### La Laguna and Anaga

**San Cristóbal de La Laguna** keeps its original 15th-century street grid and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Two or three hours is plenty at an easy pace.

**Anaga**, the mountains in the island's north-east, has one of the best-preserved laurel forests in the Canaries. By car it's 25-35 minutes from Santa Cruz and 15-20 from La Laguna. The guided walk starts at the **Cruz del Carmen** visitor centre; choose the short option on a 6-hour call.

### Mount Teide: real timings and risks

| Leg | Approximate time |
| --- | --- |
| Port → cable car base station (about 65 km of mountain road) | 1 hr 15 min |
| Queue, cable car ride (under 8 min) and La Rambleta viewpoints (3,555 m) | 1 hr 30 min - 2 hr |
| Back to the port (plus afternoon traffic on the TF-5) | 1 hr 15 min - 1 hr 30 min |
| **Minimum total** | **4 hr 30 min - 5 hr** |

- **Cable car:** about **€42 return** (€21 children), with a **timed online booking**; there's rarely space at the ticket office. From late October to March it runs 9:00-17:00, last ride up at 16:00.
- **Summit (3,715 m):** the final stretch needs a separate permit from Tenerife ON (€15 for non-residents of the Canaries). There's no time on a port day: enjoy the La Rambleta viewpoints instead.
- **Risks:** the cable car **closes in strong wind or ice**, sometimes without notice and mostly in winter. Bring a warm layer: it's cold up there even when the port is at 25°C.

The Teide tour with cable car and transfer takes 7 hours and picks up at points in the north and south of the island. **Before booking, check there's a pick-up in Santa Cruz** and that the return leaves you at least an hour before all aboard; if not, it won't work for you.

## When should you be back on board?

- **All aboard** is usually 30 to 60 minutes before sailing. It's in the daily programme.
- From La Laguna, allow 40 minutes on the tram plus 15 minutes' walk to the ship.
- If you go up Teide, add **an hour of slack**: the motorway down to Santa Cruz gets congested in the late afternoon.

## Sundays and public holidays

- In Santa Cruz and La Laguna many shops close on Sundays and the mood is quiet; cafés and the main museums stay open.
- The tram runs every day, less often on Sundays and holidays.
- The Teide cable car is busier at weekends: book a slot in advance.

## Common mistakes

1. **Booking a tour that picks up in the south.** Costa Adeje is well over an hour from the port: confirm a Santa Cruz pick-up.
2. **Going up Teide with less than 8 hours** or without a cable car slot.
3. **Heading up in a T-shirt.** At 3,555 m there can be ice and wind in winter.
4. **Forgetting the afternoon traffic** on the motorway from La Laguna to Santa Cruz.
