## Where ships dock at the Tenerife cruise port

At the **port of Santa Cruz de Tenerife**, on the **Muelle Sur** or the **Muelle de Enlace**. From the ship to **Plaza de España** it is a **10-20 minute walk** along a flat, signposted path, depending on your berth. You don't need a shuttle, although some cruise lines run one if the ship is at the far end of the quay.

Watch out: many Tenerife hotels and tours are in the **south** (Costa Adeje, Los Cristianos), well over an hour from the port. When you book anything, check that it leaves from Santa Cruz or picks up in the capital.

## Getting around: Santa Cruz to La Laguna tram, bus and taxi

- **On foot:** Plaza de España, Calle del Castillo and the Auditorium, in 10-20 minutes.
- **Tram line 1:** links the Santa Cruz **Intercambiador** with **Trinidad** in La Laguna: 12.5 km, 21 stops and about **40 minutes**, for **€1.35** a single ticket. Trams run every 5 minutes at weekday peak times and every 10-15 minutes the rest of the day. Trinidad is a few minutes' walk from the old town.
- **Taxi:** in Santa Cruz it costs €1.35 per km by day and €1.55 at night; to La Laguna, about 20 minutes. For Teide or Anaga, agree a **fixed price** for the outward trip, waiting time and return before getting in.
- **TITSA buses:** lines leave Santa Cruz for Las Teresitas, Taganana and the Anaga coast; the one up to **Cruz del Carmen** (line 076) leaves from La Laguna. They run infrequently: on a port call, a taxi or tour is better.

## Things to do in Tenerife on a cruise, by time ashore

| Time ashore | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| **4 hours** | **Santa Cruz** on foot (Plaza de España, Auditorium, Nuestra Señora de África market) and the **tram to La Laguna** to stroll its old town and cathedral. |
| **6 hours** | La Laguna in the morning and **Anaga** in the afternoon, or a tasting at the Casa del Vino in El Sauzal, or Las Teresitas beach. |
| **8 hours or more** | **Mount Teide** with the cable car, or the north: [Loro Parque](/en/guides/tenerife/loro-parque-tickets-prices) in Puerto de la Cruz, about 40 minutes by motorway. |

**San Cristóbal de La Laguna** keeps its 15th-century street layout and is a World Heritage Site. You can see it in two or three unhurried hours.

**Anaga from Santa Cruz:** the north-eastern massif holds one of the best-preserved laurel forests in the Canaries, 25-35 minutes by car from Santa Cruz and 15-20 from La Laguna. The guided walk starts at the **Cruz del Carmen** visitor centre; on a 6-hour call, choose the short option.

**El Sauzal**, on the north coast, is under half an hour by motorway: a tasting at the Casa del Vino or a winery is a good plan for 5-6 hours if you don't want to go up the mountain. Get there by taxi.

## Mount Teide from the cruise port: timings and risks

- **Outward:** about 65 km of mountain road to the cable car base station, **1h 15min**.
- **Queue, cable car (under 8 minutes) and La Rambleta viewpoints (3,555 m):** 1h 30min-2h.
- **Return:** 1h 15min-1h 30min, plus afternoon traffic on the TF-5.
- **Minimum total: 4h 30min-5h.**

The **cable car** costs about **€42 return** (€21 children), with **online booking by time slot**: there are hardly ever places at the ticket office. From late October to March it runs 9:00-17:00, with the last ascent at 16:00. The **summit (3,715 m)** needs a separate permit from Tenerife ON (€15 for non-residents of the Canaries); there's no time on a port call, so stay at La Rambleta.

**Risks:** the cable car **closes in strong wind or ice**, sometimes without warning and especially in winter. Bring a warm layer: it's cold up there even when it's 25 degrees at the port. If you book a Teide tour, check that it **picks up in Santa Cruz** and gets you back at least an hour before "all aboard". More detail in [climbing Mount Teide](/en/guides/tenerife/climbing-mount-teide).

## Tenerife shore excursions: ship's tour or independent?

- **Price:** the ship's tour is the most expensive; independently, the tram costs €1.35 and tours start at €16.
- **If there's a delay:** on the ship's tour, the ship waits for you; independently, getting back on time is your responsibility.
- **Teide:** the ship's tour is the safest option; going independently is cheaper but carries a timing risk.
- **Santa Cruz, La Laguna and Anaga:** easy on your own, no need for the ship's tour.

## Tips for your port call

- **When to be back:** "all aboard" is usually 30 to 60 minutes before departure (it's in the daily programme). From La Laguna, allow 40 minutes on the tram and 15 on foot to the ship. If you go to Teide, add **an hour's margin**.
- **Sundays and holidays:** in Santa Cruz and La Laguna many shops close and it's quiet, but terraces and the main museums stay open. The tram runs every day, less often. The cable car gets busier at weekends: book ahead.
- **Don't book a tour that picks up in the south:** Costa Adeje is well over an hour from the port.
- **Don't go up in short sleeves:** at 3,555 m there can be ice and wind in winter.
