**Riding up to Teide by quad or buggy** is a different way to see the island: you cross pine forest, reach the lunar landscapes of Las Cañadas and, at sunset, watch the sun drop over the sea of clouds. There are many routes, with different vehicles, starting points and inclusions. Here's how to choose.

The key: **as a couple, a buggy** works out better because you pay per vehicle; **alone or for more adrenaline, a quad**. And if you can, pick **the sunset departure**.

## Quad or buggy

- **Quad:** you sit as on a motorbike, more direct and adventurous. Single or double quads (the passenger sits behind).
- **Buggy:** two seats side by side, a steering wheel and seat belts. More comfortable, stable and sheltered from the wind.

## Where they leave from

- **South (Chío, Arona, La Escalona, Las Chafiras):** the most common; many include pick-up in Costa Adeje, Las Américas and Los Cristianos.
- **North (Puerto de la Cruz):** up through the Orotava valley and the pine forest.
- **Other routes:** buggies to the Masca valley or through banana farms, without entering the park.

## What to know

- **Roads only in Teide:** off-road driving is banned in the national park. Be wary of anyone promising dirt tracks there.
- **Licence:** the driver needs a valid licence; many operators ask for the physical card and an international permit for non-EU licences.
- **Age:** drivers usually must be over 18 (some ask 21); children ride as passengers from 6-7 or a minimum height.
- **Deposit and insurance:** some routes ask for a deposit or offer optional extra insurance.
- **Price:** quads are usually per person; buggies per vehicle.

## Day or sunset

- **By day:** better light for photos of Las Cañadas and warmer.
- **At sunset:** the sunset over the clouds is spectacular, but it's much colder.

## What to bring

Long trousers, closed shoes, a sweatshirt or jacket, sunglasses and your original driving licence. A scarf for the dust helps.

To see Teide on foot, read [climbing Mount Teide](/en/guides/tenerife/climbing-mount-teide); for the night, [stargazing at Teide](/en/guides/tenerife/stargazing-teide-tenerife).

## In one sentence

Buggy for two, quad if you're alone, a sunset route on the road up to Las Cañadas, and warm clothes for the way down.
