**The seabed off southern Tenerife** has volcanic rock, arches, turtles, rays and shoals of fish, and the water is mild all year. That makes it one of the best places in Spain to dive for the first time. This guide explains how a try dive works, where to do it and what to do if you'd rather stay dry.

The key: for your first time, a **try dive with turtles** in Costa Adeje is the most popular. For calm water, **Abades bay** is very sheltered; and for one-to-one attention there are **private try dives**.

## How a try dive works

1. **Briefing:** the instructor shows you the gear, how to breathe through the regulator, equalise your ears and use hand signals.
2. **Practice:** first in shallow water, standing up, until you feel comfortable.
3. **Dive:** 40 to 50 minutes at shallow depth, always next to the instructor, who controls your buoyancy.

It doesn't give you a certificate, but if you like it you can go on to a course.

## Where to dive

- **Costa Adeje:** areas with frequent turtles, very popular for try dives.
- **Los Cristianos and Las Galletas:** lots of dive centres and volcanic seabeds with arches and fish.
- **Abades:** on the east coast, a calm, sheltered bay, ideal for beginners.

## What to know

- **Health:** you'll fill in a medical questionnaire; with asthma, heart problems, epilepsy or recent surgery you may need a doctor's certificate.
- **Flying:** wait **12-24 hours** before flying.
- **Included:** almost always full gear and insurance; photos are usually extra.
- **Groups:** look for 2-4 students per instructor at most.
- **Water:** around 19 °C in winter and 23-24 °C at the end of summer; you'll get a wetsuit.

## Without diving: submarine and Sea Trek

- **Submarine:** from southern Tenerife it takes passengers down to see the seabed for about 50 minutes, without getting wet.
- **Sea Trek:** you walk on the seabed with a helmet that gives you constant air, no swimming needed.

If you'd rather stay at the surface, see [snorkelling and kayaking in Tenerife](/en/guides/tenerife/snorkelling-and-kayaking-tenerife).

## In one sentence

Book a try dive with turtles or in Abades early in your trip, leave 24 hours before your flight and enjoy Tenerife's volcanic seabed.
