**Teide is Spain's highest peak (3,715 m)** and the heart of a World Heritage National Park. You can visit it in many ways: by car through Las Cañadas, by cable car almost to the top, on foot with a permit or at night under the stars. This guide helps you choose.

## The cable car

It leaves from the **base station (2,356 m)** on the TF-21 road and climbs in about **8 minutes** to **La Rambleta (3,555 m)**. At the top there are three trails:

- **La Fortaleza viewpoint:** views over the north of the island and the La Orotava valley.
- **Pico Viejo viewpoint:** the neighbouring crater and, in the distance, La Gomera, La Palma and El Hierro.
- **Telesforo Bravo:** the path to the summit, permit only.

> **⚠️ Warning:** the cable car closes in strong wind or ice, especially in winter. Book a time slot, bring a jacket and closed shoes: you can't go up in sandals or heels.

## The summit permit

The **Telesforo Bravo** trail (about 650 m long with 160 m of climbing) leads to the crater. You need a **free National Parks permit** for a set day and time slot, which sells out well in advance. The alternative is to sleep at the **Altavista Refuge** (3,260 m) and climb at dawn before 9:00, when no permit is required.

## Hiking up

The classic route starts at **Montaña Blanca** and climbs past the refuge to La Rambleta: about 1,200 m of ascent and 5-6 hours up. Only for experienced, acclimatised hikers. Many walk up and come down by cable car.

## Las Cañadas and the Roques de García

Even if you don't go up, the park is worth the trip: the **Roques de García circuit** (about 3.5 km, 2 hours) loops around the most famous rock formations with Teide behind. Stop at the TF-21 viewpoints, such as **Minas de San José**, and at the **Parador**.

## Teide's stars

Thanks to the altitude and the **Sky Law** that protects the island from light pollution, Tenerife is one of the best places in the world for stargazing. There are sunset tours with **telescopes** and Starlight guides, and visits to the **Teide Observatory**. Bring winter clothes: nights are very cold.

## Practical tips

- **Parking:** scarce at the cable car and the Roques. Arrive before 9:00 or go on a tour.
- **Altitude sickness:** walk slowly and drink water. Not suitable for pregnant women or people with heart or breathing problems.
- **Weather:** it can be 15 degrees cooler than the coast, and it snows in winter.
- **Sun:** it burns much faster at altitude; wear sunscreen and sunglasses.
