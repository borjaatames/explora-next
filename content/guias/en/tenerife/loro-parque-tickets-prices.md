## Loro Parque ticket prices in 2026

We checked the official shop, loroparque.com, on 4 November 2026:

- **Adults (over 11):** €44.
- **Children aged 3 to 11:** €32.
- **Under 3:** free.
- **Twin Ticket (Loro Parque + Siam Park):** €78 adults and €57 children.

The ticket covers the whole park and the **four shows**: parrots, dolphins, orcas and sea lions. Food, parking and special animal experiences cost extra.

**Official website or GetYourGuide:** the price is the same, €44 per adult. The conditions differ:

- **Official website:** **no refunds**, although the ticket doesn't expire and you can use it another day.
- **GetYourGuide:** free cancellation up to **24 hours before**, with a full refund, and the ticket on your phone.

If your dates are fixed, it makes no difference where you buy. If you depend on the weather or on how the kids wake up, free cancellation is insurance at no cost.

## Twin Ticket with Siam Park: when it pays off

The Twin Ticket combines Loro Parque with [Siam Park](/en/guides/tenerife/siam-park-tickets-prices), the water park in Costa Adeje run by the same group. It saves you **€10** compared with two separate tickets, and you have **15 days** from the first visit to use the second. At Siam Park you show the Twin Ticket QR code and the wristband you get on day one.

It pays off if you are going to both parks on the same trip. If you only want one, buy the single ticket: they are at opposite ends of the island (Puerto de la Cruz in the north, Costa Adeje in the south), about 75 km apart.

## Loro Parque opening hours, visit length and free train

- **Opening hours:** every day of the year, **9:30 to 17:30**. Show times are given at the entrance and on the park's website.
- **How long:** 5 to 6 hours if you want to see all four shows without rushing.
- **Arrive at opening time:** first thing there are fewer people at Planet Penguin and the aquarium, and you don't miss the first shows.
- **Free train:** it leaves from **Plaza de los Reyes Católicos** in Puerto de la Cruz from 9:00, every 20 minutes.
- **From the south:** from Costa Adeje or Los Cristianos it is about 75-90 minutes by car. Excursions with transfer included save you the drive.

## Cheaper tickets: the discounts that exist (and those that don't)

- **Canary Islands residents:** special price, **ticket office only**, proving residence registration (certificate, ID or another valid document) with photo ID.
- **Groups of 20 or more:** special price on request from the park.
- **Spanish police and Guardia Civil:** free for the local force and 25% off for the rest of Spain, for them, their partner and children, ticket office only.
- **Large families:** no discount.

## Orcas and animal welfare

Loro Parque keeps orcas and dolphins and says its shows are part of its education and conservation work through the Loro Parque Fundación. Some animal welfare organisations criticise keeping cetaceans in captivity. If that matters to you, you can visit the park and skip the cetacean shows, or see [whales and dolphins in the wild](/en/guides/tenerife/whale-watching-tenerife) off the south coast.

## Tips

- **Buy only at loroparque.com** or on a well-known platform: several websites with similar names pose as the official one.
- **Don't get the Twin Ticket if you are only visiting one park:** it only saves money if you use both within 15 days.
- **Don't arrive at midday:** you miss shows and find longer queues at the indoor exhibits.
- **Don't count on a refund** for the official ticket; if your plans might change, buy with free cancellation.
