**Watching the sunset above the clouds and then the Milky Way with the naked eye** is one of the great things to do in Tenerife. Teide National Park combines altitude, dry air and a sky protected by law, which is why the Canary Islands Institute of Astrophysics has an observatory here. Here's how to do it.

The key: a **sunset + stargazing tour with telescopes** is the most complete option and saves you driving at night. If you only want the sky, there are **short stargazing sessions** of about 2 hours.

## Why the Teide sky is special

- **Altitude:** the park is above 2,000 metres, above much of the atmosphere and humidity.
- **Sea of clouds:** on many evenings a layer of cloud sits below and hides the light from the coastal towns.
- **Sky Law (1988):** it limits light pollution on the island to protect the observatories of the Canary Islands Institute of Astrophysics.

## Types of tour

- **Sunset + stars (3-5 h):** hotel pick-up, sunset above the clouds and stargazing with telescopes and a laser to point out constellations. Some include dinner or a picnic.
- **Stargazing only (about 2 h):** you meet the guide in the park and look through telescopes.
- **With astrophotography:** you take home photos of the sky or a galaxy taken with the guide's equipment.
- **By quad, buggy or convertible at sunset:** more adventure and less astronomy; you see the sunset but there are no telescopes.

## When to go

- **Moon:** new-moon and waning nights are best; at full moon you see far fewer stars, though the Moon through a telescope is spectacular.
- **Season:** all year. Summer is usually clearer and the Milky Way is high; winter is colder.
- **Weather:** with high cloud or strong wind the tour may be cancelled or moved.

## Before you book

- **Cold:** at night at that altitude it can drop below 0 °C. Some tours lend jackets or blankets; ask.
- **Altitude:** if you have heart or breathing problems, check first; some tours don't recommend it.
- **Pick-up:** many leave from the south (Costa Adeje, Las Américas, Los Cristianos), others from the north (Puerto de la Cruz). Check the listing.
- **Children:** welcome, but you get back late.

## What to bring

A warm jacket, hat and gloves, closed shoes, water and a snack if not included. A red-light torch helps you not dazzle others.

If you want to go up by day, see [climbing Mount Teide](/en/guides/tenerife/climbing-mount-teide). More ideas in [what to see in Tenerife](/en/guides/tenerife/what-to-see-in-tenerife).

## In one sentence

Pick a moonless night, book a sunset tour with telescopes, dress for winter and you'll see one of the best skies in the world.
