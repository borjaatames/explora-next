## Siam Park ticket prices in 2026

We checked them on 4 November 2026. Siam Park changes prices in summer, so confirm the one for your date on the official website, siampark.net, before paying.

- **General admission:** €44 for over-12s and €32 for children aged 3 to 11. **Under-3s go free**.
- **Canary Islands residents:** reduced rate, with proof of residence at the entrance.
- **Twin Ticket with Loro Parque:** €78 per adult and €57 per child. Each park is used on a different day.
- **Fast Pass:** bought separately and **doesn't include entry**. The limited one costs about €38 and is sold only at the ticket office, with a daily quota; the unlimited one, about €99.
- **All-inclusive:** about €165 per person, with unlimited Fast Pass, food and drink, towel and locker.

In **July and August** some prices go up a few euros. On GetYourGuide general admission costs the same, €44, with free cancellation. Tickets are for the day, with no fixed time slot.

## Siam Park opening hours and getting there

Siam Park is open **every day of the year**:

- **30 October to 30 April:** 10:00 to 17:00.
- **1 May to 29 October:** 10:00 to 18:00.

**Without a car:** the park runs **free buses** with stops in Playa de las Américas, Los Cristianos and Costa Adeje; they leave in the morning and return in the afternoon, until closing time. From the north of the island there are paid buses that must be booked in advance.

**By car:** **parking costs about €7 a day** and spaces are limited and can't be reserved.

## How to avoid the queues

Siam Park rarely puts up a sold-out sign, but in July, August, Easter and school holidays the queues for the Tower of Power and the big slides go past an hour. What works best:

1. **Arrive at opening time, 10:00,** and start with the big rides. By mid-morning there are queues.
2. **Go on a weekday and outside high season.** From November to March it's much quieter.
3. **In summer, decide on the Fast Pass early.** The limited one is sold at the ticket office and can sell out before midday.
4. **Buy your ticket before leaving the hotel:** you go straight to the turnstile and don't lose time at the ticket office.

## What to bring, height limits and what costs extra inside

- **Water shoes or flip-flops:** the ground gets scorching at midday and there are lots of stairs up to the slides.
- **Towel:** you can rent or buy one inside, so better bring your own.
- **Money for a locker:** they cost about €7 or €10 depending on size.
- **Height limits:** the Tower of Power requires **1.49 m and age 12**, and most slides ask for 1.10 to 1.25 m. For little ones there's the Lost City and the beach.

Sunloungers, parasols, rings and life jackets are included in the ticket.

## Siam Park, Loro Parque and the rest of Tenerife

With a week on the island, the usual plan is one day at Siam Park, another at Loro Parque in Puerto de la Cruz and another at Teide. The Twin Ticket saves money on the parks; for Teide, book the cable car well ahead, as we explain in [Teide tickets sold out](/en/guides/tenerife/teide-tickets-sold-out). If you're planning the whole trip, see [what to see in Tenerife](/en/guides/tenerife/what-to-see-in-tenerife).

## Tips

- **The Fast Pass doesn't include entry:** they're two separate purchases.
- **Buy at siampark.net or from trusted agencies:** some websites imitate the official one and add surcharges.
- **Don't arrive at midday in August:** you lose the best hours and get the longest queues.
- **If you're a resident, bring your ID:** without it the reduced rate doesn't apply.
