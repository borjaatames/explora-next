Siam Park in Costa Adeje is Tenerife's best-known water park: Thai-themed slides, an artificial wave of almost three metres and the Tower of Power, a 28-metre drop through an aquarium. Tickets are for the day with no fixed time, so getting one is rarely the problem. The real questions are which ticket to buy and how to avoid spending half the day queuing.

## Siam Park prices in 2026

We checked these on 4 November 2026. Siam Park changes some prices in summer, so confirm yours on the official site `siampark.net` before paying.

- **Day ticket:** €44 for ages 12 and over, €32 for children aged 3 to 11. **Under-3s go free.**
- **Canary Islands residents:** reduced rate, with proof of residence at the entrance.
- **Twin Ticket with Loro Parque:** €78 per adult and €57 per child. You use each park on a different day.
- **Fast Pass:** sold separately and **does not include admission**. The Limited Fast Pass costs about €38 and is only sold at the ticket office, with a daily cap; the Unlimited one costs about €99.
- **All Inclusive:** about €165 per person, with Unlimited Fast Pass, food and drink, towel and locker.

Some prices go up by a few euros in **July and August**. On GetYourGuide the day ticket costs the same €44, with free cancellation.

## Opening times and getting there

Siam Park is open **every day of the year**:

- **30 October to 30 April:** 10:00 to 17:00.
- **1 May to 29 October:** 10:00 to 18:00.

Without a car, the park's **free shuttle buses** stop in Playa de las Américas, Los Cristianos and Costa Adeje: they run out in the morning and back in the afternoon until closing. Paid buses from the north of the island have to be booked in advance. If you drive, **parking costs about €7 a day** and spaces are limited and cannot be booked.

## How to beat the queues

Siam Park rarely puts up a sold-out sign, but in July, August, Easter and school holidays the queues for the Tower of Power and the big slides go past an hour. What works best:

1. **Arrive at opening, 10:00,** and do the big rides first. By mid-morning the queues are building.
2. **Go on a weekday outside peak season.** From November to March there are far fewer people.
3. **In summer, decide on the Fast Pass early.** The Limited one is sold at the ticket office and can sell out before midday.
4. **Buy your ticket before leaving the hotel,** so you go straight to the turnstiles.

## What to bring and what costs extra

- **Water shoes or flip-flops.** The ground gets very hot at midday and there are lots of stairs up to the slides.
- **A towel.** You can rent or buy one inside, but bringing your own is cheaper.
- **Money for a locker.** Lockers cost about €7 or €10 depending on size.
- **Check the height limits.** The Tower of Power requires 1.49 m and age 12+, and most slides need 1.10 to 1.25 m. Younger children have the Lost City and the beach.

Sun loungers, parasols, rubber rings and life vests are included in the ticket.

## Siam Park, Loro Parque and the rest of Tenerife

With a week on the island, a common plan is one day at Siam Park, one at Loro Parque in Puerto de la Cruz and one on Mount Teide. The Twin Ticket saves money on the parks, and the Teide cable car is worth booking ahead: see [Teide tickets sold out](/en/guides/tenerife/teide-tickets-sold-out). If you are planning the whole trip, read [what to see in Tenerife](/en/guides/tenerife/what-to-see-in-tenerife).

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Buying a Fast Pass thinking it includes entry.** It doesn't: they are two separate purchases.
2. **Buying on copycat sites** that add surcharges. The official site is `siampark.net`; trusted resellers charge the same price.
3. **Arriving at midday in August.** You miss the best hours and get the longest queues.
4. **Forgetting your ID if you are a resident.** Without it, the reduced rate doesn't apply.

## In one sentence

Buy the €44 day ticket, or the Twin Ticket if you are also going to Loro Parque, arrive at 10:00 and start with the big rides; in summer, decide on the Fast Pass first thing.
