**Tenerife is one of the best places in Europe to see whales and dolphins**, and all year round. Between its south-west coast and La Gomera lives a stable population of **short-finned pilot whales** and **bottlenose dolphins**, in a protected marine area recognised as a *Whale Heritage Site*. The key is choosing the right boat.

## Which species you'll see

- **Short-finned pilot whale:** the star. It lives in family groups and is seen on almost every trip.
- **Bottlenose dolphin:** also resident, curious and common.
- **With luck:** Atlantic spotted dolphin, common dolphin, Bryde's whale and, more rarely, sperm whale.

## Where the boats leave from

The ports of **Los Cristianos**, **Puerto Colón** (Costa Adeje) and **Los Gigantes** are minutes from the cetacean area. From Los Gigantes you also see the cliffs from the sea.

## How to choose a responsible boat

- Look for operators with the Canary Islands government's **whale-watching licence** (the *Barco Azul* badge).
- Avoid boats that **chase** the animals or get too close: the law requires keeping a distance.
- Prefer **small or eco-friendly boats**, quieter and more stable.
- Be wary of "swim with dolphins" offers: it's **forbidden**.

> **⭐ Tip:** if you get seasick, choose a wide-hulled boat or a catamaran and the first departure of the day, when the sea is usually calmer.

## When to go

All year, because the main species are resident. **Mornings** usually have the calmest sea. Winter can bring more swell.

## What to bring

Sunscreen, a cap, swimwear and a towel (many trips include a swim stop), a light jacket and a seasickness tablet if you need one.

## Other ways to see the sea

The **kayak** tour from Los Cristianos follows the volcanic coast and sometimes meets dolphins or turtles. And the **Masca Gorge** hike ends with a boat ride beneath the Los Gigantes Cliffs. More ideas in [what to see in Tenerife](/en/guides/tenerife/what-to-see-in-tenerife).

## In one sentence

Choose a licensed boat that doesn't chase the animals, head out in the morning from the south-west and you'll have every chance of seeing wild pilot whales.
