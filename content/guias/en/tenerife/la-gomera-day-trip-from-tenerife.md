**La Gomera is the green island you see from southern Tenerife.** Mist-covered laurel forest, deep ravines and quiet villages, less than an hour away by boat. It's one of the most beautiful day trips from Tenerife and easy to arrange. Here's how.

## What you see in a day

- **Garajonay National Park:** laurel forest, a humid forest that covered southern Europe millions of years ago. A World Heritage Site since 1986.
- **Roque de Agando:** a volcanic rock plug visible from the road across the centre of the island.
- **San Sebastián de La Gomera:** the capital, with the Church of the Assumption, linked to Columbus's stop before his first voyage, and the Torre del Conde.
- **Silbo gomero:** the island's whistled language, on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list; many tours include a demonstration at lunch.

## How to get there

- **Ferry from Los Cristianos:** about 50 minutes on the fast boat to San Sebastián. Several companies and daily departures.
- **Organised tour:** pick-up in south Tenerife, ferry, bus or jeep on La Gomera, guide and often lunch.
- **On your own:** book the ferry ahead and hire a car on La Gomera; the roads are mountainous and winding.

## What to know

- **Documents:** the ferry requires ID or passport.
- **Length:** a full day, 9 to 11 hours with transfers.
- **Weather:** Garajonay can be misty and cool even when the coast is sunny.
- **Seasickness:** the crossing can be choppy; take a tablet beforehand.
- **Reduced mobility:** many tours don't recommend it because of the walks and roads.

For the rest of your trip, see [what to see in Tenerife](/en/guides/tenerife/what-to-see-in-tenerife). The channel between Tenerife and La Gomera is also prime [whale-watching](/en/guides/tenerife/whale-watching-tenerife) water.

## In one sentence

A 50-minute ferry from Los Cristianos, a day in Garajonay and at Roque de Agando, and back in the evening: the best day trip away from Tenerife.
