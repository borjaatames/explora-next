## The best coves in Mallorca by area

They are not all good for the same thing: choose by the day and by the wind, which leaves some sheltered and others choppy.

| Area | Top coves | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| South | Es Trenc, Cala Pi | Long and wild / narrow cove between cliffs |
| Southeast | Mondragó, Caló des Moro, Cala Llombards | Turquoise, small and photogenic |
| Northeast | Cala Agulla, Cala Mesquida | Wide, with dunes and pine woods |
| North | Formentor, Playa de Muro | Family-friendly, calm water |
| Tramuntana | Sa Calobra, Cala Tuent, Cala Deià | Dramatic, between mountains |

## The essentials: Es Trenc, Cala Mondragó and Caló des Moro

**Es Trenc (south).** The most famous unspoilt beach: white sand and Caribbean-blue water, no buildings, next to the salt flats. It has hardly any facilities (three kiosks); bring shade and water.

**Cala Mondragó (southeast).** Inside a **natural park**, with two calm stretches of sand (s'Amarador and Mondragó). Turquoise water in a protected setting; **ideal for families**.

**Caló des Moro (southeast).** The cove from the photos: tiny, wedged between rocks, with unreal water. Beautiful but **it gets packed**; parking is minimal and you go down steps and rocks.

**Sa Calobra and Cala Tuent (Tramuntana).** Spectacular coves at the foot of the mountains, at the end of the island's most hair-raising road. Arrive early or go by boat.

**Playa de Muro / Alcúdia (north).** The big family beach: fine sand, long and shallow, with every facility.

## Getting there and parking without the queues

The golden rule in July and August: arrive **before 9:00** or **after 18:00**, or go by boat or on an excursion. At midday in August there is no room even for your towel.

- **Car parks:** Es Trenc (Ses Covetes and by the salt flats, on the Colònia de Sant Jordi road), Cala Mondragó and Sa Calobra have paid car parks that fill up by mid-morning. Parking on the verge or on private tracks gets you fined.
- **Caló des Moro:** the access streets are reserved for residents; park at the entrance to Cala Llombards and walk **1.5 km (20-25 minutes)**.
- **Cala Varques:** reached along a dirt track of about **40 minutes**; the shortcut through private estates from the Ma-4014 is no longer allowed.
- **Formentor (2026 rules):** from **15 May to 18 October, 10:00 to 22:00**, private cars can only reach the beach car park while spaces last; once it is full, a barrier closes the road. The stretch to the lighthouse is for authorised vehicles only, with fines for everyone else. Alternatives: **TIB line 334** from Alcúdia and Port de Pollença, the boat from Port de Pollença, or the [minibus and boat excursion](/en/cities/mallorca/activities/formentor-minibus-boat-tour-viewpoint-beach), which stops at the Es Colomer viewpoint and the beach.
- **Sa Calobra without the bends:** the boat from Port de Sóller (**from €35** return, 45-60 minutes beneath the cliffs) avoids 12 km of the island's twistiest road.

## Mallorca beaches with or without facilities: what to bring

| With facilities (beach bar, parasols) | Unspoilt (bring everything) |
| --- | --- |
| Playa de Muro, Cala Agulla, Mondragó | Es Trenc, Caló des Moro, Cala Varques |

- **Prices:** at beaches with facilities, two sunloungers and a parasol cost **€18-30 a day**.
- **Unspoilt coves have no water for sale:** bring **2 litres per person** and a parasol.
- **Water shoes:** some coves have rocks or pebbles.
- **Sea temperature:** 24-27 °C from July to September and below 20 °C before June.
- **Jellyfish:** they arrive with the easterly wind (levante); check the lifeguard flags and warning apps before you set off.
- **Nudism:** accepted on the more secluded stretches of Es Trenc, Cala Varques and part of Cala Mesquida.

## Tips and mistakes to avoid

1. **Don't go to the small coves at midday in summer.** No parking and packed out; getting up early is the only way to park at the popular coves.
2. **Don't expect facilities at unspoilt coves.** Es Trenc has just three kiosks and Cala Varques none.
3. **Check the wind before you choose.** Depending on where it blows from, one cove or another will be sheltered or choppy.
4. **Look after the posidonia.** The seagrass meadows, protected by law throughout the Balearics, are what make the water so clear.
5. **Caló des Moro is not for reduced mobility or pushchairs:** the way down is over rocks and steps.
