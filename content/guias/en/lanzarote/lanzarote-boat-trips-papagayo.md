**The Papagayo beaches are the main reason to get on a boat in Lanzarote.** They're a string of golden-sand coves with turquoise water at the island's southern tip, inside a protected natural area, and they look even better from the sea. There are catamarans with food and open bar, short mini-cruises, kayaking and full-day trips to La Graciosa. Here's how to choose.

The key: for **a beach day from the sea**, a 4-hour catamaran to Papagayo with food. If you're **short on time or have young children**, a mini-cruise from Playa Blanca. If you like paddling, kayak and snorkel.

## Types of trip

- **Catamaran to Papagayo (4 h):** the classic. It sails along the south coast, anchors off the coves for swimming and snorkelling, and serves food and drinks on board. Some have a slide, kayaks or paddleboards.
- **Mini-cruise from Playa Blanca (1.5-3 h):** shorter and cheaper, with a swim stop. A good choice with children.
- **Kayak and snorkel (3-3.5 h):** you paddle from Playa Blanca to the Papagayo coves with a guide and swim in very clear water. No experience needed.
- **Catamaran with water sports (3 h):** from Puerto del Carmen, with activities in the water for active groups.
- **Full day to La Graciosa (6 h):** catamaran with lunch, swimming and kayaking on the neighbouring island to the north.

## Which port to leave from

- **Playa Blanca (Marina Rubicón):** the closest to Papagayo; short crossings.
- **Puerto Calero:** a quiet marina between Puerto del Carmen and Playa Blanca, with upmarket catamarans.
- **Puerto del Carmen:** the resort with the most hotels; from here the sail to Papagayo is longer.

Many trips include pick-up from hotels near the departure port. Check the listing before booking.

## Tips

- **Book a morning trip** if you get seasick: the trade wind usually picks up in the afternoon.
- **Bring** swimwear on, a towel, reef-safe sunscreen, a cap and a sweatshirt for the way back.
- **Northerly wind** sometimes makes the sea choppy off Puerto del Carmen; Playa Blanca is usually more sheltered.
- **With children,** choose the mini-cruise or a catamaran with a toilet and shade.
- **If the sea is rough,** the operator may cancel and offer another date or a refund.

## When to go

You can sail **all year**. The water is around 18 °C in winter and 22-23 °C at the end of summer. In summer the wind is stronger, but the south of the island is fairly sheltered.

If you'd like to visit the neighbouring island on your own, see [how to get to La Graciosa](/en/guides/lanzarote/how-to-get-to-la-graciosa). For the rest of the trip, [what to see in Lanzarote](/en/guides/lanzarote/what-to-see-in-lanzarote).

## In one sentence

To see Papagayo from the sea, take a 4-hour catamaran with food and a swim from Playa Blanca, Puerto Calero or Puerto del Carmen; if you're short on time, a short mini-cruise from Playa Blanca.
