**La Geria is one of the most unusual farming landscapes in the world.** Thousands of black hollows, each with a vine and its crescent-shaped stone wall, cover the volcanic slopes of central Lanzarote. This is where Malvasía Volcánica, the island's best-known wine, is made, and there are wineries along the road where you can taste it. Here's how to visit.

The key: if you have a car, **drive the LZ-30** and stop at a couple of wineries. To **understand the wine**, book a guided visit with a tasting. If you'd rather not drive, choose a **walking route** or a tour combining La Geria with Timanfaya.

## What makes La Geria unique

- **Hollow cultivation:** each vine is planted in a hollow dug through the ash down to fertile soil, sheltered from the wind by a volcanic stone wall.
- **The ash:** black picón holds the moisture from the dew, making it possible to farm with almost no rain.
- **Protected landscape:** La Geria is a protected natural area; the vineyards share the landscape with volcanic cones and lava flows.

## Ways to visit

- **On your own by car:** the LZ-30 road crosses the valley between Uga and Masdache. There are wineries with shops and bars, viewpoints and places to park.
- **Guided winery visit:** Bodega El Grifo, one of the oldest in the Canaries, offers a visit with a tasting of six wines with its sommelier. Finca Testeina has a short vineyard visit with a wine and chocolate tasting.
- **Walking route with tasting:** a guided hike between volcanoes and vineyards, ending with local wine and cheese.
- **On-road buggy:** a guided route to El Cuervo volcano through La Geria.
- **Combined tour:** half-day or full-day tours with Timanfaya, El Golfo and La Geria, with a winery stop.

## Tips

- **If you're driving, don't drink:** choose a guided visit with transport or pick a designated driver.
- **Go in the morning or late afternoon:** less heat and better light on the black landscape.
- **Buy at the winery:** prices are usually good and some wines are only found on the island.
- **Combine it with Timanfaya:** it's right next door, and most southern routes pass through La Geria.

## When to go

You can visit La Geria **all year**. In spring the vines sprout and the green against the black is spectacular; the harvest is from late July to August.
