**Lanzarote has volcanic seabeds with arches, caves and plenty of life**: schools of fish, rays, moray eels and, with luck, angel sharks. The water is clear and there are sheltered coves where you can try scuba with no experience. There's also the Museo Atlántico, an underwater sculpture museum off Playa Blanca. Here's how to choose.

The key: to **try diving**, a try dive at Playa Chica or Costa Teguise. If you've already tried it or want something different, **a dive at the Museo Atlántico**. To **see fish without a tank**, guided snorkelling; and if you'd rather stay dry, the submarine.

## Try dive

- **Who it's for:** anyone who can swim and is in good health, no certificate needed. The minimum age is usually 8-10.
- **What it's like:** a briefing on land, practice in shallow water and one or two dives with the instructor, down to about 12 m.
- **Where:** **Playa Chica** (Puerto del Carmen), the island's best-known dive cove, with easy shore entry; and **Costa Teguise**, with calm seabeds.
- **What's included:** all the gear and often photos.

## Museo Atlántico

An underwater sculpture museum in Las Coloradas bay, Playa Blanca, at about 12-14 m. You visit it diving with an authorised centre; there are also beginner dives with an instructor. Visibility is usually good, and marine life has gradually settled among the pieces.

## Snorkelling and submarine

- **Guided snorkelling (2 h):** at Playa Chica or Costa Teguise, with gear and an instructor. Suitable for all levels.
- **Kayak and snorkel at Papagayo:** paddle to turquoise coves and swim from the kayak.
- **Submarine from Puerto Calero (1 h):** goes down to about 30 m without getting wet. Ideal with children or for non-swimmers.

## If you're already certified

Lanzarote has well-known dives such as the **Cathedral**, a large cave off Puerto del Carmen, and walls full of life on the east coast. Centres run shore and boat dives.

## Tips

- **Tell the centre** if you take medication or have ear, heart or breathing problems.
- **Don't fly** for 12-18 hours after diving.
- **Don't touch or feed the animals** and keep your fins off the seabed.
- **The water is a little cooler** than on other islands: the centre will give you a suitable wetsuit.

## When to go

You can dive **all year**. The water is around 18 °C in winter and 22-23 °C at the end of summer. The east and south coasts are usually more sheltered.
