## Órzola-La Graciosa ferry: companies, timetables and prices

**Líneas Romero** and **Biosfera Express** run the route with fast passenger boats. The crossing takes **about 25 minutes** and arrives at the port of Caleta de Sebo.

- **Biosfera Express (October 2026 prices):** €16 one way and **€29 return** per non-resident adult; children €8 and €15. Canary Islands residents: €4 one way and €7.25 return.
- **Líneas Romero:** returns **from €28** for adults and from €15 for children aged 2 to 11; Canary Islands residents get 75% off.
- **Biosfera Express timetable:** from Órzola at 8:00, 9:00, 10:30, 11:30, 13:00, 16:30, 17:30, 18:30 and 19:30; from La Graciosa at 7:00, 8:30, 9:30, 10:30, 12:00, 15:30, 16:30, 17:30 and 18:30. Líneas Romero has its own timetable, with departures throughout the day.
- **Watch the midday gap:** with Biosfera Express there are no departures from Órzola between 13:00 and 16:30, or from La Graciosa between 12:00 and 15:30.
- **Bikes and pets:** the companies accept them; mention it when you buy your ticket.

In July, August, Easter and at weekends, buy ahead, especially the return. And check the **last boat back** before heading to the beaches: miss it and you'll have to sleep on the island, where accommodation sells out in summer.

## La Graciosa day trip: on your own or by catamaran?

The **ferry on your own** is best if you want to explore the island and reach its beaches. The [catamaran trip](/en/cities/lanzarote/activities/la-graciosa-catamaran-cruise-lunch) (from €66) includes pick-up, the ferry, paella on board and a swim off Playa Francesa, but on land you only set foot in Caleta de Sebo: it's the option if you'd rather not organise anything.

## Getting to Órzola

**Órzola** is the fishing village at the northern tip of Lanzarote, about **35 minutes' drive from Arrecife** on the LZ-1. There's **free parking** in the village, but it fills up fast in summer and at weekends; the ferry companies have parking for their passengers, with different conditions for each.

By bus, **Arrecife Bus line 9** links Arrecife and Órzola with a few departures a day which, according to La Graciosa's tourism website, connect with the boats. Check the timetable on the Arrecife Bus website before relying on it.

## Getting around the island

On La Graciosa **you can't bring or rent a car**. You have three options:

- **On foot:** the southern beaches are less than an hour from Caleta de Sebo along flat sand tracks, with no shade the whole way.
- **By bike:** there are several rental shops by the port, with mountain bikes and e-bikes. The tracks have stretches of soft sand; an e-bike helps a lot.
- **By 4x4 taxi:** they wait at the port taxi rank from 8:00. According to the island's tourism website (visitlagraciosa.com), a beach transfer costs from €10 per person return, and the northern circuit (Las Conchas, Lambra and Pedro Barba) about €25 per person in 90 minutes.

## La Graciosa beaches

- **Playa Francesa:** the nearest in the south, with golden sand and calm water, about 30-40 minutes on foot. It's where the catamarans anchor.
- **La Cocina and Montaña Amarilla:** at the southern tip, at the foot of the yellow volcano that gives the area its name. Turquoise water and few people.
- **Las Conchas:** in the north, just over 5 km from Caleta de Sebo, facing the islet of Montaña Clara. It's the most spectacular, but has **strong currents** and the island's beaches have **no lifeguards**. In rough seas, walk and climb Montaña Bermeja rather than swim.
- **Pedro Barba:** a small hamlet of holiday homes on the east coast, with hardly any permanent residents.

## Practical tips

- **Water, food and sun protection:** outside Caleta de Sebo there are no shops, bars or shade. Bring a hat and more water than you think you'll need.
- **Footwear:** sandals for the sand, but trainers if you're walking the tracks or climbing Montaña Amarilla.
- **Cash:** carry some in case a shop or taxi doesn't take cards.
- **Where to eat:** Caleta de Sebo has several fish restaurants by the port; at lunchtime in high season they fill up.
- **Staying overnight:** there are guesthouses and apartments in Caleta de Sebo; staying a night lets you see the island without the day-trippers.
- **Respect the environment:** it's part of the Chinijo Archipelago Natural Park and surrounded by a marine reserve; stay on the tracks and don't climb fragile formations.
