**Famara is Lanzarote's surf beach.** Six kilometres of sand beneath a 600-metre cliff, waves almost all year and a small village, Caleta de Famara, full of schools and surf atmosphere. It's one of the best places in the Canaries to catch your first wave, and most people manage to stand up in a single lesson. Here's how to choose yours.

The key: to **try it**, a 2-2.5-hour lesson; to **really progress**, the 4-hour one or several lessons in a row. If you're staying in the south without a car, choose a school with hotel pick-up.

## What a lesson is like

- **Duration:** 2 to 4 hours, with a briefing on the sand and most of the time in the water.
- **What's included:** a soft beginner board, wetsuit and instructor. Some add video or photos to correct your technique.
- **Groups:** small, with several students per instructor; private lessons are available.
- **Level:** schools have beginner and improver groups. Mention your level when booking.
- **Age:** many accept children from 6-8 if they can swim.

## Famara and other spots

- **Famara beach:** sandy bottom, beach breaks and plenty of space. The best place to start.
- **La Santa and the north-west coast:** powerful, dangerous reef breaks, for expert surfers only.
- **South coast:** fewer waves; southern schools usually take students to Famara.

## Tips

- **Book early in your trip:** if you like it, you can go again.
- **Bring** swimwear, a towel, water-resistant sunscreen and something warm for afterwards: it's often windy at Famara.
- **Follow your instructor:** Famara can have currents.
- **Enjoy the village:** Caleta de Famara has sandy streets, terraces facing the cliff and fresh fish.

## When to go

You can surf **all year**. From autumn to spring more swell arrives and the waves are bigger; in summer they're usually smaller, perfect for beginners, though windier. The water is around 18 °C in winter and 22 °C at the end of summer.

From Famara the north of the island and the view over La Graciosa are close by: see [what to see in Lanzarote](/en/guides/lanzarote/what-to-see-in-lanzarote) and [how to get to La Graciosa](/en/guides/lanzarote/how-to-get-to-la-graciosa).

## In one sentence

To learn to surf in Lanzarote, take a 2-4-hour lesson at Famara with a local school, with pick-up if you're staying in the south, at any time of year.
