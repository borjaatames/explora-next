**Seville is the best base for exploring western Andalusia.** Within two hours you have another World Heritage city (Córdoba), the white villages of the Cádiz hills, the sea at Cádiz, the sherry bodegas of Jerez and, a little further, Mérida, Río Tinto or Gibraltar. This guide sorts them by distance and tells you, for each one, whether you're better off going on your own, on a group tour or privately.

## Day trips at a glance

| Destination | From Seville | How to go | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Itálica | 15 min by car | Bus or car | A half-day, Roman ruins |
| Córdoba | 45 min by high-speed train · 1 h 30 by car | Train on your own or tour | Mosque-Cathedral and Jewish Quarter |
| Cádiz and Jerez | 1 h 45 by train to Cádiz · 1 h to Jerez | Train or tour | Sea, old town and bodegas |
| Doñana | 1 h to El Rocío | Tour (the park is visited with a guide) | Nature and birds |
| Río Tinto | 1 h 15 by car | Car or tour | Red mining landscape and mining train |
| Ronda and white villages | 1 h 45-2 h by car | Car or tour | The gorge, Setenil, Zahara, Grazalema |
| Mérida | 2 h by car | Car or tour | Roman theatre and amphitheatre |
| Gibraltar | 2 h 15 by car | Tour (cross the border on foot) | The Rock, the monkeys and the tunnels |

Times are by road or train in normal conditions; in summer and on bank holidays, allow a bit more.

## Córdoba: the easiest and best value

By **high-speed train**, Seville to Córdoba takes about 45 minutes, so it's the only one on this list that's easy on your own. Head straight to the **Mosque-Cathedral** (buy your ticket ahead), wander the **Jewish Quarter** and cross the **Roman bridge** at sunset. In May it coincides with the patios festival.

- **On your own:** train plus a Mosque-Cathedral ticket. Cheapest for 1 or 2.
- **Group tour:** [small group with high-speed train](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cordoba-premium-small-group-tour-high-speed-train-from-seville) from €110 per person, or [by coach](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cordoba-full-day-essential-mezquita-tour-from-seville) from €109.
- **Private:** [Córdoba by train with tickets and a private tour](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cordoba-by-train-private-day-trip-from-seville) (€432 for a group of 2, €668 for 4) or [by private car](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cordoba-private-day-trip-from-seville) (from €379 per person, minimum 2).

## Ronda and the white villages

The most beautiful trip for scenery, and the hardest without a car: buses to the hill villages are few. **Ronda** takes half a day (Puente Nuevo, the gorge, the bullring) and the rest goes to **Setenil de las Bodegas**, **Zahara de la Sierra** or **Grazalema**.

- **Group tour:** [white villages and Ronda](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/pueblos-blancos-ronda-day-trip-from-seville) from €47 per person; it's the day trip from Seville with the most reviews (over 4,000).
- **Private:** [Ronda with or without a guide](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/ronda-private-day-trip-from-seville-with-or-without-guide) (€500 for a group of 2, €720 for 4) or [white villages and Ronda](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/white-villages-ronda-private-day-trip-from-seville) (from €389 per person, minimum 2).

## Cádiz and Jerez

**Cádiz** is 1 h 45 by commuter train and is best explored on foot: the cathedral, La Viña, La Caleta beach. **Jerez** is halfway and the place to visit a sherry bodega. Together they make a long day, but it can be done.

- **Group tour:** [Cádiz and Jerez](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cadiz-jerez-de-la-frontera-day-trip-from-seville) from €95 per person.
- **Private:** [Cádiz and Jerez with a guide and bodega](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cadiz-jerez-private-day-trip-from-seville-winery) (€870 for a group of 2, €1,160 for 4).

## Mérida, Río Tinto and Gibraltar: further, better organised

- **Mérida** (2 h): its Roman theatre and amphitheatre are among the best preserved in Spain. [Private day trip](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/merida-roman-private-day-trip-from-seville) from €394 per person, minimum 2.
- **Río Tinto mines** (1 h 15): the red river, the English quarter and the mining train. [Private day trip](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/rio-tinto-mines-private-day-trip-from-seville) from €388 per person, minimum 2.
- **Gibraltar** (2 h 15): you cross the border on foot and your guide picks you up to go up the Rock. [Private day trip with tickets](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/gibraltar-private-day-trip-from-seville) (€860 for a group of 2, €1,040 for 4). Bring the ID your nationality requires.
- **Tangier** (Morocco): only on an [organised tour](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/tangier-morocco-day-trip-from-seville) with the ferry included; it's about 15 hours.

## Nature near Seville

- **Doñana**: the national park is visited with a guide in an authorised vehicle. [Full-day tour](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/donana-full-day-tour-from-seville).
- **Cerro del Hierro** (Sierra Norte): old mines turned into a maze of rock. [Private hike with picnic](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cerro-del-hierro-private-hike-picnic-from-seville).
- **Aracena and the dehesa**: free-range Iberian pigs and ham. [Dehesa day trip](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/iberian-ham-farm-aracena-day-trip-from-seville).
- **Itálica**, 15 minutes away, is the shortest escape: a [2-hour private visit](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/italica-private-guided-tour-santiponce) or a [group tour with the monastery](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/italica-roman-ruins-medieval-monastery-from-seville) from €42.

## Group or private: when it pays off

A group tour is always cheaper for 1 or 2 people. On a private tour you pay for the group (or with a minimum number of people), so **the price per person drops a lot from 4 people**:

| Day trip | Group tour, per person | Private, 2 people | Private, 4 people |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Córdoba | €109-110 | €216 per person | €167 per person |
| Ronda | €47 (with white villages) | €250 per person | €180 per person |
| Cádiz and Jerez | €95 | €435 per person | €290 per person |
| Gibraltar | — | €430 per person | €260 per person |

Prices from the tour pages at the date of this guide; they may vary by date.

What you get privately: **pickup from your accommodation**, departure when you choose, stops at your pace and nobody else in the vehicle. For families with children, older travellers or groups of friends it usually pays off; for a couple on a tight budget, the group tour or the train on your own.

## Tips for choosing

1. **One trip per day.** Córdoba and Cádiz can be combined with a free afternoon in Seville; Ronda, Mérida or Gibraltar take the whole day.
2. **In summer, leave early.** At midday in July and August Córdoba and the countryside easily pass 40 °C.
3. **Book the key tickets.** The Mosque-Cathedral and, in high season, the Jerez bodegas fill up.
4. **If there are 4 or more of you, compare the private option.** Work out the price per person before deciding: the gap with the coach may be smaller than it looks.
