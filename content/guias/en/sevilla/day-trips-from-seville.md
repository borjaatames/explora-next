## Seville to Córdoba day trip: the easiest and best value

By **AVE**, Seville-Córdoba takes about 45 minutes, so it's the only one on this list that's easy to do on your own. Head straight to the **Mezquita-Cathedral** (buy your ticket first), wander the **Jewish Quarter** and cross the **Roman bridge** at sunset. If you go in May, it coincides with the patios festival.

- **On your own:** train and Mezquita ticket. Cheapest for 1 or 2 people.
- **Group:** by AVE in a small group from €110 per person, or by coach from €109.
- **Private:** [Córdoba by train with tickets and a private tour](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cordoba-by-train-private-day-trip-from-seville) (€432 for a group of 2, €668 for 4), or by private car from €379 per person (minimum 2).

## Ronda day trip from Seville and the white villages

The most beautiful trip for scenery, and the one that works worst without a car: buses to the mountain villages are few. **Ronda** takes half a day (Puente Nuevo, the gorge, the bullring) and the rest is shared between **Setenil de las Bodegas**, **Zahara de la Sierra** or **Grazalema**.

- **Group:** white villages and Ronda from **€47 per person**; it's the day trip from Seville with the most reviews (over 4,000).
- **Private:** Ronda with or without a guide (€500 for a group of 2, €720 for 4), or white villages and Ronda from €389 per person (minimum 2).

## Cádiz and Jerez

**Cádiz** is 1 h 45 away by commuter train and best explored on foot: the cathedral, La Viña quarter, La Caleta. **Jerez** is halfway and the place to visit a sherry winery. Together they make a long day, but it's doable.

- **Group:** [Cádiz and Jerez](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cadiz-jerez-de-la-frontera-day-trip-from-seville) from €95 per person.
- **Private:** with a guide and winery, €870 for a group of 2 and €1,160 for 4.

## Mérida, Río Tinto and Gibraltar from Seville: better organised

- **Mérida** (2 h): the Roman theatre and amphitheatre are among the best preserved in Spain. Private trip from €394 per person, minimum 2.
- **Río Tinto mines** (1 h 15): the red river, the English quarter and the mining train. Private trip from €388 per person, minimum 2.
- **Gibraltar** (2 h 15): you cross the border on foot and the guide picks you up there to go up the Rock. Private trip with tickets: €860 for a group of 2, €1,040 for 4. Bring your passport or ID card depending on your nationality.
- **Tangier** (Morocco): only on an organised tour, ferry included; it's about 15 hours.

## Nature near Seville

- **Doñana:** the national park is visited with a guide in an authorised vehicle, on a full-day trip.
- **Cerro del Hierro** (Sierra Norte): old mines turned into a rock labyrinth, ideal for hiking.
- **Aracena and the dehesa:** free-range Iberian pigs and ham.
- **Itálica**, 15 minutes away, is the shortest trip: open-air Roman ruins in half a morning. In a group with the monastery, from €42.

## Private day trips from Seville or group: when it pays off

A group tour is always cheaper for 1 or 2 people. Private trips are priced per group (or with a minimum number of people), so **the price per person drops a lot from 4 people**:

What you get with private: **pick-up from your accommodation**, departure at the time you choose, stops at your pace and nobody else in the vehicle. For families with children, older travellers or groups of friends it usually pays off; for a couple on a tight budget, the group tour or the train on your own.

## Tips for choosing

1. **One trip per day.** Córdoba and Cádiz can be combined with a free afternoon in Seville; Ronda, Mérida or Gibraltar take the full day.
2. **In summer, leave early.** At midday in July and August Córdoba and the countryside easily go above 40 °C.
3. **Book key tickets.** The Mezquita-Cathedral and, in high season, the Jerez wineries fill up.
4. **If there are 4 or more of you, compare private.** Work out the price per person before deciding: the gap with the coach may be smaller than it looks.
5. **With children,** Itálica, Doñana or Cádiz (with a beach in the centre), and no more than 2 hours on the road each way.
