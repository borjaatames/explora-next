## Sunset from the rooftops

This is the one we recommend for a special occasion. A guide just for you takes you up to **two rooftops** in the centre, the second being the **Setas viewpoint** (Metropol Parasol), just as the Giralda and the domes light up, and tells you legends that only make sense from up there. Setas entry and **a drink at the end** are included.

- **Price:** €192 per person, minimum 2 (groups of 2 to 6).
- **Start time:** 5 pm in winter, 5.30 pm in autumn, 6.30 pm in spring and 8 pm in summer.
- Meet at Calle Adriano 33, El Arenal. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## A carriage ride, but with a guide

The carriages waiting by the cathedral are a Seville classic. Ours is a **private hour-and-a-half ride** with a guide specialising in Andalusian history who explains what you pass, with a soft drink and, at the end, a **traditional local wine**. It costs **€298 per carriage** (up to 4 people) and leaves from Calle Adriano, by the bullring. When booking, enter 1 as the number of people: the operator asks for this because the price is per carriage.

## Flamenco for two

For a couple, a small room works better than a big dinner-show venue:

- **Casa de la Memoria:** an intimate one-hour show in the centre, €24 per person.
- **Tablao El Arenal:** fifty years of flamenco next to the bullring, from €45 per person.

To compare every venue, see our guide to [flamenco in Seville](/en/guides/sevilla/flamenco-seville-which-tablao-to-choose).

## Cook together

The **Andalusian cooking class** is a five-hour private experience: you are picked up at your accommodation, shop at a traditional food market and cook century-old recipes in a traditional house with a cook who has more than 45 years in the trade. Then you eat what you have made. Sessions at 11 am (with lunch) or 5.30 pm (with dinner). It costs €398 per person; mention any food intolerance when booking.

## Lower-budget plans

- **Mysterious Seville at sunset:** a private 90-minute walk through the legends, crimes and mysteries of the old town, from the steps of the Setas. €125 per group of 1 to 4, so €62.50 each for a couple.
- **Guadalquivir cruise:** 60 to 90 minutes from the Muelle de Turismo, with a live guide and one drink (soft drink, beer, wine or water), €25 per person.
- **Setas viewpoint on your own:** €16 per person, with the ticket valid for two days from first use.

## A perfect evening for two

1. **Late afternoon:** a stroll through María Luisa Park and Plaza de España (free), or the carriage ride.
2. **Sunset:** rooftops and the Setas with a cocktail, or the viewpoint on your own.
3. **Afterwards:** flamenco at Casa de la Memoria.
4. **Dinner:** tapas in Santa Cruz or across the river in Triana; ideas in [Seville tapas with criteria](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-tapas-with-criteria).

## Practical tips

- **Book the sunset ahead:** the rooftop tour is private (2 to 6 people) and depends on guide availability, especially in spring.
- **Summer:** in July and August the heat lasts well into the evening; save the carriage ride and walks for after 8 pm.
- **Proposals:** the rooftops and the carriage ride are private, with no strangers around. If you have something planned, tell the operator when you book.
- **Cancellation:** all of these plans offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking flamenco at sunset time:** in spring and summer the sun sets late. Do flamenco afterwards.
- **Taking a street carriage without agreeing the price and duration** before you get in.
- **Leaving the cooking class for your last day:** it takes five hours with pick-up, so it fits better on day one or two.
