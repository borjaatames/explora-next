## A sunset rooftop in Seville

This is the one for a special occasion. A guide just for you takes you up to **two rooftops** in the centre; the second is the **Setas viewpoint** (Metropol Parasol), just as the Giralda and the domes light up. Along the way you hear legends that only make sense from up on the roofs.

- **Price:** €192 per person, minimum 2 (groups of 2 to 6). Setas entry and **a drink at the end** are included.
- **Duration:** 2 h 30 min.
- **Start time:** 5 pm in winter, 5.30 pm in autumn, 6.30 pm in spring and 8 pm in summer.
- **Meeting point:** Calle Adriano 33, El Arenal.

As it is private and depends on the guide being free, book ahead, especially in spring.

## Seville horse carriage ride: price and how to choose

The carriages waiting by the cathedral are a classic of romantic Seville. If you hire one in the street, **ask the price and the length of the ride before you get in**: each driver has his own fares and routes.

The alternative is a **private 90-minute ride with a guide** specialising in Andalusian history, who explains what you pass:

- **Price:** €298 per carriage, up to 4 people, with a soft drink and a **traditional local wine** at the end.
- **Departure:** Calle Adriano, by the Maestranza bullring.
- When booking, enter 1 as the number of people: the price is per carriage.

In July and August the heat lasts until late; ride after 8 pm.

## Flamenco for two

For a couple, a small room works better than a big dinner-show venue:

- **Casa de la Memoria:** an intimate one-hour show in the centre, **€24** per person.
- **Tablao El Arenal:** fifty years of flamenco next to the Maestranza, **from €45** per person.

Watch the timing: in spring and summer the sun sets late, so book flamenco after sunset. To compare every venue one by one, see our guide to [flamenco in Seville](/en/guides/sevilla/flamenco-seville-which-tablao-to-choose).

## Cook together

The **Andalusian cooking class** is a five-hour private experience:

1. You are picked up at your accommodation.
2. You shop at a traditional food market.
3. You cook century-old recipes in a traditional house with a cook who has more than 45 years in the trade.
4. You eat what you have made for lunch or dinner.

Sessions at **11 am** (with lunch) and **5.30 pm** (with dinner). It costs **€398 per person** (minimum 2); mention any food intolerance when booking. As it takes five hours, it fits better on your first or second day than on the last.

## Seville for couples on a budget

- **Plaza de España and María Luisa Park:** free, and among the loveliest spots in the city in the late afternoon.
- **Setas viewpoint on your own:** €16 per person, with the ticket valid for two days from first use.
- **Guadalquivir cruise:** 60 to 90 minutes from the Muelle de Turismo, with a live guide and one drink (soft drink, beer, wine or water), €25 per person.
- **Mysterious Seville at sunset:** a private 90-minute walk through the legends, crimes and mysteries of the old town, from the steps of the Setas. €125 per group (1 to 4 people), €62.50 each for a couple.

Viewpoint, cruise and flamenco at Casa de la Memoria add up to about **€65 per person** for a full evening.

## A perfect evening in romantic Seville

1. **Late afternoon:** a walk through María Luisa Park and Plaza de España, or the carriage ride.
2. **Sunset:** rooftops and the Setas with a cocktail, or the viewpoint on your own.
3. **Afterwards:** flamenco at Casa de la Memoria.
4. **Dinner:** tapas in Santa Cruz or across the river in Triana; ideas in [where to eat tapas in Seville](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-tapas-with-criteria).

If you are planning a **proposal**, the rooftops and the carriage ride are private, with no strangers around: tell the operator when booking. All these plans have **free cancellation up to 24 hours before**.
