The key to this one week in Andalusia is the order: west to east, sleeping in each city instead of doing out-and-back day trips, and with the two critical tickets bought before you leave home.

| Day | City | Plan | Where you sleep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Seville | Alcázar, Cathedral, Santa Cruz, Plaza de España | Seville |
| 2 | Seville | Triana, the river and flamenco | Seville |
| 3 | Córdoba | Jewish Quarter, patios, Roman bridge | Córdoba |
| 4 | Córdoba → Granada | Mezquita first thing, train, Albaicín | Granada |
| 5 | Granada | Alhambra and zambra in the Sacromonte | Granada |
| 6 | Granada → Málaga | Alcazaba and La Malagueta | Málaga |
| 7 | Ronda | Day trip and back to Málaga | Málaga |

## Day 1: monumental Seville

**Morning.** **Real Alcázar** at 9:30, the first slot (open 9:30-17:00 from October to March and until 19:00 from April to September): 2 h 30 with the gardens. Opposite, the **Cathedral and the Giralda** (1 h 30), open Monday to Saturday 10:45-19:00 and on Sundays from 14:30. If you'd like both monuments explained without queuing at all, with a guide just for you:

**Lunch** in El Arenal, five minutes away, rather than at the terraces facing the Cathedral. **Afternoon:** the Santa Cruz quarter and, 20 minutes' walk through the Murillo Gardens, **Plaza de España** as the sun goes down. **Evening:** intimate flamenco at Casa de la Memoria (one hour, from €24).

**Where to stay in Seville:** **El Arenal** (by the Cathedral, quieter and a bit cheaper), **Santa Cruz** (the postcard, more touristy) or **Triana** (more local, 15 minutes from the centre).

## Day 2: Triana and the river

Cross the Isabel II bridge to **Triana**: the market, Calle Betis and tapas on Calle Pureza. In the afternoon, **Casa de Pilatos** (€12) or the **Fine Arts Museum** (free for EU citizens), and sunset by the river. The hour-by-hour plan is in [Seville in 2 days](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-in-2-days).

## Day 3: Córdoba

**Morning.** AVE at around 9:00, in Córdoba at 9:45; 20-25 minutes' walk to the old town. Start with the **Alcázar de los Reyes Cristianos** (€7; gardens only for now, the rest is under restoration) and lose yourself in the **Jewish Quarter**: Calleja de las Flores, the Synagogue and Plaza del Potro.

**Lunch:** salmorejo and flamenquín in a tavern in the Jewish Quarter or San Basilio, away from the streets around the Mezquita.

**Afternoon:** the patios of the **Palacio de Viana** (€8.50 patios only) and, at dusk, the **Roman bridge** with the Mezquita lit up behind it.

**Where to stay:** in the **Jewish Quarter** or **San Basilio**, five minutes from the Mezquita. That's what makes the day 4 trick possible.

## Day 4: the Mezquita and the train to Granada

**8:30, Mezquita-Cathedral.** Monday to Saturday, from 8:30 to 9:30, entry is **free** for individual visitors, with no groups: the best hour of the day, and free. If you'd rather have the full visit with explanations, from 10:00 with a €15 ticket or with a guide.

**Midday:** train to Granada (1 h 30). **Afternoon:** drop your bags, walk down the **Carrera del Darro** and go up to the **Albaicín** on the C32 minibus from Plaza Nueva (€1.60) or on foot via the Cuesta del Chapiz (25 minutes). Sunset at the **Mirador de San Nicolás** and free tapas with every beer on Calle Navas.

## Day 5: the Alhambra

**8:30, Alhambra.** The whole day revolves around your Nasrid Palaces time: there's only **a 30-minute window** and it's a 30-45 minute walk from the entrance to the palaces. The Alcazaba, Nasrid Palaces and Generalife add up to about 4 hours. If there are no tickets left, the [guided tour with entry included](/en/cities/granada/activities/alhambra-generalife-guided-tour) (from €49) has its own quota.

**Afternoon:** the Royal Chapel (€7) with the tombs of the Catholic Monarchs, and the Cathedral. **Evening:** zambra in a Sacromonte cave, €22-30 with a drink. The details are in [Granada in 1 day](/en/guides/granada/granada-in-1-day).

**Where to stay in Granada:** the **centre between Plaza Nueva and Gran Vía**, the **Realejo** (more local) or the **lower Albaicín** (Alhambra views, steep streets and high prices). Avoid the station area and Camino de Ronda.

## Day 6: Granada → Málaga

A relaxed morning in Granada (the Realejo or the Cartuja Monastery, €7) and the **direct Avant to Málaga** after lunch: 1 h 18. **Afternoon:** **Alcazaba and Gibralfaro** (€10 combined ticket), the Roman Theatre from outside and the Cathedral (€10 with audio guide). **Evening:** sardine espetos in La Malagueta or Pedregalejo.

**Where to stay in Málaga:** the **historic centre** or **Soho**, 15 minutes' walk from María Zambrano station.

## Day 7: Ronda

Bus from Málaga station (1 h 45-2 h, about eight a day; fewer direct ones at weekends) or a tour, such as [Ronda and Setenil de las Bodegas](/en/cities/malaga/activities/ronda-setenil-day-trip-from-malaga) (from €34). The Puente Nuevo over the gorge, the bullring, the old town (La Ciudad) and the walk down to the viewpoints; back to Málaga in the afternoon. All the options are in [Ronda from Málaga](/en/guides/malaga/ronda-from-malaga).

Málaga airport is 12 minutes from the centre on the C-1 commuter train (€1.80): you can fly out on the evening of day 7 or the morning of day 8.

**Car variant:** rent in Granada on day 6, drive to Ronda (about 2 hours), sleep there (at night, without the day-trippers, it's a different town) and on day 7 drive down to Málaga through the mountains (1 h 45) to return the car at the airport.

## Seville, Córdoba, Granada by train: journeys and prices

| Journey | Train | Indicative price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seville → Córdoba | AVE or Avant, about 45 min, over 25 departures a day | from about €12 booked ahead; €30-40 last minute |
| Córdoba → Granada | Direct, 1 h 30, few departures a day | from about €23 |
| Granada → Málaga | Direct Avant, 1 h 18 | from about €18 |
| Málaga → Ronda | No direct train; bus, 1 h 45 to 2 h | from about €10-12 |

Buy trains on the Renfe website as soon as you have dates: low fares disappear as the train fills up, and **Córdoba-Granada has few departures**, so plan day 4 around the timetable. Between Granada and Málaga there's also an **Alsa bus** (1 h 45, about €13, over 20 a day). The stations in Córdoba, Granada and Málaga are 15-25 minutes' walk from the centre.

## What to book, and how far ahead

- **Alhambra (€22.27):** 3-4 weeks; up to 3 months in high season.
- **Real Alcázar of Seville (€15.50):** 1-2 weeks; the 9:30 slot goes fast.
- **Cathedral and Giralda (€13 online):** a few days.
- **Mezquita-Cathedral (€15):** a few days; the free slot can't be booked.
- **Zambra or tablao (€22-30):** 2-3 days; weekends fill up.

**Indicative budget:** about **€800-1,250 per person** for the 7 days, with mid-range accommodation in a double room (€50-90 a night). Granada makes food much cheaper: the tapa comes free with your drink.

## Tips for Andalusia without a car

- **With children:** in Córdoba prioritise the Alcázar de los Reyes Cristianos and its gardens, and in Granada take the minibus up to the Alhambra. Under-12s go free at the Alhambra but **need a named ticket**. More ideas in [Seville with kids](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-with-kids) and [Granada with kids](/en/guides/granada/granada-with-kids).
- **June to September:** be out until 13:00, indoors or siesta from 14:00 to 18:00, and back out at 19:00.
- **Don't buy the Alhambra when you arrive:** in high season there are none left.
- **Don't do Córdoba as a day trip from Granada:** 4 hours on the road in one day; from Seville it's 45 minutes.
- **Don't rent a car for the whole route:** you'll pay €25-30 a day in city parking to use it for two days.
- **Don't try to fit Cádiz, Ronda and the white villages into the same week.** If you have a spare day, Cádiz and Jerez from Seville, in [day trips from Seville](/en/guides/sevilla/day-trips-from-seville); with ten days, see the [10-day Spain itinerary](/en/guides/madrid/spain-itinerary-10-days).
