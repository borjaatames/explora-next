## Seville hen party fines: the rules

Seville's bylaw on conduct in public spaces was amended in early 2024 and lets the local police fine anyone **up to €750** for being in the street:

- naked or in just underwear;
- wearing clothing or accessories depicting genitals;
- with sex-themed dolls or objects.

A group T-shirt, a sash or a flower in your hair are no problem at all.

## Hen do activities in Seville: flamenco and tapas in Triana

The most complete afternoon-to-evening plan. It starts in **Plaza del Altozano**, in Triana, with a **professional flamenco show**, then a food guide takes you round **two or three tapas bars** chosen on the day. Tapas and **four drinks per person** are included, alcoholic or not.

- **Duration:** about 4 hours.
- **Price per group:** €310 for 1 or 2, €540 for 4 and **€720 for 6** (€120 per person).
- **Departures:** 18:30 and 19:00, with free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## A tuk tuk to start the afternoon

For a photo-filled start: a [private electric tuk tuk](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/private-tuk-tuk-tour-seville), **up to 4 people**, round Plaza de España, María Luisa Park, the Maestranza and Triana, with stops.

- **1 hour:** €89 for 2 and €99 for up to 4. For 6 people you need two vehicles (€188).
- **2 hours:** €155 for 2 and €165 for up to 4; adds the Torre del Oro, La Cartuja and La Macarena.
- It leaves from the **Saba car park at Plaza de Armas**, with no hotel pick-up, and you see the monuments from outside.

## Seville stag party ideas by day: the river

- **Kayak (€17 per person, 2 hours):** along the Guadalquivir past the Torre del Oro and the Triana bridge, with an instructor, from the Triana canoe club. **Everyone must be able to swim** and it can be cancelled in bad weather. In summer, go first thing or at sunset.
- **Guided cruise (€25 per person, 1-1.5 hours):** from the Muelle de Turismo, next to the Torre del Oro, with one drink included (soft drink, beer, wine or water). A relaxed, cheap plan.

## Flamenco on your own

If you skip the private tour, a one-hour show costs **€24-25 per person**:

- **Teatro Flamenco Triana** (€25), in the neighbourhood where much of flamenco was born.
- **Casa de la Memoria** (€24), an intimate venue in the centre.

## A quieter hen party

The [private visit to a haute couture flamenco dress atelier](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/seville-haute-couture-flamenco-dress-atelier-private-visit) (at 10:00, 2 hours, 2 to 6 people, **€214 per person**) shows how a dress is made from sketch to embroidery, with its designers, and you can try on real dresses.

It pairs well with the **private tapas tour in Triana** at 13:00 (3 hours, €211 per person, 2 to 6): four bars, four tapas and four drinks per person, including the Triana Market.

## Practical tips

- **Book ahead:** two or three weeks in spring. Private tours have a cap (6 on the Triana tapas tour, 4 per tuk tuk): for big groups, book several at the same time.
- **Heat:** from June to September, keep outdoor activities for first thing or sunset.
- **Seville hours:** tapas happen at midday and late afternoon. That's why the private tapas tour runs at 13:00 and 19:30, and the flamenco and tapas tour leaves at 18:30 or 19:00.
- **Mixed or non-drinking groups:** drinks on the Triana tour can be alcohol-free, and the tuk tuk, kayak and dress atelier aren't about drinking.
- **Common mistakes:** walking round the centre with sex-themed joke accessories (up to €750 fine), booking the kayak without checking everyone can swim, and squeezing 8 people into one tuk tuk (it takes 4).
- **Pub crawls and party boats:** we don't offer them in Seville; for the night, go on to the flamenco and tapas tour.

Prices are those of each activity on 1 November 2026 and may vary by date.
