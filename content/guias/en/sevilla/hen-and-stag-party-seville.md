Seville gets a lot of hen and stag parties, and the city council knows it: since 2024 it has fined certain excesses in the street. What works best here is not an all-nighter but a long afternoon and evening of tuk tuk, river, tapas and flamenco. This is what we sell that fits, with real prices. We do not offer a pub crawl or party boat in Seville, so you will not find one here.

## The rules: what Seville fines

The public-space civic ordinance was amended in early 2024 and lets local police fine anyone **up to €750** for being in the street:

- naked or in underwear only;
- in clothing or accessories depicting genitals;
- with sexual-themed dolls or objects.

Matching T-shirts, a sash or a flower in your hair are absolutely fine.

## The full plan: private flamenco and tapas in Triana

It starts in Plaza del Altozano, Triana, with a **professional flamenco show** and carries on with a food guide through **two or three tapas bars** chosen on the day. Tapas and **four drinks per person** (alcoholic or not) are included. It is just your group and priced per group: **€310 for 1 or 2, €540 for 4 and €720 for 6** (€120 each). Departures at 6.30 pm and 7 pm, with free cancellation up to 24 hours before.

## To start the afternoon: private tuk tuk

An electric tuk tuk just for you, **up to 4 people**, around Plaza de España, María Luisa Park, the Maestranza bullring and Triana, with photo stops. The one-hour version costs €89 for 2 and €99 for 4; six people need two vehicles (€188). The two-hour version adds the Torre del Oro, La Cartuja and La Macarena. It leaves from the Saba car park at Plaza de Armas, with no hotel pick-up, and you see the monuments from outside.

## Daytime on the river

- **Kayaking:** two hours on the Guadalquivir past the Torre del Oro and Triana Bridge, with an instructor, from the Triana canoe club. **You must be able to swim**, and it can be cancelled in bad weather. In summer, go early or at sunset.
- **Guided cruise:** one to one and a half hours from the Muelle de Turismo by the Torre del Oro, with one drink included (soft drink, beer, wine or water).

## Flamenco on your own

If you skip the private tour, a one-hour show costs **€24 to €25 per person**: **Teatro Flamenco Triana**, in the neighbourhood that shaped so much of flamenco, or **Casa de la Memoria**, an intimate space in the centre. ## A quieter hen do

The **private visit to a haute couture flamenco dress workshop** (10 am, 2 to 6 people) shows how a dress goes from sketch to embroidery, with the designers themselves, and you can try on real flamenco dresses. Pair it with the **private Triana tapas tour** at 1 pm: four bars, four tapas and four drinks each, plus Triana Market.

## Practical tips

- **Booking ahead:** two or three weeks in spring. Private tours are capped (6 on the Triana tapas tour, 4 per tuk tuk): for bigger groups, book several at the same time.
- **Heat:** from June to September, keep outdoor plans for early morning or sunset.
- **Seville hours:** tapas happen at lunchtime and in the late evening. That is why the private tapas tour runs at 1 pm and 7.30 pm, and the flamenco and tapas tour at 6.30 pm or 7 pm.
- **Mixed or alcohol-free groups:** the Triana tour drinks can be soft drinks, and the tuk tuk, kayak and dress workshop do not revolve around drinking.

## Common mistakes

- **Walking around the centre with sexual-themed novelty accessories:** that is up to €750 in fines.
- **Booking the kayak without checking everyone can swim.**
- **Trying to fit 8 people into one tuk tuk:** it takes 4, so book two.
