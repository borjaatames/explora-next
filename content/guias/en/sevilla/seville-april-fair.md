## Seville April Fair 2027: dates and calendar

| Day | Date | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 12 April | **Pescaíto** night: fried fish dinner in the casetas and, at midnight, the **alumbrado**, the lighting-up of the gateway and thousands of lanterns |
| Tuesday to Saturday | 13-17 April | Fair by day (horses and carriages) and fair by night (dancing and sevillanas until the early hours) |
| Sunday | 18 April | Last day; at midnight, **fireworks** over the river and close |

The Fair starts two weeks after Easter Sunday, which in 2027 falls on 28 March. The City Council confirms the dates when it presents the official poster; we'll update them then.

## Seville fair public casetas: how to get in without knowing anyone

The fairground has over a thousand casetas, and almost all belong to families, clubs, companies or associations: no invitation, no entry. These **are open to everyone**:

- **District casetas:** free entry, sevillanas and rumba music, closing around 3:00. The best plan if you're on your own.
- **Municipal caseta (Caseta de Sevilla):** with a terrace and indoor area, performances from 16:00 to 3:00 and tables you can book at the tourist office.
- **With locals:** if you know someone, ask them to take you; it's very common to be invited into their friends' casetas.

In the casetas you pay for what you have, and some use vouchers: **bring cash**.

## Fair by day, fair by night and the lighting-up

- **By day (14:00 to 19:00):** the most photogenic time. Horses and carriages parade, and the casetas serve food. A horse carriage costs about €95 an hour (2025 rate).
- **By night (from 20:00):** once the horses leave and the albero is cleaned, the dancing and sevillanas start and go on until the early hours.
- **The alumbrado:** at midnight on Monday the gateway and lanterns are switched on; it's the official start.

If you can only go one day, choose a **Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon**: there's atmosphere and slightly fewer people than at the weekend.

## What to drink, eat and wear

- **Drinks:** fino and manzanilla, and above all **rebujito**, manzanilla with lime soda and lots of ice, ordered by the jug.
- **Food:** fried fish, ham, Spanish omelette and montaditos.
- **Clothes:** you don't have to dress in flamenco style. Women usually wear a flamenco dress and men a suit and tie. If you don't have one, smart clothes and **comfortable shoes**: the ground is albero and you walk a lot.

## Getting there

- **Metro line 1:** Plaza de Cuba, Parque de los Príncipes and Blas Infante stops, running non-stop during the Fair.
- **Tussam shuttle buses:** from Prado de San Sebastián, €1.60 a ride (2025 price).
- **Car:** the area around the fairground is closed to traffic and parking is impossible. Taxis and ride-hailing have special fares.

## Things to do at the Seville April Fair beyond the casetas

- **Calle del Infierno:** the funfair next to the fairground, with a Ferris wheel, roller coasters and raffle stalls. A safe bet with children.
- **Bullfights at La Maestranza:** the Fair is the bullring's main season. The cheapest seats, high up in the sun, were around €31 in 2025. If you don't want to see a bullfight, the guided tour of the bullring and its museum is the alternative.
- **Guided tour of the Fair:** the City Council runs one-hour tours (€16 in 2025) from the kiosk at the gateway.

**Common mistakes:**

- **Going on Saturday night with no plan:** it's the busiest day and the public casetas fill up. Arrive before 22:00.
- **Expecting to find a hotel in March:** the Fair is booked months ahead.
- **Wearing heels or new shoes:** the albero ruins them in an afternoon.
- **Taking photos inside a private caseta without asking:** it's a family party, not a stage set.
