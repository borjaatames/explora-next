A day in Seville from Madrid by AVE is **perfectly doable**: 2h 30min by train, a historic centre you cover on foot, and four essentials done well. The trick is **giving up on seeing everything and choosing well**. This isn't a compressed [Seville in 3 days](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-in-3-days); it's a different plan for someone with 10–11 real hours in the city.

Alcázar, Cathedral, Plaza de España and the river all sit within about 2 km², so you walk between them with no transport.

## The train

- **Out:** the first AVE from Madrid Atocha, around **6:55–7 AM**, arrives at Sevilla Santa Justa at **9:30 AM**. If you arrive at 11 AM you lose 1.5–2 hours, and a quality day in Seville with them.
- **Back:** the AVE around **9:30 PM** (Madrid around midnight) or, with less risk, the **7:30 PM** (Madrid by 10 PM).
- **Price:** return from **€55–60** booked 2–3 weeks ahead; bought on the day, **€130–160**.
- **Operators:** Renfe AVE, Iryo and Ouigo; Iryo and Ouigo tend to be cheaper.
- **Santa Justa is 1.5 km from the centre:** 20 minutes on foot or about €5 by taxi. Leave luggage in the station's left-luggage office.

## The day plan

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 9:30 AM | Arrive Santa Justa, left luggage, taxi to the centre |
| 10 AM | **Royal Alcázar** (online ticket, 10 AM slot) |
| 12:30 PM | **Cathedral and Giralda**, right opposite |
| 2 PM | Lunch on Mateos Gago or Plaza de Doña Elvira (not Plaza Virgen de los Reyes) |
| 3:30 PM | **Plaza de España** and Parque de María Luisa |
| 5:30 PM | **Triana**: the bridge, the market, Calle Betis |

| 8:30 PM | Quick tapas in Triana |
| 9 PM | Taxi to Santa Justa for the 9:30 PM AVE |

## The four essentials

### 10 AM – noon: Royal Alcázar

The oldest royal palace still in use in Europe, and far less crowded at opening than at midday.

- **Online ticket essential:** a timed ticket from **€20** (as of 2026). Without one you queue 30–45 minutes or get turned away, because it sells out in high season. **Book at least 2 weeks ahead.**
- **Don't miss:** Patio de las Doncellas, the Hall of Ambassadors and **the gardens**, which half of visitors skip.
- **Skip the Cuarto Real Alto** today: an extra 25 minutes and €5.50 that don't pay off on a tight day.

More in [how to visit the Alcázar of Seville](/en/guides/sevilla/alcazar-of-seville-how-to-visit).

### 12:30–2 PM: Cathedral and Giralda

- **The largest Gothic cathedral in the world**, with Christopher Columbus's tomb.
- **Patio de los Naranjos:** what remains of the old mosque.
- **Climb the Giralda by ramps, not stairs** (they were built for going up on horseback): the best view of the centre.

Allow 1.5–2 hours. The **combined Cathedral + Giralda + Iglesia del Salvador ticket** is **€20 online or €21 at the gate** (as of 2026); free for under-13s with an adult. Buy online: the midday queue can reach 30 minutes.

### 3:30–5 PM: Plaza de España

A 15-minute walk from the Cathedral through Parque de María Luisa. Built for the 1929 Ibero-American Exposition, it has a tiled bench for every Spanish province: find yours for the photo. **Free**; a rowing boat on the canal costs around €6. Come in the afternoon: at midday in summer it hits 42°C / 108°F with no shade.

### 5:30–8:30 PM: Triana at sunset

- **Cross the Triana Bridge** and look back at the old town.
- **Mercado de Triana:** tapas at the market counters; good if you need to eat fast.
- **Castillo de San Jorge** (free): under the market, the former seat of the Inquisition.
- **Calle Betis:** the riverside street, with bars and terraces facing the old town as its lights come on. The best close to the day.

## What you can skip today

- **Barrio de Santa Cruz:** pretty but heavily touristed; you cross a slice of it walking between the Alcázar and the Cathedral, and that's enough.
- **Torre del Oro** (€3) and **Las Setas** (€15): viewpoints that don't pay off today; the Giralda already gives you the view, and Las Setas costs you 30 minutes' round trip.
- **Casa de Pilatos** (€12, a solid hour) and the **Museum of Fine Arts**: save them for a second visit.
- **Flamenco:** the good shows are at night, and at 9 PM you're heading for the train.

## Eating on an express visit

You have about 1.5 hours. Leave the Cathedral and walk **5 minutes to Calle Mateos Gago, Calle Águilas or Plaza de Doña Elvira**: 4–5 tapas and two beers in 45 minutes for **€20–25**. Order salmorejo, pringá, spinach with chickpeas and jamón; for standing tapas, the bars on Mateos Gago.

Avoid:

- **Menus in eight languages on Plaza Virgen de los Reyes:** inflated prices, weak quality.
- **"Mixed tapas + sangria + paella":** tapas are ordered individually, the sangria is cheap wine with lemonade and paella is Valencian.
- **Anywhere with someone at the door pulling you in.**

## Common mistakes

1. **No online Alcázar ticket.** In high season you can be locked out.
2. **Staying on the old-town side** and never crossing to Triana.
3. **Reaching Santa Justa five minutes before departure.** Arrive 30–45 minutes early.
4. **Buying souvenirs next to the Cathedral.** Real ceramics are sold in Triana's workshops.

## If you miss the last train

- **Last-minute hotel:** €60–100, back to Madrid the next morning.
- **Night bus:** Alsa runs Seville–Madrid around 11 PM, arriving around 6 AM.
- **One-way rental car:** €100–150 with a Madrid drop-off, and about 6 hours of night driving.

If the day looks tight, aim for the 7:30 PM AVE: two hours less in Seville, but no risk of being stranded. When you come back, [Seville in 3 days](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-in-3-days) has the full version.
