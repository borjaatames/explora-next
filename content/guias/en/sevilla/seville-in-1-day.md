## The day plan: what to see in Seville in a day

| Time | What |
| --- | --- |
| 9:30 | Arrival, left luggage, taxi to the centre |
| 10:00 | **Alcázar** (online ticket, 10:00 slot) |
| 12:30 | **Cathedral and Giralda**, right opposite |
| 14:00 | Lunch on Mateos Gago or Doña Elvira (not on Virgen de los Reyes) |
| 15:30 | **Plaza de España** and María Luisa Park |
| 17:30 | **Triana**: bridge, market, Calle Betis |
| 19:30 | Sunset on Betis as the old town lights up |
| 21:00 | Taxi to Santa Justa, 21:30 AVE |

### 1. Real Alcázar (10:00-12:00)

The oldest royal palace still in use in Europe and the star monument. **An online ticket is essential** (€15.50 general admission at alcazarsevilla.org): without one, 30-45 minutes of queuing or being turned away, because it sells out in high season. Don't miss the Patio de las Doncellas, the Hall of the Ambassadors and **the gardens**, which half of visitors skip. Skip the Cuarto Real Alto: 25 minutes and €6 extra that aren't worth it today. More in [how to visit the Alcázar](/en/guides/sevilla/alcazar-of-seville-how-to-visit).

### 2. Cathedral and Giralda (12:30-14:00)

The largest Gothic cathedral in the world, Columbus's tomb, the Patio de los Naranjos from the old mosque and the climb up the Giralda **by ramp** (it was built to be climbed on horseback). Combined ticket with the Church of El Salvador: **€13 online or €14 at the ticket office**, free for under-13s. Buy online: the midday queue reaches half an hour.

### 3. Plaza de España (15:30-17:00)

15 minutes from the Cathedral through María Luisa Park. It was built for the 1929 Exhibition and has a tiled bench for each Spanish province: find yours. Free; a rowing boat on the canal costs about €6. Go **in the afternoon**: at midday in summer there's no shade.

### 4. Triana at sunset (17:30-20:30)

The authentic Seville that almost nobody sees in a day. Cross the bridge and look back; have tapas at the counters of the **Triana Market**; go down to the **Castillo de San Jorge** (free, under the market, the former seat of the Inquisition) and finish on **Calle Betis** as the lights of the centre come on across the river.

## AVE Madrid-Seville: times and prices

- **Outbound:** the first AVE from Atocha, around **7:00**; you reach Santa Justa at **9:30**. If you arrive at 11:00, forget doing Seville properly in a day.
- **Return:** the **21:30** (in Madrid at midnight) or, with less risk, the **19:30**.
- **Price:** return from **€55-60** booked 2-3 weeks ahead; the day before, €130-160. Iryo and Ouigo are usually cheaper than Renfe.
- **Santa Justa is 1.5 km from the centre:** 20 minutes on foot or €5 by taxi. If you have a suitcase, use the station's left luggage.

## What you can skip today

- **Santa Cruz:** pretty but touristy; you walk through it between the Alcázar and the Cathedral, and that's enough.
- **Torre del Oro** (€3) and **the Setas** (€16): viewpoints that aren't worth it today; the Giralda already gives you the view.
- **Casa de Pilatos** (€12, 1 hour) and the **Fine Arts Museum**: for your next visit.
- **Flamenco:** the good shows are at night and at 21:00 you're on the train.

## Where to eat on a Seville day trip

You have 1 h 30. Leave the Cathedral and walk 5 minutes to **Mateos Gago, Águilas or Plaza de Doña Elvira**: 4-5 tapas and two small beers in 45 minutes for **€20-25**. Salmorejo, pringá, spinach with chickpeas, ham.

Avoid the eight-language menus on Plaza Virgen de los Reyes, the "tapas + sangria + paella" signs (paella is from Valencia, and that sangria is bad wine with lemonade) and anywhere with someone touting at the door.

## Common mistakes and plan B

1. **Turning up without an Alcázar ticket:** book two weeks ahead.
2. **Not catching the first AVE:** you lose 2 hours of daylight.
3. **Staying in the centre** and never crossing to Triana.
4. **Plaza de España at midday in summer.**
5. **Trying to see everything** and seeing nothing properly.
6. **Reaching Santa Justa five minutes before departure:** allow 30-45 minutes.
7. **Buying souvenirs by the Cathedral:** the real ceramics are in Triana.

**If you miss the last train:** a last-minute hotel (€60-100), the Alsa night bus around 23:00 (arriving in Madrid around 6:00) or a one-way rental car (€100-150). If the day is tight, aim for the 19:30 AVE: two fewer hours in Seville and zero risk. And when you come back with more time, there's [Seville in 3 days](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-in-3-days).
