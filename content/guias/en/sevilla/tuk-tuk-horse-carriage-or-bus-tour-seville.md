## Tuk tuk or horse carriage in Seville: which to choose depending on who's travelling

- **Couple on a tight budget:** the hop-on hop-off bus (€28 each) or, better still, on foot: the small-group walking tour costs €25. Central Seville is flat and walkable.
- **Family of 4:** a 1-hour tuk tuk, €99 for all four. No walking in the heat, and commentary from the driver.
- **Group of 6:** two tuk tuks (€188, €31.33 per person) or the bus if you'll use it all day.
- **First time in the city:** the 2-hour tuk tuk, which adds the Torre del Oro, the Cartuja, the Macarena and the city walls to the short route.
- **Special occasion (anniversary, proposal, parents):** the private horse carriage with a guide.

**The break-even point:** with 4 people, the 1-hour tuk tuk (€24.75) is cheaper than the bus (€28). With 2, the bus wins (€28 versus €44.50). With 5 or 6 you need two tuk tuks and the bus is cheaper again. The private horse carriage doesn't compete on price: even full, it works out at €74.50 per person.

## Private tuk tuk: what it's like

- **Pros:** just your group, electric, with a **local driver-guide** who explains things and stops for photos, plus covers and blankets if it rains or turns cool (on the 2-hour tour).
- **Cons:** up to 4 people; the short route focuses on Plaza de España, María Luisa Park, the Maestranza and Triana.
- **Departure:** Saba car park at Plaza de Armas.

## Horse and carriage ride in Seville: private or from the stand

- **Pros:** Seville's classic image. The [private ride](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/seville-private-horse-carriage-ride-guide) comes with a coachman and a guide specialising in Andalusian history, a soft drink and, at the end, a local wine. It leaves from Calle Adriano, in El Arenal.
- **Cons:** the price, and some people prefer not to ride out of concern for the horses' welfare, especially in the hottest hours.
- **Street-stand carriages:** they wait by the Cathedral or at Plaza de España, have no guide (just a coachman) and charge fares regulated by the City Council. **Agree the price and length before you get in.**

## Is the Seville hop-on hop-off bus worth it?

If you make the most of it, yes. For **€28** you get **24 hours** to hop on and off as often as you like, an audio guide in more than 15 languages, **two walking tours** (Triana and the old town) and entry to the Church of El Salvador and the Macarena Museum. It's wheelchair accessible and runs every 30-40 minutes.

Against it: the audio guide is recorded and the 75-minute loop follows the main avenues, without entering the Santa Cruz quarter. It's the best option for solo travellers, couples, older people or those with reduced mobility, and anyone who wants to get around all day. For a single loop without getting off, a tuk tuk shared by 4 is better value.

## Common mistakes

1. **Getting into a street-stand carriage without agreeing price and length.**
2. **Booking a tuk tuk for 5.** It takes 4: the fifth person means paying for another vehicle.
3. **Buying the bus for a single loop.**
4. **Doing any of the three at midday in summer.** Go early or at sunset.
5. **Expecting to see Santa Cruz from the bus.** That neighbourhood is explored on foot.

If you'd rather understand the city with a live guide and pay less, the best way to see Seville is still on foot or by bike. To fit it into your visit, see [Seville in 1 day](/en/guides/sevilla/seville-in-1-day).
