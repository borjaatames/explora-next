**In Seville, the hop-on hop-off bus costs €28 per person per day, a private tuk tuk €89-99 per vehicle of up to 4 people (1 hour) and a private guided horse carriage €298 per carriage.** Per person, the order changes with group size: for 4, the tuk tuk is the cheapest of the three.

## Quick answer by who's travelling

- **Couple on a budget:** [hop-on hop-off bus](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/seville-hop-on-hop-off-bus-tour-24h) (€28 each) or, better still, on foot: the [small-group walking tour](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/seville-small-group-city-highlights-walking-tour) costs €25. Central Seville is flat and very walkable.
- **Family of 4:** [1-hour tuk tuk](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/private-tuk-tuk-tour-seville), €99 for all four (€24.75 each). No walking in the heat, and the driver explains as you go.
- **Party of 6:** two tuk tuks (€188, €31.33 each) or the bus if you'll use it all day.
- **First visit:** the [2-hour tuk tuk](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/private-tuk-tuk-tour-seville-2-hours), which adds the Torre del Oro, La Cartuja, the Macarena and the city walls to the short route.
- **Special occasion (anniversary, proposal, parents):** the [private guided horse carriage](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/seville-private-horse-carriage-ride-guide).

## Break-even: when is the tuk tuk cheaper?

**For 4 people, the 1-hour tuk tuk (€24.75) is cheaper than the bus (€28).** For 2, the bus wins (€28 against €44.50). For 5 or 6 you need two tuk tuks and the bus is cheaper again. The private horse carriage doesn't compete on price: even full, it's €74.50 each.

Bear in mind you're not buying the same thing: the bus is valid for 24 hours and includes two walking tours plus entry to the Iglesia del Salvador and the Macarena Museum; the tuk tuk and carriage are a closed loop that ends where it began.

## Pros and cons

### Private tuk tuk

- **For:** just your group, electric, a **local driver-guide** who explains and stops for photos, plus covers and blankets if it rains or turns chilly (on the 2-hour version). It leaves from the Saba car park at Plaza de Armas.
- **Against:** up to 4 people; the short route focuses on Plaza de España, María Luisa Park, the Maestranza bullring and Triana.

### Horse carriage

- **For:** Seville's classic image. The private ride has a coachman and a guide versed in Andalusian history, a soft drink and, at the end, a local wine. It leaves from Calle Adriano in El Arenal.
- **Against:** the price, and some people prefer not to ride for the horses' sake, especially in the hottest hours. Street carriages have a coachman only, no guide.

### Hop-on hop-off bus

- **For:** the cheapest for 1-2 people, hop on and off as often as you like, audio guide in over 15 languages, wheelchair accessible and extras (two walking tours, entry tickets). Buses every 30-40 minutes.
- **Against:** recorded commentary and a 75-minute loop along the avenues: it doesn't go into the Santa Cruz quarter.

## Who each one suits

- **Bus:** solo travellers and couples, older travellers or those with limited mobility, anyone wanting to get around all day.
- **Tuk tuk:** families, groups of 3-4, hot days, anyone short on time.
- **Horse carriage:** a special occasion or the classic photo, with the budget for it.
- **On foot or by bike:** those who'd rather understand the city with a live guide and spend less.

## Common mistakes

1. **Getting into a street carriage without agreeing price and duration.** Fares are regulated; ask first.
2. **Booking a tuk tuk for 5.** It seats 4: the fifth person means another vehicle.
3. **Buying the bus for a single loop.** If you won't get off, a tuk tuk shared by 4 is better value.
4. **Doing any of them at midday in summer.** Go early or around sunset.
5. **Expecting to see Santa Cruz from the bus.** That quarter is explored on foot.
