**From Seville, a group day trip costs €47-109 per person and a private one €125-435 per person, depending on where you go and how many of you there are.** The "from" price of a private tour is misleading because it covers the whole vehicle: split between 4 or 6 it falls sharply. Below are the sums, done with the real prices on our tour pages, so you can decide in a minute.

## Quick answer by who's travelling

- **Couple or solo traveller:** group tour. Ronda and the white villages for €47, Córdoba for €109-110. Private would cost you between two and eight times as much.
- **Family of 4:** depends on the destination. For Córdoba, the [private trip by train](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/cordoba-by-train-private-day-trip-from-seville) works out at €167 each against €110 for the group tour: €228 more in total, with a guide just for you. For Ronda or Cádiz the gap is too big unless you have small children or someone with limited mobility.
- **Party of 6:** private gets close. Ronda without a guide, €125 each; Córdoba by train, €147.
- **First time in Andalusia:** a guided group tour. You learn the same and the guide is included, whereas some private options (Ronda without a guide) are driver only.
- **Tight budget:** group, no question. Or go independently: Córdoba is about 45 minutes away by AVE high-speed train.

## How many people before private costs the same?

**From Seville, never.** Even with 6 people, private doesn't reach the group price for the same destination. What changes is how much extra you pay per person:

| Private surcharge vs. group | 2 people | 4 people | 6 people |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Córdoba by train vs. AVE group | +€106 | +€57 | +€37 |
| Ronda without guide vs. white villages group | +€203 | +€133 | +€78 |
| Cádiz and Jerez | +€340 | +€195 | +€135 |

The closest case is **Córdoba by train for 6**: €37 more per head for a private guide in the Mezquita and your own timetable. Gibraltar and the Caminito del Rey have no group version from Seville in our catalogue, so there the comparison is with doing it yourself.

## Pros and cons

### Group tours

- **For:** price, a guide almost always included and nothing to organise. The [white villages and Ronda](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/pueblos-blancos-ronda-day-trip-from-seville) tour has over 4,000 reviews.
- **Against:** a fixed meeting point in the centre and a punctual morning departure, set stops and coach-sized groups (the standard Córdoba tour takes 15-30 people). Some need at least 4 people speaking the same language to run in your language.

### Private trips

- **For:** pickup from your hotel or flat (except Córdoba by train, which starts at Santa Justa station), departure time of your choice, stops at your pace and nobody else in the vehicle.
- **Against:** the price for a couple, and they don't all include the same things. Ronda has a driver-only version; in Cádiz and Jerez the winery visit is in a small group; on the Caminito the trail guide is shared.

## Who each one suits

- **Group:** solo travellers, couples, students, anyone who wants it sorted at the lowest price.
- **Small group** (Córdoba by AVE, max. 8): those who want a closer guide without paying for private. Note that lunch isn't included.
- **Private:** families with small children (car seats, breaks, nap times), older travellers or those with limited mobility, groups of 5-6 friends, and anyone combining destinations their own way.

## Common mistakes

1. **Comparing the private "from" price with the group's per-person price.** €500 to Ronda looks ten times €47; for 6 people it's €125 each.
2. **Assuming private means guided.** Check the "includes" line on each page: some are transport only.
3. **Booking private as a solo traveller.** You pay the price for two.
4. **Forgetting the language minimum on group tours.** If fewer than 4 people book your language, the operator offers another language, another date or a refund.
5. **Going to Córdoba by private car as a couple.** It's €379 each; the train version is €216.
