## Seville day trips, group or private: the quick answer

- **Couple or solo traveller:** group. Ronda and the white villages for €47, Córdoba for €109-110. Private would cost you two to eight times more.
- **Family of 4:** depends on the destination. To Córdoba, the private train trip works out at €167 per person against €110 for the group: €228 more in total, with a guide just for you. To Ronda or Cádiz, the gap is too big unless there are small children or someone with reduced mobility.
- **Group of 6:** private gets close. Ronda without a guide, €125 per person; Córdoba by train, €147.
- **First time in Andalusia:** group with a guide. You learn the same and the guide is included; on some private trips (Ronda without a guide) you only get a driver.
- **Tight budget:** group, no question. Or on your own: Córdoba is about 45 minutes away by AVE.

## When does a private tour from Seville cost the same?

**In Seville, never.** Even with 6 people, private doesn't reach the group price for the same destination. What changes is how much extra you pay per person:

| Private surcharge over group | 2 people | 4 people | 6 people |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Córdoba by train vs. group by AVE | +€106 | +€57 | +€37 |
| Ronda without guide vs. white villages group | +€203 | +€133 | +€78 |
| Cádiz and Jerez | +€340 | +€195 | +€135 |

The closest case is **Córdoba by train with 6 people**: €37 more each for a private guide in the Mezquita and a schedule that suits you. It includes train tickets and Mezquita entry, and leaves from Santa Justa.

## Pros and cons of each option

**Group**

- **For:** the price, a guide almost always included and nothing to organise. The [white villages and Ronda](/en/cities/sevilla/activities/pueblos-blancos-ronda-day-trip-from-seville) tour has over 4,000 reviews.
- **Against:** a fixed meeting point in the centre and a punctual morning departure, set stops and coach-sized groups (the standard Córdoba tour takes 15-30 people). Some need at least 4 people speaking the same language to run in yours.

**Private**

- **For:** pick-up at your hotel or apartment (except Córdoba by train, which leaves from Santa Justa), your choice of departure time, stops at your pace and nobody else in the vehicle.
- **Against:** the price for a couple, and not all of them include the same things. Ronda has a driver-only version; in Cádiz and Jerez the winery visit is in a small group; on the Caminito the trail guide is shared.

## Seville private tours for families, and who each option suits

- **Group:** solo travellers, couples, students, anyone who wants the plan sorted at the lowest price.
- **Small group** (Córdoba by AVE, max. 8): anyone who wants a closer guide without paying for private. Note: lunch isn't included.
- **Private:** families with small children (car seats, stops, nap times), older travellers or people with reduced mobility, groups of 5-6 friends, and anyone who wants to combine destinations their own way.

## Common mistakes

1. **Comparing the private "from" price with the group per-person price.** €500 to Ronda looks ten times more than €47; with 6 people it's €125 each.
2. **Assuming private includes a guide.** Check what each trip includes: some versions are transport only.
3. **Booking private when travelling solo.** You pay the price of two.
4. **Forgetting the language minimum on group tours.** If 4 people aren't reached, the operator offers another language, another date or a refund.
5. **Going to Córdoba by private car as a couple.** It's €379 per person; the train version costs €216.
