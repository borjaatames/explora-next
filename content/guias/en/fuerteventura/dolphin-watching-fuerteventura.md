**The waters off southern Fuerteventura, by the Jandía peninsula, are one of the best areas in the Canaries to see wild dolphins.** Boats, speedboats and sailboats leave Morro Jable harbour every day to look for them, and many trips add a swim stop. Here's how to choose.

The key: if you're staying in the south, **go from Morro Jable**: a 2-hour trip is enough. If you're staying in the north, choose a **catamaran from Corralejo** that combines dolphins and Lobos.

## Types of trip

- **Boat with drinks and a swim (2 h):** the most popular. Looks for dolphins off Jandía and stops for a swim. Good with children.
- **Speedboat with snorkelling (2 h):** small group, faster, with a stop to swim with snorkelling gear. You'll get a little wet.
- **Sailboat with snorkelling (3.5 h):** calm sailing with dolphins, swimming and snorkelling.
- **Sailing catamaran (4.5 h):** more time at sea and smaller groups.
- **From Corralejo (5 h):** a catamaran that looks for dolphins and stops at Lobos island.

## Which species you'll see

- **Bottlenose dolphin:** the most frequent, in groups close to the coast.
- **Atlantic spotted dolphin:** very active; often comes to the bow.
- **Pilot whales and rorquals:** occasional; best not counted on.

## How to choose a responsible operator

- **It keeps its distance** and slows down near the animals.
- **It doesn't promise swimming with dolphins:** approaching them in the water is not allowed.
- **Read recent reviews** in the listing before booking.

## Practical tips

- **Book a morning trip:** the wind picks up at midday and the sea gets rougher.
- **If you get seasick,** avoid the speedboat and choose a big boat or sailboat; sit in the middle.
- **Bring** swimwear, a towel, a cap, sunscreen and a sweatshirt.
- **Protect your phone** from spray, especially on the speedboat.

## When to go

There are trips **all year**. The south of Jandía is fairly sheltered, though in summer the trade wind blows hard in the afternoon.

If you'd prefer a longer boat trip with food, see [Fuerteventura boat trips](/en/guides/fuerteventura/fuerteventura-boat-trips). To plan your days in the south, [things to do in Morro Jable](/en/guides/fuerteventura/things-to-do-in-morro-jable).

## In one sentence

To see dolphins in Fuerteventura, a 2-hour trip from Morro Jable (boat with a swim or speedboat with snorkelling), ideally in the morning; from the north, a catamaran with dolphins and Lobos.
