**Corralejo is the liveliest town in northern Fuerteventura**: an old fishing port with streets full of restaurants, a natural park of white dunes just outside town and Lobos island 15 minutes away by boat. It's also one of the Canaries' surf capitals. Here's what to do and how to plan it.

The key: spend **half a day on Lobos**, another on the **dunes and Grandes Playas**, and if you fancy something active, add a **surf lesson** or a **buggy tour** in the north.

## What to see and do

- **Corralejo dunes:** a natural park of white sand running down to the sea, with the Grandes Playas along the coast. At sunset, the light on the dunes is spectacular.
- **Lobos island:** a protected islet off the harbour, with a trail around it, the Martiño lighthouse and La Concha lagoon for swimming. Reached by ferry or catamaran.
- **Old town:** streets by the harbour with terraces, the church and the seafront. There's a walking tour with tastings of Majorero cheese, mojo and other local products.
- **Surfing:** schools for all levels; instructors take students to the beach with the best waves of the day.
- **Buggy in the north:** dirt tracks between volcanoes and lava fields, near Lajares and Calderón Hondo.

## Lobos: ferry or catamaran

- **Ferry (about 4 h on the island):** the cheapest. It drops you at the Lobos quay and you return when you like within the timetable.
- **Catamaran with lunch (4 h):** sails around the island, stops for swimming and snorkelling and includes food and drinks.
- **Full tour:** some trips combine Lobos, "popcorn beach" and the dunes in one day.

## Tips

- **Book Lobos access in advance:** daily places are limited.
- **Bring water, a cap and sunscreen** to the dunes and Lobos: there's no shade.
- **Don't take sand or stones** from the dunes, or the "popcorn" from Bajo de la Burra beach: these are protected areas.
- **The wind** blows hard on many days; choose the morning for Lobos and boat trips.

## When to go

Corralejo is great **all year**. In summer the trade wind is stronger; in winter more waves arrive for surfing. The water is around 18-19 °C in winter and 22-23 °C at the end of summer.

To plan your Lobos visit, see [how to get to Lobos island](/en/guides/fuerteventura/lobos-island-how-to-get-there). For the rest of the island, [what to see in Fuerteventura](/en/guides/fuerteventura/what-to-see-in-fuerteventura).

## In one sentence

In Corralejo, spend half a day on Lobos (ferry or catamaran), an afternoon on the dunes and Grandes Playas and, if you like action, a surf lesson or a buggy tour in the north.
