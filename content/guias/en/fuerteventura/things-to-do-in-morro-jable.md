**Morro Jable is the southernmost town in Fuerteventura**, on the Jandía peninsula: miles of white sand beach, a harbour with plenty of boat trips and, beyond the mountains, wild Cofete beach. Here's what to do and how to combine sea and land.

The key: book **one boat trip** (dolphins or catamaran) and **one jeep trip** to Cofete; spend the rest of the time on the beach.

## At sea

- **Dolphin boat (2 h):** a short trip with drinks and a swim stop. The most popular and cheapest.
- **Speedboat with snorkelling (2 h):** small group, more speed and a swim stop with snorkelling gear.
- **Catamaran with food and drinks (4 h):** sailing along the Jandía coast, swimming, lunch and open bar.
- **Glass-bottom boat (4 h):** see the seabed from inside the boat; good with children.
- **Sailboat (3.5 h):** calmer, with snorkelling or lunch depending on the trip.

## On land

- **Matorral beach and the lighthouse:** miles of sand with the Jandía salt marsh behind; perfect for a sunset walk.
- **Jeep to Cofete (3-5 h):** crosses the mountains on a track to Cofete, 12 km of unspoilt sand, and Villa Winter. Some routes continue to the Punta Jandía lighthouse.
- **El Puertito de la Cruz:** a small fishing village at the tip of the peninsula, with fresh fish.

## Tips

- **Don't drive to Cofete in a hire car** if the insurance doesn't cover tracks; a jeep with a driver avoids the risk.
- **Don't swim at Cofete:** it has very strong currents and no lifeguards.
- **Book boat trips in the morning,** when the sea is calmer.
- **Bring** sunscreen and a cap: there's hardly any shade in Jandía.

## When to go

Morro Jable is great **all year**. The south of Jandía is fairly sheltered from the trade wind, though it blows hard in summer. The water is around 19 °C in winter and 23 °C at the end of summer.

For Cofete on your own, see [how to get to Cofete and Villa Winter](/en/guides/fuerteventura/cofete-villa-winter-how-to-get-there). For the rest of the island, [what to see in Fuerteventura](/en/guides/fuerteventura/what-to-see-in-fuerteventura).

## In one sentence

In Morro Jable, take a dolphin boat or a catamaran with lunch, a jeep to Cofete, and spend the rest of the time on Matorral beach.
