**Fuerteventura is the island of beaches, and from the sea you can see why**: golden coves, turquoise water and long stretches of coast without buildings. There are catamarans with food and open bar, sailboats, glass-bottom boats and trips to Lobos island. Here's how to choose depending on where you're staying.

The key: choose the **port closest to your hotel**. In the south, Morro Jable; in the centre-east, Caleta de Fuste; in the north, Corralejo and Lobos. If you only want to see dolphins, see our dedicated guide.

## Morro Jable (south)

- **Catamaran with food and drinks (4 h):** sails along the Jandía coast, with swimming, snorkelling, lunch and open bar.
- **Glass-bottom boat (4 h):** underwater windows to see the seabed, plus food and a swim. Good with children.
- **Sailboat (3.5 h):** calmer, with lunch or snorkelling depending on the trip.

## Caleta de Fuste (centre-east)

- **Catamaran to Pozo Negro (4 h):** sails south to Pozo Negro bay, with paella, drinks and a swim. Very close to the airport and the Caleta hotels.

## Corralejo (north)

- **Catamaran to Lobos island (4 h):** sails around Lobos, stopping to swim and snorkel in very clear water, with food and drinks. Small-group versions with paella are available.
- **Catamaran with dolphins and Lobos (5 h):** combines looking for dolphins with a stop at Lobos.

## Tips

- **Book a morning trip:** the trade wind usually picks up in the afternoon and the sea gets rougher.
- **Bring** swimwear on, a towel, reef-safe sunscreen, a cap and a sweatshirt.
- **If you get seasick,** choose a big catamaran and avoid speedboats.
- **With children,** the glass-bottom boat or a catamaran with shade and a toilet.
- **If the sea is rough,** the operator may cancel and offer another date or a refund.

## When to go

You can sail **all year**. The water is around 19 °C in winter and 23 °C at the end of summer. In summer the wind is stronger, especially in the afternoon.

To visit Lobos on your own by ferry, see [how to get to Lobos island](/en/guides/fuerteventura/lobos-island-how-to-get-there). If you're staying in the south, [things to do in Morro Jable](/en/guides/fuerteventura/things-to-do-in-morro-jable).

## In one sentence

A 4-hour catamaran with food and a swim from the port nearest your hotel: Morro Jable in the south, Caleta de Fuste in the centre or Corralejo towards Lobos, ideally in the morning.
