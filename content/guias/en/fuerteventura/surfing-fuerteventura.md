**Fuerteventura is one of Europe's great surf destinations**: waves almost all year, mild water and beaches for every level. Schools in Corralejo and the south work simply: they pick you up, check the forecast and take you to the beach with the best waves that day. Here's how to choose your lesson.

The key: if you're staying in the **north**, take a lesson from Corralejo; in the **south** (Costa Calma, Morro Jable), a lesson at La Pared. For a first time, one 3.5-4-hour lesson is enough.

## Surf areas

- **North (Corralejo):** the area with the most schools. Depending on wind and waves, lessons go to northern beaches or to El Cotillo on the west coast.
- **El Cotillo:** sandy beaches with beach breaks on the north-west coast; it also has calm lagoons for swimming.
- **South-west (La Pared):** a sandy beach on the west coast a few minutes from Costa Calma, with more consistent waves than the rest of the south.
- **Northern reefs:** powerful waves over rock, for expert surfers only.

## What a lesson is like

- **Duration:** 3.5-4 hours, with transport, theory on the sand and about 2 hours in the water.
- **What's included:** soft board, wetsuit, instructor and usually transport from the school; some pick up from hotels.
- **Groups:** small, by level; private lessons are available.
- **Age:** many schools accept children from 6-8 if they can swim.

## Tips

- **Book early in your trip:** if you like it, take several lessons in a row to progress.
- **Bring** swimwear, a towel, water-resistant sunscreen, water and something warm: the wind cools you down.
- **Listen to your instructor:** some beaches have currents.
- **Wind** is part of Fuerteventura; schools look for the most sheltered spots.

## When to go

You can surf **all year**. From October to April there are more waves; in summer they're smaller and the trade wind blows hard, the season the island shares with windsurfing and kitesurfing. The water is around 19 °C in winter and 23 °C at the end of summer.

If you're staying in the north, combine surfing with our guide to [things to do in Corralejo](/en/guides/fuerteventura/things-to-do-in-corralejo). For the rest of the island, [what to see in Fuerteventura](/en/guides/fuerteventura/what-to-see-in-fuerteventura).

## In one sentence

To learn to surf in Fuerteventura, take a 3.5-4-hour lesson with transport from Corralejo in the north or at La Pared in the south, at any time of year.
