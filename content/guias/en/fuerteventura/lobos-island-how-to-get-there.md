**Lobos Island is two kilometres from Fuerteventura and a world away from Corralejo.** It's a volcanic islet of about 4.7 km², a natural park since 1982, with a turquoise lagoon, a 19th-century lighthouse and a volcano whose crater opens to the sea. Nobody lives there and there are no roads. That's exactly why access is limited: **without the council permit you can't go ashore**, and in summer 2026 the council stepped up controls and surveillance.

## The permit: step by step

Lobos has required prior authorisation since 2019, and the current system runs on **lobospass.com**, the official website of the Cabildo de Fuerteventura.

1. **Go to lobospass.com** when booking opens for your date. The council and the Ferry Lobos company say you can apply **3 days** ahead; some websites say 5. Don't rely on either: check the site itself a few days before.
2. **Choose your slot:** **morning (10:00-14:00)** or **afternoon (14:00-18:00)**. Each has a quota of **200 people**, under the islet's management plan.
3. **Enter the details** of up to **three people** per request: name, surname and ID card or passport. For larger groups, make several requests.
4. **Save the QR code** on your phone or print it. You'll be asked for it when you land and it can be checked on the island.

The permit is **free, personal and non-transferable**. Don't pay websites offering to "handle it" for you. The council has announced a future access fee, with no amount set yet.

**What about tours?** Some ferry tickets include park entry (like the [return ticket with entry](/en/cities/fuerteventura/activities/lobos-island-ferry-corralejo-park-entry)) and some catamarans handle the permit. Always ask when booking and check it appears in your confirmation.

## Boats from Corralejo

They all leave from **Corralejo harbour** and the crossing takes **15-20 minutes**. There are several companies (Ferry Lobos, Nortour, Corralejo Charter), plus water taxis and catamarans.

- **Price:** with Ferry Lobos, **€17** return for adults, **€11** for children aged 4 to 11, free under 3 (Canary residents €14 and €9), fees and insurance included.
- **Ferry Lobos winter timetable (11 Sep-13 Jun):** outbound at 9:45, 11:00, 12:30 and 13:30; return at 14:00, 16:15 and 17:15.
- **Summer timetable (13 Jun-10 Sep):** more departures, from 9:45 to 15:30, with returns until 18:15.
- **You choose your return when you board** on the way out and get your return boarding pass.

The key is to **match the boat to your slot**: with a morning permit, take the first boat (9:45) and return at 14:00; with an afternoon permit, leave around 13:30 and take the last boat back. Times change with sea conditions and season: confirm them when you buy.

## What to see on Lobos

- **La Concha beach** (also called La Calera): a lagoon of white sand and still water five minutes from the jetty. The best place to swim.
- **El Puertito:** fishermen's huts and rock pools by the jetty; the only restaurant is here.
- **Las Lagunitas and the Marrajo salt pans:** wetlands and old salt pans on the way to the lighthouse.
- **Martiño lighthouse (1865):** at the northern tip, with views of Lanzarote.
- **La Caldera (127 m):** the highest point, a volcano with its crater open to the sea. A short, steep climb with views of the Corralejo Dunes.

**Recommended route:** jetty, El Puertito, Las Lagunitas, Martiño lighthouse, La Caldera and back to La Concha for a swim. It takes about **three hours** at an easy pace, with no shade. It's also the start of the first Fuerteventura stage of the **GR 131** long-distance trail.

## Eating on Lobos

There's **only one restaurant**, by El Puertito, known for its fish and paella. It fills up: if you want to eat there, put your name down when you arrive. Always bring **water and food** just in case.

## Park rules

- Walk **only on the marked trails**; some sections may be temporarily closed.
- **Not allowed:** camping, drones, taking stones, sand or shells, shellfish gathering and spearfishing.
- **No pets** on land.
- Take your rubbish back to Corralejo.

## What we DON'T recommend

1. **Buying the ferry and forgetting the permit.** They're two separate things.
2. **Booking a return that doesn't fit your slot.** If you overstay, you risk a fine.
3. **Going without water or a hat.** There's no shade and the sun is strong even with the wind.
4. **Going when there's a big swell.** Boats may cancel or change times; keep a spare day.
5. **Staying at La Concha the whole time.** The loop to the lighthouse and La Caldera is the best of the island.

## In one sentence

Request your permit at lobospass.com as soon as it opens, match the ferry to your slot and give Lobos three hours on foot: it's the most beautiful trip in northern Fuerteventura.
