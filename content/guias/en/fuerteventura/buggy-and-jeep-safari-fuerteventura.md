**Fuerteventura is the island of dirt tracks**: volcanoes, sandy plains, reddish ravines and wild beaches the tarmac never reached. Exploring it by buggy or jeep is one of the most fun excursions, and the best way to reach Cofete. Here are the routes by area and how to choose.

The key: choose the route **near your hotel**. If you want to **drive**, a 2-3-hour buggy. If you want to **see Cofete**, a jeep safari with a driver from Morro Jable or Costa Calma.

## Buggy routes by area

- **North (Corralejo):** tracks between volcanoes and lava fields near Lajares and Calderón Hondo, with views of Lobos and Lanzarote. The most popular.
- **Centre (Caleta de Fuste):** a guided route stopping at a Majorero cheese farm and the Salinas del Carmen salt pans.
- **South (Costa Calma):** tracks to the La Pared coast on the west side, with dunes and cliffs. More powerful Can-Am buggies are also available.
- **South (Jandía):** through the natural park to El Puertito at the tip of the island.

## Jeep safari to Cofete

- **Cofete (3-4 h):** crosses the Jandía mountains on a track to Cofete beach, 12 km of unspoilt sand, and Villa Winter.
- **Cofete and Punta Jandía (5 h):** adds the Punta Jandía lighthouse and El Puertito.
- **Cofete and Roque del Moro (4 h):** stops at viewpoints and at the rock facing the beach.

## Tips

- **Bring your licence** if you're driving the buggy.
- **Clothes and shoes** that can get dirty, sunglasses and a scarf for the dust.
- **A 4-seat buggy** is cheaper per person for families or friends.
- **Don't swim at Cofete:** very strong currents and no lifeguards.
- **Stick to marked tracks:** the island is a Biosphere Reserve and the ground is easily damaged.

## When to go

**All year.** In summer avoid the middle of the day; in winter the weather is perfect for a buggy. The wind can raise a lot of dust, so the scarf is useful.

For Cofete on your own, see [how to get to Cofete and Villa Winter](/en/guides/fuerteventura/cofete-villa-winter-how-to-get-there). If you're staying in the north, [things to do in Corralejo](/en/guides/fuerteventura/things-to-do-in-corralejo).

## In one sentence

To drive, a 2-3-hour buggy near your hotel (Corralejo, Caleta de Fuste, Costa Calma or Jandía); for Cofete, a jeep safari with a driver, with no risk to your hire car.
