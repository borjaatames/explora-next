Cádiz is the easiest port call in Andalusia: **you walk off the ship and five minutes later you're in Plaza de San Juan de Dios**, opposite the town hall. The real decision isn't how to get into town but whether to stay in Cádiz or head to Seville, and that depends on how long your ship is in port.

## Where do cruise ships dock in Cádiz?

Ships berth along the quays of the Cádiz-Ciudad basin (Reina Sofía, Reina Victoria, Marqués de Comillas, Ciudad and Alfonso XIII). There are two terminals:

| Terminal | Facilities | Way into town |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ciudad quay | Tourist information, shops, free wifi | Straight onto Paseo de Canalejas and Plaza de San Juan de Dios |
| Alfonso XIII quay | The larger one: tourist information, shops, free wifi | A few minutes' walk to the same spot |

From either, the centre is about a **5-minute walk**. The train station is roughly 900 metres away (11 minutes) and the bus station 1 km.

## Getting around

| Option | Use it for | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walking | The whole old town | Flat and compact: the best option |
| City bus, line 1 | La Victoria beach and the newer part of town | Stops at Plaza de España, 300 metres away |
| Taxi | Reduced mobility or luggage | Ranks within 5 minutes of the port |
| Train | Jerez (about 35 min) or Seville (1 h 25-1 h 50) | Station an 11-minute walk away |
| Catamaran | El Puerto de Santa María | 30-minute crossing |

## What to see in 4, 6 or 8 hours

| Time ashore | Plan | What to book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 hours | The Cathedral and its Clock Tower, El Pópulo and the Roman Theatre, Plaza de las Flores and the Central Market, Torre Tavira and its camera obscura | Torre Tavira and Cathedral guided tour, from €38 |
| 6 hours | All that plus fried fish for lunch in La Viña and a walk round La Caleta to Santa Catalina castle | Bay catamaran trip, from €19 |
| 8 hours or more | Seville on a port-pickup tour, or Jerez by train for a sherry bodega | Seville shore excursion, from €119 |

The **Torre Tavira and Cathedral guided tour** starts in Plaza de San Juan de Dios, right by the port, and lasts 2 hours, so it fits any port call.

## Getting to Seville from the ship (and the risk of missing it)

Seville is **about 90 minutes along the AP-4 motorway**. Once there you need at least 4 hours for the Cathedral, the Alcázar and Santa Cruz, so the whole day comes to 7-8 hours. There are three ways to do it:

**1. Cruise-passenger shore excursion (from €119 per person).** Picks you up and drops you back at the port, lasts 8 hours and includes an English-language guided walk through Santa Cruz, the Archivo de Indias, the Cathedral and the Alcázar, in groups of 10 to 18. The drive is on a large coach and **the Alcázar ticket isn't included**: request it from the operator as soon as you book. Timings follow your actual docking time and the operator includes a back-to-ship guarantee.

[actividad: sevilla-excursion-cruceristas-puerto-cadiz]

**2. Private trip (from about €600 per group).** Your own driver, quayside pickup (give your ship's name when booking), a 2-hour guided walk and free time; 8 h 30 in all. For 4 people it's about €880. If you want to go inside the **Alcázar and Cathedral with an official guide**, the version with tickets costs about €598 for 2 and €840 for 4, but it's **non-refundable**.

[actividad: sevilla-excursion-privada-desde-cadiz]

**3. By train on your own.** There are around 20 departures a day from Cádiz to Seville, from 1 h 25 on the Alvia to 1 h 50 on Media Distancia trains, from about €10 each way. It's the cheapest option, but the risk is all yours: if the return train is late or you miss one, the ship sails without you. We'd only recommend it with **9-10 hours in port** and a return train that reaches Cádiz **at least 1 h 30 before all aboard**. On Sundays, Seville Cathedral doesn't open to visitors until 2.30pm.

## What about Jerez?

Jerez is the easy option: about **35 minutes by train** (from around €6 each way) from Cádiz station. Six hours gives you time for a sherry bodega, the centre and the Alcázar of Jerez. We don't sell Jerez tours from the port, so go independently and book the bodega visit on its website.

## Ship's tour, cruise-passenger tour or private?

| Option | Price per person (2 travellers) | If something runs late |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cruise line excursion | Whatever your cruise line charges | The ship waits for its own tours |
| Cruise-passenger tour from the port | €119 + Alcázar ticket | The operator works to the ship's timetable and gives a back-to-ship guarantee |
| Private from the port | About €300 (about €220 for 4) | The driver works to your timetable; the ship won't wait |
| Train on your own | From about €20 return | All the risk is yours |

The only tour the ship waits for is its own. With anyone else, the operator takes responsibility for timing, so pick one designed for cruise passengers and read what its guarantee covers.

## What time should you be back?

- **All aboard** is usually 30-60 minutes before sailing and is given in ship's time: check the daily programme.
- Staying in Cádiz on your own, it's enough to be at the terminal **an hour before all aboard**.
- If you've gone to Seville or Jerez by train, allow **1 h 30 of margin** on arrival in Cádiz, plus the 11-minute walk to the terminal.
- Take your cruise card and photo ID.

## Sundays and public holidays

- **Cádiz Central Market:** closed on Sundays.
- **Cádiz Cathedral:** Sunday visits in the afternoon only.
- **Roman Theatre and Cádiz Museum:** closed on Mondays; open on Sundays.
- **In Seville:** the Cathedral opens on Sundays from 2.30pm to 7pm (Monday to Saturday, 10.45am to 6pm).
- **Shops:** many small shops close on Sundays. Bars and restaurants open as normal.

## Common mistakes

1. **Taking the train to Seville with under 9 hours in port.** You'll spend more time on the train than in Seville.
2. **Booking the Seville tour and forgetting the Alcázar ticket.** You have to request it from the operator.
3. **Taking a taxi into town.** It's a 5-minute walk.
4. **Planning the Central Market for a Sunday.** It's closed.
5. **Booking the private trip with tickets without checking the cancellation terms.** It's non-refundable.
