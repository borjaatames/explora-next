## The great Costa de la Luz beaches

- **Bolonia beach (Tarifa).** Golden sand, a **giant dune** and, right beside it, the Roman ruins of **Baelo Claudia**. One of the most spectacular. 1h 15min from Cádiz.
- **Zahara de los Atunes.** Kilometres of sand and a fishing village with a good vibe and almadraba tuna. 1h 15min.
- **Los Caños de Meca.** Bohemian and sheltered beneath the **Trafalgar** lighthouse, with coves and pine woods. 1 hour.
- **El Palmar.** The **surf** beach, wide and famous for its sunsets. 1 hour.
- **La Barrosa (Chiclana).** Very long and family-friendly, with services. 30 minutes.
- **Tarifa beaches (Los Lances, Valdevaqueros).** **Wind** paradise: kite and windsurfing, with Africa on the horizon. 1h 30min.

## The levante and when to go

The **levante** (land wind) can make a day at an open beach like Bolonia or Los Lances impossible **in any season**. If it's blowing hard, head for a sheltered cove or switch to the old town. In Tarifa, though, the wind is the draw for water sports.

- **Swimming season:** **May to October**. The Atlantic is cooler than the Mediterranean: **18-20 °C in June** and **21-23 °C in August and September**.
- **July and August** fill the urban beaches and villages; the wild beaches cope better.
- **October is the secret month:** mild water, empty beaches and little levante.

## Practicalities: getting there, tides and facilities

- **Transport:** there's no practical public transport to the province's beaches: car or excursion.
- **Parking:** at Bolonia, Los Caños and El Palmar spaces near the sand are few and fill by mid-morning in August. Never on the verge: you'll be fined.
- **Tides:** the Atlantic has a **2-3 metre** difference between high and low tide. At low tide La Caleta and Bolonia double in size and natural pools appear; at high tide some coves at Los Caños have no sand left. Check the day's tide table.
- **Baelo Claudia:** the Roman city sits on Bolonia beach itself. **Free for EU citizens**; in summer, visits run in time slots (check museosdeandalucia.es or 956 106 797).
- **Beach bars:** Zahara, Los Caños and El Palmar have them (almadraba tuna in Zahara, May to June); Bolonia has few and simple ones; Cortadura and the Tarifa beaches have none along long stretches. Bring water and shade.
- **Flags:** the Atlantic has rip currents and yellow flags are common. With children, choose lifeguarded beaches (La Victoria, La Barrosa, Zahara).

## Tips and common mistakes

1. **Ignoring the wind forecast.** A levante day ruins an open beach.
2. **Going to Bolonia without seeing Baelo Claudia.** It's right next door and spectacular.
3. **Forgetting the tides.** They change the beach a lot depending on the time.
4. **Confusing urban beaches with wild ones.** They're very different experiences.
5. **Not booking in Zahara or Los Caños in August:** small villages that fill up.
6. **Underestimating distances.** From Cádiz city to Bolonia or Tarifa is well over an hour.
