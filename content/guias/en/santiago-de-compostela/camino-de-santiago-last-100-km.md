## Where to start: the Camino from Sarria, Tui or Ferrol

| Route | Start | Distance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camino Francés | **Sarria** | ~114 km |
| Portuguese Way | Tui | ~118 km |
| English Way | Ferrol | ~118 km |

For the Compostela you must walk **at least 100 km** (or 200 km by bike). That's why **Sarria** is the most popular start on the Camino Francés: it's just over the minimum. Watch out on the English Way: from A Coruña it's 75 km and doesn't qualify; you have to start in Ferrol.

## The Camino Francés stages from Sarria

| Stage | Route | Approx. km |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sarria → Portomarín | 22 |
| 2 | Portomarín → Palas de Rei | 25 |
| 3 | Palas de Rei → Arzúa | 29 |
| 4 | Arzúa → O Pedrouzo | 19 |
| 5 | O Pedrouzo → Santiago | 20 |

Five comfortable stages (you can split the third). **Monte do Gozo**, 5 km from Santiago, gives you the first view of the cathedral towers.

**Pace:** pilgrims walk from **7:00 to 13:00** to avoid the heat and arrive before the hostels fill up. At 4 km/h, a 22 km stage takes 5 h 30 with stops.

## The pilgrim credential and the Compostela

- **Credential:** the "passport" where you collect stamps. It costs **€2-3** and is sold at the Pilgrim's Office in Santiago, in parishes and hostels along the Camino, or by Camino associations before you leave. In Sarria it's sold at the Magdalena monastery and several hostels. Without it you can't sleep in public albergues.
- **Stamps:** on the last 100 km you need **two a day** (bars, hostels, churches).
- **The Compostela is free;** the distance certificate, showing the kilometres, costs €3. Collect it at the **Pilgrim's Office** (Rúa das Carretas, 33), open daily **10:00-18:00**, except 25 December and 1 January (on those days it's issued at the cathedral). It works with a QR number: get yours on arrival and come back at your time.
- **Pilgrims' Mass:** from 16 October 2026, until further notice, the 12:00 and 19:30 masses are held in the nearby church of San Agustín, due to works in the cathedral's main chapel. The cathedral remains open for free from 7:00 to 21:00; more in [how to visit Santiago Cathedral](/en/guides/santiago-de-compostela/santiago-cathedral-how-to-visit).

## Budget, accommodation and getting to Sarria

- **Daily budget:** between **€35 and €45 a day** in hostels and **€60-80** with a private room.
  - Public albergue: €10 (no booking; they open at 13:00 and fill up first come, first served).
  - Private albergue: €15-22. Guesthouse or hostal: €40-70 for a double.
  - Pilgrim's menu: €12-15; breakfast: €3-5.
- **Book in high season:** Sarria, as the mass starting point, fills up; book at least the first night, and in the smaller stage towns too.
- **Backpack transfer:** €6-8 per stage; leave it at hostel reception before 8:00 and it's collected for the next one. You need your destination accommodation booked.
- **Getting to Sarria:** by train from Madrid (5-6 hours, changing in Ourense or direct depending on the day) or from Santiago and A Coruña changing in Monforte; by bus from Lugo (45 minutes). Many pilgrims fly to Santiago, leave their suitcase in left luggage and go to Sarria by train or shared taxi (around €90 for the taxi).

## When to go and what to bring

| Season | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| **May-June and September** | Ideal: good weather and atmosphere |
| **July and August** | Hot and very busy (Sarria fills up) |
| **October-April** | Quiet; rain and some hostels closed |
| **Xacobeo Holy Year** | More pilgrims than ever |

- **Broken-in footwear:** don't wear new boots on the Camino.
- **Rain jacket or poncho:** it rains often in Galicia.
- **Layers** and a light pack: every gram counts after a few days.
- **First-aid kit:** Vaseline on your feet before setting off, seamless socks, Compeed, ibuprofen and sunscreen. There are pharmacies at every stage end.

## Tips and common mistakes

1. **Don't start in A Coruña expecting the Compostela:** it's only 75 km.
2. **Don't wear new boots:** it's the mistake that causes the most blisters.
3. **Don't do Sarria in August without booking:** it's the busiest start of the year.
4. **Don't forget the two daily stamps** on the last 100 km.
5. **Look after your feet from day one:** blisters ruin the Camino.
