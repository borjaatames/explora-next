**Santiago Cathedral is the goal of the Camino and the heart of the city**, but its visit confuses many people: is there a fee? what's the botafumeiro? can you go up on the roof? The short answer is that **entering the temple is free**, but the best parts (the Pórtico de la Gloria, the rooftops, the museum) are paid, timed visits. This guide tells you what to see, what to book and how to organise yourself.

## What's free

- **The temple.** Open **daily from 7:00 to 21:00**, entry through the **Puerta de Platerías**. In summer it closes at 20:30 on Saturdays for the pilgrims' vigil.
- **The Apostle's tomb.** The crypt with the relics of St James, beneath the high altar.
- **The embrace of the Apostle** (when enabled), behind the altar. In season, expect a queue of 15–40 minutes for the tomb and the embrace.
- **The Masses**, including the Pilgrim's Mass (see below). Tourist visits stop during services.

## What's paid (and worth it)

| Visit | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Pórtico de la Gloria | Master Mateo's carved entrance (1188), with its restored original colour; timed entry, no photos |
| Rooftops and Carraca tower | Guided walk over the granite roof, with views over the old town |
| Cathedral Museum | Cloister, Treasury, Chapel of Relics and Pazo de Xelmírez |

Tickets are sold at the **ticket offices on Praza do Obradoiro** and online at visitas.catedraldesantiago.es. The museum opens **daily from 10:00 to 20:00**.

> **Tip:** the **Pórtico de la Gloria** has a very limited capacity: since the 2018 restoration it's visited in small groups, at the time printed on your ticket. In summer the slots sell out days ahead, so book before you travel or take a tour that includes it.

The rooftop visit is cancelled in heavy rain or strong wind, which is common in Santiago. Book it early in your stay so you have a spare day if it's called off.

## The botafumeiro

The **botafumeiro** is the giant censer (over 50 kg) that eight *tiraboleiros* swing across the transept. It **doesn't fly every day**. According to the cathedral, it swings on the big solemnities:

- **6 January** (Epiphany), **Easter Sunday**, **Ascension**, **Pentecost** and **Christ the King**.
- **23 May** (Clavijo), **25 July** (St James), **15 August** (Assumption) and **1 November** (All Saints).
- **8 December**, **25 December** and **30 December** (Translation of St James).

On other days it only swings when a pilgrim group has requested it, and that isn't announced far in advance. If your date matches a solemnity, arrive an hour before the Mass.

## The Pilgrim's Mass

The Pilgrim's Mass is held daily at **12:00** and **19:30**, the Masses that crown the Camino. To get a seat, arrive about 45 minutes early.

> **Warning:** since **Friday 16 October 2026**, and until further notice, the Pilgrim's Masses (9:30, 12:00 and 19:30) are held in the **nearby church of San Agustín**, because the cathedral's main chapel is under works. Only the 7:30 Mass stays in the cathedral. Visits to the cathedral are not affected. The notice doesn't say whether the botafumeiro will swing during the works, so check the cathedral website before planning around it.

## Practical tips

- **Large backpacks aren't allowed** inside for security reasons. Pilgrims can leave them in storage near the Pilgrim's Office on Rúa das Carretas.
- **Dress respectfully:** it's a working temple.
- **Order of the day:** go into the temple early (from 7:00 it's quiet), do your timed visit mid-morning and see Obradoiro, Platerías and Quintana squares around it.
- The **Holy Door** (Quintana) opens only in a Holy Year.
- Combine the cathedral with the rest of the old town in [Santiago in one day](/en/guides/santiago-de-compostela/santiago-in-one-day). If you're arriving on foot, see [the last 100 km of the Camino](/en/guides/santiago-de-compostela/camino-de-santiago-last-100-km).

## What we don't recommend

1. **Assuming you'll see the botafumeiro.** It only flies on the dates above or when a group pays for it.
2. **Arriving at 12:00 sharp for the Mass.** It'll be full; go earlier, and check where it's held.
3. **Skipping the Pórtico de la Gloria because you didn't book.** It's the cathedral's jewel.
4. **Booking the rooftops on your last day.** Rain or wind can cancel it.
5. **Turning up with a huge backpack.** You won't get in; use storage.
