## What to see in Santiago Cathedral: free and paid

**Free:**

- **The church.** Going in to pray or look around costs nothing.
- **The Apostle's tomb**, in the crypt beneath the high altar.
- **The embrace of the Apostle** (when available), behind the altar.

**Paid (and worth it):**

| Visit | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| **Pórtico de la Gloria** | Master Mateo's restored masterpiece (timed ticket) |
| **Rooftops** | Guided visit over the granite roof, with views |
| **Cathedral Museum** | Cloister, Treasury, Relics Chapel and Pazo de Xelmírez |

## Prices, hours and booking: Pórtico de la Gloria and rooftops

- **The church** opens every day **7:00-21:00** (entry through the Puerta de Platerías), free; in summer it closes at 20:30 on Saturdays for the pilgrims' vigil. The embrace of the Apostle and the crypt have queues of **15-40 minutes** in high season.
- **Pórtico de la Gloria:** paid visit in small groups, at the time on your ticket and **no photos**. In summer places sell out days ahead: book at visitas.catedraldesantiago.es or at the ticket offices on Praza do Obradoiro, or choose a tour that includes it.
- **Rooftops:** paid guided visit over the roof. Cancelled in rain or strong wind, both common in Santiago: book a day with spare time to try again.
- **Museum:** open every day **10:00-20:00**. Official prices change: check them on the cathedral website.

If you want to secure the Pórtico without fighting for places, there are guided tours that include it along with the old town.

## The botafumeiro: when it swings

The **botafumeiro** is the giant censer (over 50 kg) that eight *tiraboleiros* swing across the transept at 65 km/h. **It doesn't swing every day.** According to the cathedral, it swings on the major feast days:

- **6 January** (Epiphany), **Easter Sunday**, **Ascension**, **Pentecost** and **Christ the King**.
- **23 May** (Clavijo), **25 July** (Saint James), **15 August** (Assumption) and **1 November** (All Saints).
- **8, 25 and 30 December** (Translation of the Apostle).

The rest of the year it only swings when a pilgrim group pays for it, and this isn't announced in advance: hence so much disappointment. The official calendar is on the cathedral website; if your date matches, arrive an hour early.

## The Pilgrim's Mass (now in San Agustín)

This is the mass that marks the end of the Camino, every day at **12:00** and **19:30**. To get a seat, arrive **45 minutes early**; pilgrims with a freshly stamped Compostela have a reserved area. At the 12:00 mass they read out where that day's pilgrims set off from. There's no sightseeing during mass.

**Notice:** since **16 October 2026**, until further notice, the Pilgrim's Masses (9:30, 12:00 and 19:30) are held in the nearby **church of San Agustín**, due to works in the cathedral's main chapel. Only the 7:30 mass remains in the cathedral. Visits are not affected. The notice doesn't say whether the botafumeiro will swing during the works: check the cathedral website before planning around it.

## Tips and common mistakes

1. **Book the Pórtico and rooftops in high season:** they sell out.
2. **Don't confuse the free church with the paid visits:** they're different things.
3. **Don't assume you'll see the botafumeiro:** check the calendar.
4. **Don't arrive at mass on the dot:** it'll be full; go early and check where it's held.
5. **Leave large backpacks in left luggage:** you won't be let in. At the Pilgrim's Office (Rúa das Carretas) it's €3-4 a day.
6. **Dress respectfully:** it's a working church.
7. The **Holy Door** (Quintana) only opens in a **Holy Year**.
