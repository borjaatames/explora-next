The **Alhambra** is the most sought-after visit in Andalusia and from Málaga it's an easy day trip. The one thing you can't improvise is the **ticket**: the Nasrid Palaces are visited at a fixed time and in high season they sell out weeks ahead.

## Three ways to do it

| Option | Travel | Tickets | Approximate price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Own or hire car | 1 h 30 each way | You buy them on the official website | Fuel, parking and ticket |
| Regular bus | just under 2 h | You buy them | Fare and ticket |
| [Private trip](/en/cities/malaga/activities/granada-alhambra-private-day-trip-from-malaga) | Pickup from your accommodation | Included, with a private guide | €397 per person (minimum 2) |

If you arrive by cruise ship, there's a [shore excursion from Málaga port](/en/cities/malaga/activities/alhambra-granada-shore-excursion-malaga-port) designed to get you back to the ship on time.

## How to plan the day on your own

1. **Buy your ticket first** on the official website, choosing a Nasrid Palaces time around midday so you arrive with time to spare.
2. **Leave Málaga at 8 AM:** you reach Granada around 9:30.
3. **Alhambra:** about 3 hours (Alcazaba, Nasrid Palaces, Palace of Charles V and Generalife).
4. **Lunch and head down to the Albaicín** to see the Alhambra from the San Nicolás viewpoint.
5. **Return** mid-afternoon to avoid driving back in the dark.

## The private trip, in detail

About **9 hours**: pickup in Málaga, a **guided visit of about 3 hours** to the Alhambra with the general ticket (Nasrid Palaces, Alcazaba and Generalife), free time for lunch and a **guided walk through the Albaicín**. If you're staying in Granada, you can finish there. It's the most comfortable option if you don't want to fight for tickets or drive.
