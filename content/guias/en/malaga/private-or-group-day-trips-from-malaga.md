Coaches leave Málaga every day for Ronda, Nerja, Gibraltar or Córdoba, and almost all of those trips also exist **privately**: a car or van just for your group. The price gap looks huge when you look at the "from" price, but it changes a lot once you divide it among your group. This guide does the maths for you.

## What you gain privately

- **Pickup from your accommodation** in Málaga, no early trek to a meeting point.
- **Your own timing:** leave when you like and stop where you want.
- **Nobody else in the vehicle:** it makes a difference with small children, older travellers or when you want to go at your own pace.
- **Less waiting:** no collecting other travellers from several hotels.

## What you gain in a group

- **Price:** for 1 or 2 people it's 4 to 10 times cheaper.
- **A guide included** on most, whereas some private trips are driver only (check each page).
- **Company:** if you're travelling solo, you meet people.

## Our recommendation

1. **Solo or a couple:** group tour, unless you have a specific reason (reduced mobility, timing).
2. **3 or 4 of you:** do the maths. For Ronda or Córdoba, private works out at about €180-230 per person: more expensive, but a very different day.
3. **5 or more, or with small children:** private is usually the best option for comfort.
