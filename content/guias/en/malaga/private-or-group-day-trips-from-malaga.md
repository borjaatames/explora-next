## What you gain with a private tour

- **Pick-up from your accommodation** in Málaga, with no early trip to a meeting point.
- **Your own schedule:** leave when you like and stop wherever you fancy.
- **Nobody else in the vehicle:** it makes a difference with young children, older people or when you want to go at your own pace.
- **Less waiting:** no collecting other travellers from several hotels.

## What you gain in a group

- **Price:** for 1 or 2 people it's **between 4 and 10 times cheaper**.
- **Guide included** on most, whereas some private trips are driver only (check each listing).
- **Atmosphere:** if you're travelling solo, you meet people.
- **Trade-offs:** fixed schedule, meeting point and stops to pick up other travellers.

## Group or private: which to choose

1. **Travelling solo or as a couple:** a group trip, unless you have a specific reason (reduced mobility, timing).
2. **3 or 4 of you:** do the maths. For Ronda or Córdoba, private works out at around **€180-230 per person**: more expensive, but a very different day.
3. **5 or more, or with young children:** private is usually the best option for comfort.
4. **Older travellers or reduced mobility:** door-to-door pick-up and your own pace matter more than price.
