## Things to do in Ronda, stop by stop

**The Puente Nuevo.** It joins El Mercadillo (the newer part) with the old town over the gorge. From the top it's less impressive than you'd expect: you cross it in a minute and the parapet blocks the view. The good stuff is on either side: the **Aldehuela viewpoint**, next to the bridge and facing the Santo Domingo convent, shows the drop to the river; the **balconies of the Alameda del Tajo** look out over the mountains. Inside the bridge there's a small interpretation centre, an extra rather than a reason to go.

**The Real Maestranza bullring (1h).** Late 18th century and one of the oldest in Spain, with a stone ring and a double arcade. Even if bullfighting doesn't interest you, the building is worth it. If you'd like someone to tell you the story, there's a 2-hour private walking tour of the bullring and the Puente Nuevo (€200 for 1 or 2 people).

**The old town, La Ciudad (1h).** Cross the bridge and the noise drops by half. The **Mondragón Palace** (municipal museum, with Mudéjar courtyards) and the church of **Santa María la Mayor** (€4), built over the former mosque, are the two visits. The rest is wandering: Plaza Duquesa de Parcent and the streets running down to the bridges.

**The Casa del Rey Moro (1h).** The palace itself is still closed; you visit the **gardens** and the **"mine"**, a stairway cut into the rock, about 200 steps, down to the river. Going down is easy; coming back up isn't.

**The Arab Baths (30 min).** Among the best preserved in Spain, beside the Puente Árabe in the lower part of town.

**The Puente Nuevo from below.** From Plaza del Campillo a path leads down to the Arco del Cristo and the viewpoints at the bottom of the gorge: about 15–20 minutes down and a bit more back up, with dirt stretches. It's the image of Ronda you know, and you only get it from here. It matters more than any museum.

**If you have time left:** Setenil de las Bodegas, the village of houses under the rock, is about 20 minutes away by car.

## Bullring, Arab Baths and Casa del Rey Moro: prices and hours

- **Bullring:** **€10, or €12 with audio guide** (Real Maestranza website, rmcr.org). Open every day from 10:00: until 18:00 November to February, until 19:00 in March and October and until 20:00 April to September.
- **Arab Baths:** **€4.50**, free for under-14s. November to February, Tuesday to Friday 9:30 to 18:00; Monday and Saturday 10:00 to 14:00 and 15:00 to 18:00; **Sundays and holidays 10:00 to 15:00**.
- **Casa del Rey Moro:** open every day, 10:00 to 20:00 from October to April. According to its website, tickets are sold only at the ticket office.
- **Santa María la Mayor:** €4.

## Getting there

- **From Málaga:** 1h 30 to 1h 45 by car (about 100 km); about 2 hours by bus. By train there's a change at Antequera-Santa Ana and it isn't worth it. All the options, with prices, in [Ronda from Málaga](/en/guides/malaga/ronda-from-malaga).
- **From Seville:** about 2 hours by car; there's also a direct bus.
- **By car:** park in one of the car parks in El Mercadillo and forget about it. The old town isn't made for cars.
- **No car and no timetables:** the [group trip to Ronda and Setenil](/en/cities/malaga/activities/ronda-setenil-day-trip-from-malaga), from €34, or a private trip from Málaga.

## Where to eat

Ronda's cooking is mountain food, not coastal: **oxtail stew (rabo de toro)**, **lamb**, **cured meats** and **Serranía de Ronda wine**, from its own wineries. Steer clear of terraces with a bridge view and menus in five languages: you pay for the view and the food is average. Around **Plaza del Socorro** and in the streets of El Mercadillo there are tapas bars with local customers and normal prices.

## Common mistakes

1. **Arriving at midday.** The bridge is full of tour groups.
2. **Staying at the top.** Without the walk down into the gorge, you haven't really seen the Puente Nuevo.
3. **Leaving the Arab Baths for a Sunday afternoon.** They close at 15:00.
4. **Bringing a pushchair or wearing flip-flops.** Slopes, steps and dirt paths.
5. **Driving into the old town.** Narrow streets and no parking: park in El Mercadillo and walk across.
