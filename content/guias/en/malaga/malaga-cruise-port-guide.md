## Things to do in Malaga from a cruise ship: 4, 6 or 8 hours

| Hours ashore | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 4 hours | Alcazaba (€7), Roman Theatre from Calle Alcazabilla, Cathedral (€10) and Calle Larios; back along Muelle Uno |
| 6 hours | All of the above, plus Gibralfaro castle (combined ticket with the Alcazaba, €10), the Picasso Museum (€13) and tapas in the centre |
| 8 hours | Granada and the Alhambra on an excursion from the port, or Málaga at a slow pace with grilled sardines for lunch and the beach at La Malagueta |

- **Alcazaba:** 9:00 to 18:00 from November to March and until 20:00 from April to October; last entry one hour before closing.
- **Cathedral:** Monday to Friday 10:00 to 20:00 and Saturdays until 18:00.
- **With a guide:** there's a guided visit to the Alcazaba and Roman Theatre from €13 and the "Málaga esencial" route from €32.76. The **private Málaga esencial tour** costs €200 per group for 1 or 2 people (2 hours) and €280 for 4; you meet in Plaza de la Constitución, about 10 minutes' walk from the shuttle stop (no port pick-up).

## Where the ship docks

The port has three cruise terminals, all run by Cruceros Málaga:

- **Levante** (two terminals, four berths): the big ships, which means almost all of them. They're **2–2.5 km from Plaza de la Marina**.
- **Palmeral de las Sorpresas** (pier 2): small and luxury ships, one berth, about **5 minutes' walk from the Cathedral**.

Your daily programme gives the terminal. If it's Levante, as you leave the port area La Malagueta beach is on your left and the harbour promenade straight ahead.

## Malaga cruise port to city centre: shuttle bus, taxi or on foot

| Option | Time | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On foot | 25–30 min | Free |
| Shuttle to Plaza de la Marina | About 10 min | About €5 return |
| Taxi | About 10 min | Minimum €9.45 |
| Cruise line shuttle | About 10 min | Set by your cruise line |

- **Walking** is the best option in good weather: a flat stroll past La Farola, Muelle Uno and the Centre Pompidou, and the route is already part of the visit.
- **The port shuttle bus** links the Levante terminals with the pedestrian entrance at Plaza de la Marina **only while a ship is docked**. The price quoted by cruise lines is around €5 return: confirm it when you get off.
- **Taxis** have municipal rates: from the Levante Maritime Station the minimum fare is **€9.45**; on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays rate 2 applies (€2.27 flag fall and €1.25 per km). Worth it for 3 or 4 people.
- **Cruise line shuttle:** check the ship's app to see if it's free.

## Alhambra shore excursion from Malaga

Granada is **1h 30min by road** and the Alhambra visit takes about 3 hours. With the return trip, that's at least 6 hours, so **don't try it with less than 8 hours in port**.

- **Cruise line excursion:** the ship waits for its own excursions. The zero-risk option.
- **Shore excursion for cruise passengers:** 8 hours, pick-up and drop-off at the port, English-speaking guide and groups of up to 18. It costs **€119 per person**, but **the ticket isn't included**: right after booking, send the operator each traveller's name, passport and date of birth so they can get it for you (€23). It fits its length to the docking time and includes the operator's return-to-ship guarantee.
- **Private trip to Granada:** €397 per person (minimum 2), with tickets and a walk through the Albaicín, 9 hours. The listing mentions pick-up at your accommodation: write to the operator before booking with the ship's name and departure time.
- **On your own:** fine for Málaga, not for Granada. Without a time slot for the Nasrid Palaces, which sell out, the trip makes no sense.

## Back-on-board time, Sundays and holidays

- **"All aboard"** is the deadline to be on the ship, usually 30 to 60 minutes before sailing. It's in the daily programme and given in **ship's time**.
- **Rule of thumb:** on your own, be at the terminal **1 hour before all aboard**; if you rely on the shuttle, take it 30 minutes earlier still, as there are queues at the last minute.
- Carry your **ship card** and ID for port security, and note the port agent's phone number from the programme.

**If your port day falls on a Sunday or holiday:** the **Cathedral** only opens to visitors from 14:00 to 18:00 (and not during Mass); the **Alcazaba and Gibralfaro** are **free from 14:00**; the **Picasso Museum** is open (free for the last two hours; closed on 25 December and 1 and 6 January); **Atarazanas Market** is closed; and under the Cruise Shops scheme **more than 40 shops** in the centre, Soho and La Malagueta open, each with its own hours.

## Common mistakes

1. **Going to Granada on your own without a ticket** for the Nasrid Palaces.
2. **Assuming the ticket is included** in the shore excursion: you have to ask the operator for it.
3. **Waiting for the last shuttle.** Head back with an hour to spare.
4. **Walking up to Gibralfaro at midday in summer.** It's a steep climb: go up by taxi and walk down.
5. **Planning the Cathedral for a Sunday morning:** it doesn't open to visitors until 14:00.
