## What to choose, by who's travelling

- **Couple with a car:** go independently, with the official-guide ticket or the €10 general ticket if you find one on the official website.
- **Couple without a car or first-timers:** the group tour from Málaga, €72. It has more than 19,000 reviews averaging 4.9.
- **Family of 4:** group (€288 in total) or private with a guide (€856, €214 per person). The private option picks you up from your accommodation and the guide goes at your pace, which shows with children aged 8-12.
- **Group of 6:** private with a guide, €156 per person. Still double the group price, but with no coach schedule and no other travellers on the trail.
- **Tight budget:** independently by train or car, €12.50 per person for ticket and shuttle.

The route is always closed on Mondays and children under 8 aren't allowed. What it's like and what to bring is in [how to visit the Caminito del Rey](/en/guides/malaga/caminito-del-rey-how-to-visit).

## Caminito del Rey private tour price: from how many people does it pay off?

**Against the €72 group tour, never.** The extra cost per person of the cheapest private option is €233 for a couple (€305 with private transport), €142 for 4 and €84 for 6.

The interesting part is **within the private options**:

- **For 2 people**, the private-transport-only version (€305) is cheaper than the private-guide one (€396).
- **From 4 people**, it flips: with a private guide you pay €214 per person, versus €240 with private transport and a shared guide. For 6, €156 versus €180.

If you're paying for private as 4 or more, take the private-guide option: it's cheaper and more exclusive.

## Independent, group or private: pros and cons

### Independently

- **For:** the cheapest option and you go at your own time.
- **Against:** you have to get a ticket (it sells out weeks ahead in spring and autumn), organise the return by shuttle because the route is one-way, and get there by car or on a train with few departures (about 40-50 minutes to El Chorro).

### Group

- **For:** ticket included (the big advantage when the official website is sold out), official guide, coach from central Málaga and insurance for under-65s.
- **Against:** fixed departure point, coach timetable and language groups shared with other travellers.

### Private

- **For:** pick-up from your hotel or apartment, your own schedule, tickets included and, with the private-guide version, nobody else on the trail with you.
- **Against:** price for couples, and the private-transport-only version has a shared guide on the trail.

## Common mistakes

1. **Planning the trip and looking for tickets afterwards.** Ticket (or tour) first, everything else after.
2. **Forgetting the shuttle.** Without it you can't get back to your starting point.
3. **Paying for private transport as 4.** The private-guide option is cheaper.
4. **Bringing children under 8.** They can't enter, in a group or private.
5. **Going on a Monday.** It's closed; it can also close in strong wind or heavy rain.
