## Ronda private tour from Malaga

Pick-up at your accommodation and three options:

- **Ronda at your own pace:** from **€580 for 2**.
- **With a private guided tour:** **€720 for 2**.
- **Ronda and Setenil:** **€800 for 2**.

For 4 people it's from €720, about **€180 per person**, so from 4 people it beats the group tour. Full details on the [private tour page](/en/cities/malaga/activities/ronda-private-day-trip-from-malaga).

## What to see in Ronda in a day

- **The Puente Nuevo and the gorge**, from above and from the viewpoint below (the Camino de los Molinos).
- **La Ciudad**, the old town: the Mondragón Palace, the Town Hall square and the church of Santa María la Mayor.
- **The Real Maestranza bullring**, one of the oldest in Spain.
- **The Arab Baths**, next to the Puente Viejo.
- If you have time left, **Setenil de las Bodegas**, the village of houses under the rock, 20 minutes away.

If you go independently and want someone to tell you the story, there's a 2-hour private walking tour of Ronda (€200 for 2 people or €260 for 4). The full stop-by-stop route is in [Ronda in one day](/en/guides/malaga/ronda-in-one-day).

## Tips

1. **Leave early:** from 11:00 the coaches arrive and the bridge fills up.
2. **Comfortable shoes:** the old town's slopes and the walk down to the viewpoint are tiring.
3. **With kids, a baby carrier beats a pushchair:** the viewpoints are very high and the streets are steep.
4. **In summer, water and a hat:** Ronda is high up, but at midday in July it's hot.
