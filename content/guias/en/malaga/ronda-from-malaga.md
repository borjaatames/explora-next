**Ronda** is the most popular day trip from Málaga, and rightly so: a town perched over a gorge more than 100 metres deep, with the **Puente Nuevo** joining both sides. It's just under two hours away and there are four ways to go. This guide helps you choose.

## How to get from Málaga to Ronda

| Option | Time | Approximate price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Own or hire car | 1 h 45 | Fuel and parking | Anyone who wants freedom and a mountain drive |
| Bus | about 2 h | Regular route ticket | Travellers without a car and in no hurry |
| [Group tour](/en/cities/malaga/activities/ronda-setenil-day-trip-from-malaga) | 9 h in total | from €44 per person | Solo travellers and couples; includes Setenil |
| [Private trip](/en/cities/malaga/activities/ronda-private-day-trip-from-malaga) | 8 h in total | €580 (2) · €720 (4) | Families and groups who want their own pace |

There's no direct train: you have to change, and it takes longer than the bus.

## What to see in Ronda in a day

- **The Puente Nuevo and the gorge**, from above and from the viewpoint below (the Molinos path).
- **La Ciudad**, the old town: the Mondragón Palace, the town hall square and Santa María la Mayor church.
- **The bullring** of the Real Maestranza, one of the oldest in Spain.
- **The Arab Baths**, next to the Puente Viejo.
- If you have time, **Setenil de las Bodegas**, the village of houses under the rock, 20 minutes away.

If you go on your own and want someone to tell you the story, the [2-hour private walking tour of Ronda](/en/cities/malaga/activities/ronda-private-walking-tour-puente-nuevo) costs €200 for 2 people or €260 for 4.

## The private trip from Málaga, in detail

Pickup from your accommodation and three options: **Ronda on your own** (from €580 for 2), **with a private guided tour** (€720 for 2) or **Ronda and Setenil** (€800 for 2). For 4 people it's from €720, about **€180 per person**. Full details on the [private trip page](/en/cities/malaga/activities/ronda-private-day-trip-from-malaga).

## Tips

1. **Leave early:** from 11 AM the coaches arrive and the bridge gets crowded.
2. **Comfortable shoes:** the old-town slopes and the walk down to the viewpoint are tiring.
3. **In summer, water and a hat:** Ronda is high up, but at midday in July it's hot.
