## Malaga Christmas lights: Calle Larios show times

In 2025 the design was called **"Natividad de luz"** (Nativity of light): stars, columns and large medallions with nativity scenes along the whole street, more than **2.7 million LED points** in the centre and around 500 decorated streets in the neighbourhoods.

- **Shows with music:** **18:30, 20:30 and 22:00**, every day from 28 November to 4 January. On **24 and 31 December and 5 January** the lights are on, but there's no show.
- **Lighting hours:** 18:30 to midnight Sunday to Wednesday; until 2:00 Thursday to Saturday and on the eve of public holidays; on the 24th and 31st, until 6:00.
- **Cathedral mapping:** "El pescador de sueños" (The dream fisherman), 8 to 9 minutes, at **19:00, 20:30 and 22:00**. The easiest combo is the 18:30 show on Larios and the 19:00 one at the Cathedral.
- **The Alameda "forest":** the Alameda Principal is lit to look like a forest under the fig trees, and **Paseo del Parque** adds 14 arches and 36 light trees next to the market.

The 20:30 show on a Saturday is the busiest; the 18:30 one or a weekday is far more enjoyable.

## What to do in Malaga at Christmas: an evening done right

1. **17:30, Paseo del Parque:** the Christmas market in the last light.
2. **18:30, Calle Larios:** the first show, the least crowded.
3. **19:00, Cathedral:** the mapping, five minutes' walk along Calle Molina Lario.
4. **19:30, City Hall nativity scene** (open 17:00 to 21:00) or Calle San Juan, decorated with crystal lamps and garlands.
5. **20:30, tapas:** the centre has sweet-wine taverns and old-school barrel bodegas.

To avoid choosing bars, the **wine and tapas tour** visits four places with 8 tapas and 4 wines in three and a half hours, in English.

## New Year's Eve, Three Kings Parade and public holidays

- **Chimes in Plaza de la Constitución:** in 2025 bags of grapes and party favours were handed out from 21:30 to 23:45, while stocks lasted.
- **Parade (5 January):** the Kings arrive at the City Hall at 17:30 and the parade leaves at 18:00. The City Hall is next to Paseo del Parque: find a spot away from the start for a better view.

**Monuments on public holidays:**

- **Picasso Museum:** on 24 and 31 December and 5 January, 10:00 to 15:00; **closed on 25 December and 1 and 6 January**. Otherwise from November to February, 10:00 to 18:00; €13, free on Sundays for the last two hours.
- **Alcazaba and Gibralfaro Castle:** closed on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January. The rest of the winter they open 9:00 to 18:00 (checked on the official website on 10 November 2026).
- **Alcazaba:** November to March, 9:00 to 18:00 (last entry 17:00); €7, or €10 with Gibralfaro castle, and **free on Sundays from 14:00**.

## The beach in winter and a day trip

According to AEMET, December in Málaga has an **average high of 17.5 °C** and about 160 hours of sunshine, and January 16.8 °C. It's not swimming weather for most people, but it is for strolling along **La Malagueta and Pedregalejo**, grilled sardines at lunchtime and, on a clear day, a **catamaran trip around the bay** at sunset. For a chilly evening, the **Hammam Al Ándalus Arab baths** in the centre.

With a spare day, the **Alhambra** is heat-free in winter (there's a private trip from Málaga that includes the Albaicín). Closer, **Nerja and Frigiliana** make an easy group day trip.

## Common mistakes

- **Going on the 24th or 31st to see the Larios show:** there's no music those nights.
- **Turning up for the 20:30 show on a Saturday:** it's the most crowded.
- **Driving into the centre in December:** the car parks around Larios fill up every evening. Walk, or take the bus or metro.
- **Assuming everything opens on the 25th, 1st and 6th:** the Picasso Museum is closed all three days, and the Alcazaba and Gibralfaro also close on the 24th, 25th, 31st and 1st.
