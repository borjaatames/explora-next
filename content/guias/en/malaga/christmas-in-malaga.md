## The Calle Larios lights: show times

Malaga puts on one of the most photographed Christmas displays in Spain. In 2025 the design was called **Natividad de luz**: stars, columns and huge medallions with nativity scenes along the whole street, more than 2.7 million LED points in the centre and about 500 decorated streets in the neighbourhoods.

- **Showings with music:** 18:30, 20:30 and 22:00, every day from 28 November to 4 January. On **24 and 31 December and 5 January** the lights come on but there's no show.
- **Lighting hours:** 18:30 to midnight Sunday to Wednesday; until 2:00 Thursday to Saturday and on the eve of holidays; until 6:00 on the 24th and 31st.
- **Cathedral projection:** El pescador de sueños, 8 to 9 minutes long, at 19:00, 20:30 and 22:00. You can chain it with the 20:30 Larios show if you move fast, but it's easier to catch the 18:30 on Larios and the 19:00 at the cathedral.
- **The Alameda "forest":** the Alameda Principal is lit to look like a forest under its ficus trees, and the **Paseo del Parque** adds 14 arches and 36 light trees next to the market.

## A December evening, done right

1. **17:30, Paseo del Parque:** the Christmas market in the last of the light.
2. **18:30, Calle Larios:** the first showing, the least crowded.
3. **19:00, the cathedral:** the projection, five minutes' walk up Calle Molina Lario.
4. **19:30, the City Hall nativity scene** (open 17:00 to 21:00) or Calle San Juan, decorated with crystal chandeliers and garlands.
5. **20:30, tapas:** the centre is full of old sweet-wine taverns and barrel bodegas.

If you don't want to pick bars yourself, the **wine and tapas tour** covers four places with 8 tapas and 4 wines in three and a half hours, in English. For families or anyone who prefers their own group, the **private tapas tour** starts at the Atarazanas market.

## With kids

- **Alice Christmas** at La Concepción Botanical Garden, on the edge of town: an illuminated trail from 18:30 to 22:30. Tickets are sold on lucesdelaconcepcion.es.
- **The city nativity scene** in the City Hall courtyard, plus a **guide to 73 nativity scenes** around town at belenes.malaga.eu.
- **Giant Kings:** in 2025 Melchior stood on Calle Alcazabilla, Caspar on Plaza Félix Sáenz and Balthazar on Calle Compañía.
- **A children's party with puppets** and storytelling in December, as part of the city programme.

## New Year's Eve and Three Kings

- **Midnight on Plaza de la Constitución:** in 2025 bags of grapes and party favours were handed out from 21:30 to 23:45, while stocks lasted.
- **Three Kings Parade (5 January):** the Kings reach City Hall at 17:30 and the parade sets off at 18:00. City Hall stands by the Paseo del Parque: find a spot well away from the start for a better view.

## The beach in winter

According to AEMET, December in Malaga has an **average high of 17.5 °C** and about 160 hours of sunshine, and January 16.8 °C. It's not swimming weather for most people, but it's perfect for a stroll along **La Malagueta and Pedregalejo**, sardine skewers at lunchtime and, on a clear day, a **catamaran trip round the bay** at sunset. After the beach, the **Hammam Al Ándalus** Arab baths in the centre are the cure for a chilly evening.

## Common mistakes

- **Going to see the Larios show on the 24th or 31st:** there's no music those nights.
- **Turning up for the 20:30 showing on a Saturday:** it's the busiest. The 18:30 one, or any weekday, is far more enjoyable.
- **Driving into the centre in December:** the car parks around Larios fill up every evening. Walk, or take the bus or metro.
- **Assuming everything opens on the 25th, 1st and 6th:** the Museo Picasso is closed on all three.
