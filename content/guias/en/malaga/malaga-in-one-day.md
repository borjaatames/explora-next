## The route, hour by hour

| Time | What | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:00 | Alcazaba | €7 (€10 with Gibralfaro) |
| 10:15 | Climb to Gibralfaro | Included in the combined ticket |
| 11:30 | Roman Theatre, outside or inside | Free |
| 12:00 | Cathedral | €10 with audio guide |
| 13:30 | Atarazanas Market and lunch | Whatever you eat |
| 15:30 | Picasso Museum and Plaza de la Merced | €13 |
| 17:30 | Muelle Uno and Paseo del Parque | Free |
| 18:30 | Sunset and grilled sardines at La Malagueta | €10–15 |

These are winter hours (November to March). In summer everything opens later in the afternoon and sunset comes around 21:30: stretch out lunch and leave the Picasso for when the heat drops.

The classic mistake is doing it backwards: arriving at 11:00, starting with the Cathedral and climbing to Gibralfaro at two in the afternoon. If you have two days, see [Málaga in two days](/en/guides/malaga/malaga-in-two-days).

## What to see in Malaga, stop by stop

**The Alcazaba (1h 15min).** The best-preserved Andalusi palace-fortress in Spain: courtyards, fountains and terraced gardens climbing the hillside. At 9:00 it's almost empty. If you'd like someone to tell the story, the [guided visit to the Alcazaba and Roman Theatre](/en/cities/malaga/activities/alcazaba-roman-theatre-malaga-guided-tour) costs from €13 with the ticket.

**Gibralfaro (45 min).** Walk up the path along the wall, about **20–30 minutes uphill with no shade**, or take **bus 35**. There's little to see inside; what you pay for is the view: the port, the bullring, La Malagueta and the whole bay.

**The Roman Theatre (20 min).** At the foot of the Alcazaba, visible from Calle Alcazabilla.

**The Cathedral (1h).** "La Manquita" (the one-armed lady), because it lacks a tower. The **roof can't be visited**: it's under works until the end of 2027. There's a skip-the-line guided tour from €24 and a pack combining Cathedral, Alcazaba and Theatre with tickets.

**The Picasso Museum (1h 30min).** More than 200 works in the Buenavista palace. Buy the [ticket with audio guide](/en/cities/malaga/activities/picasso-museum-malaga-ticket) for a set time; if it's sold out, we explain what to do in [Picasso Museum: tickets sold out](/en/guides/malaga/picasso-museum-malaga-tickets-sold-out).

**Muelle Uno and La Malagueta.** The port turned promenade, with the Pompidou's Cube at the end, and ten minutes further on the city beach. It's the natural end of the day. If you'd rather watch the sunset from the water, the catamaran around the bay starts at €13.

**What we'd leave out in a single day:** the Pompidou, the Carmen Thyssen and Picasso's Birthplace. They're good, but they don't fit without cutting the above.

## Malaga Alcazaba tickets and the day's opening hours

- **Alcazaba and Gibralfaro:** **€7 each or €10 combined** (reduced €3 and €5) on the official website, alcazabaygibralfaro.malaga.eu. 9:00 to 18:00 from November to March and until 20:00 from April to October; last entry one hour before.
- **Roman Theatre:** the interpretation centre is free, open Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 18:00 and Sundays until 16:00, and **closed on Mondays**.
- **Cathedral:** **€10 with audio guide**. Monday to Friday 10:00 to 20:00, Saturdays until 18:00 and **Sundays only 14:00 to 18:00**.
- **Picasso Museum:** **€13**, free for under-17s and for everyone on Sundays for the last two hours. 10:00 to 18:00 from November to February; until 19:00 from March to June and in September and October; until 20:00 in July and August.

## Malaga cruise port day

With a **6–8 hour** stop, do the route as far as the Picasso and head back along Muelle Uno, which is on the way to the terminal. With **4 hours**, stick to the Alcazaba, Theatre and Cathedral. Timings from each terminal are in our [Málaga cruise port guide](/en/guides/malaga/malaga-cruise-port-guide).

## Where to eat

- **Atarazanas Market:** stalls with fried fish, anchovies and marinated olives. Open in the morning (until about 15:00) and **closed on Sundays**: if you're there on a Sunday, change the plan.
- **Around Plaza de la Merced and Carretería:** bar-counter taverns with neighbourhood prices, ten minutes from the Picasso.
- **La Malagueta, in the evening:** grilled sardine skewers (espetos) at a beach bar. Simple, and the most Málaga thing you'll eat.

Avoid the terraces with laminated menus opposite the Cathedral and on Calle Larios. More ideas in [eating in Málaga](/en/guides/malaga/eating-in-malaga).

## Tips and common mistakes

1. **Climbing to Gibralfaro at midday in summer.** Go first thing or take bus 35.
2. **Going to the Cathedral on a Sunday morning.** It doesn't open to visitors until 14:00.
3. **Visiting the Roman Theatre on a Monday.** The interpretation centre is closed; you can see it just as well from Calle Alcazabilla, but you can't go in.
4. **Counting on the Cathedral roof.** It can't be visited until the works finish.
5. **Squeezing the Caminito del Rey or Ronda into the same day.** It doesn't fit: that's what [day trips from Málaga](/en/guides/malaga/day-trips-from-malaga) are for.
