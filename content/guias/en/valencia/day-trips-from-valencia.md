## La Albufera and El Palmar

Twenty minutes from the centre by car (or about 45 minutes on EMT bus 24 or 25 from Plaza de la Reina), the **Albufera natural park** is a large lagoon ringed by rice paddies and traditional barracas, where paella was born. Take a **boat ride** among the rice fields at sunset —one of the loveliest plans near the city— and eat a **real Valencian paella** in **El Palmar**, the rice capital. A perfect half day. We link it to day 2 of [Valencia in 2 days](/en/guides/valencia/valencia-in-2-days).

## Sagunto

Half an hour by train or car, **Sagunto** crowns a hill with its **castle** of Iberian, Roman and Moorish walls, and keeps a **Roman theatre** still in use. The views over the farmland and the sea are magnificent. Half a day.

## Xàtiva

Forty-five minutes by train, **Xàtiva** has one of the most spectacular castles in the region: a double fortress on the ridge, with an old town of fountains and palaces. Birthplace of the Borgia family. A relaxed day.

## Peñíscola

An hour and a half away, on the **Costa del Azahar**, **Peñíscola** is a walled town on a peninsula, with **Pope Luna's castle** (a *Game of Thrones* location) and a beach at its feet. It combines monument and a swim: a perfect day in summer.

## Requena and the wine route

An hour away, **Requena** is wine and cava country, with a medieval old quarter (La Villa) and century-old wineries to visit. Ideal for wine lovers.

## With kids or on a hot day: the San José Caves

In La Vall d'Uixó, north of Sagunto, the **San José Caves** hold the longest navigable underground river in Europe, visited by small boat. Kids usually love it, and it's cool when the castles are baking. The cave has steps and low ceilings. Without a car, the organised half day includes transport from central Valencia.

## Practical: trains, prices and timings

- **Cercanías from Estació del Nord:** Sagunto, 30 minutes (line C-6, every 20–30 minutes, about €4); Xàtiva, 45 minutes (C-2, every 30 minutes, €5–6); Gandía, 1 hour (C-1). The 10-trip pass works out cheaper than singles, and under-6s travel free.
- **La Albufera:** EMT buses 24 and 25 to El Palmar (45 minutes, €1.50). Boats leave from the village jetties: the standard ride lasts 40–45 minutes, costs about €5 per adult and needs no booking. The sunset trip (about an hour, around €7) is the one everyone wants, so book it or arrive early.
- **Sagunto Castle:** free; open 10:00–20:00 in summer (18:00 in winter), closed on Mondays. It's a 30-minute walk up from the station. The Roman theatre next to it is also free.
- **Xàtiva Castle:** €6, a 30-minute climb from the old town or a tourist train (€4). Closed on Mondays. The climb at midday in summer is tough: go at 10:00.
- **Peñíscola:** 1 h 30 by car on the AP-7; Pope Luna's castle costs €5, and in August the walled town is jammed from 11:00. Without a car, take the train to Benicarló-Peñíscola (1 h 30), then a bus or taxi for the last 7 km.
- **Requena:** 1 hour by car. Wineries (Murviedro, Pago de Tharsys, Hispano Suizas) open by appointment only, €10–25 with a tasting. The cellars under Requena's old town cost €4. There's a regional train from Valencia (1 h 10), but the wineries are outside town.

## Getting around

Sagunto, Xàtiva and Gandía work well **by local train**, and La Albufera by bus. For Peñíscola, Requena or inland villages, a **car** gives more freedom.

## What we don't recommend

1. **Skipping La Albufera.** It's the closest trip and the one that sets Valencia apart.
2. **Trying two far-off trips on the same day.** Pick one and enjoy it calmly.
3. **Going to Peñíscola without counting the road time.** An hour and a half each way; leave early.

## Common mistakes

> **⚠️ Warning:** the slip-ups we see most.

1. **Not booking a table in El Palmar** at weekends, or turning up for the sunset boat without a booking (it's the first to fill up).
2. **Forgetting that many castles close at midday or on Mondays.** Check the hours.
3. **Underestimating the summer heat** at shadeless castles (Sagunto, Xàtiva); go first thing.

## In one sentence

From Valencia, within an hour you have La Albufera and its paella, Roman and Moorish castles in Sagunto and Xàtiva, and a beach with history in Peñíscola: day trips for every taste without going far.
