## The best day trips from Valencia

| Destination | Distance | Ideal for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| La Albufera and El Palmar | 20 min | Paella, boat and rice fields |
| Sagunto | 30 min | Castle and Roman theatre |
| Xàtiva | 45 min | Double castle and old town |
| Requena | 1 h | Wine, cava and medieval old town |
| Gandía / Dénia | 1 h | Northern Costa Blanca beaches |
| Peñíscola | 1 h 30 | Pope Luna's castle and the beach |

The closest and most essential is **La Albufera**. The rest you choose by taste: history (Sagunto, Xàtiva), a beach with a castle (Peñíscola) or wine (Requena). Pick **one far-off trip per day** and enjoy it calmly.

## La Albufera and El Palmar: boat and paella

The **Albufera natural park** is a large lagoon ringed by rice paddies and traditional barracas, the land where paella was born. It's 20–25 minutes from the centre by car.

- **Getting there:** **EMT buses 24 and 25** from Plaza de la Reina to El Palmar (about 45 minutes, **€1.50**; roughly every 25–35 minutes since June 2026).
- **Boat ride:** boats leave from the village jetties. The standard ride lasts 40–45 minutes, costs **about €5** per adult and needs no booking. The **sunset** trip (about an hour, **around €7**) is the one everyone wants: book it or arrive early.
- **Paella:** eat a **real Valencian paella** in **El Palmar**, the rice capital. At weekends, book a table.

A perfect half day. We link it to day 2 of [Valencia in 2 days](/en/guides/valencia/valencia-in-2-days).

## Sagunto and Xàtiva: castles a short train ride away

**Sagunto**, half an hour away, crowns a hill with its **castle** of Iberian, Roman and Moorish walls, and keeps a **Roman theatre** still in use. The views over the farmland and the sea are magnificent.

- Cercanías line **C-6** from Estació del Nord: 30 minutes, every 20–30 minutes, **about €4**.
- **Free castle**, and the Roman theatre is free too. Open 10:00–20:00 in summer (18:00 in winter), **closed on Mondays**. It's a 30-minute walk up from the station.
- Half a day.

**Xàtiva** (Játiva), 45 minutes away, has one of the most spectacular castles in the region: a **double fortress** on the ridge, above an old town of fountains and palaces. It's the birthplace of the Borgia family.

- Cercanías line **C-2**: 45 minutes, every 30 minutes, **€5–6**.
- **Castle: €6** (free on Tuesdays). Closed on Mondays. A 30-minute climb from the old town, or a tourist train for €4.
- A relaxed day. In summer, start the climb at 10:00.

The 10-trip Cercanías pass works out cheaper than singles, and under-6s travel free.

## Peñíscola and Requena: coast and wine

**Peñíscola**, on the **Costa del Azahar**, is a walled town on a peninsula, with **Pope Luna's castle** (a *Game of Thrones* location) and a beach at its feet. It combines monument and a swim: a perfect day in summer.

- **1 h 30 by car** on the AP-7. Without a car, take the train to Benicarló-Peñíscola (1 h 30), then a bus or taxi for the last 7 km.
- **Castle: €5**. In August the walled town is jammed from 11:00: leave early.

**Requena**, an hour away, is wine and cava country, with a medieval old quarter (La Villa) and century-old wineries.

- Wineries (Murviedro, Pago de Tharsys, Hispano Suizas) open **by appointment only**, **€10–25** with a tasting.
- The cellars under Requena's old town cost **€4**.
- There's a regional train (1 h 10), but the wineries are outside town: better by car.

## With kids or on a hot day: the San José Caves

In La Vall d'Uixó, north of Sagunto, the **San José Caves** hold the **longest navigable underground river in Europe**, visited by small boat. Kids usually love it, and it's cool inside when the castles are baking. There are steps and low ceilings. Without a car, the organised half day includes transport from central Valencia.

## Getting around and mistakes to avoid

Sagunto, Xàtiva and Gandía (Cercanías C-1, 1 hour) work well **by train**, and La Albufera by bus. For Peñíscola, Requena or inland villages, a **car** gives more freedom.

- **Don't skip La Albufera:** it's the closest trip and the one that sets Valencia apart.
- **Don't chain two far-off trips** on the same day.
- **Count the road time to Peñíscola:** an hour and a half each way.
- **Check opening hours:** many castles close at midday or on Mondays.
- **Mind the summer heat** at shadeless castles (Sagunto, Xàtiva): go first thing.
- **Book** a table in El Palmar at weekends, and the sunset boat.
