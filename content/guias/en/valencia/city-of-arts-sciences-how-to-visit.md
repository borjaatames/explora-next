## What each building in the City of Arts and Sciences is

You don't need to go inside everything: the complex, with its pools reflecting the white buildings, is free and one of the most photogenic places in Spain.

| Building | What it is | Worth going in? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oceanogràfic | Europe's largest aquarium | Yes, the must-see |
| Science Museum (Museu de les Ciències) | Hands-on "do touch" museum | Yes, with kids or if you're curious |
| Hemisfèric | IMAX cinema and planetarium | Depends on the session and age |
| Palau de les Arts | Opera and concerts | Only for a performance |
| Umbracle | Garden walkway with sculptures | Free, a stroll |
| Àgora / Pont de l'Assut | Structures and bridge | Outside only |

## Oceanogràfic, Science Museum and Hemisfèric: how to plan your visit

- **Outside (free):** wander among the buildings and through the **Umbracle**, the raised garden with palms and sculptures. At sunset, the reflections in the water make the best photo. This alone justifies the visit.
- **Oceanogràfic (3-4 hours):** sharks, belugas, penguins, the underwater tunnel, the dolphinarium and the underwater restaurant. Go **first thing**. Dolphin shows run at fixed times, three or four a day: check the board when you arrive.
- **Science Museum (2-3 hours):** three floors where you touch everything. It works very well with children from 5-6 and with curious adults.
- **Hemisfèric (45-50 minutes):** IMAX dome cinema and planetarium. Check the programme: there are children's sessions and documentaries. If you're short of time, it's the easiest to skip.

A good order: the Oceanogràfic in the morning and the museum or Hemisfèric after lunch.

## Oceanogràfic tickets and prices: how not to overpay

- **Oceanogràfic:** about **€40** per adult online and **€30** for children aged 4 to 12 (free for under-4s); a little more at the ticket office.
- **Science Museum** and **Hemisfèric:** **€9** each.
- **Combined tickets:** all three is around **€50**, and Oceanogràfic plus Hemisfèric €45. If you're going into two, the combined ticket always pays off.

Depending on your plan:

- **Architecture only:** free, don't buy anything.
- **With kids:** the Oceanogràfic, Science Museum and Hemisfèric combo.
- **Adults with time:** the Oceanogràfic on its own, plus the Science Museum if you like science.

Combined tickets sell out in high season and the Oceanogràfic ticket office has long queues: **buy online** and choose the first slot.

## Opening hours and getting there

- **Oceanogràfic:** opens at 10:00 and closes at 18:00 in winter and 20:00 in summer (some days in July and August, until midnight).
- **Science Museum:** 10:00 to 19:00-21:00.
- **All of them open on Mondays.**
- **Bus:** lines 95 (from Plaza del Ayuntamiento), 35 and 15, for €1.50.
- **Metro:** Alameda (L3 and L5), a 20-minute walk.
- **Bike:** by Valenbisi, 15 minutes through the Turia from the centre, with a docking station at the door.
- **Car:** the Umbracle underground car park costs €7-8 a day.

## Tips

- **Don't buy tickets to everything:** you'll be worn out. Prioritise the Oceanogràfic.
- **Don't go at midday in summer:** there's no shade between the buildings and the white is dazzling. Bring sunglasses and a cap, and save the Science Museum or Hemisfèric for the hottest hours in July and August.
- **Don't allow too little time:** many people book two hours for the Oceanogràfic and leave feeling they haven't seen it.
- **Check the Hemisfèric programme** before buying; most sessions don't admit under-3s.
- **Food:** inside the Oceanogràfic there's an underwater restaurant (expensive) and a self-service, and you can bring your own food (there are picnic areas). Outside, the Umbracle bars and, 10 minutes away, those on Calle Menorca.
- **With a pushchair:** no problem anywhere on site, and the Oceanogràfic lends them free.

It fits perfectly into the afternoon of your [first day in Valencia](/en/guides/valencia/valencia-in-2-days).
