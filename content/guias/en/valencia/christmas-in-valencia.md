## Valencia Christmas lights and fireworks on Plaza del Ayuntamiento

In 2025 Valencia switched on Christmas with a piromusical, live music and a **25-metre tree** on Plaza del Ayuntamiento, next to the city nativity scene. Around the city:

- More than **300 light arches** and 60 sparkling arches on **Calle de la Paz**.
- A giant kite by the **Torres de Serranos** and an illuminated nativity scene on Calle del Hospital.
- A walk-through sphere and bell on Plaza de Segrelles and Plaza del Patriarca.

The most Valencian touch is the **Saturday piromusicales at 18:30**: three minutes of fireworks set to a song, free. Get there ten minutes early. In 2025 city-centre shopkeepers also set up an ice rink, carousel, train and Ferris wheel between Plaza del Ayuntamiento and Plaza de San Agustín.

## Valencia Christmas markets and nativity scenes

- **Christmas Craft Fair (Plaza de la Reina):** 30 wooden stalls at the foot of the Cathedral. In 2025, 10:30–14:00 and 16:30–21:00; on the 24th and 31st, mornings only.
- **City-centre market (Calle Hospital and Avenida del Oeste):** the traditional popular market, with nativity figures, decorations and toys, 9:00–22:00. In 2025 it stayed open until 2:00 on Christmas Eve and until 5:00 on the night before Three Kings.
- **Ciutat de les Arts:** a Christmas market with 50 stalls by the Umbracle.
- **Mercado de Colón:** the nativity figure market in early December and a craft show from mid-December to Three Kings, in a modernista market worth a visit in itself.
- **Nativity scenes:** besides the city one, in 2025 the **Bancaja monumental nativity scene**, with more than 1,000 figures, returned to Valencia after 30 years, on Plaza de Tetuán.

## With kids: Expojove 2026 and more

- **Expojove** is the big plan: ten days of games, workshops and play areas at Feria València, from **26 December 2026 to 4 January 2027**, 11:00–20:00, **from €3**. On the 31st it opens only 10:00–14:00, and on 1 January 16:00–21:00. Get there by metro or bus 62.
- **Saturday piromusical:** three minutes, not too crowded if you arrive a little early.
- **Ciutat de les Arts i les Ciències:** extends hours and activities over Christmas and publishes its calendar on cac.es. The Science Museum and Hemisfèric closed on 25 December in 2025.

## New Year's Eve and the Three Kings Parade in Valencia

- **Chimes on Plaza del Ayuntamiento:** in 2025 there were children's chimes in the afternoon and a party at night.
- **The Kings' arrival (5 January):** in 2026 they came ashore at **16:30** at Plaza de la Sal in the Marina Norte.
- **The parade:** at **17:30** from Puente de las Flores, along Porta de la Mar, General Tovar, Calle de la Paz, Plaza de la Reina and San Vicente to Plaza del Ayuntamiento. Calle de la Paz is narrow: for a better view, stand around **Porta de la Mar**.

## Things to do in Valencia at Christmas: an evening plan and tips

A December evening, done right:

1. **17:00, Plaza de la Reina:** the craft fair and the Cathedral in the late-afternoon light.
2. **17:45, Calle de la Paz:** the sparkling arches, after dark.
3. **18:30, Plaza del Ayuntamiento:** the piromusical if it's Saturday, the tree and the nativity scene.
4. **19:15, Mercado de Colón:** horchata or hot chocolate under the modernista ironwork.
5. **20:00, tapas in Ruzafa or El Carmen.** The evening tapas tour takes you to four neighbourhood bars, with around 10–12 dishes and local drinks, in groups of up to 10.

Mistakes worth avoiding:

- **Reaching the piromusical at 18:35:** it's over; it lasts three minutes.
- **Visiting the Silk Exchange on the 25th, 1st or 6th:** it's closed on all three days. Otherwise it opens Monday to Saturday 10:00–19:00, and Sundays and holidays 10:00–14:00.
- **Buying Expojove tickets for the afternoon of the 31st:** it closes at 14:00 that day.
- **Parking in the centre on 5 January:** roads close from mid-afternoon between the Marina and Plaza del Ayuntamiento. Take the metro.
