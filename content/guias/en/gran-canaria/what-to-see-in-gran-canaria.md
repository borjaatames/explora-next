**Gran Canaria is much more than its southern beaches.** A round island barely 50 kilometres across packs in a desert of dunes, pine forests at almost 2,000 metres, ravines with cave houses and a capital with one of the best-preserved colonial old towns in the Canaries. That's why it's called a **miniature continent**. The most common mistake is never leaving Maspalomas: the interior is less than an hour away and it's what makes the island unique.

## How many days you need

With **four or five days** you'll see both sides: one for **Las Palmas and Vegueta**, one for the **summits** (Roque Nublo, Tejeda, Pico de las Nieves), one for the **north** (Teror, Arucas, Agaete) and the rest for beach, sea and Puerto de Mogán. With three days, choose between interior and capital; with a week, add the wild west coast and a ravine such as Guayadeque.

## The south: dunes, beaches and Puerto de Mogán

The **Maspalomas Dunes** are the island's icon: a nature reserve of more than 400 hectares of sand by the sea, with La Charca lagoon and the lighthouse behind. Next door are **Playa del Inglés** and **Meloneras**, the big sun-and-beach resorts. Further west, **Puerto de Mogán** is the prettiest fishing village: canals, white houses with bougainvillea and a harbour made for sunset. In between, **Puerto Rico** is where the dolphin-watching boats leave from.

> **⭐ Tip:** walk the dunes at sunrise or late afternoon. At midday the sand burns and the light is harsh for photos. Everything is in our [Maspalomas Dunes guide](/en/guides/gran-canaria/maspalomas-dunes-guide).

## The summits: Roque Nublo and Tejeda

The centre of the island is another world. **Roque Nublo**, a monolith of around 80 metres at more than 1,800 m, is reached by a short trail; next to it is **Pico de las Nieves** (1,949 m), the island's highest point, and on clear days you can see Tenerife's Teide. The village of **Tejeda** and **Cruz de Tejeda** are the perfect base for lunch and views of **Roque Bentayga**. Nearby, **Artenara** and **Risco Caído** are a World Heritage Site for their aboriginal caves. Read our [Roque Nublo hiking guide](/en/guides/gran-canaria/roque-nublo-hike).

Heading back south, the **Fataga ravine** (the valley of a thousand palms) and the **Chira reservoir** are the setting for the buggy and jeep trips.

## Las Palmas: Vegueta and Las Canteras

The capital deserves at least a day. **Vegueta**, the founding district (1478), has **Santa Ana Cathedral**, the **Casa de Colón** and cobbled streets with wooden balconies. On the other side of the city, **Las Canteras** beach has about 3 km of sand sheltered by a natural reef, La Barra, ideal for swimming and snorkeling right in town.

## The north and east: towns and ravines

**Teror**, with the Basílica del Pino and its balconies; **Arucas**, with its neo-Gothic church and banana plantations; **Firgas** and its tiled promenade; and **Agaete**, with Puerto de las Nieves and the only valley in Europe where coffee is grown. In the east, the **Guayadeque ravine** hides houses and even a chapel carved into the rock.

## Getting around

**Buses** (Global) connect Las Palmas, the airport and the south very well. For the interior and the northern towns, it's practical to **hire a car** or book a tour: mountain roads are narrow and winding, and parking at Roque Nublo is limited.

## Where to stay

**Maspalomas, Playa del Inglés and Meloneras** for sun and beach; **Puerto de Mogán** for something quieter and charming; **Las Palmas** for city life, food and easy trips north. The interior has rural houses in Tejeda or Artenara for sleeping under the stars.

## Eating in Gran Canaria

Try **papas arrugadas with mojo**, **queso de flor de Guía**, **sancocho canario** (salted fish with potatoes and mojo), **gofio** and, for dessert, Tejeda's **bienmesabe**. To drink, **Arucas rum** and Monte Lentiscal wines.

## What we DON'T recommend

1. **Never leaving the south.** The interior is what sets the island apart.
2. **Going up to the summits without a jacket.** It can be 25 °C on the beach and 8 °C on Pico de las Nieves.
3. **Leaving the paths in the dunes.** It's a nature reserve and it's forbidden.
4. **Going to Roque Nublo at midday at the weekend.** The car park fills up; go early.

## In one sentence

Gran Canaria is dunes, summits above the clouds, a colonial capital and balconied towns: in four or five days you see a whole continent without leaving the island.
