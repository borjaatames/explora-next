## How many days: Gran Canaria in 4 days (or 5)

With **four or five days** you see both sides of the island:

- **One day in the south:** Maspalomas Dunes and Puerto de Mogán.
- **One day in the summits:** Roque Nublo, Pico de las Nieves and Tejeda.
- **One day in Las Palmas:** Vegueta and Las Canteras.
- **One day in the north:** Teror, Arucas and Agaete.
- **The rest** for beach and sea.

With **three days**, choose between the interior and the capital. With **a week**, add the wild west coast and the Guayadeque ravine.

## The south: Maspalomas Dunes and Puerto de Mogán

The **Maspalomas Dunes**, a nature reserve of more than 400 hectares by the sea with La Charca and the lighthouse in the background, are the island's icon. Walk them at sunrise or late in the day: at midday the sand burns, and you may only walk on the marked trails. More in the [Maspalomas Dunes guide](/en/guides/gran-canaria/maspalomas-dunes-guide).

- **Playa del Inglés** and **Meloneras**, next to the dunes: promenades, beach and restaurants.
- **Puerto de Mogán**, to the west: the prettiest fishing village, with canals, white houses draped in bougainvillea and postcard sunsets.
- **Puerto Rico**, where the dolphin-watching boats leave from. The Puerto Rico-Mogán boat costs €10-12.
- **Palmitos Park** (€36), the big family day out in the south.

## The summits: Roque Nublo and Tejeda

Another world, an hour and a half from the south:

- **Roque Nublo:** an 80-metre monolith at over 1,800 m, reached by a short trail. Read the [Roque Nublo hiking guide](/en/guides/gran-canaria/roque-nublo-hike).
- **Pico de las Nieves** (1,949 m): on clear days you can see Mount Teide.
- **Tejeda and Cruz de Tejeda:** the base for lunch and views of **Roque Bentayga**.
- **Artenara and Risco Caído**, a World Heritage Site for its Aboriginal caves.
- **Fataga ravine and Chira reservoir**, on the way down to the south, the setting for buggy and jeep routes.

## Vegueta, Las Palmas and the north of the island

**Vegueta**, the founding district of Las Palmas (1478), brings together **Santa Ana cathedral**, **Casa de Colón** (€4, free on Sundays) and cobbled streets with wooden balconies. On the other side of the city, **Las Canteras**: 3 km of sand sheltered by the La Barra reef, for swimming and snorkelling right in the centre.

**The north:**

- **Teror**, with the Basílica del Pino and its Sunday morning market (chorizo de untar spreadable sausage and cheese).
- **Arucas**, with its neo-Gothic church, banana plantations and rum (you can [tour the Arehucas distillery](/en/cities/gran-canaria/activities/arehucas-rum-distillery-tour-tasting)).
- **Firgas** and **Agaete**, with Puerto de las Nieves and the only valley in Europe where coffee is grown.

**The east:** the **Guayadeque** ravine, with houses and a chapel carved into the rock.

## Getting around Gran Canaria

- **Car:** essential for the interior and the north. Book weeks ahead in winter (the Canaries' high season), go for a small car (the GC-60, GC-600 and GC-210 are narrow with tight bends) and fill up before heading up: there are no petrol stations at the top.
- **Distances from Maspalomas:** Las Palmas, 45 min on the motorway; Mogán, 25 min; Tejeda and Roque Nublo, 1 h 30; Teror, 1 h 15. The interior is measured in hours, not kilometres.
- **Buses:** line 30 links Las Palmas and Maspalomas in 50 minutes (every 20 min, about €7); line 1 runs along the coast to Mogán; to the interior, lines 18 and 305, with few departures: check the return time before going up.
- **Free:** the dunes, Roque Nublo, Vegueta, Las Canteras and the Botanical Garden.
- **Weather:** the south, 300 days of sun; Las Palmas and the north, "panza de burro" (low cloud) in summer; the summits can freeze in winter. Always keep an extra layer in the car.

## Where to stay, what to eat and tips

**Where to stay:** Maspalomas, Playa del Inglés and Meloneras for sun and beach; Puerto de Mogán for charming calm; Las Palmas for city life, food and the north; rural houses in Tejeda or Artenara to sleep under the stars.

**What to eat:** papas arrugadas with mojo, flor de Guía cheese, sancocho, gofio, bienmesabe from Tejeda, Arucas rum and Monte Lentiscal wines. For tapas, the **Mercado del Puerto** in Las Palmas, Thursday to Saturday evenings; Vegueta has a market on Saturdays.

**Common mistakes:**

1. **Never leaving the south:** the interior is less than an hour away and is the best of the island.
2. **Going up to the summits without a jacket:** it can be 25 °C on the beach and 8 °C on Pico de las Nieves.
3. **Leaving the trails in the dunes:** it's a nature reserve and it's forbidden.
