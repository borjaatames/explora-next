**Gran Canaria is one of the best places in Europe to catch your first wave.** The water is mild all year, there are sandy beaches with gentle waves and schools with qualified instructors right on the beach. Most people manage to stand up on the board in a single lesson. Here's where to learn and how to choose a lesson.

The key: if you're staying in **Maspalomas or Playa del Inglés**, book a lesson right there; if you're in **Las Palmas**, learn at La Cícer. For a first time, a 2.5-3-hour lesson is enough.

## Where to surf

- **Playa del Inglés and Maspalomas:** gentle beach breaks over sand, ideal for beginners. Most of the southern schools are here.
- **La Cícer (Las Canteras, Las Palmas):** the southern end of the city beach, with consistent waves and a surfy vibe. A good choice if you're staying in the capital.
- **El Confital and the north coast:** more powerful reef breaks, for experienced surfers only.

## What a lesson is like

- **Duration:** 2.5 to 4 hours, with breaks.
- **What's included:** a soft beginner board, wetsuit, rash vest and instructor. Some schools add photos or hotel transfer.
- **How it goes:** warm-up and briefing on the sand, practice standing up on the board, then into the water, always where you can stand.
- **Groups:** usually small, with several students per instructor; private lessons cost more.
- **Age:** many schools accept children from 6-8 if they can swim.

## Tips

- **Book early in your trip:** if you enjoy it, you can repeat or rent a board another day.
- **Bring** swimwear underneath, a towel, water-resistant sunscreen and drinking water.
- **Don't worry about fitness:** being able to swim is enough; tired arms are normal.
- **Follow the flags and your instructor,** and don't surf in marked swimming areas.

## When to go

You can surf **all year**. The water is around 19 °C in winter and 23-24 °C at the end of summer; the school's wetsuit is enough. In winter there are more waves in the north; in summer the south is calmer and perfect for your first steps.

Combine the lesson with the [Maspalomas dunes](/en/guides/gran-canaria/maspalomas-dunes-guide), a few minutes from Playa del Inglés. For the rest of the trip, [what to see in Gran Canaria](/en/guides/gran-canaria/what-to-see-in-gran-canaria).

## In one sentence

For your first wave, a 2.5-3-hour lesson at Playa del Inglés or Maspalomas (or La Cícer if you're in Las Palmas), with board and wetsuit included, at any time of year.
