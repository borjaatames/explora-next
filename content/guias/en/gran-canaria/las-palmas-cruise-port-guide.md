## Where does the ship dock? The Santa Catalina cruise terminal

At the **Muelle de Santa Catalina**, on the Las Palmas isthmus, next to **Santa Catalina park** and the El Muelle shopping centre. If many ships are in port at once, some may dock at another pier: check the daily programme and whether the cruise line runs a shuttle.

| From the pier | Distance | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Santa Catalina park | A few minutes | On foot |
| Las Canteras beach | 10-15 min | On foot |
| Vegueta and Triana (old town) | About 4-5 km | Bus line 1 or taxi |
| Maspalomas (south) | 45-60 min | Global bus line 30 or excursion |

## Getting around: from the cruise terminal to Vegueta and beyond

- **City bus line 1 (Puerto-Teatro).** City buses (guaguas) are the yellow ones. Line 1 crosses the city north to south and ends by the Pérez Galdós theatre, between Triana and Vegueta. A **single ticket costs €1.40**, paid on board.
- **Taxi.** With the urban fare in force since October 2025 (€3.05 flag fall on weekdays, €0.85 per km and a €0.70 port access supplement), Vegueta costs **about €8-10**. On Saturdays and Sundays the per-km rate is slightly higher.
- **Global bus line 30** (the blue intercity buses). Leaves Santa Catalina for Faro de Maspalomas for **€6.80**, roughly every 20 minutes on weekdays.

## Things to do in Las Palmas on a cruise, by time ashore

| Time ashore | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| **4 hours** | Bus or taxi to **Vegueta**: Santa Ana cathedral (climb the tower for the view), Casa de Colón and Plaza de Santa Ana. Back via Triana and some time at **Las Canteras**. |
| **6 hours** | The above without rushing, lunch in Vegueta or on the Las Canteras promenade, and a swim or a walk to La Puntilla. With kids, the **Poema del Mar** aquarium by the port. |
| **8 hours or more** | The island's interior (**Roque Nublo**, Tejeda) on an excursion, or the south (**Maspalomas** and Puerto de Mogán). Only if the ship is in port most of the day. |

### Vegueta and Triana

**Vegueta** is the city's founding district: cobbled streets, wooden balconies and **Santa Ana cathedral**.

- **Cathedral:** Monday to Friday 10:00-18:00 (last entry 17:30) and Saturdays 10:00-16:00; on Sundays only the tower, 10:00-14:00.
- **Casa de Colón:** 9:00-19:00 on weekdays and 9:00-15:00 at weekends.

If you want to understand it all quickly, there are two-and-a-half-hour private guided walks through Triana and Vegueta with a tapa included.

### Las Canteras

**Las Canteras beach** has more than two kilometres of sand and a promenade along its full length. A natural rock bar offshore calms the water along much of the beach. Just 10-15 minutes' walk from the pier, it's the ideal plan for your last hours before boarding.

### Poema del Mar

The **Poema del Mar** aquarium is on the Muelle Sanapú, in the same port area: the easiest plan with children or on a rainy day.

### Roque Nublo and Maspalomas: only with time

- **Roque Nublo.** It's over an hour of mountain road. The La Goleta car park is closed: you park in Cruz de Los Llanos or Tejeda and go up by shuttle bus. Between 9:00 and 17:00 you must **book a slot** at reservasroquenublo.com. The trail is about 3 km there and back, 1-1.5 hours. Allow at least 5 hours in total.
- **Maspalomas.** The dunes are 45-60 minutes from the port. Bus 30 is cheap, but with the journey there, back and waiting, it eats half a day.

The excursion from Las Palmas to Roque Nublo, Pico de las Nieves and Tejeda lasts 8 hours and picks up in the centre early in the morning: **it only works if you're ashore before pick-up** and the ship sails in the evening. Check the meeting point and time on your voucher.

## Gran Canaria shore excursions: ship's tour or independent?

| | Ship's excursion | Independent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | Highest | Bus €1.40, taxi €8-10 |
| **If you're late** | The ship waits | You're responsible for getting back |
| **Las Palmas city** | Unnecessary | Very easy on your own |
| **Interior or south** | Safer on a tight call | Only with 8+ hours and a margin |

A **private tour from the port** is the middle ground: the panoramic tour by taxi and on foot covers viewpoints, Las Canteras, Santa Catalina and Vegueta in three and a half hours. If you arrive by cruise, tell the provider when booking so they can set the meeting point.

## When should you be back on board?

- **All aboard** is usually 30-60 minutes before departure. It's in the daily programme.
- From Vegueta, allow 30 minutes by bus and on foot; by taxi, about 15 minutes.
- From the interior or the south, leave **at least an hour's margin**: the motorway into Las Palmas gets busy in the afternoon.

## Tips: Sundays and common mistakes

**Sundays and public holidays:** Las Canteras fills with locals and is at its liveliest. Casa de Colón opens 9:00-15:00 and the cathedral only lets you climb the tower, 10:00-14:00. Many shops in Triana close and buses run less often.

**Mistakes to avoid:**

1. **Walking to Vegueta.** It's 4-5 km along avenues: the bus costs €1.40.
2. **Planning Roque Nublo without booking a slot** or with less than 8 hours.
3. **Leaving Vegueta for Sunday afternoon.** The cathedral only opens the tower in the morning and Casa de Colón closes at 15:00.
4. **Forgetting sunscreen.** At Las Canteras the sun is strong even in winter.
