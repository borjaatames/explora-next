**Puerto de Mogán is the prettiest village on Gran Canaria's south coast.** Its canals and little bridges have earned it the nickname "the little Venice of the Canaries", and among white houses trimmed in colour and bougainvillea there's a calm beach, a harbour full of boats and plenty of sea activities. Here's what to do and how to plan your visit.

The key: half a day is enough to **see the village**; if you want to **get out on the water**, book a morning activity and save the afternoon for a stroll and fresh fish at the harbour.

## What to see in the village

- **The fishermen's quarter:** pedestrian streets with two-storey white houses, bougainvillea and small canals with bridges. The most photogenic part.
- **The harbour:** fishing boats, yachts and the fish market; boat trips and the submarine leave from here.
- **Mogán beach:** golden sand sheltered by the breakwater, with calm water for swimming.
- **The viewpoint:** climb the streets behind the village for the best view of the harbour and the valley.

## Sea activities

- **Submarine (40 min):** goes down a few metres to see fish and the seabed through large windows. Ideal with children and for those who'd rather stay dry.
- **Kayaking to the sea caves (3-4 h):** you paddle along the volcanic cliffs, into caves and stop to snorkel. Adventure versions include jumps from the rocks.
- **Dolphin-watching boat (4 h):** leaves from the harbour towards the west coast, with a swim stop.
- **Boat trip with wine:** a short sunset outing with a wine pairing, in a small group.

## The Friday market

Every **Friday morning** the harbour fills with stalls selling clothes, crafts, souvenirs and local produce. It's lively but crowded: if you go, arrive before 10:00 or join a trip that includes transport from the south.

## How to get there

- **By car:** take the GC-1 motorway to the end and then the GC-500. There are paid car parks at the entrance to the village; on Fridays they fill up early.
- **By bus:** direct routes from Maspalomas, Playa del Inglés, Puerto Rico and Las Palmas.
- **By boat:** the scheduled boat links Arguineguín, Puerto Rico and Mogán; arriving by sea is part of the fun.
- **On a tour:** some island day tours and market trips include hotel pick-up.

## Tips

- **Avoid Friday** if you want peace and quiet; on other days the village is much more relaxed.
- **Book kayaks and boats in the morning,** when the sea is usually calmer.
- **Eat at the harbour:** fresh fish of the day and wrinkled potatoes with mojo.
- **At sunset** the tour groups leave and the light over the harbour is lovely.

## In one sentence

Half a day in Mogán is enough to stroll the harbour, swim at its beach and do one sea activity (submarine, kayak or boat); any day but Friday is calmer, unless you're going for the market.
