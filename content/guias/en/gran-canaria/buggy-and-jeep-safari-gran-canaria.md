**Inland southern Gran Canaria is a maze of ravines, palm groves and dirt tracks** a few minutes from the beaches of Maspalomas. Exploring it by buggy or jeep is one of the island's most popular trips. There are two ways to do it: drive a buggy yourself on tracks for 2-3 hours, or ride in a jeep with a driver up to the reservoirs and mountain villages. Here's how to choose.

The key: if you want to **drive and get dusty**, choose a 2-3-hour buggy tour. If you'd rather **see the scenery without driving**, a half-day jeep safari takes you further and higher.

## Buggy: what to expect

- **Where:** dirt tracks through the southern ravines, between Maspalomas, Fataga, Ayagaures and inland Mogán.
- **How:** you drive in a line behind a guide, with stops at viewpoints for photos. The tracks are bumpy and winding, but no experience is needed.
- **2- or 4-seat buggy:** the 4-seater is cheaper per person for families or friends.
- **Pick-up:** many tours collect you from hotels in Maspalomas, Playa del Inglés and Puerto Rico; some also leave from Las Palmas.

## Jeep safari: what to expect

- **Where:** mountain roads and tracks towards the centre of the island, with reservoirs such as Chira or Soria, the Fataga ravine and villages like San Bartolomé de Tirajana.
- **How:** a guide drives and you enjoy the views. Stops at viewpoints, in a village and sometimes to taste local products.
- **Duration:** half a day (about 5 hours), with pick-up in the south.

## Tips

- **Bring your licence** if you're driving the buggy: without it you can't go.
- **Clothes and shoes** that can get dirty; sunglasses and a scarf for the dust.
- **Early morning or late afternoon is best** in summer, when the sun in the ravines is less intense.
- **If you get carsick,** the jeep on mountain roads has lots of bends: sit in the front.
- **If pregnant or with back problems,** ask first; the tracks are very bumpy.

## When to go

**All year.** In winter the interior is greener and cooler; in summer the ravines are hot, so pick a morning slot.
