The Andalucía Playa is in Novo Sancti Petri, Chiclana, with the best access to La Barrosa beach, a long stretch of fine sand on the Atlantic recognised for its ecological quality.

It's a 5-star with gardens, three outdoor pools and an indoor one, a spa, a gourmet restaurant and an all-inclusive option. Some rooms have a semi-private pool. Children have the Star Camp club.

Golfers have a course next door, with a free shuttle. Cádiz, Vejer and Jerez are all under an hour's drive.
