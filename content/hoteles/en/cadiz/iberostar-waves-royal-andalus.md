The Royal Andalus (formerly Iberostar Royal Andalus) is in Novo Sancti Petri, next to its sibling the Andalucía Playa, with direct access to La Barrosa: more than five kilometres of fine sand with a Blue Flag.

It's the family hotel of the pair: all-inclusive, the Aquafun water area, the Star Camp club and family rooms. There's also a spa (extra charge) and a 24-hour gym.

Its other big draw is golf: the 36-hole Real Novo Sancti Petri, designed by Seve Ballesteros, with a free shuttle.
