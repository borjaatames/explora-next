The Bouganville Playa (formerly Iberostar Bouganville Playa) has one of the handiest locations in Costa Adeje: right on the San Eugenio promenade, 20 metres from El Bobo beach and beside Puerto Colón, where many boat trips along the south coast depart.

It's a family-focused 4-star with three children's pools with slides, the Star Camp club and an all-inclusive option. A Star Prestige area offers more peace for those who want it.

El Bobo is a small, dark-sand beach; if you'd prefer pale sand, Fañabé and Playa del Duque are a stroll along the coast.
