The Sábila is Iberostar's adults-only choice in Costa Adeje: a clean-lined, light-filled 5-star built for switching off, in the Torviscas and Fañabé area.

Food is its strong suit. Instead of a classic buffet there's a food market with seven different kitchens. The spa works with aloe vera, the plant the hotel is named after ("sábila" is the Canarian word for aloe).

Torviscas and Fañabé beaches and the Puerto Colón marina are a few minutes' walk away, as are the restaurants and shops along the promenade.
