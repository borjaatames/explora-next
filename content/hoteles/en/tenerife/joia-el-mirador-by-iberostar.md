El Mirador stands on Costa Adeje's so-called golden mile, right on Playa del Duque and close to the golf course. It used to be the Iberostar Grand El Mirador; today it belongs to JOIA, the chain's luxury brand.

The décor is romantic and carefully detailed, and every room is a suite. The infinity pool looks out over the Atlantic and La Gomera. In the Star Prestige area, La Balconada, there's butler and concierge service.

It's the place for a calm couples' getaway: the beach in front, a spa with a hydrotherapy circuit and à la carte restaurants, with no children.
