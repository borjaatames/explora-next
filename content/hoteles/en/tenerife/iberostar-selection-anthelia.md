The Anthelia sits on the front line of Fañabé beach, one of the easiest beaches in Costa Adeje, surrounded by gardens. It's a large resort built for spending the day on site: a saltwater pool, two freshwater pools and three for children, five restaurants and three bars.

What sets it apart is how well it works for families, with the Star Camp club and the pirate-ship area, while keeping 5-star touches such as the spa (extra charge) and sea-view suites.

From here you can walk along the promenade to Playa del Duque or Puerto Colón, and the South airport is about a quarter of an hour away.
