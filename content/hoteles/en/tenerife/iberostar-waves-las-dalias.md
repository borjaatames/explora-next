Las Dalias (formerly Iberostar Las Dalias) is Iberostar's family all-inclusive in Costa Adeje. It isn't on the seafront: El Bobo beach is about a 10-minute walk and Playa de las Américas about 24.

In return you get spacious rooms, family rooms for groups of up to six adults, two pools (one heated from November to May), shows and the Star Camp club, with activities for three age groups. Adults have a jacuzzi to themselves.

It's a practical choice if having everything sorted with children matters most, close to the promenade and Puerto Colón.
