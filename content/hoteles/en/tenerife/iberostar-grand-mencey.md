The Grand Mencey is the hotel with the most history in Santa Cruz: open for more than 75 years, it keeps its Canarian architecture, courtyard and gardens, and has hosted royalty, actors and musicians. Today it's the capital's only 5-star hotel.

The big draw is the spa, over 4,500 m², with a water circuit, sauna, whirlpool and treatments (extra charge). Iberostar suggests Papa Negra's "sprunch": 90 minutes in the circuit followed by brunch.

For us it's the best base for the less beach-focused side of Tenerife: Anaga, La Laguna and Puerto de la Cruz are all closer than from the south.
