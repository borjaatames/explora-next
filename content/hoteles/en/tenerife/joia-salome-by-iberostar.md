The Salomé is a small hotel inside a big one. It sits within the Anthelia complex, facing Fañabé beach, but runs separately: adults only, suites only and a butler on hand around the clock. It used to be called the Iberostar Grand Salomé.

The advantage is that you get the calm of a boutique hotel, with two pools looking out to sea, plus everything the resort offers: restaurants, bars, spa and gardens.

The gourmet breakfast is served on the Terraza Mirador, and the Costa Adeje promenade starts at the door.
