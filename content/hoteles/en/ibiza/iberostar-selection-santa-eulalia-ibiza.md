The Santa Eulalia faces s'Argamassa beach, one of Ibiza's quietest, surrounded by pine woods and native plants. It's an adults-only hotel built for switching off, far from the party scene in the west of the island.

There's a pool, a spa with massages and treatments, a chill-out area, a gym and an all-inclusive option. Star Prestige rooms give access to reserved services and areas.

A few minutes away are the Punta Arabí hippy market and the centre of Santa Eulària, with its promenade and marina.
