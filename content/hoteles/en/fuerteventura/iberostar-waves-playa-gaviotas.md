The Playa Gaviotas (formerly Iberostar Playa Gaviotas) sits right on the Jandía beaches, with direct access to the sand. It's the liveliest of the three Iberostar hotels in Las Gaviotas: all-inclusive, a Canarian buffet, several bars (the tapas bar is a favourite) and even a bowling alley with games.

There's the Star Camp club for children and a Star Prestige area for guests aged 15 and over. The spa (extra charge) and indoor pool are shared with the neighbouring Gaviotas Park.

The Morro Jable lighthouse and the Playa del Matorral promenade are about three kilometres away.
