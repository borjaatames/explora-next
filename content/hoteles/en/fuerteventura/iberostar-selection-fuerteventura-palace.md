The Fuerteventura Palace is in Las Gaviotas, Jandía, facing one of the longest white-sand beaches in the Canaries. It was recently renovated and is geared towards couples looking to unwind.

There are four pools, one of them indoor and heated, a spa with aromatherapy and massages, yoga and speciality restaurants, with an all-inclusive option. The Star Prestige area adds exclusive services.

Jandía is at the island's southern tip: ideal for staying by the sea, but a long way from the airport and Corralejo.
