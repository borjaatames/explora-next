The Gaviotas Park (formerly Iberostar Playa Gaviotas Park) has been renovated for families and couples. It's in Las Gaviotas, Jandía, facing the beach, next to its two siblings, the Fuerteventura Palace and the Playa Gaviotas.

Rooms are spacious suites and junior suites, some with direct pool access and family suites of up to 74 m². It's all-inclusive, with five pools, the Star Camp club and a handy loan service for children's equipment.

Jandía's beaches, with white sand and calm water, are among the island's best for children.
