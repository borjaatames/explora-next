This 5-star sits right on Playa de Palma, five minutes from the airport and a quarter of an hour from the city centre. It's hard to find a handier base for a short Mallorca break.

The design is modern and bright, with a spa (extra charge) and an adults-only Star Prestige area whose rooftop has an infinity pool looking out to sea. At sunset, the Sunset Champagne Bar is the place to be.

Dining is more limited than at a resort: one restaurant serving Mallorcan cooking and four bars, with the promenade full of alternatives right outside.
