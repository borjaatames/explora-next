The Club Cala Barca (formerly Iberostar Club Cala Barca) is a huge resort: Iberostar says it covers more than ten football pitches. It's in Barca Trencada, in south-east Mallorca, with direct access to a sandy cove surrounded by pines.

Everything revolves around families: ten pools (five for children, with a pirate ship), an Aquafun area, padel, a diving club and the Star Camp programme for kids and teens, all on an all-inclusive basis. It's also certified for coeliacs.

Mondragó Natural Park and the village of Santanyí are close, but the airport is about 50 minutes away: it's a hotel for settling in.
