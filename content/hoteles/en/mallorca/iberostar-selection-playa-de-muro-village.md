The Playa de Muro Village is Iberostar's 5-star family hotel in the north of Mallorca, a step from Playa de Muro, one of the beaches travellers rate most highly in Spain. Almost the whole hotel is family suites.

For kids there's a water park with slides, the Adventure Park and the Star Camp club; for grown-ups, a spa, tennis and facilities for cyclists. There's an indoor pool and an all-inclusive option with three restaurants.

S'Albufera Natural Park, with its boardwalks for birdwatching, is right next door, and Alcudia is an easy bike ride away.
