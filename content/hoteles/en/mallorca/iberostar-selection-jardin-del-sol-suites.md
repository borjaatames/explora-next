The Jardín del Sol Suites is an adults-only hotel in Costa de la Calma, in a quiet part of Santa Ponsa Bay in the south-west of the island. It's on the seafront, beside a small, sheltered cove.

Every room is a suite or junior suite, and some have an outdoor hot tub. There are three pools plus a heated indoor one, a spa, a gourmet restaurant and an all-inclusive option.

It's a good spot for a calm couples' break, and golfers will like it: there are five courses nearby. A car makes it easier to get around the island.
