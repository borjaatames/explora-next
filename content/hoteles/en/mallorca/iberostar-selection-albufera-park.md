The Albufera Park is a family resort on the Playa de Muro seafront, designed for kids to have a great time: 46 m² family rooms, the Star Camp club, a children's pool and a splash pool.

What sets it apart is the sheer choice: seven pools, a new rooftop with a pool and sea views, and an all-inclusive with six restaurants and three bars. It also accepts pets (extra charge), which is unusual for a beach resort.

Next door is S'Albufera Natural Park, one of the best places in Mallorca for birdwatching.
