The Cala Domingos (formerly Iberostar Cala Domingos) is in Calas de Mallorca on the south-east coast, with direct access to the turquoise water of Cala Domingos Petit. Most rooms are bungalow-style cottages spread across a large complex surrounded by countryside.

It's all-inclusive, with three pools, a Mediterranean restaurant overlooking the cove in July and August, plenty of sport and the Star Camp programme. There's in-resort transport for getting around the grounds.

The best moment, according to the hotel itself, is sunset from the bar terrace.
