The Bahía de Palma is Iberostar's adults-only hotel in Playa de Palma: right on the seafront promenade, with direct access to the sand and the airport three kilometres away.

The rooftop is the highlight, with a pool and bar overlooking the whole bay. There's also a spa of more than 400 m² and seafront restaurants using local produce, and the décor draws on Mallorcan tradition.

Rooms are on the compact side, so look at the superior rooms or junior suites if you'll spend time in them.
