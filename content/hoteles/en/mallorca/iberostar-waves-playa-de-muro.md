The Waves Playa de Muro (formerly Iberostar Playa de Muro) opens straight onto Playa de Muro, almost six kilometres of sand that Tripadvisor users voted Spain's best beach in 2022.

It's a family hotel with rooms with big terraces, a play pool and the Star Camp club, but also plenty of sport: facilities for cyclists and tennis players, a gym, a spa and a heated indoor pool.

Compared with its neighbour, the 5-star Playa de Muro Village, it's a simpler 4-star with smaller rooms.
