The Es Trenc is in Colonia de Sant Jordi, in the south of Mallorca: the area of turquoise-water beaches such as Es Trenc, and the departure point for boats to Cabrera National Park. It was fully renovated in 2025.

Every room has a sea view and a private terrace. There are two pools, a rooftop with views and Salivent, a Mediterranean restaurant using seasonal produce. In summer there's a free shuttle to the beach.

It's a long way from Palma and the airport, so it's more a hotel for staying put and enjoying the south than a base for touring the whole island.
