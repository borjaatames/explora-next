The Alcudia Park (formerly Iberostar Alcudia Park) sits right on Playa de Muro, the beach Tripadvisor users voted Spain's best in 2022. It's a bright, modern hotel designed for families.

Kids get Aquafun, a play pool and the Star Camp club; there's also a heated indoor pool, a 24-hour gym and a spa. For a couples' break, Star Prestige rooms give access to a rooftop lounge overlooking the bay.

Puerto de Alcudia and S'Albufera Natural Park are a stroll along the coast.
