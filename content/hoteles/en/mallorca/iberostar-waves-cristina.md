The Cristina (formerly Iberostar Cristina) is Iberostar's family hotel in Playa de Palma, a five-minute walk from the beach and three kilometres from the airport. It runs on all-inclusive or half board, with the Els Molins buffet and its live cooking.

It's very focused on sport and wellbeing: tennis, padel, spinning, yoga by the sea, a bike garage and a spa with a heated indoor pool. Children have the Star Camp club and their own pool.

Iberostar describes it as a zero-CO2 hotel running on 100% renewable electricity, with its own vegetable garden.
