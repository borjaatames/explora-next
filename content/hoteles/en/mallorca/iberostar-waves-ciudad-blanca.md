The Ciudad Blanca (formerly Iberostar Ciudad Blanca) is right on Alcudia Bay, under five kilometres from the walled town, one of Mallorca's prettiest.

Unlike Iberostar's other hotels in the area, you stay in equipped studios and apartments, with more room for families. There are three pools, the Star Camp club, the Sea Soul Beach Club on the beach itself and an all-inclusive option. Pets are welcome (extra charge).

It's a good base for mixing the beach with trips to Alcúdia, Pollença or Cap de Formentor.
