The Albufera Playa is right on Playa de Muro, for many the best beach in Mallorca, and surrounded by S'Albufera Natural Park. It has been fully renovated and has pine and olive groves within the grounds.

There are swim-up rooms, an indoor pool and an all-inclusive with six restaurants and three bars. The adults-only Star Prestige area has a rooftop with a pool and views.

It suits active couples and families alike, with the Star Camp club for children.
