The Pinos Park (formerly Iberostar Pinos Park) is in a peaceful corner of eastern Mallorca, inside a large pine forest and right on Font de sa Cala beach in Capdepera.

Everything is designed for families: the Aquafun area with slides and a waterfall, the Star Camp club, sport and shows, with an all-inclusive option. Family rooms are 39 m².

It's a hotel for people who want beach and nature without the bustle of the big resorts. Cala Ratjada and the other north-east coves are close by.
