The Llaüt Palma looks out over the Mediterranean above a pine wood in Playa de Palma, a few minutes from the beach and four kilometres from the airport. It's a good base for exploring the island: Palma is a quarter of an hour's drive away.

Wellbeing is what sets it apart. There's a 1,300 m² spa with a heated pool, yoga and Pilates, plus Katagi Blau, an Asian-Mediterranean fusion restaurant with sea views that locals go to as well.

The hotel is free of single-use plastics and rents out bikes and electric cars for touring Mallorca.
