The Marbella Coral Beach sits on the seafront between Marbella and Puerto Banús, a few minutes from the marina and five kilometres from the centre. Marbella enjoys a mild microclimate thanks to the surrounding mountains.

There's a beach club, a pool, a spa with sauna and a 24-hour gym, plus an all-inclusive option. Rooms are spacious, and some have a private jacuzzi or access to a shared pool.

It's a good base for combining beach, golf and trips to Ronda, Gibraltar or the white villages.
