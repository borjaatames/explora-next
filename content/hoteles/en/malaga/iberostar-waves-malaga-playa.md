The Málaga Playa (formerly Iberostar Málaga Playa) is in Torrox Costa, in the Axarquía east of Málaga. It's a spacious, Andalusian-style hotel with gardens, direct beach access and a buffet with an outdoor terrace.

It's designed for families: 31 m² rooms, studios and apartments, a play pool, the Star Camp club and a heated indoor pool in winter, plus a spa and an all-inclusive option.

The area is great for day trips: Nerja and its caves, Frigiliana and the other white villages of the Axarquía are close, and the Alhambra is about an hour away.
