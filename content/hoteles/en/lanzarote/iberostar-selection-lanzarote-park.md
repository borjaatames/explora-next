The Lanzarote Park sits on the Playa Blanca seafront in the south of the island. Its design was overseen by César Manrique, and its shape echoes three "zocos", the semicircular volcanic-stone walls used in Lanzarote to shelter vines, opening onto the ocean.

It's a 5-star for families and couples alike: four heated pools (two for children) plus an indoor one, Star Camp, teen activities, a spa and a Star Prestige area with its own restaurant. All-inclusive is available.

It's a very comfortable base for exploring the south: Papagayo, Los Hervideros, El Golfo and Timanfaya are all under half an hour away.
