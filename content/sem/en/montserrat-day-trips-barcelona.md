faqs:
  titulo: "What you ask before booking"
  items:
    - pregunta: "How long does it take to reach Montserrat from Barcelona?"
      respuesta: "Approximately 1 hour by bus or van (60 km northwest of Barcelona). Tours usually depart between 8:00 and 10:00 from central Barcelona (Estació del Nord, Passeig de Gràcia or Plaça Catalunya, depending on the operator). The full day lasts 7-10 hours and the half day 5-6. If you book the winery tour, add 2-3 extra hours for the tasting and lunch."

    - pregunta: "What exactly is the Moreneta and why do people want to see her?"
      respuesta: "The Moreneta (the Black Virgin) is a 12th-century polychrome carving in the basilica's camarín. She has been Catalonia's patron saint since 1881 and one of the country's most important spiritual symbols. To touch the Virgin's hand (the tradition) you have to queue separately inside the basilica — this is NOT included in every tour, so check that your option says «Black Madonna entry» or «with Virgin Negra». The Escolanía, the boys' choir, performs on school days around 13:00."

    - pregunta: "Do I need the rack railway or the funicular?"
      respuesta: "The rack railway (Cremallera de Montserrat) is the historic lift that climbs from the valley to the monastery in 12-15 minutes. Most tours take you directly to the monastery by bus, so the rack railway is optional and usually an operator upgrade. The Sant Joan funicular is a different one: it goes FROM the monastery to one of the highest viewpoints. If you love views it's worth it; if your goal is the basilica, it's not essential."

    - pregunta: "Which one should I pick if I'm short on time in Barcelona?"
      respuesta: "The classic half day (Julià Travel, €50) covers the essentials in 5-7 hours: rack railway + basilica + Moreneta + free time. If you only have a half day available and want the most efficient option, that's the default. If you have a whole free day, the full-day Castlexperience tour with winery and tapas (€47) adds a food experience that justifies the extra time and barely raises the price."

    - pregunta: "And if I want to go at my own pace, no large group?"
      respuesta: "The small-group options (4-8 people) are the best balance of cost and comfort. Mont Escape Tours and UNIque Tours Barcelona operate with vans of up to 8 seats, certified mountain guides and included easy hike. If you want total privacy, the In Out Barcelona private tour (€204/person) includes hotel pickup and lets you stop wherever you like."

    - pregunta: "What should I bring and how should I dress?"
      respuesta: "Comfortable closed shoes (there are ramps and trails), layered clothing (the mountain is 1,236 m above sea level and noticeably colder than Barcelona, even in summer) and, if you plan to enter the basilica, a dress code: covered shoulders, no shorts, no sandals. In winter bring warm or waterproof clothing; in summer, water and sun protection."

    - pregunta: "Are the winery options worth it?"
      respuesta: "Yes, especially Oller del Mas (a 10th-century castle reconverted into an organic winery with awards) and the 12th-century Bages farmhouse. The wine-tasting options cost €3-15 more than the classic half day and add 2-3 hours, but for that price you get lunch and tasting. If you're interested in Catalan oenology, pay the difference."
