faqs:
  titulo: "What you want to know before booking"
  items:
    - pregunta: "Why are there no tickets-only options, and why are many non-refundable?"
      respuesta: "Both come from the same cause. The Alhambra Trust (Patronato) strictly limits entry tickets and they sell out weeks — or, in high season, months — in advance: in practice, getting a tickets-only entry for the date you want is nearly impossible. That's why the market works with packages of entry plus guide — the operator buys the slots in advance and guarantees your access. And because those tickets aren't refunded to the operator, the norm is a partial refund (50-60%) or a non-refundable product. It's not a downside of the operator, it's how the Alhambra works. We show the exact policy on each option, and you'll see that some private tours do offer free cancellation up to 24h before."

    - pregunta: "Do I need to book in advance?"
      respuesta: "Yes, and the earlier the better. The Alhambra is one of the most-visited monuments in Spain and tickets sell out weeks in advance, especially in high season and at weekends. Booking ahead is the only way to guarantee access to the Nasrid Palaces on the date you want."

    - pregunta: "What exactly does the visit include?"
      respuesta: "The options on this page include access to the Nasrid Palaces — the jewel of the Alhambra — plus the Generalife and its gardens and the Alcazaba. The difference between them is the format: group guide, small group, private or night visit. Each card details what its price includes and its cancellation policy."

    - pregunta: "What is the assigned time slot for the Nasrid Palaces?"
      respuesta: "The Nasrid Palaces are visited within a specific, fixed time slot assigned by the Trust; it's the only part of the Alhambra with a strict time. If you don't enter within your slot, you lose access to the palaces. That's why it's important to arrive at the meeting point with time to spare: the guide manages the group's entry within the booked slot."

    - pregunta: "What language is the tour in?"
      respuesta: "All the options on this page are available at least in Spanish and English with a live guide; several also offer French, German or Italian. You choose the language when booking."

    - pregunta: "Which should I pick: group, small group or private?"
      respuesta: "Group is the most affordable and the most-booked option, perfect if you want a good guided tour without overspending. Small group limits the number of people, so you hear the guide better and the pace is more comfortable. Private is a dedicated guide just for you: entirely at your own pace, ideal if you're travelling with kids, as a family, or want to go deeper. The night visit is a separate experience, with the palaces illuminated."

    - pregunta: "Can I visit the Alhambra with children?"
      respuesta: "Yes. The Alhambra is suitable for all ages, although it involves a fair amount of walking over uneven ground. For families with young children, the private tour is the most comfortable because it sets its own pace and lets you stop whenever needed. The night visit, being shorter, also works well with kids."
