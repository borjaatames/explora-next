faqs:
  titulo: "What you're wondering before you book"
  items:
    - pregunta: "Why book here and not on the official website?"
      respuesta: "The Sagrada Familia only sells tickets online and works with limited-capacity time slots that sell out weeks in advance. In 2026, with the Gaudí Year and the inauguration of the Tower of Jesus, demand is even higher and the official site runs out of availability more often. The operators on this page hold pre-booked allocations, so they can guarantee you access on the date you want even when the official site shows «sold out». On top of that, the guided options give you the context that makes you actually understand what you're looking at."

    - pregunta: "Do the tickets include going up the towers?"
      respuesta: "Only the options marked «with towers». Going up one of the two towers —Nativity or Passion, you choose one— is an extra on top of the basic ticket. Bear in mind you go up by lift but come down a narrow spiral staircase: it is not wheelchair accessible, it is not recommended if you have vertigo or claustrophobia, and children under 6 cannot go up. Each card states whether towers are included."

    - pregunta: "How far in advance should I book?"
      respuesta: "The sooner the better. We recommend 4-8 weeks ahead to get a good time slot. In 2026, because of the Gaudí Year, it is worth doubling that if you are visiting at Easter, on long weekends or in summer: the Sagrada Familia receives around 4.5 million visitors a year and demand this year is exceptional."

    - pregunta: "Which should I choose: fast-track entry, guided tour or with towers?"
      respuesta: "Fast-track entry (access to the basilica, usually with an audio guide) is enough for a first visit: what really impresses —the stained glass and the tree-like columns— you see with the basic ticket. The group guided tour adds an official guide who explains the symbolism in depth. The «with towers» option adds going up one tower. If you are torn between the towers extra and spending that budget on another Gaudí attraction, the latter usually pays off more."

    - pregunta: "Is there a dress code?"
      respuesta: "Yes. The Sagrada Familia is an active Catholic church and enforces a dress code at the entrance: covered shoulders (no tank tops), skirts and trousers below mid-thigh, no swimwear or see-through clothing, and no going in barefoot or with a cap inside the nave. Dress as you would to enter a European church so you don't get a nasty surprise at the door."

    - pregunta: "What will I see inside?"
      respuesta: "The central nave, with its leaning columns that branch out like trees; the stained-glass windows, among the most spectacular in the world, in cool tones on the Nativity façade (east) and warm tones on the Passion façade (west); both façades from the inside; and, in the basement, the crypt where Gaudí is buried and the Gaudí Museum, which is where the whole project comes together. Allow 1h 30min - 2h without towers, 2h 30min - 3h with towers."

    - pregunta: "Can I visit with children?"
      respuesta: "Yes. Children under 11 enter free (maximum two per adult), but they need their own named ticket, issued in the same booking. The basilica is suitable for all ages; the only restriction is the towers, which children under 6 cannot go up."
